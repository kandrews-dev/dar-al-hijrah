## Working principles (reminder)
1. **Lessons taught on-page** — PDFs are optional aids only.
2. **Only Imams of Ahl al-Sunnah** as sources: Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb, Ibn Bāz, Ibn ʿUthaymīn, al-Albānī, al-Saʿdī, al-Fawzān, al-Mubārakfūrī.
3. **No verbatim copyrighted text** — explanations in our own words, with attribution.
4. **All builds happen in Claude Code** — chat for visual mockups only.
