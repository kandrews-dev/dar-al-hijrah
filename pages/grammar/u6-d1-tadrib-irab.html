تدريب الإعراب ١

Parsing Practice 1

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Parse complete simple sentences word by word, from first word to last.
  • Identify the فاعل (doer), مبتدأ (subject), خبر (predicate), مفعول به (direct object) and مضاف إليه (possessor) in real sentences.
  • Recite an iʿrāb line the madrasah way — type, role, case, sign.
  • Move from knowing the rules to using them.
Where we are: Year 1 taught — and examined — every rule of the Ājurrūmiyyah core: the three cases, their signs, the verbal and nominal sentences, the abrogators, and the genitives. Year 2 is التطبيق (application). Each drill takes real sentences and parses them completely, exactly as students recite iʿrāb aloud in a madrasah. The rules do not change from here — they get used.

How to recite iʿrāb — كيف تُعرِب

The madrasah formula has four parts, said in order for every word: its type (noun, verb or particle) · its role (fāʿil, mubtadaʾ, khabar…) · its case · its sign. Recited in full for زَيْدٌ in قَامَ زَيْدٌ:

«زَيْدٌ: اسمٌ، فاعلٌ مرفوعٌ، وعلامةُ رفعِهِ الضمةُ الظاهرةُ على آخرِهِ»

"Zayd: a noun; the doer, in rafʿ; and the sign of its rafʿ is the visible ḍammah on its end." Say it aloud every time — the tongue memorises what the eye forgets.

أمثلة مُعرَبة Five Worked Examples — read each aloud

المثال ١ — verbal sentence: فعل + فاعل

قَامَ زَيْدٌ

قَامَ — فعلٌ ماضٍ مبنيٌّ على الفتح (a past-tense verb, fixed on fatḥah).
زَيْدٌ — فاعلٌ مرفوعٌ وعلامةُ رفعِهِ الضمةُ الظاهرة (the doer, marfūʿ; its sign of rafʿ is the visible ḍammah).
Two words, and the sentence is complete: "Zayd stood."

المثال ٢ — nominal sentence: مبتدأ + خبر

المَسْجِدُ كَبِيرٌ

المَسْجِدُ — مبتدأٌ مرفوعٌ وعلامةُ رفعِهِ الضمةُ الظاهرة (the subject, marfūʿ by the ḍammah).
كَبِيرٌ — خبرٌ مرفوعٌ وعلامةُ رفعِهِ الضمةُ الظاهرة (the predicate, marfūʿ by the ḍammah).
Both parts marfūʿ, exactly as the rule says: "The mosque is big."

المثال ٣ — verb + doer + object

نَصَرَ اللهُ المُؤْمِنِينَ

نَصَرَ — فعلٌ ماضٍ مبنيٌّ على الفتح (a past-tense verb).
اللهُ — لفظُ الجلالةِ فاعلٌ مرفوعٌ وعلامةُ رفعِهِ الضمةُ (the Majestic Name, the doer, marfūʿ by the ḍammah).
المُؤْمِنِينَ — مفعولٌ به منصوبٌ وعلامةُ نصبِهِ الياءُ لأنه جمعُ مذكرٍ سالم (the direct object, manṣūb; its sign of naṣb is the yāʾ, because it is a sound masculine plural).
Note the sign: not a fatḥah — the sound masculine plural takes ياء in naṣb: "Allah helped the believers."

المثال ٤ — an abrogator enters: إنّ

إِنَّ اللهَ غَفُورٌ

إِنَّ — حرفُ توكيدٍ ونصبٍ (a particle of emphasis and naṣb).
اللهَ — لفظُ الجلالةِ اسمُ إنَّ منصوبٌ وعلامةُ نصبِهِ الفتحةُ (the Majestic Name, the ism of إنّ, manṣūb by the fatḥah).
غَفُورٌ — خبرُ إنَّ مرفوعٌ وعلامةُ رفعِهِ الضمةُ (the khabar of إنّ, marfūʿ by the ḍammah).
The mirror rule at work: إنّ lowers its noun to naṣb and leaves its predicate marfūʿ — "Indeed Allah is Forgiving."

المثال ٥ — iḍāfah inside a nominal sentence

كِتَابُ اللهِ نُورٌ

كِتَابُ — مبتدأٌ مرفوعٌ وعلامةُ رفعِهِ الضمةُ، وهو مضافٌ (the subject, marfūʿ by the ḍammah — and it is a muḍāf: no tanwīn, no ال).
اللهِ — لفظُ الجلالةِ مضافٌ إليه مجرورٌ وعلامةُ جرِّهِ الكسرةُ (the Majestic Name, muḍāf ilayhi, majrūr by the kasrah).
نُورٌ — خبرٌ مرفوعٌ وعلامةُ رفعِهِ الضمةُ (the predicate, marfūʿ by the ḍammah).
All three cases in one line — رفع on the mubtadaʾ and khabar, جر on the muḍāf ilayhi: "The Book of Allah is light."

Memorize — احفظ

نوعُ الكلمةِ · موقعُها · حالتُها · علامتُها

The four-part iʿrāb formula: the word's type · its role · its case · its sign. Say all four, in order, for every word of every sentence — that recitation is iʿrāb, and it is how the madrasah has always drilled it.
