الحال والتمييز

The Circumstance & the Specification

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Define الحال and know that it describes the state of the doer/object during the action — answering كيف؟ (How?).
  • Define التمييز and know that it removes ambiguity from a vague noun — often after numbers, weights, and measures.
  • Recognise that both are منصوب and usually نكرة (indefinite).
  • Tell الحال and التمييز apart, and both from the المفعول به.
  • Spot each in Qur'anic āyāt.
Where we are: Still in Unit 4 — المنصوبات. We have covered المفعول به (the object) and الظرف (time/place). Now two more accusatives that both answer a question about the sentence: الحال (in what state?) and التمييز (clarifying what exactly?). With these, you can read most Qur'anic accusatives with confidence.
أولاً: الحال Part One — al-Ḥāl (the Circumstance)

الحال اسمٌ منصوبٌ نكرةٌ يبيّن هيئة صاحبه حين وقوع الفعل

The ḥāl is an indefinite, accusative noun that describes the state or manner of its owner (صاحب الحال) at the moment the action happens. It answers the question كيف؟ (How? / In what state?). Its owner (the doer or object it describes) is usually definite.

الجملة الحال Translation
جاءَ الطالبُ ماشياً ماشياً The student came walking.
رجعَ القائدُ منصوراً منصوراً The commander returned victorious.

فَخَرَجَ مِنْهَا خَائِفًا يَتَرَقَّبُ

So he left it, fearful and vigilant. (al-Qaṣaṣ 28:21)

خَائِفًا = حال منصوب — it describes the state of Mūsā عليه السلام as he left: afraid

شَهِدَ اللَّهُ أَنَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ … قَائِمًا بِالْقِسْطِ

Allah bears witness that there is no god but Him… maintaining justice. (Āl ʿImrān 3:18)

قَائِمًا = حال منصوب — describing the state: upholding justice

ثانياً: التمييز Part Two — al-Tamyīz (the Specification)

التمييز اسمٌ نكرةٌ منصوبٌ يُذكر لبيان المقصود من اسمٍ مبهمٍ قبله

The tamyīz is an indefinite, accusative noun mentioned to clarify an ambiguous noun before it — to say in what respect something is meant. It very often follows numbers, weights, and measures, which are vague until specified.

الجملة التمييز Translation
عندي عشرونَ كتاباً كتاباً I have twenty books. (twenty of what?)
طابَ محمدٌ نفساً نفساً Muḥammad became good in soul.

وَاشْتَعَلَ الرَّأْسُ شَيْبًا

And the head has flared up with grey hair. (Maryam 19:4)

شَيْبًا = تمييز منصوب — clarifies in what respect the head "flared up": with greyness

إِنِّي رَأَيْتُ أَحَدَ عَشَرَ كَوْكَبًا

Indeed I saw eleven stars. (Yūsuf 12:4)

كَوْكَبًا = تمييز منصوب after the number — eleven of what? stars

الحال vs التمييز — telling them apart

Both are منصوب and usually نكرة, but they do different jobs:
الحال describes a state/manner during the action → answers كيف؟ (How was he/it?). جاءَ ماشياً — came how? walking.
التمييز clarifies an ambiguous amount or noun → answers «ما نوعه؟» (of what kind?). عشرونَ كتاباً — twenty of what? books.

Memorize the two definitions — احفظ التعريفين

الحال يبيّن الهيئة (كيف؟) · التمييز يزيل الإبهام (ما نوعه؟)

The ḥāl shows the state (How?); the tamyīz removes ambiguity (Of what kind?). Both are منصوب and نكرة. Build two sentences with a ḥāl and two with a tamyīz, then check each against its question.
