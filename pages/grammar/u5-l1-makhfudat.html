المخفوضات والإضافة

The Genitives & Iḍāfah

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the third and final case: الخفض (الجر), and that a noun in it is مجرور.
  • Know the حروف الجر (prepositions) that put the following noun into khafḍ.
  • Understand the الإضافة (possessive construct): مضاف + مضاف إليه, where the second noun is مجرور.
  • Recognise كسرة (ِ) as the primary sign of khafḍ.
  • Parse بِسْمِ اللَّهِ and other Qur'anic phrases — and see the whole iʿrāb picture complete.
Where we are in the Ājurrūmiyyah: You have learned two of the three cases — الرفع (المرفوعات) and النصب (المنصوبات). This lesson is the third and last: الخفض (also called الجر). Ibn Ājurrūm says the genitives come about in three ways: by a particle (حرف جر), by iḍāfah, and by following a genitive word (a tābiʿ). We take the first two here.
أولاً: الخفض بحرف الجر Part One — Genitive by a Preposition

Whenever one of the حروف الجر comes before a noun, that noun is مجرور — in the khafḍ case, marked by كسرة (ِ). Memorise the common ones:

الحرف Meaning مثال Example
مِنْfromمِنَ المسجدِfrom the mosque
إلىtoإلى البيتِto the house
عَنْabout / fromعَنِ الصلاةِabout the prayer
عَلَىon / uponعَلَى المكتبِon the desk
فِيin / atفِي القلبِin the heart
الباء (بِـ)with / byبِاللهِby Allah
اللام (لِـ)for / toلِلَّهِfor Allah
الكاف (كَـ)like / asكَالبدرِlike the full moon

Also among them: رُبَّ (many a), حتى (until), and the particles of oath و / تـ (as in وَاللهِ, تَاللهِ). Every noun directly after these is مجرور.

ثانياً: الخفض بالإضافة Part Two — Genitive by Iḍāfah

الإضافة نسبةٌ بين اسمين، الأول «مضاف» والثاني «مضاف إليه» مجرور

Iḍāfah is a relationship of possession or belonging between two nouns — often translated with "of". The first noun is the مضاف, the second is the مضاف إليه, and the مضاف إليه is always مجرور. Two rules for the مضاف: it drops its tanwīn and its ال.

الإضافة مضاف مضاف إليه (مجرور) Meaning
كتابُ اللهِكتابُاللهِthe Book of Allah
بابُ المسجدِبابُالمسجدِthe door of the mosque
رسولُ اللهِرسولُاللهِthe Messenger of Allah

إعراب — parsing بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

بِـ — a حرف جر (preposition).
اسْمِ — مجرور by the بـ (sign: كسرة); and it is a مضاف.
اللَّهِ — مضاف إليه, مجرور by the iḍāfah (sign: كسرة).
One tiny phrase, both causes of khafḍ at work — a preposition and an iḍāfah. This is the phrase you begin everything with; now you can parse it.

أمثلة قرآنية Examples from the Qur'an

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds. (al-Fātiḥah 1:2)

لِلَّهِ = مجرور by the لـ · الْعَالَمِينَ = مضاف إليه مجرور (sign = ياء, sound masc. plural)

فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌ

In their hearts is a disease. (al-Baqarah 2:10)

قُلُوبِ = مجرور by في (sign = كسرة) · and مضاف to the pronoun هم

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ

Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind. (al-Nās 114:1)

بِرَبِّ = مجرور by the بـ, and مضاف · النَّاسِ = مضاف إليه مجرور (كسرة)

Memorize — احفظ

يُخفَض الاسم بحرف الجر، وبالإضافة · وعلامته الكسرة

A noun is put into khafḍ by a preposition or by iḍāfah, and its sign is the kasrah. Open the Fātiḥah and find every مجرور: after each حرف جر, and every مضاف إليه. You now have a tool for the whole Qur'an.

🎉 The iʿrāb foundation is complete

You now know all three cases — الرفع · النصب · الخفض — their signs, and their most common causes, along with the three kinds of word and the verbal & nominal sentences. This is the core framework of al-Ājurrūmiyyah. From here the path deepens into the tawābiʿ (naʿt, ʿaṭf, tawkīd, badal) and then Qaṭr al-Nadā — but with these eleven lessons you can already begin to parse the Book of Allah.
