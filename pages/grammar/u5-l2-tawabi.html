التوابع: النعت والعطف والتوكيد والبدل

The Followers — Naʿt, ʿAṭf, Tawkīd & Badal

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know that the التوابع are words that follow the case of the word before them.
  • Recognise the four main tawābiʿ: النعت, العطف, التوكيد, and البدل.
  • See how each one follows the متبوع in case and meaning.
  • Spot the tawābiʿ in simple Qur'anic phrases.
Where we are in the Ājurrūmiyyah: You have now learned the three core cases — الرفع, النصب, and الخفض — and their most common causes. This lesson introduces the التوابع, the words that do not take an independent case, but instead follow the case of the word before them.
أولاً: النعت Part One — al-Naʿt (the Adjective)

النعت اسمٌ تابعٌ لِما قبله في الإعراب والتعريف والتنكير والجنس والعدد

The naʿt is an adjective that follows the noun it describes. It follows it in case, in definiteness, in gender, and in number. If the original noun is majrūr, the adjective is also majrūr.

الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

The Most Merciful, the especially Merciful. (al-Fātiḥah 1:3)

الرَّحِيمِ = نعتٌ تابعٌ لِرَحْمَٰنِ؛ both are majrūr because the phrase is in a genitive context.

ثانياً: العطف Part Two — al-ʿAṭf (the Conjunction)

العطف اسمٌ أو فعلٌ يتبع ما قبله بوساطةِ حرفِ العطف

The ʿaṭf is a word joined to the word before it by a conjunction such as وَ, فَ, ثُمَّ, أو. It follows the case of the word it is attached to.

خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ

He created the heavens and the earth. (al-Anʿām 6:1)

الْأَرْضَ = عطفٌ على السَّمَاوَاتِ، so it follows the same case as the first word: both are manṣūb in this context.

ثالثاً: التوكيد Part Three — al-Tawkīd (Emphasis)

التوكيد يُقوّي معنى المتبوع ويُتبعه في الإعراب

Tawkīd strengthens the meaning of the thing before it. It may be lafẓī (repeating the same word) or maʿnawī, using words like نفس، عين، كل، جميع، أجمع.

فَسَجَدَ الْمَلَائِكَةُ كُلُّهُمْ أَجْمَعُونَ

So the angels prostrated, all of them together. (al-Ḥijr 15:30)

كُلُّهُمْ أَجْمَعُونَ = توكيدٌ لِلْمَلَائِكَةِ؛ the emphasis follows the case of the original noun.

رابعاً: البدل Part Four — al-Badal (Substitution)

البدل اسمٌ يتبعُ المتبوعَ في الإعراب، ويحلُّ محلهُ أو يوضّحه

The badal is a substitute or clarifier that replaces the earlier word or explains it. It takes the same case as the word before it.

اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ

Guide us to the straight path — the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favour. (al-Fātiḥah 1:6–7)

صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ = بدلٌ من «الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ»؛ the badal follows the case of the word it substitutes — both manṣūb.

The one rule that unites them

All four types are تابع. They do not take an independent case from their own role. Instead, they follow the case of the متبوع — whether that case is rafʿ, naṣb, or khafḍ.
