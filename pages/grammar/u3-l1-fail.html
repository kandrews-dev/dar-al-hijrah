الفاعل والجملة الفعلية

The Doer & the Verbal Sentence

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Recognize a verbal sentence (jumla fiʿliyya).
  • Find the fāʿil (the doer) in any sentence.
  • Know that the fāʿil is always marfūʿ.

You now know the words (Lesson 1), the cases (Lesson 2) and their signs (Lesson 3), and the verbs (Lesson 4). Time to build a sentence. Arabic has two kinds; this lesson is the first: the verbal sentence, which begins with a verb.

الجملة الفعليةThe Verbal Sentence

A verbal sentence starts with a verb, and its core is two pieces: verb + doer (fiʿl + fāʿil). The fāʿil is the one who carried out the action — and it is always marfūʿ (rafʿ, shown by a ḍammah on a single noun).

SentenceالمثالVerb / Doer
Zayd stoodقامَ زيدٌقامَ = verb · زيدٌ = fāʿil (ḍammah)
The Muslim prayedصلَّى المسلمُصلَّى = verb · المسلمُ = fāʿil
Allah helped the believersنَصَرَ اللهُ المؤمنينَنَصَرَ = verb · اللهُ = fāʿil

The simple method — الطريقة

Find the verb, then ask: "Who did it?" The answer is the fāʿil, and you mark it marfūʿ (ḍammah). In the Qur'an: «إِنَّمَا يَخْشَى اللَّهَ مِنْ عِبَادِهِ الْعُلَمَاءُ» — who fears? الْعُلَمَاءُ (the scholars) — the fāʿil, marfūʿ with a ḍammah.

الفاعلُ مرفوعٌ

"The doer is marfūʿ." Open any āyah that begins with a verb, find the action, and ask "who did it?" — that word will carry a ḍammah.
