العَلَاقَةُ بَيْنَ الآبَاءِ وَالأَبْنَاء

The Relationship Between Parents & Children

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the vocabulary of this relationship — from عَلَاقَةٌ to بَارٌّ.
  • Read Luqmān's advice to his son directly from Sūrat Luqmān, verse by verse.
  • Meet the Qur'an's own balance: Ismāʿīl's dutifulness, and Ibrāhīm's disavowal of an unrighteous father.
  • Read the sobering contrast: Nūḥ's son, who did not listen, and drowned.

The عَلَاقَة (relationship) between parents and children has occupied every people, in every time and place — but the Qurʾān alone draws its sound foundations with real clarity. This lesson reads six real passages the Qurʾān gives us on this relationship: a father's advice, a son's dutifulness, a shared act of worship, a son's disavowal of falsehood, and a father's grief over a son who would not listen.

١ — وصية لقمان لابنه 1 — Luqmān's Advice to His Son

وَإِذْ قَالَ لُقْمَانُ لِابْنِهِ وَهُوَ يَعِظُهُ يَا بُنَيَّ لَا تُشْرِكْ بِاللَّهِ ۖ إِنَّ الشِّرْكَ لَظُلْمٌ عَظِيمٌ

"And [mention] when Luqmān said to his son while advising him: 'O my son, do not associate partners with Allah — indeed, association [with Him] is a great injustice.'"

Sūrat Luqmān 31:13 · سورة لقمان

يَا بُنَيَّ أَقِمِ الصَّلَاةَ وَأْمُرْ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَانْهَ عَنِ الْمُنكَرِ وَاصْبِرْ عَلَىٰ مَا أَصَابَكَ ۖ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ مِنْ عَزْمِ الْأُمُورِ * وَلَا تُصَعِّرْ خَدَّكَ لِلنَّاسِ وَلَا تَمْشِ فِي الْأَرْضِ مَرَحًا ۖ إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخْتَالٍ فَخُورٍ * وَاقْصِدْ فِي مَشْيِكَ وَاغْضُضْ مِنْ صَوْتِكَ ۚ إِنَّ أَنكَرَ الْأَصْوَاتِ لَصَوْتُ الْحَمِيرِ

"O my son, establish prayer, enjoin what is right, forbid what is wrong, and be patient over what befalls you — indeed, that is of the matters [requiring] determination. And do not turn your cheek [in contempt] toward people, nor walk through the earth exultantly — Allah does not like everyone self-deluded and boastful. Be moderate in your pace and lower your voice; indeed, the most disagreeable of sounds is the voice of donkeys."

Sūrat Luqmān 31:17-19 · سورة لقمان

Notice يَا بُنَيَّ — "O my little son" — a tender diminutive Luqmān repeats before every piece of advice. His first concern is ʿaqīdah (شِرْك before anything else), then worship and its two arms (أَمْر بِالْمَعْرُوف، نَهْي عَنِ الْمُنكَر) held up by صَبْر, then the manners that carry it all: no arrogance, no boastful walk, a moderate pace, a lowered voice. Even حَمِير — donkeys, the broken plural from Vocab 34 — makes a return, in the very last word of the passage.

٢ — بِرُّ إسماعيل بأبيه 2 — Ismāʿīl's Dutifulness to His Father

If Luqmān struck the greatest example in fatherhood, Ismāʿīl struck the greatest example in sonship — a father's love answered with a son's obedience, even at the hardest possible test.

فَلَمَّا بَلَغَ مَعَهُ السَّعْيَ قَالَ يَا بُنَيَّ إِنِّي أَرَىٰ فِي الْمَنَامِ أَنِّي أَذْبَحُكَ فَانظُرْ مَاذَا تَرَىٰ ۚ قَالَ يَا أَبَتِ افْعَلْ مَا تُؤْمَرُ ۖ سَتَجِدُنِي إِن شَاءَ اللَّهُ مِنَ الصَّابِرِينَ * فَلَمَّا أَسْلَمَا وَتَلَّهُ لِلْجَبِينِ * وَنَادَيْنَاهُ أَن يَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ * قَدْ صَدَّقْتَ الرُّؤْيَا ۚ إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجْزِي الْمُحْسِنِينَ * إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ الْبَلَاءُ الْمُبِينُ * وَفَدَيْنَاهُ بِذِبْحٍ عَظِيمٍ

"And when he reached with him [the age of] exertion, he said: 'O my son, indeed I have seen in a dream that I am sacrificing you, so look — what do you think?' He said: 'O my father, do as you are commanded; you will find me, if Allah wills, of the patient.' So when they had both submitted, and he laid him upon his forehead, We called out to him: 'O Ibrāhīm, you have fulfilled the vision.' Indeed, We thus reward the doers of good. Indeed this was the clear trial. And We ransomed him with a great sacrifice."

Sūrat al-Ṣāffāt 37:102-107 · سورة الصافات

Allah answered Ibrāhīm's own duʿāʼ for a righteous son with exactly this: «رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِنَ الصَّالِحِينَ * فَبَشَّرْنَاهُ بِغُلَامٍ حَلِيمٍ» — "My Lord, grant me [a child] from among the righteous. So We gave him good tidings of a forbearing boy" (al-Ṣāffāt 37:100-101). And notice the son answers يَا أَبَتِ — the same tender address, returned.

٣ — بناء الكعبة معًا 3 — Building the Kaʿbah Together

وَإِذْ يَرْفَعُ إِبْرَاهِيمُ الْقَوَاعِدَ مِنَ الْبَيْتِ وَإِسْمَاعِيلُ رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

"And [mention] when Ibrāhīm was raising the foundations of the House, and Ismāʿīl [with him, saying]: 'Our Lord, accept [this] from us. Indeed You are the Hearing, the Knowing.'"

Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:127 · سورة البقرة

Father and son build the Sacred House side by side, and call upon their Lord together as they work — رَبَّنَا وَاجْعَلْنَا مُسْلِمَيْنِ لَكَ, "Our Lord, make us both submitting to You" (al-Baqarah 2:128), through to the duʿāʼ for a Messenger to be raised among their descendants — the very duʿāʼ answered centuries later in Muḥammad ﷺ (al-Baqarah 2:129).

٤ — تبرُّؤ إبراهيم من أبيه 4 — Ibrāhīm's Disavowal of His Father

Dutifulness to parents does not mean agreeing with them wherever their own path leads — even to the Fire. Ibrāhīm refused to follow his father Āzar into idol-worship, and once it became clear his father was an enemy of Allah, he stopped even seeking forgiveness for him:

وَمَا كَانَ اسْتِغْفَارُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ لِأَبِيهِ إِلَّا عَن مَّوْعِدَةٍ وَعَدَهَا إِيَّاهُ فَلَمَّا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُ أَنَّهُ عَدُوٌّ لِّلَّهِ تَبَرَّأَ مِنْهُ ۚ إِنَّ إِبْرَاهِيمَ لَأَوَّاهٌ حَلِيمٌ

"And the request of forgiveness by Ibrāhīm for his father was only because of a promise he had made to him. But when it became clear to him that he was an enemy to Allah, he disassociated himself from him. Indeed, Ibrāhīm was compassionate and forbearing."

Sūrat al-Tawbah 9:114 · سورة التوبة

٥ — ابن نوح الذي لم يستمع 5 — Nūḥ's Son, Who Would Not Listen

Nor does a parent's love mean covering a child's wrongdoing, or making excuses for it in the name of tenderness. Nūḥ called his son to the ship as the Flood rose:

وَهِيَ تَجْرِي بِهِمْ فِي مَوْجٍ كَالْجِبَالِ وَنَادَىٰ نُوحٌ ابْنَهُ وَكَانَ فِي مَعْزِلٍ يَا بُنَيَّ ارْكَب مَّعَنَا وَلَا تَكُن مَّعَ الْكَافِرِينَ * قَالَ سَآوِي إِلَىٰ جَبَلٍ يَعْصِمُنِي مِنَ الْمَاءِ ۚ قَالَ لَا عَاصِمَ الْيَوْمَ مِنْ أَمْرِ اللَّهِ إِلَّا مَن رَّحِمَ ۚ وَحَالَ بَيْنَهُمَا الْمَوْجُ فَكَانَ مِنَ الْمُغْرَقِينَ ... وَنَادَىٰ نُوحٌ رَّبَّهُ فَقَالَ رَبِّ إِنَّ ابْنِي مِنْ أَهْلِي وَإِنَّ وَعْدَكَ الْحَقُّ وَأَنتَ أَحْكَمُ الْحَاكِمِينَ * قَالَ يَا نُوحُ إِنَّهُ لَيْسَ مِنْ أَهْلِكَ ۖ إِنَّهُ عَمَلٌ غَيْرُ صَالِحٍ ۖ فَلَا تَسْأَلْنِ مَا لَيْسَ لَكَ بِهِ عِلْمٌ ۖ إِنِّي أَعِظُكَ أَن تَكُونَ مِنَ الْجَاهِلِينَ

"And it sailed with them through waves like mountains, and Nūḥ called out to his son, who was apart [from them]: 'O my son, board with us and be not with the disbelievers.' He said: 'I will take refuge on a mountain to protect me from the water.' [Nūḥ] said: 'There is no protector today from the command of Allah, except for whom He has mercy.' And the waves came between them, and he was among the drowned... And Nūḥ called to his Lord and said: 'My Lord, indeed my son is of my family, and indeed Your promise is true, and You are the most just of judges.' He said: 'O Nūḥ, indeed he is not of your family; indeed, he was [of] unrighteous conduct. So do not ask Me for that about which you have no knowledge. I advise you, lest you be among the ignorant.'"

Sūrat Hūd 11:42-46 · سورة هود

A father's love could not save a son who chose the mountain over the ship, disbelief over the call. The Qurʾān draws the line clearly: kinship of blood is not enough without إيمان and obedience.

٦ — خير أمة 6 — The Best Community

كُنتُمْ خَيْرَ أُمَّةٍ أُخْرِجَتْ لِلنَّاسِ تَأْمُرُونَ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَتَنْهَوْنَ عَنِ الْمُنكَرِ وَتُؤْمِنُونَ بِاللَّهِ

"You are the best nation produced [as an example] for mankind — you enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong and believe in Allah."

Sūrat Āl ʿImrān 3:110 · سورة آل عمران

Fourteen centuries ago, the Qurʾān gave fathers and children each their right and each their duty: cooperation upon righteousness and taqwā, leaving sin and transgression, enjoining good and forbidding evil — the same pair of verbs from Luqmān's advice, now describing an entire ummah.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Read Luqmān's advice aloud as a family, verse by verse, and let each person pick one instruction from it to focus on this week — ṣalāh, gentle speech, a lowered voice, patience. Then discuss together: what does it mean that Ibrāhīm and Ismāʿīl both said يَا بُنَيَّ / يَا أَبَتِ to each other even during the hardest trial of their lives?

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي
