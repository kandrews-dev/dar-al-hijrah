أَفْعَالُ الكِتَابِ الأَوَّل

Book 1 — Verb Reference

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • See the 14 verbs you already know from Book 1, in one place.
  • Notice how the same verb changes shape for الماضي (past), المضارع (present), and الأمر (command).
  • Get a gentle first look at verb conjugation, before the full Ṣarf track teaches the rules behind it.
  • Everything here is Arabic and English only — no transliteration.

Across Vocab 4, 5, 6, and 8 you met fourteen verbs — أَسْتَيْقِظُ (I wake up), أُصَلِّي (I pray), أُحِبُّ (I love), and more. Each was taught in one shape only. This page collects all fourteen and shows the same verb in three of its shapes side by side: its dictionary form (المَاضِي), how you say "I" do it (المُضَارِع), and how you tell someone "you" do it (الأَمْر). You do not need to memorize any rule here — just notice the pattern. The full ṣarf track later explains why the shapes change the way they do.

Notice: most verbs simply add أ to the front for "I," and just change one vowel for the command. A few — نَامَ، أَكَلَ، صَلَّى — bend the rule in a small, learnable way. The full Ṣarf track (Year 2+) explains exactly why, for every verb in the language, not just these fourteen.

Memorize — احفظ

كَتَبَ ← أَكْتُبُ ← اُكْتُبْ

One root, three shapes: he wrote (past) → I write (present) → write! (command). Say this chain aloud for two or three other verbs from the table above, until the pattern feels natural.
