الأَطْفَالُ وَالقِرَاءَة

Children & Reading

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know why reading is called a key to knowledge — and its place in the very first revelation.
  • Learn the four stages a child passes through on the way to loving books.
  • Learn practical vocabulary for raising a reading-loving child.
  • Read a connected passage using the unit's vocabulary.

القِرَاءَةُ مِفْتَاحٌ مِنْ مَفَاتِيحِ المَعْرِفَة — reading is a key among the keys of knowledge, and one of the greatest causes of a society's progress. It is fitting, then, that the very first command revealed to the Prophet ﷺ was the command to read:

اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ

"Read in the name of your Lord who created."

Sūrat al-ʿAlaq 96:1 · سورة العلق

Educators are keen to begin teaching children to read early. This lesson walks through the four natural stages a child passes through on the way there.

المَرَاحِلُ الأَرْبَع The Four Stages
  1. Handling by hand (السَّنَة الأُولَى) — the child shows a passing interest in books: mouthing them, pulling and tearing pages. Give old magazine pages for this stage, not real books.
  2. Pointing to pictures (الشَّهْر ١٥) — strong interest in pictures develops. The mother plays the leading role, turning pages while the child watches.
  3. Naming things (الشَّهْر ١٨) — the child starts using words drawn from the pictures' meanings, building his vocabulary: pointing and saying "camel," "car," or asking "what is this?"
  4. Love of easy short stories (بَعْدَ سَنَتَيْن) — the child now calls looking at a book "reading," loves hearing a story for every picture, and begins to notice letters as real things on the page.
النَّصّ Mini-Passage

كَانَتِ ابْنَتِي فِي عَامِهَا الأَوَّلِ تُمْسِكُ الكِتَابَ وَتُمَزِّقُ أَوْرَاقَهُ. وَبَعْدَ شُهُورٍ، أَصْبَحَتْ تُشِيرُ إِلَى الصُّوَرِ وَتَنْظُرُ إِلَيْهَا طَوِيلًا. ثُمَّ بَدَأَتْ تُسَمِّي الأَشْيَاءَ: هَذَا كِتَابٌ، هَذِهِ صُورَةٌ. وَالآنَ، بَعْدَ عَامَيْنِ، تُحِبُّ أَنْ تَسْمَعَ قِصَّةً عَنْ كُلِّ صُورَةٍ.

Read it aloud twice before peeking: In her first year, my daughter would hold the book and tear its pages. After some months, she began pointing to pictures and looking at them for a long time. Then she began naming things: "This is a book, this is a picture." And now, after two years, she loves to hear a story for every picture.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

If there is a young child in the family, name which of the four stages they are in right now. If not, recall your own children's (or your own) first books — what was the first story you remember loving?
