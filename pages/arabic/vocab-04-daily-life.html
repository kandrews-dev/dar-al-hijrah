الحياة اليومية

Daily Life — Times & Actions

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Name the parts of the day: الصباح · الظهر · المساء · الليل.
  • Say your first present-tense "I" verbs: I wake, I pray, I eat, I go, I study, I sleep.
  • Notice the أ that begins an "I" verb (أَفْعَل).
  • Describe a simple daily routine tied to the five prayers.
  • Ask ماذا تفعل؟ — "What do you do?" — and answer.

Until now we have named things. Now we learn to say what we do — the verb. For a Muslim the day already has a rhythm built into it: the five prayers. Learning to describe your day in Arabic is learning to narrate a life centred on the remembrance of Allah.

أوقات اليوم The Times of the Day
الكلمة Pronunciation English
الصَّباحaṣ-ṣabāḥthe morning
الظُّهْرaẓ-ẓuhrmidday / noon
العَصْرal-ʿaṣrthe afternoon
المَساءal-masāʾthe evening
اللَّيْلal-laylthe night

You already know two of these from the prayer names — الظهر · العصر — and even the sūrah العصر. Familiar words are the easiest to keep.

أفعال المضارع (أنا) Your First "I" Verbs

A present-tense verb for "I" begins with the letter أ (hamzah). Learn the pattern once and every new verb follows it:

  • أَسْتَيْقِظُ — astayqiẓu — I wake up
  • أُصَلِّي — uṣallī — I pray
  • آكُلُ — ākulu — I eat
  • أَذْهَبُ — adhhabu — I go
  • أَدْرُسُ — adrusu — I study
  • أَنامُ — anāmu — I sleep

You will learn the full conjugation (you / he / she…) on the naḥw & ṣarf track. For now, master the "I" form so you can talk about your own day.

يومي مع الصلوات My Day With the Prayers
الجملة English
أَسْتَيْقِظُ لِصَلاةِ الفَجْرِ.I wake up for the Fajr prayer.
أَذْهَبُ إلى المَدْرَسةِ في الصَّباحِ.I go to school in the morning.
أُصَلِّي الظُّهْرَ ثُمَّ آكُلُ.I pray Ẓuhr, then I eat.
أَدْرُسُ في المَساءِ.I study in the evening.
أَنامُ بَعْدَ صَلاةِ العِشاءِ.I sleep after the ʿIshāʾ prayer.

Useful joining words: ثُمَّ (then), بَعْدَ (after), قَبْلَ (before), في (in/at).

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Each family member describes their real day in Arabic, in order, using the prayers as anchors: أستيقظ لصلاة الفجر … ثُمَّ … بعد صلاة العشاء أنام. See who can string together the most sentences with ثُمَّ and بَعْدَ without slipping into English.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

أَسْتَيْقِظُ لِصَلاةِ الفَجْرِ · أُصَلِّي · أَدْرُسُ · أَنامُ بَعْدَ العِشاءِ

Learn to tell your whole day in Arabic around the five prayers. When your language and your day are both built on the ṣalāh, the Arabic sticks — and so does the routine of worship.
