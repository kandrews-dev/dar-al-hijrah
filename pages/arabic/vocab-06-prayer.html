الصلاة والمسجد

Prayer & the Mosque

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Name the five daily prayers in Arabic, in order.
  • Know the key words of the مسجد (mosque) and the صلاة (prayer).
  • Name the parts of the prayer: ركوع · سجود · ركعة.
  • Ask متى الصلاة؟ (when is the prayer?) and أين المسجد؟ (where is the mosque?).
  • Use the words you already know (times of day, "I" verbs) to talk about praying.

The ṣalāh is the heart of a Muslim's day and the second pillar of Islam. Much of its language is already Arabic on your tongue — الله أكبر · السلام عليكم. This lesson gathers the words of the prayer and the mosque so you can speak about the most important act of your day in its own language.

متى؟ · أين؟ Asking "When?" and "Where?"
  • متى صلاةُ المغربِ؟ — matā ṣalātu al-maghrib? — When is the Maghrib prayer?
  • أين المسجدُ؟ — ayna al-masjid? — Where is the mosque?
  • أذهبُ إلى المسجدِ لِصلاةِ الجماعةِ — I go to the mosque for the congregational prayer.
  • سمعتُ الأذانَ — samiʿtu al-adhān — I heard the adhān.

You already know أين؟ (where) from the classroom lesson and أذهب (I go) from daily life — new words lock in fastest when you build them on old ones.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

At each adhān today, ask one another in Arabic: ما هذه الصلاة؟ (which prayer is this?) and answer هذه صلاةُ العصرِ. Before leaving for the masjid, say أذهبُ إلى المسجدِ لِصلاةِ الجماعةِ. Name every prayer of the day in Arabic.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

الفَجْر · الظُّهْر · العَصْر · المَغْرِب · العِشاء

Learn the five prayers in Arabic until they are automatic — then narrate your whole day around them (from Vocab 4). When your language is built on the ṣalāh, both your Arabic and your worship grow together.
