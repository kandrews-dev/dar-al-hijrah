خريطة السنوات الخمس

The 5-Year Arabic Roadmap

One clear path from your first Arabic letters to reading Siyar Aʿlām al-Nubalāʾ and the tafsīr in their own words. Each year advances every track together — lugha (fluency), naḥw (syntax), ṣarf (word-forms), and later balāghah, poetry, and reading the turāth — the way the great institutes build a student. Below is exactly what each year covers, its texts, and the outcome you should reach.

Years 1–2 · التأسيس Foundations Years 3–4 · التخصص The Sciences Year 5 · الإتقان Mastery
Year 1 · السنة الأولى

القراءة والتأسيس

Reading & First Foundations

Building now

Learn to read Arabic script with the vowels, build a first store of everyday vocabulary, and meet the very first rules of grammar. The whole year is about confidence with the letters and simple sentences.

Lugha · اللغة

The alphabet & reading with ḥarakāt; Bayna Yadayk Book 1 (Units 1–8): greetings, the classroom, family, daily life, food, prayer.

Naḥw · النحو

al-Ājurrūmiyyah begins: what speech (كلام) is, the three kinds of word (اسم · فعل · حرف), and iʿrāb.

Ṣarf · الصرف

Awareness only: masculine/feminine, singular/dual/plural, and the past/present/command of the verb.

Outcome: read any vowelled Arabic aloud, hold a simple self-introduction, and name the three kinds of word. Year 1 complete: Vocab 1–24 + 3 exams · Naḥw L1–L12 + exam · Ṣarf L1–L8 + exam.

Year 2 · السنة الثانية

بناء الجملة

Building the Sentence

Move from words to full sentences: the doer and the done-to, the noun-sentence and the verb-sentence, and how words change their endings. Vocabulary widens toward everyday fluency.

Lugha · اللغة

Bayna Yadayk Book 2: travel, work, health, shopping, the weather — longer dialogues and short reading passages.

Naḥw · النحو

Ājurrūmiyyah continued: the marfūʿāt (fāʿil, mubtadaʾ & khabar, kāna & inna) and the manṣūbāt (esp. المفعول به).

Ṣarf · الصرف

The verb-forms begin: past/present/imperative conjugation, and the pronouns attached to verbs.

Outcome: parse a simple sentence (who is the fāʿil? the mubtadaʾ?), and understand short vowelled passages without a dictionary for every word.

Year 3 · السنة الثالثة

إتقان النحو والصرف

Command of Grammar & Word-Forms

Finish the foundational grammar and morphology, and begin reading real (graded) texts. This is the year fluency turns into understanding a scholar's lesson.

Lugha · اللغة

Bayna Yadayk Book 3, plus graded readers — the bridge from textbook Arabic to real texts.

Naḥw · النحو

Complete al-Ājurrūmiyyah (the majrūrāt, tawābiʿ) and begin Qaṭr al-Nadā of Ibn Hishām.

Ṣarf & Balāghah

Ṣarf: the ten verb-forms & patterns (Shadhā al-ʿArf). Intro balāghah: the beauty of the Qurʾān's expression.

Outcome: read an unvowelled simple text and supply the correct iʿrāb; follow a recorded dars in Arabic.

Year 4 · السنة الرابعة

البلاغة والنصوص

Rhetoric & Classical Texts

Add the sciences of eloquence and start reading authentic classical prose and poetry with guidance. The student now reads the turāth with only occasional help.

Naḥw · النحو

Finish Qaṭr al-Nadā and begin the Alfiyyah of Ibn Mālik with its commentary.

Balāghah · البلاغة

The three sciences — maʿānī, bayān, badīʿ (al-Balāghah al-Wāḍiḥah) — applied to the Qurʾān.

Shiʿr & Nuṣūṣ

The metres of poetry (ʿarūḍ) and reading real texts: passages of tafsīr, hadith, and the sīrah.

Outcome: appreciate why a Qurʾānic phrase is expressed as it is; scan a line of poetry; read classical prose with a dictionary.

Year 5 · السنة الخامسة

الإتقان والقراءة الحرة

Mastery & Independent Reading

Consolidate everything and read the great books unaided — then learn to teach Arabic to others, the truest sign that a science has been mastered.

Naḥw & Ṣarf

Complete the Alfiyyah and resolve the harder chapters — the standard of the accomplished student.

Nuṣūṣ · النصوص

Independent reading of Siyar Aʿlām al-Nubalāʾ, tafsīr, and the mutūn in the original.

Taʿlīm · التعليم

Composition (taʿbīr) and learning to teach Arabic — passing the language on.

Outcome: read the classics of the turāth without aid, understand a scholar's book directly, and teach the language to others.

How the years fit together — ملاحظة منهجية

The years are a guide, not a cage. A diligent student moves faster; a young one takes longer. What matters is the order: fluency and reading first (Years 1–2), then the grammar and morphology that let you read anything (Years 3–4), then eloquence and free reading (Years 4–5). Grammar stays light inside the lugha lessons and is taught in depth on the separate naḥw track — exactly how the Egyptian and Madinah institutes do it. Every lesson is taught fully on-page; the classical texts are the destination, not a prerequisite.
