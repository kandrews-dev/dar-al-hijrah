Ṣarf Track · Lesson 13 · Year 2Year 2

اسْمُ المَكَانِ وَاسْمُ الآلَة

The Place-Noun and the Instrument-Noun

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Form the place-noun (اسْمُ المَكَان) on مَفْعَل / مَفْعِل.
  • Form the instrument-noun (اسْمُ الآلَة) on مِفْعَال · مِفْعَل · مِفْعَلَة.
  • Explain, as ṣarf, a dozen words you already know — مَسْجِد، مَدْرَسَة، مَكْتَبَة، مَطْبَخ، مِفْتَاح.
  • Read the names المَغْرِب and المَشْرِق as ṣarf — and see that a prayer you pray daily is named on the scale.
Where we are in the ṣarf track: back in Ṣarf 1, a seed was planted: the pattern مَفْعَل — "the place of the action." Ṣarf 8 gave you the doer and the done-to. Today that seed flowers: the last two everyday members of the word-family — where the action happens, and what tool it happens with. After this lesson the family portrait is complete: verb · doer · done-to · maṣdar · place · instrument. One root, six shapes, and you can read them all.
اسْمُ المَكَان Part One — The Place-Noun

اسمُ المكانِ اسمٌ يدلُّ على موضعِ الفعلِ، ووزنُه مَفْعَلٌ أو مَفْعِلٌ

Pour a root into the mould مَفْعَل and you get the place where its action happens. When the muḍāriʿ carries a kasrah on its middle letter, the place-noun follows it onto مَفْعِل — جَلَسَ / يَجْلِسُ gives مَجْلِسٌ, the place of sitting. Now look at the words below — you have known every one of them for years without knowing you were reading ṣarf:

الكلمة الجذر Literally
مَسْجِدٌس ج دthe place of سُجُود — the prostration-place
مَدْرَسَةٌد ر سthe place of study — note the ة can join the pattern
مَكْتَبَةٌك ت بthe place of books and writing — the library
مَطْبَخٌط ب خthe place of cooking — the kitchen
مَغْرِبٌغ ر بthe place / time of the sun's setting — the Maghrib prayer is named by ṣarf
مَشْرِقٌش ر قthe place of the sun's rising — the east

Sit with that middle row for a moment: every ṣalāh of your life has been prayed inside a place-noun. The مَسْجِد is where سُجُود happens; المَغْرِب is prayed when the sun reaches its setting-place. A small honesty note: a handful of famous words — مَسْجِد، مَغْرِب، مَشْرِق — keep a kasrah the Arabs preferred even though the strict rule would expect a fatḥah. Like قُلْ in Ṣarf 4, take them as heard; the pattern's meaning is untouched.

اسْمُ الآلَة Part Two — The Instrument-Noun

اسمُ الآلةِ اسمٌ يدلُّ على أداةِ الفعلِ، وأوزانُه مِفْعَالٌ ومِفْعَلٌ ومِفْعَلَةٌ

The instrument-noun names the tool the action is done with. Its three classical patterns are مِفْعَال · مِفْعَل · مِفْعَلَة — and all three open with a kasrah under the mīm. That kasrah is the tell-apart from the place-noun's fatḥah, so watch the mīm closely:

الكلمة الجذر Literally
مِفْتَاحٌف ت حthe opening tool — the key
مِيزَانٌو ز نthe weighing tool — the scale: the very مِيزَان of الميزان الصرفي from Ṣarf 1, and of the scales of the Day of Judgement
مِصْبَاحٌص ب حthe light-giving tool — the lamp
مِكْنَسَةٌك ن سthe sweeping tool — the broom

Notice مِيزَان: its root is و ز ن, but the weak و of last lesson's مثال family softened into ي after the kasrah of the mīm. Even the word for "scale" was itself weighed and bent on the scale.

The tell-apart is one vowel wide. Fatḥah on the mīm → place: مَـ. Kasrah on the mīm → instrument: مِـ. So مَفْتَح would be a place of opening, while مِفْتَاح is the key in your hand. One ḥarakah moves a word from a building to a tool — this is why the ḥarakāt were never decoration.
أَمْثِلَةٌ قُرْآنِيَّة Examples from the Qur'an

وَنَضَعُ الْمَوَازِينَ الْقِسْطَ لِيَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ

And We shall set up the scales of justice for the Day of Resurrection. (al-Anbiyāʼ 21:47)

المَوَازِين is the plural of مِيزَان — the instrument-noun of weighing, standing at the heart of the ʿaqīdah of the Last Day: real, just scales set up on the Day of Resurrection, in which deeds are weighed. You studied that day in the Aqeedah lesson on اليوم الآخر — today ṣarf hands you the very shape of its word.

اللَّهُ نُورُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ مَثَلُ نُورِهِ كَمِشْكَاةٍ فِيهَا مِصْبَاحٌ

Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth. The likeness of His light is as a niche in which there is a lamp. (al-Nūr 24:35, opening)

مِصْبَاحٌ — kasrah on the mīm, pattern مِفْعَال from ص ب ح — the light-giving instrument, set inside the Parable of Light. The instrument-noun you learned five minutes ago is a working part of one of the most majestic similitudes in the Qur'an.

Memorize — احفظ

Fatḥah-mīm for the place, kasrah-mīm for the tool. This week, every time you enter a مَسْجِد or pick up a مِفْتَاح, name the pattern before you let go of the word.
