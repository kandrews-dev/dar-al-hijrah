اسم الفاعل واسم المفعول

The Doer-Noun and the Done-To-Noun — Year-1 Capstone

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Form اسم الفاعل and اسم المفعول from the bare verb — the moulds فَاعِل and مَفْعُول.
  • Form them from the increased verbs with the مُـ prefix.
  • Know why مُسْلِم and مُؤْمِن and مُعَلِّم are all doer-nouns.
  • Close the Year-1 ṣarf arc — from root and scale to the nouns that grow out of verbs.
Where we are in the ṣarf track: this is the capstone of Year-1 ṣarf. The arc so far: the root and the scale (Lesson 1) → the past, the present and the command through all their forms (Lessons 2–6) → the increased doors (Lesson 7). One step remains: the nouns that grow out of verbs — the names of the doer and the done-to. Master this and you can read a word like مُسْلِم and see its whole family tree.
من الفعل المجرد Part One — From the Bare Verb

اسم الفاعل من الثلاثي على وزن فَاعِل، واسم المفعول على وزن مَفْعُول

You have known this since Lesson 1 — it was planted there as a seed, and today it flowers. From any bare three-letter verb, pour the root into فَاعِل and you get the doer; pour it into مَفْعُول and you get the done-to — the one the action lands on. Two moulds, and every bare verb in the language will fill them:

الفعل اسم الفاعل اسم المفعول Meaning
كَتَبَكَاتِبٌمَكْتُوبٌhe wrote → the writer / the written
عَلِمَعَالِمٌمَعْلُومٌhe knew → the knower (scholar) / the known
ظَلَمَظَالِمٌمَظْلُومٌhe wronged → the wrongdoer / the one wronged
خَلَقَخَالِقٌمَخْلُوقٌhe created → the creator / the created
Pause at the last row with reverence. الخَالِق is among the names of Allah — the Creator — and everything else, without exception, is مَخْلُوق — created. The ṣarf pair carries an ʿaqīdah lesson in its very shape: the doer-noun and the done-to noun stand on opposite pans of the scale, and the Creator and the created are never confused.
من الفعل المزيد Part Two — From the Increased Verb

من غير الثلاثي: خُذِ المضارعَ وأَبْدِلْ حرفَ المضارعة مِيمًا مضمومةً — بكسرِ ما قبل الآخِر للفاعل، وفتحِه للمفعول

For the increased verbs of Lesson 7 the moulds فَاعِل and مَفْعُول no longer fit — the word is too big. So the rule changes, and it is beautifully simple: take the muḍāriʿ and replace its prefix with مُـ. Then one ḥarakah decides everything: the doer keeps a kasrah before the end, the done-to takes a fatḥah.

المضارع بعد مُـ Meaning
يُعَلِّمُمُعَلِّم / مُعَلَّمthe teacher, the one teaching (kasrah) / the one taught (fatḥah)
يُسْلِمُمُسْلِمthe one who submits — the word Muslim itself!
يُؤْمِنُمُؤْمِنthe believer
يُسَافِرُمُسَافِرthe traveller

One ḥarakah and the meaning flips: مُعَلِّم teaches، مُعَلَّم is taught. And now three words you have carried since the beginning finally explain themselves: مُسْلِم from أَسْلَمَ (يُسْلِمُ)، مُؤْمِن from آمَنَ (يُؤْمِنُ)، مُعَلِّم from عَلَّمَ (يُعَلِّمُ) — all three are doer-nouns wearing the same مُـ, each grown from one of the doors of Lesson 7.

مثال قرآني An Example from the Qur'an

إِنَّ الْمُسْلِمِينَ وَالْمُسْلِمَاتِ وَالْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ

Indeed, the Muslim men and the Muslim women, the believing men and the believing women… (al-Aḥzāb 33:35, opening)

Four doer-nouns in a row — the Muslims and the Muslim women, the believers and the believing women — every one of them built by the مُـ rule you have just learned, and every one wearing the sound-plural endings you met on the naḥw track. One ayah, and both wings of your Arabic — ṣarf inside the word, naḥw at its ending — beat together.

🎉 The Year-1 ṣarf arc is complete

You now own: the root & scale · the three tenses through their fourteen forms · the amr · doors II–IV · and the doer and done-to nouns — the complete Year-1 ṣarf foundation. The ladder ahead is named so you know where the road leads: the remaining doors of the mazīd, the maṣdar, and the classical text al-Bināʾ wa-l-Asās — but those belong to later years. Today, when you meet a word like مُسْلِم, you can weigh it, name its door, and read its whole family tree.

Memorize — احفظ

فَاعِل · مَفْعُول — مُفَعِّل · مُفَعَّل — مُسْلِم · مُؤْمِن

Two moulds for the bare verb, the مُـ rule for the increased — kasrah for the doer, fatḥah for the done-to. This week, whenever you meet a word beginning with مُـ, ask: which verb does it come from, and is it doing or done-to?

Further reading — optional aids

These are optional supports only. The lesson is complete on-page. The full theory of the derived nouns — the ṣifah mushabbahah, the noun of instrument, the noun of place and time, and the maṣdar of every door — comes later on the ladder, in al-Bināʾ wa-l-Asās and then Shadhā al-ʿArf. For Year 1, the two moulds and the مُـ rule of today are the whole requirement — and they will carry you a very long way through the Book of Allah.
