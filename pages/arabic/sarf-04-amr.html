فعل الأمر

The Command Verb

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what فعل الأمر (the command verb) is.
  • Form the amr from the muḍāriʿ: تَكْتُبُ → اُكْتُبْ.
  • Know the two great Qur'anic commands اِقْرَأْ (read!) and قُلْ (say!).
  • Complete the three verb types: ماضٍ · مضارع · أمر.
Where we are in the ṣarf track: Lesson 2 gave you the past verb, Lesson 3 the present. Today the أمر (command) completes the set. The naḥw track's verbs lesson already told you that Ibn Ājurrūm names exactly three types of verb — ماضٍ، مضارع، أمر. There you learned their names; here you learn to build the third.
ما هو فعل الأمر؟ Part One — What is Fiʿl al-Amr?

فعل الأمر ما دلَّ على طلبِ وقوعِ الفعل من المخاطَب

The fiʿl al-amr is the verb of command or request addressed to the listener: اُكْتُبْ — write! Where the past verb reports and the present verb describes, the amr asks. It always speaks to the one in front of you (المخاطَب — the addressed person), which is why revelation itself, teaching and commanding, is full of it.

كيف نبني الأمر؟ Part Two — Forming the Amr from the Muḍāriʿ

The amr is carved out of the muḍāriʿ in three moves: remove the prefix letter (the ت of تَكْتُبُ), give the last letter a sukūn (the final ḍammah falls away), and if the letter now standing first carries a sukūn — unpronounceable in Arabic — add a hamzat waṣl (همزة الوصل) in front as a stepping stone: تَكْتُبُ → كْتُبْ → اُكْتُبْ.

المضارع الأمر Meaning Note
تَكْتُبُاُكْتُبْwrite!hamzat waṣl added — regular
تَقْرَأُاِقْرَأْread!hamzat waṣl added — regular
تَذْهَبُاِذْهَبْgo!hamzat waṣl added — regular
تَقُولُقُلْsay!special — hollow verb, shortened; memorise as is
تَأْكُلُكُلْeat!special — irregular, hamzah dropped; memorise as is

Why اُكْتُبْ with ḍammah but اِقْرَأْ with kasrah? The hamzat waṣl copies its vowel from the middle of the verb: middle ḍammah (ـكْتُـبُ) → اُ, otherwise → اِ. The last two rows are shortened forms the Arabs smoothed down over time — take them as whole words, exactly as they appear in the Qur'an.

الأنواع الثلاثة كاملة Part Three — The Three Types, Complete

One root, ك ت ب, now stands before you in all three shapes Ibn Ājurrūm names in al-Ājurrūmiyyah:

الفعل Meaning Type
كَتَبَhe wroteماضٍ — past
يَكْتُبُhe writesمضارع — present
اُكْتُبْwrite!أمر — command

With this lesson you own every type of verb the matn names. When the naḥw track says "the verb is of three kinds", you can now build all three from any sound root.

أمثلة قرآنية Examples from the Qur'an

اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ

Read in the name of your Lord who created. (al-ʿAlaq 96:1)

The very first word ever revealed — اِقْرَأْ — is a فعل أمر. Revelation opened with a command to read, addressed to the Prophet ﷺ in the cave of Ḥirāʼ. Notice also خَلَقَ at the end: a past verb in the same ayah.

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ

Say: He is Allah, One. (al-Ikhlāṣ 112:1)

قُلْ — the shortened amr of تَقُولُ — opens all three Quls you learned on the Quran tab: al-Ikhlāṣ, al-Falaq and al-Nās. Every time you recite them you begin with a command verb.
