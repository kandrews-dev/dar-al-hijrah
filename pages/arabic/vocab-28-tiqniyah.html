التقنية

Technology

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the everyday technology words — حَاسُوبٌ, هَاتِفٌ, شَبَكَةٌ, شَاشَةٌ and their friends.
  • Send and describe a رِسَالَةٌ (message) in Arabic, from writing it to pressing send.
  • See the ṣarf patterns hiding inside tech words: هَاتِف, مَوْقِع, أَرْسَلَ.
  • Use technology with adab — a tool in your hand, not a master over you.

Vocab 27 taught you the words that carry the news; this lesson gives you the machines that carry the words. The حَاسُوب in your bag, the هَاتِف in your pocket, the شَبَكَة that stretches around the earth — each one already speaks Arabic if you listen closely. And listen you should: hiding inside these very modern words are the very old patterns of your ṣarf track, and inside the most ordinary word of all — رِسَالَة — hides the noblest root in the language.

Look how much ṣarf is hiding in one table. هَاتِفٌ sits on فَاعِل, the doer pattern — the same shape as كَاتِب (writer) — so the phone is literally "the one who calls out". مَوْقِعٌ is an اسم مكان, a place-noun cut from وَقَعَ exactly the way مَسْجِد is cut from سَجَدَ — a website is a place you visit, even though it exists nowhere you can walk to. And أَرْسَلَ stands on door IV, the أَفْعَلَ door: the hamzah at the front is the same one that turns نَزَلَ (it came down) into أَنْزَلَ (He sent it down). The engineers who named these machines reached, without knowing it, for patterns as old as the language itself.

جوهرة الجذر — The root-family gem: ر س ل

رِسَالَةٌ · أَرْسَلَ · رَسُولٌ

Three words in this lesson's orbit grow from the same three letters, ر س ل: رِسَالَةٌ the message, أَرْسَلَ the sending, and رَسُولٌ — the Messenger ﷺ himself, the one who is sent. Put them together and you can say the greatest sentence technology will ever borrow: Allah أَرْسَلَ His رَسُول with the رِسَالَة. Every time your thumb taps "send", you are using a word that revelation used first — technology borrowed its vocabulary from revelation's own root.

النَّصّ Mini-Passage — the new words walking together

كَتَبَ أَحْمَدُ رِسَالَةً عَلَى الْحَاسُوبِ إِلَى ابْنِ عَمِّهِ فِي الْمَدِينَةِ. وَضَعَ فِي الرِّسَالَةِ صُورَةَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْجَدِيدِ مِنَ الْأَخْبَارِ. ثُمَّ ضَغَطَ عَلَى الزِّرِّ وَأَرْسَلَ الرِّسَالَةَ عَبْرَ الشَّبَكَةِ. وَصَلَتِ الرِّسَالَةُ إِلَى هَاتِفِ ابْنِ عَمِّهِ فِي لَحْظَةٍ. فَرَدَّ ابْنُ عَمِّهِ: مَا شَاءَ اللهُ، مَسْجِدٌ جَمِيلٌ.

Read it aloud twice before peeking: Ahmad wrote a message on the computer to his cousin in Madinah. He put into the message a picture of the new mosque from the news. Then he pressed the button and sent the message across the network. The message reached his cousin's phone in an instant. So his cousin replied: mā shāʼ Allāh — a beautiful mosque. Five sentences, seven of the ten table words — and did you recognize the mosque? It is the very one Vocab 27's news announced: فُتِحَ مَسْجِدٌ جَدِيدٌ. The news became a صُورَة, the صُورَة rode a رِسَالَة, and the رِسَالَة crossed continents in a لَحْظَة. That is what these ten words can do when they walk together.

أدب — Technology is a tool

The حَاسُوب and the هَاتِف are tools — and a tool takes the value of what you do with it. In your hand it can carry صلة الرحم (keeping the ties of kinship) across any distance: a grandmother hears her grandchildren's voices from another continent, a رِسَالَة of salām reaches a cousin in a لَحْظَة. It puts knowledge at a fingertip: the Qurʼān, the books of the scholars, this very مَوْقِع you are reading. But the same rule you learned in Vocab 27 stands guard over every screen: فَتَبَيَّنُوا — verify. Before you forward a message, a صُورَة, a claim — verify it, because pressing أَرْسَلَ makes you a carrier of whatever the رِسَالَة contains. The tool obeys the hand; make sure the hand obeys Allah.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Tonight, send one family member a short Arabic رِسَالَة — a greeting plus one sentence is enough: السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ، كَيْفَ حَالُكَ؟ — or borrow a line straight from the passage. A grandparent, an aunt, a cousin in another country: let the شَبَكَة do what it does best and carry صلة الرحم across the distance. Then gather in the evening and read the replies together — and notice how it feels when Arabic arrives on your own شَاشَة from someone you love.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

أَرْسَلَ اللهُ رَسُولَهُ بِالرِّسَالَةِ

Memorize the ر س ل family line — Allah sent His Messenger with the Message — until every message you send points you back to the same three letters, and to the One who sent the greatest Messenger ﷺ with the greatest Message.
