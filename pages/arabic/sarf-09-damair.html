الضمائر المتصلة بالفعل

Attached Pronouns on the Verb — the First Year-2 Ṣarf Lesson

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the eight attached object pronouns: ـنِي · ـنَا · ـكَ · ـكِ · ـهُ · ـهَا · ـكُمْ · ـهُمْ.
  • Attach them to verbs you already own — كَتَبَهُ (he wrote it).
  • Recognise them inside Qur'anic words you have already memorized — أَنزَلْنَاهُ، اهْدِنَا.
  • Tell the object ـنَا (done to us) apart from the doer ـنَا (we did it).
Where we are in the ṣarf track: all of Year 1 conjugated the verb for its doer — who did it: he, she, you, we. Year 2 opens with the other side of the action — the pronouns that carry the object, the one the action lands on, and they attach to the end of the verb. This means one Arabic word can hold a verb, a doer and an object all at once: نَصَرَكُمْ — "he helped you all" — one word, a whole sentence.
ضمائر النصب المتصلة Part One — The Attached Object Pronouns

الضميرُ المتصلُ يلتصقُ بآخِرِ الفعلِ ويدلُّ على مَن وقعَ عليه الفعلُ

The attached pronoun clings to the end of the verb and names the one the action lands on. There are eight of them for you to master, and they never stand alone — they always ride on the back of another word. Take a verb you know — كَتَبَ (he wrote) or نَصَرَ (he helped) — and watch each pronoun click on:

الضمير Meaning مثال The example means
ـنِيmeنَصَرَنِيhe helped me
ـنَاusنَصَرَنَاhe helped us
ـكَyou (m.)نَصَرَكَhe helped you (m.)
ـكِyou (f.)نَصَرَكِhe helped you (f.)
ـهُhim / itكَتَبَهُhe wrote it
ـهَاher / it (f.)كَتَبَهَاhe wrote it (f.)
ـكُمْyou (pl.)نَصَرَكُمْhe helped you all
ـهُمْthem (m.)نَصَرَهُمْhe helped them

Notice that the verb itself — كَتَبَ، نَصَرَ — never changes its shape. The pronoun simply clicks onto the end, the way the doer endings of Year 1 clicked on. Verb + doer + object, all inside one word — no other word in the sentence is needed.

نَا الفاعل ونَا المفعول Part Two — The Two نَا: Doer or Done-To?

إنْ سُكِّنَ ما قبلَ «نا» فهي فاعلٌ، وإنْ فُتِحَ فهي مفعولٌ

Here Year 2 meets Year 1. In Ṣarf 5 you learned the doer ending نَا — "we did it" — and today you have met an object نَا — "done to us". They look identical, but one ḥarakah tells them apart, and it sits on the letter before the نا:

الكلمة Before the نا Which نا؟ Meaning
ظَلَمْنَاsukūn — ـمْـdoerwe wronged
ظَلَمَنَاfatḥah — ـمَـobjecthe wronged us
Now open the duʿāʼ of Ādam and Ḥawwāʼ: رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنْفُسَنَا — "our Lord, we have wronged ourselves." Three نا in five words, and each one is doing a different job: in رَبَّنَا the نا hangs on a noun — our Lord; in ظَلَمْنَا the sukūn before it tells you it is the doer — we wronged; and in أَنْفُسَنَا it hangs on the noun أَنْفُس — ourselves. Read the duʿāʼ slowly once more and watch each نا do its work.
أمثلة قرآنية Examples from the Qur'an

إِنَّا أَنزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ

Indeed, We sent it down in the Night of Decree. (al-Qadr 97:1)

Peel أَنزَلْنَاهُ apart and three lessons meet in one word: أَنْزَلَ — he sent down, a door-IV verb from Ṣarf 7 · نَا — We, the doer, with the sukūn before it (Ṣarf 5) · هُ — it, the object: the Qur'an itself. Verb, doer and object in a single word — and you can now name every piece.

اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ

Guide us to the straight path. (al-Fātiḥah 1:6)

اهْدِ is an amr — a command-form verb, from Ṣarf 4 — and نَا is the object: guide us. You have been using attached pronouns in every rakʿah of every ṣalāh you have ever prayed — today you finally see them.
