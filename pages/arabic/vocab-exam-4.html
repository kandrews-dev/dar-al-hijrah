SECTION ATrue or False — صحيح أم خاطئ10 marks · 1 each

Question 1

صَيْدَلِيَّةٌ The word صَيْدَلِيَّةٌ means “pharmacy” — the place where the وَصْفَة (prescription) is filled.

Question 2

حَاسُوبٌ The word حَاسُوبٌ means “phone.”

Question 3

شَجَرَةٌ / شَجَرٌ The word شَجَرَةٌ with the ة means one single tree, while شَجَرٌ without it means “trees” as a whole.

Question 4

تَاجِرٌ The word تَاجِرٌ means “profit.”

Question 5

قُمَامَةٌ The word قُمَامَةٌ means “garden.”

Question 6

مُمَرِّضٌ The word مُمَرِّضٌ (nurse) sits on the pattern مُفَعِّل — the doer of door II — and shares its root م ر ض with مَرِيضٌ (sick).

Question 7

لَا شِفَاءَ إِلَّا شِفَاؤُكَ In the Prophet's ﷺ duʿāʼ for the sick (al-Bukhārī & Muslim), the word شِفَاء appears three times.

Question 8

أَعْلَنَ The word أَعْلَنَ means “he clicked.”

Question 9

خَسَارَةٌ The word خَسَارَةٌ means “loss” — the opposite of رِبْحٌ (profit).

Question 10

أُسْتَاذٌ The word أُسْتَاذٌ means “student.”

SECTION DComplete the Sentence — أكمل الجملة5 marks · 1 each

Question 26

Question 27

Question 28

Question 29

Question 30

SECTION EShort Answer — أجب بإيجاز5 marks · 1 each

Write your answer in Arabic (transliteration accepted). Marks are awarded on submission based on key words found in your answer.

Question 31

ر س ل One root, a whole family — the message, the Messenger, and “he sent.” Name two Arabic words built from the root ر س ل.

Question 32

«اشْفِ وَأَنْتَ الشَّافِي…» In the Prophet's ﷺ duʿāʼ for the sick (al-Bukhārī & Muslim), one word — the maṣdar meaning “cure, healing” — is repeated three times. Write that word in Arabic.

Question 33

«مَا مِنْ مُسْلِمٍ يَغْرِسُ غَرْسًا…» The Prophet ﷺ said: no Muslim plants a plant, and a bird or a person or a beast eats from it, except that it counts for him as a ____ (al-Bukhārī & Muslim). Write the missing word in Arabic.

Question 34

جَامِعَةٌ The word جَامِعَةٌ (university) is built from the root ج م ع — “gathering.” Name two other words from the ج م ع family that you have learned.

Question 35

«التَّاجِرُ الصَّدُوقُ الأَمِينُ مَعَ النَّبِيِّينَ وَالصِّدِّيقِينَ وَالشُّهَدَاءِ» The Prophet ﷺ said the truthful, trustworthy merchant will be with three groups on the Day of Judgement (al-Tirmidhī — ḥasan). Name two of the three groups in Arabic.
