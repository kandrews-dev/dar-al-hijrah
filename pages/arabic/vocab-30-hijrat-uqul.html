هِجْرَةُ العُقُول

Brain Drain — the Migration of Minds

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the vocabulary of skilled emigration — from عَقْلٌ and its broken plural عُقُولٌ to كَفَاءَةٌ and اغْتِرَابٌ.
  • Read real statistics in Arabic using نِسْبَةٌ مِئَوِيَّةٌ (percentage) and إِحْصَائِيَّةٌ (statistic).
  • Recognize the جمع تكسير (broken plural) pattern فُعُول through عَقْل ← عُقُول.
  • Reflect on the hadith of the believers as one body — distance does not sever the tie.

Every generation asks the same question the Book 3 passage opens with: minds do not walk on legs like people, or fly like birds — so what does it mean when we say عُقُول "migrate"? This lesson gives you the vocabulary to read about a real phenomenon discussed across the Arab and Muslim world: talented graduates, doctors and engineers who study or settle abroad, and the numbers behind that story.

The star of this table is عُقُول itself. Most plurals you have met so far add letters to the end — مُعَلِّمُونَ, مُسْلِمَاتٌ. But عَقْل becomes عُقُول by changing its internal shape entirely: this is جَمْعُ تَكْسِير — a "broken plural," so called because the singular's pattern is "broken" and rebuilt on a new one, here فُعُول. Arabic has dozens of these patterns and no single rule predicts which noun takes which one — they are learned word by word, the way this lesson teaches عَقْل ← عُقُول.

آيةٌ عن العقل An Āyah on the Mind — al-Anfāl 8:22

إِنَّ شَرَّ الدَّوَابِّ عِندَ اللَّهِ الصُّمُّ الْبُكْمُ الَّذِينَ لَا يَعْقِلُونَ

"Indeed, the worst of living creatures in the sight of Allah are the deaf and dumb who do not use their reason (لَا يَعْقِلُونَ)."

Sūrat al-Anfāl 8:22 · سورة الأنفال

Notice يَعْقِلُونَ — the same root ع ق ل as عَقْل and عُقُول in your table. In the Qurʾān, العقل is never praised as raw cleverness; it is praised when it is used — to recognize truth, to reflect, to obey. A society's عُقُول are a trust, not just a talent to be measured in degrees and statistics.

النَّصّ Reading — real numbers, in your own words

تَتَحَدَّثُ الإِحْصَائِيَّاتُ عَنْ نِسْبَةٍ كَبِيرَةٍ مِنَ الأَطِبَّاءِ وَالمُهَنْدِسِينَ المُسْلِمِينَ الَّذِينَ يَعِيشُونَ فِي الاغْتِرَابِ، بَعِيدًا عَنْ أَوْطَانِهِمْ. كَثِيرٌ مِنَ الخِرِّيجِينَ الحَاصِلِينَ عَلَى المَاجِسْتِير أَوِ الدُّكْتُورَاه لَا يَعُودُونَ إِلَى بِلَادِهِمْ بَعْدَ الدِّرَاسَةِ. وَهَذَا مَا يُسَمِّيهِ النَّاسُ «هِجْرَةَ العُقُول».

Read it aloud twice before peeking: Statistics speak of a large percentage of Muslim doctors and engineers who live abroad, far from their homelands. Many graduates who hold a master's or a doctorate do not return to their countries after their studies. This is what people call "the migration of minds." The real Book 3 passage builds this picture with concrete numbers — for example, that a large share of famous doctors in England are of foreign, often Muslim, origin, and that after the Second World War a majority of Egyptian graduates who emigrated to the United States were scientists and engineers, many holding advanced degrees, while only a small number of students from some Arab countries ever returned home after studying in the West.

حديث — One body, however far apart

«مَثَلُ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ فِي تَوَادِّهِمْ وَتَرَاحُمِهِمْ وَتَعَاطُفِهِمْ مَثَلُ الْجَسَدِ، إِذَا اشْتَكَى مِنْهُ عُضْوٌ تَدَاعَى لَهُ سَائِرُ الْجَسَدِ بِالسَّهَرِ وَالْحُمَّى»

"The believers in their mutual love, mercy and compassion are like one body: when one limb aches, the whole body responds with sleeplessness and fever" (al-Bukhārī and Muslim, from al-Nuʿmān ibn Bashīr, agreed upon — muttafaq ʿalayh).

Whatever land a Muslim's عَقْل and skill come to serve, this hadith is the reminder underneath هِجْرَةُ العُقُول: the ummah is one جَسَد (body). Distance changes the address, not the tie — the وَطَن of the heart stays the same body as every believer everywhere.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

As a family, name three fields where كَفَاءَات (skilled people) are needed most in your own community — medicine, teaching, dīn — and discuss in Arabic where you would like your own عُقُول to be of use, near or far, remembering the hadith of the one body: «مَثَلُ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ... مَثَلُ الْجَسَدِ».

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي
