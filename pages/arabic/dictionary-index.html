المُعْجَم

The Graded Dictionary

Bayna Yadayk teaches the words of daily life. This dictionary adds the words of the mutūn, tafsīr, and university-level Arabic — sourced word-by-word from the Islamic University of Madinah's own Level 3 & 4 glossaries, verified and de-duplicated against every lugha lesson already on this site. The goal: a student of this program should not meet a word in a matn or a Madinah classroom that this site never prepared him for.

Method — كيف بُني هذا المعجم

Every entry here is read directly from a real source PDF — Muʿjam Kalimāt al-Mustawā al-Thālith, the Islamic University of Madinah's own Level 3 vocabulary glossary (2,567 words, Institute for Teaching Arabic Language) — never reconstructed from memory. Each headword is checked against every word already taught across Bayna Yadayk Books 1–3 on this site; only genuinely new words are added here, so nothing is ever taught twice. Entries are built letter by letter, root by root, exactly as the source orders them.
