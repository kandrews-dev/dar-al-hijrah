البيت والمدينة

Home & the City

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the core words for the house and the city.
  • Ask and answer أين؟ (where?) about common places.
  • Use words like بيت · شارع · مدرسة · مكتبة in simple sentences.
  • Begin to describe distance and location: قريب · بعيد.
  • Connect this lesson to the earlier words of daily life and the prayer.

Once you can greet, name your family, and say what you eat, the next step is to speak about the places where life happens. The بيت (house) is where we rest, study, and pray; the مدينة (city) is where we move from one place to another. This lesson gives you the words you need to talk about your surroundings in a simple, everyday way.

في المدينة In the City
  • الشَّارِعُ — the street
  • السُّوقُ — the market
  • المَسْجِدُ — the mosque
  • المَدْرَسَةُ — the school
  • المَكْتَبَةُ — the library
  • الحَدِيقَةُ — the garden

Use أين؟ for asking where something is, and قريبٌ / بَعِيدٌ for near and far.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Walk around the house or the neighborhood and name each place in Arabic: هذا بيتٌ, هذه مدرسةٌ, أين السوقُ؟. Then answer with السوقُ قريبٌ or السوقُ بعيدٌ.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

بَيْتٌ · مَدْرَسَةٌ · مَكْتَبَةٌ · شَارِعٌ · سُوقٌ

Learn these words until the city feels like a map in your mind. Say where you are, where you are going, and what is near or far.
