اللغة العربية

Arabic — the Path to Fluency

Arabic is the key that opens everything else — the Qurʾān, the Sunnah, and the great books like Siyar Aʿlām al-Nubalāʾ — in their own words. This is not vocabulary sprinkled into lessons; it is a complete course that builds a student, word by word, into a reader of the classics. Modelled on the legacy of al-Azhar, the Islamic University of Madinah, King Saud University, and the great Arabic institutes.

How we teach it — منهجنا

A complete course — no skipping. Every word of Bayna Yadayk is there to build fluency, so we teach the whole book, unit by unit, in order. Grammar is kept light within the lugha track and taught in depth on the separate naḥw track — exactly how the Egyptian and Madinah institutes do it. Audio is linked from the start for listening and pronunciation; video lessons come later.
