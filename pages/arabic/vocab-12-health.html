الصحة والعافية

Health & Wellness

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the common words for health and being sick.
  • Ask and answer كيف حالكَ؟ and هل أنت مريض؟.
  • Use words like طبيب · مستشفى · دواء · صداع · حرارة.
  • Talk about feeling well or needing help in simple Arabic.

Health vocabulary is very useful because it connects everyday life to the words people use when they are not feeling well. This lesson helps you ask about someone’s condition and say what they need.

كيف حالكَ؟ How are you?
  • كيف حالُكَ؟ — How are you?
  • أنا بِخَيْرٍ. — I am well.
  • أنا مَرِيضٌ. — I am sick.
  • أحتاجُ دَوَاءً. — I need medicine.

These phrases are small and powerful: they let you ask about someone’s condition and respond in a polite, useful way.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Ask “كيف حالُكَ؟” and answer with one of the new health phrases: “أنا بِخَيْرٍ” or “أنا مَرِيضٌ”. This helps the words become a real conversation, not just a list.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

صِحَّةٌ · مَرِيضٌ · طَبِيبٌ · مُسْتَشْفَى · دَوَاءٌ

Remember these health words. They are the first vocabulary you need for asking how someone is and saying you need help.
