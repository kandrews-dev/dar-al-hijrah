الطعام والشراب

Food & Drink

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Name common foods (الطعام) and drinks (الشراب) in Arabic.
  • Say أُريدُ (I want) and أُحِبُّ (I like) + a noun.
  • Ask هل تُريدُ…؟ (do you want…?) and answer نعم / لا.
  • Know the Sunnah phrases of eating: بسم الله · الحمد لله.
  • Order or offer food in a few simple sentences.

Food is one of the first things a child talks about — and the table is full of Sunnah: eating with the right hand, saying بسم الله before and الحمد لله after, and never blaming the food. Learning these words in Arabic turns every meal into practice.

أُريدُ · أُحِبُّ "I want" & "I like"

Two very useful "I" verbs. Put a noun straight after them:

  • أُريدُ ماءً — urīdu māʾan — I want water
  • أُحِبُّ التَّمْرَ — uḥibbu at-tamr — I like dates
  • هل تُريدُ شايًا؟ — hal turīdu shāyan? — Do you want tea?
  • لا، شُكرًا — lā, shukran — No, thank you

Notice the little ـً (tanwīn) on the food after أُريد — that "-an" ending marks the object of the verb (the مفعول به you will study in naḥw). Just hear it for now: ماءً · شايًا.

آداب الطعام The Manners of Eating

The Prophet ﷺ taught: say بِسْمِ الله before eating, eat with the right hand, eat from what is nearest to you, and praise Allah when finished — الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ. And he ﷺ never once criticised food: if he liked it he ate it, and if not he simply left it. Every one of these is a small act of worship you can practise at today's meal.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Set the table and run a mini "restaurant" in Arabic. One person is the host asking ماذا تُريد؟ / هل تُريد…؟, the others answer أريدُ… · أحبُّ…. Everyone must say بسم الله before eating and الحمد لله after — in Arabic.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

بِسْمِ الله … الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ · أُريدُ … · أُحِبُّ …

Two Sunnah phrases and two verbs turn every meal into an Arabic lesson and an act of worship. Begin with the Name of Allah, end with His praise — and ask for what you want in His language.
