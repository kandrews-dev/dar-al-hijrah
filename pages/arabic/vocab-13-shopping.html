التَّسَوُّقُ وَالسُّوق

Shopping & the Market

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the common words for shopping and the market.
  • Ask and answer بِكَمْ هَذَا؟ and أريد شراء....
  • Use words like سوق · متجر · سعر · بضاعة · موز.
  • Talk about buying and selling in simple everyday Arabic.

Shopping vocabulary lets you move from talking about places and travel to real life in the market. This lesson gives you the phrases needed to buy something, ask for prices, and use the new words naturally.

كَلِمَاتُ التَّسَوُّق Shopping Words
الكلمة English
سُوقٌmarket
مَتْجَرٌstore
سِعْرٌprice
بِضَاعَةٌgoods
مَوْزٌbanana
ثَمَنٌcost / price
بِكَمْ هَذَا؟ How much is this?
  • بِكَمْ هذا؟ — How much is this?
  • هَذَا سِعْرُهُ خَمْسَةُ دَرَاهِمَ. — Its price is five dirhams.
  • أُرِيدُ شِرَاءَ كِيلُو مَوْزٍ. — I want to buy a kilo of bananas.
  • أَيْنَ المَتْجَرُ؟ — Where is the store?

These helpful shopping phrases make the market vocabulary practical and ready for real conversation.
