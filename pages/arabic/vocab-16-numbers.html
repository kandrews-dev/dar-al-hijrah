الأعداد ١–١٠

Numbers 1–10

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Count from وَاحِد to عَشَرَة.
  • Ask “how many?” with كَمْ؟.
  • Say what you have with عِنْدِي.
  • Recognise the numbers you already meet in the dīn — the five prayers, the five pillars.

You have been using numbers since Vocab 13 — every price in the market was a number. This lesson lines them all up: one to ten, the question كَمْ؟, and counting the things around you.

You already live with these numbers: الصَّلَوَاتُ الخَمْسُ (the five prayers), أَرْكَانُ الإِسْلَامِ الخَمْسَةُ (the five pillars), and every بِخَمْسَةِ دَرَاهِمَ in the market from Vocab 13.

كم؟ How many?
  • كَمْ كِتَابًا عِنْدَكَ؟ — How many books do you have?
  • عِنْدِي ثَلَاثَةُ كُتُبٍ. — I have three books.
  • فِي اليَوْمِ خَمْسُ صَلَوَاتٍ. — In the day there are five prayers.
  • فِي الأُسْبُوعِ سَبْعَةُ أَيَّامٍ. — In the week there are seven days.

Notice عِنْدِي (I have) and عِنْدَكَ (you have) — the word عِنْدَ with the pronoun endings you met in the ṣarf track.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Count things around the house out loud in Arabic — chairs, books, pens — then ask each other “كَمْ قَلَمًا عِنْدَكَ؟”. Counting real things fixes the numbers far faster than reciting the list.
