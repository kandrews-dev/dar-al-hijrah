التعليم والجامعة

Education & University

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Move up from the مَدْرَسَة of Vocab 8 to the جَامِعَة (university) — the next house of knowledge.
  • Speak about كُلِّيَّة (faculty), مُحَاضَرَة (lecture), اِمْتِحَان (exam) and دَرَجَة (grade).
  • Read a mini-passage, fully vowelled, about a student's day at university.
  • Take رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا as the student's motto — and see why the Islamic University of Madinah belongs inside this site's own story.

Back in Vocab 8 you learned the world of the مَدْرَسَة (school) — the classroom, the teacher, the pen. This lesson climbs one storey higher, to the جَامِعَة (university): faculties, lectures, exams, grades, and the long research that ends in a degree. And there is a reason this lesson feels close to home. This site is called دار الهجرة — the house of the hijrah — and the city of the hijrah, المدينة, is home to the Islamic University where students from every land gather to study the sciences you are studying here in miniature. Today's words are the words of that gathering.

Read the Note column slowly — it is half the lesson. Three of these words carry secrets you were trained to see: اِمْتِحَانٌ wears an اِفْتِعَال maṣdar shape, بَحْثٌ is a bare maṣdar, and جَامِعَةٌ hides a root the next box will open fully. A year of ṣarf, and suddenly a page of university vocabulary reads like family faces.

يوم في الجامعة Reading — A Student's Day at University
  • اِبْنُ عَمِّي طَالِبٌ فِي الجَامِعَةِ الإِسْلَامِيَّةِ فِي المَدِينَةِ. — My cousin (my uncle's son) is a student at the Islamic University in Madinah.
  • هُوَ يَدْرُسُ فِي كُلِّيَّةِ الشَّرِيعَةِ وَيَحْضُرُ مُحَاضَرَاتٍ فِي الفِقْهِ. — He studies in the Faculty of Sharīʿah and attends lectures in fiqh.
  • يَذْهَبُ إِلَى المَكْتَبَةِ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ وَيَكْتُبُ بَحْثًا. — He goes to the library every day and writes a research paper.
  • اِجْتَهَدَ فِي دُرُوسِهِ، فَنَجَحَ فِي الاِمْتِحَانِ، الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ. — He worked hard at his studies, so he passed the exam — al-ḥamdu lillāh.
  • وَبَعْدَ سَنَوَاتٍ يَأْخُذُ الشَّهَادَةَ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ. — And after some years he will take his degree, if Allah wills.

Read it twice: once for meaning, once out loud for the ḥarakāt. Notice نَجَحَ فِي — "passed" takes في before the exam it was passed in. And notice how many of today's ten words this little passage already uses.

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا The Student's Motto

«وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا»

And say: My Lord, increase me in knowledge.

سورة طه — Ṭā-Hā 20:114

أدب الطلب — The manners of seeking knowledge

You met this duʿāʼ on Ṣarf 10, and here it becomes a life: it is the only thing Allah commanded His Prophet ﷺ to ask for more of — not wealth, not rank, but عِلْم. Let it be the motto over every مُحَاضَرَة and every اِمْتِحَان: رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا. And one root to take with you — جَامِعَةٌ (the gathering-place of knowledge), الجُمُعَةُ (the day the community gathers), and اِجْتِمَاعٌ (a meeting, a gathering) are all built from ج م ع. One root, a whole family — exactly the way of seeing that the ṣarf track spent a year teaching you.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Around the table tonight, each person says what they would love to study and why, in simple Arabic: أُحِبُّ أَنْ أَدْرُسَ الفِقْهَ — I would love to study fiqh; أُرِيدُ أَنْ أَدْرُسَ فِي كُلِّيَّةِ الحَدِيثِ — I want to study in the Faculty of Ḥadīth. Then the "why", as simply as you can: لِأَنَّهُ عِلْمٌ نَافِعٌ — because it is beneficial knowledge. Dream out loud in Arabic — that is what this vocabulary is for.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا — جَامِعَةٌ · كُلِّيَّةٌ · مُحَاضَرَةٌ · اِمْتِحَانٌ

Keep the student's duʿāʼ on your tongue — My Lord, increase me in knowledge — and carry the four pillars of university life with it: university, faculty, lecture, exam.
