المَاءُ أَصْلُ الحَيَاةِ وَسِرُّهَا

Water: the Origin of Life and Its Secret

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the vocabulary of water and life — from عُنْصُرٌ to تَوَازُنٌ.
  • Read three real āyāt naming water as the origin of every living thing (21:30, 20:53, 24:45).
  • Learn the real Book 3 numbers: how the body gets its water, and how it loses it.
  • Know why protecting water from pollution and waste is a duty, not a preference.

الماء (water) is the first عُنْصُر (element) making up every living cell — there is no life at all without it. This lesson reads three āyāt that say so directly, then the real numbers Book 3 gives for how water moves through the human body, and why the Qurʾān's naming of water as حياة (life) itself makes protecting it an act of gratitude, not just hygiene.

١ — الماء أصل كل شيء حي 1 — Water, the Origin of Every Living Thing

وَجَعَلْنَا مِنَ الْمَاءِ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ حَيٍّ ۖ أَفَلَا يُؤْمِنُونَ

"And We made from water every living thing — so will they not believe?"

Sūrat al-Anbiyāʼ 21:30 · سورة الأنبياء

وَأَنزَلَ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ مَاءً فَأَخْرَجْنَا بِهِ أَزْوَاجًا مِّن نَّبَاتٍ شَتَّىٰ

"And He sent down rain from the sky, and We produced thereby pairs of vegetation of various kinds."

Sūrat Ṭā-Hā 20:53 · سورة طه

وَاللَّهُ خَلَقَ كُلَّ دَابَّةٍ مِّن مَّاءٍ

"And Allah has created every [living] creature from water."

Sūrat al-Nūr 24:45 · سورة النور

Three āyāt, three kinds of life: 21:30 names water the origin of كُلّ شَيْءٍ حَيّ (every living thing) in general; 20:53 names it the origin of plant life; 24:45 names it the origin of animal life. Some scholars define life itself as a "watery phenomenon" — no single living creature exists without water, though some can endure long dryness by going dormant, wrapped in membranes, until they either find water or die. But not one can grow or reproduce without it.

٢ — قصة الماء مع الإنسان 2 — The Story of Water with Man

The source text traces water through a whole human life: it begins as a نُطْفَة swimming in water, then a fetus in the womb, carried every necessity of life by water; then a newborn's first food is his mother's milk, whose consistency water dominates. Water is even present in sorrow, shed as tears. And here is a striking fact the text draws out: a person can bear hunger patiently for many days, but cannot bear thirst for more than a day and a night, or at most a few days, rarely beyond four.

٣ — أرقام حقيقية: الماء في الجسم 3 — Real Numbers: Water in the Body

A person gets his need of water from three main sources: about 47% from drinking water and other liquids; 39% from solid food — meat, vegetables, fruit and bread all contain water; and the remaining 14% is produced by the body's own metabolism. Water leaves the body mainly through urine (about two-thirds, and normal urine itself is 95% water), with the rest lost through sweat, breath, and the intestines.

Water regulates blood pressure, acidity, and body heat, keeping a precise تَوَازُن (balance) between what the body takes in and gives out. Lose about 1% of body weight in water, and the skin and hair dry out; lose 5%, and the throat and skin dry with a total collapse; lose over 10%, and death approaches. Strikingly, too much water is also dangerous — it can cause nausea, raised blood pressure, confusion, seizures, and eventually death. Water asks for balance, not excess in either direction.

٤ — واجب المحافظة على الماء 4 — The Duty to Protect Water

Water serves man beyond drinking — in cleanliness, cooking, industry, and transport by river, sea, and ocean; history itself records wars fought over it, and civilizations that flourished or perished by how well they managed it. Since it has become clear that water is a great نِعْمَة from Allah — الماء أصل الحياة وسرها — the source text closes with a direct duty: we must protect this نعمة from every source of تَلَوُّث, and not be wasteful (إِسْرَاف) in how we use it.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

As a family, check one tap or one habit in the house for إسراف (wastefulness) in water use this week, and fix it together. Then recite the three āyāt aloud and ask: if Allah made every living thing from water, what does that mean for how carefully we should use it?
