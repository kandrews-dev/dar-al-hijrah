السفر والمواصلات

Travel & Transportation

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the common words for travel and transportation.
  • Ask and answer أين المحطة؟ and كيف أذهب؟.
  • Use words like رحلة · محطة · قطار · سيارة · حافلة · مطار.
  • Talk about moving from one place to another in simple Arabic.

Once you can talk about home, school, and time, the next useful step is to talk about movement. Travel words help you describe how you go somewhere, where you wait, and what you use to arrive.

أين؟ وكيف؟ Where? · How?
  • أين المحطةُ؟ — Where is the station?
  • كيف أذهبُ إلى المدرسةِ؟ — How do I go to school?
  • أذهبُ بالسيارةِ — I go by car.
  • أركبُ القطارَ — I ride the train.

Small travel phrases make your Arabic more useful immediately, especially when you are moving around town or visiting a new place.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

On a map or in the neighborhood, point and say: أين المحطةُ؟ and أذهبُ بالسيارةِ. A few travel words are enough to make your Arabic much more practical.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

رِحْلَةٌ · مَحَطَّةٌ · قِطَارٌ · سَيَّارَةٌ · مَطَارٌ

Keep these travel words in your pocket. They are simple, but they let you speak about the world around you.
