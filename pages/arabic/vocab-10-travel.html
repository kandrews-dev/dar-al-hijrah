السفر والمواصلات

Travel & Transportation

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the common words for travel and transportation.
  • Ask and answer أين المحطة؟ and كيف أذهب؟.
  • Use words like رحلة · محطة · قطار · سيارة · حافلة · مطار.
  • Talk about moving from one place to another in simple Arabic.

Once you can talk about home, school, and time, the next useful step is to talk about movement. Travel words help you describe how you go somewhere, where you wait, and what you use to arrive.

أين؟ وكيف؟ Where? · How?
  • أين المحطةُ؟ — Where is the station?
  • كيف أذهبُ إلى المدرسةِ؟ — How do I go to school?
  • أذهبُ بالسيارةِ — I go by car.
  • أركبُ القطارَ — I ride the train.

Small travel phrases make your Arabic more useful immediately, especially when you are moving around town or visiting a new place.

تَعْبِير — Expression

ضَرَبَ فِي الأَرْضِ

المَعْنَى

سَافَرَ وَخَرَجَ مِنْ بَلَدِهِ لِلتِّجَارَةِ أَوِ الجِهَادِ أَوْ طَلَبِ الرِّزْقِ.

peek — English

He travelled and set out from his land to trade, fight, or seek provision.

الصُّورَة

جِذْرُ ض ر ب يَعْنِي "ضَرَبَ" حِسِّيًّا — كَأَنَّ المُسَافِرَ يَضْرِبُ الأَرْضَ بِخُطُوَاتِهِ وَهُوَ يَقْطَعُهَا، فَصَارَ التَّعْبِيرُ يَعْنِي: سَافَرَ بَعِيدًا.

peek — English

The root ض ر ب means "to strike" physically — as if the traveller strikes the earth with his footsteps as he crosses it. The image of striking the ground, step after step, became the idiom for travelling far.

فِي القُرْآن

«وَإِذَا ضَرَبْتُمْ فِي الْأَرْضِ فَلَيْسَ عَلَيْكُمْ جُنَاحٌ أَن تَقْصُرُوا مِنَ الصَّلَاةِ»

Sūrat al-Nisāʾ 4:101 · سورة النساء

peek — English

"And when you travel throughout the land, there is no blame upon you for shortening the prayer." This is the very āyah that permits the traveller to shorten his ṣalāh.

عَائِلَةُ الجَذْر — ض ر ب
  • «فَاضْرِبُوا فَوْقَ الْأَعْنَاقِ» — the literal strike (الأنفال 8:12)
  • «وَتِلْكَ الْأَمْثَالُ نَضْرِبُهَا لِلنَّاسِ» — to "strike" an example, i.e. to give one (العنكبوت 29:43)
  • «ضُرِبَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ الذِّلَّةُ» — humiliation "struck upon" them, i.e. imposed on them (البقرة 2:61)
peek — English

One root, four faces: to strike (literally), to travel ("strike out" across the land), to present an example ("strike" a parable for people), and to impose ("strike" humiliation upon someone). Same three letters, ض ر ب, every time.

إِذَا لَمْ يَسْتَقِمِ المَعْنَى الحَرْفِيّ، فَهُوَ تَعْبِير — اُنْظُرْ إِلَى الجَذْرِ وَالصُّورَةِ وَالسِّيَاق. If the literal meaning doesn't stand, it's a تعبير — look at the root, the image, and the context. (سَيَّارَة above is the same lesson in one word: a "car" today, a "caravan" — «وَجَاءَتْ سَيَّارَةٌ», Yūsuf 12:19 — in the Book.)

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

On a map or in the neighborhood, point and say: أين المحطةُ؟ and أذهبُ بالسيارةِ. A few travel words are enough to make your Arabic much more practical.

رِحْلَةٌ · مَحَطَّةٌ · قِطَارٌ · سَيَّارَةٌ · مَطَارٌ

Keep these travel words in your pocket. They are simple, but they let you speak about the world around you.
