مِنْ يَوْمِيَّاتِ وَلِيد

From a Newborn's Diary

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the vocabulary of a newborn's first hours — from وَلِيدٌ to ابْتِسَامَة.
  • Read an imaginative first-person story told from a newborn's own point of view.
  • Meet the real companion this lesson's newborn is named after — Anas ibn Mālik — and the Prophet's ﷺ real duʿāʼ for him.
  • Close Book 3 of the Lugha track — every real unit, now on the site.

Book 3's final unit is its most creative: a newborn named أَنَس narrates his own first, bewildering night in the world — separated from his mother, examined, and finally reunited — in his own voice. This lesson reads his story exactly as Book 3 tells it.

النَّصّ From a Newborn's Diary — told in his own voice

أَنَا ضَيْفٌ جَدِيدٌ فِي هَذِهِ الدُّنْيَا، عُمْرِي أَيَّامٌ قَلِيلَةٌ. وُلِدْتُ فِي أُسْرَةٍ مُسْلِمَةٍ، وَسَمَّانِي أَبِي «أَنَسًا» — وَهَذَا اسْمُ خَادِمِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ الصَّحَابِيِّ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ. وَكَثِيرًا مَا أَسْمَعُ أَبِي يَقُولُ: «أَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ أَنْ يَجْعَلَكَ مِثْلَ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ».

I am a new guest in this world; my age is a few days. I was born into a Muslim family, and my father named me "Anas" — the name of the servant of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, the Companion Anas ibn Mālik, may Allah be pleased with him. And I often hear my father say: "I ask Allah to make you like Anas ibn Mālik."

كُنْتُ أَنْتَظِرُ أَنْ تَضُمَّنِي أُمِّي إِلَى صَدْرِهَا وَتُقَبِّلَنِي، وَلَكِنْ لَمْ أَجِدْهَا! وَجَاءَتِ امْرَأَةٌ تَلْبَسُ مَلَابِسَ بَيْضَاءَ تَحْمِلُنِي عَارِيًا، وَتَغْسِلُ جَسَدِي! ثُمَّ لَفَّتْنِي فِي قَمِيصٍ أَخْضَرَ، وَحَمَلَتْنِي هَذِهِ المَرْأَةُ بِسُرْعَةٍ، وَدَخَلَتْ بِي إِلَى غُرْفَةٍ مَكْتُوبٍ عَلَيْهَا «الحَضَانَة». أَرَدْتُ أَنْ أَقُولَ: لَا، أَوْ أُنَادِيَ أَبِي، وَلَكِنِّي لَمْ أَسْتَطِعْ!

I was waiting for my mother to hold me to her chest and kiss me, but I didn't find her! A woman wearing white clothes came, carrying me unclothed, and washed my body! Then she wrapped me in a green garment, and carried me quickly, and brought me into a room labelled "Nursery." I wanted to say no, or call for my father, but I couldn't!

أَهَكَذَا يَا أُمِّي؟! أَهَكَذَا يَا أَبِي؟! تَتْرُكَانِي وَحِيدًا فِي أَوَّلِ لَيْلَةٍ فِي الدُّنْيَا! وَبَيْنَمَا أَنَا كَذَلِكَ، إِذْ بِي أَسْمَعُ صَوْتَ بُكَاءٍ قَرِيبٍ مِنِّي، فَنَظَرْتُ، فَإِذَا رَضِيعٌ صَغِيرٌ يَنَامُ فِي صُنْدُوقٍ مِثْلِ صُنْدُوقِي! وَفَجْأَةً ارْتَفَعَ صَوْتُ البُكَاءِ عَالِيًا، وَإِذَا بِعَشَرَةِ أَطْفَالٍ فِي الغُرْفَةِ يَبْكُونَ لِبُكَاءِ هَذَا الرَّضِيعِ، وَقَدِ اسْتَيْقَظُوا جَمِيعًا مِنَ النَّوْمِ، فَمَا كَانَ مِنِّي إِلَّا أَنْ بَكَيْتُ أَنَا أَيْضًا!

Is this how it is, mother?! Is this how it is, father?! You leave me alone on my first night in the world! And while I was like that, I suddenly heard a crying sound close to me, so I looked, and there was a small infant sleeping in a box like mine! Suddenly the sound of crying rose loud, and ten children in the room began crying at this infant's crying — they had all woken from sleep — so I too could do nothing but cry! Notice فَمَا كَانَ مِنِّي إِلَّا أَنْ بَكَيْتُ — "there was nothing from me except that I cried" — a whole idiom of helplessness in five words, and one you now own.

الصحابي الحقيقي — The Real Anas ibn Mālik

«اللَّهُمَّ أَكْثِرْ مَالَهُ وَوَلَدَهُ، وَبَارِكْ لَهُ فِيمَا أَعْطَيْتَهُ»

The real Anas ibn Mālik served the Messenger of Allah ﷺ for ten years from the age of ten, and his mother Umm Sulaym once asked the Prophet ﷺ to make duʿāʼ for him. He said: "O Allah, increase his wealth and children, and bless him in what You have given him" (al-Bukhārī & Muslim). Anas later said he had buried more than a hundred of his own children and grandchildren, and lived to be over one hundred years old — the duʿāʼ answered in full. This story's newborn is named after a man whose whole life shows what a father's hope for his son can become.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Retell Anas's first night in your own words, in Arabic if you can. Then ask any parents in the family to share, in simple Arabic or English, one real memory of a newborn's first night — and close by learning the story of the real Anas ibn Mālik together: ten years serving the Prophet ﷺ, and a duʿāʼ answered for the rest of his long life.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي
