الفصل

The Classroom — Things Around You

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Name the common objects of the الفصل (classroom) and the desk in Arabic.
  • Use the demonstratives هذا (this — masculine) and هذه (this — feminine) correctly.
  • Ask and answer ما هذا؟ / ما هذه؟ (what is this?) with a simple جملة اسمية (nominal sentence).
  • Say where something is using على (on), في (in), and أين؟ (where?).
  • Read short sentences like هذا كتابٌ · هذه سبّورةٌ · القلمُ على المكتبِ.

After greetings, the fastest way into Arabic is to name what is around you. In this lesson the room becomes the textbook: every object you can point at is a new word. Master pointing and asking — هذا؟ ما هذا؟ — and you already hold the key to endless conversations.

هذا وهذه "This" — Masculine & Feminine

هذا كتابٌ · هذه سبّورةٌ

This is a book · This is a blackboard.

Arabic has no word for "is" in the present — you simply place two words side by side: هذا كتابٌ = "this [is] a book." Which "this" you use depends on the gender of the noun, not the object: هذا for a masculine word (كتاب, قلم, باب) and هذه for a feminine word (سبّورة, نافذة, حقيبة). A quick rule: most words ending in ة (tāʾ marbūṭah) are feminine, so they take هذه. This two-word sentence — a subject with no verb — is called a جملة اسمية (nominal sentence).

ما هذا؟ · ما هذه؟ Asking "What Is This?"
السؤال / الجواب Question / Answer
ما هذا؟ — هذا قلمٌ. What is this? — This is a pen. (masculine)
ما هذه؟ — هذه ساعةٌ. What is this? — This is a clock. (feminine)
هل هذا كتابٌ؟ — نعم، هذا كتابٌ. Is this a book? — Yes, this is a book.
هل هذه نافذةٌ؟ — لا، هذه سبّورةٌ. Is this a window? — No, this is a blackboard.
أين؟ · على وفي Where? — "on" and "in"

أين (ayna) means "where?" To answer, use two small but essential prepositions:
القلمُ على المكتبِ — the pen is on the desk (على = on).
الكتابُ في الحقيبةِ — the book is in the bag (في = in).
Notice the word after على and في takes a kasrah (the "-i" ending: المكتبِ، الحقيبةِ) — prepositions put the following noun into the majrūr (genitive) state. You'll meet this again in the naḥw track; for now, just hear the "-i" ending. Also useful: هنا (here) and هناك (there).

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Walk around the house with the children. Point at ten objects and ask ما هذا؟ or ما هذه؟. They answer in a full sentence: هذا بابٌ · هذه ساعةٌ. Then hide an object and ask أين الكتابُ؟ — the first to answer correctly with على or في wins a point.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

ما هذا؟ — هذا كتابٌ · ما هذه؟ — هذه سبّورةٌ · أين القلمُ؟ — على المكتبِ

Point, ask, answer. Once هذا / هذه and ما هذا؟ are automatic, you can name your whole world in Arabic — one object at a time. Keep the "-un" ending (كتابٌ) and the "-i" after prepositions (المكتبِ) in your ear.
