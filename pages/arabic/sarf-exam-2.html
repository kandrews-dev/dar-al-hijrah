SECTION ATrue or False — صحيح أم خاطئ10 marks · 1 each

Question 1

الضمير المتصل — the attached pronoun — clings to the end of the verb, names the one the action lands on, and never stands alone.

Question 2

ظَلَمْنَا A sukūn on the letter before the نا — as in ظَلَمْنَا — tells you the نا is the object, "us".

Question 3

نَصَرَكُمْ One Arabic word such as نَصَرَكُمْ can hold a verb, a doer and an object all at once — a whole sentence in a single word.

Question 4

المصدر names the action together with its tense and its doer.

Question 5

The maṣdar of the bare three-letter verb follows many different patterns and is learned by hearing — the scholars call it سَمَاعِيّ.

Question 6

The increased doors are the same — each increased verb's maṣdar must also be heard from the Arabs, with no rule to build it.

Question 7

اِسْتَغْفَرَ The door اِسْتَفْعَلَ carries the meaning of seeking the action — اِسْتَغْفَرَ means he sought forgiveness.

Question 8

The door اِنْفَعَلَ means doing the action together, mutually — as in تَعَاوَنَ, they cooperated.

Question 9

كَتَبَ · دَرَسَ · جَلَسَ Every verb you conjugated in Year 1 was a sound verb — no weak letter anywhere in its root — and that is why the tables always behaved.

Question 10

مِفْتَاح A kasrah on the mīm — as in مِفْتَاح — marks the noun of place.
