بقية الأبواب

The Remaining Doors — Completing the Ten

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Complete the famous ten doors of the verb — the bare فَعَلَ plus nine increased.
  • Recognise the five common doors of today: تَفَعَّلَ · تَفَاعَلَ · اِنْفَعَلَ · اِفْتَعَلَ · اِسْتَفْعَلَ.
  • Know each door's flavor of meaning and its one regular maṣdar.
  • Treasure the pair اِسْتَغْفَرَ ← اِسْتِغْفَار — the door of seeking, on your tongue every day.
Where we are in the ṣarf track: Ṣarf 7 opened the first three increased doors — فَعَّلَ · فَاعَلَ · أَفْعَلَ — and promised that the rest would come later on the ladder. Today is later. The five common doors below complete the famous ten that every institute of Arabic has its students memorise: the bare فَعَلَ plus nine increased. As always, recognition remains the Year-2 goal — see the door, name it, feel its flavor. Mastery of every fine rule comes further up the ladder.
الأبواب الخمسة الباقية Part One — The Five Remaining Doors

خمسةُ أبوابٍ تُتِمُّ العشرةَ المشهورةَ، ولكلِّ بابٍ مَعْنَاهُ ومصدرُهُ القياسيُّ

Five doors complete the famous ten, and each door carries its own flavor of meaning and one regular maṣdar — unlike the bare verb of Ṣarf 10, whose maṣdar had to be learned by ear, every verb that walks through one of these doors gets its maṣdar by rule. Learn one small table and you own both the verb and its action-noun:

الباب الوزن المصدر مثال Meaning flavor
الخامستَفَعَّلَتَفَعُّلعَلَّمَ ← تَعَلَّمَTaking the action on oneself, gradually. عَلَّمَ he taught → تَعَلَّمَ he LEARNED — he took the knowledge onto himself, piece by piece. Maṣdar: التَّعَلُّم — learning.
السادستَفَاعَلَتَفَاعُلتَعَاوَنَDoing it together, mutually. تَعَاوَنَ they cooperated — each side helped the other. Maṣdar: التَّعَاوُن — mutual help.
السابعاِنْفَعَلَاِنْفِعَالاِنْكَسَرَThe action happening TO something — passive-like. كَسَرَ he broke it → اِنْكَسَرَ it broke — no one named as breaker; the breaking simply landed on it. Maṣdar: الاِنْكِسَار.
الثامناِفْتَعَلَاِفْتِعَالاِجْتَمَعَDoing it for oneself, with effort. جَمَعَ he gathered things → اِجْتَمَعَ they gathered — brought themselves together. Maṣdar: الاِجْتِمَاع — a gathering, a meeting.
العاشراِسْتَفْعَلَاِسْتِفْعَالغَفَرَ ← اِسْتَغْفَرَSEEKING the action. غَفَرَ he forgave → اِسْتَغْفَرَ he SOUGHT forgiveness — he asked for the action to be done for him. Maṣdar: الاِسْتِغْفَار — the seeking of forgiveness.

Read the additions aloud and watch them move: a تَـ in front for the fifth and sixth doors, and a hamzat waṣl — the light اِ that drops in connected speech — opening the seventh, eighth and tenth. The trick of Ṣarf 7 still rules everything: find the three root letters first, and whatever is left over is the addition telling you which door you are standing in. And notice the gift in the third column — each of these doors has exactly one maṣdar pattern, no guessing, no dictionary.

باب الطلب The Gem — اِسْتَفْعَلَ, the Door of Seeking

اِسْتَغْفَرَ طَلَبَ المغفرةَ، واِسْتَعَانَ طَلَبَ العونَ، واِسْتَعَاذَ طَلَبَ المعاذَ

Of the five, hold this door closest: اِسْتَفْعَلَ means to seek the root's action — and your daily adhkār are built on it. اِسْتَغْفَرَ — to seek forgiveness. اِسْتَعَانَ — to seek help: and here is the Fātiḥah again, because إِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ carries نَسْتَعِينُ — the نحن muḍāriʿ of اِسْتَعَانَ: You alone we ask for help. اِسْتَعَاذَ — to seek refuge: the whole أَعُوذُ بِاللهِ family. Every time you say them, you are walking through the tenth door — asking Allah for what only He gives.

أمثلة قرآنية Examples from the Qur'an

وَتَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْبِرِّ وَالتَّقْوَىٰ

And cooperate with one another upon righteousness and taqwā. (al-Māʼidah 5:2)

تَعَاوَنُوا — the door تَفَاعَلَ wearing the ـُوا ending you have known since Ṣarf 5: cooperate together. The mutual flavor of the sixth door is exactly what the ayah commands — righteousness is not done alone; each helps the other. Its maṣdar التَّعَاوُن names the cooperation itself.

فَقُلْتُ اسْتَغْفِرُوا رَبَّكُمْ إِنَّهُ كَانَ غَفَّارًا

So I said: seek forgiveness of your Lord — indeed, He is ever-Forgiving. (Nūḥ 71:10)

اسْتَغْفِرُوا — the amr plural of اِسْتَغْفَرَ: Nūḥ عليه السلام commanding his people to seek forgiveness — the tenth door in the mouth of a prophet. And look at the end of the ayah: غَفَّارًا — the same root غ ف ر on the intensive pattern فَعَّال — ever-Forgiving, again and again. One ayah, one root, two patterns: they seek repeatedly, He forgives more.

Memorize — احفظ

فَعَلَ · فَعَّلَ · فَاعَلَ · أَفْعَلَ · تَفَعَّلَ · تَفَاعَلَ · اِنْفَعَلَ · اِفْتَعَلَ · اِسْتَفْعَلَ

Nine doors in one chant — and the ninth, the rare اِفْعَلَّ, is kept for colors and defects: اِحْمَرَّ — it turned red, from the same root as أحمر (Vocab 17). Recite the chant until it runs like water; from today, every verb you ever meet lives behind one of these ten doors.
