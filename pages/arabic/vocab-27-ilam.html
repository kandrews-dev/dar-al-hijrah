الإعلام والأخبار

Media & News

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the vocabulary of news and media, from خَبَرٌ (news item) to إِعْلَانٌ (announcement).
  • Ask what happened with مَاذَا حَدَثَ؟
  • Read a mini-passage of simple news, fully vowelled.
  • Learn the Qur'anic adab of verifying news before spreading it — فَتَبَيَّنُوا.

News is everywhere — in the صَحِيفَة on the table, on the إِذَاعَة in the car, on every قَنَاة on the screen. This lesson gives you the Arabic to name it all, a tiny news passage to read on your own, and — most important of all — the Qur'an's own rule for what a Muslim does with news before passing it on. Ten words, one question, one āyah, one rule.

Notice the pairs: صَحِيفَةٌ gives you its person, the صَحَفِيّ who writes it; and أَعْلَنَ gives you its noun, the إِعْلَان he made. Words in Arabic travel in families — catch one member and you can usually name the rest.

لطيفة — Old friends hiding in the news

خَبَرٌ is the very word the naḥw track uses for the predicate — and the name is no accident: the خبر of a sentence tells about the mubtadaʾ exactly the way a news item tells about the world. Then look at the door-IV family: أَعْلَنَ (he announced) sits on أَفْعَلَ with the hamzah out front, and إِعْلَانٌ is its maṣdar on the shape إِفْعَال. Even إِذَاعَةٌ (radio) belongs to that door — an إِفْعَالَة-like maṣdar shape from أَذَاعَ, "to broadcast, to spread abroad". The media vocabulary is the ṣarf track reading you the headlines.

مَاذَا حَدَثَ؟ What Happened?
  • مَاذَا حَدَثَ؟ — What happened?
  • حَدَثَ شَيْءٌ جَمِيلٌ الْيَوْمَ! — Something beautiful happened today!
  • سَمِعْتُ الخَبَرَ فِي الإِذَاعَةِ. — I heard the news on the radio.
  • قَرَأْتُ الإِعْلَانَ فِي الصَّحِيفَةِ. — I read the announcement in the newspaper.

مَاذَا is the question-word "what?", and حَدَثَ is a plain door-I past verb — so مَاذَا حَدَثَ؟ is literally "what happened?", the question every news report exists to answer.

قراءة — خَبَرٌ سَعِيدٌ Mini-Passage — A Happy Piece of News
  • قَرَأَتِ الأُسْرَةُ خَبَرًا سَعِيدًا فِي الصَّحِيفَةِ. — The family read a happy piece of news in the newspaper.
  • فُتِحَ مَسْجِدٌ جَدِيدٌ فِي المَدِينَةِ. — A new mosque has been opened in the city.
  • قَرَأَ الأَبُ الخَبَرَ بِصَوْتٍ عَالٍ. — The father read the news aloud.
  • شَكَرُوا اللهَ عَلَى هَذَا الخَبَرِ الجَمِيلِ. — They thanked Allah for this beautiful news.
  • قَالَ الأَبُ: نُصَلِّي فِيهِ يَوْمَ الجُمُعَةِ إِنْ شَاءَ اللهُ. — The father said: we will pray in it on Friday, if Allah wills.

Read it twice: once slowly, tracking every ḥarakah, and once at speaking speed, as if you were the أَب reading it aloud to the family. Five sentences, and every content word in them came from this lesson's table or an earlier vocab lesson — that is what it feels like when the vocabulary starts paying you back.

فَتَبَيَّنُوا Verify Before You Share

«يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِن جَاءَكُمْ فَاسِقٌ بِنَبَإٍ فَتَبَيَّنُوا»

O you who believe, if a corrupt person comes to you with news, then verify it.

سورة الحجرات — al-Ḥujurāt 49:6

Here is the adab that turns this vocabulary lesson into a life rule. The āyah says: if a فَاسِق — a corrupt, unreliable person — brings you news, do not swallow it and do not spread it: verify it. Two treasures hide in the wording. First, نَبَأ is another Arabic word for news — the very نبأ of سورة النبأ on the Quran tab, "the great news" the people of Makkah were disputing about. Second, فَتَبَيَّنُوا is a door-V command in the plural — the تَفَعَّلَ family you met in Ṣarf 11 — "seek to make it clear for yourselves". In an age when a false خَبَر can circle the world in a minute, the Muslim's rule is fourteen centuries old and one word long: verify before you share.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Each evening this week, one person becomes the family's صَحَفِيّ and tells everyone خَبَرًا وَاحِدًا — one piece of news — from their day, in Arabic. The rest of the family opens the broadcast with مَاذَا حَدَثَ؟ and the reporter answers with حَدَثَ… Keep it to one sentence at first; by the end of the week, try two. And hold every report to this lesson's standard: only true news, and nothing passed on without تَبَيُّن.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

فَتَبَيَّنُوا — «إِن جَاءَكُمْ فَاسِقٌ بِنَبَإٍ فَتَبَيَّنُوا»

One word, one rule, for life: verify before you share. When news reaches you, remember al-Ḥujurāt 49:6 — check it before you believe it, and check it again before you pass it on.
