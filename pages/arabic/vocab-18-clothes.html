الملابس

Clothes

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the main clothing words from ثَوْبٌ (garment) to حِذَاءٌ (shoes).
  • Say what you are wearing with أَلْبَسُ (I wear / I am wearing).
  • Combine clothes with the colors from Vocab 17: a white shirt, black shoes.
  • Know the Sunnah of wearing white clothing.

Last lesson gave you the colors; this lesson gives them something to describe. With a handful of clothing words and one verb — أَلْبَسُ (I am wearing) — you can say what you and everyone around you have on, and you will meet a Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ about what to wear.

ماذا تلبس؟ What are you wearing?
  • مَاذَا تَلْبَسُ؟ — What are you wearing?
  • أَلْبَسُ قَمِيصًا أَبْيَضَ. — I am wearing a white shirt.
  • الثَّوْبُ جَمِيلٌ. — The garment is beautiful.
  • الحِذَاءُ أَسْوَدُ. — The shoes are black.

The color adjectives from Vocab 17 now describe real things — each one follows its noun like a نعت: قَمِيصٌ أَبْيَضُ، حِذَاءٌ أَسْوَدُ.

سنة — The Sunnah of white clothing

«الْبَسُوا مِنْ ثِيَابِكُمُ الْبَيَاضَ فَإِنَّهَا مِنْ خَيْرِ ثِيَابِكُمْ»

The Prophet ﷺ commanded: “Wear white from your garments, for they are among your best garments” (Abū Dāwūd & al-Tirmidhī; graded authentic by al-Albānī).

Look at the first word: الْبَسُوا is a فعل أمر — the command verb you just learned in Ṣarf 4. The Prophet ﷺ is commanding the believers, so the verb takes the command form. Notice also ثِيَاب — the plural of ثَوْبٌ from the table above.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Each morning while getting dressed, say in Arabic what you are wearing and its color: أَلْبَسُ قَمِيصًا أَبْيَضَ، وَالحِذَاءُ أَسْوَدُ. Two lessons — clothes and colors — practised together in one minute a day.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

ثَوْبٌ · قَمِيصٌ · سِرْوَالٌ · حِذَاءٌ · أَلْبَسُ

Run the core clothing words out loud until they come without thinking — and keep the Sunnah of white clothing in mind whenever you dress.
