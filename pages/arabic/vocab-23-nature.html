الطبيعة

Nature

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the main nature words, from بَحْرٌ (sea) to زَهْرَةٌ (flower).
  • See creation as آيَات (signs) pointing to the Creator.
  • Reuse the weather words of Vocab 14 and the colors of Vocab 17.
  • Spot the form-IV verb أَنْزَلَ from Ṣarf 7 in the Qur'an.

Step outside and everything you see was placed there — the sea that never spills over, the mountain that never moves, the star that never loses its way. The Qur'an keeps pointing at these things and asking: who made them? Eight words in this lesson and you can name the biggest things Allah created, from the earth under your feet to the moon above your head.

آيَات Signs of Allah

لطيفة — Creation points to its Creator

The Qur'an has a special name for the things in your table: آيَات — signs. The sea, the mountain, the star — each one points beyond itself to the One who made it. And you already own some of these signs: الشَّمْس (the sun), السَّمَاء (the sky) and المَطَر (the rain) were all Vocab 14 — today's eight join them, and your picture of the world in Arabic is nearly complete. One more thing to notice: شَجَرَةٌ and زَهْرَةٌ end in ة, so they take هَذِهِ — the very feminine rule you learned pointing at the animals in Vocab 22.

«أَلَمْ تَرَ أَنَّ اللَّهَ أَنزَلَ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ مَاءً فَتُصْبِحُ الْأَرْضُ مُخْضَرَّةً»

Do you not see that Allah sends down water from the sky, and the earth becomes green?

سورة الحج — al-Ḥajj 22:63

Count the treasures in this one āyah. أَنزَلَ (He sent down) is the form-IV door you built in Ṣarf 7 — the hamzah at the front turns "come down" into "send down". مَاء (water) was Vocab 5, back at the food table. And مُخْضَرَّة (turned green) carries the green of Vocab 17 — أَخْضَر — inside it. Three lessons meeting in a single verse: rain falls from السَّمَاء, and الأَرْض turns green.

«وَالْجِبَالَ أَوْتَادًا»

And the mountains as pegs.

سورة النبأ — al-Nabaʼ 78:7

الجِبَال is the plural of your word جَبَلٌ — Allah drove the mountains into the earth like tent pegs, holding it steady. This āyah comes from سورة النبأ on the Quran tab — the sūrah that opens Juzʾ ʿAmma, so you may already carry these very words in your memorization.

جُمَل Little Sentences
  • البَحْرُ وَاسِعٌ. — The sea is vast.
  • الجَبَلُ كَبِيرٌ. — The mountain is big.
  • هَذِهِ شَجَرَةٌ جَمِيلَةٌ. — This is a beautiful tree — note the feminine adjective جَمِيلَة following شَجَرَة.
  • القَمَرُ فِي السَّمَاءِ. — The moon is in the sky.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

On a walk, or simply looking out of the window, name what you see in Arabic: هَذِهِ شَجَرَةٌ! هَذَا نَهْرٌ! القَمَرُ فِي السَّمَاءِ! And over each sign, say سُبْحَانَ اللهِ — glorified is Allah — because these are not just vocabulary words; they are آيَات, and naming them is a chance to remember the One who made them.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

بَحْرٌ · جَبَلٌ · نَهْرٌ · شَجَرَةٌ · نَجْمٌ · قَمَرٌ · أَرْضٌ · زَهْرَةٌ

Recite the eight nature words out loud until they come without stopping — sea, mountain, river, tree, star, moon, earth, flower.
