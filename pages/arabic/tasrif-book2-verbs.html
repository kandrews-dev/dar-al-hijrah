أَفْعَالُ الكِتَابِ الثَّانِي

Book 2 — Verb Reference

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • See the 7 new verbs Book 2 adds, beyond the 14 you already know from Book 1.
  • Notice a few new shapes: hollow verbs (سَارَ), assimilated verbs (وَصَلَ), and a door-VIII verb (اِحْتَاجَ).
  • Keep building the same three-shape habit — الماضي، المضارع، الأمر — before the full Ṣarf track names the rules.
  • Arabic and English only — no transliteration.

Book 2 (Vocab 10-24) is mostly nouns — numbers, colors, body parts, animals, the natural world. Only seven genuinely new verbs appear across its fifteen lessons, each already met in Vocab 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, or 18. This page collects all seven in one place, the same way the Book 1 page did for its fourteen.

Notice سَارَ and وَصَلَ: their middle or first root letter seems to "disappear" in المضارع and الأمر — this is your first hint of حروف العلة (weak letters), which the full Ṣarf track (sarf-12) explains in depth. For now, just recognize the pattern when you see it.

Memorize — احفظ

وَصَلَ ← أَصِلُ ← صِلْ

One root, three shapes: he arrived (past) → I arrive (present) → arrive! (command). Notice the و of وَصَلَ disappears in the other two shapes — a preview of the "weak verb" chapter later in the Ṣarf track.
