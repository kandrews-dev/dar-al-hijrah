يَوْمٌ فِي حَيَاةِ نَاشِئ

A Day in a Youth's Life

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Learn the vocabulary of a Muslim youth's sunnah daily routine, waking to sleeping.
  • Know the authentic duʿāʾ for waking, and the virtue of praying in jamāʿah.
  • Learn the etiquette of the road: ghaḍḍ al-baṣar and good speech.
  • Read a connected passage describing the youth's day in his own words.

Unit 2 of Book 3 follows a young Muslim through one ordinary day — and shows how the Sunnah fills every part of it: the moment he wakes, the walk to the masjid, his time in study, and the moment he lies down to sleep. Nothing here is exotic; everything here is available to you today.

أَذْكَارُ الصَّبَاح Waking & the Virtue of Congregational Prayer

The very first words on the youth's tongue when he wakes:

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ

"All praise is for Allah who gave us life after having caused us to die, and to Him is the resurrection."

al-Bukhārī

He is keen to pray the sunnah of Fajr and then the obligatory prayer in the mosque with the congregation, because of what the Prophet ﷺ said:

صَلَاةُ الْجَمَاعَةِ أَفْضَلُ مِنْ صَلَاةِ الْفَذِّ بِسَبْعٍ وَعِشْرِينَ دَرَجَةً

"Prayer in congregation is more excellent than praying alone by twenty-seven degrees."

al-Bukhārī & Muslim, from Ibn ʿUmar

فِي الطَّرِيقِ وَالمَدْرَسَة On the Road & at School

Before some light exercise to strengthen his body, the youth recalls:

الْمُؤْمِنُ الْقَوِيُّ خَيْرٌ وَأَحَبُّ إِلَى اللَّهِ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِ الضَّعِيفِ

"The strong believer is better and more beloved to Allah than the weak believer."

Muslim, from Abū Hurayrah

On his way to school he keeps the آداب الطريق: spreading salām, good speech with people, enjoining what is right, and غَضُّ البَصَر — lowering the gaze. At school he strives for taqwā, keeps the company of the righteous, guards his prayers, and is truthful and sincere with his classmates. The Prophet ﷺ said Allah loves that whoever does a task should perfect it — يُتْقِنَهُ.

قَبْلَ النَّوْم Before Sleep

The youth avoids too much سَهَر (staying up late), because it harms his health and costs him the بَرَكَة he expects the next morning at Fajr. Before sleeping he recites Āyat al-Kursī, Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ, and the Muʿawwidhatayn (al-Falaq and al-Nās) — and so his day closes the way it opened: full of the Sunnah, full of good.

النَّصّ Mini-Passage

أَسْتَيْقِظُ قَبْلَ الفَجْرِ، وَأَقُولُ: الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا. أُصَلِّي الفَجْرَ فِي المَسْجِدِ مَعَ الجَمَاعَةِ. فِي الطَّرِيقِ أَغُضُّ بَصَرِي وَأُلْقِي السَّلَامَ. وَقَبْلَ النَّوْمِ أَقْرَأُ آيَةَ الكُرْسِيِّ، ثُمَّ أَنَامُ مُبَكِّرًا.

Read it aloud twice before peeking: I wake before Fajr, and I say: "Praise be to Allah who gave us life after having caused us to die." I pray Fajr at the mosque with the congregation. On the road I lower my gaze and give salām. And before sleeping I recite Āyat al-Kursī, then I sleep early.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Walk through your own morning together and name the sunnah in each step: waking, wuḍūʾ, Fajr in jamāʿah, the walk to school or work. Where does your own day already match the youth's day in this lesson — and where is there room to grow?

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ

Say this the moment you wake tomorrow — before your feet touch the floor.
