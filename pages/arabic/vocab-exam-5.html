This is the final examination of the lugha spine — it reviews everything from Vocab 31–36: the professions of the dīn, ḥajj and ʿumrah, hospitality and generosity, character, the Muslim world, and seeking knowledge. Every word here was taught in those lessons. Bismillāh.

SECTION ATrue or False — صحيح أم خاطئ10 marks · 1 each

Question 1

مُؤَذِّنٌ The word مُؤَذِّنٌ means “caller to prayer” — the doer of door II أَذَّنَ, on the pattern مُفَعِّل.

Question 2

لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ In the talbiyah, the words لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ mean “all praise and favour are Yours.”

Question 3

مَائِدَةٌ The word مَائِدَةٌ means “dining table” — and the fifth sūrah of the Qur'an carries this name.

Question 4

بَخِيلٌ The word بَخِيلٌ means “generous.”

Question 5

عَاصِمَةٌ The word عَاصِمَةٌ means “capital city” — the فَاعِلَة-patterned word for the leading city of a بَلَد.

Question 6

حَلْقَةٌ The word حَلْقَةٌ means “library.”

Question 7

قَاضٍ · مُفْتٍ The words قَاضٍ (judge) and مُفْتٍ (muftī) are the manqūṣ pair — the yāʼ hides in the bare form and returns with ال: القَاضِي، المُفْتِي.

Question 8

صَبُورٌ The word صَبُورٌ sits on the intensive فَعُول pattern — not merely patient once, but deeply and constantly patient.

Question 9

عَرَفَةُ The word عَرَفَةُ is an ordinary noun that accepts tanwīn like any other.

Question 10

جَوَابٌ The word جَوَابٌ means “question.”

SECTION DComplete the Sentence — أكمل الجملة5 marks · 1 each

Question 26

____ أَخْلَاقًا The Prophet ﷺ said that the nearest of you to him in seating (مَجْلِسًا — an اسم مكان) on the Day of Judgement will be ____ أَخْلَاقًا — “the best of you in character” (al-Tirmidhī, graded ḥasan-ṣaḥīḥ by al-Albānī).

Question 27

Question 28

Question 29
