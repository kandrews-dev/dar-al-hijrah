Ṣarf Companion · Verb Reference Hub

تَصْرِيفُ الأَفْعَال

Verb Reference — All 3 Books

Every verb taught across the Lugha track's three vocabulary books — 48 verbs in total — collected here in three short reference pages. Each verb is shown in three shapes: المَاضِي (past, "he ..."), المُضَارِع (present, "I ..."), and الأَمْر (command, "you ...!"). No transliteration, no full six-person paradigm — just enough to notice how Arabic verbs change shape, before the full Ṣarf track explains why.

Book 1 · 14 verbs

الكِتَابُ الأَوَّل

نَامَ، أَكَلَ، كَتَبَ، and 11 more — full scaffolding, no peek-toggle.

Book 2 · 7 verbs

الكِتَابُ الثَّانِي

رَكِبَ، سَارَ، وَصَلَ, and 4 more — first weak-letter verbs appear.

Book 3 · 27 verbs

الكِتَابُ الثَّالِث

صَبَرَ، أَحْسَنَ، حَافَظَ, and 24 more — seven augmented doors in use.
Why this exists: the vocab books teach each verb once, in whatever shape a sentence needed. The full Ṣarf track teaches the rules behind every shape, using its own paradigm verbs. These three pages sit in between — a plain, ungraded reference showing the verbs you already know, three shapes side by side, so the Ṣarf track's rules land on familiar ground instead of unfamiliar examples.
