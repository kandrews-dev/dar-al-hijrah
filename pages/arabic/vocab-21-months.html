الشهور الهجرية

The Hijri Months

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the two key words شَهْر (month) and سَنَة (year).
  • Name the twelve months of the Hijri year in order, from مُحَرَّم to ذُو الحِجَّة.
  • Know the four sacred months named in the tafsīr of al-Tawbah 9:36.
  • Know رمضان as the month of fasting and ذو الحجة as the month of ḥajj.

In Vocab 19 you learned the seven days of the week — now the calendar grows a step wider. A شَهْر (month) is measured by the moon, and twelve of them make a سَنَة (year). This is the calendar of the Muslims: the year of the هِجْرَة (the Prophet's ﷺ migration to al-Madīnah) is its year one, and every act of worship with a season — fasting, ḥajj, zakāh — runs on these twelve moons.

الشهور الاثنا عشر The Twelve Months
# الشهر Note
1مُحَرَّمMuḥarram — sacred; the year opens with it
2صَفَرṢafar — an ordinary month; no bad luck in it
3رَبِيع الأَوَّلRabīʿ al-Awwal — "the first spring"; the month the Prophet ﷺ arrived in al-Madīnah in the hijrah
4رَبِيع الآخِرRabīʿ al-Ākhir — "the last spring"
5جُمَادَى الأُولَىJumādā al-Ūlā — "the first Jumādā"
6جُمَادَى الآخِرَةJumādā al-Ākhirah — "the last Jumādā"
7رَجَبRajab — sacred; it stands alone between the others
8شَعْبَانShaʿbān — the doorway to Ramaḍān; the Prophet ﷺ fasted much in it
9رَمَضَانRamaḍān — the month of the Qur'an and fasting
10شَوَّالShawwāl — ʿĪd al-Fiṭr and the six fasts
11ذُو القَعْدَةDhū al-Qaʿdah — sacred; the first of the three sacred months in a row
12ذُو الحِجَّةDhū al-Ḥijjah — sacred; ḥajj and ʿĪd al-Aḍḥā

Say the list slowly and patterns appear: two months called رَبِيع, two called جُمَادَى — each pair marked أَوَّل/آخِر (first/last) or أُولَى/آخِرَة — and two beginning with ذُو ("possessor of"): ذُو القَعْدَة and ذُو الحِجَّة, the month that possesses the ḥajj. The names count nothing; unlike the days of the week, each month carries its own story.

مِنْهَا أَرْبَعَةٌ حُرُمٌ Four of Them Are Sacred

«إِنَّ عِدَّةَ الشُّهُورِ عِندَ اللَّهِ اثْنَا عَشَرَ شَهْرًا فِي كِتَابِ اللَّهِ يَوْمَ خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ مِنْهَا أَرْبَعَةٌ حُرُمٌ»

"Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve months, in the decree of Allah, the day He created the heavens and the earth — of them, four are sacred." (al-Tawbah 9:36)

The twelve months are not a human invention — Allah fixed them the very day He created the heavens and the earth. The scholars of tafsīr, such as al-Saʿdī, agree that the four sacred months are ذُو القَعْدَة، ذُو الحِجَّة، مُحَرَّم — three in a row — and رَجَب, standing alone. Sacred (حُرُم) means their sanctity is weightier: good deeds in them are greater, and wrongdoing in them is worse.

مَا هَذَا الشَّهْرُ؟ What month is this?
  • فِي السَّنَةِ اثْنَا عَشَرَ شَهْرًا. — In the year are twelve months.
  • نَصُومُ فِي رَمَضَانَ. — We fast in Ramaḍān.
  • الحَجُّ فِي ذِي الحِجَّةِ. — Ḥajj is in Dhū al-Ḥijjah.
  • مَا هَذَا الشَّهْرُ؟ — What month is this?

Notice فِي رَمَضَانَ — the name keeps a fatḥah even after فِي, because رَمَضَان is ممنوع من الصرف (barred from full inflection). And ذُو bends to ذِي after a preposition: فِي ذِي الحِجَّةِ.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Tonight, find out together what today's Hijri date is — the day, and especially the month — and let one person ask مَا هَذَا الشَّهْرُ؟ so everyone answers with its name. Then count the twelve months aloud in order, from مُحَرَّم to ذُو الحِجَّة, going around the room one month per person until the whole year has gone round without a mistake.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

مُحَرَّم · صَفَر · رَبِيع الأَوَّل · رَبِيع الآخِر · جُمَادَى الأُولَى · جُمَادَى الآخِرَة · رَجَب · شَعْبَان · رَمَضَان · شَوَّال · ذُو القَعْدَة · ذُو الحِجَّة

Recite the twelve months in order, out loud, until the whole year runs off your tongue without stopping — from مُحَرَّم to ذُو الحِجَّة.

Further reading — optional aids

These are optional supports only. The lesson is complete on-page. In everyday speech you will often hear شَهْر before the month-name — شَهْرُ رَمَضَانَ، شَهْرُ مُحَرَّمٍ — both forms are correct. The naming of the four sacred months follows the agreed tafsīr of al-Tawbah 9:36, as explained by al-Saʿdī and others, and any Hijri calendar app or printed calendar will let you check today's month against the list you memorized.
