وَقْتُ الفَرَاغِ وَالهِوَايَات

Hobbies & Free Time

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Know the common hobby words: قِرَاءَة · سِبَاحَة · رَسْم · رِيَاضَة.
  • Ask and answer مَا هِوَايَتُكَ؟.
  • Say what you do after school with أَلْعَبُ.
  • Speak about وَقْتُ الفَرَاغِ (free time) in simple Arabic.

What do you do when the work is done? This lesson gives you the words for hobbies and free time — reading, swimming, sport — so you can ask someone what they love to do and answer for yourself.

أَلْعَبُI play

Notice: قِرَاءَةٌ (reading) is built from the same root as أَقْرَأُ (I read) from Vocab 8 — the letters ق ر أ. Whole families of Arabic words grow from one root; the new Ṣarf track teaches exactly how.

ما هوايتك؟ What is your hobby?
  • مَا هِوَايَتُكَ؟ — What is your hobby?
  • هِوَايَتِي القِرَاءَةُ. — My hobby is reading.
  • أَلْعَبُ كُرَةَ القَدَمِ بَعْدَ المَدْرَسَةِ. — I play football after school.
  • أَسْبَحُ فِي الصَّيْفِ. — I swim in the summer.

You already know بَعْدَ (after) from Vocab 9 and الصَّيْف (summer) from Vocab 14 — watch how the old words keep working inside the new sentences.

تَمْرِين أسري — Family Practice

Go around the table and ask each person “مَا هِوَايَتُكَ؟” — everyone answers with “هِوَايَتِي …” and their own hobby. The question-and-answer pattern is the same one you learned with names in Vocab 1.

تَعْبِير

لَعِبٌ

المَعْنَى

اللَّعِبُ فِي وَقْتِهِ نَشَاطٌ مُبَاحٌ وَمُفِيدٌ، وَلَكِنَّ القُرْآنَ يُحَذِّرُ مِنْ أَنْ تُصْبِحَ الحَيَاةُ كُلُّهَا "لَعِبًا" بِلَا هَدَفٍ وَلَا غَايَةٍ جَادَّةٍ.

peek — English

Play, in its proper time, is a permissible and healthy activity — but the Qurʼān warns against letting the whole of life itself become mere "play," with no serious purpose or goal.

الصُّورَة

اللَّعِبُ هُوَ النَّشَاطُ الَّذِي لَا يُقْصَدُ بِهِ غَايَةٌ جَادَّةٌ، بَلِ المُتْعَةُ وَحْدَهَا. فَإِذَا وُصِفَتِ الحَيَاةُ الدُّنْيَا كُلُّهَا بِأَنَّهَا "لَعِبٌ"، فَهَذَا تَحْذِيرٌ مِنَ العَيْشِ بِلَا هَدَفٍ حَقِيقِيٍّ يَتَجَاوَزُ المُتْعَةَ العَابِرَةَ.

peek — English

Play is activity aimed at no serious end, only enjoyment. So when the whole of worldly life is described as "play," it is a warning against living with no real purpose beyond fleeting pleasure.

فِي القُرْآن

«وَمَا هَٰذِهِ الْحَيَاةُ الدُّنْيَا إِلَّا لَهْوٌ وَلَعِبٌ ۚ وَإِنَّ الدَّارَ الْآخِرَةَ لَهِيَ الْحَيَوَانُ»

Sūrat al-ʿAnkabūt 29:64 · سورة العنكبوت

peek — English

"And this worldly life is not but diversion and play. And indeed, the home of the Hereafter — that is the [real] life" — a reminder that pleasure has its place, but is not life's purpose.

عَائِلَةُ الجَذْر — ل ع ب
  • «وَمَا خَلَقْنَا السَّمَاءَ وَالْأَرْضَ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا لَاعِبِينَ» — creation itself was not made without purpose, "as if playing" (الأنبياء 21:16)
peek — English

Just as the heavens and earth were not created "playing" — without purpose — your own hobby time is best enjoyed in its place, never mistaken for the whole point of life.

إِذَا لَمْ يَسْتَقِمِ المَعْنَى الحَرْفِيّ، فَهُوَ تَعْبِير — اُنْظُرْ إِلَى الجَذْرِ وَالصُّورَةِ وَالسِّيَاق. If the literal meaning doesn't stand, it's a تعبير — look at the root, the image, and the context.
