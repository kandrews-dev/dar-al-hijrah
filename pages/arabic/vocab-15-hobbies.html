وقت الفراغ والهوايات

Hobbies & Free Time

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the common hobby words: قِرَاءَة · سِبَاحَة · رَسْم · رِيَاضَة.
  • Ask and answer مَا هِوَايَتُكَ؟.
  • Say what you do after school with أَلْعَبُ.
  • Speak about وَقْتُ الفَرَاغِ (free time) in simple Arabic.

What do you do when the work is done? This lesson gives you the words for hobbies and free time — reading, swimming, sport — so you can ask someone what they love to do and answer for yourself.

Notice: قِرَاءَةٌ (reading) is built from the same root as أَقْرَأُ (I read) from Vocab 8 — the letters ق ر أ. Whole families of Arabic words grow from one root; the new Ṣarf track teaches exactly how.

ما هوايتك؟ What is your hobby?
  • مَا هِوَايَتُكَ؟ — What is your hobby?
  • هِوَايَتِي القِرَاءَةُ. — My hobby is reading.
  • أَلْعَبُ كُرَةَ القَدَمِ بَعْدَ المَدْرَسَةِ. — I play football after school.
  • أَسْبَحُ فِي الصَّيْفِ. — I swim in the summer.

You already know بَعْدَ (after) from Vocab 9 and الصَّيْف (summer) from Vocab 14 — watch how the old words keep working inside the new sentences.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Go around the table and ask each person “مَا هِوَايَتُكَ؟” — everyone answers with “هِوَايَتِي …” and their own hobby. The question-and-answer pattern is the same one you learned with names in Vocab 1.
