الفعل المضارع

The Present Verb

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what الفعل المضارع (the present-future verb) is.
  • Memorise the four prefix letters أ · ن · ي · ت (حروف المضارعة).
  • Conjugate أَكْتُبُ · نَكْتُبُ · يَكْتُبُ · تَكْتُبُ.
  • Recognise that the vocab verbs you already use — أَدْرُسُ، أَذْهَبُ، أُصَلِّي — are all muḍāriʿ.
Where we are in the ṣarf track: Lesson 2 turned the past verb through its endings — the doer sat at the end of the word (كَتَبْـتُ). The present verb works the other way round: the doer is announced at the front, by one of four prefix letters. You have been using this verb since Vocab 4 without knowing its name.
ما هو المضارع؟ Part One — What is the Muḍāriʿ?

الفعل المضارع ما دلَّ على حدثٍ يقع الآن أو في المستقبل

The muḍāriʿ tells of an action happening now or in the future: يَكْتُبُ — he writes / he is writing. Where the past verb looked back at a finished deed, the muḍāriʿ is the verb of ongoing life — and of most of the Qur'an's descriptions of Allah's dealings with creation.

حروف المضارعة: أ ن ي ت Part Two — The Four Prefix Letters

Every muḍāriʿ begins with one of four letters — أ · ن · ي · ت — and the letter tells you the doer before you reach the root:

الحرف الفعل Meaning The doer it announces
أأَكْتُبُI writeأنا — I
ننَكْتُبُwe writeنحن — we
ييَكْتُبُhe writesهو — he · هم يَكْتُبُونَ they
تتَكْتُبُshe writes / you writeهي — she · أنتَ — you (m.) · أنتِ تَكْتُبِينَ you (f.)

The scholars gather the four letters in the word أَنَيْتُ — say it once and you own them all. Notice ت serves both "she" and "you"; the sentence around it tells you which.

أفعالك أنت Part Three — You Already Speak in Muḍāriʿ

Every "I …" verb from the vocab track is a muḍāriʿ beginning with أ. Read your own history as a ṣarf student:

أمثلة قرآنية Examples from the Qur'an

إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ

You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. (al-Fātiḥah 1:5)

نَعْبُدُ and نَسْتَعِينُ both open with the ن of "we" — two muḍāriʿ verbs you recite in every rakʿah. Worship is not a finished past act; the muḍāriʿ makes it ongoing, renewed prayer after prayer.

وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ وَأَنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ

And Allah knows, while you know not. (al-Baqarah 2:216)

يَعْلَمُ — the ي of "He" · تَعْلَمُونَ — the ت of "you (plural)". Same root ع ل م, two prefixes, two doers.
