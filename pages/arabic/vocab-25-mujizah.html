المُعْجِزَةُ الخَالِدَة

The Eternal Miracle

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Learn the vocabulary of iʿjāz — why the Qurʾān is called Allah's miracle.
  • Read three verified Qurʾānic proofs for why and how the Qurʾān was revealed and preserved.
  • Learn the grammar rule of kāna wa akhawātuhā — "kāna and her sisters."
  • Read a connected passage using both the new vocabulary and the new grammar together.

Welcome to Book 3, Unit 1. Every prophet before Muḥammad ﷺ was given a miracle tied to his own time and place — Mūsā's staff, Ṣāliḥ's she-camel — and when those prophets passed, so did the living miracle; only the story remained. The Qurʾān is different: it is الْمُعْجِزَةُ الخَالِدَة, the miracle that does not pass away. This unit asks: what makes it a miracle, and how do we know?

الكلمة English Note

تَحَدَّىhe challengedroot ح-د-ي — the Qurʾān تَحَدَّى all of mankind and jinn together (17:88)

ثَلَاثَةُ أَدِلَّة Three Verified Proofs

Why gradual revelation? To steady the Prophet's ﷺ heart against the hardship of the daʿwah:

وَقَالَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا لَوْلَا نُزِّلَ عَلَيْهِ الْقُرْآنُ جُمْلَةً وَاحِدَةً ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ لِنُثَبِّتَ بِهِ فُؤَادَكَ وَرَتَّلْنَاهُ تَرْتِيلًا

"And those who disbelieve say, 'Why was the Qurʾān not revealed to him all at once?' Thus [it is] that We may strengthen your heart thereby, and We have spaced it distinctly."

Sūrat al-Furqān 25:32 · سورة الفرقان

Why does it remain, when earlier miracles did not? Because Allah Himself guaranteed its preservation — no earlier scripture was given that promise:

إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ

"Indeed, it is We who sent down the message [i.e., the Qurʾān], and indeed, We will be its guardian."

Sūrat al-Ḥijr 15:9 · سورة الحجر

What is the standing challenge? That mankind and jinn together, helping one another, could never produce its equal:

قُل لَّئِنِ اجْتَمَعَتِ الْإِنسُ وَالْجِنُّ عَلَىٰ أَن يَأْتُوا بِمِثْلِ هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنِ لَا يَأْتُونَ بِمِثْلِهِ وَلَوْ كَانَ بَعْضُهُمْ لِبَعْضٍ ظَهِيرًا

"Say, 'If mankind and the jinn gathered in order to produce the like of this Qurʾān, they could not produce the like of it, even if they were to each other assistants.'"

Sūrat al-Isrāʾ 17:88 · سورة الإسراء

قَوَاعِدُ اللُّغَة Grammar — Kāna wa Akhawātuhā ("Kāna and Her Sisters")

Book 3 begins weaving grammar directly into every reading. Compare these two sentences:

القُرْآنُ مَحْفُوظٌ

The Qurʾān is preserved. (plain sentence)

كَانَ القُرْآنُ مَحْفُوظًا

The Qurʾān was preserved. (with kāna)

In the first sentence, مَحْفُوظٌ is the khabar and takes ḍammah (nominative). Once كَانَ enters, it takes the subject as its own ism (still nominative) but pushes the khabar into the accusative — مَحْفُوظًا.

كَانَ، صَارَ، أَصْبَحَ، أَضْحَى، أَمْسَى، ظَلَّ، لَيْسَ، مَا دَامَ، مَا زَالَ، مَا بَرِحَ — ten "abrogating verbs" (afʿāl nāsikhah) that enter a nominal sentence, keep the subject (mubtadaʾ) as their own ism in the nominative, and put the predicate (khabar) into the accusative as their khabar. Each carries its own shade of meaning: كَانَ places the khabar in the past; أَصْبَحَ — in the morning; مَا زَالَ — continuing without a break.

النَّصّ Mini-Passage — vocabulary and grammar together

كَانَ لِكُلِّ نَبِيٍّ مُعْجِزَةٌ، وَكَانَتْ مُعْجِزَةُ مُوسَى العَصَا. أَمَّا مُعْجِزَةُ مُحَمَّدٍ ﷺ فَظَلَّتِ القُرْآنَ الخَالِدَ. نَزَلَ القُرْآنُ مُنَجَّمًا لِيُثَبِّتَ قَلْبَ الرَّسُولِ ﷺ. تَحَدَّى القُرْآنُ الإِنْسَ وَالجِنَّ، وَمَا زَالَ التَّحَدِّي قَائِمًا إِلَى يَوْمِ القِيَامَةِ.

Read it aloud twice before peeking: Every prophet had a miracle, and Mūsā's miracle was the staff. As for Muḥammad's ﷺ miracle, it remained the eternal Qurʾān. The Qurʾān was revealed in stages to steady the Messenger's ﷺ heart. The Qurʾān challenged mankind and jinn, and the challenge still stands until the Day of Resurrection. Find all four sisters of kāna at work: كَانَ، كَانَتْ، ظَلَّتِ، مَا زَالَ — each pushing its khabar into the accusative.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Take turns building sentences with kāna's sisters about your own day: أَصْبَحْتُ نَشِيطًا (I became energetic this morning), ظَلَلْتُ صَائِمًا (I remained fasting). Then recall together: what makes the Qurʾān different from every miracle before it?

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ

"Indeed, it is We who sent down the message, and indeed, We will be its guardian." (al-Ḥijr 15:9) — memorize this āyah and reflect: no book in history has this guarantee except the Qurʾān.
