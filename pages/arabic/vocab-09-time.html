الوَقْتُ وَالرُّوتِين

Time & Daily Routine

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the common words for time and daily routine.
  • Use الآن · ثمَّ · قبلَ · بعدَ in simple sentences.
  • Say what you do in the morning, in the evening, and today.
  • Connect this lesson to the earlier words for school, prayer, and the city.

Once you know where you are and what you do, the next step is to talk about when you do it. Time words let your Arabic become more natural, because real speech is full of routines: what happens now, later, before, after, in the morning, and at night.

أَسْمَاءُ الوَقْت Words of Time
الكلمة English
الآنnow
ثُمَّthen / later
قَبْلَbefore
بَعْدَafter
صَبَاحًاin the morning
مَسَاءًin the evening
اليَوْمُ وَالأُسْبُوع The Day and the Week
  • اليوم — today / the day
  • الأُسْبُوع — the week
  • أذهبُ إلى المدرسةِ صباحًا — I go to school in the morning
  • أقرأُ بعدَ الصلاةِ — I read after prayer

These little time words make your Arabic feel more alive because you can describe your day in order.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Describe your day in order: أذهبُ إلى المدرسةِ صباحًا, ثمَّ أقرأُ بعدَ الصلاةِ, and end with أرقدُ مساءً. Even a small routine teaches you how to speak in time.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

الآن · ثمَّ · قبلَ · بعدَ · صباحًا · مساءً

Use these little time words every day. The more you attach them to your routine, the more natural your Arabic becomes.
