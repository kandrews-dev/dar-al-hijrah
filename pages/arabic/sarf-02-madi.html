تصريف الفعل الماضي

Conjugating the Past Verb

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what الفعل الماضي (the past verb) is.
  • Conjugate كَتَبَ through the main persons — he, she, you, I, we, they.
  • Recognise the endings ـتُ · ـتَ · ـتْ · ـنَا · ـُوا and what each one tells you.
  • Apply the same endings to any sound verb: دَرَسَ، ذَهَبَ، لَعِبَ.
Where we are in the ṣarf track: Lesson 1 gave you the root and the scale. Now we do the first real تصريف — taking one verb and turning it through its persons. The word تصريف comes from the same root as صرف itself: to turn a word through its forms. Master this one table and you have mastered the pattern for thousands of verbs.
الفعل الماضي Part One — The Past Verb

الفعل الماضي ما دلَّ على حدثٍ وقع وانقضى

The past verb tells of an action that happened and finished: كَتَبَ — he wrote. In Arabic you do not need a separate word for "he", "I" or "we": the ending of the verb carries the doer inside it. Change the ending, and you change who did it.

تصريف «كَتَبَ» Part Two — Turning كَتَبَ Through Its Persons
الضمير الفعل Meaning Ending
هُوَكَتَبَhe wrote—
هِيَكَتَبَتْshe wroteـَتْ
هُمْكَتَبُواthey (m.) wroteـُوا
أَنْتَكَتَبْتَyou (m.) wroteـْتَ
أَنْتِكَتَبْتِyou (f.) wroteـْتِ
أَنَاكَتَبْتُI wroteـْتُ
نَحْنُكَتَبْنَاwe wroteـْنَا

There are more forms (the duals, the feminine plurals — هُمَا كَتَبَا، هُنَّ كَتَبْنَ), and they follow the same logic; we will complete the full fourteen in a later lesson. These seven are the ones you will meet constantly.

الطريقة تعمل مع كل فعل Part Three — The Same Mould Fits Every Sound Verb

Swap the root, keep the endings. You already know these verbs from the vocab track in their "I" forms — أَدْرُسُ، أَذْهَبُ، أَلْعَبُ. Here are their past-tense families:

الفعل he … I … we …
دَرَسَhe studiedدَرَسْتُدَرَسْنَا
ذَهَبَhe wentذَهَبْتُذَهَبْنَا
لَعِبَhe playedلَعِبْتُلَعِبْنَا
أمثلة قرآنية Examples from the Qur'an

كَتَبَ رَبُّكُمْ عَلَىٰ نَفْسِهِ الرَّحْمَةَ

Your Lord has decreed mercy upon Himself. (al-Anʿām 6:54)

كَتَبَ — the very verb of our table, in the "he" form: it happened and stands decreed.

إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ

Indeed, it is We who sent down the Reminder, and indeed, We are its Guardian. (al-Ḥijr 15:9)

نَزَّلْنَا ends in the ـْنَا you just learned — the "We" of majesty. And حَافِظُونَ is on the doer pattern فَاعِل from Lesson 1. The Qur'an is now showing you your own lessons.

Memorize — احفظ

كَتَبَ · كَتَبَتْ · كَتَبُوا · كَتَبْتَ · كَتَبْتِ · كَتَبْتُ · كَتَبْنَا

Recite the seven forms aloud until they run like water. Then do the same with دَرَسَ and ذَهَبَ — same endings, new root.
