العَمَلُ وَالمِهَن

Work & Professions

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the basic words for work, jobs, and professions.
  • Ask and answer أَيْنَ تَعْمَل؟ and أعمل في....
  • Use words like طبيب · معلم · مهندس · شركة · سوق · مكتب.
  • Connect the workplace vocabulary to your travel words and daily routine.

This lesson helps you speak about what people do and where they work. In real Arabic, asking about someone’s profession and the place they go every day is one of the most useful conversations.

الكلمة English

مُعَلِّمٌteacher
شَرِكَةٌcompany
سُوقٌmarket
أَيْنَ تَعْمَل؟ Where do you work?
  • أين تعملُ؟ — Where do you work?
  • أعملُ في المدرسةِ. — I work at the school.
  • هو طبيبٌ في المستشفى. — He is a doctor at the hospital.
  • تعملُ المعلمةُ في الشركةِ. — The teacher works at the company.

These phrases connect the job words with real places. They also prepare you for using the travel vocabulary from the previous lesson.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Ask a family member “أين تعملُ؟” and answer with one of the new job words, for example أعملُ في المدرسةِ or هو طبيبٌ في الشركةِ. Practicing this question turns vocabulary into a real conversation.
