السُّنَّةُ النَّبَوِيَّة

The Prophetic Sunnah

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Define the Sunnah — sayings, actions, and tacit approvals of the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Know why the Sunnah is itself revelation (waḥy), not the Prophet's ﷺ own opinion.
  • Learn why obeying the Sunnah is obligatory as the second source of Islamic law.
  • Know how seriously the early Muslims guarded the accuracy of ḥadīth transmission.

This unit connects directly to what you already study in the Ḥadīth tab. Here, the same foundational ideas — why the Sunnah matters, why it must be obeyed, how it was preserved — are read and practiced in connected Arabic.

تَعْرِيفُ السُّنَّة Defining the Sunnah — and Why It Is Revelation

The Sunnah is the sayings, actions, and tacit approvals of the Messenger ﷺ. It came to explain the Qurʾān:

وَأَنزَلْنَا إِلَيْكَ الذِّكْرَ لِتُبَيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ مَا نُزِّلَ إِلَيْهِمْ

"And We revealed to you the message that you may make clear to people what was sent down to them."

Sūrat al-Naḥl 16:44 · سورة النحل

And the Sunnah is not the Prophet's ﷺ own opinion — it is waḥy, revelation:

وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ الْهَوَىٰ ۝ إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحْيٌ يُوحَىٰ

"Nor does he speak from [his own] inclination. It is not but a revelation revealed."

Sūrat al-Najm 53:3–4 · سورة النجم

وُجُوبُ الاتِّبَاع Why Obedience Is Obligatory

The Sunnah is the second source of Islamic legislation, after the Qurʾān. To follow it is obligatory; to oppose it is forbidden — the Muslims are unanimous on this:

وَمَا آتَاكُمُ الرَّسُولُ فَخُذُوهُ وَمَا نَهَاكُمْ عَنْهُ فَانتَهُوا

"And whatever the Messenger has given you — take it; and what he has forbidden you — refrain from."

Sūrat al-Ḥashr 59:7 · سورة الحشر

حِفْظُ السُّنَّة How the Sunnah Was Guarded

The early Muslims began tadwīn (written compilation) of the Sunnah early, and became the most rigorous of all nations in verifying what they wrote and transmitted. Fabricating a ḥadīth against the Prophet ﷺ was treated as one of the gravest of sins:

مَنْ كَذَبَ عَلَيَّ مُتَعَمِّدًا فَلْيَتَبَوَّأْ مَقْعَدَهُ مِنَ النَّارِ

"Whoever lies about me deliberately, let him take his seat in the Fire."

al-Bukhārī & Muslim

This is exactly why the site's own Ḥadīth lessons always name who narrated a ḥadīth and who graded it authentic — the same standard the early Muslims themselves insisted upon.

النَّصّ Mini-Passage

السُّنَّةُ النَّبَوِيَّةُ وَحْيٌ مِنَ اللَّهِ، وَلَيْسَتْ رَأْيًا مِنَ الرَّسُولِ ﷺ. هِيَ المَصْدَرُ الثَّانِي بَعْدَ القُرْآنِ، فَاتِّبَاعُهَا وَاجِبٌ. حَرَصَ المُسْلِمُونَ عَلَى التَّثَبُّتِ فِي نَقْلِ كُلِّ حَدِيثٍ. وَمَنْ كَذَبَ عَلَى الرَّسُولِ ﷺ مُتَعَمِّدًا، فَقَدِ ارْتَكَبَ ذَنْبًا عَظِيمًا.

Read it aloud twice, then check your understanding.

peek — English translationThe Prophetic Sunnah is revelation from Allah, not an opinion from the Messenger ﷺ. It is the second source after the Qurʾān, so following it is obligatory. The Muslims were keen to verify carefully every ḥadīth they transmitted. And whoever lies about the Messenger ﷺ deliberately has committed a tremendous sin.
