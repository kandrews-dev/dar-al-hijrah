الطقس والفصول

Weather & Seasons

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the main weather words: الطَّقْس · الشَّمْس · المَطَر · الرِّيح.
  • Ask and answer كَيْفَ الطَّقْسُ اليَوْمَ؟.
  • Describe the weather with حَارّ and بَارِد.
  • Name the four seasons in Arabic.

Weather is one of the easiest conversations to begin in any language. This lesson gives you the words for the sky above you — sun, rain, wind — and the four seasons, so you can describe any day in simple, correct Arabic.

كلمات الطقس Weather Words
الكلمة English
طَقْسٌweather
شَمْسٌsun
مَطَرٌrain
رِيحٌwind
سَحَابٌclouds
سَمَاءٌsky
حَارٌّhot
بَارِدٌcold
الفصول الأربعة The Four Seasons
الفصل English
الصَّيْفُsummer
الشِّتَاءُwinter
الرَّبِيعُspring
الخَرِيفُautumn

Notice: فَصْلٌ means a season here — but you already met it in Vocab 8 meaning a classroom. One word, two meanings; the sentence around it tells you which one is intended.

كيف الطقس اليوم؟ How is the weather today?
  • كَيْفَ الطَّقْسُ اليَوْمَ؟ — How is the weather today?
  • الطَّقْسُ حَارٌّ فِي الصَّيْفِ. — The weather is hot in summer.
  • يَنْزِلُ المَطَرُ فِي الشِّتَاءِ. — The rain comes down in winter.
  • السَّمَاءُ صَافِيَةٌ. — The sky is clear.

These sentences follow the mubtadaʾ–khabar pattern you know from the naḥw track: الطَّقْسُ حَارٌّ is simply "the weather (is) hot".

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Each morning this week, ask “كَيْفَ الطَّقْسُ اليَوْمَ؟” and answer by looking outside: “الطَّقْسُ حَارٌّ” or “الطَّقْسُ بَارِدٌ”. A one-line habit that makes the words permanent.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

طَقْسٌ · شَمْسٌ · مَطَرٌ · رِيحٌ · حَارٌّ · بَارِدٌ

Remember these weather words, and the four seasons: الصَّيْف · الشِّتَاء · الرَّبِيع · الخَرِيف.
