الحيوانات

Animals

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the common animal words, from جَمَلٌ (camel) to سَمَكٌ (fish).
  • Name the sūrahs of the Qur'an that carry animal names.
  • Say “What is this? This is a…” — مَا هَذَا؟ هَذَا جَمَلٌ. — with the animals.
  • See Allah's provision for every creature that moves on the earth.

Allah filled the earth with creatures — some carry loads across deserts, some roar in the forest, and some are so small you could miss them, yet whole sūrahs of the Qur'an are named after them. Eight words in this lesson and you can point at almost any animal — on a walk, in a picture book, at the zoo — and name it in Arabic.

حيوانات في القرآن The Qur'an's Animals

لطيفة — Whole sūrahs carry animal names

Animals matter so much in the Qur'an that entire sūrahs are named after them: البَقَرَة (2, the Cow) · النَّحْل (16, the Bee) · النَّمْل (27, the Ant) · العَنْكَبُوت (29, the Spider) · الفِيل (105, the Elephant). Three of them — the cow, the bee, and the ant — are words in your table above. And you have already read one of these sūrahs yourself: سورة الفيل on the Quran tab, the story of the army with the elephant.

«وَمَا مِن دَابَّةٍ فِي الْأَرْضِ إِلَّا عَلَى اللَّهِ رِزْقُهَا»

And there is no creature moving on the earth except that its provision is upon Allah.

سورة هود — Hūd 11:6

Every creature in your table — the camel in the desert, the fish in the sea, the ant under a stone — is fed by Allah. And look at the word رِزْق (provision): it is the very same word from Nawawī Hadith 4, one of the four things written for you before you were born. The rizq of the ant and your own rizq come from the same Lord.

مَا هَذَا؟ What is this?
  • مَا هَذَا؟ — What is this?
  • هَذَا جَمَلٌ. — This is a camel.
  • هَذِهِ بَقَرَةٌ. — This is a cow — note هَذِهِ for feminine.
  • النَّحْلَةُ تَصْنَعُ العَسَلَ. — The bee makes the honey — عَسَل from Vocab 5.

The rule is simple: point at جَمَلٌ، أَسَدٌ، حِصَانٌ، طَائِرٌ or سَمَكٌ with هَذَا, but at the ة-ending words — بَقَرَةٌ، نَحْلَةٌ، نَمْلَةٌ — with هَذِهِ.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

On any walk, or with any picture book, play مَا هَذَا؟ — one person points at an animal and asks, the other answers: هَذَا طَائِرٌ! هَذِهِ نَحْلَةٌ! Then the bonus round: which of the animals you spotted have a sūrah named after them? The cow, the bee and the ant score extra — and whoever remembers سورة الفيل can tell its story on the way home.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

جَمَلٌ · أَسَدٌ · حِصَانٌ · بَقَرَةٌ · نَحْلَةٌ · نَمْلَةٌ · طَائِرٌ · سَمَكٌ

Recite the eight animals out loud until they come without stopping — camel, lion, horse, cow, bee, ant, bird, fish.
