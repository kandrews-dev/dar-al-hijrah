أَفْعَالُ الكِتَابِ الثَّالِث

Book 3 — Verb Reference

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • See the 27 new verbs Book 3 adds — by far the richest verb set of the three books.
  • Meet several augmented doors already (door II, III, IV, V, VI, VIII, X) — the full names come later in Ṣarf Lesson 7, but you are already using them.
  • Keep the same habit: الماضي، المضارع، الأمر, side by side, for every verb you meet.
  • Arabic and English only — no transliteration.

Book 3 (Vocab 25-40) is where the Lugha track turns toward real hadith, real proverbs, and real family life — and its vocabulary turns from mostly nouns to mostly verbs. Twenty-seven new verbs appear, several already built on augmented patterns (أَحْسَنَ، تَحَمَّلَ، اِعْتَرَفَ) that Book 1-2 never used. This page collects all twenty-seven, in the order their lessons taught them.

Notice the augmented shapes: أَتْقَنَ، أَحْسَنَ، أَخْلَفَ (door IV, add أ) · سَبَّبَ، عَظَّمَ-type doubling (door II) · عَاتَبَ، حَافَظَ (door III, add ا after the first letter) · تَحَمَّلَ، تَكَيَّفَ، تَبَرَّأَ (door V, add تَ) · تَحَدَّى، تَرَاوَحَ (door VI) · اِعْتَرَفَ (door VIII, add تْ inside) · اِسْتَعْبَدَ (door X, add اسْتَ). The full names and rules for all ten doors are in Ṣarf Lesson 7 and Lesson 11.

Memorize — اِحْفَظْ

صَبَرَ ← أَصْبِرُ ← اِصْبِرْ

One root, three shapes: he was patient (past) → I am patient (present) → be patient! (command). This is the same صَبَرَ the Qurʼān commands in وَاصْبِرْ — you already know how to use it.
