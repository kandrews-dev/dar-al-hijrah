أيام الأسبوع

Days of the Week

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Name the seven days of the week from الأَحَد to السَّبْت.
  • See how five of the day-names grow straight out of the numbers from Vocab 16.
  • Ask and answer مَا اليَوْمُ؟ (What day is it?).
  • Know the virtue of يوم الجمعة (Friday) and its Sunnahs.

You already counted from وَاحِد (one) to خَمْسَة (five) in Vocab 16 — and here is the reward: the Arabic week is built out of those very numbers. Learn seven words and you can name every day, ask what day it is, and greet the best day of all — يوم الجمعة (Friday) — the way the Prophet ﷺ taught.

أيام الأسبوع The Seven Days
اليوم English Root idea
الأَحَدSundayfrom وَاحِد (one) — day one
الاِثْنَيْنMondayfrom اِثْنَان (two) — day two
الثُّلَاثَاءTuesdayfrom ثَلَاثَة (three)
الأَرْبِعَاءWednesdayfrom أَرْبَعَة (four)
الخَمِيسThursdayfrom خَمْسَة (five)
الجُمُعَةFridayfrom الجَمْع — the gathering
السَّبْتSaturdayits own word — the day of rest

Look closely at that third column — the numbers from Vocab 16 are hiding inside the week! Five of the seven days are simply the numbers one to five dressed up as names: الأَحَد is day one, الاِثْنَيْن day two, and so on up to الخَمِيس, day five. You already knew most of this lesson before you opened it. The two exceptions carry meanings of their own: الجُمُعَة is the day the Muslims gather (الجَمْع) for the Friday prayer, and السَّبْت stands alone as the day of rest.

مَا اليَوْمُ؟ What day is it?
  • مَا اليَوْمُ؟ — What day is it?
  • اليَوْمُ الجُمُعَةُ. — Today is Friday.
  • نَذْهَبُ إِلَى المَسْجِدِ يَوْمَ الجُمُعَةِ. — We go to the mosque on Friday.
  • غَدًا يَوْمُ السَّبْتِ. — Tomorrow is Saturday.

Notice the fatḥah in يَوْمَ الجُمُعَةِ — it is manṣūb as a ظرف زمان (adverb of time), exactly as naḥw L9 taught.

سنة — The best day of the week

«خَيْرُ يَوْمٍ طَلَعَتْ عَلَيْهِ الشَّمْسُ يَوْمُ الْجُمُعَةِ»

The Prophet ﷺ said: “The best day upon which the sun has risen is Friday” (Muslim).

Because الجُمُعَة is the best of days, it has its own Sunnahs: perform الغُسْل (the full bath) before the prayer, go to the mosque early, and increase in الصلاة على النبي ﷺ (sending ṣalāh upon the Prophet ﷺ) throughout the day. One vocabulary word — and a whole weekly act of worship attached to it.

تمرين أسري — Family Practice

Each morning, one person asks مَا اليَوْمُ؟ and everyone answers with the right day: اليَوْمُ الثُّلَاثَاءُ. Then on Thursday night, plan the Friday Sunnahs together — who will make الغُسْل first, when you will leave early for the mosque, and remind each other to send ṣalāh upon the Prophet ﷺ throughout the day.

Daily Commitment — التزام يومي

الأَحَد · الاِثْنَيْن · الثُّلَاثَاء · الأَرْبِعَاء · الخَمِيس · الجُمُعَة · السَّبْت

Recite the seven days in order, out loud, until the week runs off your tongue without stopping — from الأَحَد to السَّبْت.

Further reading — optional aids

These are optional supports only. The lesson is complete on-page. In everyday speech you will often hear يَوْم before the day-name — يَوْمُ الأَحَدِ، يَوْمُ الاِثْنَيْنِ — both forms are correct, and the ظرف زمان lesson in the naḥw track explains why يَوْمَ takes a fatḥah when it answers “when?”.
