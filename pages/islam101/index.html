أساسيات الإسلام

Islam 101 — The Foundations

The starting point for every student. Here we learn the Three Fundamental Principles every person will be asked about: Who is your Lord? What is your religion? Who is your Prophet? A clear, gentle foundation that the other tabs — Aqeedah, Fiqh, and the rest — build upon. Based on al-Uṣūl al-Thalātha and the Quran and Sunnah.
