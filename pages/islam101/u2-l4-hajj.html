الحج

The Pilgrimage — the journey to the House

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know the conditions for الحج to be obligatory.
  • Know the four أركان الحج — without which it is not valid.
  • Walk through the days of الحج in order.
  • Understand the difference between الحج and العمرة.
  • Hear the reward Allah promised the accepted pilgrim.

The fifth and final pillar — and the one that takes a lifetime to prepare for. Once in your life, if you are able, you travel to Allah's House in مكة.

الوجوب والشروط Part 1 — When ḥajj is obligatory

وَلِلَّهِ عَلَى النَّاسِ حِجُّ الْبَيْتِ مَنِ اسْتَطَاعَ إِلَيْهِ سَبِيلًا

"To Allah is owed by mankind the pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find a way to it."

آل عمران 3:97

الحج is obligatory once in a lifetime on every Muslim who meets these conditions:

  • Islam — non-Muslims do not perform it.
  • Sanity (العقل) — and reaching البلوغ (puberty).
  • Freedom — the slave has no obligation.
  • Ability (الاستطاعة) — bodily health to bear the journey, and the wealth for travel beyond your family's needs, debts paid.
  • For a woman — a مَحْرم (a man she cannot marry — husband, father, brother, son…) who travels with her, by the Prophet's ﷺ explicit command.

If you are not yet able, you intend it for the day Allah enables you. Anyone who delays it after becoming able sins; if he dies before fulfilling it, الحج is paid for from his estate.

أركان الحج الأربعة Part 2 — The four pillars of ḥajj

Without any of these four, الحج is not valid:

١. الإحرام1. al-Iḥrām — entering the state of pilgrimage

Forming the intention for ḥajj at the appointed station (الميقات) — the men wear two unsewn white cloths, the women in modest dress. From this moment certain things become forbidden (perfume, cutting hair/nails, intimacy, hunting…).

٢. الوقوف بعرفة2. Wuqūf bi-ʿArafah — standing at ʿArafah

Being present on the plain of عرفة on the 9th of Dhū al-Ḥijjah, even for a moment, between noon and dawn of the 10th. The Prophet ﷺ said: "الحج عرفة" — "The ḥajj is ʿArafah." Miss it, miss the ḥajj.

٣. طواف الإفاضة3. Ṭawāf al-Ifāḍah — the pouring-out circumambulation

Seven circuits around the الكعبة — after returning from منى on the 10th. Counter-clockwise, beginning at the Black Stone, making duʿāʾ throughout.

٤. السعي بين الصفا والمروة4. al-Saʿy — walking between al-Ṣafā & al-Marwah

Seven trips between the two hills الصفا and المروة — retracing the steps of هاجر (mother of Ismāʿīl), who ran between them searching for water for her thirsty child. From this came the well of زمزم.

أنساك الحج الثلاثة Part 3 — The three types of ḥajj
NameالنسكWhat you do
TamattuʿالتمتُّعʿUmrah first, then exit ihram, then a new ihram for ḥajj on 8th Dhū al-Ḥijjah. Requires a sacrificial animal. (Preferred for foreigners by many of the Salaf.)
IfrādالإفرادḤajj alone, no ʿumrah with it. No sacrifice required.
QirānالقِرانʿUmrah and ḥajj together in one ihram. Requires a sacrifice.
أيام الحج Part 4 — The days of ḥajj — in order
  • 8th Dhū al-Ḥijjah · يوم التروية — enter ihram for ḥajj, go to مِنى; pray the five (shortened) and sleep there.
  • 9th · يوم عرفة — the great day. Stand on عرفة making duʿāʾ until sunset. Then to مزدلفة for the night; combine المغرب and العشاء; gather pebbles.
  • 10th · يوم النحر · ʿEid al-Aḍḥā — stone the great جمرة العقبة · sacrifice (if doing tamattuʿ/qirān) · shave or cut the hair · ṭawāf al-ifāḍah and saʿy.
  • 11th, 12th (and 13th) · أيام التشريق — in Minā, stoning all three jamarāt each afternoon. Many pilgrims leave after the 12th; staying for the 13th is more complete.
  • Farewell ṭawāf · طواف الوداع — last ṭawāf before leaving Makkah. The Prophet ﷺ commanded: "None of you may leave until his last meeting is with the House."
العمرة Part 5 — ʿUmrah — the lesser visit

العمرة is a shorter visit — possible at any time of the year — with just ihram, ṭawāf, saʿy, and shaving/cutting. The Prophet ﷺ said:

العُمْرَةُ إِلَى العُمْرَةِ كَفَّارَةٌ لِمَا بَيْنَهُمَا، وَالحَجُّ المَبْرُورُ لَيْسَ لَهُ جَزَاءٌ إِلَّا الجَنَّةُ

"An ʿumrah to the next is an expiation for what is between them — and the accepted ḥajj has no reward except Paradise."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

فضل الحج Part 6 — The reward of an accepted ḥajj

مَنْ حَجَّ لِلَّهِ فَلَمْ يَرْفُثْ وَلَمْ يَفْسُقْ رَجَعَ كَيَوْمِ وَلَدَتْهُ أُمُّهُ

"Whoever performs ḥajj for the sake of Allah, and commits no obscenity (rafath) and no disobedience (fusūq) — returns like the day his mother gave birth to him."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

A fresh start. Returning home with the sins of a lifetime forgiven. This is why pilgrims weep so much in عرفة — they know what Allah has prepared for those who answer His call.

للأسرة — Planning the journey

From this lesson on, الحج sits in the family's intentions. Save for it. Talk about it. Learn its rites slowly with the children — through pictures, videos, stories from those who have gone. The intention itself is rewarded. And on the 9th of Dhū al-Ḥijjah, even those not on ḥajj fast that day if able — the Prophet ﷺ said it expiates the sins of the year before and the year after. (Muslim)

احفظ التلبية — Memorize the call of the pilgrim

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

"Here I am, O Allah, here I am — here I am, no partner have You, here I am. Indeed all praise, all favour, and all dominion belong to You — no partner have You." (al-Bukhārī & Muslim) Teach the children this التلبية — it is the call of every pilgrim from every land, in one voice.
