Unit 3 · Lesson 2

آداب الكلام

Manners of Speech

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Memorize the great rule: "…let him speak good or stay silent."
  • Know what الغِيبة and النميمة are — and the verse that defined them.
  • Know the sins of the tongue and the saving habits.
  • Make the home a place where only good is spoken.

The Prophet ﷺ said most of what casts people into the Fire is the harvest of their tongues. (al-Tirmidhī · authentic) The tongue is small; its account is heavy.

القاعدة الكبرىPart 1 — The great rule

مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ فَلْيَقُلْ خَيْرًا أَوْ لِيَصْمُتْ

"Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day — let him speak good or stay silent."

رواه البخاري ومسلم

A two-option rule. Before speaking, ask: is this good? If not — الصمت. ʿUmar said: "Whoever speaks much errs much; whoever errs much sins much; whoever sins much — the Fire is more fitting for him."

الغِيبةPart 2 — al-Ghībah (backbiting)

أَتَدْرُونَ مَا الْغِيبَةُ؟ قَالُوا: اللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَعْلَمُ. قَالَ: ذِكْرُكَ أَخَاكَ بِمَا يَكْرَهُ. قِيلَ: أَفَرَأَيْتَ إِنْ كَانَ فِي أَخِي مَا أَقُولُ؟ قَالَ: إِنْ كَانَ فِيهِ مَا تَقُولُ فَقَدِ اغْتَبْتَهُ، وَإِنْ لَمْ يَكُنْ فِيهِ فَقَدْ بَهَتَّهُ

"Do you know what backbiting is?" They said: Allah and His Messenger know best. He said: "Mentioning your brother with what he dislikes." It was said: What if what I said is true of him? He said: "If it is true of him, you have backbitten him. If it is not — you have slandered him."

رواه مسلم

وَلَا يَغْتَب بَّعْضُكُم بَعْضًا ۚ أَيُحِبُّ أَحَدُكُمْ أَن يَأْكُلَ لَحْمَ أَخِيهِ مَيْتًا فَكَرِهْتُمُوهُ

"And let none of you backbite one another — would any of you like to eat the flesh of his dead brother? You would hate it!"

الحجرات 49:12

النميمةPart 3 — al-Namīmah (carrying tales)

النميمة is carrying speech between people to corrupt the relationship — telling so-and-so what someone said about him to stir trouble. The Prophet ﷺ said:

لَا يَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ نَمَّامٌ

"The one who carries tales will not enter Paradise."

رواه البخاري ومسلم

عادات تحفظ اللسانPart 4 — Saving habits
  • Truthfulness always — even in jest. The Prophet ﷺ warned: "Woe to the one who lies to make people laugh — woe to him, woe to him." (Abū Dāwūd · ḥasan)
  • Kind word = ṣadaqah. "The good word is charity." (Bukhārī & Muslim)
  • Spread peace — say السلام عليكم to those you know and those you don't. The Prophet ﷺ made it a sign of īmān.
  • Listen more than you speak. Allah gave you two ears and one tongue.
  • Don't argue, even when right — the Prophet ﷺ guaranteed a house at the edge of Paradise for the one who leaves arguing. (Abū Dāwūd · ḥasan)
  • End the gathering with كفّارة المجلس: «سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، أَسْتَغْفِرُكَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْكَ» — Allah forgives what was said carelessly.

للأسرة — A rule for the home

In our home: we do not speak about people who are not present, except with good. If a sibling, friend, or relative comes up in conversation — if what we'd say would upset them to hear, we don't say it. Teach the children to say: "Let's not talk about people. Let's talk about ideas."

احفظ — Memorize

مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ فَلْيَقُلْ خَيْرًا أَوْ لِيَصْمُتْ

The simplest test before any word leaves your mouth: is it good? If not — silence is better.
