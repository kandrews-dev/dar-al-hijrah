الصلاة

The Prayer — the link with Allah

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know the names and times of the five daily صلوات.
  • Understand the weight of the prayer — and what is lost without it.
  • Memorize the first حديث on the prayer's account on the Day of Judgement.
  • Learn the children's plan: ages 7 and 10, gently and firmly.

The صلاة is the second pillar — and the one Allah and His Messenger ﷺ gave the most weight to after the شهادتان. It is the believer's five appointments with Allah every day, no other deed touches it.

الصلوات الخمس وأوقاتها Part 1 — The five prayers and their times
#الصلاةTimeRakʿahs
1الفجرDawn — before sunrise2
2الظُّهرJust after the sun passes its zenith (midday)4
3العصرMid-afternoon, when shadows equal objects4
4المغربJust after sunset, until twilight ends3
5العشاءFrom the end of twilight until midnight (or fajr at latest)4

That's 17 rakʿahs of obligatory فريضة every day — the believer's spine. Each prayer must be performed in its time; missing it without excuse is one of the gravest of sins.

منزلتها في الدين Part 2 — Its place in the religion

The صلاة is unique:

  • It is the only pillar prescribed in the heavens — on the night of الإسراء والمعراج, the Prophet ﷺ received the order for it directly from his Lord.
  • It is the last of the religion to be lost — when it is lost, nothing remains.
  • It is the first matter to be judged on the Day of Resurrection — if it is sound, the rest of the deeds are sound.
  • It is the line between Islam and kufr — the Prophet ﷺ said: "Between a man and shirk and kufr is leaving the prayer." (Muslim)

إِنَّ أَوَّلَ مَا يُحَاسَبُ بِهِ الْعَبْدُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ مِنْ عَمَلِهِ صَلَاتُهُ، فَإِنْ صَلُحَتْ فَقَدْ أَفْلَحَ وَأَنْجَحَ، وَإِنْ فَسَدَتْ فَقَدْ خَابَ وَخَسِرَ

"The first of a servant's deeds to be reckoned on the Day of Resurrection is his prayer — if it is sound, he has succeeded and prospered; if it is corrupt, he has failed and lost."

رواه الترمذي والنسائي · صحيح — al-Tirmidhī, al-Nasāʾī · authentic

من ثمار الصلاة Part 3 — Some of its fruits

إِنَّ الصَّلَاةَ تَنْهَىٰ عَنِ الْفَحْشَاءِ وَالْمُنكَرِ

"Indeed the prayer keeps one away from obscenity and from wickedness."

العنكبوت 29:45

أَرَأَيْتُمْ لَوْ أَنَّ نَهَرًا بِبَابِ أَحَدِكُمْ يَغْتَسِلُ فِيهِ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ خَمْسَ مَرَّاتٍ، هَلْ يَبْقَى مِنْ دَرَنِهِ شَيْءٌ؟ قَالُوا: لَا يَبْقَى مِنْ دَرَنِهِ شَيْءٌ. قَالَ: فَذَلِكَ مَثَلُ الصَّلَوَاتِ الْخَمْسِ، يَمْحُو اللهُ بِهِنَّ الْخَطَايَا

"What do you think — if there were a river at the door of one of you, and he bathed in it five times every day, would any dirt remain on him?" They said: "Nothing." He ﷺ said: "Such is the example of the five prayers — by them Allah wipes away sins."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

حكم تارك الصلاة Part 4 — The ruling on the one who abandons it

The Companions held a near-consensus that whoever abandons the prayer denying its obligation has left Islam. Whoever abandons it out of laziness while still believing in it has fallen into one of the gravest sins, and the imams differed: some considered it كفر أكبر (Imam Aḥmad), others a major sin short of kufr (Mālik & Shāfiʿī). Either way the matter is extreme — and a Muslim should never let himself slide there.

فَخَلَفَ مِن بَعْدِهِمْ خَلْفٌ أَضَاعُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَاتَّبَعُوا الشَّهَوَاتِ ۖ فَسَوْفَ يَلْقَوْنَ غَيًّا

"Then came after them successors who lost the prayer and followed desires — they will meet Ghayy (a valley in Hell)."

مريم 19:59

تعليم الأبناء الصلاة Part 5 — Teaching the children

مُرُوا أَوْلَادَكُمْ بِالصَّلَاةِ وَهُمْ أَبْنَاءُ سَبْعِ سِنِينَ، وَاضْرِبُوهُمْ عَلَيْهَا وَهُمْ أَبْنَاءُ عَشْرٍ، وَفَرِّقُوا بَيْنَهُمْ فِي الْمَضَاجِعِ

"Order your children with the prayer when they are seven years old, and discipline them for it when they are ten — and separate them in their sleeping places."

رواه أبو داود · صحيح — Abū Dāwūd · authentic

A clear, gentle plan from the Prophet ﷺ. At seven, you teach the child the prayer and order them with it — gently, beautifully, with encouragement. At ten, you discipline them for missing it — without harm, without anger, but firmly. By البلوغ (puberty), the habit is built. Pray with your children; let them stand beside you. They learn what they see.

قاعدة للأسرة — A family rule

In our home: the prayer comes first. We stop what we are doing when the أذان is called. We do not delay it for work, food, friends, or screens. The Prophet ﷺ — at the end of his life, in his final illness — would still ask: "Have the people prayed?" If he would not skip a prayer, neither will we.
