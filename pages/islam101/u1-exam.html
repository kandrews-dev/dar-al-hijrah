Instructions: Answer all questions. This exam covers all of Islam 101 Unit 1 — the Three Fundamental Principles (Lessons 0–4): seeking knowledge, knowing your Lord, your religion, your Prophet ﷺ, and the two testimonies. Bismillāh.

SECTION ATrue or False — صحيح أم خاطئ10 marks · 1 each

Question 1

ذكر الله في سورة العصر أربعة أمور للنجاة من الخسران. Sūrat al-ʿAṣr mentions four matters by which a person is saved from loss.

Question 2

أول الأصول الثلاثة هو معرفة النبي ﷺ. The first of the three principles is "Knowing your Prophet ﷺ."

Question 3

خلق الله الجن والإنس لعبادته وحده. Allah created jinn and mankind to worship Him alone.

Question 4

دين الإسلام ثلاث مراتب: الإسلام والإيمان والإحسان. The religion of Islam has three levels: Islām, Īmān, and Iḥsān.

Question 5

أركان الإسلام الخمسة تبدأ بالشهادتين. The five pillars of Islam begin with the two testimonies (shahādah).

Question 6

بُعث النبي محمد ﷺ إلى العرب خاصة. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was sent to the Arabs in particular.

Question 7

نُبّئ النبي ﷺ بـ«اقرأ» من سورة العلق. The Prophet ﷺ was made a prophet with "Iqraʾ" from Sūrat al-ʿAlaq.

Question 8

معنى «لا إله إلا الله»: لا خالق إلا الله. "Lā ilāha illā Allāh" means "there is no creator except Allah."

Question 9

لكلمة التوحيد ركنان: نفيٌ وإثباتٌ. The word of tawhid has two pillars: negation (nafy) and affirmation (ithbāt).

Question 10

تنفع الشهادة باللسان وإن كذّب بها القلب. Saying the shahādah with the tongue benefits a person even if his heart rejects it.

SECTION DApply the Concept — طبّق المفهوم5 marks · 1 each

Question 26

Question 27

Question 28

Question 29

Question 30
