أَرْكَانُ الإِسْلَامِ الخَمْسَة

The Five Pillars of Islam

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Memorize the ḥadīth of «بُنِيَ الإسلامُ على خمسٍ» in Arabic.
  • Name the five pillars in order — and what each one means.
  • Understand what a رُكن is and why these five hold up the religion.
  • Be ready to walk through each pillar in detail in the next lessons.

Welcome to Unit 2 — the unit your wife and children can grow up on. We take the five pillars of Islam (أركان الإسلام) one by one, slowly and clearly. This first lesson lays the whole frame; the next five take each pillar in its own lesson.

الحَدِيثُ الجَامِع The Foundation Ḥadīth

بُنِيَ الإسلامُ على خمسٍ: شهادةِ أن لا إلهَ إلا اللهُ وأنَّ محمدًا رسولُ اللهِ، وإقامِ الصلاةِ، وإيتاءِ الزكاةِ، وحَجِّ البيتِ، وصومِ رمضانَ

"Islam is built upon five — the testimony that there is no god but Allah and that Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah, establishing the prayer, paying the zakāh, pilgrimage to the House, and fasting Ramaḍān."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim — narrated by Ibn ʿUmar

The Prophet ﷺ used the image of a building. A house needs pillars; remove a pillar and the house falls. So Islam stands on five pillars — five great acts of worship — and a Muslim's life is to keep building these pillars stronger across his life.

مَا مَعْنَى رُكْن؟ What is a رُكن (pillar)?
الرُّكنar-rukn · a pillar / a corner

A core support — the part of a building or an act of worship without which it does not stand. The five أركان الإسلام are the five things that, together, make up the body of Islam in practice.

الأَرْكَانُ الخَمْسَة The Five — in order
#الرُّكنMeaningWhat it builds in you
1الشَّهَادَتَانThe two testimoniesThe right belief — the door to Islam
2الصلاةThe Prayer (five daily)The link with Allah — five times a day
3الزكاةThe obligatory charityPurifying wealth — and the heart from greed
4الصومFasting RamaḍānSelf-restraint — taqwā built in 30 days
5الحجPilgrimage to the HouseA whole-life journey to Allah's House — once

Notice the ordering — from the heart outward. الشَّهَادَتَان first (belief); then الصلاة (daily, personal); then الزكاة (yearly, with your wealth); then الصوم (a whole month each year); then الحج (once in a lifetime, if able). The religion grows wider as you walk through them.

الرُّكْنُ الأَوَّل Pillar 1 — The Two Testimonies (recap)

The first pillar is what you affirmed in Unit 1: that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah, and that Muḥammad ﷺ is His Messenger.

لا إلهَ إلا اللهُ ، محمدٌ رسولُ اللهِ

"There is no god but Allah; Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah."

الشَّهَادَتَان · the doorway to Islam — full lesson in U1 L4

The remaining four pillars are actions — each one is one lesson of this unit. Open them in order with the children.

فائدة — A note for the family

Islam has five pillars — and الإيمان (faith) has six pillars — and الإحسان (excellence in worship) has one: that you worship Allah as though you see Him. All three together are the religion. You memorize the five pillars here; the six articles of faith are in Aqeedah U3; الإحسان appears in the great حديث جبريل.
