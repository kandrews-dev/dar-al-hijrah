معرفة نبيكم محمد ﷺ

The Third Principle: Knowing Your Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Answer "Who is your Prophet?" with his name and lineage
  • State key facts of his life — birth, prophethood, hijrah, death
  • Explain the message he was sent with
  • State the four things belief in the Messenger ﷺ requires
من نبيك؟"Who Is Your Prophet?"

The third and final question: Who is your Prophet? (مَن نَبِيُّك؟). The answer:

نَبِيِّي مُحَمَّدٌ ﷺ"My Prophet is Muhammad ﷺ"

مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ عَبْدِ المُطَّلِبِ بْنِ هَاشِمٍ، وَهَاشِمٌ مِنْ قُرَيْشٍ، وَقُرَيْشٌ مِنَ العَرَبِ، وَالعَرَبُ مِنْ ذُرِّيَّةِ إِسْمَاعِيلَ بْنِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ عَلَيْهِمَا السَّلَامُ

Muhammad, son of ʿAbdullāh, son of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib, son of Hāshim. Hāshim is from Quraysh, and Quraysh are from the Arabs — from the descendants of Ismāʿīl, son of Ibrāhīm ﷺ.

حياته في سطورHis Life at a Glance
BornIn Makkah, in the Year of the Elephant (ʿĀm al-Fīl).
Lifespan63 years — 40 before prophethood, and 23 as a Prophet and Messenger.
Made a ProphetAt age 40, with «اقْرَأْ» — "Read!" (Sūrat al-ʿAlaq 96:1).
Made a MessengerWith «يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُدَّثِّرُ» — "O you who are covered!" (Sūrat al-Muddaththir 74:1) — commanded to warn.
The HijrahHe migrated from Makkah to Madinah after 13 years of calling, fleeing with his religion.
Sent toAll of mankind — Arab and non-Arab, jinn and humans, until the Last Day.
بماذا أُرسل؟What Was His Message?

He ﷺ was sent with the same message as every prophet before him (recall Aqeedah): to call people to worship Allah alone and abandon all false gods. For thirteen years in Makkah he called to tawhid; then the prayers were ordained, and he migrated to establish the religion.

يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُدَّثِّرُ ۝ قُمْ فَأَنذِرْ ۝ وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرْ ۝ وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرْ ۝ وَالرُّجْزَ فَاهْجُرْ

"O you who are covered! Arise and warn. Magnify your Lord. Purify your garments. And keep away from idols."

Sūrat al-Muddaththir 74:1–5 · سورة المدثر

Allah perfected the religion through him, and he did not pass away until he had conveyed the message in full and advised the ummah completely. He is the last of the prophets — there is no prophet after him.

ما يقتضيه الإيمان به ﷺWhat Belief in the Messenger ﷺ Requires

Saying "Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah" is not just words on the tongue. It requires four things of us:

1. Obey his commands

Do what he ﷺ ordered — "Whoever obeys the Messenger has obeyed Allah" (4:80).

2. Believe his reports

Accept as true everything he told us — of the past, the present, and the unseen.

3. Avoid what he forbade

Stay away from what he prohibited — "whatever the Messenger forbids you, abstain from it" (59:7).

4. Worship as he taught

Worship Allah only in the way he ﷺ prescribed — "Pray as you have seen me pray" (Bukhārī).

التمارينExercises
Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
نبينا محمد بن عبد الله من قريش من العرب. Our Prophet Muhammad ibn ʿAbdillāh is from Quraysh, from the Arabs.
عاش النبي ﷺ ثلاثاً وستين سنة. The Prophet ﷺ lived sixty-three years.
بُعث النبي ﷺ إلى العرب فقط. The Prophet ﷺ was sent to the Arabs only.
نُبّئ باقرأ وأُرسل بالمدثر. He was made a prophet with "Iqraʾ" and a messenger with "al-Muddaththir."
يجوز عبادة الله بطريقة لم يشرعها النبي ﷺ. It is allowed to worship Allah in a way the Prophet ﷺ did not prescribe.
