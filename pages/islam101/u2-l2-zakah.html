الزكاة

Zakāh — Purifying your wealth

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Understand the meaning of الزكاة — and why Allah called it that.
  • Know the three conditions: wealth above النِّصاب, a full حَوْل (lunar year), and free Muslim ownership.
  • Know the rate — 2.5% on gold, silver, cash and trade goods.
  • Know the eight categories who may receive it (التوبة 9:60).
  • Hear the warning Allah gave to those who withhold it.

الزكاة is the third pillar. Allah pairs it with the prayer over thirty times in the Qur'an — because the prayer purifies the soul, and الزكاة purifies the wealth.

معنى الزكاة Part 1 — What الزكاة means

In the Arabic language, زكا means to grow, to be pure, to be blessed. So الزكاة is named for what it does to your wealth: it purifies the remaining 97.5% and Allah blesses what remains. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Charity does not decrease wealth." (Muslim)

خُذْ مِنْ أَمْوَالِهِمْ صَدَقَةً تُطَهِّرُهُمْ وَتُزَكِّيهِم بِهَا

"Take from their wealth a charity by which you purify them (تطهّر) and cleanse them (تزكّي)."

التوبة 9:103

شروط وجوبها Part 2 — When zakāh becomes due

Zakāh becomes obligatory on a person when three conditions meet:

النِّصابan-niṣāb · the minimum threshold

Wealth must reach the minimum amount Allah set. For gold: 85 grams. For silver: 595 grams. For cash & trade goods: the cash equivalent of one of those (today usually calculated by the silver niṣāb, which protects the poor better).

الحَوْلal-ḥawl · the lunar year

The wealth must remain in your ownership for a full lunar year. (Crops and fruit are different — they are zakāh-able at harvest.)

المِلْك التامfull Muslim ownership

The wealth must be yours fully, in your hand, not owed away. Zakāh is on Muslims only.

المقدار Part 3 — The rate (how much)
Wealth typeRateNote
Gold, silver, cash, trade goods2.5% (a quarter of a tenth)The "default" zakāh most families pay yearly
Crops & fruit watered by rain10% (al-ʿushr)At harvest, no حَوْل
Crops watered by irrigation/effort5%Half — the effort reduces the rate
Livestock (camels, cattle, sheep)Detailed Sunnah tablesGrazing herds at niṣāb thresholds
Buried treasure (rikāz)20% (a fifth)One-time when found

For the modern family: every lunar year, total up your cash, savings, gold, silver and trade goods (after subtracting immediate debts). If you are above niṣāb, 2.5% is owed.

المصارف الثمانية Part 4 — The eight recipients (التوبة 9:60)

إِنَّمَا الصَّدَقَاتُ لِلْفُقَرَاءِ وَالْمَسَاكِينِ وَالْعَامِلِينَ عَلَيْهَا وَالْمُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمْ وَفِي الرِّقَابِ وَالْغَارِمِينَ وَفِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ وَابْنِ السَّبِيلِ ۖ فَرِيضَةً مِّنَ اللَّهِ

"The charities are only for the poor, and the needy, and those who collect them, and those whose hearts are to be reconciled, and for freeing captives, and the debt-ridden, and in the path of Allah, and the wayfarer — an obligation from Allah."

التوبة 9:60

  1. الفقراء — the poor, who have little or nothing.
  2. المساكين — the needy, who have something but not enough.
  3. العاملون عليها — those appointed to collect and distribute it.
  4. المؤلَّفة قلوبهم — those whose hearts are reconciled — new Muslims, or those whose Islam is being strengthened.
  5. في الرِّقاب — freeing slaves (and historically, ransoming Muslim captives).
  6. الغارمون — the debt-ridden who cannot pay.
  7. في سبيل الله — in Allah's path, primarily jihād under a legitimate authority.
  8. ابن السبيل — the stranded traveller, even if rich at home.

Zakāh may not be given to one's own parents, children, or spouse (you already maintain them) — nor to the wealthy who don't need it, nor for building mosques (though voluntary صدقة can be).

عقوبة مانع الزكاة Part 5 — The warning to those who withhold it

وَالَّذِينَ يَكْنِزُونَ الذَّهَبَ وَالْفِضَّةَ وَلَا يُنفِقُونَهَا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ فَبَشِّرْهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ ۝ يَوْمَ يُحْمَىٰ عَلَيْهَا فِي نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ فَتُكْوَىٰ بِهَا جِبَاهُهُمْ وَجُنُوبُهُمْ وَظُهُورُهُمْ

"And those who hoard gold and silver and do not spend it in the way of Allah — give them tidings of a painful punishment: on the Day when it will be heated in the fire of Hell, and their foreheads, sides, and backs will be branded with it…"

التوبة 9:34–35

Ibn ʿUmar said: any wealth on which zakāh is paid is no longer "hoarded" — even if buried under the earth; and any wealth from which zakāh is withheld is "hoarded" — even if held in plain sight. The verse is about withholding the right of Allah, not about owning wealth.

قاعدة للأسرة — A practical rule

Pick a date in the lunar calendar (Ramaḍān is common — easy to remember, and rewards multiply). On that date every year, total up all cash, gold, silver, and money owed to you that you'll receive. If above niṣāb, pay 2.5%. Give it directly to a known poor family if you can — the Prophet ﷺ preferred immediate hand-to-hand. Teach the children to count it with you. Their hand on yours, learning where it goes.

"And establish the prayer and give the zakāh." (البقرة 2:43 — repeated throughout the Qur'an) The two pillars Allah keeps pairing — the prayer purifies the soul, the zakāh purifies the wealth.
