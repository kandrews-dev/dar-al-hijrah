الصَّوْم — صَوْمُ رَمَضَان

Fasting — the Month of Ramaḍān

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know what الصوم means and why Allah prescribed it.
  • Know the two pillars of the fast: النية and الإمساك.
  • Know what breaks the fast — and what does not.
  • Know the reward, and the place of لَيْلَةُ القَدْر in the last ten nights.

الصوم is the fourth pillar. Allah made it obligatory on every able Muslim to fast the month of رمضان — thirty days a year of restraining the body so the heart can rise.

لِمَاذَا الصَّوْم؟ Part 1 — Why we fast

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ

"O you who believe, fasting has been prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you — so that you may attain تقوى."

البقرة 2:183

The whole purpose Allah gave us: «لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ» — that you may attain التقوى (Allah-consciousness). When you leave even permissible food and drink for the sake of Allah, leaving the forbidden becomes easy. The fast trains the soul.

تَعْرِيفُ الصَّوْم Part 2 — What الصوم is
الصومaṣ-Ṣawm — fasting

Abstaining (الإمساك) from food, drink, and marital intimacy — from الفجر (dawn) until المغرب (sunset) — with the intention (النية) of worshipping Allah.

So the fast has two pillars: (1) the niyyah in the heart from the night before, and (2) the abstention from dawn to sunset. Without the intention there is no fast — only hunger.

مَا يُفْطِرُ وَمَا لَا يُفْطِر Part 3 — What breaks the fast — and what doesn't

مَنْ نَسِيَ وَهُوَ صَائِمٌ فَأَكَلَ أَوْ شَرِبَ فَلْيُتِمَّ صَوْمَهُ، فَإِنَّمَا أَطْعَمَهُ اللهُ وَسَقَاهُ

"Whoever forgets while fasting and eats or drinks — let him complete his fast, for it was only Allah who fed him and gave him drink."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

مِنْ سُنَنِ الصَّوْم Part 4 — From the Sunnah of the fast
  • السحور — a pre-dawn meal. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Take suḥūr, for in suḥūr is blessing." (Bukhārī & Muslim) Even a sip of water.
  • الإفطار — break the fast quickly at sunset. The Prophet ﷺ used to break with fresh dates, then dried dates, then water — and pray المغرب.
  • Increase القرآن, الذكر, الصدقة — Jibrīl would meet the Prophet ﷺ each night of Ramaḍān to review the Qur'an with him.
  • التراويح — the night prayer of Ramaḍān. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever stands [in prayer] Ramaḍān out of faith and seeking reward, his previous sins are forgiven." (Bukhārī & Muslim)
  • الاعتكاف — staying in the masjid in the last ten nights, as the Prophet ﷺ did every year.
فَضْلُ الصَّوْم Part 5 — The reward

كُلُّ عَمَلِ ابْنِ آدَمَ لَهُ، الْحَسَنَةُ بِعَشْرِ أَمْثَالِهَا إِلَى سَبْعِمِائَةِ ضِعْفٍ — قَالَ اللهُ: إِلَّا الصَّوْمَ فَإِنَّهُ لِي وَأَنَا أَجْزِي بِهِ

"Every deed of the son of Ādam is for him — a good deed is multiplied ten to seven-hundred times. Allah said: 'Except the fast — for it is for Me, and I shall reward for it.'"

رواه البخاري ومسلم · ḥadīth qudsī

إِذَا جَاءَ رَمَضَانُ فُتِّحَتْ أَبْوَابُ الْجَنَّةِ، وَغُلِّقَتْ أَبْوَابُ النَّارِ، وَصُفِّدَتِ الشَّيَاطِينُ

"When Ramaḍān arrives, the gates of Paradise are opened, the gates of the Fire are closed, and the devils are chained."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

لَيْلَةُ القَدْر Part 6 — The Night of Decree

لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ

"The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months."

القدر 97:3 — worship in this one night outweighs ~83 years

The Prophet ﷺ told us to seek it in the last ten nights of Ramaḍān, especially the odd ones (21, 23, 25, 27, 29). The duʿāʾ for it that ʿĀʾishah asked the Prophet ﷺ:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

"O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love to pardon — so pardon me."

رواه الترمذي · صحيح — al-Tirmidhī · authentic

زَكَاةُ الفِطْر Part 7 — Zakāt al-Fiṭr (end of Ramaḍān)

Before the عيد الفطر prayer — the Prophet ﷺ made obligatory on every Muslim — man and woman, free and slave, young and old — to give صاع (~2.5–3 kg) of the staple food of the land (dates, barley, rice, flour) to the poor, before the ʿEid prayer. It is "a purification of the faster from idle and obscene talk, and food for the needy." (Abū Dāwūd)

للأسرة — A family rhythm for Ramaḍān

Children begin practising the fast around age 7 — half-days, then full days, with their parents beside them. Make السحور a happy meal — the Prophet ﷺ called it blessed. Break together at sunset with dates and water, then pray المغرب. Read Qur'an as a family. In the last ten nights, the children wake for some part of the night — even a few minutes — to ask Allah for pardon. They will remember those nights all their lives.

احفظ — Memorize

شَهْرُ رَمَضَانَ الَّذِي أُنزِلَ فِيهِ الْقُرْآنُ هُدًى لِّلنَّاسِ

"The month of Ramaḍān — in which the Qur'an was sent down as guidance for mankind." (البقرة 2:185) The month when Allah's Book began. Open it, every Ramaḍān, with your family.
