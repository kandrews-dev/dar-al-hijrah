Unit 4 · Lesson 2

شروط لا إله إلا الله السبعة

The Seven Conditions of the Kalimah

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know that لا إله إلا الله is not just words — it has conditions.
  • Memorize the seven conditions the Salaf extracted from the Qur'an & Sunnah.
  • Check your own kalimah against them.

The hypocrites in Madinah said the kalimah with their tongues — and Allah declared them in the lowest depths of the Fire. Why? Because they did not meet its conditions. The Salaf gathered seven from the texts; a Yemeni poet versified them. Know each one — then test yourself by it.

الشروط السبعةThe Seven Conditions
١. العلم1. al-ʿIlm — Knowledge

Knowing the kalimah's meaning — what is negated (worship of other than Allah), and what is affirmed (worship of Allah alone). Without knowing this, you don't know what you said. Proof: «فَاعْلَمْ أَنَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ» — "Know that there is no god but Allah" (محمد 47:19).

٢. اليقين2. al-Yaqīn — Certainty

Without any doubt. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever you meet behind this wall, testifying that there is no god but Allah, certain in his heart of it — give him glad tidings of Paradise." (Muslim) Doubt nullifies it.

٣. القَبول3. al-Qabūl — Acceptance

Accepting with the heart and the tongue what the kalimah requires — not the way of Quraysh, who heard it and turned away in arrogance: «إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا إِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ يَسْتَكْبِرُونَ» (الصافات 37:35).

٤. الانقياد4. al-Inqiyād — Submission

Acting on it with the limbs — bowing to Allah's commands, leaving His prohibitions. Acceptance is in the heart; submission is in the action. «وَمَن يُسْلِمْ وَجْهَهُ إِلَى اللَّهِ وَهُوَ مُحْسِنٌ فَقَدِ اسْتَمْسَكَ بِالْعُرْوَةِ الْوُثْقَىٰ» (لقمان 31:22).

٥. الصِّدق5. al-Ṣidq — Truthfulness

Saying it from a truthful heart — not, as the hypocrites did, with the tongue while denying it inside. «يَقُولُونَ بِأَلْسِنَتِهِم مَّا لَيْسَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمْ» — "They say with their tongues what is not in their hearts" (الفتح 48:11).

٦. الإخلاص6. al-Ikhlāṣ — Sincerity

For Allah alone — purified of any partner. The Prophet ﷺ said: "The happiest of people with my intercession on the Day of Judgement is the one who said لا إله إلا الله sincerely from his heart." (Bukhārī)

٧. المحبة7. al-Maḥabbah — Love

Loving the kalimah, what it requires, and the people who carry it — and hating what opposes it. «وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا أَشَدُّ حُبًّا لِّلَّهِ» — "Those who believe are more intense in love for Allah" (البقرة 2:165).

قاعدة — A working rule

The seven are not "seven things you do." They are seven states your heart and limbs are in when you say it. العلم guards against ignorance, اليقين against doubt, القَبول against arrogance, الانقياد against abandoning the deed, الصِّدق against hypocrisy, الإخلاص against shirk, المحبة against cold half-belief. Each closes a door the Shayṭān will try to push you through.
