الأَذْكَارُ اليَوْمِيَّة

Daily Remembrances — the believer's protection

أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس — Objectives

  • Know what الذكر is and why Allah commanded so much of it.
  • Memorize a small core set for morning, evening, after prayer, and before sleep.
  • Make adhkār a fixed rhythm — like meals.

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ ذِكْرًا كَثِيرًا

"O you who believe, remember Allah with much remembrance."

الأحزاب 33:41

بَعْدَ كُلِّ صَلَاةٍ مَفْرُوضَةAfter each obligatory prayer

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ (٣) ، اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ ، تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ ، سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ (٣٣) ، الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ (٣٣) ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ (٣٣) ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

Astaghfirullāh (3x) · "O Allah, You are al-Salām and from You is salām — blessed are You, O Possessor of Majesty and Honour." · Subḥān Allāh (33) · al-Ḥamdu lillāh (33) · Allāhu akbar (33) · "Lā ilāha illā Allāh, alone with no partner — His is the dominion, His is the praise, and He is over all things capable."

رواه مسلم — sins like the foam of the sea are forgiven for the one who completes the 33+33+33+1

أَذْكَارُ الصَّبَاحِ وَالمَسَاءMorning & evening — the core set

Recited once at morning (after فجر) and once at evening (after عصر):

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ... · قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ ... · قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ ...

Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ · al-Falaq · al-Nās — three times each at morning and evening.

رواه أبو داود · صحيح — "they suffice you against everything"

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ

"I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He created."

رواه مسلم · 3× evening — "nothing will harm him that night"

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ ، وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

"In the name of Allah — with whose name nothing on earth or in heaven causes harm — and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."

رواه الترمذي · صحيح · 3× morning & evening

رَضِيتُ بِاللَّهِ رَبًّا ، وَبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا ، وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ ﷺ نَبِيًّا

"I am pleased with Allah as my Lord, with Islam as my religion, and with Muḥammad ﷺ as my prophet."

رواه أبو داود · صحيح · 3× morning & evening — "Allah promised to please him on the Day of Judgement"

عِنْدَ الاسْتِيقَاظOn waking

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا ، وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ

"Praise be to Allah who has given us life after death, and to Him is the resurrection."

رواه البخاري ومسلم

قَبْلَ النَّوْمBefore sleep

Brush the dust off the bed, then recite — آية الكرسي (no devil will approach you until morning · Bukhārī), the last two āyāt of سورة البقرة (sufficient · Bukhārī & Muslim), the three قلs and blow into the palms then wipe the body three times, and:

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

"In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live."

رواه البخاري ومسلم

للأسرة — Start small, stay daily

Don't try to do all the adhkār at once — pick three: (1) after-prayer (33+33+33+1) · (2) morning + evening (the three قل's, the perfect words, "I am pleased") · (3) before sleep (آية الكرسي, three قل's blown on palms). Do these every day for a month — they'll become as natural as brushing teeth. Then add more. The book to use is Ḥiṣn al-Muslim (the Fortress of the Muslim) by Saʿīd al-Qaḥṭānī.

اِحْفَظْ — Memorize

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ

"Verily, by the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest." (الرعد 13:28) The cure for every anxious heart.
