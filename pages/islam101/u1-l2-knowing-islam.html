معرفة دين الإسلام

The Second Principle: Knowing Your Religion

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Define Islam as submission to Allah with tawhid
  • Name the three levels of the religion
  • List the five pillars of Islam
  • Recall the six pillars of īmān
ما دينك؟"What Is Your Religion?"

The second question, the second principle: What is your religion? (مَا دِينُك؟). The answer: my religion is Islam.

الإِسْلَامُIslam — definition

الاسْتِسْلَامُ لِلَّهِ بِالتَّوْحِيدِ، وَالانْقِيَادُ لَهُ بِالطَّاعَةِ، وَالبَرَاءَةُ مِنَ الشِّرْكِ وَأَهْلِهِ

To submit to Allah with tawhid, to comply with Him through obedience, and to free oneself from shirk and its people.

The word Islam (root س-ل-م) means submission and carries the meaning of peace (salām) — the peace that comes from surrendering to your Maker.

مراتب الدين الثلاثThe Three Levels of the Religion

The religion has three ascending levels — taught in the famous Ḥadīth of Jibrīl (you studied this in Aqeedah). Each contains the one before it:

الإسلام

Islām — 5 pillars

The outward acts of worship.

الإيمان

Īmān — 6 pillars

The inner beliefs of the heart.

الإحسان

Iḥsān

"Worship Allah as though you see Him."

أركان الإسلام الخمسةThe Five Pillars of Islam

The first level — Islam — stands on five pillars, named by the Prophet ﷺ: "Islam is built upon five..." (Bukhārī & Muslim).

1

الشهادتان

The Shahādatayn

2

الصلاة

Prayer

3

الزكاة

Zakāh

4

الصوم

Fasting Ramadan

5

الحج

Ḥajj (if able)

You will study each of these in depth in the Fiqh tab: prayer, zakāh, fasting, and Ḥajj each have their own units. Here in Islam 101 we learn the big picture; there, the details.

أركان الإيمان الستةThe Six Pillars of Īmān

The second level — Īmān — stands on six pillars (the inner beliefs of the heart), also from the Ḥadīth of Jibrīl:

بالله

in Allah

وملائكته

His Angels

وكتبه

His Books

ورسله

His Messengers

واليوم الآخر

the Last Day

والقدر

the Decree

These six are the whole of Aqeedah Unit 3. Together, the 5 pillars of Islam (Fiqh) and the 6 pillars of Īmān (Aqeedah) are the body and soul of the religion — and Islam 101 is your map to both.

التمارينExercises
Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
الإسلام هو الاستسلام لله بالتوحيد. Islam is submission to Allah with tawhid.
مراتب الدين ثلاث: الإسلام والإيمان والإحسان. The levels of the religion are three: Islām, Īmān, and Iḥsān.
أركان الإسلام ستة. The pillars of Islam are six.
الشهادتان أول أركان الإسلام. The Shahādatayn are the first pillar of Islam.
أركان الإيمان ستة منها الإيمان بالقدر. The pillars of īmān are six, including belief in the decree.
