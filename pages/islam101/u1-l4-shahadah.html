The Two Testimonies — The Key to Islam

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • State the two testimonies and why they are the door to Islam
  • Give the true meaning of Lā ilāha illā Allāh — and the common mistake

مفتاح الإسلامThe Key to Islam

The three principles you have learned — knowing your Lord, your religion, and your Prophet ﷺ — are all gathered and declared in two short sentences. A person enters Islam by them, and they are the first pillar of Islam:

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللهِ

"I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah."

الشهادتان · The Twin Testimony

The Prophet ﷺ said he was commanded to fight people "until they testify that there is no deity but Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah" (Bukhārī & Muslim). To bear witness (ashhadu) means to know it with the heart, say it with the tongue, and act upon it with the limbs.

معنى لا إله إلا اللهThe Meaning of Lā ilāha illā Allāh
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ"There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah"

لَا مَعْبُودَ بِحَقٍّ إِلَّا اللهُ

Its meaning is: there is none worshipped in truth except Allah (lā maʿbūda bi-ḥaqqin illā Allāh). Worship belongs to Him alone, and to direct any of it to other than Him is false.

⚠️ A common mistake. It does not mean merely "there is no god," nor "there is no creator besides Allah" — the idol-worshippers of Makkah believed Allah was the Creator, yet that did not make them Muslims (see 39:38). The word that must be added is bi-ḥaqq — "in truth / rightfully." There are many things worshipped besides Allah, but none of them deserves it. Only Allah is worshipped rightfully.

ركنا الكلمةIts Two Pillars: Negation & Affirmation

Lā ilāha illā Allāh is built on two pillars. Together they are pure tawhid: deny worship to everything false, then affirm it for Allah alone.

Pillar 1 · النَّفْي

لَا إِلَٰهَ

Negation (nafy). "There is no deity…" — rejecting and denying worship to every false object of worship besides Allah.

Pillar 2 · الإِثْبَات

إِلَّا اللهُ

Affirmation (ithbāt). "…except Allah" — establishing and affirming all worship for Allah alone, with no partner.

فَمَن يَكْفُرْ بِالطَّاغُوتِ وَيُؤْمِن بِاللهِ فَقَدِ اسْتَمْسَكَ بِالْعُرْوَةِ الْوُثْقَىٰ

"So whoever disbelieves in false gods (ṭāghūt) and believes in Allah has grasped the most trustworthy handhold."

Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:256 · سورة البقرة

Notice the order in the verse: first disbelieving in false gods (the negation), then believing in Allah (the affirmation) — exactly the two pillars of the testimony.

شروط لا إله إلا الله السبعةThe Seven Conditions

Saying the testimony benefits a person only when its conditions are met — it is not magic words said heedlessly. The scholars gathered seven conditions from the Quran and Sunnah. Each one has an opposite that cancels it:

1

Knowledge العِلْم

opposite: ignorance — الجَهْل (al-jahl)

Knowing its meaning — what it affirms and what it denies. "So know that there is no deity but Allah" (47:19).

2

Certainty اليَقِين

opposite: doubt — الشَّكّ (al-shakk)

Being absolutely certain, with no doubt. The believers are "those who then do not doubt" (49:15).

3

Acceptance القَبُول

opposite: rejection — الرَّدّ (al-radd)

Accepting all that it requires with heart and tongue. The arrogant of old "used to be arrogant when told Lā ilāha illā Allāh" (37:35).

4

Submission الانْقِيَاد

opposite: abandoning it — التَّرْك (al-tark)

Complying and acting upon it, not just affirming it. "Whoever submits his face to Allah while doing good has grasped the firmest handhold" (31:22).

5

Truthfulness الصِّدْق

opposite: lying — الكَذِب (al-kadhib)

Saying it sincerely from the heart, not as the hypocrites who say it with their tongues only (2:8–9).

6

Sincerity الإِخْلَاص

opposite: shirk & showing off — الشِّرْك وَالرِّيَاء

Purifying the deed for Allah alone. "The happiest with my intercession is the one who said Lā ilāha illā Allāh sincerely from his heart" (Bukhārī).

7

Love المَحَبَّة

opposite: hatred — الكَرَاهَة (al-karāhah)

Loving this word, its meaning, and the people of tawhid. "Those who believe are stronger in love for Allah" (2:165).

A memory aid (one famous arrangement): ʿilm, yaqīn, qabūl, inqiyād, ṣidq, ikhlāṣ, maḥabbah.

شهادة أن محمداً رسول اللهThe Second Testimony: Muhammad rasūl Allāh
مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللهِ"Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah"

He ﷺ is a human servant of Allah who is not worshipped, and a Messenger who is not to be denied. Worship belongs to Allah; following belongs to His Messenger ﷺ.

As you learned in Lesson 3, testifying to his messengership requires four things of us:

1. Obey his commands

Do what he ﷺ ordered.

2. Believe his reports

Accept as true all he told us.

3. Avoid what he forbade

Leave what he prohibited (59:7).

4. Worship as he taught

Only in the way he ﷺ prescribed.

So the two testimonies together answer all three principles: Lā ilāha illā Allāh establishes knowing your Lord and your religion (worship Him alone), and Muhammad rasūl Allāh establishes knowing your Prophet (follow him in how you worship).
