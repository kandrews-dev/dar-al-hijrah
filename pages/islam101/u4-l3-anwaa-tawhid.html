أَنْوَاعُ التَّوْحِيدِ ضِمْنَ الكَلِمَة

The Three Categories of Tawḥīd — all in the Kalimah

أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس — Objectives

  • Know the three categories of التوحيد the Salaf identified.
  • See all three contained in لا إله إلا الله.
  • Recognize what nullifies the kalimah — and where to read more.

The Salaf studied every verse of the Qur'an where Allah spoke about Himself, and saw that التوحيد appears in three distinct types — each one required, none enough on its own. And all three are folded into the single sentence لا إله إلا الله.

أَنْوَاعُ التَّوْحِيدِ الثَّلَاثَةPart 1 — The three types
١. توحيد الربوبية1. Tawḥīd al-Rubūbiyyah — Allah's Lordship

Affirming that Allah alone is the Creator, Sustainer, Owner, and Controller of all that exists. He alone gives life, takes life, sends rain, decrees affairs. "All praise belongs to Allah, the Lord of all the worlds" (الفاتحة 1:2). Even most مشركون affirmed this; it is not, by itself, what makes a person a Muslim.

٢. توحيد الألوهية2. Tawḥīd al-Ulūhiyyah — Allah alone is to be worshipped

Directing every act of worship — prayer, fear, hope, reliance, vows, slaughter, love — to Allah alone. This is the type the prophets were sent for; the type the مشركون refused; the type that لا إله إلا الله commands directly.

٣. توحيد الأسماء والصفات3. Tawḥīd al-Asmāʾ wa-l-Ṣifāt — Allah's names & attributes

Affirming for Allah only what He affirmed for Himself (and what His Messenger ﷺ affirmed for Him) — without تحريف (distortion), تعطيل (denial), تكييف (asking "how"), or تمثيل (likening to creation). The full treatment is in Aqeedah U4.

الأَنْوَاعُ الثَّلَاثَةُ فِي الكَلِمَةPart 2 — All three contained in the kalimah

A single sentence — لا إله إلا الله — holds all three:

Where in the kalimahWhich typeWhy
«الله»الربوبية + الأسماء والصفاتThe Name الله contains every attribute of perfection and every Lordship
«إله»الألوهية«إله» means the one worshipped — so the entire kalimah is structured around worship
«لا … إلا»الألوهية specificallyThe negation + affirmation is the directing of worship to Allah alone

The dispute between the Prophet ﷺ and his people was over the second type. They had the first (Rubūbiyyah) — they refused the second (Ulūhiyyah). When you say لا إله إلا الله, you are correcting that refusal.

مَا يَنْقُضُ الكَلِمَةPart 3 — What nullifies the kalimah

To say the kalimah and then do what destroys its meaning is to take the kalimah back. The full list — نواقض الإسلام العشرة by Imam Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb — is the subject of Aqeedah U6. The biggest, the one mentioned first:

إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يَغْفِرُ أَن يُشْرَكَ بِهِ وَيَغْفِرُ مَا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَن يَشَاءُ

"Indeed Allah does not forgive that partners be set up with Him; but He forgives what is besides that for whom He wills."

النساء 4:48 — الشرك is the line

للأسرة — A daily test

Teach the children three short questions: "Who made me? — Allah." (Rubūbiyyah) · "Who do I worship? — Allah alone." (Ulūhiyyah) · "What is Allah like? — There is nothing like Him; He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing." (Asmāʾ wa-l-Ṣifāt). Three answers, the whole tawḥīd.
