لِمَاذَا نَطْلُبُ العِلْم؟

Why We Seek Knowledge — The Four Matters

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Explain why every Muslim must seek knowledge
  • Recite Sūrat al-ʿAṣr and explain its four matters
  • Name the three great questions of the grave
  • Understand how this unit (the Three Principles) maps the whole religion
العِلْمُ قَبْلَ القَوْلِ وَالعَمَلKnowledge Comes Before Speech & Action

Welcome to Islam 101 — the foundation that the whole school is built upon. Before a person can worship Allah correctly, they must first know: know their Lord, know their religion, and know their Prophet ﷺ. That is why the scholars say: العِلْمُ قَبْلَ القَوْلِ وَالعَمَلِ — "Knowledge comes before speech and action." Allah Himself ordered the Prophet ﷺ to first know, then act:

فَاعْلَمْ أَنَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَاسْتَغْفِرْ لِذَنبِكَ

"So know that there is no god but Allah, and seek forgiveness for your sin."

Sūrat Muhammad 47:19 · سورة محمد

Notice the order: "know" (فَاعْلَمْ) comes before the action of seeking forgiveness. This entire unit is built on a small but mighty book — al-Uṣūl al-Thalātha (The Three Fundamental Principles).

سُورَةُ العَصْرA Whole Roadmap in One Short Sūrah

Imām al-Shāfiʿī said: "If Allah had revealed no proof to creation except this sūrah, it would have sufficed them." In just three verses, Sūrat al-ʿAṣr lays out the path of success and the path of loss:

🌙 Sūrat al-ʿAṣr — سُورَةُ العَصْر

وَالْعَصْرِ ۝ إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ لَفِي خُسْرٍ ۝ إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِ

"By time! Indeed, mankind is in loss — except those who believe, do righteous deeds, advise one another to truth, and advise one another to patience." — Sūrat al-ʿAṣr 103

المَسَائِلُ الأَرْبَعThe Four Matters We Must Learn

From this sūrah, the scholars draw four matters every Muslim must learn — and they map exactly onto the three verses:

1

العِلْم

Knowledge — knowing Allah, His Prophet ﷺ, and the religion of Islam with its evidences. (From "those who believe.")

2

العَمَل بِه

Acting upon it — knowledge is meant to be lived, not just memorised. (From "and do righteous deeds.")

3

الدَّعْوَة إِلَيْه

Calling others to it — sharing the good with kindness and wisdom. (From "advise one another to truth.")

4

الصَّبْر عَلَى الأَذَى فِيه

Patience upon the harm that comes along the way. (From "advise one another to patience.")

This is the order of a complete Muslim life: learn → live it → share it → stay patient. Our school follows the same order — we begin, always, with knowledge.

الأَسْئِلَةُ الثَّلَاثَةThe Three Great Questions

The "Three Fundamental Principles" are the answers to three questions every single person will be asked in the grave (you met these in Aqeedah — the questioning of the grave). Knowing them well, here, is preparing your answer for then. The rest of this unit takes each one in turn:

التَّمَارِينExercises
Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
العلم يكون قبل القول والعمل. Knowledge comes before speech and action.
المَسَائِلُ الأَرْبَع: العلم والعمل والدعوة والصبر. The four matters are: knowledge, action, calling, and patience.
سُورَةُ العَصْر تأمر بالعلم فقط دون العمل. Sūrat al-ʿAṣr orders knowledge only, without action.
الأصول الثلاثة هي أجوبة أسئلة القبر الثلاثة. The Three Principles are the answers to the three questions of the grave.
قال الشافعي إن سُورَةُ العَصْر لا تكفي الناس. al-Shāfiʿī said Sūrat al-ʿAṣr would not suffice the people.
