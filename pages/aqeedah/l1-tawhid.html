ما هو التوحيد؟

What Is Tawhid?

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Define tawhid in Arabic and English — تعريف التوحيد
  • Name and explain the three categories of tawhid — أقسام التوحيد الثلاثة
  • Explain the meaning of لا إله إلا الله — negation and affirmation
  • Cite Quranic evidence that tawhid is the purpose of creation
لماذا نبدأ بالتوحيد؟ Why We Begin With Tawhid

Everything in Islam begins with one idea: tawhīd (توحيد). It is the first thing the Prophets called to, the first words of the shahādah (الشهادة), and the reason we were created. Before we can study what corrupts faith — shirk — we must first understand clearly what faith is.

The word tawhid comes from the root و-ح-د, which means "to be one" or "to make one." It is built on the verb form وَحَّدَ (waḥḥada) — "to declare something to be one and alone." So when we say a person makes tawhid of Allah, it means they single Allah out — believing He is One, alone, with no partner.

التوحيد Tawhid — Islamic Definition

إِفْرَادُ اللهِ تَعَالَى بِمَا يَخْتَصُّ بِهِ مِنَ الرُّبُوبِيَّةِ وَالأُلُوهِيَّةِ وَالأَسْمَاءِ وَالصِّفَاتِ

To single out Allah alone in all that belongs uniquely to Him — His Lordship, His right to be worshipped, and His Names and Attributes.

In short: believing Allah is One, that He alone is the Lord, that He alone deserves worship, and that His Names and Attributes belong to Him alone in their perfection.

أقسام التوحيد الثلاثة The Three Categories of Tawhid

The scholars, studying the Quran and Sunnah, explained that tawhid has three categories. These are not three different tawhids — they are three aspects of singling out Allah. A believer must hold all three together.

توحيد الربوبية

Oneness of Lordship

Allah alone is the Creator, Owner, Provider, and Controller of everything that exists.

توحيد الألوهية

Oneness of Worship

Allah alone deserves all worship — prayer, supplication, love, fear, hope, and sacrifice.

توحيد الأسماء والصفات

Oneness of Names & Attributes

Affirming Allah's Names and Attributes as He described them — without distortion, denial, or resemblance to creation.

1

توحيد الربوبية — Oneness of Lordship

This is to believe that Allah alone is al-Rabb (الرَّبّ) — the Lord — who created everything, owns everything, provides for everything, and controls all affairs. He has no partner in His Lordship.

Important: Even the idol-worshippers of Makkah affirmed this category — when asked "Who created the heavens and earth?" they answered "Allah." (29:61) Believing Allah is the Creator is necessary, but by itself it is not enough to make a person a Muslim — that is why the next category matters most.

2

توحيد الألوهية — Oneness of Worship

This is to direct all worship to Allah alone — and nothing else. This is the category that the Messengers were sent to establish, and the one the disbelievers rejected. It is the meaning of لا إله إلا الله.

This is the heart of the matter. The whole struggle of the Prophets was not to convince people that Allah exists — most already believed that — but that Allah alone deserves to be worshipped. Worship (عبادة) includes prayer, duʿāʾ, sacrifice, vows, ultimate love, fear, and hope.

3

توحيد الأسماء والصفات — Names & Attributes

This is to affirm all the Names and Attributes that Allah affirmed for Himself in the Quran and that the Prophet ﷺ affirmed for Him — in a way that suits His majesty, without four mistakes:

بلا تحريف

No distortion of meaning

بلا تعطيل

No denial / emptying

بلا تكييف

No asking "how"

بلا تمثيل

No resemblance to creation

The guiding verse: لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ — "There is nothing like Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing." (42:11)

معنى لا إله إلا الله The Meaning of "Lā ilāha illā Allāh"

The greatest sentence in existence — لا إله إلا الله — is built on two pillars: a negation and an affirmation.

لا إله

النفي — The Negation

"There is no deity worthy of worship" — rejecting all false objects of worship.

إلا الله

الإثبات — The Affirmation

"...except Allah" — affirming worship for Allah alone.

Notice the order: first we clear away everything false (lā ilāha), then we affirm the truth (illā Allāh). A heart cannot truly worship Allah until it has first emptied itself of all false attachments. This is why the kalimah begins with negation — like cleaning a cup before filling it with pure water.

A common misunderstanding: "lā ilāha illā Allāh" does not only mean "there is no creator except Allah" — the disbelievers of Makkah agreed with that. It means "there is no one worthy of worship except Allah." The word إله (ilāh) means "the one who is worshipped," not merely "the one who creates."

أدلة من القرآن والسنة Proofs from Quran and Sunnah

وَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ

"I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me."

Sūrat al-Dhāriyāt 51:56 · سورة الذاريات

This verse states the purpose of creation: tawhid in worship. Every human and jinn was created for this one reason — to worship Allah alone. This is the foundation everything else is built upon.

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ۝ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ۝ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ۝ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ

"Say: He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, and there is none comparable to Him."

Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ 112 · سورة الإخلاص — تعدل ثلث القرآن

Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ is called the surah of tawhid — the Prophet ﷺ said it equals a third of the Quran. In four short verses it establishes that Allah is One (أَحَد), self-sufficient (الصَّمَد), has no children or parents, and has nothing comparable to Him.

«حَقُّ اللهِ عَلَى الْعِبَادِ أَنْ يَعْبُدُوهُ وَلَا يُشْرِكُوا بِهِ شَيْئًا»

"The right of Allah upon His servants is that they worship Him and associate nothing with Him."

Narrated by al-Bukhārī and Muslim — Ḥadīth of Muʿādh · رواه البخاري ومسلم

تدريب القواعد Grammar Drill — The تَفْعِيل Pattern (Form II Verbal Noun)

The word تَوْحِيد follows a famous Arabic pattern: تَفْعِيل. This is the verbal noun (maṣdar) of Form II verbs (فَعَّلَ), which often carry the meaning of making something happen. So وَحَّدَ → تَوْحِيد = "to make/declare one → declaring oneness."

Pattern:

فَعَّلَ (Form II verb) ← → تَفْعِيل (verbal noun)

Each word below is on the تَفْعِيل pattern. Write its three-letter root.

Exercise A صحيح أم خاطئ — True or False
التوحيد هو إفراد الله بما يختص به. Tawhid means singling out Allah alone in what belongs uniquely to Him.
أقرّ مشركو مكة بتوحيد الربوبية لكنهم لم يحققوا توحيد الألوهية. The pagans of Makkah affirmed Oneness of Lordship but rejected Oneness of Worship.
«لا إله إلا الله» تعني فقط أنه لا خالق إلا الله. "Lā ilāha illā Allāh" only means that there is no creator except Allah.
خلق الله الجن والإنس لعبادته وحده. Allah created jinn and mankind to worship Him alone.
أقسام التوحيد ثلاثة توحيدات منفصلة لا يلزم اجتماعها. The categories of tawhid are three separate tawhids that need not be held together.
