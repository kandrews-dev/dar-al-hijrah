الإيمان بالقدر

Belief in Qadar — the Divine Decree

Year 2+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Explain what belief in qadar means — its good and its bad
  • List and explain the four levels of qadar
  • Reconcile Allah's decree with human free will and responsibility
  • Correct common misconceptions about using qadar as an excuse
الركن السادس: الإيمان بالقدر Pillar 6 — Belief in al-Qadar

Qadar (القَدَر) is Allah's decree — that He knew, wrote, willed, and created everything that happens, by His perfect wisdom. The sixth pillar is to believe in qadar, its good and its bad (as the Ḥadīth of Jibrīl stated in L.0). "Bad" here refers to things that seem bad to us (hardship, loss) — but Allah's decree in itself is always wise and good.

إِنَّا كُلَّ شَيْءٍ خَلَقْنَاهُ بِقَدَرٍ

"Indeed, We created everything according to a decree (qadar)."

Sūrat al-Qamar 54:49 · سورة القمر

مراتب القدر الأربع The Four Levels of Qadar

Belief in qadar is not complete until one believes in all four levels. A useful way to remember them: Allah knew it, wrote it, willed it, and created it.

1

العِلْم

al-ʿIlm — His Knowledge

Allah knew everything that would happen, in eternity, before it occurred — the deeds of every creature. (Recall al-ʿAlīm, Unit 2 L.3.) "Allah has full knowledge of all things." (65:12)

2

الكِتَابَة

al-Kitābah — His Writing

Allah wrote the decree of all things in the Preserved Tablet (al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ) fifty thousand years before creating the heavens and earth. "Did you not know that Allah knows all that is in the heaven and earth? Indeed, that is in a Record." (22:70)

3

المَشِيئَة

al-Mashīʾah — His Will

Nothing happens except by Allah's will. What He wills is, and what He does not will is not. "And you do not will except that Allah wills." (76:30) Nothing escapes His will — not even an atom moves without it.

4

الخَلْق

al-Khalq — His Creation

Allah is the Creator of all things — including the actions of His servants. "Allah created you and what you do." (37:96) Everything besides Allah is created, sustained, and brought into being by Him alone.

القدر وحرية الاختيار Divine Decree & Human Free Will

A natural question: if Allah decreed everything, are we forced? The balanced answer of Ahl al-Sunnah: humans have a real will and real choice — you genuinely decide to pray or to lie — but your will operates within Allah's will and by the ability He grants. We are neither puppets with no choice, nor independent creators acting outside His will.

✓ We truly choose

Allah gave us will and ability, and commanded and forbade us. We are rewarded and punished for our choices — which only makes sense if the choices are real.

✓ Within Allah's will

Our will does not overpower His. He knew, wrote, willed, and created the outcome. Our freedom is real but not independent of Him.

إزالة الشبهات Clearing Common Misconceptions

✗ "If it's all decreed, why should I do anything? I'll just rely on qadar."

When a Companion asked this, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Act, for everyone is made easy towards that for which he was created." (Bukhārī & Muslim) We are commanded to take the means — qadar is no excuse for laziness. ʿUmar said, fleeing a plague: "We flee from the decree of Allah to the decree of Allah."

✗ "I sinned because Allah decreed it — so it isn't my fault."

This is the argument of the disbelievers, which the Quran rejects (6:148). Qadar is a hidden matter we learn after events; no one knows their decree beforehand, so it cannot be a basis for choice. You chose freely, so you are responsible. We use qadar to find patience in calamity, never as an excuse for sin.

✗ "Then duʿāʾ and medicine are pointless — the outcome is fixed."

Duʿāʾ and means are themselves part of qadar. Allah decreed the outcome through its causes: He decreed health through medicine, and answered prayers through duʿāʾ. The Prophet ﷺ said duʿāʾ benefits regarding what has descended and what has not. So we act and pray and trust.

ثمرات الإيمان بالقدرThe Fruits — Peace of Heart
  • Contentment in hardship: "No disaster strikes except by Allah's permission; whoever believes in Allah, He guides his heart." (64:11) The believer is not crushed by loss.
  • Freedom from arrogance and despair: success is from Allah (no pride), and loss was decreed (no despair).
  • Courage and reliance: knowing the time of death and provision are fixed frees the believer from fear of people and want.

«عَجَبًا لِأَمْرِ المُؤْمِنِ، إِنَّ أَمْرَهُ كُلَّهُ خَيْرٌ... إِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ سَرَّاءُ شَكَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ، وَإِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ ضَرَّاءُ صَبَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ»

"How wonderful is the affair of the believer — all of it is good! If good befalls him he is grateful, and that is good for him; and if harm befalls him he is patient, and that is good for him."

Narrated by Muslim · رواه مسلم
