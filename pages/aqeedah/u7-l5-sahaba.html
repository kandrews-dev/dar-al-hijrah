الصَّحَابَةُ وَآلُ البَيْت

The Companions & the Family of the Prophet ﷺ

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know who the الصحابة are and why Allah honoured them.
  • Know their ranking: the four caliphs, the ten promised Paradise, then the rest.
  • Know our duty: love them, ask Allah's pleasure on them, refrain from speaking ill of what happened among them.
  • Honour آلُ البَيْت (the Prophet's ﷺ family) without exaggeration.

Belief in the الصحابة is part of Ahl al-Sunnah's creed — Ibn Taymiyyah included it in al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah. They are the men and women Allah chose to see the Prophet ﷺ, believe in him, and die upon that. The Qur'an itself called them just, righteous, and pleased-with.

فَضْلُ الصَّحَابَة Part 1 — Their virtue

وَٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ ٱلْأَوَّلُونَ مِنَ ٱلْمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلْأَنصَارِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُم بِإِحْسَٰنٍ رَّضِىَ ٱللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُواْ عَنْهُ

"The forerunners — the first of the Emigrants and the Anṣār — and those who followed them in goodness: Allah is pleased with them, and they with Him."

التوبة 9:100

لَّقَدْ رَضِىَ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ إِذْ يُبَايِعُونَكَ تَحْتَ ٱلشَّجَرَةِ

"Allah was indeed pleased with the believers when they pledged allegiance to you under the tree."

الفتح 48:18 — the بيعة الرضوان

لَا تَسُبُّوا أَصْحَابِي، فَوَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ، لَوْ أَنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ أَنْفَقَ مِثْلَ أُحُدٍ ذَهَبًا مَا بَلَغَ مُدَّ أَحَدِهِمْ وَلَا نَصِيفَهُ

"Do not insult my Companions. By the One in whose Hand is my soul — if one of you spent the like of Uḥud in gold, it would not reach the mudd of one of them, nor half of it."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

تَرْتِيبُهُمْ فِي الفَضْل Part 2 — Their ranking in virtue

Ahl al-Sunnah hold the order the Companions themselves held — and that ʿUmar publicly affirmed:

RankWho
1أبو بكر الصدّيق — Abū Bakr
2عمر بن الخطّاب — ʿUmar
3عثمان بن عفّان — ʿUthmān
4عليّ بن أبي طالب — ʿAlī
5The remaining six of العشرة المبشّرين بالجنة — the ten promised Paradise: Ṭalḥah, al-Zubayr, ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿAwf, Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ, Saʿīd ibn Zayd, and Abū ʿUbaydah ibn al-Jarrāḥ
6The people of بدر (Badr), then the people of the بيعة الرضوان, then the rest

The four are الخلفاء الراشدون — the Rightly-Guided Caliphs — and the Prophet ﷺ said: "Hold to my Sunnah and the Sunnah of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs after me; bite onto it with your molars." (Abū Dāwūd, al-Tirmidhī).

مَا جَرَى بَيْنَهُم Part 3 — What happened among them — and our duty

History records that disputes happened among the Companions — most notably the فتنة after ʿUthmān's martyrdom: the battles of الجمل and صفّين, and the dispute over governance. Ahl al-Sunnah's rule is plain — Ibn Taymiyyah stated it in al-Wāsiṭiyyah:

قاعدة أهل السنة — The settled rule

We refrain (نُمسك) from what happened among the Companions. The reports about their disputes are not all authentic, and what is authentic — they were either:

  • Right in their ijtihād — and have two rewards (the Prophet's ﷺ ruling on a judge);
  • Mistaken in their ijtihād — and have one reward, with the mistake forgiven.

We love all of them, ask Allah's pleasure on all of them (رضي الله عنهم), and we do not make their disputes a subject of our discussions. The Rāfiḍah insult them (especially the three caliphs); the Khawārij declared ʿAlī and others kuffār. Ahl al-Sunnah are between — loving and honouring them all.

آلُ البَيْت Part 4 — The family of the Prophet ﷺ

آلُ البَيْت are the family of the Prophet ﷺ: his wives — the Mothers of the Believers (أمّهات المؤمنين) — and his close relatives upon whom charity is forbidden: the children of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib (Banū Hāshim). Among them his daughter فاطمة, her husband عليّ, and their sons الحسن والحسين — the two grandsons of the Prophet ﷺ and "the leaders of the youth of Paradise."

Ahl al-Sunnah love them for Allah's sake — but we do not raise them above their station. They are not infallible; they are not gods or partners with Allah; they are not the only righteous of the Ummah. The Rāfiḍah extreme deifies them; some go even further. The Nawāṣib went the opposite — hating ʿAlī and his family. Ahl al-Sunnah love both the Companions and the family — and the loved are loved together.
