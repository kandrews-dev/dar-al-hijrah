Unit 7 · Lesson 4

الإيمان — قوله وعمله وزيادته ونقصانه

Iman — Speech & Action · Increases & Decreases

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know the three-part definition of الإيمان held by Ahl al-Sunnah.
  • Know that الإيمان has many branches (over 70).
  • See the proof that الإيمان increases and decreases.
  • Understand why this was the great Salafi line against the Khawārij, Murjiʾah and Muʿtazila.

You affirmed the six pillars of الإيمان in U3. Now we deepen what إيمان itself is — what it includes, how it grows, how it weakens. Wrong answers here split the early sects from Ahl al-Sunnah; right answers protect both your faith and your hope.

تعريف الإيمان عند أهل السنة Part 1 — The definition of Ahl al-Sunnah

The Imams of the Salaf — al-Bukhārī, Aḥmad, al-Shāfiʿī, al-Awzāʿī and others — were united on this definition. الإيمان is:

قول وعمل واعتقادSpeech, action, and belief — together

قول اللسان (speech of the tongue — declaring the شهادتان); عمل الجوارح (action of the limbs — prayer, zakāh, ḥajj); and اعتقاد القلب (belief in the heart — affirming Allah, love, fear, hope). All three. None alone is enough.

The Murjiʾah said الإيمان is only belief in the heart (and maybe the tongue) — so a person who does nothing is "fully believing." This made the door easy to enter and sin's danger small. The Khawārij went the other way: any major sin removes الإيمان entirely. Ahl al-Sunnah walked the middle: الإيمان is all three together, and a major sin reduces it without erasing it.

شُعَب الإيمان Part 2 — The branches of Iman

الإِيمَانُ بِضْعٌ وَسَبْعُونَ — أَوْ بِضْعٌ وَسِتُّونَ — شُعْبَةً، فَأَفْضَلُهَا قَوْلُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَأَدْنَاهَا إِمَاطَةُ الْأَذَى عَنِ الطَّرِيقِ، وَالْحَيَاءُ شُعْبَةٌ مِنَ الْإِيمَانِ

"Iman has over seventy — or over sixty — branches. The highest of them is the saying of لا إله إلا الله; the lowest is removing harm from the road; and modesty is a branch of Iman."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

So الإيمان is a tree with many branches. Some are huge — the شهادتان, the prayer. Some are small — moving a stone from the path. Some are of the heart (love of Allah, hope, fear, gratitude); some of the tongue (dhikr, truthfulness); some of the limbs (prayer, charity, justice).

You add branches as you grow — and lose branches when you sin. But the root — the شهادتان with sincere belief — remains, unless something uproots the tree itself (the نواقض of U6).

يزيد بالطاعة وينقص بالمعصية Part 3 — It increases with obedience, decreases with sin

This is one of the great Salafi creed points. The proofs are many:

إِنَّمَا ٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ذُكِرَ ٱللَّهُ وَجِلَتْ قُلُوبُهُمْ وَإِذَا تُلِيَتْ عَلَيْهِمْ ءَايَٰتُهُۥ زَادَتْهُمْ إِيمَٰنًا

"The believers are only those whose hearts tremble when Allah is mentioned, and when His verses are recited to them, it increases them in Iman."

الأنفال 8:2 — explicit: Iman grows by hearing the Qur'an

وَيَزْدَادَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنًا

"…and that those who believe may increase in Iman."

المدثر 74:31 — see also آل عمران 3:173, التوبة 9:124, الفتح 48:4

مَنْ رَأَى مِنْكُمْ مُنْكَرًا فَلْيُغَيِّرْهُ بِيَدِهِ، فَإِنْ لَمْ يَسْتَطِعْ فَبِلِسَانِهِ، فَإِنْ لَمْ يَسْتَطِعْ فَبِقَلْبِهِ — وَذَلِكَ أَضْعَفُ الْإِيمَانِ

"Whoever sees a wrong, let him change it with his hand; if he cannot, then with his tongue; if he cannot, then with his heart — and that is the weakest of Iman."

رواه مسلم · Muslim — the very word "weakest" proves Iman has degrees

So obedience increases الإيمان; sin decreases it. ʿUmar would say to his companions: "Come, let us increase our Iman" — and they would sit and remember Allah. Reading the Qur'an, hearing the names of Allah, doing a quiet act of obedience — all are real increases. Backbiting, looking at what is forbidden, missing a prayer — all are real decreases. You feel it in your heart.

منزلة بين المنزلتين عند أهل السنة Part 4 — The Salafi position vs. the deviant sects

When a Muslim commits a major sin (short of shirk and the other نواقض), where does he stand?

SectVerdict on the sinning Muslim
KhawārijA kāfir — out of Islam · eternal in the Fire
MuʿtazilaNeither believer nor disbeliever — a middle station · eternal in the Fire
MurjiʾahA complete believer · their sin harms them not
Ahl al-SunnahA believer of weakened Iman · a sinful Muslim · under Allah's will in the Hereafter — if He wills, He forgives; if He wills, He punishes — and not eternal in the Fire

الخلاصة — The settled view

الإيمان = speech + action + belief. It has many branches. It increases with obedience and decreases with sin. A Muslim who falls into a major sin (short of the نواقض) remains a believer of weakened Iman — not a kāfir, not "fully faithful." Ask Allah for thabāt; do the deeds that grow it; flee the sins that erode it.
