The Names of Mercy

الرحمة في الإسلام Mercy — At the Heart of Knowing Allah

Of all Allah's names, the names of mercy come first. Every chapter of the Quran but one opens with بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ — and Sūrat al-Fātiḥah, which we recite in every rakʿah, names Allah as al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm right after praising Him as Lord of the worlds.

The Prophet ﷺ taught that Allah's mercy overwhelms His anger. In a ḥadīth qudsī, Allah says:

«إِنَّ رَحْمَتِي تَغْلِبُ غَضَبِي»

"Indeed My mercy prevails over My anger."

Narrated by al-Bukhārī and Muslim · رواه البخاري ومسلم

In this lesson we study four names that all flow from this ocean of mercy. Three share the root ر-ح-م, and one — al-Wadūd — adds the meaning of love.

الرَّحْمَٰن

al-Raḥmān

The Most Merciful (universal, vast mercy)

ر-ح-م

al-Raḥmān is the One whose mercy is vast and all-encompassing — it reaches every creature, believer and disbeliever, in this world. It is a name exclusive to Allah; no one else may be called by it. The form فَعْلَان (faʿlān) in Arabic signals fullness and intensity — so al-Raḥmān means mercy that has reached its absolute extent.

Evidence — الدليل

الرَّحْمَٰنُ عَلَى الْعَرْشِ اسْتَوَىٰ

"The Most Merciful rose over the Throne." — Ṭāhā 20:5

Effect on the heart — أثره

Fills the believer with hope and gratitude — knowing that the One above the Throne, in full majesty, relates to creation through mercy.

الرَّحِيم

al-Raḥīm

The Bestower of Mercy (special, to the believers)

ر-ح-م

al-Raḥīm describes Allah's mercy as it is delivered and applied — especially to the believers. The form فَعِيل (faʿīl) points to an ongoing, active quality. Scholars summarise the pair: al-Raḥmān = the One possessing vast mercy; al-Raḥīm = the One who shows that mercy especially to His believing servants.

Evidence — الدليل

وَكَانَ بِالْمُؤْمِنِينَ رَحِيمًا

"And He is ever Merciful to the believers." — al-Aḥzāb 33:43

Raḥmān vs. Raḥīm — الفرق

al-Raḥmān = mercy in its vastness (all creation). al-Raḥīm = mercy in its delivery (special to believers, here and in the Hereafter).

Why two names from one root? The Quran pairs them — الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ — to teach that Allah's mercy is both infinitely vast (Raḥmān) and personally reaching you (Raḥīm). It is not a distant mercy; it is mercy that arrives.

الوَدُود

al-Wadūd

The Most Loving (loving and beloved)

و-د-د

al-Wadūd comes from الوُدّ (wudd) — pure, deep love. Scholars explain it carries two meanings at once: Allah loves His righteous servants, and He is the One most deserving to be loved. His love is shown in action — forgiving, guiding, and drawing the servant near.

Evidence — الدليل

وَهُوَ الْغَفُورُ الْوَدُودُ

"And He is the Forgiving, the Most Loving." — al-Burūj 85:14

Effect on the heart — أثره

Turns worship into a relationship of love, not mere fear — the servant strives to be among those Allah loves.

الرَّؤُوف

al-Raʾūf

The Most Kind / Compassionate

ر-أ-ف

al-Raʾūf comes from الرأفة (raʾfah) — the most tender, gentle form of mercy, the kindness that wants to spare you harm. If raḥmah is mercy that gives good, raʾfah is the gentleness that shields from difficulty. It is often the most intense and tender expression of compassion.

Evidence — الدليل

إِنَّ اللَّهَ بِالنَّاسِ لَرَءُوفٌ رَّحِيمٌ

"Indeed Allah, to the people, is Most Kind and Merciful." — al-Baqarah 2:143

Effect on the heart — أثره

Reassures the believer that Allah's commands themselves are an act of kindness — meant to spare us harm in this life and the next.

تدريب القواعد Grammar Drill — Intensive Forms: فَعْلَان vs. فَعِيل

Both رَحْمَٰن and رَحِيم come from the same root ر-ح-م, but use different intensive patterns (صيغ المبالغة). The pattern shapes the shade of meaning. Identify the root of each name below.

الاسم — Name Pattern الجذر — Root Meaning
الرَّحْمَٰن فَعْلَان vast mercy
الرَّحِيم فَعِيل delivered mercy
الوَدُود فَعُول deep love
الرَّؤُوف فَعُول tender kindness
التمارين Exercises
Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
الرحمن اسم خاص بالله لا يُسمى به غيره. al-Raḥmān is a name exclusive to Allah; no one else may be named by it.
الرحيم يدل على رحمة الله الواصلة للمؤمنين خاصة. al-Raḥīm points to Allah's mercy as delivered especially to the believers.
الودود يعني أن الله يحب عباده فقط دون أن يكون محبوباً. al-Wadūd means only that Allah loves His servants, not that He is loved.
قال الله في الحديث القدسي: «إن رحمتي تغلب غضبي». Allah said in a ḥadīth qudsī: "My mercy prevails over My anger."
الرأفة أعمّ وأوسع من الرحمة. Raʾfah (kindness) is broader and more general than raḥmah (mercy).

أي اسم يجمع معنى المحبة من الله ولله؟ Which name carries the meaning of both loving and being loved?

«وهو الغفور الودود» — في أي سورة؟ "And He is the Forgiving, the Most Loving" — which surah?

من أي جذر يأتي اسم «الرؤوف»؟ From which root does the name "al-Raʾūf" come?

Exercise Cطابق الاسم بمعناه — Match Name to Meaning

Choose the name that best fits each description.

"His mercy is so vast it encompasses every creature in existence."

"He loves His righteous servants and is most worthy of being loved."

"His tender gentleness seeks to spare His servants from harm."

Exercise Dتفكر وأجب — Reflection

لماذا بدأ الله أكثر سور القرآن باسميه الرحمن الرحيم؟ Why did Allah open almost every surah of the Quran with His names al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm?
