الوَاسِطِيَّة · الدَّرْسُ السَّادِسYear 3

صَادِقُونَ مَصْدُوقُونَ وَالجَمْعُ بَيْنَ النَّفْيِ وَالإِثْبَات

The Messengers’ Chain, and Negation with Affirmation

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Explain صَادِقُونَ مَصْدُوقُونَ as a two-sided authentication of the messengers.
  • Say who the author means by الَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ عَلَيْهِ مَا لَا يَعْلَمُونَ, and name the two āyahs against them.
  • Explain why al-Ṣāffāt 37:180–182 glorifies Allah but sends peace upon the messengers.
  • State the method of النَّفْيِ وَالإِثْبَاتِ and give examples of each side.
صَادِقُونَ مَصْدُوقُونَ Truthful, and Told the Truth

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

ثُمَّ رُسُلُهُ صَادِقُونَ مَصْدُوقُونَ، بِخِلَافِ الَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ عَلَيْهِ مَا لَا يَعْلَمُونَ.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Two words that look like a flourish and are actually an argument. الصِّدْق is the correspondence of a report to reality. So:

صَادِقُونَtruthful in what they reported about Allah — they did not misreport
مَصْدُوقُونَtold the truth in the revelation that reached them by way of the angels — because it is from Allah, so they do not speak from desire

And al-Fawzān names what the pair amounts to: تَوْثِيقٌ لِسَنَدِ الرُّسُلِ — an authentication of the messengers' chain of transmission. If you have studied the terminology of ḥadīth, you already know how much work that phrase is doing. A report is sound when the chain is sound at every link. Here the links are named: Allah spoke, a truthful angel carried it, a truthful messenger conveyed it. The truth was told to them, and they told it to creation.

Which is why, al-Fawzān says, accepting what they described Allah with is obligatory. The obligation does not rest on our finding the attribute reasonable. It rests on the chain.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

And the contrast. بِخِلَافِ الَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ عَلَيْهِ مَا لَا يَعْلَمُونَ — those who speak about Allah without knowledge: about His law, His religion, and His names and attributes — merely by their conjectures and imaginings, or by what they receive from the devils. al-Fawzān lists them, and the list is deliberately unflattering: the lying false-prophets, the innovators, the heretics, the magicians, the soothsayers, the astrologers, and the evil scholars.

Note the last entry. The danger is not only from the obviously disreputable; a man may hold a chair and still be speaking about Allah from nothing.

هَلْ أُنَبِّئُكُمْ عَلَىٰ مَنْ تَنَزَّلُ الشَّيَاطِينُ تَنَزَّلُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ أَفَّاكٍ أَثِيمٍ

"Shall I inform you upon whom the devils descend? They descend upon every sinful liar."

Sūrat al-Shuʿarāʾ 26:221–222 · سُورَةُ الشُّعَرَاء

فَوَيْلٌ لِلَّذِينَ يَكْتُبُونَ الْكِتَابَ بِأَيْدِيهِمْ ثُمَّ يَقُولُونَ هَٰذَا مِنْ عِنْدِ اللَّهِ

"So woe to those who write the scripture with their own hands, then say: this is from Allah."

Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:79 · سُورَةُ البَقَرَة

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān then draws the whole chain into one conclusion, and it is the hinge of the book's method. Since Allah knows Himself and others best; since He is most truthful in speech; since His messengers are truthful in everything they report of Him; and since the intermediary who brought them the revelation is a truthful one from among His noble angels —

— then reliance must fall on what Allah and His messengers said, especially in the chapter of the names and attributes, in what is negated and what is affirmed.

And what is to be rejected is named just as plainly: what the innovators said — those who claim المَجَاز in the names and attributes and negate them by every available means of negation, turning away from what the messengers brought, relying on their own desires, or imitating men unfit to be followed.

تَسْبِيحٌ لَهُ، وَسَلَامٌ عَلَيْهِم Glory to Him, Peace upon Them

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَلِهَذَا قَالَ: ﴿سُبْحَانَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ ۝ وَسَلَامٌ عَلَى الْمُرْسَلِينَ ۝ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ﴾ — فَسَبَّحَ نَفْسَهُ عَمَّا وَصَفَهُ بِهِ المُخَالِفُونَ لِلرُّسُلِ، وَسَلَّمَ عَلَى المُرْسَلِينَ لِسَلَامَةِ مَا قَالُوهُ مِنَ النَّقْصِ وَالعَيْبِ.

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ وَسَلَامٌ عَلَى الْمُرْسَلِينَ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

"Glory be to your Lord, the Lord of Might, above what they describe. And peace be upon the messengers. And praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds."

Sūrat al-Ṣāffāt 37:180–182 · سُورَةُ الصَّافَّات

الشَّرْح · the explanation

This is the author's proof for everything above, and he reads it with great care. al-Fawzān gives the المُفْرَدَات first:

  • سُبْحَانَ — a verbal noun from التَّسْبِيح, which is declaring free of defect.
  • رَبِّكَ — the Owner, the Master, the One who nurtures His creation by His favours.
  • العِزَّة — strength, dominance, inviolability. And joining Rabb to al-ʿIzzah is the annexation of the described to its description.
  • عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ — what the opponents of the messengers describe Him with, of what does not befit His majesty.
  • وَسَلَامٌ — said to be from salām, the greeting; and said to be from salāmah, safety from harms.

Now look at what the āyah does, which is the author's point. Allah glorified Himself from what the opponents of the messengers described Him with — and in the very next breath sent peace upon the messengers, for the soundness of what they said, free of deficiency and defect.

Two parties, two verdicts, side by side. Everything said of Allah falls into one or the other: either it is of a piece with what the messengers brought — and peace is upon them — or it is what Allah declared Himself free of.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān closes the section with his own list of what the āyahs yield — مَا يُسْتَفَادُ مِنَ الآيَاتِ — and it is worth having in full:

  1. Declaring Allah free of what the misguided and the ignorant describe Him with, of what does not befit His majesty.
  2. The truthfulness of the messengers, and the obligation to accept what they brought and what they reported of Allah.
  3. That sending peace upon the messengers and honouring them is legislated.
  4. Rejecting everything that contradicts what the messengers brought — especially what concerns the names and attributes of Allah.
  5. That praising Allah and thanking Him for His favours is legislated — and the greatest of those favours is the favour of tawḥīd.

That fifth point is easy to read past. The sūrah ends with وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ — and al-Fawzān reads the praise as being, above all, for having been given tawḥīd. Not for health or wealth. For this.

الجَمْعُ بَيْنَ النَّفْيِ وَالإِثْبَات Negation and Affirmation, Together

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَهُوَ سُبْحَانَهُ قَدْ جَمَعَ فِيمَا وَصَفَ وَسَمَّى بِهِ نَفْسَهُ بَيْنَ النَّفْيِ وَالإِثْبَاتِ. فَلَا عُدُولَ لِأَهْلِ السُّنَّةِ وَالجَمَاعَةِ عَمَّا جَاءَ بِهِ المُرْسَلُونَ، فَإِنَّهُ الصِّرَاطُ المُسْتَقِيمُ.

This sentence is the key to the next forty pages of the matn. Every attribute the author is about to bring will arrive on this pattern.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān marks this as the method Allah laid down in His Book for establishing His names and attributes — and the method believers are obliged to walk in this weighty chapter. It has two sides, and they are not optional halves:

SideWhat it doesExamples
النَّفْيnegating what contradicts perfection — the kinds of defect and deficiencynegating the rival (النِّدّ), the partner, drowsiness and sleep (السِّنَة وَالنَّوْم), death, and weariness (اللُّغُوب)
الإِثْبَاتaffirming the attributes of perfection and descriptions of majestythe names gathered in al-Ḥashr 59:23–24

Notice what is negated. Not attributes — deficiencies. The negation in the Qurʾān never empties Allah of a perfection; it removes from Him what would be a defect. That single observation is what separates the Qurʾān's negation from the negation of the muʿaṭṭilah, who used the same word for the opposite work.

لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ

"Neither drowsiness overtakes Him, nor sleep."

Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:255 · سُورَةُ البَقَرَة

هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْمَلِكُ الْقُدُّوسُ السَّلَامُ الْمُؤْمِنُ الْمُهَيْمِنُ الْعَزِيزُ الْجَبَّارُ الْمُتَكَبِّرُ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ

"He is Allah, besides whom there is no god: the Sovereign, the Pure, the Peace, the Giver of security, the Overseer, the Mighty, the Compeller, the Supreme. Glory be to Allah above what they associate."

Sūrat al-Ḥashr 59:23 · سُورَةُ الحَشْر

Read that āyah against the table. Eight names affirmed — and then سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ, a negation, in the same verse. The method is not something the scholars imposed on the Qurʾān; it is how the Qurʾān speaks.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

فَلَا عُدُولَ لِأَهْلِ السُّنَّةِ — no inclining, no deviation. Rather, says al-Fawzān, they are those who follow in the messengers' footsteps, lit by their lights. And of that following: affirming for Allah the attributes of perfection and declaring Him free of what does not befit Him — for the messengers established this great foundation, and the enemies of the messengers turned away from it.

فَإِنَّهُ الصِّرَاطُ المُسْتَقِيمُ — and this is the author's reason for the whole sentence. There is no turning aside because what the messengers brought is the straight path itself. Not a route to it. It.

True or False صَحٌ أَمْ خَطَأٌ
النَّفْيُ فِي القُرْآنِ يَنْفِي النَّقَائِصَ لَا الصِّفَاتِ. The negation in the Qurʾān negates deficiencies, not attributes.
أَهْلُ السُّنَّةِ يَعْدِلُونَ عَمَّا جَاءَ بِهِ المُرْسَلُونَ إِذَا خَالَفَ العَقْلَ. Ahl al-Sunnah turn aside from what the messengers brought when it contradicts the intellect.
الصِّدْقُ هُوَ مُطَابَقَةُ الخَبَرِ لِلْوَاقِعِ. Truthfulness is the correspondence of a report to reality.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ وَسَلَامٌ عَلَى الْمُرْسَلِينَ

Two verdicts in one breath: Allah declared free of what His opponents said, and peace upon those who spoke soundly of Him. Every statement you will ever read about Allah belongs on one side of that line or the other.

لِأَغْرَاضِ التَّعْلِيمِ فَقَطْ — هَذَا المَوْقِعُ لَا يُفْتِي. لِلْفَتَاوَى، يُرْجَعُ إِلَى العُلَمَاءِ المَوْثُوقِين.
