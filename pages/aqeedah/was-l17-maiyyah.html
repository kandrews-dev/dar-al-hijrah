المَعِيَّةُ العَامَّةُ وَالخَاصَّة

The Maʿiyyah of Allah: General and Particular

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Distinguish المَعِيَّةُ العَامَّة from المَعِيَّةُ الخَاصَّة, and say what each one entails.
  • Explain why مَعَكُمْ is always glossed by al-Fawzān with بِعِلْمِهِ in the general kind.
  • Reconcile Allah’s being مَعَ خَلْقِه with His being عَالِيًا عَلَيْهِم, in the two ways al-Fawzān gives.
  • Give the Arabs’ own example that shows المَعِيَّة does not require touching or being level with.
٢٠ · إِثْبَاتُ مَعِيَّةِ اللهِ لِخَلْقِه Section 20 — The Maʿiyyah of Allah with His Creation

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَقَوْلُهُ: ﴿وَهُوَ مَعَكُمْ أَيْنَ مَا كُنْتُمْ وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ﴾، وَقَوْلُهُ: ﴿مَا يَكُونُ مِنْ نَجْوَى ثَلَاثَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ رَابِعُهُمْ وَلَا خَمْسَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ سَادِسُهُمْ وَلَا أَدْنَى مِنْ ذَلِكَ وَلَا أَكْثَرَ إِلَّا هُوَ مَعَهُمْ أَيْنَ مَا كَانُوا﴾، وَقَوْلُهُ: ﴿لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَنَا﴾، ﴿إِنَّنِي مَعَكُمَا أَسْمَعُ وَأَرَى﴾، ﴿إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الَّذِينَ اتَّقَوْا وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ مُحْسِنُونَ﴾، ﴿وَاصْبِرُوا إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ﴾، ﴿كَمْ مِنْ فِئَةٍ قَلِيلَةٍ غَلَبَتْ فِئَةً كَثِيرَةً بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ وَاللَّهُ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ﴾.

The author places this section immediately after the Throne and the highness, and that placement is the argument. Having just established that Allah is above His creation, he now brings every text that says He is with them — because the muʿaṭṭilah had used these very āyāt to cancel the first set. al-Fawzān will show that the two are not in competition, but he does the work in the right order: first establish what each kind of maʿiyyah actually entails, and only then reconcile.

النَّوْعُ الأَوَّل · المَعِيَّةُ العَامَّة The First Kind — the General Maʿiyyah

يَعْلَمُ مَا يَلِجُ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَمَا يَخْرُجُ مِنْهَا وَمَا يَنْزِلُ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ وَمَا يَعْرُجُ فِيهَا وَهُوَ مَعَكُمْ أَيْنَ مَا كُنْتُمْ وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ

"He knows what penetrates into the earth and what comes out of it, and what descends from the heaven and what ascends into it; and He is with you wherever you are — and Allah is Seeing of what you do."

Sūrat al-Ḥadīd 57:4 · سُورَةُ الحَدِيد

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān glosses it exactly: وَهُوَ مَعَكُمْ أَيْنَ مَا كُنْتُمْ — He is with you by His knowledge; watching over you, witnessing your deeds — wherever you were and wherever you are, on land or at sea, by night or by day. And He hears your speech and sees your place.

And this is the place of the shāhid from the noble āyah: in it is the affirming of the general maʿiyyah. Then وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ — nothing of your deeds is hidden from Him.

Notice that the āyah itself supplies the gloss. It opens with يَعْلَمُ and closes with بَصِيرٌ. The maʿiyyah sits between two statements of knowing and seeing — the context tells you what kind of withness is meant before any theologian arrives to argue about it.

مَا يَكُونُ مِنْ نَجْوَى ثَلَاثَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ رَابِعُهُمْ وَلَا خَمْسَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ سَادِسُهُمْ وَلَا أَدْنَى مِنْ ذَلِكَ وَلَا أَكْثَرَ إِلَّا هُوَ مَعَهُمْ أَيْنَ مَا كَانُوا ثُمَّ يُنَبِّئُهُمْ بِمَا عَمِلُوا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ إِنَّ اللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ

"There is no secret counsel of three but He is the fourth of them, nor of five but He is the sixth of them, nor fewer than that nor more, but He is with them wherever they are. Then He will inform them of what they did on the Day of Rising. Indeed Allah is Knowing of everything."

Sūrat al-Mujādilah 58:7 · سُورَةُ المُجَادِلَة

الشَّرْح · the explanation

النَّجْوَى is the secret, so the meaning is: no private conferring of three comes to be… And إِلَّا هُوَ رَابِعُهُمْ وَلَا خَمْسَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ سَادِسُهُمْ means making Himself their fourth and their sixth — in the sense that He shares with them in being informed of that private counsel; He knows what they confer about and nothing of it is hidden from Him.

And why three and five in particular? al-Fawzān gives the two explanations: either the wording came out in terms of what is customary, or the cause of revelation was the private conferring of three in one incident and five in another. Otherwise He سُبْحَانَهُ is with every number, few or many — and the āyah says so itself: وَلَا أَدْنَى مِنْ ذَلِكَ وَلَا أَكْثَرَ, neither fewer than the number mentioned, such as one or two, nor more than it, such as six or seven.

The cause of revelation, as al-Fawzān reports it: the Jews used to confer privately among themselves, and the believers thought they were conferring about what would harm them, and grieved over it. When it went on and increased, they complained to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and he ordered the Jews not to confer privately apart from the Muslims. They did not stop, and went back to their private conferring — so Allah revealed these āyāt. The answer given to the believers' anxiety was not surveillance; it was: He is already the fourth of every three.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

أَيْنَ مَا كَانُوا — the meaning is the encompassing of His knowledge, سُبْحَانَهُ, of every private counsel that occurs from them in any place. Then ثُمَّ يُنَبِّئُهُمْ بِمَا عَمِلُوا — He will inform them, and recompense them for it; and in this is a threat to them and a rebuke.

And now the structural observation that settles the meaning of the whole āyah — مُقْتَضَاهَا العِلْم: this general maʿiyyah, whose entailment is knowledge. al-Fawzān points out that Allah opened the āyah with knowledge — أَلَمْ تَرَ أَنَّ اللَّهَ يَعْلَمُ — and sealed it with knowledge — إِنَّ اللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ. The maʿiyyah is enclosed on both sides by ʿilm. The verse interprets itself.

النَّوْعُ الثَّانِي · المَعِيَّةُ الخَاصَّة The Second Kind — the Particular Maʿiyyah

إِذْ يَقُولُ لِصَاحِبِهِ لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَنَا

"When he said to his companion: do not grieve — indeed Allah is with us."

Sūrat al-Tawbah 9:40 · سُورَةُ التَّوْبَة

الشَّرْح · the explanation

This is an address from the Prophet ﷺ to his companion Abū Bakr رضي الله عنه when the two of them were in the cave at the time of the Hijrah and the mushrikūn had caught up with them. So Abū Bakr grieved, out of fear for the Prophet ﷺ from the harm of the disbelievers.

Note what he grieved about: not himself. And the answer: لَا تَحْزَنْ — leave off the grieving — إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَنَا — with His aid, His help and His support.

And then al-Fawzān's two-step conclusion, which is as much consolation as it is doctrine: وَمَنْ كَانَ اللهُ مَعَهُ فَلَنْ يُغْلَبَ، وَمَنْ لَا يُغْلَبُ لَا يَحِقُّ لَهُ أَنْ يَحْزَنَ — and whoever has Allah with him will never be overcome; and whoever is not overcome, it is not fitting for him to grieve.

And the shāhid from the āyah: in it is the affirming of the particular maʿiyyah, belonging to the believers, whose entailment is victory and support.

قَالَ لَا تَخَافَا إِنَّنِي مَعَكُمَا أَسْمَعُ وَأَرَى

"He said: do not fear, I am with the two of you — I hear and I see."

Sūrat Ṭā-Hā 20:46 · سُورَةُ طَه

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Spoken to Mūsā and Hārūn عليهما السلام: do not fear Pharaoh. And إِنَّنِي مَعَكُمَا is a reason given for the prohibition — that is: with you both, by granting the two of you victory and by help against Pharaoh. Grammatically the āyah does not merely reassure; it explains the reassurance.

أَسْمَعُ — your speech and his speech; وَأَرَى — your place and his place; nothing of your affair is hidden from Me.

And the shāhid: the affirming of the particular maʿiyyah on Allah's part for His allies, by victory and support — as there is in it also the affirming of hearing and sight for Him سُبْحَانَهُ وَتَعَالَى. One āyah, carrying two sections of this book at once.

إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الَّذِينَ اتَّقَوْا وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ مُحْسِنُونَ

"Indeed Allah is with those who have taqwā, and those who are doers of good."

Sūrat al-Naḥl 16:128 · سُورَةُ النَّحْل

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān reads the two descriptions as the two halves of the religion. الَّذِينَ اتَّقَوْا — they abandoned the forbidden things and the acts of disobedience, in their differing kinds — and وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ مُحْسِنُونَ — by discharging the acts of obedience and undertaking what they were commanded with. Leaving and doing.

So He سُبْحَانَهُ is with these, by His support and His aid and His help; and this is a particular maʿiyyah, and it is the place of the shāhid from the noble āyah.

Which is the practical point of the whole section. The general maʿiyyah is not earned and cannot be escaped — He is the fourth of every three, including the three plotting against you. The particular maʿiyyah is conditional, and the āyah names the two conditions.

وَاصْبِرُوا إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ

"And be patient — indeed Allah is with the patient."

Sūrat al-Anfāl 8:46 · سُورَةُ الأَنْفَال

الشَّرْح · the explanation

وَاصْبِرُوا — this is a command to patience, and it is the restraining of the self. What is intended by it here is patience upon the hardships of the war that is between the Muslims and the disbelievers. Then He gave the reason for this command with His saying إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ — so He سُبْحَانَهُ is with the patient in every affair in which patience is called for.

And al-Fawzān closes it with al-Shawkānī, whose words are worth keeping as they stand: and how excellent is this maʿiyyah — the one granted it is not overcome by any overcomer, and its possessor is not got at from any direction, however many the directions be.

كَمْ مِنْ فِئَةٍ قَلِيلَةٍ غَلَبَتْ فِئَةً كَثِيرَةً بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ وَاللَّهُ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ

"How many a small company has overcome a large company, by the permission of Allah — and Allah is with the patient."

Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:249 · سُورَةُ البَقَرَة

الشَّرْح · the explanation

بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ — by His will, His decree and His volition. The small company does not win by being brave; it wins by permission. Take the بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ out of the āyah and it becomes a slogan; leave it in and it is tawḥīd.

وَاللَّهُ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ — this is the place of the shāhid from the noble āyah: the affirming of the maʿiyyah of Allah سُبْحَانَهُ for those patient upon jihād in His path; and it is a particular maʿiyyah whose entailment is victory and support.

مَا يُسْتَفَادُ مِنْ مَجْمُوعِ الآيَات What the Āyāt Yield Together

الشَّرْح · the explanation

المَعِيَّةُ نَوْعَان — they conveyed the affirming of the maʿiyyah, and that it is of two kinds:

المَعِيَّةُ العَامَّةas in the first two āyāt. Its entailment: His encompassing of His creation, and His knowledge of their deeds — the good of them and the evil — and His recompensing them for them.
المَعِيَّةُ الخَاصَّةwith His believing servants. Its entailment: victory, support and preservation. Indicated by the five remaining āyāt the author brought.

Two kinds, and only one of them is a privilege. Both are real, and neither is a figure of speech.

لَا تَنَافِي بَيْنَ المَعِيَّةِ وَالعُلُوّ No Contradiction Between the Maʿiyyah and the Highness

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Now the resolution, and al-Fawzān states it as a flat denial: وَمَعِيَّتُهُ سُبْحَانَهُ لَا تُنَافِي عُلُوَّهُ عَلَى خَلْقِه — and His maʿiyyah سُبْحَانَهُ does not contradict His highness above His creation and His istiwāʾ upon His Throne. He then gives two grounds.

الأَوَّل · His nearness is not the creature’s nearness. For His nearness سُبْحَانَهُ and His maʿiyyah are not like the nearness of the created thing and the maʿiyyah of a created thing with a created thing — because لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ البَصِيرُ (al-Shūrā 42:11). The same āyah that governed every attribute in Lesson 6 governs this one.

الثَّانِي · Maʿiyyah in the language does not mean contact. Because al-maʿiyyah is unrestricted accompaniment — it does not require touching, nor being on the same level. The Arabs say: مَا زِلْنَا نَمْشِي وَالقَمَرُ مَعَنَا — we kept walking and the moon was with us — even though it is above them, and the distance between them and it is far.

That example is the whole answer, and it costs nothing to understand. No Arab who says the moon was with us is claiming the moon walked beside him, and no Arab hearing it imagines he is. مَعَ is a word about accompaniment, not about position — so an āyah affirming it was never in competition with an āyah affirming height.

So the highness of Allah جَلَّ جَلَالُهُ and His maʿiyyah with His creation — there is no contradiction between the two of them. And more clarification of this is to come, إِنْ شَاءَ اللهُ.

Keep that last promise in view: the author returns to this. What has been settled here is the linguistic point. The fuller treatment of how the attributes are affirmed without taʿṭīl or tamthīl comes later in the book.

Check Your Understanding · MCQ أَسْئِلَة

What does al-Fawzān say the general maʿiyyah entails?

His presence in every place
His encompassing of His creation, His knowledge of their deeds, and His recompensing them
His victory and support for them
His touching of the creation

How does al-Mujādilah 58:7 interpret its own maʿiyyah?

By mentioning the Throne
By opening with knowledge and sealing with knowledge, so the maʿiyyah sits enclosed between them
By naming the Jews explicitly
By mentioning the Day of Rising

Why does the āyah mention three and five in particular?

Because those numbers are blessed
Either because the wording came out in terms of what is customary, or because the cause of revelation involved three in one incident and five in another
Because the plotters were exactly eight
To exclude larger numbers

What did Abū Bakr رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ grieve about in the cave?

For himself, fearing capture
Out of fear for the Prophet ﷺ from the harm of the disbelievers
For the believers left in Makkah
For the loss of his wealth

What are the two conditions of the particular maʿiyyah in al-Naḥl 16:128?

Knowledge and action
Taqwā — abandoning the forbidden — and iḥsān: discharging the obedience commanded
Hijrah and jihād
Patience and gratitude

Which āyah does al-Fawzān cite to show His nearness is unlike the creature’s?

وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ (al-Ikhlāṣ 112:4)
لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ (al-Shūrā 42:11)
اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ (al-Baqarah 2:255)
الرَّحْمَنُ عَلَى الْعَرْشِ اسْتَوَى (Ṭā-Hā 20:5)

What is the Arabs’ own example that maʿiyyah requires no contact?

"The sea is with the sailor"
"We kept walking and the moon was with us" — though it is above them and far off
"The shadow is with the man"
"The wind is with the traveller"
True or False صَحٌ أَمْ خَطَأٌ
المَعِيَّةُ تَقْتَضِي المُمَاسَّةَ وَالمُحَاذَاةَ. Maʿiyyah requires touching and being on the same level.
المَعِيَّةُ الخَاصَّةُ مُقْتَضَاهَا النَّصْرُ وَالتَّأْيِيدُ. The particular maʿiyyah entails victory and support.
مَعِيَّةُ اللهِ تُنَافِي عُلُوَّهُ عَلَى خَلْقِهِ. Allah’s maʿiyyah contradicts His highness above His creation.
مَنْ كَانَ اللهُ مَعَهُ فَلَنْ يُغْلَبَ. Whoever has Allah with him will never be overcome.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَنَا

Two men in a cave, the search party at the mouth of it, and the whole of the answer is a statement about Allah rather than about the odds. al-Fawzān's chain is worth carrying: whoever has Allah with him will never be overcome — and whoever is not overcome has no right to grieve.
