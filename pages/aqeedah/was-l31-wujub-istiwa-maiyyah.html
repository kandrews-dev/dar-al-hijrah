وُجُوبُ الإِيمَانِ بِالعُلُوِّ وَالمَعِيَّةِ مَعًا

The Obligation of Believing in the Highness and the Withness Together

شَرْحُ الشَّيْخِ صَالِحٍ الفَوْزَان · ص ١١٤–١١٦
Sharḥ al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah, Ṣāliḥ al-Fawzān — pp. 114–116.

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • State the objection the author is forestalling, in its own words.
  • Give all four of al-Fawzān’s answers to it.
  • Define المُتَوَاتِر as al-Fawzān defines it.
  • Say what الرَّقِيب and المُهَيْمِن each add to the question of nearness.
وُجُوبُ الإِيمَانِ بِاسْتِوَاءِ اللهِ عَلَى عَرْشِهِ وَعُلُوِّهِ وَمَعِيَّتِهِ وَأَنَّهُ لَا تَنَافِي بَيْنَهُمَا The Obligation of Believing in the Istiwāʾ, the ʿUluww and the Maʿiyyah — and That They Do Not Conflict

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَقَدْ دَخَلَ فِيمَا ذَكَرْنَاهُ مِنَ الإِيمَانِ بِاللهِ الإِيمَانُ بِمَا أَخْبَرَ اللهُ بِهِ فِي كِتَابِهِ وَتَوَاتَرَ عَنْ رَسُولِهِ ﷺ وَأَجْمَعَ عَلَيْهِ سَلَفُ الأُمَّةِ: مِنْ أَنَّهُ سُبْحَانَهُ فَوْقَ سَمَاوَاتِهِ عَلَى عَرْشِهِ، عَلِيٌّ عَلَى خَلْقِهِ، وَهُوَ سُبْحَانَهُ مَعَهُمْ أَيْنَمَا كَانُوا، يَعْلَمُ مَا هُمْ عَامِلُونَ… وَلَيْسَ مَعْنَى قَوْلِهِ ﴿وَهُوَ مَعَكُمْ﴾ أَنَّهُ مُخْتَلِطٌ بِالخَلْقِ، فَإِنَّ هَذَا لَا تُوجِبُهُ اللُّغَةُ، وَهُوَ خِلَافُ مَا أَجْمَعَ عَلَيْهِ سَلَفُ الأُمَّةِ، وَخِلَافُ مَا فَطَرَ اللهُ عَلَيْهِ الخَلْقَ؛ بَلِ القَمَرُ آيَةٌ مِنْ آيَاتِ اللهِ مِنْ أَصْغَرِ مَخْلُوقَاتِهِ، وَهُوَ مَوْضُوعٌ فِي السَّمَاءِ، وَهُوَ مَعَ المُسَافِرِ وَغَيْرِ المُسَافِرِ أَيْنَمَا كَانَ. وَهُوَ سُبْحَانَهُ فَوْقَ عَرْشِهِ رَقِيبٌ عَلَى خَلْقِهِ، مُهَيْمِنٌ عَلَيْهِمْ، مُطَّلِعٌ عَلَيْهِمْ… إِلَى غَيْرِ ذَلِكَ مِنْ مَعَانِي رُبُوبِيَّتِهِ.

al-Fawzān explains the author's motive for singling these two out: the author رَحِمَهُ اللهُ specified these two matters — the istiwāʾ upon the Throne, and His maʿiyyah with the creation — for a warning, in order to remove the difficulty, for one might imagine that there is a conflict between them. Parts 1 and 2 brought the texts; this section is about the student's own objection.

هُوَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍ ثُمَّ اسْتَوَى عَلَى الْعَرْشِ يَعْلَمُ مَا يَلِجُ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَمَا يَخْرُجُ مِنْهَا وَمَا يَنْزِلُ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ وَمَا يَعْرُجُ فِيهَا وَهُوَ مَعَكُمْ أَيْنَ مَا كُنْتُمْ وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ

"He it is who created the heavens and the earth in six days, then rose over the Throne. He knows what penetrates into the earth and what comes out of it, and what descends from the heaven and what ascends into it; and He is with you wherever you are — and Allah is Seeing of what you do."

Sūrat al-Ḥadīd 57:4 · سُورَةُ الحَدِيد

Notice why the author chose this āyah above all others for the purpose. It states both in one breath — the istiwāʾ upon the Throne, and «He is with you wherever you are» — as He gathered the two together in His saying… So whoever holds that the two conflict is holding that a single āyah contradicts itself.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

And here is the objection stated at full strength, as al-Fawzān puts it: one might suppose that this is like the attributes of the created, and that He is mixed with them —

— so how can He be above His creation, established upon His Throne, and be with His creation, near to them, without mixing?!

That is a fair question, and the author does not dismiss it. He answers it — and al-Fawzān lays the answer out in four distinct grounds, which are worth taking one at a time, because they come from four different places: the language · the consensus · the fiṭrah · and the transmitted texts.

الوَجْهُ الأَوَّل · اللُّغَةُ لَا تُوجِبُه First Ground — The Language Does Not Require It

الشَّرْح · the explanation

That this is not required by the language of the Arabs in which the Noble Qurʾān was sent down: for the word مَعَ in the language is for مُطْلَقِ المُصَاحَبَة — unrestricted accompaniment. It does not convey mixing or blending, nor adjacency, nor contact.

And al-Fawzān gives two examples from ordinary speech, the first of which is new here:

— for you say «زَوْجَتِي مَعِي», my wife is with me — while you are in one place and she is in another.

— and you say «مَا زِلْنَا نَسِيرُ وَالقَمَرُ مَعَنَا», we kept travelling and the moon was with us — and it is in the heaven; and it is with the traveller and the non-traveller, wherever he is.

The first example is the more telling of the two, because it is not even about distance in the sky. A man says «my wife is with me» while she is at home and he is at work, and no listener is confused. With is a word about relation, not about position — and the one who insists that مَعَ must mean mixed in among is making a claim about Arabic that no speaker of Arabic makes.

And then the argument from the lesser to the greater, which is al-Fawzān's habitual move: and if it is correct to say this concerning the moon — and it is a small created thing — then how should it not be said concerning the Creator, who is greater than everything?

الوَجْهُ الثَّانِي · خِلَافُ إِجْمَاعِ السَّلَف Second Ground — It Contradicts the Consensus of the Salaf

الشَّرْح · the explanation

That this statement contradicts what the predecessors of the Ummah were agreed upon — of the Ṣaḥābah, the Tābiʿūn and those who followed them (القُرُونُ المُفَضَّلَة — and they are the most excellent generations), who are the examples to be followed.

And al-Fawzān states what exactly they agreed on, in two clauses:

they were agreed that Allah is established upon His Throne, high above His creation, بَائِنٌ مِنْهُمْ — separate from them
and they were agreed that He is with His creation بِعِلْمِهِ — by His knowledge سُبْحَانَهُ وَتَعَالَى — as they explained His saying وَهُوَ مَعَكُمْ with exactly that

بَائِنٌ مِنْهُمْ is the word to keep from this ground. The Salaf did not merely affirm the height; they stated the separateness — which is the precise denial of the ḥulūliyyah position that Allah is indwelling in His creation. And they did not leave وَهُوَ مَعَكُمْ without a reading: they read it as His knowledge, which is what Lesson 17 found the two āyāt of the general maʿiyyah doing themselves.

الوَجْهُ الثَّالِث · خِلَافُ الفِطْرَة Third Ground — It Contradicts the Fiṭrah

الشَّرْح · the explanation

That this contradicts what Allah created the creation upon — that is, what He implanted in their very natures: for the creation were created upon the acknowledgement of Allah's highness above His creation.

And the evidence he gives for it is not a text at all. It is an observation about what people do:

For the creation turn toward Allah, at hardships and calamities, in the direction of HEIGHT — they do not turn to the right or to the left — without anyone having guided them to that. That is only by the requirement of the fiṭrah upon which Allah created people.

This is an argument anyone can test, and it does not require literacy. Watch where a person's hands and eyes go when something terrible happens. Not sideways, and not down. And the ones who do it have mostly never read a line of this book — which is the point: a doctrine that has to be taught against the grain of every instinct is a doctrine with a case to answer.

الوَجْهُ الرَّابِع · خِلَافُ الكِتَابِ وَالمُتَوَاتِرِ مِنَ السُّنَّة Fourth Ground — It Contradicts the Book and the Mutawātir Sunnah

الشَّرْح · the explanation

That this contradicts what Allah informed of in His Book and what was transmitted مُتَوَاتِرًا from His Messenger ﷺ — that He سُبْحَانَهُ وَتَعَالَى is upon His Throne, high above His creation, and He is with them wherever they are.

And since the author rested the matn on the word وَتَوَاتَرَ, al-Fawzān defines it — and this is a definition from مُصْطَلَحِ الحَدِيث worth learning here, where it is being used, rather than in the abstract:

المُتَوَاتِرُ مِنَ النُّصُوصِ: هُوَ مَا رَوَاهُ جَمَاعَةٌ تُحِيلُ العَادَةُ تَوَاطُؤَهُمْ عَلَى الكَذِبِ، عَنْ مِثْلِهِمْ، مِنَ الابْتِدَاءِ إِلَى الانْتِهَاءِ.

— the mutawātir among the texts is what a group transmitted whose colluding upon a lie is ruled out by ordinary experience, from a group like themselves, from the beginning of the chain to its end.

Read the three parts of it. A group large enough that collusion is not credible — not a count of narrators but a judgement about what human beings plausibly do. From a group like themselves — the breadth has to hold at every link. From beginning to end — a chain that is broad at one layer and narrows to one man at another is not mutawātir.

That is why it outranks even the ṣaḥīḥ report of Lesson 22: a ṣaḥīḥ ḥadīth asks you to trust the uprightness and precision of particular men, while a mutawātir text asks you to believe only that a crowd does not conspire.

And the āyāt and the aḥādīth on this are many — among them the āyah the author mentioned. وَاللهُ أَعْلَم.

الرَّقِيبُ وَالمُهَيْمِن al-Raqīb and al-Muhaymin

الشَّرْح · the explanation

The author closes the passage with وَهُوَ سُبْحَانَهُ فَوْقَ عَرْشِهِ رَقِيبٌ عَلَى خَلْقِهِ مُهَيْمِنٌ عَلَيْهِمْ مُطَّلِعٌ عَلَيْهِمْ, and al-Fawzān reads this as a confirming and an emphasising of what preceded — the height and the withness — by mentioning two of His names.

إِنَّ اللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَيْكُمْ رَقِيبًا

"Indeed Allah is ever Watchful over you."

Sūrat al-Nisāʾ 4:1 · سُورَةُ النِّسَاء

الشَّرْح · the explanation

الرَّقِيب — is the One who watches over the states of His servants; and in that is an indication of His nearness to them.

هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْمَلِكُ الْقُدُّوسُ السَّلَامُ الْمُؤْمِنُ الْمُهَيْمِنُ

"He is Allah, beside whom there is no god — the Sovereign, the Most Holy, the Author of Peace, the Granter of Security, the Overseer."

Sūrat al-Ḥashr 59:23 · سُورَةُ الحَشْر

الشَّرْح · the explanation

المُهَيْمِن — is the Witness over His creation, the One acquainted with their deeds, the Watcher over them.

Both names do the same work for the argument, and it is worth seeing how. A watchman is not the thing he watches. The very meaning of raqīb and muhaymin requires someone over something — so these two names affirm the nearness (He is attending to each of them) in language that presupposes He is not one of them. The author did not choose them at random.

إِلَى غَيْرِ ذَلِكَ مِنْ مَعَانِي رُبُوبِيَّتِه — al-Fawzān: that is, the requirement of His lordship سُبْحَانَهُ is that He be above His creation بِذَاتِهِ — by His very Self — and be acquainted with their deeds, and be near to them by His knowledge and His encompassing; disposing of their affairs, recording their deeds, and recompensing them for them.

So the whole difficulty dissolves into a distinction the student has now met in four different places: above them by His Self, with them by His knowledge. Not two halves of a compromise, but two true statements about different things.

True or False صَحٌ أَمْ خَطَأٌ
كَلِمَةُ «مَعَ» فِي اللُّغَةِ تُفِيدُ الاخْتِلَاطَ وَالمُمَاسَّةَ. The word maʿa in Arabic conveys mixing and contact.
أَجْمَعَ السَّلَفُ عَلَى أَنَّ اللهَ بَائِنٌ مِنْ خَلْقِهِ. The Salaf agreed that Allah is separate from His creation.
الخَلْقُ يَتَّجِهُونَ عِنْدَ الشَّدَائِدِ نَحْوَ العُلُوِّ بِمُوجَبِ الفِطْرَةِ. At calamities people turn upward by the requirement of the fiṭrah.
هُوَ سُبْحَانَهُ فَوْقَ خَلْقِهِ بِعِلْمِهِ، وَمَعَهُمْ بِذَاتِهِ. He is above His creation by His knowledge, and with them by His Self.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

عَالٍ عَلَى خَلْقِهِ بَائِنٌ مِنْهُمْ، وَهُوَ مَعَهُمْ بِعِلْمِهِ

The consensus of the Salaf in one line, and the whole answer to the difficulty: above them by His Self, with them by His knowledge. Two true statements about different things — which is why there was never a contradiction to resolve.
