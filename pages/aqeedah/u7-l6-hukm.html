Unit 7 · Lesson 6

الحكم بما أنزل الله

Ruling by What Allah Revealed

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know that judging by Allah's revelation is an obligation — and part of التوحيد.
  • Apply this to three levels: yourself, your home, and the wider world.
  • Learn the Salafi distinction between كفر أكبر (which puts one outside Islam) and كفر أصغر (which does not).
  • Know the rule: judgement of specific rulers is the work of qualified scholars.

The closing lesson of U7 — and a great test of التوحيد itself. التحاكم (turning to a judge for a verdict) is an act of worship: the one you turn to as the ultimate authority is the one you have, in that moment, taken as your Lord. Allah is the only true Lawgiver: «إِنِ الْحُكْمُ إِلَّا لِلَّهِ» ("Judgement belongs only to Allah" — يوسف 12:40).

وجوب التحاكم إلى شرع الله Part 1 — The obligation

فَلَا وَرَبِّكَ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ حَتَّىٰ يُحَكِّمُوكَ فِيمَا شَجَرَ بَيْنَهُمْ ثُمَّ لَا يَجِدُواْ فِىٓ أَنفُسِهِمْ حَرَجًا مِّمَّا قَضَيْتَ وَيُسَلِّمُواْ تَسْلِيمًا

"No — by your Lord — they do not believe until they make you the judge in what they dispute, then find no resistance in themselves to what you decide, and submit fully."

النساء 4:65 — Allah swears by Himself: real Iman requires this turning

Three things this verse demands: (1) turn to the Prophet ﷺ — and now to his Sunnah — as judge; (2) have no internal resistance to his ruling; (3) submit fully in action. Anything less is incomplete Iman.

وَأَنِ ٱحْكُم بَيْنَهُم بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ وَلَا تَتَّبِعْ أَهْوَآءَهُمْ

"And judge between them by what Allah has sent down, and do not follow their desires."

المائدة 5:49

ثلاثة مستويات Part 2 — Three levels you must apply it on
١. نفسك1. Your own self

The first حكم in your life is your inner verdict: when a desire pulls you one way and Allah's command pulls another, you submit to Allah. That submission is the daily التحاكم.

٢. بيتك2. Your home

Your family disputes — marriage, inheritance, raising children, business agreements — return to Allah's ruling, not to "what other families do" or "what the culture wants." This is the level over which a husband and a wife have real agency.

٣. المجتمع والدولة3. Society & the wider world

The rule of a Muslim land is by Allah's Sharīʿah. The believer wishes it for his society and prays for the rulers' uprightness — without rebellion against them; the Prophet ﷺ forbade rebellion as long as the ruler establishes the prayer and has not committed open kufr known by clear proof.

كفر أكبر وكفر أصغر Part 3 — The careful Salafi distinction

Allah said three times in سورة المائدة about those who do not rule by what He revealed:

وَمَن لَّمْ يَحْكُم بِمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ ... ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ ... ٱلْفَٰسِقُونَ

"…whoever does not judge by what Allah revealed — they are the disbelievers (الكافرون) … the wrongdoers (الظالمون) … the rebellious (الفاسقون)."

المائدة 5:44, 45, 47 — three verses on the same topic

The Companion Ibn ʿAbbās explained these verses with a careful distinction — followed by all the Salaf:

كفر أكبر — يُخرج من الملّةKufr akbar — removes a person from Islam

When the person rules by other than Allah's law while believing that the other law is better, or equal, or that Allah's law is unfit for the times, or denying Allah's right to rule. The kufr here is in the heart's belief.

كفر أصغر — لا يُخرج من الملّةKufr aṣghar — does NOT remove a person from Islam

When the person rules by other than Allah's law in a particular case — out of desire, bribery, fear, partiality — while still believing Allah's law is right and his own act is sinful. Ibn ʿAbbās said: "It is a kufr less than kufr, a ẓulm less than ẓulm, a fisq less than fisq."

قاعدة جامعة — Two non-negotiable rules

(1) Know the rule; don't apply it to individuals. Like the نواقض in U6 — the categories are clear from the texts, but judgement of this particular ruler, this state, this judge belongs to qualified scholars who know the person, the context, the conditions, and the barriers (الموانع).

(2) The Salafi path is not rebellion. Ibn Bāz and Ibn ʿUthaymīn (rh) both held that even where a ruler has fallen into kufr aṣghar — or worse — the people advise him privately, pray for him publicly, and do not khurūj (armed rebellion) which brings greater fitnah. This is the way of the Salaf, against the way of the Khawārij. Reform begins with reforming yourself.

للأسرة Part 5 — What this means for the family

The lesson here is not about politics. It is about where your heart turns when it must decide. Teach the children that «إِنِ الْحُكْمُ إِلَّا لِلَّهِ» starts inside them: when something is forbidden — even if their friends do it, even if their school normalizes it — they say "Allah said no." That is real التوحيد in action.

احفظ — Memorize

"Judgement belongs only to Allah." (يوسف 12:40) — the kalimah of every dispute. When the children face a hard choice, ask: "Who is the Judge here?" The answer leads them home.
