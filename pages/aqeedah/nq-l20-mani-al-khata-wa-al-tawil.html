مَوَانِعُ التَّكْفِير · الخَطَأ وَالتَّأْوِيل وَالخَاتِمَة

The Barriers to Takfīr — Error, Interpretation, and the Seal

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • State مَانِعُ الخَطَأ — the slip of the tongue from joy or anger — with the sharḥ’s own example, and why the absence of intent removes the ruling.
  • Give the daleel of honest error (al-Baqarah 2:286) and Ibn Taymiyyah’s principle that the scholars of the Muslims are not declared disbelievers for «مُجَرَّدِ الخَطَأِ المَحْض».
  • State Imām Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb’s ruling on التَّأْوِيلُ الفَاسِد of a clear text, with the case of Iblīs.
  • Use the Khawārij to show that sincere interpretation does not shield from مُرُوق when the text is clear.
  • Draw the line the other way — where taʾwīl does excuse (a place of ikhtilāf and shubhah) — with the sharḥ’s two worked cases.
  • Give the khātimah of the matn and Ibn Bāz’s gloss of «خَيْرِ خَلْقِه».
  • State, over all four barriers, the one principle the whole section was built to establish — the ruling on the act versus the ruling on the man.

مِنْ كَلَامِ الشَّيْخِ ابْنِ بَاز · ص ٢٧٢–٢٨٠
Subul al-Salām Sharḥ Nawāqiḍ al-Islām — pp. 272–280, gathering Ibn Bāz’s words on this clause.

مَانِعُ الخَطَأ The Barrier of Honest Error

The third barrier is the gentlest, and the easiest to picture. A word of kufr may leave a Muslim’s tongue with no will behind it at all — carried out by a surge of joy or grief or rage. He did not mean it, and he is not touched by it.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Ibn Bāz states it on the absence of intent: «قَدْ يَنْطِقُ المُسْلِمُ بِكَلِمَةِ الكُفْرِ لِغَلَبَةِ فَرَحٍ أَوْ غَضَبٍ أَوْ نَحْوِهِمَا، فَلَا يَكْفُرُ بِهَا؛ لِعَدَمِ القَصْد.»

The example is the man of the lost mount. He had despaired of it in the desert, then found it — and, «أَخْطَأَ مِنْ شِدَّةِ الفَرَح», meaning to say «O Allah, You are my Lord and I am Your servant», his tongue stumbled into: «اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ عَبْدِي وَأَنَا رَبُّك». The gravest inversion of words — and no kufr, because the heart behind them never moved toward it.

رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِن نَّسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا

Our Lord, do not take us to task if we forget or err.

سُورَةُ البَقَرَة · 2:286

الشَّرْح · the explanation

The footnote raises the barrier from a man to a principle. Ibn Taymiyyah: «اتَّفَقَ أَهْلُ السُّنَّةِ وَالجَمَاعَةِ عَلَى أَنَّ عُلَمَاءَ المُسْلِمِينَ لَا يَجُوزُ تَكْفِيرُهُمْ بِمُجَرَّدِ الخَطَأِ المَحْض؛ بَلْ كُلُّ أَحَدٍ يُؤْخَذُ مِنْ قَوْلِهِ وَيُتْرَك، إِلَّا رَسُولَ اللهِ ﷺ.» Over this verse, the sharḥ notes, the Prophet ﷺ reported that Allah said «قَدْ فَعَلْت» — I have done it. The error of a heart that sought the truth, he adds in Minhāj al-Sunnah, is nearer to Allah’s pardon than the deliberate sin of one who knew.

مَانِعُ التَّأْوِيل The Barrier of Interpretation — Cut Both Ways

This is the barrier most often turned into a skeleton key, so the sharḥ is careful to cut it on both edges. Not every interpretation excuses; and not every interpretation condemns. The whole skill is the line between them.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

One edge — a corrupt taʾwīl of a clear text is no excuse. Imām Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb: «التَّأْوِيلُ الفَاسِدُ فِي النُّصُوصِ لَيْسَ عُذْرًا لِصَاحِبِه؛ كَمَا أَنَّهُ سُبْحَانَهُ لَمْ يَعْذِرْ إِبْلِيسَ فِي شُبْهَتِهِ الَّتِي أَبْدَاهَا؛ بَلْ كَانَ ذَلِكَ التَّأْوِيلُ زِيَادَةً فِي الكُفْر»؛ وَ«الشُّبْهَةُ إِذَا كَانَتْ وَاضِحَةَ البُطْلَانِ لَا عُذْرَ لِصَاحِبِهَا».

Iblīs is the first case of «I had my reasoning»: he read his being made of fire against the plain command to prostrate — and the reading did not excuse him; it deepened the sin. «وَلَا يُقَاسُ الخَطَأُ فِي أَصْلِ الدِّينِ وَشَهَادَةِ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ بِالخَطَأِ فِي غَيْرِه.»

الشَّرْح · the explanation

The decisive case is the Khawārij — because no one ever doubted their sincerity. They «تَأَوَّلُوا النُّصُوصَ عَلَى غَيْرِ تَأْوِيلِهَا، فَقَاتَلُوا أَهْلَ الإِسْلَامِ وَتَرَكُوا أَهْلَ الأَوْثَان، وَكَفَّرُوا عَلِيًّا» — and the footnote says it plainly: «وَهُمْ بِالإِجْمَاعِ لَمْ يَفْعَلُوا مَا فَعَلُوا إِلَّا بِاجْتِهَادٍ وَتَقَرُّبٍ إِلَى اللهِ؛ فَلَمْ يَكُنِ التَّأْوِيلُ هُنَا مَانِعًا لَهُمْ مِنَ المُرُوق.»

And the Prophet ﷺ placed them, for all their worship, outside: «إِنَّهُمْ شَرُّ قَتْلَى تَحْتَ أَدِيمِ السَّمَاء، وَأَنَّهُمْ يَمْرُقُونَ مِنَ الإِسْلَامِ ثُمَّ لَا يَعُودُونَ إِلَيْه». So a sincere interpretation of a clear text is not a shield — it can be the very thing that carries a man through the religion and out the other side.

حَيْثُ يَكُونُ التَّأْوِيلُ عُذْرًا Where Interpretation Does Excuse

Now the other edge, so the barrier is not read as a hammer. Where the matter is a genuine place of scholarly difference, carrying a real doubt, the one who errs in it is not declared a disbeliever — though he may be mistaken, or sinning.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Q — شَيْخٌ أَفْتَى بِأَنَّ المَرْأَةَ الَّتِي تَسْتَحِلُّ كَشْفَ وَجْهِهَا — مَعَ عِلْمِهَا بِمَا وَرَدَ — تَكْفُر؟

Ibn Bāz — «الحُكْمُ بِكُفْرِهَا لَيْسَ بِجَيِّد؛ لِأَنَّهَا مَسْأَلَةُ خِلَافٍ بَيْنَ العُلَمَاءِ وَفِيهَا شُبْهَة؛ لَكِنَّهَا عَلَى كُلِّ حَالٍ تَكُونُ عَاصِيَةً بِذَلِك.» A disputed matter with a live doubt is a place of sin, not of takfīr.

Q — مَنْ أَنْكَرَ حَدِيثَ عَائِشَةَ فِي سِحْرِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ؟

Ibn Bāz — «لَا يَصِيرُ حُكْمُهُ حُكْمَ الكَفَرَة؛ لَهُ شُبْهَة؛ لَكِنْ يُعْلَمُ أَنَّهُ مُخْطِئ.» Mistaken, and told so — not cast out.

But the line holds firm where the matter is plain. One who denies the Dajjāl, or the descent of ʿĪsā, or Yaʾjūj and Maʾjūj — after the people of knowledge have made it clear to him and he persists — «فَيَكُونُ كَافِرًا... لَا شَكَّ فِي كُفْرِهِ وَلَا تَوَقُّف», only denial of al-Mahdī alone admitting تَوَقُّفًا. The doubt excuses where a doubt can honestly stand; it does not manufacture one over a matter known by necessity.

خَاتِمَةُ المَتْن The Seal of the Book

Imām Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb ends the whole matn not on a ruling but on fear and on ṣalāh upon the Messenger ﷺ.

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَكُلُّهَا مِنْ أَعْظَمِ مَا يَكُونُ خَطَرًا، وَمِنْ أَكْثَرِ مَا يَكُونُ وُقُوعًا، فَيَنْبَغِي لِلْمُسْلِمِ أَنْ يَحْذَرَهَا وَيَخَافَ مِنْهَا عَلَى نَفْسِه. نَعُوذُ بِاللهِ مِنْ مُوجِبَاتِ غَضَبِهِ وَأَلِيمِ عِقَابِه، وَصَلَّى اللهُ عَلَى خَيْرِ خَلْقِهِ مُحَمَّد.

And all of them are among the gravest of dangers and the most frequent in occurrence — so it befits the Muslim to beware of them and to fear them for himself. We seek refuge in Allah from the causes of His anger and the pain of His punishment; and may Allah send blessings upon the best of His creation, Muḥammad.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Ibn Bāz pauses on the last two words of the book — «خَيْرِ خَلْقِه». «هُوَ أَفْضَلُ الكَوْن، هُوَ أَفْضَلُ المَخْلُوقَات ﷺ، سَيِّدُ وَلَدِ آدَم، وَأَفْضَلُ الخَلْق، وَخَيْرُ البَرِيَّة ﷺ.»

There is a fitness in it. A book that walked, clause by clause, through the ten ways a heart can leave Islām, and then through the barriers that guard a man from being wrongly cast out, closes by turning the reader’s face to the one whose way is the opposite of every nullifier — and asking blessings upon him.

And this is the whole section in one line. Across four barriers — coercion, ignorance, honest error, interpretation — the book has taught a single discipline: the ruling on an act and the ruling on a man are two rulings. A deed may be a nullifier in its very type, and the person who did it may yet not be a disbeliever — because he was compelled, or ignorant of a hidden matter, or slipped, or held a doubt a doubt could honestly hold. The barriers are real. They are also only barriers: lift them, with a cause of takfīr established, and the ruling falls. To name a deed kufr is one thing; to pass judgement on a soul is another — and the book that was most willing to do the first spends its final pages teaching caution in the second.

«لَيْسَ عُذْرًا لِصَاحِبِه... بَلْ كَانَ ذَلِكَ التَّأْوِيلُ زِيَادَةً فِي الكُفْر» — as Iblīs was not excused by the doubt he raised
It is always a valid excuse
It excuses in every matter of creed

Why are the Khawārij the decisive proof that interpretation does not always excuse?

They «تَأَوَّلُوا النُّصُوصَ عَلَى غَيْرِ تَأْوِيلِهَا» and «بِالإِجْمَاع لَمْ يَفْعَلُوا مَا فَعَلُوا إِلَّا بِاجْتِهَادٍ وَتَقَرُّبٍ إِلَى الله» — yet their taʾwīl did not shield them from مُرُوق from Islām
They had no interpretation at all
They were excused and never left Islām
They interpreted correctly

What did the Prophet ﷺ say of the Khawārij, whom the text places inside this barrier?

«شَرُّ قَتْلَى تَحْتَ أَدِيمِ السَّمَاء... يَمْرُقُونَ مِنَ الإِسْلَامِ ثُمَّ لَا يَعُودُونَ إِلَيْه»
That they are the best of the ummah
That they are excused by their sincerity
That they must never be opposed

So when does interpretation excuse, and when does it not?

It excuses in a genuine place of ikhtilāf and shubhah; it does not excuse a corrupt taʾwīl of a text that is clear — «الشُّبْهَةُ إِذَا كَانَتْ وَاضِحَةَ البُطْلَانِ لَا عُذْرَ لِصَاحِبِهَا»
It always excuses, in every case
It never excuses, in any case
It excuses only kings and scholars

A shaykh ruled that a woman who uncovers her face believing it permitted, knowing the texts of ḥijāb, disbelieves. How did Ibn Bāz answer?

«الحُكْمُ بِكُفْرِهَا لَيْسَ بِجَيِّد؛ لِأَنَّهَا مَسْأَلَةُ خِلَافٍ بَيْنَ العُلَمَاءِ وَفِيهَا شُبْهَة؛ لَكِنَّهَا عَاصِيَةٌ بِذَلِك» — a disputed matter with a doubt is not a place of takfīr
He agreed she disbelieves
He said she commits no sin at all
He deferred the matter entirely

One who denies the ḥadīth of ʿĀʾishah on the Prophet ﷺ being bewitched — what is his ruling, by contrast with one who denies the Dajjāl or the descent of ʿĪsā?

The first «لَهُ شُبْهَة... يُعْلَمُ أَنَّهُ مُخْطِئ» — mistaken, not a disbeliever; but denying the Dajjāl or ʿĪsā’s descent after it is made clear and persisting «لَا شَكَّ فِي كُفْرِه»
Both are disbelievers immediately
Both are fully excused
Neither is ever ruled upon

Ibn Taymiyyah, in the footnote, states a principle of ahl al-sunnah on the scholars of the Muslims. What is it?

They «لَا يَجُوزُ تَكْفِيرُهُمْ بِمُجَرَّدِ الخَطَأِ المَحْض» — not declared disbelievers for mere honest error; each has his word taken and left, except the Messenger ﷺ
They may be declared disbelievers for any mistake
They are infallible like the Prophet ﷺ
Their errors are never corrected

The matn closes: «نَعُوذُ بِاللهِ مِنْ مُوجِبَاتِ غَضَبِهِ وَأَلِيمِ عِقَابِه، وَصَلَّى اللهُ عَلَى خَيْرِ خَلْقِهِ مُحَمَّد». How does Ibn Bāz gloss «خَيْرِ خَلْقِه»?

«هُوَ أَفْضَلُ الكَوْن... سَيِّدُ وَلَدِ آدَمَ وَأَفْضَلُ الخَلْقِ وَخَيْرُ البَرِيَّة ﷺ»
That he is one prophet among equals
That the phrase has no particular meaning
That it refers to the angels

Read together, what do the four barriers — coercion, ignorance, error, interpretation — establish about takfīr of a particular person?

That the ruling on an act and the ruling on a man are two rulings: the act’s name stands, but the named man is ruled a disbeliever only when a cause is present and every barrier is absent
That no act is really kufr
That no individual is ever a disbeliever
That sincerity alone decides every case
True or False صَحٌ أَمْ خَطَأٌ
مَنْ نَطَقَ بِكَلِمَةِ الكُفْرِ لِغَلَبَةِ فَرَحٍ أَوْ غَضَبٍ بِلَا قَصْدٍ لَا يَكْفُرُ بِهَا. One who utters a word of kufr through overwhelming joy or anger, without intent, does not disbelieve by it.
التَّأْوِيلُ الفَاسِدُ لِلنَّصِّ الوَاضِحِ عُذْرٌ لِصَاحِبِهِ عِنْدَ أَهْلِ السُّنَّة. A corrupt interpretation of a clear text is an excuse for its holder, in the view of ahl al-sunnah.
الخَوَارِجُ تَأَوَّلُوا النُّصُوصَ وَتَقَرَّبُوا بِاجْتِهَادِهِم، وَمَعَ ذَلِكَ مَرَقُوا مِنَ الإِسْلَام. The Khawārij interpreted the texts and drew near [to Allah] by their striving, and yet shot clean out of Islām.
المَرْأَةُ الَّتِي تَسْتَحِلُّ كَشْفَ وَجْهِهَا يُحْكَمُ بِكُفْرِهَا عِنْدَ ابْنِ بَاز. Ibn Bāz ruled that a woman who deems uncovering her face permissible disbelieves.
مَنْ أَنْكَرَ الدَّجَّالَ وَنُزُولَ عِيسَى بَعْدَ البَيَانِ وَأَصَرَّ فَلَا شَكَّ فِي كُفْرِه. One who denies the Dajjāl and the descent of ʿĪsā after clarification and persists — there is no doubt about his disbelief.
عُلَمَاءُ المُسْلِمِينَ يُكَفَّرُونَ بِمُجَرَّدِ الخَطَأِ المَحْض. The scholars of the Muslims are declared disbelievers for mere honest error.
خَتَمَ ابْنُ بَازٍ بِأَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ خَيْرُ خَلْقِ اللهِ، سَيِّدُ وَلَدِ آدَمَ وَأَفْضَلُ الخَلْق. Ibn Bāz sealed with: the Prophet ﷺ is the best of Allah’s creation, the master of the children of Ādam and the most excellent of creation.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

التَّأْوِيلُ الفَاسِدُ فِي النُّصُوصِ لَيْسَ عُذْرًا لِصَاحِبِه؛ كَمَا أَنَّهُ سُبْحَانَهُ لَمْ يَعْذِرْ إِبْلِيسَ فِي شُبْهَتِهِ الَّتِي أَبْدَاهَا، بَلْ كَانَ ذَلِكَ التَّأْوِيلُ زِيَادَةً فِي الكُفْر

Imām Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb, on the last of the four barriers — and the close of the whole book. Hold it beside its other edge. Interpretation excuses where a doubt can honestly stand — a real place of scholarly difference. It does not excuse a corrupt reading of a text that is clear; there, as with Iblīs reasoning against the plain command, the «interpretation» only deepens the sin. This is the whole discipline of the capstone: the barriers of coercion, ignorance, error and taʾwīl are real and they shield the man — but they are barriers, not a wall, and the ruling on an act was never the same as the ruling on a soul.
