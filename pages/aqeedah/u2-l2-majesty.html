أَسْمَاءُ العَظَمَةِ وَالمُلْك

The Names of Majesty & Sovereignty

Year 2+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Explain the five names of majesty — المَلِك، العَزِيز، الجَبَّار، المُتَكَبِّر، القَهَّار
  • Explain the two meanings of al-Jabbār (compelling and mending)
  • Understand why kibriyāʾ (pride) is a perfection for Allah but a flaw for creation
  • Describe how these names produce awe, humility, and freedom from fear of creation
العَظَمَةُ وَأَثَرُهَا فِي القَلْب Majesty — and What It Does to the Heart

The names of mercy fill the heart with hope and love. The names of majesty balance them with awe (الهيبة) and reverence. A believer worships Allah between hope and fear — like two wings of a bird. These five names teach us the greatness, power, and sovereignty of the One we worship.

Remarkably, three of them appear together in a single verse at the end of Sūrat al-Ḥashr — one of the greatest verses on Allah's names:

هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْمَلِكُ الْقُدُّوسُ السَّلَامُ ... الْعَزِيزُ الْجَبَّارُ الْمُتَكَبِّرُ ۚ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ

"He is Allah, other than whom there is no deity — the Sovereign, the Pure, the Source of Peace ... the Almighty, the Compeller, the Supreme. Exalted is Allah above whatever they associate with Him."

Sūrat al-Ḥashr 59:23 · سورة الحشر

المَلِك

al-Malik

The Sovereign King

م-ل-ك

al-Malik is the true King who owns all dominion — the heavens, the earth, and everything in them. Every other "king" is a borrower; their kingdom is small, temporary, and given to them. Allah's kingship is complete, absolute, and eternal. On the Day of Judgement every false claim of sovereignty collapses and the question rings out: لِمَنِ الْمُلْكُ الْيَوْمَ — "To whom belongs the dominion today?"

Evidence — الدليل

مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ

"Master of the Day of Judgement." — al-Fātiḥah 1:4

Effect on the heart — أثره

Frees the servant from servitude to people. The one whose King is Allah needs no other master.

العَزِيز

al-ʿAzīz

The Almighty / All-Mighty

ع-ز-ز

al-ʿAzīz joins three meanings: might that is never overcome, honour that has no equal, and strength that subdues all. He is the One who cannot be harmed, resisted, or defeated. From this name we also learn the source of true عِزّة (honour): "To Allah belongs all honour, and to His Messenger, and to the believers" (63:8).

Evidence — الدليل

وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ

"And He is the Almighty, the All-Wise." — repeated throughout the Quran

Effect on the heart — أثره

The believer seeks honour only through obedience to Allah — never through sin or pleasing creation.

الجَبَّار

al-Jabbār

The Compeller / The Restorer

ج-ب-ر

al-Jabbār carries two beautiful meanings from the same root. First, the One who compels — whose will overpowers all, so His decree is carried out without resistance. Second, from جَبْر (jabr — to set a broken bone), the One who mends and restores — who repairs the broken-hearted, enriches the poor, and relieves the distressed. So He is mighty over all, yet gentle to the broken.

Evidence — الدليل

الْعَزِيزُ الْجَبَّارُ الْمُتَكَبِّرُ

"...the Almighty, the Compeller, the Supreme." — al-Ḥashr 59:23

Effect on the heart — أثره

The broken turn to al-Jabbār to be mended. There is a prostration duʿāʾ: اللهم اجبرني — "O Allah, mend me."

المُتَكَبِّر

al-Mutakabbir

The Supreme / Possessor of all Greatness

ك-ب-ر

al-Mutakabbir is the One to whom all greatness truly belongs — who is far above every deficiency, fault, and need. Here is a crucial point: الكِبْر (pride) is blameworthy in creation because a creature has nothing to be proud of — but it is a perfection for Allah, because greatness is His by right. In a ḥadīth qudsī Allah says: «الكِبْرِيَاءُ رِدَائِي وَالعَظَمَةُ إِزَارِي» — "Pride is My cloak and greatness is My garment."

Evidence — الدليل

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ

al-Ḥashr 59:23 — listed among the names of majesty

Effect on the heart — أثره

Teaches humility: since greatness belongs to Allah alone, arrogance in a servant is a theft of what is His.

القَهَّار

al-Qahhār

The Subduer / The Dominant

ق-ه-ر

al-Qahhār is the One who dominates and subdues all things — everything in existence is utterly under His control and bends to His power. Nothing escapes Him; all creatures are subdued before His might. In the Quran this name is almost always paired with الوَاحِد (the One) — al-Wāḥid al-Qahhār — joining His oneness with His total dominance.

Evidence — الدليل

وَهُوَ الْوَاحِدُ الْقَهَّارُ

"And He is the One, the Subduer." — al-Raʿd 13:16

Effect on the heart — أثره

Produces reliance on Allah and fearlessness of creation — for all created powers are themselves subdued by Him.

تَدْرِيبُ القَوَاعِدGrammar Drill — The Intensive Pattern فَعَّال

Two names in this lesson — الجَبَّار and القَهَّار — share the intensive pattern فَعَّال (faʿʿāl), one of the ṣiyagh al-mubālaghah (forms of intensity). The doubled middle letter (شدّة) adds the meaning of abundance and overwhelming degree. Identify each name's root.

التَّمَارِينExercises
Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
المَلِك الحق هو الله، وملك المخلوقين عارية ومؤقت. The true King is Allah; the kingship of creatures is borrowed and temporary.
الجَبَّار يأتي بمعنى القهر وبمعنى جبر الكسير. al-Jabbār means both the One who compels and the One who mends the broken.
الكِبْر صفة مذمومة في حق الله كما في حق المخلوق. Pride (kibr) is blameworthy for Allah just as it is for creation.
اجتمعت أسماء العَزِيز والجَبَّار والمُتَكَبِّر في آية الحشر. The names al-ʿAzīz, al-Jabbār, and al-Mutakabbir appear together in the verse of al-Ḥashr.
العزة الحقيقية تُنال بمعصية الله وإرضاء الناس. True honour (ʿizzah) is gained by disobeying Allah and pleasing people.
