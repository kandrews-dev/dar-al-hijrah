نَوَاقِضُ الإِسْلَامِ العَشَرَة

The Ten Nullifiers of Islam

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the ten matters Imām Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb listed as nullifiers of Islam.
  • See the proof from the Qur'an for each.
  • Understand why each one removes a person from the religion.
  • Know the rule: judgement of any particular individual is left to scholars.

Nawāqiḍ al-Islām is a short, weighty treatise by Imām Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb listing ten matters that — if a person falls into them — nullify their Islam, so they may guard against them. Shaykh Ibn Bāz wrote a careful sharḥ on it. The Imam said in his opening: "Know that the nullifiers of Islam are ten…" The point of studying them is not to throw takfīr at others — that judgement belongs to scholars — but to guard your own Islam.

الأَوَّل: الشِّرْك1. Shirk in Worship

To associate a partner with Allah in His worship — like calling on the dead, slaughtering for them, making vows to them, or directing any worship to other than Allah.

إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يَغْفِرُ أَن يُشْرَكَ بِهِ وَيَغْفِرُ مَا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَن يَشَاءُ

"Indeed Allah does not forgive partners being set up with Him, but He forgives what is besides that for whom He wills."

al-Nisāʾ 4:48 — and 4:116

الثَّانِي: الوَسَائِط2. Taking Intermediaries

Whoever takes intermediaries between himself and Allah — calling on them, seeking their intercession, relying on them — has disbelieved by the consensus. This is the very excuse the mushrikīn gave (recall U5 Foundation 2): "We only call on them to bring us nearer to Allah." The road to shirk starts here.

وَيَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ مَا لَا يَضُرُّهُمْ وَلَا يَنفَعُهُمْ وَيَقُولُونَ هَٰؤُلَاءِ شُفَعَاؤُنَا عِندَ اللَّهِ

"And they worship besides Allah that which harms them not nor benefits them, and they say: 'These are our intercessors with Allah.'"

Yūnus 10:18

الثَّالِث: عَدَمُ تَكْفِيرِ المُشْرِكِين3. Not Declaring the Mushrikīn to be Disbelievers

Whoever does not declare the mushrikīn to be disbelievers, or doubts their disbelief, or declares their way correct — has himself disbelieved. The Qur'an calls them kuffār plainly; to disagree is to contradict Allah's verdict. (Ibn Bāz notes: this applies to those whose disbelief is clear and established by the texts.)

الرَّابِع: تَفْضِيلُ غَيْرِ هَدْيِهِ ﷺ4. Believing Other Guidance is Better Than the Prophet's ﷺ

Whoever believes that some guidance other than the Prophet's ﷺ is more complete, or that another's judgement is better than his — like those who prefer the laws of tyrants, or claim modern philosophies surpass the Sharīʿah — has disbelieved.

وَمَن يَبْتَغِ غَيْرَ الْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا فَلَن يُقْبَلَ مِنْهُ

"And whoever seeks a religion other than Islam — it will never be accepted from him."

Āl ʿImrān 3:85

الخَامِس: بُغْضُ شَيْءٍ مِمَّا جَاءَ بِهِ الرَّسُولُ ﷺ5. Hating Anything the Messenger ﷺ Brought

Whoever hates anything the Messenger ﷺ came with — even while practicing it — has disbelieved. To resent a ruling of Allah, even one you obey, is to oppose Him in your heart.

ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّهُمْ كَرِهُوا مَا أَنزَلَ اللَّهُ فَأَحْبَطَ أَعْمَالَهُمْ

"That is because they hated what Allah sent down — so He nullified their deeds."

Muḥammad 47:9

السَّادِس: الاسْتِهْزَاءُ بِالدِّين6. Mocking Anything from the Religion

Whoever mocks anything from the religion of the Messenger ﷺ, or its reward, or its punishment, has disbelieved. The proof is the verse about the hypocrites who mocked the Companions on the way to Tabūk:

قُلْ أَبِاللَّهِ وَآيَاتِهِ وَرَسُولِهِ كُنتُمْ تَسْتَهْزِئُونَ ۝ لَا تَعْتَذِرُوا قَدْ كَفَرْتُم بَعْدَ إِيمَانِكُمْ

"Say: Was it at Allah, His verses and His Messenger that you were mocking? Make no excuse — you have disbelieved after your faith."

al-Tawbah 9:65–66

السَّابِع: السِّحْر7. Magic (Siḥr)

Magic — both learning it and practising it. Whoever is pleased with it or does it has disbelieved. Of magic is ṣarf (turning a person's love from his spouse) and ʿaṭf (turning love to another).

وَمَا يُعَلِّمَانِ مِنْ أَحَدٍ حَتَّىٰ يَقُولَا إِنَّمَا نَحْنُ فِتْنَةٌ فَلَا تَكْفُرْ

"And they (the two angels) did not teach anyone until they said: 'We are only a trial, so do not disbelieve.'"

al-Baqarah 2:102

الثَّامِن: مُظَاهَرَةُ المُشْرِكِينَ وَمُعَاوَنَتُهُم8. Supporting the Mushrikīn against the Muslims

Backing the mushrikīn and aiding them against the Muslims — taking their side in war and in opposition to Islam. The proof:

وَمَن يَتَوَلَّهُم مِّنكُمْ فَإِنَّهُ مِنْهُمْ ۗ إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يَهْدِي الْقَوْمَ الظَّالِمِينَ

"And whoever takes them as allies from among you — then he is one of them. Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people."

al-Māʾidah 5:51

التَّاسِع: اعْتِقَادُ أَنَّ بَعْضَ النَّاسِ يَسَعُهُ الخُرُوجُ عَنْ شَرِيعَتِهِ ﷺ9. Believing One May Be Exempt From the Prophet's ﷺ Sharīʿah

Believing some people can be exempt from following the Sharīʿah of Muḥammad ﷺ — as some Sufi extremists claim about their "saints" (al-Khiḍr and others). The Prophet ﷺ said: "By the One in whose Hand is my soul — were Mūsā alive among you, he would have no choice but to follow me." (Aḥmad, Dārimī.) The Sharīʿah binds everyone.

العَاشِر: الإِعْرَاضُ عَنْ دِينِ اللَّه10. Turning Away From Allah's Religion

Turning away from the religion of Allah — neither learning it nor acting upon it. Not the one who is too busy with daily life and learns what he can, but the one who knowingly refuses to learn or act on the religion as a whole.

وَمَنْ أَظْلَمُ مِمَّن ذُكِّرَ بِآيَاتِ رَبِّهِ ثُمَّ أَعْرَضَ عَنْهَا ۚ إِنَّا مِنَ الْمُجْرِمِينَ مُنتَقِمُونَ

"And who is more unjust than one who is reminded of his Lord's verses and then turns away from them? Indeed, We will take retribution from the criminals."

al-Sajdah 32:22

A vital closing — قاعدة مهمة

Imām Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb closed with: "There is no difference in any of these nullifiers between the one who jokes, the one who is serious, and the one who is fearful — except the one who is forced." A joke is not an excuse. The exception is genuine coercion.

Two essentials to keep in mind as you study this: (1) the purpose is to guard your own Islam, not to police others. (2) Applying these to a specific person (takfīr al-muʿayyan) requires conditions and barriers known only to qualified scholars — ignorance, misunderstanding, coercion, and so on. Know the rule; leave the verdict to the scholars.
