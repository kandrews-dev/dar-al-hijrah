أسماء تُقرّبنا Names That Draw Us Near

We end this unit with names of generosity, provision, and forgiveness — and then learn how to actually live by all the names we have studied. Recall from L.0 that the third and highest level of iḥṣāʾ is to act upon the names. A name we know but never call upon, and never let change us, has not truly been "encompassed."

وَلِلَّهِ الْأَسْمَاءُ الْحُسْنَىٰ فَادْعُوهُ بِهَا

"And to Allah belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them."

Sūrat al-Aʿrāf 7:180 · سورة الأعراف

الرَّزَّاق

al-Razzāq

The Ever-Providing

ر-ز-ق

al-Razzāq (intensive form فَعَّال) is the One who provides for every creature, again and again, without end — the food of bodies and the nourishment of hearts (knowledge, faith, contentment). No creature moves on earth except that its provision is upon Allah (11:6). He provides for the believer and the disbeliever, the grateful and the ungrateful.

Evidence — الدليل

إِنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ الرَّزَّاقُ ذُو الْقُوَّةِ الْمَتِينُ

"Indeed Allah is the Ever-Providing, Owner of Power, the Firm." — al-Dhāriyāt 51:58

Living by it — العمل به

Seek provision through lawful means and trust (tawakkul); never disobey Allah out of fear of poverty.

الغَفُور

al-Ghafūr

The All-Forgiving

غ-ف-ر

al-Ghafūr is the One who forgives abundantly and conceals sins — for the root غ-ف-ر means both to pardon and to cover. He forgives the greatest sins for the one who repents, and conceals the faults of His servants rather than exposing them. Despair of His forgiveness is itself a grave error: "Do not despair of the mercy of Allah" (39:53).

Evidence — الدليل

إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا

"Indeed Allah forgives all sins." — al-Zumar 39:53

Living by it — العمل به

Never despair; seek forgiveness often (istighfār); and conceal others' faults as you hope Allah conceals yours.

التَّوَّاب

al-Tawwāb

The Ever-Relenting / Acceptor of Repentance

ت-و-ب

al-Tawwāb is the One who turns again and again to His servants in acceptance — التوبة means "to return." Beautifully, Allah turns to the servant twice: He first grants the servant the success to repent, then He accepts that repentance. However many times a servant falls and returns, al-Tawwāb keeps accepting.

Evidence — الدليل

إِنَّهُ هُوَ التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ

"Indeed He is the Acceptor of Repentance, the Merciful." — al-Baqarah 2:37

Living by it — العمل به

Return immediately after every slip; never let a sin become a habit through despair of acceptance.

الكَرِيم

al-Karīm

The Most Generous / Noble

ك-ر-م

al-Karīm joins generosity and nobility. He gives without being asked, gives before being thanked, and is not diminished by giving. His generosity is so vast that He is shy to turn away empty the hands a servant raises to Him in duʿāʾ. He is also noble — exalted above every fault.

Evidence — الدليل

يَا أَيُّهَا الْإِنسَانُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ الْكَرِيمِ

"O man, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Most Generous?" — al-Infiṭār 82:6

Living by it — العمل به

Be generous to others, expect good from Allah (ḥusn al-ẓann), and feel shame to disobey such a Generous Lord.

الوَهَّاب

al-Wahhāb

The Bestower of Gifts

و-ه-ب

al-Wahhāb (intensive فَعَّال) is the One who gives freely and continuously, expecting nothing in return — for a هبة (hibah) is a gift given without price or condition. He bestows guidance, children, mercy, and every blessing purely out of His grace. The believers pray to Him with His own name: "...and grant us from Yourself mercy. Indeed, You are the Bestower."

Evidence — الدليل

وَهَبْ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحْمَةً ۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ الْوَهَّابُ

"...grant us mercy from Yourself; indeed, You are the Bestower." — Āl ʿImrān 3:8

Living by it — العمل به

Ask Allah generously for every need — His treasures never run out; and give to others without seeking return.

كيف نعبد الله بأسمائه؟ How Do We Worship Allah Through His Names?

The command فَادْعُوهُ بِهَا ("call upon Him by them") includes two kinds of calling:

دعاء المسألة

Calling to ask

Pair your request with the fitting name: "O Razzāq, provide for me", "O Ghafūr, forgive me", "O Tawwāb, accept my repentance."

دعاء العبادة

Calling through worship

Let each name move you: al-Raqīb → watchfulness, al-Karīm → generosity, al-Ghafūr → forgiving others. The name shapes the deed.

A warning (from L.0): Calling upon Allah by His names is worship — and worship belongs to Allah alone. We never call upon the dead, saints, or anyone besides Allah by these names. That would be the very shirk we studied in Unit 1. The names draw us to Allah directly, with no intermediary.

طبّق الاسم — Apply the Name Which Name Fits the Situation?

For each situation, tap the name a believer would most fittingly call upon. Feedback appears after you choose.

1. A father has lost his job and is anxious about feeding his family. Which name does he turn to?

al-Razzāq — the Ever-Providing. He seeks lawful means and trusts that his provision is with Allah (11:6), without disobeying Allah out of fear of poverty.

2. A young man feels crushed by guilt over past sins and fears he is beyond forgiveness. Which name reassures him?

al-Ghafūr — the All-Forgiving. "Indeed Allah forgives all sins" (39:53). Despair itself is the error; he turns back with istighfār.

3. Someone has repented before but slipped again, and wonders if it is even worth returning. Which name answers him?

al-Tawwāb — the One who keeps accepting return, however many times the servant comes back. He returns to Allah immediately rather than letting the sin settle.

4. A student is alone and could easily cheat without anyone knowing. Which name should be present in his heart?

al-Raqīb — the Ever-Watchful (from L.3). Though no human sees him, Allah does. This murāqabah is the heart of iḥsān.
