وُجُوبُ الإِيمَانِ بِالرُّؤْيَةِ · وَمَوَاضِعُهَا

The Obligation of Believing in the Seeing, and Where It Occurs

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Give al-Fawzān’s argument for why denying the ruʾyah touches three pillars of faith.
  • Say what عِيَانًا establishes, and against whom.
  • Define العَرَصَات and name the two places of the seeing.
  • State the three positions on who sees Him in the first place — without resolving them.
وُجُوبُ الإِيمَانِ بِرُؤْيَةِ المُؤْمِنِينَ رَبَّهُمْ · وَمَوَاضِعُ الرُّؤْيَة The Obligation of Believing in the Ruʾyah — and Where It Occurs

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَقَدْ دَخَلَ أَيْضًا فِيمَا ذَكَرْنَاهُ مِنَ الإِيمَانِ بِهِ وَبِكُتُبِهِ وَبِرُسُلِهِ: الإِيمَانُ بِأَنَّ المُؤْمِنِينَ يَرَوْنَهُ عِيَانًا بِأَبْصَارِهِمْ، كَمَا يَرَوْنَ الشَّمْسَ صَحْوًا لَيْسَ دُونَهَا سَحَابٌ، وَكَمَا يَرَوْنَ القَمَرَ لَيْلَةَ البَدْرِ لَا يُضَامُّونَ فِي رُؤْيَتِهِ. يَرَوْنَهُ سُبْحَانَهُ وَهُمْ فِي عَرَصَاتِ القِيَامَةِ، ثُمَّ يَرَوْنَهُ بَعْدَ دُخُولِ الجَنَّةِ، كَمَا يَشَاءُ اللهُ.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān begins with the question the author's wording raises: why is belief in the seeing placed inside belief in Allah, His Books and His Messengers? And the answer is an argument worth having, because it applies to every item of creed established by report:

That Allah سُبْحَانَهُ informed of it in His Book, and His Messenger ﷺ informed of it — so whoever does not believe in it is belying Allah, and His Books, and His Messengers. For the one who believes in Allah and His Books and His Messengers believes in everything they informed of.

Follow the structure of it. Faith in a messenger is not a feeling about him; it is the acceptance of what he reported. So a man cannot say «I believe in Allah and His Book and His Prophet» and then set aside a thing all three of them stated. The denial is not a separate error standing beside his faith — it is a contradiction inside it.

And that is why the author did not give the ruʾyah a pillar of its own. It is not an extra article; it is a test of whether the three he already listed were meant.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

عِيَانًا — with a kasrah on the ع; that is: a realised seeing in which there is no obscurity — so it is not figurative, as the muʿaṭṭilah say.

One word, and it closes the only escape left. Everything in Lessons 21 and 29 established that He is seen; عِيَانًا establishes that the seeing is seeing — not a knowing, not an unveiling of the heart, not a manner of speaking about nearness. The author put it in because he knew where the reinterpretation would go.

كَمَا يَرَوْنَ الشَّمْسَ صَحْوًا لَيْسَ دُونَهَا سَحَابٌ، وَكَمَا يَرَوْنَ القَمَرَ لَيْلَةَ البَدْرِ — that is: a real seeing in which there is no hardship, as the āyāt and the aḥādīth indicated, whose explanation came before.

Keep Lesson 29's clause fixed to both comparisons: تَشْبِيهٌ لِلرُّؤْيَةِ بِالرُّؤْيَةِ، لَا تَشْبِيهٌ لِلْمَرْئِيِّ بِالمَرْئِيّ — a likening of the seeing to the seeing, not of the One seen to the thing seen. The sun and the moon are named for one property only — that you look at them and there is nothing in the way.

مَوْضِعَانِ لِلرُّؤْيَة Two Places of the Seeing

This is a statement of the places in which the seeing occurs, and that is in two places. The author is not merely affirming the doctrine again; he is locating it — and the two locations are not the same event.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

المَوْضِعُ الأَوَّل — فِي عَرَصَاتِ القِيَامَة. And the word first: العَرَصَات is the plural of عَرْصَة, and it is the wide place in which there is no building; and «the ʿaraṣāt of the Resurrection» are مَوَاقِفُ الحِسَاب — the standing-places of the Reckoning.

An open plain with nothing built on it — which is the picture the word carries, and part of why the two comparisons of the sun and the moon fit: in that place there is nothing in the way.

And is the seeing in this place particular to the believers? On the question there are three statements:

١it has been said: He is seen in the ʿaraṣāt by the believers, the hypocrites and the disbelievers
٢and it has been said: by the believers and the hypocrites only, not the disbelievers
٣and it has been said: by the believers only

al-Fawzān states the three and closes with وَاللهُ أَعْلَم — and Allah knows best. He does not choose between them, and neither does this lesson. It is worth noticing that he can leave a question open here without any loss: the doctrine — that the believers see their Lord — is settled by mutawātir texts and consensus, and this is a question about who else, at one of the two places. Being precise about what is established is also being precise about what is not.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

المَوْضِعُ الثَّانِي — the believers see Him after their entering the Garden, as that is established in the proofs from the Book and the Sunnah; and some of those proofs have been mentioned with their explanation before — Lesson 21's وَزِيَادَةٌ and وَلَدَيْنَا مَزِيدٌ, and Lesson 29's ḥadīth of the full moon — and the objections of those who deny the seeing, along with the refutation of them, were also mentioned.

And on the word الجَنَّة: in the language it is the garden, the orchard; and what is intended by it here is the abode which Allah has prepared for His allies — and it is the abode of bliss that is absolute and complete.

Absolute and complete is not decoration. It is the reason the ziyādah of Lesson 21 had to be the looking at His Face: a bliss described as complete cannot have its best thing withheld.

كَمَا يَشَاءُ اللهُ "As Allah Wills"

الشَّرْح · the explanation

The author ends the fasl on three words, and al-Fawzān will not let them pass as a pious flourish: كَمَا يَشَاءُ اللهُ — that is: مِنْ غَيْرِ إِحَاطَةٍ وَلَا تَكْيِيفٍ لِرُؤْيَتِه — without any encompassing of Him, and without any describing of the manner of the seeing of Him.

Both halves of that gloss have been earned earlier in the track, and it is worth seeing them land together:

مِنْ غَيْرِ إِحَاطَةLesson 21: لَا تُدْرِكُهُ الْأَبْصَارُ negates encompassing, not seeing — they see Him and do not take Him in
وَلَا تَكْيِيفLesson 4: the third of the four closed doors — and impossible rather than merely forbidden, since no one has the knowledge it would require

So the final clause of the section applies the book's whole method to its last doctrine. The seeing is real, with the eyes, without obscurity — against the muʿaṭṭilah. And it is not comprehended and not pictured — against the mushabbihah, and against our own curiosity. Affirm, and do not ask how.

With this the author closes Part 3. The standing of Ahl al-Sunnah among the sects; the obligation of holding the highness and the withness together; the meaning of His being «in the heaven»; the obligation of holding His nearness; that the Qurʾān is His speech in reality; and the obligation of holding the seeing. Six sections, and every one of them is a wājib rather than a proof — because Parts 1 and 2 had already brought the proofs. Part 3 is where the reader is told what he is now responsible for.

True or False صَحٌ أَمْ خَطَأٌ
مَنْ لَمْ يُؤْمِنْ بِالرُّؤْيَةِ كَانَ مُكَذِّبًا لِلَّهِ وَلِكُتُبِهِ وَلِرُسُلِهِ. Whoever does not believe in the seeing is belying Allah, His Books and His Messengers.
الرُّؤْيَةُ مَجَازٌ لَا حَقِيقَةٌ. The seeing is figurative, not real.
رَجَّحَ الفَوْزَانُ أَحَدَ الأَقْوَالِ الثَّلَاثَةِ فِي العَرَصَاتِ. al-Fawzān favoured one of the three positions on the ʿaraṣāt.
يَرَى المُؤْمِنُونَ رَبَّهُمْ مِنْ غَيْرِ إِحَاطَةٍ وَلَا تَكْيِيفٍ. The believers see their Lord without encompassing Him and without a described manner.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

يَرَوْنَهُ سُبْحَانَهُ وَهُمْ فِي عَرَصَاتِ القِيَامَةِ، ثُمَّ يَرَوْنَهُ بَعْدَ دُخُولِ الجَنَّةِ، كَمَا يَشَاءُ اللهُ

Two places, and then three words that govern both. Learn the ending with the rest or you will be tempted to picture it: as Allah wills — without encompassing Him, and without asking how.
