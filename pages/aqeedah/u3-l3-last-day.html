Belief in the Last Day

Year 2+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Describe the life of the grave (barzakh) and its questioning

الركن الخامس: الإيمان باليوم الآخر Pillar 5 — Belief in the Last Day

The Last Day (اليوم الآخر) is the Day of Resurrection, when all people are raised and judged. Belief in it includes everything Allah and His Messenger ﷺ told us comes after death: the life of the grave, the resurrection, the gathering, the reckoning, the scales, the bridge, and the final destination — Paradise or Hell. So central is this belief that the Quran almost always pairs it with belief in Allah: "...whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day."

وَأَنَّ السَّاعَةَ آتِيَةٌ لَّا رَيْبَ فِيهَا وَأَنَّ اللَّهَ يَبْعَثُ مَن فِي الْقُبُورِ

"And that the Hour is coming — there is no doubt about it — and that Allah will resurrect those in the graves."

Sūrat al-Ḥajj 22:7 · سورة الحج

الحياة في البرزخ The Life of the Grave (al-Barzakh)

Between death and resurrection lies the barzakh — the life of the grave. Recall from L.1 that two angels question the deceased. The Prophet ﷺ taught they ask three questions:

مَن رَبُّكَ؟

"Who is your Lord?"

مَا دِينُكَ؟

"What is your religion?"

مَن نبيُّك؟

"Who is your prophet?"

The believer, whom Allah keeps firm, answers: "Allah is my Lord, Islam is my religion, Muhammad ﷺ is my prophet." The grave then becomes a garden from Paradise. This is why everything in Units 1–3 matters — these three answers are the whole curriculum. The grave is either a garden of bliss or a pit of punishment (40:46).

تسلسل أحداث اليوم الآخر The Sequence of the Last Day

The major events, in order:

النَّفْخُ في الصُّور

1. The Blowing of the Trumpet

Isrāfīl (from L.1) blows it: the first blast ends the world; the second raises the dead.

البَعْث

2. The Resurrection (al-Baʿth)

All people are raised from their graves, barefoot and uncircumcised, exactly as first created.

الحَشْر

3. The Gathering (al-Ḥashr)

All creation is gathered on one plain; the sun draws near and people sweat according to their deeds.

الحِسَاب

4. The Reckoning (al-Ḥisāb)

Each person is questioned and shown their record — given in the right hand (success) or the left (loss).

المِيزَان

5. The Scales (al-Mīzān)

Deeds are weighed with perfect justice: "We place the scales of justice for the Day of Resurrection." (21:47)

الحَوْض والشَّفَاعَة

6. The Pool & the Intercession

The believers drink from the Prophet's Pool (al-Ḥawḍ); he ﷺ is granted the Great Intercession.

الصِّرَاط

7. The Bridge (al-Ṣirāṭ)

A bridge over Hell that all must cross — people pass according to their deeds, to Paradise beyond it.

الجنة والنار Paradise & Hell — Real and Everlasting

الجَنَّة

al-Jannah — Paradise

The eternal abode of the believers — delights "no eye has seen, no ear has heard." Its greatest joy is seeing the Face of Allah.

النَّار

al-Nār — Hellfire

The abode of punishment for those who died upon disbelief and shirk (recall Unit 1) — a severe and lasting torment.

Both are real, created, and exist now; they do not perish. A believer who entered the Fire for sins (not for shirk) will eventually be brought out by Allah's mercy and intercession — but the people of pure faith abide in Paradise forever.

ثمرات الإيمان باليوم الآخرWhy This Belief Changes Everything
  • It gives life meaning: this world is a test, not the goal — every deed has a lasting consequence.
  • It brings justice: every wrong unanswered in this life will be perfectly settled — comfort for the oppressed.
  • It balances hope and fear: the believer strives for Paradise and flees the Fire, living between the two.

يُسأل الميت في قبره عن ربه ودينه ونبيه. The deceased is asked in the grave about his Lord, his religion, and his prophet.
الجنة والنار لم تُخلقا بعد. Paradise and Hell have not yet been created.
إسرافيل هو الموكل بالنفخ في الصور. Isrāfīl is the angel entrusted with blowing the Trumpet.
