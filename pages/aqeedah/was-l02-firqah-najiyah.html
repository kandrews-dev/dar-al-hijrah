الفِرْقَةُ النَّاجِيَةُ المَنْصُورَة

The Saved and Aided Group

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Define العَقِيدَة and say where the word comes from.
  • Explain النَّاجِيَة and المَنْصُورَة as al-Fawzān glosses them.
  • Say why they are called أَهْلَ السُّنَّة, and why the sects are not.
  • Give Ibn Masʿūd’s definition of the jamāʿah.
الفِرْقَةُ النَّاجِيَةُ المَنْصُورَة The Saved and Aided Group

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

أَمَّا بَعْدُ: فَهَذَا اعْتِقَادُ الفِرْقَةِ النَّاجِيَةِ المَنْصُورَةِ إِلَى قِيَامِ السَّاعَةِ، أَهْلِ السُّنَّةِ وَالجَمَاعَةِ.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

العَقِيدَة — the word itself repays a look. al-Fawzān traces it: you say اعْتَقَدْتُ كَذَا when you take a thing as a creed, meaning you tied your heart and conscience upon it. Its origin is عَقْدُ الحَبْلِ — the tying of a rope. So a creed is not an opinion you hold; it is a knot you have tied. Loose knots are not creeds.

النَّاجِيَة — the group that came through safe from destruction and from evils, in this world and the next, and attained happiness. المَنْصُورَة — the group given support against whoever opposes it. Both descriptions are taken from a ḥadīth:

لَا تَزَالُ طَائِفَةٌ مِنْ أُمَّتِي عَلَى الحَقِّ مَنْصُورَةً، لَا يَضُرُّهُمْ مَنْ خَذَلَهُمْ حَتَّى يَأْتِيَ أَمْرُ اللهِ

"A group of my ummah will not cease to be upon the truth, given victory; those who abandon them will not harm them, until the command of Allah comes."

رَوَاهُ البُخَارِيُّ وَمُسْلِمٌ · al-Bukhārī and Muslim

الشَّرْح · the explanation

إِلَى قِيَامِ السَّاعَةِ — and here al-Fawzān makes a careful point that is easy to miss. The “Hour” in the ḥadīth is, for the believers, the hour of their own death: the wind that comes and takes the soul of every believer. As for the Hour that ends the world, it does not come upon them at all — it comes only upon the worst of people.

He proves it from Muslim: لَا تَقُومُ السَّاعَةُ حَتَّى لَا يُقَالَ فِي الأَرْضِ: اللهُ اللهُ — “the Hour will not come until it is no longer said on the earth: Allah, Allah” — and from a report al-Ḥākim narrates from ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr: Allah will send a wind whose scent is musk and whose touch is silk, and it will not leave anyone with an atom's weight of faith in his heart but it will take him; then the worst of people will remain, and upon them the Hour will come.

So the promise in the ḥadīth is not that the saved group survives to the end of time. It is that it is never absent while faith is still on the earth.

لِمَاذَا «أَهْلُ السُّنَّةِ وَالجَمَاعَةِ»؟ Why This Name?

الشَّرْح · the explanation

السُّنَّة is the way the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was upon — his statements, his actions and his approvals. And al-Fawzān explains the naming by contrast, which makes it unforgettable: they are called the people of the Sunnah because they ascribe themselves to the Sunnah of the Messenger ﷺ and to nothing else — not to a position, not to a school of opinion.

The people of innovation, by contrast, are named after something else every time:

Named after…Example
their innovation itselfالقَدَرِيَّة · المُرْجِئَة
their leaderالجَهْمِيَّة
their ugly deedsالرَّافِضَة · الخَوَارِج

الجَمَاعَة in the language is a gathered group of people. But al-Fawzān is precise about who is meant here: those who have gathered upon the truth established by the Book and the Sunnah — and they are the Companions and those who followed them in excellence, even if they were few.

Which is the point Ibn Masʿūd رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ made in one sentence, and it is worth memorising as it stands:

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

الجَمَاعَةُ مَا وَافَقَ الحَقَّ، وَإِنْ كُنْتَ وَحْدَكَ، فَإِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الجَمَاعَةُ حِينَئِذٍ.

عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ مَسْعُودٍ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ — “The jamāʿah is whatever agrees with the truth, even if you are alone; for then you are the jamāʿah.”

That sentence settles a question students raise constantly: whether the majority decides. It does not. Agreement with the truth decides, and numbers follow — which is why this group is described as saved and aided rather than as large.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

الجَمَاعَةُ مَا وَافَقَ الحَقَّ وَإِنْ كُنْتَ وَحْدَكَ

"The jamāʿah is whatever agrees with the truth, even if you are alone." Ibn Masʿūd رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ. Keep it for the day being right costs you company.
