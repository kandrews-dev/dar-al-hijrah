الإِخْرَاجُ مِنَ النَّارِ بِغَيْرِ شَفَاعَة

Release from the Fire with No Intercession

شَرْحُ الشَّيْخِ صَالِحٍ الفَوْزَان · ص ١٤٥–١٤٦
Sharḥ al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah, Ṣāliḥ al-Fawzān — pp. 145–146.

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Name the cause of release from the Fire that is other than intercession.
  • Say who is brought out by it, and what the ḥadīth says about their deeds.
  • State the asymmetry al-Fawzān draws between the Garden and the Fire.
  • Explain what the author does with the remaining details, and why.
١٠ · إِخْرَاجُ أَقْوَامٍ مِنَ النَّارِ بِغَيْرِ شَفَاعَة Tenth — Bringing People Out of the Fire with No Intercession

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَيُخْرِجُ اللهُ مِنَ النَّارِ أَقْوَامًا بِغَيْرِ شَفَاعَةٍ، بَلْ بِفَضْلِهِ وَرَحْمَتِهِ. وَيَبْقَى فِي الجَنَّةِ فَضْلٌ عَمَّنْ دَخَلَهَا مِنْ أَهْلِ الدُّنْيَا، فَيُنْشِئُ اللهُ أَقْوَامًا فَيُدْخِلُهُمُ الجَنَّةَ. وَأَصْنَافُ مَا تَضَمَّنَتْهُ الدَّارُ الآخِرَةُ مِنَ الحِسَابِ وَالثَّوَابِ وَالعِقَابِ وَالجَنَّةِ وَالنَّارِ، وَتَفَاصِيلُ ذَلِكَ، مَذْكُورَةٌ فِي الكُتُبِ المُنْزَلَةِ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ، وَالآثَارِ مِنَ العِلْمِ المَأْثُورِ عَنِ الأَنْبِيَاءِ؛ وَفِي العِلْمِ المَوْرُوثِ عَنْ مُحَمَّدٍ ﷺ مِنْ ذَلِكَ مَا يَشْفِي وَيَكْفِي، فَمَنِ ابْتَغَاهُ وَجَدَهُ.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān marks why this comes after Lesson 40: when the Shaykh رَحِمَهُ اللهُ had mentioned that among the kinds of intercession which occur by Allah's permission is the intercession for bringing some of those who entered the Fire out of it — he mentioned here that the coming out of the Fire has another cause besides the intercession, and it is: the mercy of Allah سُبْحَانَهُ, and His bounty and His beneficence.

So there comes out of the Fire, of the disobedient monotheists, whoever has in his heart the least weight of a grain of faith.

Note what the previous lesson's two conditions have just been placed beside. An intercession requires His permission and His pleasure — and this requires neither, because there is no intercessor in it. The Fire is not emptied only as far as the intercessors can reach.

إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يَغْفِرُ أَنْ يُشْرَكَ بِهِ وَيَغْفِرُ مَا دُونَ ذَلِكَ لِمَنْ يَشَاءُ

"Indeed Allah does not forgive that He be associated with, and He forgives what is less than that for whom He wills."

Sūrat al-Nisāʾ 4:48 · سُورَةُ النِّسَاء

يَقُولُ اللهُ: شَفَعَتِ المَلَائِكَةُ، وَشَفَعَ النَّبِيُّونَ، وَشَفَعَ المُؤْمِنُونَ، وَلَمْ يَبْقَ إِلَّا أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ. فَيَقْبِضُ قَبْضَةً مِنَ النَّارِ فَيُخْرِجُ مِنْهَا قَوْمًا لَمْ يَعْمَلُوا خَيْرًا قَطُّ

"Allah will say: the angels have interceded, and the prophets have interceded, and the believers have interceded — and there remains none but the Most Merciful of those who show mercy. Then He will take a handful from the Fire and bring out from it a people who never did any good at all."

الحَدِيثُ المُتَّفَقُ عَلَيْهِ — al-Bukhārī and Muslim

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Read the sequence, because the whole doctrine of the section is in its order. Every intercessor goes first. The angels, then the prophets, then the believers — each one permitted, each one pleased-with, each one reaching as far as he is allowed to reach. And then they are finished, and the sentence turns.

وَلَمْ يَبْقَ إِلَّا أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ

— and there remains none but the Most Merciful of those who show mercy.

And the last clause is the one to be careful with, because it is easy to soften and should not be: قَوْمًا لَمْ يَعْمَلُوا خَيْرًا قَطُّ — a people who never did any good at all. Not people whose good was outweighed; not people whose scales were close. The last ones out are the ones with nothing to their name.

Which is why the author was careful to say بَلْ بِفَضْلِهِ وَرَحْمَتِهِ — rather, by His bounty and His mercy. There is nothing in these people for an intercession to appeal to, and no deed in the record to weigh. The grain of faith in the heart is the whole of their case, and He forgives what is less than that for whom He wills.

وَيَبْقَى فِي الجَنَّةِ فَضْل And There Remains Room in the Garden

الشَّرْح · the explanation

وَيَبْقَى فِي الجَنَّةِ فَضْلٌ — that is: a spaciousness — عَمَّنْ دَخَلَهَا مِنْ أَهْلِ الدُّنْيَا; because Allah described it with spaciousness:

وَسَارِعُوا إِلَى مَغْفِرَةٍ مِنْ رَبِّكُمْ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرْضُهَا السَّمَاوَاتُ وَالْأَرْضُ أُعِدَّتْ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ

"And hasten to a forgiveness from your Lord, and a Garden whose breadth is the heavens and the earth — prepared for those of taqwā."

Sūrat Āl ʿImrān 3:133 · سُورَةُ آلُ عِمْرَان

الشَّرْح · the explanation

فَيُنْشِئُ اللهُ — that is: He creates and brings into being — أَقْوَامًا, that is: groups — فَيُدْخِلُهُمُ الجَنَّةَ, by His bounty and His mercy.

So the Garden is not filled by the people of this world. Room is left, and Allah creates people for it. Which is a strange and generous thing to be told: the capacity was never set by how many would earn it.

And then al-Fawzān states the asymmetry, which is the finest sentence in the section and the natural close of Part 4:

— because the Garden is His mercy: He has mercy by it upon whom He wills. As for the Fire — He does not punish in it except one against whom His proof has been established and who belied His messengers.

The two are not symmetrical, and the asymmetry runs one way only. Entry into the Garden can be by sheer bounty, to people created for it and to people who never did a good deed. Entry into the Fire requires that a case was made and refused. Nobody is in it who was not shown the proof.

That is the same shape the student met on the Islam 101 track in the asymmetry of the scale, and in al-Qawāʿid al-Arbaʿ where the ḥujjah must be established before anyone is condemned. It is the governing principle of the whole of Part 4, stated at the end rather than the beginning.

وَفِيمَا وَرِثْنَا مَا يَشْفِي وَيَكْفِي And in What We Inherited There Is What Heals and Suffices

الشَّرْح · the explanation

The author closes Part 4 not with another event but with a referral. When he رَحِمَهُ اللهُ had mentioned what he mentioned of the states of the Last Day and what occurs in it, he referred to the Book and the Sunnah for knowledge of the details of the remainder, of what he did not mention — because that is of the knowledge of the unseen, which is not known except by way of revelation.

And look at the three places he names, in order: الكُتُبِ المُنْزَلَةِ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ — the Books sent down from heaven; الآثَارِ مِنَ العِلْمِ المَأْثُورِ عَنِ الأَنْبِيَاءِ — the reports of knowledge transmitted from the prophets; and العِلْمِ المَوْرُوثِ عَنْ مُحَمَّدٍ ﷺ — the knowledge inherited from Muḥammad ﷺ, of which there is what heals and what suffices.

مَا يَشْفِي وَيَكْفِي — a healing and a sufficiency. Two different claims, and both matter. Healing answers the unease of the one who wants to know and finds the subject frightening; sufficiency answers the one who suspects something essential has been withheld. Neither is a promise that everything has been told — only that nothing needed has been left out.

And then the condition, which is the last word of Part 4 and the same condition the author attached at the close of Part 1: فَمَنِ ابْتَغَاهُ وَجَدَهُ — so whoever seeks it will find it. In Lesson 21 it was whoever ponders the Qurʾān seeking guidance. Here it is whoever seeks the inherited knowledge. Twice now the author has made the promise conditional not on intelligence or access, but on wanting it.

With this Part 4 is complete. The grave and its examination; the Greater Resurrection and the Scales; the registers and the Reckoning; the Ḥawḍ, the Ṣirāṭ and the Qanṭarah; the intercessions and their conditions; and the release by mercy alone. And it ends where it began — on the author's rule from Lesson 36, that belief in the Last Day is belief in everything the Prophet ﷺ informed of: having set out the chief events, he hands the student back to the sources for the rest.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

وَلَمْ يَبْقَ إِلَّا أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ

Keep the whole sequence with it: the angels have interceded, the prophets have interceded, the believers have interceded — and then this. Every permitted intercessor has reached as far as he was allowed, and the Fire is still not finished being emptied.
