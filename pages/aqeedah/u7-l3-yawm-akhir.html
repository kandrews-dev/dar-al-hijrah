Unit 7 · Lesson 3

اليوم الآخر

The Last Day — in detail

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know the major signs (العلامات الكبرى) that precede the Hour.
  • Affirm the events of the grave (البرزخ) — the questioning, the ḍammah, the bliss or punishment.
  • Walk through the Day itself: النفخ، البعث، الحشر، الحساب، الميزان، الصراط، الحوض، الشفاعة، الجنة، النار.
  • Let this faith change how you live today.

You affirmed belief in اليوم الآخر in U3 — one of the six pillars of الإيمان. Now we widen the picture so you can see the Day in the way the Qur'an and the Sunnah described it.

علامات الساعة Part 1 — The signs of the Hour

The Prophet ﷺ described minor signs (العلامات الصُّغرى) — many already past, like his own coming, the splitting of the moon, the conquest of Jerusalem and Constantinople, drinking of wine being widespread, knowledge being lifted by the death of scholars, ignorance spreading, time seeming to contract, and tall buildings rising in the desert.

And ten major signs (العلامات الكُبرى) that immediately precede the Hour, listed in the ḥadīth of Ḥudhayfah (Muslim):

  1. الدُّخان — the Smoke.
  2. الدَّجَّال — the false messiah, whose fitnah is the greatest from Ādam to the Hour.
  3. الدَّابَّة — the Beast of the earth.
  4. The sun rising from the west — after which repentance is no longer accepted.
  5. The descent of عيسى ابن مريم — who will kill the Dajjāl, rule by the Sharīʿah of Muḥammad ﷺ, and there will be peace.
  6. يأجوج ومأجوج — Gog and Magog, released to spread corruption.
  7. Three great sinkings of the earth — in the east, the west, and the Arabian peninsula.
  8. A fire that drives the people to their final gathering.
البرزخ — حياة القبر Part 2 — The grave (al-Barzakh)

When a person dies, the soul enters البرزخ — the interval between this world and the resurrection. Two angels come and ask three questions: «مَنْ رَبُّكَ؟ ما دينُكَ؟ مَنْ نبيُّكَ؟» — "Who is your Lord? What is your religion? Who is your prophet?" The believer answers from the certainty Allah granted him; the disbeliever and the hypocrite cannot answer.

The grave is then either a meadow from the meadows of Paradise or a pit from the pits of the Fire. There is the ضمّة القبر (the squeeze of the grave) for all, and there is the عذاب القبر (the punishment of the grave) for many — and we seek refuge in Allah from it in every prayer.

يوم القيامة — مشاهد الموقف Part 3 — The Day itself, scene by scene

The Day arrives in a sequence the Qur'an and Sunnah trace step by step:

  • النَّفْخَتان — The two blowings of the Horn. Isrāfīl blows once and all creation is struck down; he blows a second time and they rise. «وَنُفِخَ فِي الصُّورِ فَصَعِقَ مَن فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَن فِي الْأَرْضِ» (الزمر 39:68).
  • البَعْث — The Resurrection. Bones, dust and all reassembled, raised barefoot and naked. «قُلْ يُحْيِيهَا الَّذِي أَنشَأَهَا أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٍ» (يس 36:79).
  • الحَشْر — The Gathering. All creation is gathered on a great open plain, the sun drawn near.
  • الحَوْض — The Pond of the Prophet ﷺ. Whiter than milk, sweeter than honey, its cups as the stars — whoever drinks from it never thirsts again. His true followers come; the innovators are turned away.
  • الشَّفاعة — The intercession. The greatest is the المقام المحمود — the praiseworthy station the Prophet ﷺ alone is granted, to open the reckoning. (Recall U5 + U6: shafāʿah is only by Allah's permission, only for the people of التوحيد.)
  • الحِساب — The Reckoning. The records of deeds are given in the right or the left, and each person is asked. The first matter to be judged is the prayer.
  • الميزان — The Scale. Deeds are weighed in true scales with real pans: «وَنَضَعُ الْمَوَازِينَ الْقِسْطَ لِيَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ فَلَا تُظْلَمُ نَفْسٌ شَيْئًا» (الأنبياء 21:47).
  • الصِّراط — The Bridge. Set over Jahannam — sharper than a sword, thinner than a hair. People cross according to their deeds: some like lightning, some walking, some crawling, some falling in.
  • الجنة والنار — The Garden and the Fire. The destinations. The Garden has degrees rising to the highest — الفردوس الأعلى, beneath the Throne of the Most Merciful. The Fire has levels of depth. Both are real, both eternal for their people, both already created.

وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ نَّاضِرَةٌ ۝ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهَا نَاظِرَةٌ

"On that Day faces will be radiant — looking at their Lord."

القيامة 75:22–23 — the greatest reward of Paradise: the seeing of Allah, which you affirmed in U4 L4.

كيف تغيّرنا هذه العقيدة اليوم؟ Part 4 — What this faith changes in us today

ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb said: "Take account of yourselves before you are taken to account; weigh your deeds before they are weighed; and prepare for the great Day of Display." Belief in اليوم الآخر is not just a creed point — it is a way of seeing every choice: every word will appear in the record, every secret revealed, every wrong you forgave a treasure stored for that Day. Pray the obligatory prayer as if you may not pray another. Forgive freely. Keep your tongue. The Day is real.
