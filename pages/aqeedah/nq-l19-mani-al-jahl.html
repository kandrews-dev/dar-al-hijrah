مَوَانِعُ التَّكْفِير · مَانِعُ الجَهْل

The Barriers to Takfīr — Ignorance

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • State the governing distinction of the barrier — الأُمُورُ الظَّاهِرَة versus الدَّقِيقَةُ الخَفِيَّة — and which admits the excuse of ignorance.
  • Say when ignorance does excuse in creed, and the one whose ruling becomes that of أَهْلِ الفَتْرَة.
  • Give the disease of the Christians against that of the Jews, and why the difference bears on whether bayān cures it.
  • Distinguish البَيَان from التَّبَيُّن, and state what alone is obligatory for the proof to stand — «قِيَامُ الحُجَّةِ فَقَط».
  • Use the man who asked to be burned as the model of a genuinely hidden matter — what exactly he denied, and what he did not.
  • Answer those who fold the grave-worshippers into «حَتَّى تُقَامَ عَلَيْهِ الحُجَّة», and give Ibn Bāz’s reply to «the particular is never declared a disbeliever».
  • Explain why the report that Muʿādh ؓ prostrated is set aside, and how that is the discipline of ḥadīth applied to a proof of takfīr’s opponents.

مِنْ كَلَامِ الشَّيْخِ ابْنِ بَاز · ص ٢٤٣–٢٧٢
Subul al-Salām Sharḥ Nawāqiḍ al-Islām — pp. 243–272, gathering Ibn Bāz’s words on this clause.

مَانِعُ الجَهْل The Barrier of Ignorance

The second barrier, and the one our age argues over most. Does a man’s not-knowing shield him from the ruling of kufr? Ibn Bāz answers with a line he draws again and again, and the whole barrier is that line.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Two kinds of matter, two different rulings:

① الأُمُورُ الظَّاهِرَة — what is «مَعْلُومٌ مِنَ الدِّينِ بِالضَّرُورَة»: that one worships Allah alone, that supplicating the dead and slaughtering to other than Allah is shirk. For one living among Muslims, reached by the Qurʾān and Sunnah, ignorance of these is no excuse.

② الأُمُورُ الدَّقِيقَةُ الخَفِيَّة — the subtle matters whose evidences are obscure. Here ignorance can excuse — «إِذَا اجْتَهَدَ وَطَلَبَ العِلْمَ أَوْ سَأَلَ أَهْلَ العِلْمِ وَحَصَلَ خَطَأ، فَهُوَ بَيْنَ أَمْرَيْن: إِنْ أَصَابَ فَلَهُ أَجْرَان، وَإِنْ أَخْطَأَ فَلَهُ أَجْر».

So the question is never «was he ignorant?» but «ignorant of what?» Of the plain root of the religion — that is not a thing a man among Muslims may plead not to have known. Of a buried detail whose proof is hard to see — that is exactly what the barrier was made to cover.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Q — الَّذِي يَدْعُو أَصْحَابَ القُبُورِ أَوِ الأَصْنَامَ وَهُوَ جَاهِلٌ لَا يَعْلَمُ أَنَّهُ شِرْك — أَيَكُونُ مُشْرِكًا؟

Ibn Bāz — «نَعَم؛ يَكُونُ مُشْرِكًا... لِأَنَّ عَلَيْهِ أَنْ يَتَعَلَّم. أَمَّا إِذَا كَانَ فِي مَكَانٍ بَعِيدٍ عَنِ المُسْلِمِينَ وَلَمْ يَبْلُغْهُ القُرْآنُ وَلَا السُّنَّة، فَهَذَا حُكْمُهُ حُكْمُ أَهْلِ الفَتْرَة... وَأَمْرُهُ إِلَى اللهِ يَوْمَ القِيَامَة؛ يُمْتَحَنُ يَوْمَ القِيَامَة.» Among Muslims: a mushrik, and taught. Beyond the reach of the message: the ruling of ahl al-fatrah, tested on the Day.

الجَهْلُ دَاء Ignorance Is a Disease — and the Two Kinds of Sickness

Ibn Bāz will not let ignorance be dressed up as innocence. The Qurʾān, he says, is «مَمْلُوءٌ مِنَ التَّنْدِيدِ بِالجَهْلِ وَأَهْلِه» — full of censure of ignorance and its people. But he also distinguishes two diseases, and the distinction decides whether teaching can cure a man.

وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكْثَرَهُمْ يَجْهَلُونَ

…but most of them are ignorant.

سُورَةُ الأَنْعَام · 6:111

الشَّرْح · the explanation

The Christians and the Jews are not sick with the same thing. «فَالنَّصَارَى عِلَّتُهُمُ الجَهْلُ وَالضَّلَال؛ فَإِذَا عُرِفُوا وَبُيِّنَ لَهُمْ رَجَعَ كَثِيرٌ مِنْهُمْ إِلَى الحَقّ. أَمَّا عِلَّةُ اليَهُودِ فَلَيْسَتِ الجَهْل، بَلْ عِلَّتُهُمُ الحَسَدُ وَالبَغْي، وَمُخَالَفَةُ الحَقِّ عَلَى بَصِيرَة.»

This matters for the barrier itself. Jahl is the sickness that bayān can reach — show the ignorant man the proof and many turn. Ḥasad is sickness against the known truth, and no clarifying reaches it. But neither is a licence: the Christian who calls to his falsehood «يَحْسَبُ أَنَّهُ عَلَى هُدًى» is still «ضَالٌّ مُضِلٌّ... إِلَى النَّار». Sincerity of feeling is not the measure; the truth reached is.

بِمَاذَا تَقُومُ الحُجَّة؟ By What the Proof Is Established

The heart of the barrier: the ḥujjah of Allah stands by the Book and the Messenger ﷺ. Where the Qurʾān and the Sunnah have reached a people, the proof has been established upon them — the reaching is the establishing.

هَٰذَا بَلَاغٌ لِّلنَّاسِ وَلِيُنذَرُوا بِهِ

This [Qurʾān] is a clear message for mankind, that they may be warned by it.

سُورَةُ إِبْرَاهِيم · 14:52

وَأُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنُ لِأُنذِرَكُم بِهِ وَمَن بَلَغَ

And this Qurʾān has been revealed to me that I may warn you by it — and whomever it reaches.

سُورَةُ الأَنْعَام · 6:19

يَا أَيُّهَا الرَّسُولُ بَلِّغْ مَا أُنزِلَ إِلَيْكَ مِن رَّبِّكَ

O Messenger, convey what has been sent down to you from your Lord.

سُورَةُ المَائِدَة · 5:67

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Ibn Bāz presses the point against a real objection. Someone said the grave-worshippers are not disbelievers «حَتَّى تُقَامَ عَلَيْهِمُ الحُجَّة». He answered: «لَا، هَذَا مِنْ جَهْلِهِم؛ عُبَّادُ القُبُورِ كُفَّار، وَاليَهُودُ كُفَّار، وَالنَّصَارَى كُفَّار؛ وَلَكِنْ عِنْدَ القَتْلِ يُسْتَتَاب، فَإِنْ تَابَ وَإِلَّا قُتِل.» «They say the ḥujjah must be established — it has been: the Qurʾān reached them.» «بَلَغَهُمُ القُرْآن... يَسْمَعُونَهُ فِي الإِذَاعَاتِ وَفِي كُلِّ شَيْءٍ وَلَا يُبَالُون.»

البَيَانُ لَا التَّبَيُّن Conveying the Proof, Not the Grasping of It

This is the precision the whole question turns on, and it is constantly blurred. What is obligatory is that the proof be conveyed and made clear — not that the person actually come to understand and accept it.

وَمَا كَانَ اللَّهُ لِيُضِلَّ قَوْمًا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَاهُمْ حَتَّىٰ يُبَيِّنَ لَهُم مَّا يَتَّقُونَ

Allah would never let a people go astray after He has guided them, until He makes clear to them what they should beware of.

سُورَةُ التَّوْبَة · 9:115

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Q — قَالَ تَعَالَى: «حَتَّى يُبَيِّنَ لَهُم» — يُبَيِّنُ أَمْ يَتَبَيَّن؟

Ibn Bāz — «إِيه، حَتَّى يُبَيِّن» — the duty is the بَيَان, the making-clear, «وَلَوْ لَمْ يَحْصُلْ تَبَيُّن». The proof is established when it is set before a man plainly; whether his heart then opens to it is another thing, and not the condition of the ruling.

Why this matters so much: make tabayyun (the person’s own conviction) the condition, and no stubborn man is ever bound by any proof — he need only refuse to be convinced. The barrier of ignorance protects the one the message never reached and the one lost in a buried detail; it does not hand every obstinate heart a veto over the plain religion.

حَدِيثُ المُحَرَّق The Man Who Asked to Be Burned — the Model of a Hidden Matter

Now the other side of the line, so it is not mistaken for harshness. When Ibn Bāz says ignorance can excuse in a subtle matter, this is his standing example — a ḥadīth in the Ṣaḥīḥayn.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

A man, fearing Allah, told his family: when I die, burn me, grind me, and scatter me in the sea — thinking that so he might escape his Lord. Allah gathered him and asked why; he said «خَوْفُكَ يَا رَبّ», and Allah forgave him. Ibn Bāz: «هَذَا جَهِلَ بَعْضَ الشَّيْءِ مِنَ الأُمُورِ الخَفِيَّةِ مِنْ كَمَالِ القُدْرَة؛ جَهِلَهَا فَعُذِر، خَوْفًا مِنَ الله.»

al-Barrāk’s footnote draws the line to the inch — and this is the whole key: «هَذَا الشَّخْصُ... إِنَّمَا أَنْكَرَ كَمَالَ تَفَاصِيلِ القُدْرَة وَلَمْ يُنْكِرْ قُدْرَةَ الله، وَلَمْ يُنْكِرِ البَعْث... وَالَّذِي حَمَلَهُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَمْرَان: الجَهْلُ وَالخَوْفُ العَظِيم.» Had he denied Allah’s power itself, or the resurrection, «مَا كَانَ مَعْذُورًا». He denied a hidden detail of how far that power reaches — and «مِثْلُهُ يُجْهَل».

Set the two examples side by side and the barrier is clear. The grave-worshipper denies the plain root — no excuse. This man affirmed the root and stumbled on a buried detail — excused. «الَّذِي يُعْذَرُ بِالجَهْلِ هُوَ الَّذِي يَجْهَلُ أَمْرًا دَقِيقًا خَفِيًّا مِثْلُهُ يَجْهَلُه؛ أَمَّا إِذَا أَنْكَرَ أَمْرًا وَاضِحًا مَعْلُومًا لِعَامَّةِ المُسْلِمِينَ فَلَا يُقْبَل.»

لَيْسَ العُذْرُ بِالجَهْلِ قِيَاسِيًّا Ignorance Is No Sliding Scale

الشَّرْح · the explanation

He refuses to let the excuse float with the times. Asked whether excusing by ignorance shifts with era and land: «لَيْسَ العُذْرُ بِالجَهْلِ مَسْأَلَةً قِيَاسِيَّةً تَخْتَلِفُ مِنْ زَمَانٍ إِلَى زَمَان، وَمَكَانٍ إِلَى آخَر؛ لِأَنَّ الجَهْلَ لَيْسَ بِعُذْرٍ بِالنِّسْبَةِ لِلْعَقِيدَة — إِلَّا إِذَا كَانَ فِي مَكَانٍ لَمْ تَبْلُغْهُ الدَّعْوَة.»

And the sharpest line of the barrier, when it was put to him that «the particular person is never declared a disbeliever»:

قِيلَ لَهُ: بَعْضُ النَّاسِ يَقُول: المُعَيَّنُ لَا يُكَفَّر. فَأَجَاب: «هَذَا الجَهْل؛ إِذَا وُجِدَ مُكَفِّرٌ كَفَر.»

Read it with the whole section. The barriers are real — coercion, ignorance, error, interpretation withhold the ruling. But they are barriers, not a wall: when a cause of takfīr is established and no barrier stands, the ruling does fall on the named man. To say otherwise — that no individual is ever ruled a disbeliever — is, Ibn Bāz says flatly, itself a kind of ignorance.

قِصَّةُ سُجُودِ مُعَاذ The Report of Muʿādh’s Prostration — Ḥadīth Discipline on a Proof

Those who stretch the excuse of ignorance over grave-worship lean on a story: that Muʿādh ibn Jabal ؓ prostrated to the Prophet ﷺ on returning from Shām, and was not declared a disbeliever. The compiler spends folios on it — and the lesson is the method as much as the verdict.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

The verdict on the prostration report: «فِي صِحَّتِهِ نَظَر» — rather ḍaʿīf, bāṭil. «تَفَرَّدَ بِهِ القَاسِمُ بْنُ عَوْفٍ الشَّيْبَانِي، وَلَمْ يَذْكُرْ أَحَدٌ مِنَ الرُّوَاةِ أَنَّ مُعَاذًا سَجَد؛ وَأَكْثَرُ الرِّوَايَاتِ عَنِ القَاسِمِ لَيْسَ فِيهَا أَنَّهُ سَجَد.» The compiler lays out seven turuq and shows al-Qāsim «اضْطَرَبَ فِي رِوَايَتِهِ اضْطِرَابًا كَبِيرًا» — once from Muʿādh directly, once through Ibn Abī Awfā, once through Zayd ibn Arqam, once with two intermediaries, once with three — «وَمِثْلُهُ لَا يَحْتَمِلُ التَّفَرُّد».

What is authentic is the other wording. «أَمَّا حَدِيثُ: (لَوْ كُنْتُ آمِرًا أَحَدًا أَنْ يَسْجُدَ لِأَحَد…): فَصَحِيحٌ ثَابِتٌ مَرْوِيٌّ عَنْ جَمَاعَةٍ مِنَ الصَّحَابَة.» Notice precisely what carries and what falls: the Prophet’s ﷺ words forbidding prostration to other than Allah are firm; the claim that Muʿādh actually did prostrate — the part the argument needs — «لَا أَصْلَ لَهُ البَتَّة».

الشَّرْح · the explanation

And even granting the question Muʿādh asked, it is no proof for today. It belonged to the earliest Islam, before the religion had settled — «هَذَا لَهُ شُبْهَةٌ فِي أَوَّلِ الإِسْلَام، لَكِنِ اسْتَقَرَّ الدِّين، وَعُرِفَ أَنَّ السُّجُودَ لِله». A passing doubt in year one, resolved by the plain command, is not a man circling a grave after the Qurʾān has filled the air around him.

فَاسْجُدُوا لِلَّهِ وَاعْبُدُوا

So prostrate to Allah and worship [Him].

سُورَةُ النَّجْم · 53:62

حَدِيثُ اللَّيْثِيِّين Knowing a Thing Is Forbidden Is Enough

One last proof the expanders raise, and its answer is a rule worth keeping. Some Bedouin of Banū Layth, offered compensation and asked «are you content?», said «لَا» — taken by some as belying the Prophet ﷺ, yet excused, they claim, by ignorance.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

The compiler answers in steps. First, the report does not have them belying him at all — «إِنَّمَا طَمِعُوا فِي الزِّيَادَة», they were angling for more, and he gave more and they were content. But grant, for argument, that their word was kufr:

«عِلْمُهُمْ أَنَّ التَّكْذِيبَ لِلْأَنْبِيَاءِ حَرَامٌ كَافٍ فِي كُفْرِهِم؛ وَلَا يُشْتَرَطُ عِلْمُهُمْ بِالأَثَرِ المُتَرَتِّبِ عَلَى التَّكْذِيب — كَمَنْ عَلِمَ بِتَحْرِيمِ السَّرِقَةِ وَلَمْ يَعْلَمْ بِالأَثَرِ المُتَرَتِّبِ عَلَيْهَا، فَلَا يُشْتَرَطُ فِي القَطْعِ عِلْمُهُ بِذَلِك.»

This is the cleanest statement of the whole barrier’s limit. A man need not know the penalty of a sin to be bound by its prohibition. The thief who never studied the ḥadd is still a thief; the one who knows belying a Prophet is forbidden is not excused by not knowing it is called «kufr». Ignorance of the label is not ignorance of the law.

Check Your Understanding · MCQ أَسْئِلَة

What is the governing distinction in the whole barrier of ignorance?

Between الأُمُورُ الظَّاهِرَة known from the religion by necessity (no excuse for one among Muslims) and الأُمُورُ الدَّقِيقَةُ الخَفِيَّة (an excuse is possible)
Between Arabs and non-Arabs
Between major and minor shirk
Between the learned and the unlettered, with no further detail

Is one who calls upon other than Allah — supplicating the dead, the idols, the stars — a mushrik even if he is ignorant and does not know it is shirk, so long as he lives among Muslims?

Yes — «يَكُونُ مُشْرِكًا... لِأَنَّ عَلَيْهِ أَنْ يَتَعَلَّم»; and he is taught, because this is of the plain matters
No, ignorance always excuses in creed
Only if he was warned three times
Only a scholar can be ruled so

When does Ibn Bāz say ignorance excuses a man in creed?

When he is «فِي مَكَانٍ لَمْ تَبْلُغْهُ الدَّعْوَة» — far from the Muslims, the Qurʾān and Sunnah never having reached him; his ruling is that of أَهْلِ الفَتْرَة
Whenever he sincerely believes he is upon the truth
In every matter of creed without exception
Only on the Day of Judgement

What is the disease of the Christians, by contrast with the Jews — and why does the distinction matter?

The Christians’ disease is الجَهْل (curable by bayān — many return when it is made clear); the Jews’ is الحَسَدُ وَالبَغْي, opposing the truth upon insight
Both have the same disease, pure stubbornness
The Christians’ disease is envy, the Jews’ ignorance
Neither is ignorant; both merely forgot

Ibn Bāz distinguishes البَيَان from التَّبَيُّن. What is obligatory for the ruling to fall?

Only the bayān — that the proof be conveyed and made clear («الوَاجِبُ قِيَامُ الحُجَّةِ فَقَط»); the person’s actually grasping it (tabayyun) is not a condition
That the person fully understand and accept it
That a muftī personally debate him
That he be given ten years to study

By what does the proof of Allah stand established upon people?

By the Qurʾān and the Sunnah — «هَٰذَا بَلَاغٌ لِّلنَّاس», «لِأُنذِرَكُم بِهِ وَمَن بَلَغ»; reaching them is the establishment of the ḥujjah
Only by a scholar debating each person face to face
Only by a ruler’s decree
By the person’s own conscience alone

The man who told his family to burn him and scatter him in the sea, fearing he could thereby escape Allah — why did Ibn Bāz and Ibn Taymiyyah hold him excused?

He denied only a subtle detail of the completeness of Allah’s power (تَفَاصِيل القُدْرَة), not Allah’s power itself nor the resurrection — a genuinely hidden matter, driven by ignorance and overwhelming fear
He denied the resurrection outright and was excused anyway
Ignorance excuses every denial
Because he later repented

Is العُذْرُ بِالجَهْل a qiyāsī matter that changes with time and place?

No — «لَيْسَ العُذْرُ بِالجَهْلِ مَسْأَلَةً قِيَاسِيَّة»; ignorance is no excuse in creed except where the daʿwah has not reached
Yes, it changes entirely by century and country
Yes, it is excused in all times now that knowledge is hard
It is never an excuse under any circumstance

Some contemporaries said one who does or says kufr is not a disbeliever «until the ḥujjah is established upon him», and folded the grave-worshippers into this. How did Ibn Bāz answer?

«لَا، هَذَا مِنْ جَهْلِهِم؛ عُبَّادُ القُبُورِ كُفَّار» — the Qurʾān reached them; the plain matters need no further bayān
He agreed with them fully
He said grave-worship is not shirk
He deferred the whole question

When someone said «the particular person is never declared a disbeliever» (المُعَيَّنُ لَا يُكَفَّر), what did Ibn Bāz reply?

«هَذَا الجَهْل؛ إِذَا وُجِدَ مُكَفِّرٌ كَفَر» — when a cause of takfīr is present and the barriers absent, the ruling falls on the person
He agreed; no individual is ever ruled a kāfir
He said only a ruler may ever do so
He said the matter is of the hidden questions

The compiler devotes pages to the report that Muʿādh ؓ prostrated to the Prophet ﷺ — a text the «excuse by ignorance» expanders lean on. What is his verdict on it?

The report of the prostration is weak, even bāṭil — al-Qāsim al-Shaybānī alone narrates it with great iḍṭirāb; what is authentic is «لَوْ كُنْتُ آمِرًا أَحَدًا أَنْ يَسْجُد…», without the prostration
It is fully authentic and proves ignorance excuses shirk
It is in al-Bukhārī
It is mutawātir

Even if the report of Muʿādh’s question were sound, why is it no proof for excusing shirk today?

It belonged to the earliest Islam before the dīn had settled — a passing shubhah resolved by «وَاسْجُدُوا لِلَّهِ وَاعْبُدُوا»; it is not a man persisting in grave-worship after the Qurʾān has reached him
Because Muʿādh was a scholar and others are not
Because prostration of greeting was always kufr
Because it happened in Yemen

The Banū Layth who said «لَا» when asked «are you content?» were defended by some as excused by ignorance. How is that answered?

Their knowing that belying a Prophet is forbidden «كَافٍ فِي كُفْرِهِم»; one need not know the consequence of a sin to be bound by its prohibition — as the thief need not know the ḥadd to be punished
They were in fact fully excused
They never said anything of the sort
The Prophet ﷺ declared them disbelievers on the spot

Imām Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb wrote that belying the Messenger ﷺ in what is established from him decisively is kufr, and «الجَهْلُ فِي مِثْلِ هَذَا لَيْسَ بِعُذْر». Why not?

Because he «يُمْكِنُهُ السُّؤَال» — he could have asked; so he is «مُفَرِّطٌ مُعْرِض», a neglecter who turned away
Because asking is forbidden
Because ignorance is always kufr
Because he lived before the Prophet ﷺ
True or False صَحٌ أَمْ خَطَأٌ
الجَاهِلُ الَّذِي يَدْعُو غَيْرَ اللهِ وَهُوَ بَيْنَ المُسْلِمِينَ قَدْ بَلَغَهُ القُرْآنُ — يَكُونُ مُشْرِكًا وَيُعَلَّم. The ignorant one who calls on other than Allah while among Muslims, the Qurʾān having reached him, is a mushrik and is taught.
العُذْرُ بِالجَهْلِ جَارٍ فِي الأُمُورِ الظَّاهِرَةِ المَعْلُومَةِ مِنَ الدِّينِ بِالضَّرُورَة. The excuse of ignorance applies in the plain matters known from the religion by necessity.
الوَاجِبُ هُوَ البَيَان — قِيَامُ الحُجَّة — وَلَوْ لَمْ يَحْصُلْ تَبَيُّنٌ مِنَ المُخَاطَب. What is obligatory is the conveying — the establishing of the proof — even if the addressed one does not come to grasp it.
الَّذِي أَمَرَ أَهْلَهُ أَنْ يُحَرِّقُوهُ أَنْكَرَ قُدْرَةَ اللهِ وَالبَعْث، وَمَعَ ذَلِكَ عُذِر. The man who told his family to burn him denied Allah’s power and the resurrection, and was excused anyway.
عِلَّةُ النَّصَارَى الجَهْل، وَعِلَّةُ اليَهُودِ الحَسَدُ وَالبَغْي. The disease of the Christians is ignorance; that of the Jews is envy and transgression.
قَالَ ابْنُ بَاز: المُعَيَّنُ لَا يُكَفَّرُ مُطْلَقًا وَإِنْ وُجِدَ المُكَفِّر. Ibn Bāz said the particular person is never declared a disbeliever, even if a cause of takfīr is present.
رِوَايَةُ سُجُودِ مُعَاذٍ لِلنَّبِيِّ ﷺ ضَعِيفَةٌ، تَفَرَّدَ بِهَا القَاسِمُ الشَّيْبَانِيُّ وَاضْطَرَبَ فِيهَا. The report that Muʿādh prostrated to the Prophet ﷺ is weak; al-Qāsim al-Shaybānī narrated it alone and with instability.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

الَّذِي يُعْذَرُ بِالجَهْلِ هُوَ الَّذِي يَجْهَلُ أَمْرًا دَقِيقًا خَفِيًّا مِثْلُهُ يَجْهَلُه؛ أَمَّا إِذَا أَنْكَرَ أَمْرًا وَاضِحًا مَعْلُومًا لِعَامَّةِ المُسْلِمِينَ فَلَا يُقْبَلُ وَلَوِ ادَّعَى أَنَّهُ يَجْهَل

Ibn Bāz, on the barrier of ignorance. This one sentence holds the whole balance. Ignorance is a real barrier — but it covers the buried detail whose proof is hard to see, the thing «مِثْلُهُ يُجْهَل», not the plain root of the religion that every Muslim among Muslims is reached by. The man who stumbled on a hidden fold of Allah’s power was excused; the man who circles a grave after the Qurʾān has reached him is not. And the measure is never how sincerely he felt — it is what the clear proof set before him.
