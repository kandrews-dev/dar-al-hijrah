الوَلَاءُ وَالبَرَاء

Loyalty for Allah & Disavowal from Shirk

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know the meaning of الوَلَاء (loving and supporting for Allah).
  • Know the meaning of البَرَاء (disowning and distancing from shirk).
  • See that these two are part of the meaning of لا إله إلا الله.
  • Apply them with wisdom — gently, justly, and with the manners of the Prophet ﷺ.

A believer's heart is not neutral. Allah commanded that we نُحبّ (love) what He loves and نُبغض (dislike) what He dislikes; that we be أولياء (allies and supporters) of the believers, and that we be برآء (free, disassociated) of shirk and its people. This is الوَلَاءُ وَالبَرَاء — and it is half of لا إله إلا الله: you cannot affirm Allah alone (إلا الله) without disowning what is worshipped besides Him (لا إله).

الوَلَاء Part 1 — Loyalty for Allah's sake

الوَلَاء means love, allegiance, friendship and support — directed first to Allah, then to His Messenger ﷺ, then to the believers. Allah said:

إِنَّمَا وَلِيُّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُۥ وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤْتُونَ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَهُمْ رَٰكِعُونَ

"Your only ally (وليّ) is Allah, His Messenger, and those who believe — those who establish prayer and give zakāh, while they bow."

المائدة 5:55 · al-Māʾidah

The believers are إخوة (brothers); their love is a commanded love, not optional. The Prophet ﷺ tied it to faith itself:

لَا يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ حَتَّى يُحِبَّ لِأَخِيهِ مَا يُحِبُّ لِنَفْسِهِ

"None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

البَرَاء Part 2 — Disavowal from shirk & its people

البَرَاء means to be free, to disown, to be clear of — first of shirk itself, and then of the worship of other than Allah. Allah gave us the example of the leader of the monotheists:

قَدْ كَانَتْ لَكُمْ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ فِىٓ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥٓ إِذْ قَالُواْ لِقَوْمِهِمْ إِنَّا بُرَءَٰٓؤُاْ مِنكُمْ وَمِمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ كَفَرْنَا بِكُمْ وَبَدَا بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَكُمُ ٱلْعَدَٰوَةُ وَٱلْبَغْضَآءُ أَبَدًا حَتَّىٰ تُؤْمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ وَحْدَهُۥٓ

"You have a beautiful example in Ibrāhīm and those with him, when they said to their people: 'We are free (برآء) of you and of what you worship besides Allah; we reject you; there has appeared between us and you enmity and hatred forever — until you believe in Allah alone.'"

الممتحنة 60:4 · al-Mumtaḥanah — the foundation verse of البَرَاء

Notice carefully: the براءة Ibrāhīm declared was from their shirk and their false gods — not a license to mistreat people. The very same Ibrāhīm was merciful, generous, and even prayed for guidance for his idol-worshipping father (until it was made clear his father was an enemy of Allah — التوبة 9:114). البَرَاء is in the heart and the religion, not in cruelty.

كَيْفَ نُطَبِّقُهَا بِحِكْمَة Part 3 — How we live it, with wisdom

This is especially important for a Muslim family in a non-Muslim environment. الوَلَاءُ وَالبَرَاء is not:

  • Hating non-Muslims as individuals.
  • Refusing to be kind, just, or generous to them.
  • Refusing to live among them, do business with them, or share food.

Allah said clearly:

لَّا يَنْهَىٰكُمُ ٱللَّهُ عَنِ ٱلَّذِينَ لَمْ يُقَٰتِلُوكُمْ فِى ٱلدِّينِ وَلَمْ يُخْرِجُوكُم مِّن دِيَٰرِكُمْ أَن تَبَرُّوهُمْ وَتُقْسِطُوٓاْ إِلَيْهِمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُقْسِطِينَ

"Allah does not forbid you — regarding those who did not fight you in religion and did not drive you from your homes — that you should treat them with goodness (تبَرّوهم) and be just (تُقْسِطوا) to them. Indeed Allah loves the just."

الممتحنة 60:8 — the verse of justice with non-hostile non-Muslims

قاعدة جامعة — A binding rule

What we love and disown is the shirk — not the person. We hate kufr; we do not hate the human Allah might one day guide. We are برآء from what they worship besides Allah, and we are upright, fair, and kind to them as people. That is how Ibrāhīm did it; that is how the Prophet ﷺ did it.

لِلْأَبْنَاء Part 4 — For the children, plainly

Love Allah, His Messenger ﷺ, and the believers. Make your closest friendships with children who love Allah and pray. Be kind to everyone — your classmates, your neighbours, anyone who has not wronged you. But never join in or praise shirk: don't bow before idols, don't celebrate festivals that mock Allah's religion, don't agree that "all gods are the same." Your heart belongs to Allah alone — your hands stay generous to all.

احفظ — Memorize

إِنَّا بُرَآءُ مِنكُمْ وَمِمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ
