الصِّفَاتُ المُثْبَتَة

The Affirmed Attributes

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Affirm what Allah has affirmed of Himself, with the proofs.
  • Know the meaning we affirm, and leave the "how" (al-kayf) to Allah.
  • Begin to recognize these attributes in the Qur'an as you read.

We come now to the heart of al-Wāsiṭiyyah: walking through the attributes Allah affirmed for Himself. Imam Mālik's rule applies to every one of them: "al-istiwāʾ is known [in meaning]; the how is unknown; believing in it is obligatory; asking about it is innovation." Affirm the meaning, leave the kayf to Allah.

الاسْتِوَاءُ عَلَى العَرْش1. al-Istiwāʾ — Rising over the Throne

الرَّحْمَٰنُ عَلَى الْعَرْشِ اسْتَوَىٰ

"The Most Merciful — over the Throne He rose."

Ṭā Hā 20:5 · mentioned in seven places in the Qur'an

We affirm that Allah rose over His Throne in a manner befitting His majesty — high, above His creation. The Salaf explained istawā with four meanings, all real: ʿalā (rose high), irtafaʿa (elevated), ṣaʿida (ascended), istaqarra (settled). We do not say "He took it as a chair to sit on" (that is tashbīh), nor "it just means He took control" (that is taʿṭīl — the way of the Jahmiyyah). We say what Allah said, and leave the kayf to Him.

النُّزُول2. al-Nuzūl — Descending to the Lowest Heaven

يَنْزِلُ رَبُّنَا تَبَارَكَ وَتَعَالَى كُلَّ لَيْلَةٍ إِلَى السَّمَاءِ الدُّنْيَا حِينَ يَبْقَى ثُلُثُ اللَّيْلِ الْآخِرُ، فَيَقُولُ: مَنْ يَدْعُونِي فَأَسْتَجِيبَ لَهُ؟

"Our Lord — Blessed and Exalted is He — descends every night to the lowest heaven when the last third of the night remains, saying: 'Who is calling on Me, that I may answer him?'"

al-Bukhārī & Muslim — agreed upon

A real descent (nuzūl ḥaqīqī), in a manner befitting His majesty, without our needing to imagine "how." This is an action-related attribute tied to His will — He descends when He wills. It is one of the great calls to stand for the last third of the night and ask Him.

الوَجْه3. al-Wajh — The Face

وَيَبْقَىٰ وَجْهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ

"And there remains the Face of your Lord, full of majesty and honor."

al-Raḥmān 55:27

We affirm a real Face for Allah, described as full of majesty and honor — without resembling the faces of creation. Sincerity in worship is described as being "for the sake of His Face" (al-Insān 76:9).

اليَدَان4. al-Yadān — The Two Hands

بَلْ يَدَاهُ مَبْسُوطَتَانِ يُنفِقُ كَيْفَ يَشَاءُ

"Rather, both His Hands are outstretched; He gives as He wills."

al-Māʾidah 5:64 — and see Ṣād 38:75

Two real Hands. The text uses the dual form — refuting those who tried to interpret "Hand" as just "power" or "favor." Allah created Ādam with His two Hands as an honor (Ṣād 38:75) — neither "power" nor "favor" would fit the meaning there.

العَيْن5. al-ʿAyn — The Eyes

وَاصْنَعِ الْفُلْكَ بِأَعْيُنِنَا وَوَحْيِنَا

"And build the ship under Our Eyes and Our revelation."

Hūd 11:37

Real Eyes — affirmed without resemblance. From this comes Allah's care, watching, and protection of His servants — "under Our Eyes" means He sees and safeguards.

الكلام6. al-Kalām — Speech

وَكَلَّمَ اللَّهُ مُوسَىٰ تَكْلِيمًا

"And Allah spoke to Mūsā directly."

al-Nisāʾ 4:164 — the maṣdar taklīm removes any room for metaphor

Allah speaks in reality, with letter and sound, when He wills, how He wills. The Qur'an is His Speech — uncreated. This was the great line Ahl al-Sunnah held against the Muʿtazila and the Jahmiyyah. We come back to this in Lesson 4 in detail.

الرَّحْمَةُ وَالرِّضَا وَالغَضَبُ وَالمَحَبَّة7. Mercy, Pleasure, Anger & Love

Real attributes — not mere "intentions to reward / punish" as the Muʿtazila claimed.

  • Mercy (raḥmah): «وَرَحْمَتِي وَسِعَتْ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ» "My mercy encompasses all things" (al-Aʿrāf 7:156).
  • Pleasure (riḍā) & love (maḥabbah): «رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ» "Allah is pleased with them and they with Him" (al-Bayyinah 98:8); «إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ الْمُحْسِنِينَ» "Allah loves those who do good" (al-Baqarah 2:195).
  • Anger (ghaḍab): «وَغَضِبَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ» "Allah is angry with him" (al-Nisāʾ 4:93).
السَّمْعُ وَالبَصَرُ وَالعِلْمُ وَالقُدْرَة8. Hearing, Sight, Knowledge & Power

إِنَّهُ هُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ ۝ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ ۝ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

"Indeed He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing… He is Knower of all things… He is over all things Powerful."

spread throughout the Qur'an

The rule for every attribute — قاعدة جامعة

For every attribute the texts affirm: (1) affirm its reality, (2) know its general meaning in the language, (3) leave the kayf (how) to Allah, (4) deny any resemblance to creation — «لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ». This is what Imam Mālik said about istiwāʾ, and it is the way of the Salaf for every attribute.
