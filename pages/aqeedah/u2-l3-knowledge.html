أَسْمَاءُ العِلْمِ وَالإِحَاطَة

The Names of Knowledge & Awareness

Year 2+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Explain the five names of knowledge — العَلِيم، السَّمِيع، البَصِير، الخَبِير، الرَّقِيب
  • Distinguish ʿilm, samʿ, baṣar, khibrah, and murāqabah
  • Cite the Quranic evidence for each name
  • Connect these names to iḥsān — worshipping Allah as though you see Him
أَنْ تَعْلَمَ أَنَّهُ يَعْلَم To Know That He Knows

These five names all point to one reality: Allah's complete knowledge and total awareness of His creation. He knows the open and the hidden, hears every whisper, sees every movement, is aware of the smallest detail, and watches over all. Nothing — not a thought in the heart nor an atom in the darkness — is outside His knowledge.

يَعْلَمُ السِّرَّ وَأَخْفَىٰ

"He knows the secret and what is even more hidden."

Sūrat Ṭāhā 20:7 · سورة طه

This knowledge is not meant to frighten us away, but to wake us up: the One we worship is never absent, never distracted, never unaware. As we will see, these names are the foundation of iḥsān — the highest level of worship.

العَلِيم

al-ʿAlīm

The All-Knowing

ع-ل-م

al-ʿAlīm is the One whose knowledge is complete and eternal — encompassing the past, the present, the future, the seen, and the unseen. He knew everything before it existed, knows it as it happens, and knows what would have been. His knowledge never increases (He never "learns") and never decreases (He never forgets).

Evidence — الدليل

وَاللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ

"And Allah is Knowing of all things." — al-Nūr 24:35

Effect on the heart — أثره

The believer is careful with secret deeds and intentions, knowing nothing is hidden from Allah.

السَّمِيع

al-Samīʿ

The All-Hearing

س-م-ع

al-Samīʿ hears every sound — all languages, all at once, the loud and the faint, even the silent speech of the heart. ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her) marvelled that Allah heard the woman who came to dispute with the Prophet ﷺ from the corner of the room, while she herself could not — and Allah revealed: قَدْ سَمِعَ اللَّهُ ("Allah has heard..."). His hearing also means He answers the one who calls.

Evidence — الدليل

لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ ۖ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ

"There is nothing like Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing." — al-Shūrā 42:11

Effect on the heart — أثره

Guards the tongue, and gives confidence in duʿāʾ — the One who hears all will hear you.

البَصِير

al-Baṣīr

The All-Seeing

ب-ص-ر

al-Baṣīr sees all things — the black ant on a black rock in the darkest night, and the hidden glance of the eye (يَعْلَمُ خَائِنَةَ الْأَعْيُنِ, 40:19). No barrier, distance, or darkness limits His sight. His seeing also carries the meaning of perfect wisdom and insight into all affairs.

Evidence — الدليل

إِنَّ اللَّهَ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ

"Indeed Allah is Seeing of what you do." — al-Baqarah 2:110

Effect on the heart — أثره

Produces shame before sin — He sees you even when no human does.

الخَبِير

al-Khabīr

The All-Aware

خ-ب-ر

al-Khabīr is aware of the innermost realities of things — the hidden details, the secrets beneath the surface, the true state of every heart and the wisdom behind every event. Where ʿilm is knowledge in general, khibrah is knowledge of the subtle, inward, and hidden. He is often called اللَّطِيفُ الْخَبِيرُ — the Subtle, the Aware.

Evidence — الدليل

وَهُوَ اللَّطِيفُ الْخَبِيرُ

"And He is the Subtle, the All-Aware." — al-Anʿām 6:103

Effect on the heart — أثره

Brings trust in Allah's wisdom — He is aware of what is truly good for you, even when you cannot see it.

الرَّقِيب

al-Raqīb

The Ever-Watchful

ر-ق-ب

al-Raqīb is the One who watches over all things constantly — no action, word, or thought escapes His watch. This name combines knowledge, hearing, and sight into ongoing مراقبة (murāqabah — watchfulness). It is the name that most directly trains the heart for iḥsān: living every moment aware that Allah is watching.

Evidence — الدليل

إِنَّ اللَّهَ كَانَ عَلَيْكُمْ رَقِيبًا

"Indeed Allah is ever, over you, a Watcher." — al-Nisāʾ 4:1

Effect on the heart — أثره

Produces murāqabah — consciousness that turns even private moments into worship.

مِنَ الأَسْمَاءِ إِلَى الإِحْسَان From the Names to Iḥsān

When a believer truly knows that Allah is al-ʿAlīm, al-Samīʿ, al-Baṣīr, al-Khabīr, and al-Raqīb, their worship rises to the highest level — iḥsān. The Prophet ﷺ defined it in the famous Ḥadīth of Jibrīl:

«أَنْ تَعْبُدَ اللَّهَ كَأَنَّكَ تَرَاهُ، فَإِنْ لَمْ تَكُنْ تَرَاهُ فَإِنَّهُ يَرَاكَ»

"It is to worship Allah as though you see Him; and though you do not see Him, He surely sees you."

Narrated by Muslim — Ḥadīth of Jibrīl · رواه مسلم

"He surely sees you" — this is exactly al-Baṣīr and al-Raqīb. The names of awareness are not abstract theology; they are the engine of sincerity. A heart that lives with these names does not need a human supervisor to do right, because it never forgets the Watcher who never sleeps.

Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ

السَّمِيع يسمع جميع الأصوات في وقت واحد دون أن يشغله صوت عن صوت. al-Samīʿ hears all sounds at once, no sound distracting Him from another.
الخَبِير يدل على العلم بالظواهر دون البواطن. al-Khabīr refers to knowing only outward appearances, not inner realities.
اسم الرَّقِيب يثمر المراقبة وهي أساس الإِحْسَان. The name al-Raqīb produces murāqabah, which is the foundation of iḥsān.
الإِحْسَان أن تعبد الله كأنك تراه فإن لم تكن تراه فإنه يراك. Iḥsān is to worship Allah as though you see Him; though you do not, He sees you.
