Unit 5 · al-Qawāʿid al-ArbaʿYear 2

القَوَاعِدُ الأَرْبَع

The Four Foundations

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Explain the matn's opening analogy: worship + tawḥīd, like prayer + purification.
  • See why affirming Allah as Creator (rubūbiyyah) is not enough.
  • Recognize the mushrikīn's arguments of "nearness" and "intercession," and tell negated from affirmed shafāʿah.
  • Name the five categories the Prophet ﷺ fought without distinction, with the proof against each.
  • Know that worshipping anything besides Allah is shirk.

al-Qawāʿid al-Arbaʿ is a short, weighty treatise by Imam Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Wahhāb. It lays down four principles by which you tell apart the tawḥīd Allah commanded from the shirk the mushrikīn fell into — so you hold to the first and flee the second. The ḥanīfiyyah — the pure way of Ibrāhīm — is to worship Allah alone, making the religion sincerely for Him: «وَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنْسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ» ("And I did not create jinn and mankind except to worship Me," al-Dhāriyāt 51:56). (Based on the matn with al-Fawzān's sharḥ.)

من المتن — The Matn's Own Opening

The author opens with a key analogy: worship is only called worship when joined with tawḥīd — just as prayer is only called prayer when joined with purification (ṭahārah). If shirk enters an act of worship, it corrupts it entirely — just as ritual impurity (ḥadath) entering purification corrupts it entirely. Since shirk mixed into worship voids the deed and can lead its doer to remain in the Fire forever, the most important thing upon you is to know these four principles clearly, so that Allah may rescue you from it.

القَاعِدَةُ الأُولَى Foundation 1 — Affirming Lordship is not enough

The disbelievers the Messenger ﷺ fought affirmed that Allah alone is the Creator, Provider and Controller (tawḥīd ar-rubūbiyyah) — yet this did not make them Muslims, nor did it protect their lives and wealth. What is required is tawḥīd al-ulūhiyyah: directing worship to Allah alone.

قُلْ مَن يَرْزُقُكُم مِّنَ السَّمَاءِ وَالْأَرْضِ ... فَسَيَقُولُونَ اللَّهُ

"Say: Who provides for you from the sky and the earth…? They will say: Allah."

Yūnus 10:31

القَاعِدَةُ الثَّانِيَة Foundation 2 — "Nearness" and "intercession"

The mushrikīn gave two excuses for calling on others besides Allah: seeking nearness (qurbah/zulfā) and seeking intercession (shafāʿah).

مَا نَعْبُدُهُمْ إِلَّا لِيُقَرِّبُونَا إِلَى اللَّهِ زُلْفَىٰ

"We only worship them so that they may bring us nearer to Allah." (proof of "nearness")

al-Zumar 39:3

وَيَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ مَا لَا يَضُرُّهُمْ وَلَا يَنفَعُهُمْ وَيَقُولُونَ هَٰؤُلَاءِ شُفَعَاؤُنَا عِندَ اللَّهِ

"And they worship besides Allah that which neither harms them nor benefits them, and they say: 'These are our intercessors with Allah.'" (proof of "intercession")

Yūnus 10:18

Intercession is two kinds: the negated and the affirmed.

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا أَنفِقُوا مِمَّا رَزَقْنَاكُم مِّن قَبْلِ أَن يَأْتِيَ يَوْمٌ لَّا بَيْعٌ فِيهِ وَلَا خُلَّةٌ وَلَا شَفَاعَةٌ

"O you who believe, spend from what We have provided you before a Day comes in which there is no bargaining, no friendship, and no intercession." (proof of negated intercession — sought from other than Allah, for what only Allah has power over)

al-Baqarah 2:254

مَن ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِندَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ

"Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission?" (proof of affirmed intercession — sought from Allah alone, for the people of tawḥīd, only by His permission)

al-Baqarah 2:255 — Āyat al-Kursī

القَاعِدَةُ الثَّالِثَة Foundation 3 — He fought them all alike

The Prophet ﷺ came to people differing in what they worshipped — some worshipped angels, some prophets, some the righteous, some trees and stones, some the sun and moon — and he fought them all without distinction, never saying that any of it was acceptable.

وَقَاتِلُوهُمْ حَتَّىٰ لَا تَكُونَ فِتْنَةٌ وَيَكُونَ الدِّينُ كُلُّهُ لِلَّهِ

"And fight them until there is no fitnah, and the religion — all of it — is for Allah." (the general proof)

al-Anfāl 8:39

وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ اللَّيْلُ وَالنَّهَارُ وَالشَّمْسُ وَالْقَمَرُ ۚ لَا تَسْجُدُوا لِلشَّمْسِ وَلَا لِلْقَمَرِ وَاسْجُدُوا لِلَّهِ الَّذِي خَلَقَهُنَّ

"...Do not prostrate to the sun or the moon, but prostrate to Allah who created them." (proof against sun/moon worship)

Fuṣṣilat 41:37

وَلَا يَأْمُرَكُمْ أَن تَتَّخِذُوا الْمَلَائِكَةَ وَالنَّبِيِّينَ أَرْبَابًا ۗ أَيَأْمُرُكُم بِالْكُفْرِ بَعْدَ إِذْ أَنتُم مُّسْلِمُونَ

"Nor could he order you to take the angels and prophets as lords. Would he order you to disbelief after you had submitted?" (proof against angel-worship)

Āl ʿImrān 3:80

وَإِذْ قَالَ اللَّهُ يَا عِيسَى ابْنَ مَرْيَمَ أَأَنتَ قُلْتَ لِلنَّاسِ اتَّخِذُونِي وَأُمِّيَ إِلَٰهَيْنِ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ ۖ قَالَ سُبْحَانَكَ مَا يَكُونُ لِي أَنْ أَقُولَ مَا لَيْسَ لِي بِحَقٍّ

"And when Allah will say, 'O ʿĪsā, son of Maryam, did you say to the people, take me and my mother as gods besides Allah?' He will say, 'Glory be to You! It was not for me to say what I had no right to say.'" (proof against prophet-worship)

al-Māʾidah 5:116

أُولَٰئِكَ الَّذِينَ يَدْعُونَ يَبْتَغُونَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمُ الْوَسِيلَةَ أَيُّهُمْ أَقْرَبُ وَيَرْجُونَ رَحْمَتَهُ وَيَخَافُونَ عَذَابَهُ

"Those they call upon are themselves seeking the means of nearness to their Lord, [striving as to] which of them would be nearest, and hoping for His mercy and fearing His punishment." (proof against worshipping the righteous)

al-Isrāʾ 17:57

القَاعِدَةُ الرَّابِعَة Foundation 4 — The later mushrikīn are worse

The mushrikīn of our time are worse than the earlier ones: the earlier ones associated partners in ease but made worship sincere to Allah in hardship, while the later ones associate partners in both ease and hardship.

فَإِذَا رَكِبُوا فِي الْفُلْكِ دَعَوُا اللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ الدِّينَ فَلَمَّا نَجَّاهُمْ إِلَى الْبَرِّ إِذَا هُمْ يُشْرِكُونَ

"When they board a ship they call upon Allah, sincere to Him in religion; but when He delivers them to land, at once they associate others with Him."

al-ʿAnkabūt 29:65

The dividing line — الخلاصة

Tawḥīd is to single out Allah in worship just as the mushrikīn singled Him out in Lordship. This is the meaning of lā ilāha illā Allāh: there is no one worthy of worship except Allah. Affirming the Creator is not enough — worship itself must be for Him alone.

Memorize & Reflect — احفظ وتدبّر

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ

"There is no one worthy of worship except Allah." The mushrikīn affirmed Allah as Creator — what they refused was to worship Him alone. That refusal is what the kalimah corrects. Ask: is all my worship — my duʿāʾ, my hope, my fear — for Allah alone?
