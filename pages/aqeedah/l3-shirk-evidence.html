أَدِلَّةُ أَنْوَاعِ الشِّرْك

The Three Types — With Their Evidence

Year 2+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Cite Quranic and Prophetic evidence for each type of shirk
  • Explain why each dalīl proves that specific category
  • Identify key Arabic words used in the evidence (نِدًّا، ظُلْمٌ، تُشْرِكْ)
  • Explain the concept of iḥbāṭ al-ʿamal (nullification of deeds)
كَيْفَ نَعْرِفُ مَا هُوَ شِرْك؟ How Do Scholars Identify Shirk?

In Islam, we do not decide what is shirk based on personal opinion or feeling. We rely on al-dalīl (الدليل) — evidence from the Quran and the authentic Sunnah. This is what scholars mean when they say a ruling is ثَابِت بالنص (thābit bi-l-naṣṣ) — established by the text.

القرآن الكريم

The Noble Quran

Over 200 verses address shirk directly or indirectly. We study which type each verse proves.

السنة النبوية

The Prophetic Sunnah

The Prophet ﷺ named specific acts as shirk — this is how we know minor shirk's boundaries.

Method for this lesson: For each type of shirk we will study (1) the dalīl (evidence), (2) key Arabic words, and (3) what the evidence proves. After learning the evidence well, you will be able to cite it from memory — a key skill in ʿaqīdah study.

أَدِلَّةُ الشِّرْكِ الأَكْبَر Evidence for Major Shirk — الأكبر
DALĪL 1 Sūrat al-Nisāʾ 4:48

إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يَغْفِرُ أَنْ يُشْرَكَ بِهِ وَيَغْفِرُ مَا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَن يَشَاءُ

"Indeed, Allah does not forgive that partners be associated with Him, but He forgives what is less than that for whom He wills."

Sūrat al-Nisāʾ 4:48 · سورة النساء

What this proves:

  • Major shirk is the only sin for which there is no automatic forgiveness if a person dies upon it
  • The phrase يُشْرَكَ بِهِ (yushraka bihi) uses the passive form — "that a partner be associated with Him" — showing the act itself, not the person, is addressed
  • The verse distinguishes shirk from all other sins (مَا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ — what is less than that)
DALĪL 2 Sūrat Luqmān 31:13

يَا بُنَيَّ لَا تُشْرِكْ بِاللَّهِ ۖ إِنَّ الشِّرْكَ لَظُلْمٌ عَظِيمٌ

"O my son, do not associate partners with Allah. Indeed, shirk is a tremendous wrongdoing."

Sūrat Luqmān 31:13 · نصيحة لقمان لابنه

What this proves:

  • The word لَظُلْمٌ begins with the emphatic Lām (لَ) — meaning "it is indeed a wrongdoing" — this is Arabic's emphatic particle
  • Shirk is called ẓulm (wrongdoing) because it places creation at the level of the Creator — the greatest injustice
  • It is called ʿaẓīm (tremendous) — the superlative shows this is not ordinary wrongdoing
  • This verse comes from a father's sincere advice to his son — showing how serious Muslims must take this warning
DALĪL 3 Sūrat al-Zumar 39:65

وَلَقَدْ أُوحِيَ إِلَيْكَ وَإِلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكَ لَئِنْ أَشْرَكْتَ لَيَحْبَطَنَّ عَمَلُكَ

"And it has certainly been revealed to you and to those before you: if you were to associate partners with Allah, your deeds would surely be nullified."

Sūrat al-Zumar 39:65 · سورة الزمر

What this proves:

  • لَيَحْبَطَنَّ عَمَلُكَ — the word iḥbāṭ (إحباط) means complete nullification of deeds, like a fire reducing a building to ash
  • This verse was addressed to the Prophet ﷺ himself — showing that this law applies to everyone without exception
  • It was revealed to every prophet — confirming that major shirk has always been the gravest prohibition
أَدِلَّةُ الشِّرْكِ الأَصْغَر Evidence for Minor Shirk — الأصغر
DALĪL 1 Musnad Aḥmad — on riyāʾ

«إِنَّ أَخْوَفَ مَا أَخَافُ عَلَيْكُمُ الشِّرْكُ الأَصْغَرُ» فَسُئِلَ عَنْهُ فَقَالَ: «الرِّيَاءُ»

"The thing I fear most for you is minor shirk." He was asked about it and said: "It is riyāʾ — showing off."

Narrated by Aḥmad, ḥasan · عن محمود بن لبيد رضي الله عنه

What this proves:

  • The Prophet ﷺ himself named riyāʾ as الشِّرْكُ الأَصْغَرُ — this is how we know the term "minor shirk" has textual basis
  • He described it as what he feared most (أَخْوَفَ — the most-feared) — because it is hidden and easy to fall into
  • This ḥadīth is the primary proof for the entire category of "minor shirk"
DALĪL 2 Musnad Aḥmad & Abū Dāwūd — on swearing

«مَنْ حَلَفَ بِغَيْرِ اللهِ فَقَدْ أَشْرَكَ»

"Whoever swears by other than Allah has committed shirk."

Narrated by Aḥmad and Abū Dāwūd, ṣaḥīḥ · رواه أحمد وأبو داود

What this proves:

  • The Prophet ﷺ used the word أَشْرَكَ (he committed shirk) — directly calling this act by the name
  • Swearing is a form of glorification — it belongs only to Allah; swearing by creation places that creation at the level of the Creator in glorification
  • This is named shirk but does not take one outside of Islam — so it falls under the minor category
DALĪL 3 Musnad Aḥmad — on "if not for Allah and you"

«لَا تَقُولُوا: مَا شَاءَ اللهُ وَشَاءَ فُلَانٌ، وَلَكِنْ قُولُوا: مَا شَاءَ اللهُ ثُمَّ شَاءَ فُلَانٌ»

"Do not say: 'As Allah willed and so-and-so willed.' Say instead: 'As Allah willed, then so-and-so willed.'"

Narrated by Aḥmad and Abū Dāwūd · رواه أحمد وأبو داود

What this proves:

  • The word وَ (wāw — "and") joins things at the same level; using it places a person's will alongside Allah's will as equal
  • The word ثُمَّ (thumma — "then") shows sequence — Allah's will first, then the person's will as a secondary cause
  • This shows that even speech can be minor shirk — reminding us to be careful with our words
أَدِلَّةُ الشِّرْكِ الخَفِيّ Evidence for Hidden Shirk — الخفي
DALĪL 1 Adab al-Mufrad / Musnad Aḥmad

«الشِّرْكُ فِيكُمْ أَخْفَى مِنْ دَبِيبِ النَّمْلِ عَلَى صَفَاةٍ سَوْدَاءَ فِي ظُلْمَةِ اللَّيْلِ»

"Shirk among you is more subtle than the creeping of ants upon a smooth black stone in the darkness of night."

Al-Bukhārī in al-Adab al-Mufrad · رواه البخاري في الأدب المفرد

What this proves:

  • The comparison to ants creeping on a smooth black stone in darkness — three layers of hiddenness — shows how subtle this shirk is
  • This is why the Prophet ﷺ taught us a specific duʿāʾ to seek refuge from it — because it is hard to see
  • The phrase فِيكُمْ ("among you") addresses believers — showing that believers, not just disbelievers, must guard against this
DALĪL 2 Sūrat al-Kahf 18:110 — on Ikhlaṣ

فَمَن كَانَ يَرْجُو لِقَاءَ رَبِّهِ فَلْيَعْمَلْ عَمَلًا صَالِحًا وَلَا يُشْرِكْ بِعِبَادَةِ رَبِّهِ أَحَدًا

"Whoever hopes to meet his Lord — let him do righteous deeds and not associate anyone in the worship of his Lord."

Sūrat al-Kahf 18:110 · سورة الكهف — آخر الآية

What this proves:

  • The phrase وَلَا يُشْرِكْ بِعِبَادَةِ رَبِّهِ أَحَدًا — "do not associate anyone in the worship of your Lord" — points to purifying the intention
  • The scholars explain this verse refers to hidden shirk — performing deeds while seeking the attention or praise of others
  • Ibn Kathīr: This verse combines the two conditions for accepted deeds — correct action (following the Prophet ﷺ) and sincere intention (for Allah alone)
DALĪL 3 Ḥadīth Qudsī — on Day of Judgement

«أَنَا أَغْنَى الشُّرَكَاءِ عَنِ الشِّرْكِ، مَنْ عَمِلَ عَمَلًا أَشْرَكَ فِيهِ مَعِي غَيْرِي، تَرَكْتُهُ وَشِرْكَهُ»

"I am the One most free of need for partners. Whoever does a deed and associates someone else with Me in it — I leave him and his shirk." (Allah says on the Day of Judgement regarding deeds done for show)

Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim · رواه مسلم

What this proves:

  • Allah says He is أَغْنَى الشُّرَكَاءِ عَنِ الشِّرْكِ — "the most free of need for partners" — He has no need for our deeds to be shared with anything
  • The key phrase: تَرَكْتُهُ وَشِرْكَهُ — "I leave him and his shirk" — the deed is abandoned, not rewarded
  • This is the grave consequence of hidden shirk — a deed done with any other intention alongside Allah's pleasure is rejected

تَدْرِيبُ القَوَاعِد Grammar Focus — The Emphatic Lām (لَ) + نون التوكيد

In Quranic Arabic, the particle لَ (Lām al-Tawkīd — the emphatic Lām) combined with نَّ (nūn al-tawkīd al-thaqīlah) creates extreme emphasis. Look at these examples from our lesson:

Emphatic phrase Literal emphasis Source
إِنَّ الشِّرْكَ لَظُلْمٌ عَظِيمٌ Shirk is indeed truly a tremendous wrongdoing 31:13
لَيَحْبَطَنَّ عَمَلُكَ Your deeds will surely and certainly be nullified 39:65
وَلَقَدْ بَعَثْنَا We have indeed and certainly sent 16:36

الحديث القدسي هو جزء من القرآن الكريم. A ḥadīth qudsī is part of the Quran.

«ثُمَّ» و«وَ» يعطيان نفس المعنى في اللغة العربية. The words "thumma" (ثم — then) and "wāw" (و — and) mean the same thing in Arabic.
