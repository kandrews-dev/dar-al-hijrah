أَهْلُ البَيْتِ وَأَزْوَاجُ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ

The Household and the Wives of the Prophet ﷺ

شَرْحُ الشَّيْخِ صَالِحٍ الفَوْزَان · ص ١٧٧–١٨١
Sharḥ al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah, Ṣāliḥ al-Fawzān — pp. 177–181.

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Say who أَهْلُ البَيْت are, and the qualification al-Fawzān attaches to loving them.
  • Give the two reasons the ḥadīth gives for loving the kinsfolk of the Prophet ﷺ.
  • State precisely in what respect the wives are أُمَّهَاتُ المُؤْمِنِين — and in what respect they are not.
  • Give al-Fawzān’s reasoning for why الثَّرِيد is the comparison chosen for ʿĀʾishah.
مَكَانَةُ أَهْلِ بَيْتِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ عِنْدَ أَهْلِ السُّنَّة The Standing of the Prophet’s ﷺ Household with Ahl al-Sunnah

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَيُحِبُّونَ أَهْلَ بَيْتِ رَسُولِ اللهِ ﷺ وَيَتَوَلَّوْنَهُمْ، وَيَحْفَظُونَ فِيهِمْ وَصِيَّةَ رَسُولِ اللهِ ﷺ حَيْثُ قَالَ: «أُذَكِّرُكُمُ اللهَ فِي أَهْلِ بَيْتِي». وَقَالَ أَيْضًا لِلْعَبَّاسِ عَمِّهِ — وَقَدِ اشْتَكَى إِلَيْهِ أَنَّ بَعْضَ قُرَيْشٍ يَجْفُو بَنِي هَاشِمٍ: «وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ حَتَّى يُحِبُّوكُمْ لِلَّهِ وَلِقَرَابَتِي». وَقَالَ: «إِنَّ اللهَ اصْطَفَى بَنِي إِسْمَاعِيلَ، وَاصْطَفَى مِنْ بَنِي إِسْمَاعِيلَ كِنَانَةَ، وَاصْطَفَى مِنْ كِنَانَةَ قُرَيْشًا، وَاصْطَفَى مِنْ قُرَيْشٍ بَنِي هَاشِمٍ».

الشَّرْح · the explanation

أَهْلُ البَيْت are the family of the Prophet ﷺ upon whom the ṣadaqah is forbidden — بَنُو هَاشِمٍ and بَنُو عَبْدِ المُطَّلِبِ. And the wives of the Prophet ﷺ and his daughters are of his household, as Allah said:

إِنَّمَا يُرِيدُ اللَّهُ لِيُذْهِبَ عَنْكُمُ الرِّجْسَ أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ وَيُطَهِّرَكُمْ تَطْهِيرًا

"Allah only wishes to remove impurity from you, O people of the House, and to purify you with a thorough purification."

Sūrat al-Aḥzāb 33:33 · سُورَةُ الأَحْزَاب

Read where that āyah sits. It comes in the middle of a passage addressed to the wives — «and remain in your houses… and establish the prayer» — and the words O people of the House fall inside it. That is why al-Fawzān lists the wives among Ahl al-Bayt without argument: the only place the Qurʾān uses the phrase, it is speaking to them.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

And the ground of the obligation: loving them and honouring them — because Allah and His Messenger have both commanded it:

قُلْ لَا أَسْأَلُكُمْ عَلَيْهِ أَجْرًا إِلَّا الْمَوَدَّةَ فِي الْقُرْبَى

"Say: I ask of you no reward for it, except affection for the kinsfolk."

Sūrat al-Shūrā 42:23 · سُورَةُ الشُّورَى

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Such as al-ʿAbbās and his sons, and ʿAlī and his sons — and then the qualification, which the matn does not state and the sharḥ does: as for one who opposes the Sunnah and does not stand upright upon the religion, then he is not taken as an ally.

That one clause separates this creed from the Rāfiḍah on one side and the Nawāṣib on the other. Kinship to the Prophet ﷺ is a real and honoured right; it is not an immunity. Loving the household is loving them as Muslims who are also his kin — not a loyalty that survives the abandoning of his Sunnah.

وَيَتَوَلَّوْنَهُمْ — that is: they love them; from الوَلَايَة, with a fatḥah on the wāw, and it is love. وَيَحْفَظُونَ فِيهِمْ وَصِيَّةَ رَسُولِ اللهِ ﷺ — that is: they act by it and apply it.

أُذَكِّرُكُمُ اللهَ فِي أَهْلِ بَيْتِي

"I remind you of Allah regarding the people of my household."

رَوَاهُ مُسْلِمٌ

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān places it: this was at غَدِير خُمّ, a pool on the road to Madīnah, which he ﷺ passed on his return from the Farewell Ḥajj, and he gave a sermon there — of which was what the Shaykh mentioned.

And أُذَكِّرُكُمُ اللهَ فِي أَهْلِ بَيْتِي means: I remind you of what Allah commanded concerning my household — of respecting them, honouring them, and undertaking their rights.

Note what the wording is and is not. It is a reminder of an existing command, not the creation of a new rank. He ﷺ does not say «obey them» or «follow them» — he says remember Allah in how you treat them.

وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ حَتَّى يُحِبُّوكُمْ لِلَّهِ وَلِقَرَابَتِي

"By the One in whose Hand is my soul, they will not believe until they love you for Allah's sake and for my kinship."

al-Fawzān cites it as رَوَاهُ الإِمَامُ أَحْمَدُ وَغَيْرُهُ

الشَّرْح · the explanation

تَنْبِيهٌ — al-Fawzān gives this ḥadīth’s attribution without stating a grading. Per the rule in Lesson 22 this page reports the attribution and supplies no grading of its own. The obligation does not rest on it alone: the āyah of al-Shūrā and the ḥadīth of Ghadīr Khumm (Muslim) establish it.

He is العَبَّاسُ بْنُ عَبْدِ المُطَّلِبِ; وَقَدِ اشْتَكَى إِلَيْهِ means he informed him of what he disliked; and الجَفَاء is the abandoning of kindness and of the ties of kinship.

لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ — that is: the complete obligatory faith, as in Lesson 45. And حَتَّى يُحِبُّوكُمْ لِلَّهِ وَلِقَرَابَتِي — that is, for two things:

لِلَّهِseeking nearness to Allah by it — because they are of His allies
وَلِقَرَابَتِيbecause they are the kinsfolk of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ — and in that is a pleasing of him and an honouring of him

The first reason is the one that supplies the qualification given above. They are to be loved «for Allah» because they are of His allies — so the love is owed on a ground that a man can forfeit, alongside a ground of kinship that he cannot.

إِنَّ اللهَ اصْطَفَى بَنِي إِسْمَاعِيلَ، وَاصْطَفَى مِنْ بَنِي إِسْمَاعِيلَ كِنَانَةَ، وَاصْطَفَى مِنْ كِنَانَةَ قُرَيْشًا، وَاصْطَفَى مِنْ قُرَيْشٍ بَنِي هَاشِمٍ، وَاصْطَفَانِي مِنْ بَنِي هَاشِمٍ

"Allah chose the children of Ismāʿīl, and chose Kinānah from the children of Ismāʿīl, and chose Quraysh from Kinānah, and chose Banū Hāshim from Quraysh, and chose me from Banū Hāshim."

رَوَاهُ أَحْمَدُ وَمُسْلِمٌ

الشَّرْح · the explanation

اصْطَفَى — that is: chose; and الصَّفْوَة is the choicest part. بَنِي إِسْمَاعِيلَ — the son of Ibrāhīm al-Khalīl عَلَيْهِمَا السَّلَام. كِنَانَة — the name of a tribe; their father is كِنَانَةُ بْنُ خُزَيْمَةَ. And بَنُو هَاشِمٍ are the descendants of هَاشِمِ بْنِ عَبْدِ مَنَافِ بْنِ قُصَيِّ بْنِ كِلَابٍ.

And the shāhid of the ḥadīth: that in it is a proof of the excellence of the Arabs, and that Quraysh are the best of the Arabs, and that Banū Hāshim are the best of Quraysh, and that the Messenger ﷺ is the best of Banū Hāshim — so he is the best of creation in himself and the best of them in lineage.

بَنُو إِسْمَاعِيل  →  كِنَانَة  →  قُرَيْش  →  بَنُو هَاشِم  →  مُحَمَّد ط

Four narrowings, each one a choosing — and the last term is a single man.

And notice what that does to the question this section is really about. The honour of Banū Hāshim is derived — it is the honour of being the house the chosen one came from. It is not a standing that competes with his, and it is not inherited independently of him. Which is why loving them is counted as honouring him in the previous ḥadīth.

مَكَانَةُ أَزْوَاجِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ · أُمَّهَاتُ المُؤْمِنِين The Wives of the Prophet ﷺ — the Mothers of the Believers

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَيَتَوَلَّوْنَ أَزْوَاجَ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ أُمَّهَاتِ المُؤْمِنِينَ، وَيُؤْمِنُونَ بِأَنَّهُنَّ أَزْوَاجُهُ فِي الآخِرَةِ؛ خُصُوصًا خَدِيجَةَ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهَا، أُمَّ أَكْثَرِ أَوْلَادِهِ، وَأَوَّلَ مَنْ آمَنَ بِهِ وَعَاضَدَهُ عَلَى أَمْرِهِ، وَكَانَ لَهَا مِنْهُ المَنْزِلَةُ العَالِيَةُ؛ وَالصِّدِّيقَةَ بِنْتَ الصِّدِّيقِ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهَا، الَّتِي قَالَ فِيهَا النَّبِيُّ ﷺ: «فَضْلُ عَائِشَةَ عَلَى النِّسَاءِ كَفَضْلِ الثَّرِيدِ عَلَى سَائِرِ الطَّعَامِ».

الشَّرْح · the explanation

That is: they love them and revere them, because they are أُمَّهَاتُ المُؤْمِنِين — and here al-Fawzān draws a distinction that a great deal of confusion turns on:

mothers INrespect and reverence, and the forbiddance of marrying them to the Ummah
but NOT inthe remaining rulings — their ruling is the ruling of unrelated women, as regards the forbiddance of seclusion with them and of looking at them

Motherhood here is a legal status, not a biological fiction. It gives reverence and it closes marriage — and it does not open what maḥram status would open. The Qurʾān itself shows this: it calls them mothers in one āyah, and in another commands the believers to address them from behind a screen.

النَّبِيُّ أَوْلَى بِالْمُؤْمِنِينَ مِنْ أَنْفُسِهِمْ وَأَزْوَاجُهُ أُمَّهَاتُهُمْ

"The Prophet is closer to the believers than their own selves, and his wives are their mothers."

Sūrat al-Aḥzāb 33:6 · سُورَةُ الأَحْزَاب

وَإِذَا سَأَلْتُمُوهُنَّ مَتَاعًا فَاسْأَلُوهُنَّ مِنْ وَرَاءِ حِجَابٍ ذَلِكُمْ أَطْهَرُ لِقُلُوبِكُمْ وَقُلُوبِهِنَّ وَمَا كَانَ لَكُمْ أَنْ تُؤْذُوا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ وَلَا أَنْ تَنْكِحُوا أَزْوَاجَهُ مِنْ بَعْدِهِ أَبَدًا

"And when you ask them for something, ask them from behind a screen; that is purer for your hearts and theirs. And it is not for you to hurt the Messenger of Allah, nor ever to marry his wives after him."

Sūrat al-Aḥzāb 33:53 · سُورَةُ الأَحْزَاب

الشَّرْح · the explanation

And he ﷺ died leaving nine: عَائِشَة · حَفْصَة · زَيْنَبُ بِنْتُ جَحْشٍ · أُمُّ سَلَمَةَ · صَفِيَّة · مَيْمُونَة · أُمُّ حَبِيبَةَ · سَوْدَة · جُوَيْرِيَة رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُنَّ.

As for خَدِيجَة, he married her before the prophethood, and did not marry another alongside her until she died. And he ﷺ married زَيْنَبَ بِنْتَ خُزَيْمَةَ الهِلَالِيَّةَ, and she remained only a short while, then died. These are the total of those he consummated marriage with: eleven رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُنَّ.

And they believe that they are his wives in the Hereafter — and in this is an honour for them and a mighty excellence.

خَدِيجَةُ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهَا Khadījah رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهَا

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān numbers the four merits the author singles out:

١the mother of most of his children — for all his children were from her except Ibrāhīm, who was from Māriyah al-Qibṭiyyah
٢the first to believe in him — unrestrictedly, on one view (which is the one the Shaykh mentions here); or the first of the WOMEN to believe, on the other
٣the first to support and aid him at the beginning of his affair — and her support of him was in the greatest of the times of need
٤she had the highest standing with him — he loved her and mentioned her often and praised her

The third is the one that is easy to read past. Anyone can support a cause that is winning. Khadījah's support came when there was no cause yet — one man, one terrified night, and a wife who believed him before there was anything to join.

الصِّدِّيقَةُ بِنْتُ الصِّدِّيق al-Ṣiddīqah, Daughter of al-Ṣiddīq

الشَّرْح · the explanation

That is: عَائِشَةُ بِنْتُ أَبِي بَكْرٍ; and الصِّدِّيق is one who goes to the utmost in truthfulness — and the Prophet ﷺ gave Abū Bakr that title.

And her excellences, as al-Fawzān lists them: she was the most beloved of the Prophet's ﷺ wives to him; he married no virgin besides her; the revelation would descend upon him while he was in her blanket; Allah declared her innocent of what the people of the slander accused her of; she was the most learned in fiqh of his wives; and the senior Companions, when a matter was difficult for them, would ask her for a ruling; and the Messenger ﷺ died in her house, between her chest and her throat, and was buried in her house.

The senior Companions used to ask her. That is not a domestic detail; it is a statement about the transmission of the religion — and it is why reviling her is not a private insult but an attack on a channel the Sharīʿah came through.

فَضْلُ عَائِشَةَ عَلَى النِّسَاءِ كَفَضْلِ الثَّرِيدِ عَلَى سَائِرِ الطَّعَامِ

"The excellence of ʿĀʾishah over women is like the excellence of tharīd over all other food."

رَوَاهُ البُخَارِيُّ وَمُسْلِمٌ وَغَيْرُهُمَا

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Why tharīd, of all comparisons? al-Fawzān works it out, and the reasoning is a small pleasure in itself:

— al-tharīd is the best of foods, because it is bread and meat.

— And the bread is from wheat, and it is the best of the staples; and the meat is the best of what is eaten with bread.

— So if the meat is the master of what accompanies, and the wheat is the master of the staple — and the two of them together are the tharīd — then the tharīd is the best.

So the comparison is not «a nice dish». It is the one food that is the best of two categories at once, which is a precise way of saying what is being claimed for her: not merely the best in one respect, but the joining of excellences that are usually found apart. And the ḥadīth is a comparison of excellences, not of persons — it says faḍl, as Lesson 29 said of the moon.

Check Your Understanding · MCQ أَسْئِلَة

Who are Ahl al-Bayt, per al-Fawzān?

ʿAlī, Fāṭimah, al-Ḥasan and al-Ḥusayn only
Banū Hāshim and Banū ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib — those upon whom ṣadaqah is forbidden — and his wives and daughters
All of Quraysh
His descendants through Fāṭimah alone

What qualification does al-Fawzān attach to taking them as allies?

That they must be known personally
That one who opposes the Sunnah and does not stand upright upon the religion is not — kinship is a right, not an immunity
That they must be descendants in the male line
That they must be living in the Ḥaramayn

What is notable about where al-Aḥzāb 33:33 sits?

It comes after the verse of the ḥijāb
It falls in the middle of a passage addressed to the WIVES — so the one place the Qurʾān uses "people of the House", it is speaking to them
It was revealed at Ghadīr Khumm
It abrogates an earlier ruling

What two reasons does the ḥadīth give for loving the kinsfolk?

Their lineage and their knowledge
For Allah — because they are of His allies; and for his kinship ﷺ — in which is a pleasing and an honouring of him
Their precedence and their jihād
Their poverty and their patience

What does the chain of choosing establish?

That lineage determines a man’s rank with Allah
That the Arabs, then Quraysh, then Banū Hāshim, then the Messenger ﷺ — so he is the best of creation in himself AND in lineage
That only Banū Hāshim may lead the prayer
That Ismāʿīl was better than Isḥāq

In what respect are the wives "Mothers of the Believers" — and in what respect not?

In every respect, as with a birth mother
In respect and reverence and the forbiddance of marrying them; but their ruling is that of unrelated women regarding seclusion and looking
Only in inheritance
Only in being addressed as "mother"

Which of Khadījah’s four merits concerns the timing of her support?

That she was the mother of most of his children
That she was the first to support and aid him at the beginning of his affair — and her support was in the greatest of the times of need
That she had the highest standing with him
That he married no one alongside her

Why is tharīd the comparison chosen for ʿĀʾishah?

Because it was the Prophet’s ﷺ favourite dish
Because it is bread and meat — the master of the staple joined to the master of what accompanies it, so it is the best of two categories at once
Because it was the food of the poor
Because it is easily shared

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

أُذَكِّرُكُمُ اللهَ فِي أَهْلِ بَيْتِي

Note what he ﷺ asked for and what he did not. Not obedience to them, and not a station — but that you remember Allah in how you treat them. The right is real, and it is a right of honour and kindness, which is exactly how Ahl al-Sunnah hold it.
