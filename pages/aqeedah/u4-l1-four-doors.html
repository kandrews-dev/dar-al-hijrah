The Four Forbidden Doors

أربعة أبواب نتجنبهاFour Doors the People of the Sunnah Refuse to Enter

In Lesson 0 you learned the golden rule: affirm and negate as revelation does, "without taḥrīf, taʿṭīl, takyīf, or tamthīl." These four words are the four doors of error that Ahl al-Sunnah keep shut. Master them, and you can recognise nearly every deviation in this subject. The first two attack the texts and their meanings; the last two attack the "how" and the likeness of the attributes.

التَّحْرِيف

al-Taḥrīf

Distorting the text or its meaning

1

Taḥrīf is to change a text away from its true meaning — either by altering the words, or (more commonly) by twisting the meaning while keeping the words. The deviants call this taʾwīl ("interpretation"), but Ahl al-Sunnah call it by its real name: distortion.

Example

Reading «يَدُ اللهِ» (the Hand of Allah) and forcing it to mean only "His power" or "His blessing" — emptying the word of its real meaning.

Why it is wrong

It makes the human mind, not revelation, the judge of what Allah may be described with — the opposite of tawqīf.

التَّعْطِيل

al-Taʿṭīl

Denying / emptying the attribute

2

Taʿṭīl is to deny an attribute that Allah affirmed for Himself, leaving the "vessel" empty. Taḥrīf is usually the method; taʿṭīl is the result — you twist the text (taḥrīf) in order to strip away the attribute (taʿṭīl).

Example

Saying "Allah does not really rise over the Throne" or "He has no real attribute of mercy, only a created effect" — denying what He affirmed.

Who fell into it

The Jahmiyyah and Muʿtazilah — the Muʿaṭṭilah — who feared that affirming attributes would liken Allah to creation.

التَّكْيِيف

al-Takyīf

Asking or describing "how?"

3

Takyīf is to specify the manner of an attribute — to say or imagine "how" Allah's hand or rising is. We affirm that Allah has these attributes; we do not claim to know the kayfiyyah (the "how"). The famous answer of Imām Mālik captures it perfectly.

الاسْتِوَاءُ مَعْلُومٌ، وَالْكَيْفُ مَجْهُولٌ، وَالإِيمَانُ بِهِ وَاجِبٌ، وَالسُّؤَالُ عَنْهُ بِدْعَةٌ

"The istiwāʾ (rising) is known, the how is unknown, believing in it is obligatory, and asking about [its how] is an innovation." — Imām Mālik ibn Anas (رحمه الله)

التَّمْثِيل

al-Tamthīl

Likening Allah to His creation

4

Tamthīl (and its near-synonym tashbīh) is to claim Allah's attribute is like the creation's — "His hand is like my hand," "His hearing is like my hearing." This is the error opposite to taʿṭīl, and it is the very thing «لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ» was revealed to forbid.

Example

Imagining Allah's "establishing over the Throne" as a created being sitting on a chair, needing it and resembling us.

Who fell into it

The Mushabbihah — anthropomorphists who affirmed the attributes but compared the Creator to the created.

القاعدة الجامعةThe Principle that Closes All Four Doors

Ahl al-Sunnah hold a precise principle that shuts every one of these doors at once — the rule of "discourse on the attributes follows discourse on the Essence" (الكلام في الصفات فرعٌ عن الكلام في الذات):

Just as we affirm that Allah has a real Essence (dhāt) unlike any other essence — we do not deny it, nor do we claim to know its "how," nor liken it to creation — so too we affirm His attributes as real, without knowing their "how," and without likening them. The attribute follows the Essence: real, unique, and beyond comparison.

Closes the door of denial

Against taḥrīf & taʿṭīl: we affirm the meaning the texts plainly carry; we do not twist or empty it.

Closes the door of resemblance

Against takyīf & tamthīl: we do not ask "how," nor liken Him to creation — for nothing is like Him.

التمارينExercises
Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
التحريف تغيير النص عن معناه الصحيح. Taḥrīf is changing a text away from its true meaning.
التكييف هو السؤال عن كيفية الصفة، وهو ممنوع. Takyīf is asking about the "how" of an attribute, and it is forbidden.
قال الإمام مالك: «الاستواء مجهول والكيف معلوم». Imām Mālik said: "The istiwāʾ is unknown and the how is known."
التمثيل هو ما نفته آية «ليس كمثله شيء». Tamthīl is exactly what "There is nothing like Him" negates.
التحريف والتعطيل يتعلقان بالكيفية والمماثلة. Taḥrīf and taʿṭīl are about the "how" and the likeness.
Exercise Bاختر الإجابة الصحيحة

من قال: «الاستواء معلوم، والكيف مجهول...»؟ Who said: "The istiwāʾ is known, the how is unknown..."?

Someone says: "Allah's 'Hand' just means His power — He has no real hand." Which door is this?

Someone says: "Allah's Hand is exactly like a human hand." Which door is this?

«الكلام في الصفات فرع عن الكلام في...»؟ "Discourse on the attributes is a branch of discourse on…"?

Exercise Cطابق الخطأ ببابه — Match the Error

Identify which of the four doors each statement falls into.

"Let us picture exactly how Allah descends each night."

"'Allah rose over the Throne' really means 'He conquered it.'"

"Allah's seeing is just like our seeing in every way."
