البدعة والسنة

Innovation & the Sunnah

أهداف الدرس — Objectives

  • Know the meaning of البدعة (innovation in religion).
  • Memorize the two ḥadīths that reject it — and understand why.
  • Learn the two conditions for any worship to be accepted by Allah.
  • Distinguish "innovation in worldly things" (allowed) from بدعة في الدين (rejected).

The religion of Allah was completed before the Prophet ﷺ left this world. Allah said: «ٱلْيَوْمَ أَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِينَكُمْ» — "Today I have perfected your religion for you" (المائدة 5:3). What is complete cannot be added to. To invent a new act of worship, or a new ritual in the religion, is to claim — even unintentionally — that the Prophet ﷺ left something incomplete. That is البدعة, and Allah rejects it.

ما هي البدعة؟ Part 1 — What is البدعة?

البدعة في الدين = a newly-invented matter in religion, done as worship, that has no basis in the Qur'an, the Sunnah, or the practice of the early believers (السلف). It can be in a belief (بدعة قولية) or in an action (بدعة عملية).

What is NOT بدعة:

  • Worldly inventions — cars, phones, modern medicine, microphones in the mosque. These are not acts of worship; they are tools.
  • New ways of organizing what the religion already taught — printing the Qur'an, recording lessons, building schools. The form is new; the worship is not.
  • Things the Prophet ﷺ did once and never forbade — these are part of the Sunnah, not innovations.
الحديثان Part 2 — The two ḥadīths that close the door

مَنْ أَحْدَثَ فِي أَمْرِنَا هَذَا مَا لَيْسَ مِنْهُ فَهُوَ رَدٌّ

"Whoever introduces into this affair of ours something that is not from it — it is rejected."

رواه البخاري ومسلم · al-Bukhārī & Muslim — «رَدّ» means refused, returned, not accepted

إيَّاكُمْ وَمُحْدَثَاتِ الْأُمُورِ، فَإِنَّ كُلَّ مُحْدَثَةٍ بِدْعَةٌ، وَكُلَّ بِدْعَةٍ ضَلَالَةٌ

"Beware of newly-invented matters — for every newly-invented matter is a بدعة, and every بدعة is misguidance."

رواه أبو داود والترمذي · صحيح — Abū Dāwūd, al-Tirmidhī · authentic

The Prophet ﷺ said كل — "every innovation is misguidance." There is no such thing in his words as "good innovation" in the religion. If a beautiful act of worship were truly part of the religion, the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions would have done it. They did not lack devotion to Allah.

شرطا قبول العمل Part 3 — The two conditions for accepted worship

Any act of worship reaches Allah only when it meets two conditions together:

الإخلاصal-Ikhlāṣ · sincerity

The deed is done for Allah alone, seeking His face, not showing off. (This was U1 + Ḥadīth 1.) Without sincerity the deed is shirk — rejected.

المتابعةal-Mutābaʿah · following the Sunnah

The deed is done in the way the Prophet ﷺ did it, not invented. Without following, the deed is بدعة — rejected.

الميزانان — The two scales

Two ḥadīths weigh every deed: «إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ» weighs the inside (الإخلاص); «مَنْ أَحْدَثَ فِي أَمْرِنَا…» weighs the outside (المتابعة). A deed is accepted only when it passes both scales. Sincere worship that was never taught by the Prophet ﷺ is still rejected.

لماذا حذّر السلف منها؟ Part 4 — Why the Salaf were so strict about it

البدعة is dangerous because:

  • It implies the religion was incomplete — and Allah said it is complete.
  • It usually pushes out a real سُنّة. As Ibn Taymiyyah said: "No people invented a بدعة except that, by it, they killed something equivalent to it from the Sunnah."
  • It is harder to repent from than a sin — because the doer thinks he is doing good. The sinner knows he is sinning; the مبتدع thinks he is being pious.
  • It is the doorway by which earlier nations were corrupted — and the Prophet ﷺ warned this Ummah would follow them.
قاعدة عملية للأسرة Part 5 — A practical rule for the family

For every act of worship — every duʿāʾ, every dhikr, every ritual, every celebration — ask two simple questions: (1) Did the Prophet ﷺ do this, or order it, or approve it? If yes, do it. (2) Is there clear evidence from the Qur'an or the Sunnah for it? If no — leave it. The Companions were the most devout of this Ummah; whatever they did not do as worship, is not worship.

احفظ — Memorize

كلُّ بِدْعَةٍ ضَلالَةٌ، وكلُّ ضَلالَةٍ في النَّار

"Every بدعة is misguidance, and every misguidance is in the Fire." — the warning of the Prophet ﷺ. Hold to the Sunnah, follow the Salaf, and ask Allah for thabāt on the clear path.
