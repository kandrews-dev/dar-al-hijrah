العَقِيدَةُ الوَاسِطِيَّة

al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah

العَقِيدَةُ الوَاسِطِيَّة is the creed of شَيْخُ الإِسْلَامِ أَحْمَدُ بْنُ تَيْمِيَّة, written at the request of a judge from Wāsiṭ — hence its name. It is short, and it is the most widely taught statement of the creed of Ahl al-Sunnah on the names and attributes of Allah: the author brings the evidence from the Qur’ān, then from the Sunnah, and affirms each attribute exactly as it came, without distortion, divestment, specifying a how, or likening.

This track takes the matn in the author’s own order, section by section, with the sharḥ of Shaykh صَالِحٌ الفَوْزَان — and Shaykh مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ صَالِحٍ العُثَيْمِين consulted where a point needs more. It is a long text, and the track is being built in order; the sections already live are below, followed by the full plan.

By the end of this track — أَهْدَافُ المَسَار

  • State the creed of Ahl al-Sunnah on the names and attributes, and its evidence.
  • Apply the four exclusions — تَحْرِيف · تَعْطِيل · تَكْيِيف · تَمْثِيل — to any statement you read.
  • Affirm every attribute the author brings, with its proof from the Qur’ān or the Sunnah.
  • Explain al-istiwāʾ, al-ʿuluww and al-maʿiyyah without contradiction between them.
  • State what belief in the Last Day and in al-qadar includes, in detail.
  • Give the position of Ahl al-Sunnah on the Companions, on īmān, and on the karāmāt of the awliyāʾ.
الدُّرُوسُ المَنْشُورَة Lessons Live Now

Lesson 1

البَسْمَلَةُ وَخُطْبَةُ الافْتِتَاح

The Basmalah and the Opening Address

Lesson 1 of al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah with the sharḥ of al-Fawzān: the basmalah, the meaning of the name Allāh, al-Raḥmān and al-Raḥīm, the two kinds of guidance, and how ʿabduhu wa rasūluhu refutes both excess and neglect concerning the Prophet ﷺ.

Lesson 2

الفِرْقَةُ النَّاجِيَةُ المَنْصُورَة

The Saved and Aided Group

Who ahl al-sunnah wa-l-jamāʿah are, why they are named after the Sunnah while the sects are named after their innovations, their leaders or their deeds, and Ibn Masʿūd on being the jamāʿah alone.

Lesson 3

أَرْكَانُ الإِيمَانِ السِّتَّة

The Six Pillars of Īmān

Īmān defined in the language and in the Sharīʿah, then each of the six pillars in turn, with the difference between a nabī and a rasūl and the stages of qadar.

Lesson 4

مِنْ غَيْرِ تَحْرِيفٍ وَلَا تَعْطِيل

The Four Doors That Are Closed

Taḥrīf, taʿṭīl, takyīf and tamthīl each defined exactly, with al-Fawzān’s concrete examples of distortion and Imām Mālik’s answer on al-istiwāʾ.

Lesson 5

مَوْقِفُ أَهْلِ السُّنَّةِ وَالإِلْحَادُ فِي الأَسْمَاء

The Position of Ahl al-Sunnah, and Deviating in the Names

Al-Shūrā 42:11 as the constitution of the names and attributes, how Ahl al-Sunnah answer the charge of likening, al-Fawzān’s five kinds of ilḥād in Allah’s names, and why He knows Himself best.

Lesson 6

صَادِقُونَ مَصْدُوقُونَ وَالجَمْعُ بَيْنَ النَّفْيِ وَالإِثْبَات

The Messengers’ Chain, and Negation with Affirmation

Ṣādiqūn maṣdūqūn as an authentication of the messengers’ chain, those who speak about Allah without knowledge, al-Ṣāffāt 37:180–182, and the method of negation with affirmation that governs every attribute in the book.

Lesson 7

الرَّفِيقُ عَلَى الصِّرَاطِ وَسُورَةُ الإِخْلَاص

The Company on the Path, and Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ

The four categories of those Allah favoured and the company they give on the ṣirāṭ, then the first proof of Part One — Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ, why it equals a third of the Qurʾān, and how it divides between affirmation and negation.

Lesson 8

آيَةُ الكُرْسِيّ · أَعْظَمُ آيَةٍ فِي كِتَابِه

Āyat al-Kursī — the Greatest Āyah

Why Āyat al-Kursī is the greatest āyah, al-Ḥayy al-Qayyūm as the greatest name, al-Fawzān’s clause-by-clause negation-and-affirmation reading of the whole āyah, the three kinds of ʿuluww, and the ḥadīth of protection through the night.

Lesson 9

هُوَ الأَوَّلُ وَالآخِرُ وَالظَّاهِرُ وَالبَاطِن

The First, the Last, the Manifest, the Hidden

Al-Ḥadīd 57:3 as the Prophet ﷺ explained it himself, Ibn al-Qayyim on the four names as two facing pairs, then tawakkul upon the Living who does not die, and al-Ḥakīm al-Khabīr.

Lesson 10

إِحَاطَةُ العِلْمِ وَالسَّمْعُ وَالبَصَرُ وَالمَشِيئَة

His Knowledge, His Hearing and Sight, His Will

The five keys of the unseen and the ruling on claiming them, kitāb mubīn as al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ, al-Shawkānī on what al-Shūrā 42:11 demolishes, and the will of Allah with what the Salaf said when something pleased them.

Lesson 11

الإِرَادَتَانِ وَإِثْبَاتُ المَحَبَّة

The Two Wills, and the Attribute of Love

Al-irādah al-kawniyyah against al-sharʿiyyah with al-Fawzān’s three differences and how each of the Jabriyyah and Qadariyyah took only half; then the attribute of love, āyat al-imtiḥān, and Ibn al-Qayyim on the two kinds of Allah’s turning.

Lesson 12

الرَّحْمَةُ وَالغَضَبُ وَالمَجِيءُ لِفَصْلِ القَضَاء

Mercy, Anger, and His Coming to Judge

Ibn al-Qayyim’s grammatical proof distinguishing al-Raḥmān from al-Raḥīm, the mercy Allah wrote upon Himself, His pleasure as the highest bliss, His anger and retribution, and the affirmation of His coming by His Essence — with why interpreting it as His command is taḥrīf.

Lesson 13

الوَجْهُ وَاليَدَانِ وَالعَيْنَان

The Face, the Two Hands, and the Eyes

Why reading the Face as the Essence or the reward is void, al-Fawzān’s three arguments that “hand” does not mean power or favour — including that Iblīs could have answered back — and the Arabic usage that explains bi-aʿyuninā beside ʿalā ʿaynī.

Lesson 14

المَكْرُ وَالعِزَّةُ وَنَفْيُ المِثْلِ وَالشَّرِيك

Stratagem, Might, and the Negating of Like and Partner

The argument that a mountain is never called “hearing and seeing,” the two kinds of kayd and why only the praiseworthy belongs to Allah, the rule that the verb is broader than the name, and that the Qurʾān is detailed in what it affirms and summary in what it denies.

Lesson 15

تَمَامُ نَفْيِ الشَّرِيكِ وَبُرْهَانُ التَّمَانُع

Completing the Negation of a Partner, and the Proof of al-Tamānuʿ

The tasbīḥ of all creation, the four attributes of al-Furqān 25:2 and the party each one answers, the two-step proof that a plurality of gods is incoherent, and the courtly analogy the Qurʾān forbids by name.

Lesson 16

الاسْتِوَاءُ عَلَى العَرْشِ وَالعُلُوُّ عَلَى المَخْلُوقَات

The Istiwāʾ upon the Throne, and the Highness above the Creation

The seven āyāt of al-istiwāʾ and the one wording they share, the four senses of istawā in Arabic, al-Fawzān’s four grounds for rejecting the reading “he seized power” — including the argument from the particle thumma — and the two differences between al-ʿuluww and al-istiwāʾ.

Lesson 17

المَعِيَّةُ العَامَّةُ وَالخَاصَّة

The Maʿiyyah of Allah: General and Particular

The two kinds of maʿiyyah and what each one entails, how Sūrat al-Mujādilah interprets its own maʿiyyah by enclosing it in knowledge, the two conditions of the particular maʿiyyah, and why being WITH the creation never competed with being ABOVE it.

Lesson 18

إِثْبَاتُ الكَلَامِ لِلَّهِ تَعَالَى

The Affirmation of Speech for Allah

How truthfulness in speech affirms speech, why the emphatic verbal noun in kallamaʾLlāhu Mūsā taklīman forecloses a figurative reading, the messengers addressed without an intermediary, and calling out versus conferring privately as two kinds of speech.

Lesson 19

القُرْآنُ كَلَامُ اللهِ وَلَا مُبَدِّلَ لِكَلِمَاتِه

The Qurʾān Is the Speech of Allah, and None Can Change His Words

How al-Tawbah 9:6 proves the recited Qurʾān is the speech of Allah, taḥrīf as reading a text against its meaning after understanding it, and the madhhab of Ahl al-Sunnah that speech is an attribute of the Essence AND of the Actions — He speaks if He wills, how He wills, with what He wills.

Lesson 20

تَنْزِيلُ القُرْآنِ مِنَ اللهِ تَعَالَى

The Sending Down of the Qurʾān from Allah

Why the sending-down is described first, the argument from the hardness of the mountain, abrogation and the wisdom in it, the Qurʾān’s two-bladed answer to the claim that a man was teaching the Prophet ط, and how the inzāl itself proves that Allah is above His creation.

Lesson 21

رُؤْيَةُ المُؤْمِنِينَ لِرَبِّهِمْ — وَخَاتِمَةُ القِسْمِ الأَوَّل

The Believers Seeing Their Lord — and the Close of Part One

Nāḍirah against nāẓirah, how the veiling of the disbelievers proves the seeing, the Prophet’s ط own tafsīr of “and more,” the three objections of the deniers answered — and the author’s closing words sealing the evidence from the Book of Allah.

Lesson 22

السُّنَّةُ وَشَرْطُ الاسْتِدْلَالِ بِالحَدِيث

The Sunnah, and the Condition for Using a Ḥadīth

Lesson 22 of al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah, opening Part 2: the Sunnah as the second foundation, the two referrals of al-Nisāʾ 4:59, the four things the Sunnah does for the Qurʾān, and the five conditions of an authentic ḥadīth — with what each one excludes.

Lesson 23

ثُبُوتُ النُّزُولِ الإِلَهِيّ

The Divine Descent to the Lowest Heaven

The ḥadīth of the descent, the two points of vowelling that carry rulings, the six attributes this one ḥadīth establishes, and the two grounds on which “His mercy descends” cannot survive the ḥadīth’s own next line.

Lesson 24

الفَرَحُ وَالضَّحِكُ وَالعَجَبُ وَالنَّظَر

That Allah Rejoices, Laughs, Wonders and Looks

Why the joy at a servant’s repentance is an attribute of perfection and not of need, the reason the ḥadīth itself gives that a killer and his victim both enter the Garden, and the two kinds of wonder — with al-qunūṭ and the nearness of the change.

Lesson 25

الرِّجْلُ وَالقَدَمُ · وَالنِّدَاءُ بِصَوْت

The Foot, and the Calling Out with a Voice

Why the Fire is filled by the placing of the Foot — a promise kept without crossing the mercy — the two refutations of the muʿaṭṭilah, and the calling out with a voice that no figurative reading can absorb, ending on the ḥadīth that every believer will be spoken to with no interpreter.

Lesson 26

العُلُوُّ فِي السُّنَّةِ · رُقْيَةُ المَرِيض

The Highness in the Sunnah — the Ruqyah of the Sick

A bedside duʿāʾ as the proof that Allah is above His creation, the two readings of “who is in the heaven,” why hallowing “Your name” covers every name, the two kinds of mercy and the test that tells them apart, and “I am the trustee of Him who is above the heaven.”

Lesson 27

العَرْشُ فَوْقَ المَخْلُوقَاتِ · وَحَدِيثُ الجَارِيَة

The Throne above Creation, and the Ḥadīth of the Slave-Girl

The Throne as the last rung of the created order, why the ḥadīth joins His height to His encompassing knowledge in one sentence, and the slave-girl whose one-word answer to “where is Allah?” the Prophet ط accepted as proof of faith.

Lesson 28

المَعِيَّةُ وَالقُرْبُ فِي السُّنَّة

The Maʿiyyah and the Nearness in the Sunnah

Four aḥādīth of nearness, the four names of the Prophetic duʿāʾ in their two pairs, and the formula that settles the whole question — near in His highness, high in His nearness.

Lesson 29

الرُّؤْيَةُ فِي السُّنَّةِ · وَمَوْقِفُ أَهْلِ السُّنَّة

The Seeing in the Sunnah, and the Position of Ahl al-Sunnah

Lesson 29 of al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah, closing Part 2: why the comparison with the full moon likens the seeing and not the One seen, the two narrations of “you will not be crowded,” the two prayers named as the price of the most excellent gift, and the four doors closed against the aḥādīth of the attributes exactly as against the āyāt.

Lesson 30

مَكَانَةُ أَهْلِ السُّنَّةِ بَيْنَ فِرَقِ الأُمَّة

The Standing of Ahl al-Sunnah among the Sects of the Ummah

Lesson 30 of al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah, opening Part 3: al-wasaṭ as the just and the choicest, and the five chāpters in which Ahl al-Sunnah stand between two extremes — each party named, traced to its origin, and its position stated in its own terms.

Lesson 31

وُجُوبُ الإِيمَانِ بِالعُلُوِّ وَالمَعِيَّةِ مَعًا

The Obligation of Believing in the Highness and the Withness Together

The objection stated at full strength, and al-Fawzān’s four answers from the language, the consensus, the fiṭrah and the mutawātir texts — with the definition of al-mutawātir, and why al-Raqīb and al-Muhaymin were not chosen at random.

Lesson 32

مَعْنَى كَوْنِهِ «فِي السَّمَاءِ» · وَالظُّنُونُ الكَاذِبَة

The Meaning of His Being "in the Heaven", and the False Suppositions

Why the texts need guarding rather than reinterpreting, the two false pictures of the sky as a floor or a canopy, the consensus that closes both doors in one sentence, and the inversion whereby the Qurʾān has Him holding the heaven up.

Lesson 33

وُجُوبُ الإِيمَانِ بِقُرْبِهِ مِنْ خَلْقِه

The Obligation of Believing in His Nearness to His Creation

The question that occasioned al-Baqarah 2:186 — near, or far? — the principle that the truth does not contradict itself, the faulty step hidden in “how is He with them while above them?”, and why being high while near belongs to Allah alone.

Lesson 34

القُرْآنُ كَلَامُ اللهِ حَقِيقَةً · حُرُوفُهُ وَمَعَانِيه

The Qurʾān Is Allah’s Speech in Reality — Its Letters and Its Meanings

The distinction between the kind of Allah’s speech and its instances, which lets Ahl al-Sunnah hold both halves at once; the four opposing positions and what each one costs; and the author’s proof that speech is ascribed to the one who originated it, not to the one who conveys it.

Lesson 35

وُجُوبُ الإِيمَانِ بِالرُّؤْيَةِ · وَمَوَاضِعُهَا

The Obligation of Believing in the Seeing, and Where It Occurs

Lesson 35 of al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah, closing Part 3: why denying the ruʾyah belies Allah, His Books and His Messengers at once; what ʿiyānan rules out; the two places of the seeing with the three reported views on the first; and the three words that govern both.

كَيْفَ يَتَّصِلُ بِمَا دَرَسْتَ How This Fits What You Have Studied

Unit 4 of the ʿAqīdah track covers the names and attributes topically — the manhaj, the four forbidden doors, the categories, the affirmed attributes, and speech and the vision. That unit is the shorter way in, and it remains the right place to start. This track is the text itself, taken in Ibn Taymiyyah’s order and at the depth of a named sharḥ. The two are meant to be read together: the unit gives you the map, the matn gives you the ground.
