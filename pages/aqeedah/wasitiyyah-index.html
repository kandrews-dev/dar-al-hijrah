العَقِيدَةُ الوَاسِطِيَّة

al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah

العَقِيدَةُ الوَاسِطِيَّة is the creed of شَيْخُ الإِسْلَامِ أَحْمَدُ بْنُ تَيْمِيَّة, written at the request of a judge from Wāsiṭ — hence its name. It is short, and it is the most widely taught statement of the creed of Ahl al-Sunnah on the names and attributes of Allah: the author brings the evidence from the Qur’ān, then from the Sunnah, and affirms each attribute exactly as it came, without distortion, divestment, specifying a how, or likening.

This track takes the matn in the author’s own order, section by section, with the sharḥ of Shaykh صَالِحٌ الفَوْزَان — and Shaykh مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ صَالِحٍ العُثَيْمِين consulted where a point needs more. It is a long text, and the track is being built in order; the sections already live are below, followed by the full plan.

By the end of this track — أَهْدَافُ المَسَار

  • State the creed of Ahl al-Sunnah on the names and attributes, and its evidence.
  • Apply the four exclusions — تَحْرِيف · تَعْطِيل · تَكْيِيف · تَمْثِيل — to any statement you read.
  • Affirm every attribute the author brings, with its proof from the Qur’ān or the Sunnah.
  • Explain al-istiwāʾ, al-ʿuluww and al-maʿiyyah without contradiction between them.
  • State what belief in the Last Day and in al-qadar includes, in detail.
  • Give the position of Ahl al-Sunnah on the Companions, on īmān, and on the karāmāt of the awliyāʾ.

Lesson 1

البَسْمَلَةُ وَخُطْبَةُ الافْتِتَاح

The Basmalah and the Opening Address

The name الله unpacked, al-Raḥmān against al-Raḥīm, the two kinds of guidance, and how عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ closes both ditches at once.

Lesson 2

الفِرْقَةُ النَّاجِيَةُ المَنْصُورَة

The Saved and Aided Group

Why they are named after the Sunnah while the sects are named after their innovations, their leader or their deeds — and Ibn Masʿūd on being the jamāʿah alone.

Lesson 3

أَرْكَانُ الإِيمَانِ السِّتَّة

The Six Pillars of Īmān

Īmān in the language and in the Sharīʿah, each pillar in turn, the nabī/rasūl distinction and the stages of qadar.

Lesson 4

مِنْ غَيْرِ تَحْرِيفٍ وَلَا تَعْطِيل

The Four Doors That Are Closed

تَحْرِيف · تَعْطِيل · تَكْيِيف · تَمْثِيل, each defined exactly — with Imām Mālik’s four clauses on al-istiwāʾ.

Taken from Shaykh al-Fawzān’s own table of contents, so the shape of the book is visible before you are inside it. These are being built in this order.

Part 7

كَرَامَاتُ الأَوْلِيَاءِ وَصِفَاتُ أَهْلِ السُّنَّة

The Karāmāt of the Awliyāʾ, and the Qualities of Ahl al-Sunnah

مَذْهَبُ أَهْلِ السُّنَّةِ فِي كَرَامَاتِ الأَوْلِيَاءِ · فَصْلٌ: صِفَاتُ أَهْلِ السُّنَّةِ وَالجَمَاعَةِ وَلِمَ سُمُّوا بِذَلِكَ · فَصْلٌ: مُكَمِّلَاتُ العَقِيدَةِ مِنْ مَكَارِمِ الأَخْلَاقِ

كَيْفَ يَتَّصِلُ بِمَا دَرَسْتَ How This Fits What You Have Studied

Unit 4 of the ʿAqīdah track covers the names and attributes topically — the manhaj, the four forbidden doors, the categories, the affirmed attributes, and speech and the vision. That unit is the shorter way in, and it remains the right place to start. This track is the text itself, taken in Ibn Taymiyyah’s order and at the depth of a named sharḥ. The two are meant to be read together: the unit gives you the map, the matn gives you the ground.
