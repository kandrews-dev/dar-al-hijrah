أنواع الشرك

The Types of Shirk

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Name the three types of shirk — الشرك الأكبر، الأصغر، الخفي
  • Explain the difference between major and minor shirk
  • Recognise hidden shirk (riyāʾ) and understand why it is dangerous
  • Use correct terminology for each category
كم نوعاً للشرك؟ How Many Types of Shirk Are There?

In the previous lesson we learned that shirk means associating partners with Allah. The scholars of Islam — building on the Quran and Sunnah — have divided shirk into three categories based on how severe they are and how they affect a person's faith.

الشرك الأكبر

Major Shirk

Takes a person outside of Islam. The most serious category — kufr.

الشرك الأصغر

Minor Shirk

Does not take one out of Islam but is a major sin that destroys good deeds.

الشرك الخفي

Hidden Shirk

Hidden in the heart — insincerity, showing off, subtle attachment to creation.

Note: Some scholars divide shirk into two categories (major and minor), with hidden shirk being a sub-type of minor. Both approaches are found in Islamic scholarship. What matters is understanding each type and avoiding all of them.

الشرك الأكبر — Major Shirk Al-Shirk al-Akbar — The Grave Category
الشرك الأكبر Major Shirk — al-Shirk al-Akbar

أَنْ يَجْعَلَ الْعَبْدُ لِلَّهِ نِدًّا فِي رُبُوبِيَّتِهِ أَوْ أُلُوهِيَّتِهِ أَوْ أَسْمَائِهِ وَصِفَاتِهِ

To set up a rival to Allah in His Lordship, Godhood, or Names and Attributes.

This is the type of shirk that the Quran most frequently warns against. It nullifies a person's Islam if done knowingly and willingly.

Major shirk is described in the Quran as ظُلْمٌ عَظِيمٌ (ẓulm ʿaẓīm — a tremendous wrong). This is because it places Allah — the Creator — on the same level as creation. Nothing could be more unjust or further from the truth.

إِنَّ الشِّرْكَ لَظُلْمٌ عَظِيمٌ

"Indeed, shirk is a tremendous wrongdoing."

Sūrat Luqmān 31:13 · قال لقمان لابنه

Examples of major shirk include:

عبادة الأصنام

Worshipping idols

Praying to, bowing before, or seeking help from statues or idols as if they have divine power.

دعاء الأموات

Calling upon the dead

Directly calling upon the deceased — prophets, saints, or others — to remove harm or grant blessings that only Allah can grant.

السحر والكهانة

Magic and fortune-telling

Practicing or believing in magic, seeking knowledge of the unseen from fortune-tellers or psychics.

طاعة في تحليل الحرام

Obeying others in making ḥarām ḥalāl

Following religious leaders who declare what Allah made ḥarām to be ḥalāl — replacing Allah's law with human authority.

Important: A Muslim who falls into major shirk out of ignorance or compulsion is in a different situation from one who knowingly rejects tawhid. The scholars carefully distinguish between the act (which may be shirk) and declaring a specific person to be a mushrik — that is a serious matter requiring evidence and conditions.

الشرك الأصغر — Minor Shirk Al-Shirk al-Aṣghar — The Subtle Danger
الشرك الأصغر Minor Shirk — al-Shirk al-Aṣghar

كُلُّ عَمَلٍ أَوْ قَوْلٍ وَرَدَ الدَّلِيلُ بِتَسْمِيَتِهِ شِرْكًا وَلَمْ يَبْلُغْ حَدَّ الشِّرْكِ الأَكْبَر

Every action or statement that the evidence names "shirk" but which does not reach the level of major shirk.

It does not take a person outside of Islam but it is still a major sin. Most importantly — it destroys the good deeds it mixes with.

The Prophet ﷺ warned most frequently about this type, calling it الشرك الأصغر. Why? Because it is subtle — a person can fall into it while thinking they are doing good.

«إِنَّ أَخْوَفَ مَا أَخَافُ عَلَيْكُمُ الشِّرْكُ الأَصْغَرُ»، قَالُوا: وَمَا الشِّرْكُ الأَصْغَرُ يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ؟ قَالَ: «الرِّيَاءُ»

"The thing I fear most for you is minor shirk." They asked: "What is minor shirk, O Messenger of Allah?" He said: "Riyāʾ (showing off)."

Narrated by Aḥmad · رواه أحمد، وحسّنه الألباني

Examples of minor shirk:

الرياء

Riyāʾ — Showing Off

Performing worship or good deeds to impress other people, not solely for Allah's sake.

e.g. praying beautifully when others are watching, but not when alone

الحلف بغير الله

Swearing by other than Allah

Taking oaths using creation — e.g. "I swear by the Prophet" or "by my life."

Oaths of this kind show a level of glorification that belongs to Allah alone.

لو أن الله وفلان

"If not for Allah and so-and-so"

Saying "if not for Allah and you, this would not have happened" — placing a person equal to Allah in causation.

Correct: "If not for Allah, then you." (i.e. Allah first, then you as a secondary means)

التمائم

Wearing amulets/charms

Wearing charms or talismans believing they independently protect from harm or bring benefit.

Benefit and harm belong to Allah alone — placing them in an object is shirk.

Why is minor shirk so dangerous? The Quran tells us: "And indeed it has been revealed to you and to those before you: if you associate partners with Allah, your deeds will surely be nullified." (39:65) Minor shirk does not nullify all deeds — but it nullifies the specific deeds it mixes with. A prayer performed for show loses its reward. A charity given for praise loses its reward.

الشرك الخفي — Hidden Shirk Al-Shirk al-Khafī — What the Heart Hides
الشرك الخفي Hidden Shirk — al-Shirk al-Khafī

مَا يَقَعُ فِي الْقَلْبِ مِنَ الاِلْتِفَاتِ إِلَى غَيْرِ اللهِ فِي بَاطِنِهِ دُونَ ظَاهِرِهِ

That which occurs in the heart — turning inwardly toward other than Allah, without it necessarily appearing externally.

This is the most subtle form. Only Allah and the person themselves fully know what is in their heart.

The Prophet ﷺ described this shirk as being more subtle than the creeping of ants on a smooth black stone in a dark night. It is not about visible actions — it is about the intentions and attachments of the heart.

«الشِّرْكُ فِيكُمْ أَخْفَى مِنْ دَبِيبِ النَّمْلِ»

"Shirk among you is more subtle than the crawl of an ant."

Narrated by al-Bukhārī in al-Adab al-Mufrad · رواه البخاري في الأدب المفرد

Examples of hidden shirk in the heart:

الخوف من المخلوقات

Fear of creation

Fearing a person, jinn, or force so much that it changes what you do or say — the way only Allah should be feared.

الرجاء من المخلوقات

Hope placed in creation

Placing complete hope for relief, success, or provision in a person or means, forgetting that Allah is the true Giver.

المحبة الصارفة عن الله

Love that diverts from Allah

Loving something so intensely that it competes with Allah's right to be loved most — to the point of disobeying Allah for it.

التوكل على الأسباب

Complete reliance on means

Relying on a job, medicine, or person with the same certainty that belongs to tawakkul in Allah — forgetting He is the true Cause.

The cure for hidden shirk is constant muḥāsabat al-nafs (محاسبة النفس) — regularly examining your heart. Ask: "Am I doing this for Allah? Is my reliance truly on Him? Is my love and fear in its proper place?" The Prophet ﷺ taught us to say: «اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أُشْرِكَ بِكَ وَأَنَا أَعْلَمُ، وَأَسْتَغْفِرُكَ لِمَا لَا أَعْلَمُ» "O Allah, I seek refuge in You from associating partners with You knowingly, and I ask Your forgiveness for what I do not know."

تدريب القواعد Grammar Drill — Superlative Patterns (أَفْعَل)

In Arabic, comparatives and superlatives follow the pattern أَفْعَل. Notice: أَكْبَر (bigger/greatest) from ك-ب-ر, and أَصْغَر (smaller/lesser) from ص-غ-ر. Match each word to its root and meaning.

الكلمة Pattern (أَفْعَل) الجذر — Root English meaning
أَكْبَر Yes — أَفْعَل greatest / largest
أَصْغَر Yes — أَفْعَل smallest / least
أَعْظَم Yes — أَفْعَل greatest / most mighty
أَخْفَى Yes — أَفْعَل most hidden / most subtle
