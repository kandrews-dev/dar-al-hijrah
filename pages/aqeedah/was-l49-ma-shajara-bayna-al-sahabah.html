الإِمْسَاكُ عَمَّا شَجَرَ بَيْنَ الصَّحَابَة

Refraining from What Occurred Between the Companions

شَرْحُ الشَّيْخِ صَالِحٍ الفَوْزَان · ص ١٨٢–١٨٧
Sharḥ al-ʿAqīdah al-Wāsiṭiyyah, Ṣāliḥ al-Fawzān — pp. 182–187.

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Name the two opposed sects this clause disowns, and say what each one does.
  • State the two things Ahl al-Sunnah do about مَا شَجَرَ بَيْنَ الصَّحَابَة — what they refrain from, and what they say.
  • Give the four excuses for the narrations of the Companions’ faults, in order.
  • List the expiators al-Fawzān gives, and say why a Companion’s sin is covered by more of them than a later man’s.
  • Explain why الاجْتِهَاد is answered with reward either way — and what that does to the whole question.
التَّبَرُّؤُ مِنَ الرَّوَافِضِ وَالنَّوَاصِب Disowning Both the Rāfiḍah and the Nawāṣib

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَيَتَبَرَّؤُونَ مِنْ طَرِيقَةِ الرَّوَافِضِ الَّذِينَ يُبْغِضُونَ الصَّحَابَةَ وَيَسُبُّونَهُمْ، وَمِنْ طَرِيقَةِ النَّوَاصِبِ الَّذِينَ يُؤْذُونَ أَهْلَ البَيْتِ بِقَوْلٍ أَوْ عَمَلٍ.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

The two previous lessons built the two halves of this clause, and here they are joined. Lesson 47 established the ranks of the Companions; Lesson 48 established the right of the household. A man can go wrong by denying either one — and the two errors are mirror images.

الرَّوَافِضFrom الرَّفْض, to abandon — they were named so because they abandoned Zayd ibn ʿAlī when he refused to revile the two Shaykhs. They hate the Companions and revile them, and they go to excess regarding ʿAlī and the household.
النَّوَاصِبFrom نَصْبُ العَدَاوَة — they set up enmity toward the household of the Prophet ﷺ, and they harm them by a word or by a deed.

Notice the wording of the matn on the Nawāṣib: «by a word or by a deed». The sin is not confined to taking up arms. A tongue that belittles the household is already inside the description.

And notice what the creed does with the pair. It does not pick a side between them. It disowns both — because both are built on the same mistake, which is making love of one group of the Prophet’s ﷺ people into a licence to attack another.

The middle position, stated as a line:

النَّوَاصِب  ←  جَفَاء  ·  أَهْلُ السُّنَّة  ·  غُلُوّ  →  الرَّوَافِض

Coldness on one side, excess on the other — and the Sunnah is not the midpoint of a compromise but the only position that gives every party its actual right.

الإِمْسَاكُ عَمَّا شَجَرَ بَيْنَ الصَّحَابَة Refraining from What Occurred Between the Companions

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَيُمْسِكُونَ عَمَّا شَجَرَ بَيْنَ الصَّحَابَةِ، وَيَقُولُونَ: إِنَّ هَذِهِ الآثَارَ المَرْوِيَّةَ فِي مَسَاوِيهِمْ مِنْهَا مَا هُوَ كَذِبٌ، وَمِنْهَا مَا قَدْ زِيدَ فِيهِ وَنُقِصَ وَغُيِّرَ عَنْ وَجْهِهِ؛ وَالصَّحِيحُ مِنْهُ هُمْ فِيهِ مَعْذُورُونَ: إِمَّا مُجْتَهِدُونَ مُصِيبُونَ، وَإِمَّا مُجْتَهِدُونَ مُخْطِئُونَ.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

مَا شَجَرَ — that is: what happened and arose between them of disputing and fighting. And al-Fawzān sets out the position of Ahl al-Sunnah toward it in two matters, which it is worth keeping apart, because they answer two different questions.

الأَوَّل
what they DO		يُمْسِكُونَ عَمَّا شَجَرَ بَيْنَهُمْ — that is, they refrain from looking into it and do not plunge into it. The reason al-Fawzān gives is not that the history is unknowable: it is because plunging into that breeds rancour and hatred toward the Companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ — and that is of the gravest of sins. So the road of safety is silence about it.
الثَّانِي
what they SAY		the excusing of the narrations reported about their faults — because in that is a defence of them and a repelling of the plotting of their enemies.

Those two together are a single discipline. Silence is not evasion, because it is paired with an answer. Ahl al-Sunnah do not refuse the subject because they have nothing to say about it; they refuse to make a courtroom of it, while still answering the charge.

الأَعْذَارُ الأَرْبَعَة The Four Excuses

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān takes the matn’s clauses in order and makes four answers of them. They are not alternatives offered at random: they are applied in sequence, and each one only comes into play if the one before it does not dispose of the report.

١  كَذِبSome of it is a lie, which their enemies invented in order to disfigure their reputation — as the Rāfiḍah do. And a lie is not given any attention at all.
٢  مُحَرَّفAnd some of it has been added to, subtracted from, and turned from its correct aspect, and falsehood has entered it — so it is distorted and not relied upon.
٣  مَعْذُورُونَAnd what is authentic of it — they are excused in it: either mujtahids who were correct, or mujtahids who erred.
٤  بَشَرAnd they do not hold that every one of the Companions is infallible from major sins and minor ones — rather sins are possible for them in general, as they are possible for any human being.

The reasoning under the second excuse is the one to carry away. al-Fawzān states it as a principle:

— because the excellence of the Companions is KNOWN, and their integrity is CERTAIN; so the known and certain is not abandoned for a distorted matter that is doubted.

That is a rule about evidence, and it holds everywhere. A certainty is not surrendered to a doubt. The Companions’ standing was established by the Qurʾān and by mutawātir narration; a report of a fault reaches us through a chain that may be broken, partisan or tampered with. The weaker claim does not get to overturn the stronger one — and noticing which of two claims is the stronger is the whole of the skill.

وَالسَّابِقُونَ الْأَوَّلُونَ مِنَ الْمُهَاجِرِينَ وَالْأَنْصَارِ وَالَّذِينَ اتَّبَعُوهُمْ بِإِحْسَانٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ

"And the first forerunners among the Muhājirūn and the Anṣār, and those who followed them in excellence — Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him."

Sūrat al-Tawbah 9:100 · سُورَةُ التَّوْبَة

That is the «known and certain» the principle refers to. Allah announced His pleasure with them in a text that is recited — and a narration of a fault, however it is dressed, arrives much later and by a much weaker road.

الاجْتِهَادُ وَأَجْرُ المُخْطِئ Ijtihād, and the Reward of the One Who Errs

الشَّرْح · the explanation

الاجْتِهَاد — the expending of one’s capacity in coming to know the Sharīʿah ruling. So the third excuse rests on a ḥadīth that settles how ijtihād is answered:

إِذَا اجْتَهَدَ الحَاكِمُ فَأَصَابَ فَلَهُ أَجْرَانِ، وَإِنِ اجْتَهَدَ وَأَخْطَأَ فَلَهُ أَجْرٌ وَاحِدٌ

"When the judge exerts himself and is correct, he has two rewards; and when he exerts himself and errs, he has one reward."

رَوَاهُ البُخَارِيُّ وَمُسْلِمٌ — عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ وَعَمْرِو بْنِ العَاصِ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُمَا

الشَّرْح · the explanation

Read what that does to the question. On neither branch is there sin. The correct one gains the reward of the effort and the reward of hitting the truth; the mistaken one still gains the reward of the effort, and his mistake is forgiven.

So al-Fawzān can close the matter cleanly: what issues from a Companion by way of error — and it is little — falls into one of two cases:

١it issued from ijtihād — and in that he is rewarded, and his error is forgiven
٢it issued from other than ijtihād — and he has of deeds, excellences and good precedents what expiates it and effaces it

There is no third case, and neither of the two leaves anything for us to prosecute. That is why the silence of the first matter is not a cover-up: once the reckoning is done, there is simply nothing left that a later man is entitled to try.

المُكَفِّرَاتُ · لِمَاذَا يُغْفَرُ لَهُمْ مَا لَا يُغْفَرُ لِمَنْ بَعْدَهُمْ The Expiators — Why They Are Forgiven What Later Men Are Not

مِنَ المَتْن · the author’s own words

وَلَهُمْ مِنَ السَّوَابِقِ وَالفَضَائِلِ مَا يُوجِبُ مَغْفِرَةَ مَا يَصْدُرُ مِنْهُمْ إِنْ صَدَرَ، حَتَّى إِنَّهُمْ يُغْفَرُ لَهُمْ مِنَ السَّيِّئَاتِ مَا لَا يُغْفَرُ لِمَنْ بَعْدَهُمْ؛ لِأَنَّ لَهُمْ مِنَ الحَسَنَاتِ الَّتِي تَمْحُو السَّيِّئَاتِ مَا لَيْسَ لِمَنْ بَعْدَهُمْ.

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān gives three grounds, and the first is proved by a case from the Sīrah that every student already knows.

١ · السَّوَابِقُ وَالفَضَائِل — they have of prior precedents and excellences what necessitates the forgiveness of what issues from them. Like the story of حَاطِبِ بْنِ أَبِي بَلْتَعَةَ — regarding what occurred from him at the conquest: he was forgiven for it because he had been present at Badr.

إِنَّ الْحَسَنَاتِ يُذْهِبْنَ السَّيِّئَاتِ

"Indeed, good deeds efface evil deeds."

Sūrat Hūd 11:114 · سُورَةُ هُود

الشَّرْح · the explanation

٢ · تَضَاعُفُ الحَسَنَات — the good deeds are multiplied for them, as he ﷺ said:

خَيْرُ القُرُونِ قَرْنِي، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ

"The best of generations is my generation, then those who follow them, then those who follow them."

رَوَاهُ البُخَارِيُّ وَمُسْلِمٌ

الشَّرْح · the explanation

And القَرْن is the people of a single, near-together age who share in some one of the intended matters; and it is also used of a span of time.

٣ · كَثْرَةُ المُكَفِّرَات — the abundance of the expiators of sins that they have. al-Fawzān lists them, and the list is the point: a sin of theirs has to pass through all of these before it could stand.

التَّوْبَةhe repented of it
الحَسَنَاتor he brought good deeds that effaced it
السَّابِقَةor it was forgiven him by the merit of his precedence
الشَّفَاعَةor by the intercession of Muḥammad ﷺ — and they are the most deserving of people of his intercession
البَلَاءor he was tried with an affliction in this world by which it was expiated from him

مَا يُصِيبُ المُؤْمِنَ مِنْ وَصَبٍ وَلَا نَصَبٍ وَلَا غَمٍّ وَلَا هَمٍّ وَلَا حَزَنٍ، حَتَّى الشَّوْكَةِ يُشَاكُهَا، إِلَّا كَفَّرَ اللهُ بِهَا مِنْ خَطَايَاهُ

"No sickness befalls the believer, nor fatigue, nor distress, nor anxiety, nor grief — not even a thorn that pricks him — but that Allah expiates by it some of his sins."

مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْهِ

And the Companions are the foremost of people in that — emigration, dispossession, hunger, wounds and the loss of their own families. Read the list of expiators against the lives they actually lived and the conclusion is not generous, it is arithmetical.

تَنْبِيهٌ مُهِمّ — al-Fawzān turns here from the old sects to a living one.

And of what has occurred from some of the later writers of this age: that they made themselves a judge between the Companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, declaring this one right and that one wrong — without any evidence, but rather by following desire and imitating those with an agenda, who attempt to insinuate against the Muslims by casting doubt upon their glorious history and their righteous predecessors — in order to pass from that to attacking Islam itself and splitting the word of the Muslims.

Mark the mechanism he describes, because it is the reason this clause of the creed is not an antiquarian matter. The attack does not begin with the religion; it begins with its carriers. Discredit the generation that transmitted the Qurʾān and the Sunnah, and the texts they carried are discredited without ever being argued with. That is why Ahl al-Sunnah guard this point so jealously — not out of sentiment about the past, but because the chain of the religion runs through those men.

فَضَائِلُهُمْ جُمْلَةً Their Excellences, Taken Together

الشَّرْح · the explanation

al-Fawzān closes the section by gathering what the whole of it rests on. Each item is the highest of its own class — which is why the total is not merely large but unmatchable.

١الإِيمَانُ بِاللهِ وَرَسُولِهِ — and it is the best of deeds
٢الجِهَادُ فِي سَبِيلِ اللهِ — and it is the summit of the peak of Islam
٣الهِجْرَةُ فِي سَبِيلِ اللهِ — and it is of the best of deeds
٤النُّصْرَةُ لِدِينِ اللهِ
٥العِلْمُ النَّافِعُ وَالعَمَلُ الصَّالِح
٦أَنَّهُمْ خَيْرُ الخَلْقِ بَعْدَ الأَنْبِيَاء

وَيَنْصُرُونَ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ أُولَئِكَ هُمُ الصَّادِقُونَ

"And they support Allah and His Messenger — it is they who are the truthful."

Sūrat al-Ḥashr 59:8 · سُورَةُ الحَشْر

كُنْتُمْ خَيْرَ أُمَّةٍ أُخْرِجَتْ لِلنَّاسِ تَأْمُرُونَ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَتَنْهَوْنَ عَنِ الْمُنْكَرِ وَتُؤْمِنُونَ بِاللَّهِ

"You are the best nation brought forth for mankind: you enjoin the right, forbid the wrong, and believe in Allah."

Sūrat Āl ʿImrān 3:110 · سُورَةُ آلِ عِمْرَان

And if this Ummah is the best of nations, then the best of this Ummah is the best of mankind after the Prophets — which is exactly the rank the ḥadīth of the generations gives them. That is the whole of Part Six in one step: their rank is not a claim made on their behalf; it is read off two āyāt and a ḥadīth, and every clause of this section has been a consequence of it.

Check Your Understanding · MCQ أَسْئِلَة

Why are the Rāfiḍah so named?

Because they rejected the Qurʾān
From الرَّفْض, abandoning — they abandoned Zayd ibn ʿAlī when he refused to revile the two Shaykhs
Because they were rejected from Madīnah
Because they abandoned the prayer

What do the Nawāṣib do, per the matn?

They deny the attributes of Allah
They set up enmity toward Ahl al-Bayt and harm them by a word or by a deed — so the sin is not confined to fighting
They exaggerate regarding ʿAlī
They reject the ḥadīth of the generations

What reason does al-Fawzān give for refraining from what occurred between the Companions?

That the reports are all lost
That plunging into it breeds rancour and hatred toward the Companions — which is of the gravest of sins; so silence is the road of safety
That the matter is not part of the creed
That only the scholars of ḥadīth may study it

What is the SECOND thing Ahl al-Sunnah do about it, alongside refraining?

They record it without comment
They excuse the narrations reported about their faults — because in that is a defence of them and a repelling of their enemies’ plotting
They curse whoever narrates it
They declare all such reports fabricated without examination

What is the principle under the second excuse (the distorted reports)?

That weak ḥadīth may be acted on in virtues
That the Companions’ excellence is KNOWN and their integrity CERTAIN — so the known and certain is not abandoned for a distorted, doubted matter
That a narration with one defect is rejected outright
That history is not a source of creed

What does the ḥadīth of the judge establish about ijtihād?

That only a judge may perform ijtihād
That on neither branch is there sin — correct brings two rewards, erring still brings one, and the error is forgiven
That a mistaken judge must be removed
That ijtihād ceased after the Companions

Which case from the Sīrah does al-Fawzān use for "prior precedents"?

The case of Kaʿb ibn Mālik and the battle of Tabūk
Ḥāṭib ibn Abī Baltaʿah — forgiven for what occurred from him at the conquest because he had been present at Badr
The three who were left behind
The story of the slanderers of ʿĀʾishah

What is القَرْن, as defined here?

A hundred years exactly
The people of a single near-together age who share in some intended matter — and it is also used of a span of time
The Companions alone
A tribe descended from one father

What mechanism does al-Fawzān warn against in modern writers?

Writing history in a foreign language
Making themselves a judge between the Companions without evidence — casting doubt on the predecessors in order to pass from there to attacking Islam and splitting the Muslims
Relying only on al-Bukhārī and Muslim
Teaching the Sīrah to children

Why is a Companion’s sin covered by more expiators than a later man’s?

Because they were granted infallibility
Because they have what later men do not: multiplied good deeds, prior precedence, the foremost claim to his ﷺ intercession, and far greater affliction
Because their sins were all minor
Because no record of their deeds was kept
True or False صَحٌ أَمْ خَطَأٌ
يَعْتَقِدُ أَهْلُ السُّنَّةِ أَنَّ كُلَّ صَحَابِيٍّ مَعْصُومٌ مِنَ الذُّنُوبِ. Ahl al-Sunnah hold that every Companion is infallible from sins.
إِيذَاءُ أَهْلِ البَيْتِ بِالقَوْلِ وَحْدَهُ مِنْ طَرِيقَةِ النَّوَاصِبِ. Harming Ahl al-Bayt by speech alone falls under the way of the Nawāṣib.
المُجْتَهِدُ المُخْطِئُ آثِمٌ وَإِنْ بَذَلَ طَاقَتَهُ. A mujtahid who errs is sinful even though he expended his capacity.
العِلْمُ المُتَيَقَّنُ بِفَضْلِ الصَّحَابَةِ لَا يُتْرَكُ لِأَثَرٍ مُحَرَّفٍ مَشْكُوكٍ فِيهِ. The certain knowledge of the Companions’ excellence is not abandoned for a distorted, doubted report.
البَلَاءُ فِي الدُّنْيَا مِنْ مُكَفِّرَاتِ الذُّنُوبِ. Affliction in this world is among the expiators of sins.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

وَالصَّحِيحُ مِنْهُ هُمْ فِيهِ مَعْذُورُونَ

Fix the order of the four answers, because the order is the argument. A lie gets no attention; a distorted report is not relied on; what is authentic is excused ijtihād; and they were never claimed to be sinless in the first place. By the time a charge has passed all four, there is nothing left to try — and that, not ignorance, is why Ahl al-Sunnah keep silent.
