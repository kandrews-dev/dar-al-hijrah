Ḥadīth 5 of 42 · Agreed upon

رَدُّ البِدْعَة

Rejecting Innovation in the Religion

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Memorize the ḥadīth of عائشة: whatever is introduced into the religion is rejected.
  • Understand أَحْدَثَ and رَدٌّ — that worship is built on الاتباع (following), not invention.
  • Learn the two conditions of every accepted deed: الإخلاص (sincerity) and المتابعة (conformity to the Sunnah).
  • See how this ḥadīth pairs with the ḥadīth of النية (Ḥadīth 1) as the two scales of deeds.
  • Distinguish بدعة in the religion from worldly inventions, which this ḥadīth does not condemn.

عَنْ أُمِّ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أُمِّ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: «مَنْ أَحْدَثَ فِي أَمْرِنَا هَذَا مَا لَيْسَ مِنْهُ فَهُوَ رَدٌّ» رَوَاهُ الْبُخَارِيُّ وَمُسْلِمٌ، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ لِمُسْلِمٍ: «مَنْ عَمِلَ عَمَلًا لَيْسَ عَلَيْهِ أَمْرُنَا فَهُوَ رَدٌّ».

The Mother of the Believers, Umm ʿAbdillāh ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her) said: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever introduces into this affair of ours that which is not from it, it is rejected." Narrated by al-Bukhārī and Muslim. And in a wording of Muslim: "Whoever does a deed that is not in accordance with our affair, it is rejected."

Narrated by ʿĀʾishah · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

الراوية أم المؤمنين The Narrator — the Mother of the Believers

عائشة (ʿĀʾishah), daughter of أبو بكر الصديق, is أم المؤمنين — a Mother of the Believers, the title the Qur'an gives to the wives of the Prophet ﷺ. She had no children, so she took her kunyah أم عبد الله from her sister's son عبد الله بن الزبير. She is among the most prolific narrators of ḥadīth in the entire Ummah and one of its greatest jurists: senior Companions would come to her with their difficult questions, and she would resolve them with knowledge and proof. Because she was the Prophet's ﷺ wife, she carried to us the Sunnah of the home — his worship at night, his character, his private conduct — a whole portion of the religion that no one outside his household could have witnessed. It is fitting that the ḥadīth guarding the Sunnah from invention comes to us through the one who guarded so much of the Sunnah itself.

معنى أَحْدَثَ ورَدّ — ميزان الأعمال الظاهر The Meaning of "Introduced" and "Rejected" — the Outward Scale of Deeds

أَحْدَثَ means to originate something new that was not there before. And أَمْرِنَا هَذَا — "this affair of ours" — is the religion itself: the دين the Prophet ﷺ was sent with. So whoever invents into the religion an act of worship, a belief, or a rite that is "not from it" — has no basis in the Book or the Sunnah — then it is رَدٌّ: literally "a rejection," meaning مَرْدُود — thrown back at its doer, unaccepted, earning no reward no matter how sincere he felt. Worship is built on الاتباع (following what the Messenger ﷺ brought), not on invention; the door of the religion was closed and sealed with ﴿الْيَوْمَ أَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِينَكُمْ﴾ (al-Māʾidah 5:3) — "This day I have perfected for you your religion." What was not religion on that day can never become religion after it.

Ibn Rajab in جامع العلوم والحكم sets this ḥadīth beside the very first of the Forty — the ḥadīth إنما الأعمال بالنيات. The two together are the scales on which every deed is weighed: the ḥadīth of النية (intention) is the inward scale — no deed counts unless it is done for Allah — and this ḥadīth is the outward scale — no deed counts unless it matches what the Messenger ﷺ brought. The second wording in Muslim widens the ruling further: «مَنْ عَمِلَ عَمَلًا لَيْسَ عَلَيْهِ أَمْرُنَا فَهُوَ رَدٌّ» — even the one who did not invent the practice himself, but merely acts upon an innovation someone else began, his deed too is thrown back.

شرطا قبول العبادة The Two Conditions of Accepted Worship

Shaykh al-ʿUthaymīn explains in his sharḥ of the Forty that from these two ḥadīths the scholars derive the two conditions without which no act of worship is accepted:

  1. الإِخْلَاص (sincerity) — the deed is done for Allah alone, seeking His face and no one else's praise. This is the fruit of Ḥadīth 1.
  2. المُتَابَعَة (conformity) — the deed is done the way the Prophet ﷺ did it and taught it. This is the fruit of Ḥadīth 5.

Lose either wing and the deed cannot fly. A deed done exactly by the Sunnah but for people's eyes is rejected for missing الإخلاص; a deed done with a weeping, sincere heart but invented into the religion is rejected for missing المتابعة. Sincerity is never a licence to innovate — the ḥadīth says "whoever introduces… it is rejected" without asking how the innovator felt. And this ḥadīth is also a proof in the other direction: it is a mercy that closes the door of burden — no one may bind you to acts of worship the Messenger ﷺ never brought.

البدعة في الدين لا في الدنيا Innovation Is in the Religion — Not in Worldly Things

Notice the precision of the wording: مَنْ أَحْدَثَ فِي أَمْرِنَا هَذَا — "into this affair of ours." The condemned بدعة (innovation in the religion) is inventing new worship, new beliefs, or new rites and attributing them to Islam. It is not the invention of worldly things. Cars, phones, aeroplanes, and microphones are not بدعة, because no one presents them as acts of worship or as part of the دين; the ḥadīth does not touch them. The scholars put it simply: the default in worldly affairs is permissibility until a proof forbids, while the default in worship is stopping until a proof allows. So the one who drives a car to the masjid has innovated nothing — and the one who invents a new form of ṣalāh, a celebration the Prophet ﷺ never legislated, or a dhikr with a manner and number Allah never revealed, has fallen into exactly what this ḥadīth throws back.

To study the definition of البدعة in detail — its categories, the doubts of those who defend it, and the reply of Ahl al-Sunnah — see the Aqeedah lesson: البدعة — Innovation in the Religion.

A gem from Ibn Rajab — فائدة

Ibn Rajab counts this ḥadīth among the tremendous foundations of Islam and draws out both its edges: by its explicit text, every deed not upon the command of Allah and His Messenger ﷺ is thrown back at its doer; and by its implication, every deed that is upon their command is accepted and counted. So the ḥadīth is not only a sword against بدعة — it is glad tidings for the follower: the one who clings to the Sunnah, even in a small and simple deed, walks a path whose acceptance the Messenger ﷺ himself has announced.

Memorize & Renew — احفظ وجدّد

مَنْ أَحْدَثَ فِي أَمْرِنَا هَذَا مَا لَيْسَ مِنْهُ فَهُوَ رَدٌّ

"Whoever introduces into this affair of ours that which is not from it, it is rejected." Memorize it word-perfect — it is the outward scale of every deed you will ever do. Before any act of worship, ask two questions: is it for Allah alone, and is it how the Messenger ﷺ did it?
