بُنِيَ الإِسْلَامُ عَلَى خَمْس

Islam is Built on Five

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Memorize the ḥadīth of the five pillars.
  • Understand the image of Islam as a building resting on five supports.
  • Know each pillar and why the shahādah is the foundation of all the rest.
  • Understand that the pillars differ in rank, and that faith is not deeds alone nor words alone.

عَنْ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَقُولُ: «بُنِيَ الإِسْلَامُ عَلَى خَمْسٍ: شَهَادَةِ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ، وَإِقَامِ الصَّلَاةِ، وَإِيتَاءِ الزَّكَاةِ، وَحَجِّ الْبَيْتِ، وَصَوْمِ رَمَضَانَ».

ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar said: I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say: "Islam is built upon five: the testimony that there is no god but Allah and that Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah, the establishment of the prayer, the giving of the zakāh, the pilgrimage to the House, and fasting Ramaḍān."

Narrated by ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

صورة البنيان The Image of the Building

Shaykh al-ʿUthaymīn explains that the Prophet ﷺ likened Islam to a building that stands on five supporting pillars. A building cannot stand without its supports; likewise the religion of a person rests upon these five. The greatest of them — the shahādatān (two testimonies) — is like the foundation on which the whole structure rests, while prayer, zakāh, fasting, and Ḥajj are the pillars raised upon it. This image teaches us that Islam is a complete, ordered whole — beliefs and acts of worship holding one another up.

الأركان الخمسة The Five Pillars
  1. الشهادتان — testifying that none is worthy of worship but Allah, and that Muḥammad is His Messenger. This is the foundation: worship for Allah alone (the meaning of lā ilāha illā Allāh), and following the way of His Messenger ﷺ.
  2. إقام الصَّلَاة — establishing the five daily prayers, upright and on time; the first deed a servant will be asked about.
  3. إيتاء الزكاة — giving the obligatory charity from wealth to its rightful recipients, purifying the soul and the wealth.
  4. حج البيت — pilgrimage to the Sacred House, once in a lifetime, for whoever is able.
  5. صوم رمضان — fasting the month of Ramaḍān.

A gem from al-ʿUthaymīn — فائدة

The pillars are not equal in rank: the shahādah is the greatest and is owed by every person at all times; the prayer comes next and is due five times a day; zakāh and fasting are due once a year in their season; and Ḥajj only once in a lifetime for the able. Knowing this order teaches the student where to place his care first — and shows that Islam is words of the tongue, belief of the heart, and deeds of the limbs together, not any one of them alone.

Explanation based on the sharḥ of Shaykh Muḥammad al-ʿUthaymīn · in our own words

لِأَغْرَاضِ التَّعْلِيمِ فَقَطْ — هَذَا المَوْقِعُ لَا يُفْتِي. لِلْفَتَاوَى، يُرْجَعُ إِلَى العُلَمَاءِ المَوْثُوقِين.
