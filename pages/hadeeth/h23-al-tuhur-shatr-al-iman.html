Ḥadīth 23 of 42 · MuslimYear 2

الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَان

Purification Is Half of Faith

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Say who narrated this ḥadīth and where it is reported, and give the two explanations of «الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَان».
  • Explain how «سُبْحَانَ الله» and «الحَمْدُ لِله» together are negation and affirmation, and that the Mīzān is real.
  • Distinguish why prayer is called nūr, ṣadaqah a burhān, and ṣabr a ḍiyāʾ.
  • Name the three kinds of ṣabr and the rank of riḍā above it.
  • Explain how the Qurʾān is «حُجَّةٌ لَكَ أَوْ عَلَيْك», and how every person sells his soul each morning — freeing it or destroying it.

شَرْحُ الشَّيْخِ ابْنِ عُثَيْمِين · ص ٢٤٣–٢٥٨
Sharḥ al-Arbaʿīn al-Nawawiyyah, Ibn ʿUthaymīn — pp. 243–258.

عَنْ أَبِي مَالِكٍ الحَارِثِ بْنِ عَاصِمٍ الأَشْعَرِيِّ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ: «الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ، وَالحَمْدُ لِلهِ تَمْلَأُ المِيزَانَ، وَسُبْحَانَ اللهِ وَالحَمْدُ لِلهِ تَمْلَآنِ — أَوْ تَمْلَأُ — مَا بَيْنَ السَّمَاءِ وَالأَرْضِ، وَالصَّلَاةُ نُورٌ، وَالصَّدَقَةُ بُرْهَانٌ، وَالصَّبْرُ ضِيَاءٌ، وَالقُرْآنُ حُجَّةٌ لَكَ أَوْ عَلَيْكَ. كُلُّ النَّاسِ يَغْدُو، فَبَائِعٌ نَفْسَهُ فَمُعْتِقُهَا أَوْ مُوبِقُهَا». رَوَاهُ مُسْلِم.

Abū Mālik al-Ḥārith ibn ʿĀṣim al-Ashʿarī (may Allah be pleased with him) said: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Purification is half of faith; ‘al-ḥamdu lillāh’ fills the Scale; ‘subḥān Allāh’ and ‘al-ḥamdu lillāh’ fill — or fills — what is between the heaven and the earth; prayer is light; charity is a proof; patience is a radiance; and the Qurʾān is a proof for you or against you. Every person goes out in the morning and sells his soul, either freeing it or destroying it." Narrated by Muslim.

Narrated by Abū Mālik al-Ashʿarī · رَوَاهُ مُسْلِم — Muslim, in his Ṣaḥīḥ (Kitāb al-Ṭahārah, bāb faḍl al-wuḍūʾ), so the ḥadīth is ṣaḥīḥ.

«الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَان» Purification Is Half of Faith

“Shaṭr” means half. Faith is either a doing or a leaving: the leaving is a kind of purification (تَطْهِير), and the doing is a bringing-into-being. So purification stands for one of the two halves of faith.

A second explanation was given: that «shaṭr al-īmān» means casting off shirk, for shirk is a najāsah — «إِنَّمَا المُشْرِكُونَ نَجَس». But the first meaning is better and more general.

إِنَّمَا الْمُشْرِكُونَ نَجَسٌ

Indeed the polytheists are impure.

سُورَةُ التَّوْبَة · 9:28

كَلِمَاتٌ تَمْلَأُ المِيزَان Words That Fill the Scale

«الحَمْدُ لِله» fills the Scale — and «سُبْحَانَ الله» joined with it fills what lies between heaven and earth. These two words are negation and affirmation: «سُبْحَانَ الله» is tanzīh — declaring Allah far above every deficiency (deficient attributes, any shortfall in His perfection, and any likeness to creation); «الحَمْدُ لِله» is the affirmation of His perfection, praising the Praised One with perfection together with love and reverence.

لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ

There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.

سُورَةُ الشُّورَى · 42:11

The Mīzān is real. Against the Muʿtazilah, who made it a figure for “establishing justice,” the correct view is that the Scale on the Day of Resurrection is a true Scale with two pans, by which deeds are weighed. «وَنَضَعُ المَوَازِينَ القِسْطَ لِيَوْمِ القِيَامَة» And how a deed or a word is weighed though it is not a body — Allah is able to make deeds and meanings into bodies; He is over all things capable.

وَنَضَعُ الْمَوَازِينَ الْقِسْطَ لِيَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ فَلَا تُظْلَمُ نَفْسٌ شَيْئًا

And We place the scales of justice for the Day of Resurrection, so no soul will be treated unjustly at all.

سُورَةُ الأَنْبِيَاء · 21:47

نُورٌ وَبُرْهَان Prayer Is Light, Charity Is a Proof
«الصَّلَاةُ نُور»The prayer — obligatory and voluntary — is a light: in the heart, in the face, in the grave, and at the gathering. Pray the true prayer in which your heart is present and your limbs are humbled, and you will feel your heart lit; and so he ﷺ said, “the coolness of my eye was placed in the prayer.”
«الصَّدَقَةُ بُرْهَان»Charity is a proof — a proof of the truthfulness of the giver’s faith. For what he gives away is wealth, the beloved thing; parting with the beloved shows that what he hopes for with Allah is more beloved to him still.
«الصَّبْرُ ضِيَاء» Patience Is a Radiance — Its Three Kinds

Ṣabr is holding the soul back from what it must be held back from. The people of knowledge make it three kinds:

١ · صَبْرٌ عَلَى الطَّاعَةPatience upon obedience — a man drawn by his soul to laziness, his bed, or idle talk, yet he binds himself to stand for the prayer. This is the highest in reward as patience, for in it is both restraint of the soul and toil of the body.
٢ · صَبْرٌ عَنِ المَعْصِيَةPatience away from disobedience — as Yūsuf ﷺ restrained himself from the wife of al-ʿAzīz when she had shut the doors and called him, with the caller strong and the barriers gone — and he did not do it.
٣ · صَبْرٌ عَلَى الأَقْدَارPatience upon the decrees — holding the heart from discontent, the tongue from wailing, and the limbs from striking the cheeks and tearing the garments when an affliction descends.

رَبِّ السِّجْنُ أَحَبُّ إِلَيَّ مِمَّا يَدْعُونَنِي إِلَيْهِ

My Lord, prison is more beloved to me than that to which they invite me.

سُورَةُ يُوسُف · 12:33

Above ṣabr is riḍā. The patient one’s heart may ache and grieve, yet he holds himself from the ḥarām; the content one’s heart follows the decree of Allah and is pleased with what He chose. So the people of knowledge say: ṣabr is obligatory, and riḍā is recommended.

Why ḍiyāʾ and not nūr? He called the prayer nūr but ṣabr ḍiyāʾ — and ḍiyāʾ carries heat, as Allah named the sun ḍiyāʾ: «جَعَلَ الشَّمْسَ ضِيَاء». Patience carries heat and bitterness, for it is hard upon the soul — so it is a radiance won through hardship.

هُوَ الَّذِي جَعَلَ الشَّمْسَ ضِيَاءً وَالْقَمَرَ نُورًا

It is He who made the sun a shining light and the moon a derived light.

سُورَةُ يُونُس · 10:5

«القُرْآنُ حُجَّةٌ لَكَ أَوْ عَلَيْك» The Qurʾān — a Proof For You or Against You

The creed of Ahl al-Sunnah on the Qurʾān. It is the speech of Allah — He truly spoke it; Jibrīl heard it and brought it down upon the heart of the Prophet ﷺ; its wording and its meaning are the speech of Allah, not created. Against this the people of innovation said it is created or a mere “expression” of His speech — and the sound view is what the Salaf held.

And it is a proof for you or against you. It is a proof for you if you give it its due and act by it, and a proof against you if you do not. Take «وَأَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَآتُوا الزَّكَاة»: the one who establishes the prayer — the Qurʾān is a proof for him; the one who does not — it is a proof against him. So weigh yourself: have I acted by it, or not?

«كُلُّ النَّاسِ يَغْدُو» Every Soul Sold Each Morning

Everyone goes out in the morning and sells his soul — that is, he burdens it with work and so “sells” it. And the sellers are two: one who frees it (مُعْتِق) and one who destroys it (مُوبِق). Whoever spends it in the obedience of Allah has freed it from the Fire; whoever spends his life in loss has destroyed it.

وَمِنَ النَّاسِ مَن يَشْرِي نَفْسَهُ ابْتِغَاءَ مَرْضَاتِ اللَّهِ

And of the people is he who sells himself, seeking the pleasure of Allah.

سُورَةُ البَقَرَة · 2:207

True freedom is obedience. Real freedom is to stand in the worship of Allah — not that a person lets his soul loose to do all it wants. As Ibn al-Qayyim said: men fled the servitude they were created for, and were tried with the servitude of the soul and the shayṭān — for whoever flees the worship of Allah stays in the bondage of the shayṭān.

True or False صَحٌ أَمْ خَطَأٌ
الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ، وَالإِيمَانُ إِمَّا فِعْلٌ وَإِمَّا تَرْك. Purification is half of faith, and faith is either a doing or a leaving.
«سُبْحَانَ الله» فِيهَا إِثْبَاتُ الكَمَالِ لَا نَفْيُ النَّقْص. «Subḥān Allāh» is an affirmation of perfection, not a negation of deficiency.
المِيزَانُ يَوْمَ القِيَامَةِ حِسِّيٌّ حَقِيقِيٌّ تُوزَنُ بِهِ الأَعْمَال. The Scale on the Day of Resurrection is real and tangible, by which deeds are weighed.
الصَّدَقَةُ بُرْهَانٌ لِأَنَّ بَذْلَ المَحْبُوبِ يَدُلُّ عَلَى صِدْقِ البَاذِل. Charity is a proof because parting with the beloved thing shows the truthfulness of the giver.
الرِّضَا بِأَقْدَارِ اللهِ أَعْلَى حَالاً مِنَ الصَّبْرِ عَلَيْهَا. Contentment with Allah’s decrees is a higher state than patience upon them.
القُرْآنُ مَخْلُوقٌ عِنْدَ أَهْلِ السُّنَّةِ وَالجَمَاعَة. The Qurʾān is created according to Ahl al-Sunnah wa-l-Jamāʿah.
الحُرِّيَّةُ الحَقِيقِيَّةُ هِيَ القِيَامُ بِطَاعَةِ اللهِ عَزَّ وَجَلّ. True freedom is to stand in the obedience of Allah.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ، وَالحَمْدُ لِلهِ تَمْلَأُ المِيزَانَ… وَالصَّلَاةُ نُورٌ، وَالصَّدَقَةُ بُرْهَانٌ، وَالصَّبْرُ ضِيَاءٌ، وَالقُرْآنُ حُجَّةٌ لَكَ أَوْ عَلَيْكَ

One ḥadīth that walks through the whole of worship: purification is half of faith; the two words of praise fill the Scale and all between heaven and earth — negation and affirmation; the prayer is a light, charity a proof of true faith, patience a radiance won through hardship (its three kinds: upon obedience, away from sin, upon the decrees); and the Qurʾān stands as a proof — for you if you act by it, against you if you do not. And every morning you sell your soul: you free it in obedience, or you destroy it. True freedom is to belong to Allah.

لِأَغْرَاضِ التَّعْلِيمِ فَقَطْ — هَذَا المَوْقِعُ لَا يُفْتِي. لِلْفَتَاوَى، يُرْجَعُ إِلَى العُلَمَاءِ المَوْثُوقِين.
