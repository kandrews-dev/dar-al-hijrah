مقدمة في مصطلح الحديث

Introduction to the Science of Hadith

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what a ḥadīth is and that the Sunnah is the second source of Islam after the Qurʾān.
  • Understand the two parts of every ḥadīth: the سَنَد (chain of narrators) and the مَتْن (text).
  • Understand the isnād system and why it is a unique protection Allah gave this ummah.
  • Know the main grades: صحيح · حسن · ضعيف · موضوع.
  • Recognise the major books of the Sunnah — the two Ṣaḥīḥs and the Six Books.
ما هو الحديث؟ What Is a Ḥadīth?

A ḥadīth is a report of what is attributed to the Prophet ﷺ — his sayings (قَوْل), his actions (فِعْل), his approvals (تَقْرِير — things done in his presence that he did not forbid), and descriptions of his character (صِفَة). The body of this guidance is called the Sunnah, and it is the second source of Islam after the Qurʾān. Obeying the Prophet ﷺ is obeying Allah, who said:

وَمَا آتَاكُمُ الرَّسُولُ فَخُذُوهُ وَمَا نَهَاكُمْ عَنْهُ فَانتَهُوا ۚ وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ

"And whatever the Messenger gives you, take it; and whatever he forbids you, abstain from it. And fear Allah." (al-Ḥashr 59:7)

The Sunnah explains, details, and completes what the Qurʾān lays down

And Allah described His Prophet ﷺ: وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ الْهَوَىٰ ۝ إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحْيٌ يُوحَىٰ — "Nor does he speak from desire; it is only a revelation revealed" (al-Najm 53:3–4). The مصطلح الحديث (science of hadith terminology) is the set of rules the scholars built to tell which reports are truly from him ﷺ and which are not — one of the greatest sciences Allah granted this ummah.

السَّنَد والمَتْن The Chain and the Text

Every ḥadīth has two parts:

  • السَّنَد (the sanad / isnād): the chain of narrators — the list of people who passed the report down, one from another, back to the Prophet ﷺ. For example: "al-Bukhārī said: ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb said: I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say…"
  • المَتْن (the matn): the actual text — the words of the Prophet ﷺ that the chain carries, e.g. "Actions are but by intentions."

الإسناد من الدين — The Isnād Is Part of the Religion

The great Imam ʿAbdullāh ibn al-Mubārak said: "The isnād is part of the religion; were it not for the isnād, whoever wished could say whatever he wished." No other nation preserved the words of its Prophet with a documented chain of trustworthy men reaching back to him. This is why a Muslim can know, with confidence, that a ḥadīth in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī truly goes back to the Prophet ﷺ — Allah protected His religion through the isnād.

درجات الحديث The Grades of a Ḥadīth

By examining the chain and text, the scholars grade a report. The main grades every beginner should know:

الدرجة Grade Meaning
صَحِيح Ṣaḥīḥ — Authentic An unbroken chain of upright (ʿadl), precise (ḍābiṭ) narrators, with no hidden defect — accepted and acted upon.
حَسَن Ḥasan — Good Like the ṣaḥīḥ but one narrator is slightly less precise — still accepted and acted upon.
ضَعِيف Ḍaʿīf — Weak A break in the chain or a weak narrator — not established as proof on its own.
مَوْضُوع Mawḍūʿ — Fabricated A lie invented and attributed to the Prophet ﷺ — the worst of all; forbidden to spread as his words.

The danger of fabrication is why the Prophet ﷺ gave a severe warning: مَنْ كَذَبَ عَلَيَّ مُتَعَمِّدًا فَلْيَتَبَوَّأْ مَقْعَدَهُ مِنَ النَّارِ — "Whoever lies about me deliberately, let him take his seat in the Fire" (al-Bukhārī & Muslim). So a Muslim never says "the Prophet ﷺ said…" unless he knows it is established.

كتب السنة The Major Books of the Sunnah

The scholars gathered the authentic Sunnah into trusted books. The most important for a beginner to know:

  • Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim — "the two Ṣaḥīḥs" (aṣ-Ṣaḥīḥān): the most authentic books after the Qurʾān. What both agree upon is called مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْه ("agreed upon").
  • The Four Sunan: Sunan Abī Dāwūd, Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī, Sunan al-Nasāʾī, and Sunan Ibn Mājah. Together with the two Ṣaḥīḥs these are الكُتُب السِّتَّة — "the Six Books."
  • Also foundational: the Muwaṭṭaʾ of Imam Mālik and the Musnad of Imam Aḥmad ibn Ḥanbal.

Two small, beloved collections make the perfect starting point for a student — and are our two Hadeeth tracks: Imam al-Nawawī's Forty Ḥadīth (al-Arbaʿīn), which gathers the foundations of the religion, and his Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn ("The Gardens of the Righteous"), a topic-by-topic collection for building good character and worship.

Reflect — تدبّر

الإِسْنَادُ مِنَ الدِّينِ، وَلَوْلَا الإِسْنَادُ لَقَالَ مَنْ شَاءَ مَا شَاءَ
