Ḥadīth 19 of 42 · al-Tirmidhī (ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ)Year 2

يَا غُلَامُ إِنِّي أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَات

O Boy, I Shall Teach You Some Words

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Say who narrated this ḥadīth and how old he was, and what «أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَات» was meant to do before the words that followed.
  • Explain «احْفَظِ اللهَ يَحْفَظْك» in both directions — what guarding Allah means, and what being guarded means.
  • Give the two clauses on asking and seeking aid, and the point of tawḥīd they establish.
  • State what the ummah can and cannot do to a person, on «لَوِ اجْتَمَعَت».
  • Give the meanings of the second riwāyah — knowing Allah in ease, and the three pairings of victory, relief and ease.

شَرْحُ الشَّيْخِ ابْنِ عُثَيْمِين · ص ٢٢٤–٢٢٩
Sharḥ al-Arbaʿīn al-Nawawiyyah, Ibn ʿUthaymīn — pp. 224–229.

عَنْ أَبِي العَبَّاسِ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: كُنْتُ خَلْفَ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ يَوْمًا فَقَالَ: «يَا غُلَامُ، إِنِّي أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَاتٍ: احْفَظِ اللهَ يَحْفَظْكَ، احْفَظِ اللهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَكَ، إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللهَ، وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللهِ. وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ الأُمَّةَ لَوِ اجْتَمَعَتْ عَلَى أَنْ يَنْفَعُوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَنْفَعُوكَ إِلَّا بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللهُ لَكَ، وَإِنِ اجْتَمَعُوا عَلَى أَنْ يَضُرُّوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَضُرُّوكَ إِلَّا بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللهُ عَلَيْكَ؛ رُفِعَتِ الأَقْلَامُ وَجَفَّتِ الصُّحُفُ». رَوَاهُ التِّرْمِذِيُّ وَقَالَ: حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ صَحِيحٌ.

Ibn ʿAbbās (may Allah be pleased with them both) said: One day I was behind the Prophet ﷺ and he said: "O boy, I shall teach you some words: Guard Allah and He will guard you; guard Allah and you will find Him before you. When you ask, ask Allah; and when you seek help, seek the help of Allah. Know that if the whole nation were to gather to benefit you with something, they would not benefit you except with something Allah had already written for you; and if they gathered to harm you with something, they would not harm you except with something Allah had already written against you. The pens have been lifted and the pages have dried."

Narrated by Ibn ʿAbbās · رَوَاهُ التِّرْمِذِيُّ — al-Tirmidhī, who said: a ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ ḥadīth. The grading named here is al-Tirmidhī’s own.

«يَا غُلَام» A Lesson Taught to a Boy

Read the setting, because it is part of the teaching. Ibn ʿAbbās was behind the Prophet ﷺ — riding with him or walking behind him — and he was a boy: the Prophet ﷺ passed away when Ibn ʿAbbās had barely reached puberty, around fifteen or less. Into that young ear the Prophet ﷺ poured one of the greatest summaries of tawḥīd in the Sunnah.

And «إِنِّي أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَات» he said to make him attentive. It is a sunnah of teaching: one who is about to say something weighty prefaces it with what draws the listener in — so the ear is ready before the words arrive.

«احْفَظِ اللهَ يَحْفَظْك» Guard Allah, and He Will Guard You

The first word, and it runs both ways. «احْفَظِ الله» — guard His limits and His sharʿ: by doing His commands and avoiding His prohibitions, and by learning of His religion what your worship and your dealings stand upon. «يَحْفَظْك» — He guards you in your religion, your family, your wealth and your self, for Allah rewards the doers of good with His good.

And the most important thing He guards is your religion. He keeps you from deviation and misguidance — for the more a person is guided, the more Allah increases him in guidance.

وَالَّذِينَ اهْتَدَوْا زَادَهُمْ هُدًى وَآتَاهُمْ تَقْوَاهُمْ

And those who are guided — He increases them in guidance and grants them their taqwā.

سُورَةُ مُحَمَّد · 47:17

And the warning folded inside the promise: one who does not guard Allah does not deserve that Allah guard him. To squander the religion of Allah is to be left to oneself —

وَلَا تَكُونُوا كَالَّذِينَ نَسُوا اللَّهَ فَأَنسَاهُمْ أَنفُسَهُمْ

And do not be like those who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves.

سُورَةُ الحَشْر · 59:19

«احْفَظِ اللهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَك» Guard Allah, and You Will Find Him Before You

«تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَك» and «تَجِدْهُ أَمَامَك» are one meaning: you will find Allah before you — guiding you to every good, drawing you near, warding off every evil — especially when you have guarded Him by seeking His aid. For when a person relies on Allah, Allah suffices him:

يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ حَسْبُكَ اللَّهُ وَمَنِ اتَّبَعَكَ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ

O Prophet, sufficient for you is Allah, and for whoever follows you of the believers.

سُورَةُ الأَنْفَال · 8:64

«إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللهَ، وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللهِ» When You Ask, Ask Allah; When You Seek Help, Seek It of Allah

Here is the heart of the ḥadīth, and the heart of tawḥīd. If you have a need, do not ask any but Allah; and if you seek aid, seek it of none but Allah — for in His hand is the dominion of the heavens and the earth.

أَمَّن يُجِيبُ الْمُضْطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكْشِفُ السُّوءَ

Is He [not best] who answers the distressed one when he calls upon Him and removes the evil?

سُورَةُ النَّمْل · 27:62

But what of asking a creature for what it can do? If you ask a person for what is within his power, know that he is a means (sabab), and the One who brings it about (al-musabbib) is Allah — had He willed, He would have stopped that person from giving you. So lean on Allah, not on the means.

And these two clauses show that asking other than Allah is a deficiency in tawḥīd — which is why asking of other than Allah, in little or much, is disliked. Even when Allah grants your need at the hand of a person, do not forget the One who made him the means.

«لَوِ اجْتَمَعَتِ الأُمَّة...» Benefit and Harm Are Only by What Allah Has Written

And the proof that steadies the heart against all people: the whole nation, from its first to its last, could not benefit you with anything except what Allah had already written for you; nor harm you except with what He had already written against you.

So where does the benefit people bring you really come from? From Allah in truth — for He is the One who wrote it for you. This drives a person to lean on Allah alone and to turn his face from the creation, who own him neither harm nor benefit.

And if harm reaches you from someone, know Allah wrote it upon you — so be content with His decree. There is no objection to your striving to repel the harm, for Allah said that the recompense of a wrong is a like wrong; but your heart rests in His writing.

«رُفِعَتِ الأَقْلَامُ وَجَفَّتِ الصُّحُف» — what Allah has written is finished: the pens have been raised and the pages have dried. There is no changing the words of Allah. The ḥadīth that began by teaching a boy to guard Allah ends by anchoring him in the decree of Allah.

الرِّوَايَةُ الأُخْرَى The Other Narration

In the other wording come further words, each a counsel for hardship:

تَعَرَّفْ إِلَى اللهِ فِي الرَّخَاءِ يَعْرِفْكَ فِي الشِّدَّةFulfil the right of Allah in ease, health and wealth, and He will know you in hardship — when health and riches fall away and your need is sharp, He acknowledges you by the good that went before.
مَا أَخْطَأَكَ لَمْ يَكُنْ لِيُصِيبَكWhat missed you was never to befall you, and what befell you was never to miss you — the whole matter is in Allah’s hand, which is a comfort both when a thing you feared comes and when a thing you loved is lost.
النَّصْرُ مَعَ الصَّبْرVictory is with patience — so a person bears up in order to attain it: patience upon obedience, away from sin, and over the painful decrees.
الفَرَجُ مَعَ الكَرْبRelief is with distress — the more affairs press, the nearer the opening; the hardship is the sign relief is at hand.

And the third pairing the Qurʾān itself states twice over: ease comes with hardship — indeed hardship is wrapped in two eases, one before and one after.

فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا * إِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

For indeed, with hardship comes ease. Indeed, with hardship comes ease.

سُورَةُ الشَّرْح · 94:5–6

Ibn ʿAbbās said: one hardship will not overcome two eases. So when affairs press hard upon you, take refuge in Allah, awaiting His relief and believing His promise.

سُؤَالُ غَيْرِ اللهِ وَالاسْتِعَانَةُ بِهِ كَمَالٌ فِي التَّوْحِيد. Asking other than Allah and seeking its aid is a perfection in tawḥīd.
لَوِ اجْتَمَعَتِ الأُمَّةُ عَلَى نَفْعِكَ لَمْ يَنْفَعُوكَ إِلَّا بِمَا كَتَبَهُ اللهُ لَكَ. If the whole nation gathered to benefit you, they would benefit you only with what Allah had written for you.
لَا يَجُوزُ السَّعْيُ فِي دَفْعِ الضَّرَرِ الَّذِي كَتَبَهُ اللهُ. It is not permitted to strive to repel a harm that Allah has written.
النَّصْرُ مَعَ الصَّبْر، وَالفَرَجُ مَعَ الكَرْب، وَمَعَ العُسْرِ يُسْرٌ. Victory is with patience, relief with distress, and with hardship comes ease.
مَنْ لَمْ يَحْفَظِ اللهَ فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَسْتَحِقُّ أَنْ يَحْفَظَهُ الله. Whoever does not guard Allah does not deserve that Allah guard him.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

احْفَظِ اللهَ يَحْفَظْكَ، احْفَظِ اللهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَكَ، إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللهَ، وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللهِ

A whole creed of reliance, taught to a boy riding behind the Prophet ﷺ. Guard Allah — His limits and His law — and He guards you, most of all in your religion. When you ask, ask Allah; when you seek help, seek it of Allah — for the creation, gathered from first to last, owns you neither a harm nor a benefit except by what He has already written, and the pens are lifted and the pages are dry. Everything the believer leans on, in one breath, returned to the One who wrote it.

دَارُ الهِجْرَة — المَدْرَسَةُ السَّلَفِيَّةُ التَّأْسِيسِيَّةُ لِلْعُلُومِ الشَّرْعِيَّة

al-Arbaʿūn al-Nawawiyyah · sharḥ of Shaykh Muḥammad ibn Ṣāliḥ al-ʿUthaymīn · in our own words

لِأَغْرَاضِ التَّعْلِيمِ فَقَطْ — هَذَا المَوْقِعُ لَا يُفْتِي. لِلْفَتَاوَى، يُرْجَعُ إِلَى العُلَمَاءِ المَوْثُوقِين.
