إنما الأعمال بالنيات

Actions are by Intentions

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Memorize and understand the ḥadīth of intentions.
  • Learn the two functions of the niyyah (intention).
  • Understand ikhlāṣ — sincerity for Allah alone — through the example of hijrah.
  • Know that the intention's place is the heart.

عَنْ أَمِيرِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أَبِي حَفْصٍ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَقُولُ: «إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى، فَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى اللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ فَهِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى اللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ، وَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ لِدُنْيَا يُصِيبُهَا أَوِ امْرَأَةٍ يَنْكِحُهَا فَهِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى مَا هَاجَرَ إِلَيْهِ».

"Actions are but by intentions, and every person shall have only what he intended. So whoever's emigration was to Allah and His Messenger, his emigration is to Allah and His Messenger; and whoever's emigration was for some worldly gain he would acquire, or a woman he would marry, his emigration is to that for which he emigrated."

Narrated by ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb · al-Bukhārī & Muslim

حديث عظيم A Foundation of the Religion

Shaykh al-ʿUthaymīn notes that the scholars counted this ḥadīth among those upon which Islam revolves. It is the scale for the inward deeds — the actions of the heart — while the ḥadīth, "Whoever introduces into this affair of ours something not from it, it is rejected," is the scale for the outward deeds. A deed is sound only when both are sound: a correct intention within, and agreement with the Sunnah without.

وظيفتا النية The Two Functions of Intention

"Actions are but by intentions" — every deed is built upon its intention and judged by it; without a sound intention an act of worship is not accepted. al-ʿUthaymīn explains that the niyyah does two things:

  1. It distinguishes worship from habit — washing the limbs as wuḍūʾ versus merely to cool off; leaving food as a fast versus as a diet.
  2. It distinguishes acts of worship from one another — the obligatory prayer from a voluntary one, Ẓuhr from ʿAṣr, the fast of Ramaḍān from a voluntary fast.
الإخلاص Sincerity — for Allah alone

"Every person shall have only what he intended" — your reward is exactly according to your intention. The Prophet ﷺ proved this with hijrah: the very same outward act — leaving Makkah for Madinah — is, for the one whose aim was Allah and His Messenger, an immense act of worship; but for the one whose aim was a worldly gain or a marriage, it earns only that worldly thing. The act is identical; the intention divides them completely. So purify your intention for Allah in everything you do.

محل النية Where the Intention Lives

The place of the intention is the heart. Pronouncing it aloud — saying "I intend to pray such-and-such" — is not from the guidance of the Prophet ﷺ. And a beautiful benefit al-ʿUthaymīn draws out: the believer can turn permissible habits into worship by his intention — eating, sleeping, earning a living — when he intends by them strength to obey Allah. With a good intention, a believer's whole life becomes worship.

A gem from al-ʿUthaymīn — فائدة

Two ḥadīths together form the balance of every deed: this one — "Actions are by intentions" — weighs the inside (sincerity), and "Whoever innovates in this affair…" weighs the outside (following the Sunnah). A deed is accepted only when it passes both scales.

"Actions are but by intentions." Before every deed today — your prayer, your study, even your meal — pause and renew your intention for Allah. Teach the children to ask: "Why am I doing this?"
