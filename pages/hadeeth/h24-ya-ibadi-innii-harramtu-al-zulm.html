Ḥadīth 24 of 42 · MuslimYear 2

يَا عِبَادِي إِنِّي حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْم

O My Servants, I Have Forbidden Injustice

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Say who narrated this ḥadīth, where it is reported, and what a ḥadīth qudsī is — and how it differs from the Qurʾān.
  • Explain «حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي» — that Allah forbade injustice upon Himself though able to do it, and that a negation affirms the opposite perfection.
  • Explain the servant’s need laid bare — all are astray, hungry and naked but for Allah — and the two kinds of guidance.
  • State how Allah forgives all sins for the one who seeks it, and the three categories sins fall into.
  • Draw out the perfection of Allah’s self-sufficiency and dominion from the “law anna” passages, and the meaning of «إِنَّمَا هِيَ أَعْمَالُكُم».

شَرْحُ الشَّيْخِ ابْنِ عُثَيْمِين · ص ٢٥٨–٢٧٧
Sharḥ al-Arbaʿīn al-Nawawiyyah, Ibn ʿUthaymīn — pp. 258–277.

عَنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ الغِفَارِيِّ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ فِيمَا يَرْوِيهِ عَنْ رَبِّهِ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ أَنَّهُ قَالَ: «يَا عِبَادِي، إِنِّي حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي وَجَعَلْتُهُ بَيْنَكُمْ مُحَرَّمًا فَلَا تَظَالَمُوا. يَا عِبَادِي، كُلُّكُمْ ضَالٌّ إِلَّا مَنْ هَدَيْتُهُ فَاسْتَهْدُونِي أَهْدِكُمْ. يَا عِبَادِي، كُلُّكُمْ جَائِعٌ إِلَّا مَنْ أَطْعَمْتُهُ فَاسْتَطْعِمُونِي أُطْعِمْكُمْ. يَا عِبَادِي، كُلُّكُمْ عَارٍ إِلَّا مَنْ كَسَوْتُهُ فَاسْتَكْسُونِي أَكْسُكُمْ. يَا عِبَادِي، إِنَّكُمْ تُخْطِئُونَ بِاللَّيْلِ وَالنَّهَارِ وَأَنَا أَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا فَاسْتَغْفِرُونِي أَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ. يَا عِبَادِي، إِنَّكُمْ لَنْ تَبْلُغُوا ضَرِّي فَتَضُرُّونِي وَلَنْ تَبْلُغُوا نَفْعِي فَتَنْفَعُونِي. يَا عِبَادِي، لَوْ أَنَّ أَوَّلَكُمْ وَآخِرَكُمْ وَإِنْسَكُمْ وَجِنَّكُمْ كَانُوا عَلَى أَتْقَى قَلْبِ رَجُلٍ وَاحِدٍ مِنْكُمْ مَا زَادَ ذَلِكَ فِي مُلْكِي شَيْئًا. يَا عِبَادِي، لَوْ أَنَّ أَوَّلَكُمْ وَآخِرَكُمْ وَإِنْسَكُمْ وَجِنَّكُمْ كَانُوا عَلَى أَفْجَرِ قَلْبِ رَجُلٍ وَاحِدٍ مِنْكُمْ مَا نَقَصَ ذَلِكَ مِنْ مُلْكِي شَيْئًا. يَا عِبَادِي، لَوْ أَنَّ أَوَّلَكُمْ وَآخِرَكُمْ وَإِنْسَكُمْ وَجِنَّكُمْ قَامُوا فِي صَعِيدٍ وَاحِدٍ فَسَأَلُونِي فَأَعْطَيْتُ كُلَّ وَاحِدٍ مَسْأَلَتَهُ مَا نَقَصَ ذَلِكَ مِمَّا عِنْدِي إِلَّا كَمَا يَنْقُصُ المِخْيَطُ إِذَا أُدْخِلَ البَحْرَ. يَا عِبَادِي، إِنَّمَا هِيَ أَعْمَالُكُمْ أُحْصِيهَا لَكُمْ ثُمَّ أُوَفِّيكُمْ إِيَّاهَا، فَمَنْ وَجَدَ خَيْرًا فَلْيَحْمَدِ اللهَ، وَمَنْ وَجَدَ غَيْرَ ذَلِكَ فَلَا يَلُومَنَّ إِلَّا نَفْسَهُ». رَوَاهُ مُسْلِم.

Abū Dharr al-Ghifārī (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Prophet ﷺ said, narrating from his Lord: "O My servants, I have forbidden injustice for Myself and made it forbidden among you, so do not wrong one another. O My servants, all of you are astray except those I guide, so seek guidance of Me and I will guide you. O My servants, all of you are hungry except those I feed, so ask Me for food and I will feed you. O My servants, all of you are naked except those I clothe, so ask Me for clothing and I will clothe you. O My servants, you sin by night and day and I forgive all sins, so seek My forgiveness and I will forgive you. O My servants, you will never reach harming Me so as to harm Me, nor benefiting Me so as to benefit Me. O My servants, were the first of you and the last, the men of you and the jinn, upon the most pious heart of any one man among you, that would add nothing to My dominion. O My servants, were they upon the most wicked heart of any one man among you, that would subtract nothing from My dominion. O My servants, were the first of you and the last, the men of you and the jinn, to stand on one plain and ask of Me, and I gave each one his request, that would diminish what I have only as a needle diminishes the sea when dipped into it. O My servants, they are only your deeds that I count for you and then repay you in full; so whoever finds good, let him praise Allah, and whoever finds otherwise, let him blame none but himself." Narrated by Muslim.

Narrated by Abū Dharr al-Ghifārī · رَوَاهُ مُسْلِم — Muslim, in his Ṣaḥīḥ (Kitāb al-Birr wa-l-Ṣilah wa-l-Ādāb, bāb taḥrīm al-ẓulm), so the ḥadīth is ṣaḥīḥ.

مَا هُوَ الحَدِيثُ القُدْسِيّ؟ What a Ḥadīth Qudsī Is

«فِيمَا يَرْوِيهِ عَنْ رَبِّه» — a qudsī ḥadīth is what the Prophet ﷺ narrates from his Lord. It is the highest rank of isnād, for the end of a chain is either the Lord (qudsī), the Prophet ﷺ (marfūʿ), a Companion (mawqūf), or a Tābiʿī (maqṭūʿ). It is not the Qurʾān, by ijmāʿ.

القُرْآنWorshipped by its recitation — ten ḥasanāt for each letter; a challenge (taḥaddī) was issued to match it; divinely preserved; recited in the prayer; touched only in purity.
الحَدِيثُ القُدْسِيّNot recited as an act of worship by the letter; no taḥaddī; among it is the ṣaḥīḥ and the ḥasan — even the weak has been ascribed to it; not a condition of the prayer.

The safest word. Scholars disputed whether the wording of a qudsī ḥadīth is from Allah or only its meaning. The safest is to say simply: it is what the Prophet ﷺ narrated from his Lord, and to leave the delving — for in it is a kind of takalluf (forced depth).

«حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي» Allah Forbade Injustice Upon Himself

“I forbade it upon Myself” means: I withheld it though able to do it. We say “though able,” for if it were impossible for Him there would be no praise in it — one is praised for leaving a thing only when he could have done it. Could Allah wrong the creation? In power, yes; but we know by His own word that He will not — «وَلَا يَظْلِمُ رَبُّكَ أَحَدًا».

وَلَا يَظْلِمُ رَبُّكَ أَحَدًا

And your Lord does not wrong anyone.

سُورَةُ الكَهْف · 18:49

And Allah has the right to decree upon Himself what He wills, for the ruling belongs to Him — «كَتَبَ عَلَى نَفْسِهِ الرَّحْمَة». A thing is forbidden or made binding upon Allah only when He decreed it so; our intellects neither forbid nor oblige anything upon Him.

كَتَبَ عَلَىٰ نَفْسِهِ الرَّحْمَةَ

He has decreed upon Himself mercy.

سُورَةُ الأَنْعَام · 6:12

Two fine points. «نَفْس» here means the Essence (الذَّات), as in «وَيُحَذِّرُكُمُ اللهُ نَفْسَه» — not an attribute among the attributes.

And no negation is made of Allah’s attributes except to affirm the opposite perfection: negating injustice affirms perfect justice (العَدْل).

وَيُحَذِّرُكُمُ اللَّهُ نَفْسَهُ

And Allah warns you of Himself.

سُورَةُ آلِ عِمْرَان · 3:28

«وَجَعَلْتُهُ بَيْنَكُمْ مُحَرَّمًا فَلَا تَظَالَمُوا» — and He forbade it between us. Ẓulm turns on two things: withholding a due owed to another, or burdening another with what is not owed. Taking the wealth of hostile disbelievers in war is no ẓulm, for it was made lawful; but the wealth and blood of the muʿāhad — one under a covenant — are protected: «مَنْ قَتَلَ مُعَاهَدًا لَمْ يَرَحْ رَائِحَةَ الجَنَّةِ» (al-Bukhārī).

ضَالٌّ، جَائِعٌ، عَارٍ Astray, Hungry, Naked — But for Him

The servant’s utter need, laid bare three times. All are astray but for His guidance; all are hungry, for none can create what bodies live by; all are naked, for we came from our mothers’ wombs bare. So ask Him — «فَاسْتَهْدُونِي أَهْدِكُم», «فَاسْتَطْعِمُونِي أُطْعِمْكُم», «فَاسْتَكْسُونِي أَكْسُكُم» — and you will find it.

هِدَايَةُ التَّوْفِيقThe guidance of success — to be set firm upon the truth. This is sought from Allah alone; no one can place it in a heart but Him.
هِدَايَةُ الدَّلَالَةThe guidance of showing the way — teaching and directing. This may be sought from the people of knowledge: “O so-and-so, instruct me in this.”

And seeking provision means asking Allah and taking the means. The sky does not rain gold or bread — «فَانْتَشِرُوا فِي الأَرْضِ وَابْتَغُوا مِنْ فَضْلِ الله». So turn to Allah for sustenance, then walk out and work for it.

فَانتَشِرُوا فِي الْأَرْضِ وَابْتَغُوا مِن فَضْلِ اللَّهِ

Then disperse through the land and seek of the bounty of Allah.

سُورَةُ الجُمُعَة · 62:10

«وَأَنَا أَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا» You Sin — and He Forgives All of It

The son of Adam is much-erring — «إِنَّهُ كَانَ ظَلُومًا جَهُولًا» — sinning by night and day. Yet Allah forgives all sins for the one who seeks His forgiveness — whether by asking in words (“O Allah, forgive me”) or by the deeds that bring forgiveness.

إِنَّهُ كَانَ ظَلُومًا جَهُولًا

Indeed, he was unjust and ignorant.

سُورَةُ الأَحْزَاب · 33:72

الكُفْرNeeds a specific repentance by ijmāʿ — good deeds do not expiate it.
الكَبَائِرThe major sins — they need a particular tawbah; the acts of obedience do not wipe them out.
الصَّغَائِرThe minor sins — expiated by the acts of obedience (prayer to prayer, Jumuʿah to Jumuʿah, Ramaḍān to Ramaḍān), so long as the major sins are avoided.
«لَنْ تَبْلُغُوا ضَرِّي… وَلَنْ تَبْلُغُوا نَفْعِي» You Cannot Harm Him, Nor Benefit Him

The One addressing them is utterly free of them. Creation cannot reach to harm Allah nor to benefit Him, for the Harmer and the Benefiter is Allah alone — out of the perfection of His riches beyond His servants. Then come the three “law anna” (“were it that…”) statements that unveil that wealth:

عَلَى أَتْقَى قَلْبWere all creation — first and last, men and jinn — upon the most pious heart of one man, that would add nothing to His dominion.
عَلَى أَفْجَرِ قَلْبWere they upon the most wicked heart of one man, that would subtract nothing from His dominion.
قَامُوا فِي صَعِيدٍ وَاحِدWere they to stand on one plain and ask, and He gave each his request, it would diminish what He has only as a needle diminishes the sea when dipped in.

And the heart is the seat of it all. Taqwā and fujūr are measured by the heart — “the most pious heart,” “the most wicked heart” — and so he ﷺ said: in the body is a morsel; when it is sound the whole body is sound, and when it is corrupt the whole body is corrupt — and it is the heart. So tend to your heart.

«إِنَّمَا هِيَ أَعْمَالُكُم» Only Your Deeds, Repaid in Full

The close of the ḥadīth restricts it all to one thing: “they are only your deeds that I count for you and repay you in full.” Allah counts every deed exactly — nothing is lost — and repays it: «فَمَنْ يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَه».

فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُ ۝ وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا يَرَهُ

So whoever does an atom’s weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom’s weight of evil will see it.

سُورَةُ الزَّلْزَلَة · 99:7–8

So the verdict is just. Whoever finds good, let him praise Allah — for enabling the deed and for rewarding it. And whoever finds otherwise, let him blame none but himself — for he was not wronged: the messengers conveyed, the Qurʾān stands as a proof, and still he left it all. The blame is on no one but him.

What does «نَفْس» mean in «حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي»?

An attribute among His attributes
The Essence (الذَّات) itself, as in «وَيُحَذِّرُكُمُ اللهُ نَفْسَهُ»
The soul of the Prophet ﷺ
A created spirit

“Negating injustice of Allah” affirms what?

That He is unable
Perfect justice (العَدْل) — for no attribute is negated of Allah except to affirm the opposite perfection
That justice is optional
Nothing at all

What are the two kinds of guidance in «فَاسْتَهْدُونِي أَهْدِكُم»?

Guidance of wealth and guidance of health
Hidāyat al-tawfīq (success — from Allah alone) and hidāyat al-dalālah (showing the way — sought also from the people of knowledge)
Guidance of the body and guidance of the tongue
Public and private guidance

What does seeking provision (الاسْتِطْعَام) require, alongside asking Allah?

Nothing — provision comes without any effort
Taking the means — the sky rains no gold; «فَانْتَشِرُوا فِي الأَرْضِ»
Only reciting duʿāʾ a fixed number
Abandoning all work and trusting fate

For whom does Allah forgive all sins, per «فَاسْتَغْفِرُونِي أَغْفِرْ لَكُم»?

Everyone, whether they seek it or not
The one who seeks His forgiveness — by words or by the deeds that bring it
Only the prophets
Only those free of all minor sins

How are sins categorised in this lesson?

Spoken, written, and intended
Kufr (needs tawbah by ijmāʿ), kabāʾir (need a particular tawbah), and ṣaghāʾir (expiated by acts of obedience)
Small, medium, and large by count
Hidden and open only

What do the three “law anna” statements establish?

That creation can increase or decrease Allah’s kingdom
The perfection of Allah’s self-sufficiency and dominion — all obedience adds nothing, all sin subtracts nothing, all giving diminishes nothing
That the jinn are more numerous than mankind
That duʿāʾ has no effect

What does «فَمَنْ وَجَدَ غَيْرَ ذَلِكَ فَلَا يَلُومَنَّ إِلَّا نَفْسَهُ» teach?

That Allah wrongs some of His servants
That the one who finds evil was not wronged — the proof was established and he left it, so the blame is on himself alone
That deeds are not recorded
That repentance is useless
True or False صَحٌ أَمْ خَطَأٌ
الحَدِيثُ القُدْسِيُّ هُوَ مَا رَوَاهُ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ عَنْ رَبِّهِ عَزَّ وَجَلّ. A qudsī ḥadīth is what the Prophet ﷺ narrated from his Lord.
القُرْآنُ وَالحَدِيثُ القُدْسِيُّ سَوَاءٌ فِي كُلِّ الأَحْكَام. The Qurʾān and the qudsī ḥadīth are the same in every ruling.
اللهُ تَعَالَى قَادِرٌ عَلَى الظُّلْمِ لَكِنَّهُ حَرَّمَهُ عَلَى نَفْسِهِ لِكَمَالِ عَدْلِه. Allah is able to do injustice, but He forbade it upon Himself out of the perfection of His justice.
هِدَايَةُ التَّوْفِيقِ تُطْلَبُ مِنَ النَّاسِ كَهِدَايَةِ الدَّلَالَة. The guidance of success is sought from people, like the guidance of showing the way.
لَوْ كَانَ الخَلْقُ كُلُّهُمْ عَلَى أَتْقَى قَلْبِ رَجُلٍ مَا زَادَ ذَلِكَ فِي مُلْكِ الله. Were all creation upon the most pious heart of one man, that would add nothing to Allah’s dominion.
مَحَلُّ التَّقْوَى وَالفُجُورِ هُوَ القَلْب. The seat of taqwā and fujūr is the heart.
مَنْ وَجَدَ غَيْرَ الخَيْرِ فَإِنَّ اللهَ قَدْ ظَلَمَهُ. Whoever finds other than good — Allah has wronged him.

Memorize & Reflect — احْفَظْ وَتَدَبَّرْ

يَا عِبَادِي، إِنِّي حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي وَجَعَلْتُهُ بَيْنَكُمْ مُحَرَّمًا فَلَا تَظَالَمُوا… إِنَّمَا هِيَ أَعْمَالُكُمْ أُحْصِيهَا لَكُمْ ثُمَّ أُوَفِّيكُمْ إِيَّاهَا

Allah speaks directly to His servants. He forbade injustice upon Himself — though able — and between us, so we wrong no one. He lays bare our need: all astray, hungry, naked but for Him, so ask Him. He forgives every sin for the one who seeks it. We cannot harm or benefit Him: all our obedience adds nothing to His dominion, all our sin takes nothing from it, all our asking diminishes His treasures only as a needle diminishes the sea. And it closes on justice: they are only your deeds, counted and repaid in full — so whoever finds good, let him praise Allah; and whoever finds otherwise, let him blame none but himself.
