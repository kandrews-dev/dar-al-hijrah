القرآن الكريم

Quran & Tafsīr

We study the Qur'an through the tafsīr of Imam ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Saʿdī — clear, concise, and built upon the understanding of the Salaf. Each lesson gives the āyāt, a translation, and al-Saʿdī's explanation in our own words. We began with al-Fātiḥah and Juzʾ ʿAmma, then Juzʾ 29, and have now completed Juzʾ 28 — the last three juzʾ of the Qurʾān.

السور

Sūrahs — al-Fātiḥah, and the last three juzʾ complete: Juzʾ ʿAmma (30), Juzʾ 29, and Juzʾ 28

Sūrah 1 · Makki

الفاتحة

Al-Fātiḥah — The Opening

The seven āyāt recited in every rakʿah — and, as al-Saʿdī shows, a summary of the whole religion.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrahs 112–114 · Juzʾ ʿAmma

الإخلاص · الفلق · الناس

The Three Quls

al-Ikhlāṣ, al-Falaq & al-Nās — the oneness of Allah and a shelter from every evil. The sūrahs the children memorize.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 111 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

المسد

Al-Masad — The Palm Fibre

Abu Lahab condemned — wealth and power cannot protect from Allah's decree. A prophecy of the unseen fulfilled exactly.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 110 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Madani

النصر

Al-Naṣr — The Help & Victory

The Conquest of Makkah foretold — and after every victory: glorify, praise, and seek forgiveness. Two hidden hints from al-Saʿdī.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 109 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الكافرون

Al-Kāfirūn — The Disbelievers

A complete public disavowal of shirk — in action and in the heart. The sūrah recited before sleep and in Fajr's sunnah rakʿāt.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 108 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الكوثر

Al-Kawthar — The Abundance

Allah's gift of overwhelming goodness — the river, the Cistern. Pray and sacrifice to Him alone. The hater is the truly cut-off one.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 107 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الماعون

Al-Māʿūn — Small Kindnesses

Denial of the Last Day shows in cruelty to orphans, neglect of the poor, heedless prayer, showing off, and withholding small kindnesses.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 106 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

قريش

Quraysh — The Bonding

The safe trade journeys Allah secured for Quraysh — food, security, and the one conclusion they demand: worship the Lord of this House alone.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 105 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الفيل

Al-Fīl — The Elephant

Abraha's army came to destroy the Kaʿbah. Allah sent birds carrying baked-clay stones. The army was destroyed — in the very year the Prophet ﷺ was born.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 104 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الهمزة

Al-Humazah — The Backbiter

Woe to the mocker and fault-finder who hoards wealth thinking it will make him live forever — and the fire that reaches into the very hearts where the disease lived.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 103 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

العصر

Al-ʿAṣr — By Time

All of mankind is in loss — except those with four qualities: faith, righteous deeds, exhorting to truth, exhorting to patience. Imam al-Shāfiʿī: "This sūrah alone would suffice."

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 102 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

التكاثر

Al-Takāthur — Competing for More

Competing for worldly gain distracts you — until you visit your graves. Then you will see the fire and be questioned about every bounty you enjoyed.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 101 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

القارعة

Al-Qāriʿah — The Striking Catastrophe

People like scattered moths, mountains like tufts of wool — then the scales that decide eternal fate: heavy deeds → bliss; light deeds → the abyss.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 100 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

العاديات

Al-ʿĀdiyāt — The Racing Horses

The five-fold oath by galloping horses — then the verdict: man is ungrateful, stingy, consumed by love of wealth. Until the graves overturn and what is hidden in hearts is exposed.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 99 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Madani

الزلزلة

Al-Zalzalah — The Earthquake

The earth convulses, casts out its dead, and testifies against people — then every atom's weight of good or evil is seen. Al-Saʿdī: the greatest encouragement for small deeds.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 98 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Madani

البيّنة

Al-Bayyinah — The Clear Proof

The clear proof came — a Messenger, pure pages, unerring texts. The two final verdicts: worst of people vs. best of people. Allah pleased with them and they pleased with Him.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 97 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

القدر

Al-Qadr — The Night of Decree

The Quran descended on this night. Better than a thousand months. Angels and Jibreel descend. Peace until dawn. Al-Saʿdī: "utterly astounding" — a lifetime of deeds in one night.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 96 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

العلق

Al-ʿAlaq — The Clinging Clot

The first five āyāt ever revealed — iqraʾ bi-smi Rabbik. Man created from a clinging clot; knowledge and the pen. Then the one who tries to prevent prayer — does he not know Allah sees?

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 95 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

التين

Al-Tīn — The Fig

Oath by the fig, olive, Ṭūr Sīnīn, and this secure city — man created in the finest form, then returned to the lowest of the low — except those who believe and do good.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 94 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الانشراح

Al-Inshirāḥ — The Expansion

Allah expanded the Prophet's chest, removed his burden, raised his mention — after hardship ease, twice. When free, strive. And to your Lord direct your longing.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 93 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الضحى

Al-Ḍuḥā — The Morning Brightness

Allah's reassurance to the Prophet ﷺ — He has not forsaken or displeased you. Three past gifts remembered; three commands that follow: do not oppress the orphan, do not reject the asker, and proclaim the bounty of your Lord.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 92 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الليل

Al-Layl — The Night

Two paths, two outcomes — the generous believer who gives and fears: the path is made easy; the miser who is self-sufficient and denies: the hard path. Allah will not be pleased with anyone who resists His guidance.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 91 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الشمس

Al-Shams — The Sun

Eleven oaths building to the verdict — whoever purifies the soul succeeds; whoever buries it fails. Thamūd's lesson: they hamstrung the she-camel and were utterly levelled. Allah did not fear the consequences.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 90 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

البلد

Al-Balad — The City

Oath by this city — man created for struggle. The two paths: the steep mountain pass of freeing a slave, feeding the orphan and the destitute; or being of those who denied Allah's signs. Two groups, two endings.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 89 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الفجر

Al-Fajr — The Dawn

Oaths by dawn and the ten nights; the destruction of ʿĀd, Thamūd, and Pharaoh. Man's two faces: arrogance in ease, despair in hardship. The satisfied soul called home — enter My Paradise.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 88 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الغاشية

Al-Ghāshiyah — The Overwhelming Event

The Overwhelming — two groups: humiliated, exhausted faces in Hell; serene, satisfied faces in the Garden with flowing streams and elevated couches. Signs of Allah's power in creation. The Prophet ﷺ's role.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 87 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الأعلى

Al-Aʿlā — The Most High

Glorify the name of your Lord Most High — six of His attributes. The Prophet ﷺ will not forget the revelation. Whoever purifies himself is successful. This was in the earlier scriptures of Ibrāhīm and Mūsā.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 86 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الطارق

Al-Ṭāriq — The Night-Comer

The piercing star — every soul has a guardian appointed over it. Man created from gushing fluid. On the Day of Judgment all hidden things are tested. The Quran is a decisive word, not amusement — Allah will repay their scheming.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 85 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

البروج

Al-Burūj — The Great Constellations

The people of the trench who burned the believers alive — unrepentant — faced the punishment of burning themselves. Those who believe and do righteous deeds: for them gardens beneath which rivers flow.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 84 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الانشقاق

Al-Inshiqāq — The Splitting Open

Heaven cleft, obedient to its Lord. Earth spread and emptied. Man toiling to his Lord. Book given in the right hand — easy reckoning; book given behind the back — calls for destruction. Stages of human life: from clot to old age.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 83 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

المطففين

Al-Muṭaffifīn — Those Who Cheat in Measure

Woe to the cheaters who demand full measure for themselves but short-change others. The scroll of the wicked (Sijjīn) vs. the scroll of the righteous (ʿIlliyyīn). On the Day of Judgment — who are the true laughers?

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 82 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

الانفطار

Al-Infiṭār — The Cleaving

Heaven cleft, stars dispersed, seas burst forth, graves overturned — every soul will know. What deceived you about your Most Generous Lord? The honourable scribes record even the heart. All command on that Day belongs to Allah.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 81 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

التكوير

Al-Takwīr — The Rolling Up

Twelve cosmic events as the Hour opens — sun rolled up, girl buried alive asked for what sin, scrolls unrolled. Then every soul knows what it presented. The revelation carried by noble Jibrīl. Human will is real — yet operates within Allah's will.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 80 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

عبس

ʿAbasa — He Frowned

He frowned and turned away — because the blind man came. Allah's rebuke: prioritise the one who comes striving and fearing. Signs in creation: water, split earth, grain. The Day of the Deafening Blast — a man flees his own family.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 79 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki

النازعات

Al-Nāziʿāt — Those Who Drag Forth

Oaths by the angels; Mūsā's mission to Firʿawn who said "I am your highest lord" — seized with punishment of both worlds. Signs in creation. The two paths at the Greatest Calamity: one who restrained his desires → Paradise.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 78 · Juzʾ ʿAmma opens here · Makki

النبأ

Al-Nabaʾ — The Great Tidings

The great tidings they disputed — eight signs of creation proving resurrection. Hell lies in wait (mirṣādā) for the transgressors. The God-fearing have gardens, grapes, an overflowing cup. The disbeliever will wish he were dust.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 77 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki

المرسلات

Al-Mursalāt — The Emissaries

Juzʾ 29 closes — a five-fold oath, then the refrain "woe that Day to the deniers" repeated ten times against every proof of the resurrection.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 76 · Juzʾ 29 · Madani

الإنسان

Al-Insān — Man (also al-Dahr)

Man created from a mixed drop to be tested — the righteous feed the poor, orphan, and captive "only for the countenance of Allah," and are rewarded with silk and springs.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 75 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki

القيامة

Al-Qiyāmah — The Resurrection

An oath by the self-reproaching soul; the vivid moment of death; radiant faces looking at their Lord vs contorted faces — and the closing proof: He who made you from a drop can raise you again.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 74 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki

المدثر

Al-Muddaththir — The Cloaked One

"Arise and warn" — the command that launched public daʿwah. The arrogant chief who mocked the Qurʾān, and the criminals of Saqar naming what put them there: no prayer, no feeding the poor.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 73 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki

المزمل

Al-Muzzammil — The Enwrapped One

The command to rise at night and recite the Qurʾān with tartīl — then Allah's mercy easing that very obligation in the sūrah's final āyah.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 72 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki

الجن

Al-Jinn — The Jinn

A group of jinn overhear the Qurʾān and declare tawḥīd in their own words — a rare window into their belief, and a warning against seeking refuge in them.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 71 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki

نوح

Nūḥ — Noah

Nūḥ's centuries of public and private daʿwah, his promise that istighfār brings rain and provision, and his final prayer against a people who chose five idols over their Lord.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 70 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki

المعارج

Al-Maʿārij — The Ascending Stairways

Man's anxious default nature — impatient in hardship, withholding in ease — contrasted with the qualities that lift the believer into honored gardens.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 69 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki

الحاقة

Al-Ḥāqqah — The Inevitable Reality

Thamūd, ʿĀd, Firʿawn, and the overturned cities destroyed for denying it — then the record given in the right hand vs the left, and the proof that the Qurʾān is not a poet's or soothsayer's word.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 68 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki

القلم

Al-Qalam — The Pen

"You are of a great moral character" — the Prophet ﷺ described, then the parable of the People of the Garden who forgot to say "in shāʾ Allāh" and forgot the poor.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 67 · Juzʾ 29 opens here · Makki

الملك

Al-Mulk — The Sovereignty

"Blessed is He in whose hand is dominion" — every proof of Allah's mastery over creation, from the birds in flight to the seven heavens. Known for its reported virtue of protection and intercession.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 66 · Juzʾ 28 · Madani

التحريم

Al-Taḥrīm — The Prohibition

Do not forbid the lawful; guard yourself and your family from the Fire; sincere repentance (tawbah naṣūḥ); and four women set as examples — two who perished near the righteous, two saved amid evil.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 65 · Juzʾ 28 · Madani

الطلاق

Al-Ṭalāq — The Divorce

The just conduct of divorce and the ʿiddah — and the great promise: whoever fears Allah, He makes for him a way out and provides from where he does not expect. Whoever relies on Allah, He suffices him.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 64 · Juzʾ 28 · Madani

التغابن

Al-Taghābun — Mutual Loss & Gain

The Day the true winners and losers of life are exposed. "Say: Yes, by my Lord, you will be resurrected." No calamity strikes but by Allah's leave — and whoever believes, He guides his heart.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 63 · Juzʾ 28 · Madani

المنافقون

Al-Munāfiqūn — The Hypocrites

The hypocrites exposed — false oaths as a shield, hollow like propped-up timber, "they are the enemy, so beware them." And the warning: let not wealth and children divert you from the remembrance of Allah.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 62 · Juzʾ 28 · Madani

الجمعة

Al-Jumuʿah — The Congregation

A Messenger raised among the unlettered to recite, purify, and teach; the parable of the donkey carrying books; and the command of the Friday prayer — hasten to Allah's remembrance and leave off trade.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 61 · Juzʾ 28 · Madani

الصف

Al-Ṣaff — The Ranks

Match your words with your deeds; Allah loves those who strive in ranks like a solid wall. ʿĪsā's glad tidings of a Messenger named Aḥmad, and the trade that saves from the painful punishment.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 60 · Juzʾ 28 · Madani

الممتحنة

Al-Mumtaḥanah — The Examined Woman

Loyalty and disavowal for Allah, and the example of Ibrāhīm — yet the balance of justice and kindness to peaceful non-Muslims: "Indeed, Allah loves those who act justly."

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 59 · Juzʾ 28 · Madani

الحشر

Al-Ḥashr — The Gathering

The exile of Banū al-Naḍīr and its lesson; "whatever the Messenger gives you, take it"; the self-sacrifice of the Anṣār; and the majestic closing āyāt of the Beautiful Names of Allah.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
Sūrah 58 · Juzʾ 28 opens here · Madani

المجادلة

Al-Mujādilah — The Pleading Woman

Allah heard the woman who pleaded about her husband; His knowledge encompasses every secret — "no counsel of three but He is the fourth"; He raises those given knowledge by degrees; and the party of Allah are the successful.

Tafsīr Vocab Quiz
