Sūrah 84 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 25 āyātYear 1

سورة الانشقاق

Sūrat al-Inshiqāq — The Splitting Asunder

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what happens to the heaven and earth on the Last Day, and what it means that they "hearken to their Lord."
  • Understand the two people: the one given their record in their right hand (easy reckoning, rejoicing family) and the one given it from behind their back (wishing for death, raging fire).
  • Know al-Saʿdī's explanation of "you will surely progress from stage to stage" — the stages of human life from drop to dust to resurrection.
  • Know why the disbeliever's delusion — "I will never return to my Lord" — is refuted by the words "His Lord was always watching him."
الآياتThe Āyāt

إِذَا السَّمَاءُ انشَقَّتْ ۝ وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْأَرْضُ مُدَّتْ ۝ وَأَلْقَتْ مَا فِيهَا وَتَخَلَّتْ ۝ وَأَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتْ ۝ يَا أَيُّهَا الْإِنسَانُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدْحًا فَمُلَاقِيهِ ۝ فَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِيَ كِتَابَهُ بِيَمِينِهِ ۝ فَسَوْفَ يُحَاسَبُ حِسَابًا يَسِيرًا ۝ وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰ أَهْلِهِ مَسْرُورًا ۝ وَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِيَ كِتَابَهُ وَرَاءَ ظَهْرِهِ ۝ فَسَوْفَ يَدْعُو ثُبُورًا ۝ وَيَصْلَىٰ سَعِيرًا ۝ إِنَّهُ كَانَ فِي أَهْلِهِ مَسْرُورًا ۝ إِنَّهُ ظَنَّ أَن لَّن يَحُورَ ۝ بَلَىٰ إِنَّ رَبَّهُ كَانَ بِهِ بَصِيرًا ۝ فَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِالشَّفَقِ ۝ وَاللَّيْلِ وَمَا وَسَقَ ۝ وَالْقَمَرِ إِذَا اتَّسَقَ ۝ لَتَرْكَبُنَّ طَبَقًا عَن طَبَقٍ ۝ فَمَا لَهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ ۝ وَإِذَا قُرِئَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْقُرْآنُ لَا يَسْجُدُونَ ۝ بَلِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا يُكَذِّبُونَ ۝ وَاللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ بِمَا يُوعُونَ ۝ فَبَشِّرْهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ ۝ إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ لَهُمْ أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ

When the heaven is rent asunder, and hearkens unto its Lord, as in truth it must — and when the earth is levelled, and casts forth all that it contains, and becomes empty, and hearkens unto its Lord, as in truth it must — then, O man who toils constantly until you meet your Lord, you will surely see the outcome of your deeds. As for the one who is given his record of deeds in his right hand, he will have an easy reckoning, and he will go back to his family rejoicing. As for the one who is given his record from behind his back, he will call out wishing for death, and he will enter the raging fire — he lived rejoicing among his family; indeed he thought that he would never return to his Lord. Nay, for His Lord was always watching him. Verily I swear by the twilight, and by the night and what it brings together, and by the moon when it is full — you will surely progress from stage to stage. Then what is the matter with them, that they do not believe, and when the Qur'an is recited to them they do not prostrate to Allah? Rather those who disbelieve insist on rejecting the Qur'an, but Allah knows best what they harbour in their hearts. So give them the tidings of a painful punishment — except those who believe and do righteous deeds: they will have a never-ending reward.

al-Inshiqāq 84:1–25

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Allah describes what will happen to the mighty structures of this world on the Last Day. The heaven — vast and towering above everything — will be rent asunder: it will split apart, its stars will be scattered, and the sun and moon will cease to shine. It will hearken to its Lord, obeying His command immediately, for it is subjugated to a Sovereign Whose command is not opposed. And in truth it must — this is its due state of submission, as all of creation belongs to Him.

يَا أَيُّهَا الْإِنسَانُ — O Man Who Toils

The earth will be spread out flat like a leather mat — mountains scattered, structures tumbled — until it is wide and spacious enough to accommodate all who will stand before Allah. It will cast forth what it contains of the dead and of buried treasures. Then the call: O man, you are toiling constantly until you meet your Lord — whether in good or evil deeds, you are heading toward that meeting. When you arrive, Allah will requite you: by grace if you are among the blessed, by justice if you are among the doomed.

The Two People — Record in the Right vs. from Behind the Back: The one given their record in their right hand will have an easy reckoning — al-Saʿdī explains this as an easy discussion before Allah; Allah will make him acknowledge his sins, until when he thinks he is doomed, Allah will say: "I concealed them for you in the previous world, and I will conceal them for you today" (Bukhārī). He will return to his family in paradise rejoicing. As for the one given his record from behind his back — in his left hand, from behind — he will call out wishing for death because of disgrace. The fire will surround him on all sides. Why? Because in this world he lived rejoicing among his family — carefree, never reflecting — and he thought he would never return to his Lord. But His Lord was always watching him — therefore divine wisdom dictates that He must resurrect him and requite him.

طَبَقًا عَن طَبَقٍ — Stage to Stage: Allah swears by the twilight (the remnant of sunlight at the onset of night), by the night and all it gathers together, and by the full moon at its most beautiful — all signs of His precise, unceasing management of creation. What He attests to: you will surely pass through stage after stage — from a drop of semen, to a clinging clot, to a lump of flesh, to the breathing of a soul, to infancy, to childhood, to the age of discernment and accountability, then death, then resurrection. All of these stages show that Allah alone controls man and that man is entirely dependent and helpless.

Despite all this evidence, many people refuse to believe. When the Qur'an is recited to them, they do not prostrate. They stubbornly reject. But among humankind are those Allah guided — they believe and do righteous deeds. For them: أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ — a never-ending reward, eternal, one that no eye has seen, no ear has heard, nor has it crossed the mind of man.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

يَا أَيُّهَا الْإِنسَانُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدْحًا فَمُلَاقِيهِ

O man, you are toiling toward your Lord, and you will meet Him. Every day of effort — every prayer, every act of patience, every moment of struggle — is moving you toward that meeting. This āyah was recited by ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib (may Allah be pleased with him) when he was stabbed: he said "By the Lord of the Kaʿbah, I have succeeded." The meeting with Allah is what the believers prepare for.
