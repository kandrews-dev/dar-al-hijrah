سورة النبأ

Sūrat al-Nabaʾ — The Great Tidings

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what النَّبَأُ الْعَظِيمُ (the great tidings) refers to and why al-Saʿdī says the people disputed it — the resurrection and recompense after death.
  • Know the eight signs of Allah's power in creation listed in this sūrah and understand how each one is a proof that He is able to resurrect the dead.
  • Know the punishment of the transgressors in Hell described in this sūrah: its duration, its heat, and جَزَاءً وِفَاقًا — a perfectly fitting recompense.
  • Know the reward of the مُتَّقِين in Paradise and the impact of the closing āyah: the disbeliever will wish he were dust.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Great Tidings and the Signs of Creation (1–16)

عَمَّ يَتَسَاءَلُونَ ۝ عَنِ النَّبَإِ الْعَظِيمِ ۝ الَّذِي هُمْ فِيهِ مُخْتَلِفُونَ ۝ كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ ۝ ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ ۝ أَلَمْ نَجْعَلِ الْأَرْضَ مِهَادًا ۝ وَالْجِبَالَ أَوْتَادًا ۝ وَخَلَقْنَاكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا ۝ وَجَعَلْنَا نَوْمَكُمْ سُبَاتًا ۝ وَجَعَلْنَا اللَّيْلَ لِبَاسًا ۝ وَجَعَلْنَا النَّهَارَ مَعَاشًا ۝ وَبَنَيْنَا فَوْقَكُمْ سَبْعًا شِدَادًا ۝ وَجَعَلْنَا سِرَاجًا وَهَّاجًا ۝ وَأَنزَلْنَا مِنَ الْمُعْصِرَاتِ مَاءً ثَجَّاجًا ۝ لِنُخْرِجَ بِهِ حَبًّا وَنَبَاتًا ۝ وَجَنَّاتٍ أَلْفَافًا

About what are they asking one another? About the great tidings — the one they disagree about. No! They will come to know. Then no! They will come to know. Did We not make the earth a resting place? And the mountains as pegs? And We created you in pairs, and made your sleep a rest, and made the night as a covering, and made the day for livelihood. And We built above you seven firm heavens, and placed a blazing lamp, and sent down from the rain-laden clouds abundant water — that We might bring forth thereby grain and vegetation, and dense intertwining gardens.

al-Nabaʾ 78:1–16

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Day of Sorting and Hell (17–30)

إِنَّ يَوْمَ الْفَصْلِ كَانَ مِيقَاتًا ۝ يَوْمَ يُنفَخُ فِي الصُّورِ فَتَأْتُونَ أَفْوَاجًا ۝ وَفُتِحَتِ السَّمَاءُ فَكَانَتْ أَبْوَابًا ۝ وَسُيِّرَتِ الْجِبَالُ فَكَانَتْ سَرَابًا ۝ إِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ كَانَتْ مِرْصَادًا ۝ لِلطَّاغِينَ مَآبًا ۝ لَّابِثِينَ فِيهَا أَحْقَابًا ۝ لَّا يَذُوقُونَ فِيهَا بَرْدًا وَلَا شَرَابًا ۝ إِلَّا حَمِيمًا وَغَسَّاقًا ۝ جَزَاءً وِفَاقًا ۝ إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا لَا يَرْجُونَ حِسَابًا ۝ وَكَذَّبُوا بِآيَاتِنَا كِذَّابًا ۝ وَكُلَّ شَيْءٍ أَحْصَيْنَاهُ كِتَابًا ۝ فَذُوقُوا فَلَن نَّزِيدَكُمْ إِلَّا عَذَابًا

Indeed the Day of Sorting has been set as an appointed time — the Day the Trumpet is blown and you come in crowds, and the heaven is opened and becomes gateways, and the mountains are set in motion and become a mirage. Indeed Hell has been lying in ambush — a home for the transgressors, to remain in it for ages and ages, tasting therein neither coolness nor any drink, except boiling water and putrid fluid — a perfectly fitting recompense. For they had no expectation of a reckoning, and they utterly denied Our signs — and We have recorded everything in a book. So taste — We will add nothing to you but punishment.

al-Nabaʾ 78:17–30

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Reward of the Righteous and the Closing (31–40)

إِنَّ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ مَفَازًا ۝ حَدَائِقَ وَأَعْنَابًا ۝ وَكَوَاعِبَ أَتْرَابًا ۝ وَكَأْسًا دِهَاقًا ۝ لَّا يَسْمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغْوًا وَلَا كِذَّابًا ۝ جَزَاءً مِّن رَّبِّكَ عَطَاءً حِسَابًا ۝ رَبِّ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا الرَّحْمَنِ لَا يَمْلِكُونَ مِنْهُ خِطَابًا ۝ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الرُّوحُ وَالْمَلَائِكَةُ صَفًّا لَّا يَتَكَلَّمُونَ إِلَّا مَنْ أَذِنَ لَهُ الرَّحْمَنُ وَقَالَ صَوَابًا ۝ ذَلِكَ الْيَوْمُ الْحَقُّ فَمَن شَاءَ اتَّخَذَ إِلَى رَبِّهِ مَآبًا ۝ إِنَّا أَنذَرْنَاكُمْ عَذَابًا قَرِيبًا يَوْمَ يَنظُرُ الْمَرْءُ مَا قَدَّمَتْ يَدَاهُ وَيَقُولُ الْكَافِرُ يَا لَيْتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَابًا

For the God-fearing there is a great triumph — gardens and grapevines, and companions of equal age, and an overflowing cup. They will hear therein neither idle talk nor falsehood — a recompense from your Lord, a generous and sufficient gift. Lord of the heavens and earth and all that is between them, the Most Merciful — they have no power to address Him. On the Day when the Spirit and the angels stand in rows — none will speak except the one whom the Most Merciful has permitted, and who speaks rightly. That is the True Day — so let whoever wills take a return path to his Lord. We have warned you of a punishment that is close — the Day when a man will look at what his hands have sent forward, and the disbeliever will say: Would that I were dust!

al-Nabaʾ 78:31–40

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens with a challenge: about what are they asking one another? About النَّبَأُ الْعَظِيمُ — the great tidings. Al-Saʿdī: this is the resurrection and recompense after death — the news that Quraysh debated and disputed. Some denied it entirely; some doubted; some acknowledged it in theory but lived as if it weren't real. Allah's response is the double threat: كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ · ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ — nay, they will come to know; then again, nay, they will come to know. The repetition is for emphasis: the knowledge will come, whether you believed or denied.

ثمانية دلائل — Eight Proofs of Allah's Power to Resurrect

Before describing the Day of Judgment, Allah marshals eight proofs from the creation humans live within: (1) the earth made as a resting place (مِهَادًا — a cradle, a spread-out bed); (2) mountains as pegs that stabilize it; (3) humans created in pairs — male and female; (4) sleep as a death-like rest that He lifts every morning; (5) night as a covering that wraps everything in darkness; (6) day for seeking livelihood; (7) seven firm heavens built above; (8) the blazing sun, the rain-clouds pouring down, bringing forth grain, vegetation, and interlocking gardens. Al-Saʿdī: He who runs all of this, gives life and death daily, and sustains creation — is He incapable of one more resurrection? The argument is unanswerable.

إِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ كَانَتْ مِرْصَادًا — Hell Lies in Ambush: Al-Saʿdī: مِرْصَاد means a place of lying in wait — as an ambush waits for the enemy to pass. Hell waits for every transgressor, watching them with patience through this short worldly life, knowing they must pass through it to their final destination. لَابِثِينَ فِيهَا أَحْقَابًا — remaining in it for ages upon ages: the durations stretch endlessly. No coolness, no drink except boiling water that scalds the insides and putrid fluid — the leaking of wounds. Then comes the most chilling description: جَزَاءً وِفَاقًا — a perfectly fitting recompense. Not more than they deserve; exactly proportional to what they did. Their disbelief was total, their denial was deliberate, their rejection of the āyāt was complete — and the punishment fits perfectly.

إِنَّ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ مَفَازًا — For the God-Fearing a Great Triumph: The contrast is abrupt and beautiful. Gardens, grapevines, companions of equal age, a cup that never empties, and — perhaps the most meaningful gift — لَا يَسْمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغْوًا وَلَا كِذَّابًا — no idle speech, no falsehood. Purity of environment after the pollution of this world. The gift is from Allah with full measure: a recompense from your Lord, a generous and sufficient gift. Al-Saʿdī on the closing āyah: it is the greatest shock for the disbeliever — standing before their Lord, seeing every deed their hands sent forward, and saying: يَا لَيْتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَابًا — would that I had been dust! The animal was dust on that Day and never faced reckoning. In that moment, the disbeliever envies the very earth beneath his feet.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

إِنَّا أَنذَرْنَاكُمْ عَذَابًا قَرِيبًا ۝ يَوْمَ يَنظُرُ الْمَرْءُ مَا قَدَّمَتْ يَدَاهُ ۝ وَيَقُولُ الْكَافِرُ يَا لَيْتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَابًا

Three āyāt that collapse the distance between now and that Day. We warned you of a punishment that is close — not distant, close. The Day when a man looks at what his hands have sent forward — everything: the prayer that was skipped, the anger that was indulged, the money that was spent, the time that was wasted. And the disbeliever will say: would that I had been dust. He will envy the very ground beneath him. The time to act on this is before that wish becomes the only thought left.
