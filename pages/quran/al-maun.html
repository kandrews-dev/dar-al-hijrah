Sūrah 107 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 7 āyāt

سورة الماعون

Sūrat al-Māʿūn — Small Kindnesses

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand who the one who يكذب بالدين (denies the Day of Judgement) is — and how their denial shows in their treatment of people.
  • Learn the three characteristics Allah describes: cruelty to orphans, neglect of the needy, and heedless / ostentatious prayer.
  • Understand what الماعون means and why withholding it is a sign of deep stinginess.
  • Connect iman to action: belief in the Last Day is inseparable from mercy and generosity.

This sūrah asks one of the most important diagnostic questions in the Qur'an: do you actually believe in the Day of Recompense? Because if you do, it will show — in how you treat orphans, the poor, and your neighbour who needs to borrow a bucket.

الآيات The Āyāt — سورة الماعون

أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ ۝ فَذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِى يَدُعُّ ٱلْيَتِيمَ ۝ وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلْمِسْكِينِ ۝ فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ ۝ ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ ۝ ٱلَّذِينَ هُمْ يُرَآءُونَ ۝ وَيَمْنَعُونَ ٱلْمَاعُونَ

Have you seen the one who denies the Day of Recompense? That is the one who drives away the orphan harshly, and does not urge others to feed the needy. So woe to those who pray — those who are heedless regarding their prayer, those who make a show of piety, yet withhold small kindnesses.

al-Māʿūn 107:1–7

التفسير Explanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

أَرَأَيْتَ الَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّينِ — "Have you seen the one who denies the Day of Recompense?" الدين here means the Day of Judgement and the recompense that Allah will give to every soul for its deeds. Al-Saʿdī says: this person does not truly believe that there will be a reckoning, so nothing restrains them from hardheartedness. Then the sūrah immediately shows what that denial looks like in practice.

يَدُعُّ الْيَتِيمَ (drives away the orphan): not merely neglects him — يَدُعّ means to push away with harshness and violence. The orphan, who has no father to protect him, comes seeking kindness and is shoved aside. This is a sign of a dead heart: someone who does not hope for reward or fear punishment has no reason to be kind.

وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَى طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ — "and does not urge others to feed the needy." Al-Saʿdī notes: if he does not even encourage others to feed the poor, it is more certain still that he does not feed them himself. The Qur'an condemned this trait as one that earns Hellfire elsewhere too: «وَلَمْ نَكُ نُطْعِمُ الْمِسْكِينَ» — "we did not feed the needy" (al-Muddaththir 74:44).

فَوَيْلٌ لِلْمُصَلِّينَ — Woe to those who pray

Notice: the condemnation is not on those who don't pray — it is on those who do pray but in the wrong way. This is a more precise warning. Two characteristics define them:

1. السهو عن الصلاة (heedlessness regarding the prayer): al-Saʿdī clarifies this is not making a mistake in prayer — everyone makes mistakes. This is being negligent about it altogether: delaying it past its time, doing its movements carelessly, abandoning its inward reality (خشوع — khushūʿ). As for the sincere believer who makes a mistake, that is not the target of this condemnation.

2. الرياء (ostentation, showing off): they perform good deeds to be seen by people, not for Allah. Al-Saʿdī says this is one of the most dangerous diseases of the heart — it voids the act, and the Prophet ﷺ called الرياء "the minor shirk."

وَيَمْنَعُونَ الْمَاعُونَ (yet withhold small kindnesses): الماعون refers to small everyday items that people customarily lend or give freely — a bucket, a hammer, a cooking pot, a book. Al-Saʿdī says: because of their great stinginess, they withhold even these — so how much more would they withhold in anything larger? The final āyah lands with precision: this is the portrait of a person who has no love for others, no generosity, no gratitude.

الدرس لنا The Lesson for Us

This sūrah links الإيمان باليوم الآخر (belief in the Last Day) directly to how you treat the people around you. A person who truly believes that every deed will be weighed, that every orphan cried over will be answered for, that every person fed will earn reward — such a person cannot be hardhearted. True faith shows outward. This is why the Prophet ﷺ said: "He does not truly believe — he does not truly believe — he does not truly believe." They asked: who, O Messenger of Allah? He said: "The one whose neighbour is not safe from his harm."

Teach the children this connection: iman is not just between you and Allah in the prayer. It shows in whether you share your things, whether you help those who have nothing, whether you pray with your heart present or just going through the motions.

Memorize & Apply — احفظ وطبّق

أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ · فَذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِى يَدُعُّ ٱلْيَتِيمَ

Memorize this sūrah and recite it with the family. Then ask: does belief in the Last Day show in our home? Do we pray with our hearts, or just with our bodies? Do we share our things freely? Do we give even the small things willingly? This sūrah is a mirror.
