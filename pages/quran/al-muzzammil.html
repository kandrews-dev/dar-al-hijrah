سورة المزمل

Sūrat al-Muzzammil — The Enwrapped One

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the opening command to stand in night prayer and recite the Qurʾān with تَرْتِيل (tartīl) — slow, clear, measured recitation.
  • Understand why the night hours are described as firmer in impression and more upright in speech.
  • Know the meaning of تَبَتُّل (exclusive devotion to Allah) and وَكِيل (Disposer of Affairs) in āyāt 8–9.
  • Know how Allah instructs patience toward deniers and the warning drawn from Pharaoh's rejection of his messenger.
  • Understand how the final āyah eased the night-prayer obligation — a clear example of ease and mercy in the Sharīʿah.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Command to Stand in Night Prayer (1–9)

يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُزَّمِّلُ ۝ قُمِ اللَّيْلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا ۝ نِّصْفَهُ أَوِ انقُصْ مِنْهُ قَلِيلًا ۝ أَوْ زِدْ عَلَيْهِ وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا ۝ إِنَّا سَنُلْقِي عَلَيْكَ قَوْلًا ثَقِيلًا ۝ إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ اللَّيْلِ هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطْئًا وَأَقْوَمُ قِيلًا ۝ إِنَّ لَكَ فِي النَّهَارِ سَبْحًا طَوِيلًا ۝ وَاذْكُرِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ وَتَبَتَّلْ إِلَيْهِ تَبْتِيلًا ۝ رَّبُّ الْمَشْرِقِ وَالْمَغْرِبِ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ فَاتَّخِذْهُ وَكِيلًا

O you who wraps himself [in his garment] — stand [in prayer] the night, except a little: half of it, or subtract from it a little, or add to it, and recite the Qurʾān with measured, unhurried recitation. Indeed We will cast upon you a heavy word. Indeed the rising of the night is firmer in impression [heart and tongue in accord] and more upright in speech; indeed for you by day is a long, occupied schedule. So remember the name of your Lord and devote yourself to Him exclusively. Lord of the East and the West — there is no deity except Him — so take Him as Disposer of your affairs.

al-Muzzammil 73:1–9

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: Patience With Those Who Deny (10–14)

وَاصْبِرْ عَلَى مَا يَقُولُونَ وَاهْجُرْهُمْ هَجْرًا جَمِيلًا ۝ وَذَرْنِي وَالْمُكَذِّبِينَ أُولِي النَّعْمَةِ وَمَهِّلْهُمْ قَلِيلًا ۝ إِنَّ لَدَيْنَا أَنكَالًا وَجَحِيمًا ۝ وَطَعَامًا ذَا غُصَّةٍ وَعَذَابًا أَلِيمًا ۝ يَوْمَ تَرْجُفُ الْأَرْضُ وَالْجِبَالُ وَكَانَتِ الْجِبَالُ كَثِيبًا مَّهِيلًا

And be patient over what they say, and part from them in a gracious manner. And leave to Me those who deny — the possessors of ease [in life] — and grant them a little respite. Indeed with Us are shackles and a blazing fire, and food that chokes, and a painful punishment — [on] the Day the earth and the mountains will convulse, and the mountains will become a heap of pouring sand.

al-Muzzammil 73:10–14

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: Pharaoh's Warning and the Easing of the Night Prayer (15–20)

إِنَّا أَرْسَلْنَا إِلَيْكُمْ رَسُولًا شَاهِدًا عَلَيْكُمْ كَمَا أَرْسَلْنَا إِلَى فِرْعَوْنَ رَسُولًا ۝ فَعَصَى فِرْعَوْنُ الرَّسُولَ فَأَخَذْنَاهُ أَخْذًا وَبِيلًا ۝ فَكَيْفَ تَتَّقُونَ إِن كَفَرْتُمْ يَوْمًا يَجْعَلُ الْوِلْدَانَ شِيبًا ۝ السَّمَاءُ مُنفَطِرٌ بِهِ ۚ كَانَ وَعْدُهُ مَفْعُولًا ۝ إِنَّ هَذِهِ تَذْكِرَةٌ ۖ فَمَن شَاءَ اتَّخَذَ إِلَى رَبِّهِ سَبِيلًا ۝ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَعْلَمُ أَنَّكَ تَقُومُ أَدْنَى مِن ثُلُثَيِ اللَّيْلِ وَنِصْفَهُ وَثُلُثَهُ وَطَائِفَةٌ مِّنَ الَّذِينَ مَعَكَ ۚ وَاللَّهُ يُقَدِّرُ اللَّيْلَ وَالنَّهَارَ ۚ عَلِمَ أَن لَّن تُحْصُوهُ فَتَابَ عَلَيْكُمْ ۖ فَاقْرَءُوا مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ ۚ عَلِمَ أَن سَيَكُونُ مِنكُم مَّرْضَى ۙ وَآخَرُونَ يَضْرِبُونَ فِي الْأَرْضِ يَبْتَغُونَ مِن فَضْلِ اللَّهِ ۙ وَآخَرُونَ يُقَاتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ ۖ فَاقْرَءُوا مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنْهُ ۚ وَأَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَآتُوا الزَّكَاةَ وَأَقْرِضُوا اللَّهَ قَرْضًا حَسَنًا ۚ وَمَا تُقَدِّمُوا لِأَنفُسِكُم مِّنْ خَيْرٍ تَجِدُوهُ عِندَ اللَّهِ هُوَ خَيْرًا وَأَعْظَمَ أَجْرًا ۚ وَاسْتَغْفِرُوا اللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ

Indeed We have sent to you [O people] a Messenger as a witness upon you, just as We sent to Pharaoh a messenger. But Pharaoh disobeyed the messenger, so We seized him with a ruinous seizure. Then how will you guard yourselves, if you disbelieve, against a Day that will turn children grey-haired? The heaven will be split apart thereby; His promise is ever fulfilled. Indeed this is a reminder, so whoever wills may take a way to his Lord. Indeed your Lord knows that you stand [in prayer] almost two-thirds of the night, or half of it, or a third of it, and [so does] a group of those with you. And Allah determines the night and the day; He has known that you will not be able to keep count of it, so He has turned to you in mercy — so recite what is easy of the Qurʾān. He has known that among you will be sick people, and others traveling throughout the land seeking of Allah's bounty, and others fighting in the cause of Allah — so recite what is easy from it, and establish prayer, and give zakāh, and loan Allah a goodly loan. And whatever good you put forward for yourselves, you will find it with Allah, better and greater in reward. And seek the forgiveness of Allah; indeed Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.

al-Muzzammil 73:15–20

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Like the sūrah before it, al-Muzzammil opens by addressing the Prophet ﷺ as he was wrapped in his garment: يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُزَّمِّلُ. Al-Saʿdī: the command that follows — قُمِ اللَّيْلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا, stand the night in prayer except a little — was preparation. Before the heavy responsibility of prophethood and daʿwah could be carried, the heart needed the strength that only the night prayer builds. Alongside it comes وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا — recite the Qurʾān with تَرْتِيل, meaning slowly, clearly, letter by letter, reflecting on the meaning rather than rushing through the words. This is the correct manner of reciting the Qurʾān at all times, not only in the night prayer.

تشريع في حركة — Revelation and Legislation in Motion

This sūrah is a clear example of how the Sharīʿah moves toward ease. Al-Saʿdī: the opening āyāt place what is close to an obligation of night prayer upon the Prophet ﷺ and the earliest believers with him — standing roughly half the night, more or less. But the final āyah (20), revealed later, shows Allah's mercy in full: He states that He knows His servants cannot maintain such a precise measure night after night, فَتَابَ عَلَيْكُمْ — so He turned to them in mercy — and eased the command to simply فَاقْرَءُوا مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ, recite what is easy of the Qurʾān. What began as a demanding standard became the recommended, flexible practice of qiyām al-layl known today. This is one of the clearest Qurʾānic illustrations of the principle that the Sharīʿah does not burden a soul beyond what it can bear, and that Allah's rulings move toward ease for those who sincerely strive.

Why the Night Is Singled Out: Al-Saʿdī explains إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ اللَّيْلِ هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطْئًا وَأَقْوَمُ قِيلًا — the night hours are more effective for concordance of heart and tongue — because the distractions of the day have quieted, the heart is more present, and recitation and reflection are undivided. By contrast, daytime brings a سَبْحًا طَوِيلًا, a long and busy schedule of worldly affairs, work, and dealings with people. The command that follows — وَاذْكُرِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ وَتَبَتَّلْ إِلَيْهِ تَبْتِيلًا — to remember Allah's name and devote oneself to Him exclusively (تَبَتُّل), together with taking Him alone as وَكِيل, the One relied upon and trusted with all of one's affairs, roots the whole sūrah in tawḥīd: worship and reliance belong to Allah alone, the Lord of the East and the West.

Patience and the Warning of Pharaoh: The middle of the sūrah instructs the Prophet ﷺ to be patient with what the deniers said and to part from them gracefully, leaving their affair to Allah, while warning that punishment awaits those given ease and luxury in this world yet who reject the truth. Al-Saʿdī draws the comparison directly: just as Allah sent Pharaoh a messenger (Mūsā عليه السلام) and Pharaoh disobeyed him and was seized with a ruinous seizure, so too every nation that is sent a messenger and disobeys him faces the same pattern of warning followed by consequence. The Qurʾān itself, the sūrah reminds, is تَذْكِرَةٌ — a reminder — so whoever wills may take a path to his Lord through it.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُزَّمِّلُ ۝ قُمِ اللَّيْلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا ۝ نِّصْفَهُ أَوِ انقُصْ مِنْهُ قَلِيلًا ۝ أَوْ زِدْ عَلَيْهِ وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا

A short, practical set of four āyāt to hold onto. O you who wraps himself [in his garment] — an intimate address, calling the one at rest to rise. Stand the night, except a little; half of it, or subtract from it a little, or add to it — flexible, measured devotion, not rigid perfectionism. And recite the Qurʾān with measured, unhurried recitation — a standing reminder that how one recites matters as much as how much.
