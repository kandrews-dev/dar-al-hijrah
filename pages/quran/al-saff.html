سورة الصف

Sūrat al-Ṣaff — The Ranks

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand why it is greatly hateful to Allah to say what one does not do — the harmony required between word and deed.
  • Know the likeness Allah loves: those who strive in ranks like a solid structure (بُنْيَانٌ مَّرْصُوصٌ) — unity and discipline.
  • Know that ʿĪsā عليه السلام gave glad tidings of a Messenger to come after him whose name is Aḥmad.
  • Grasp the promise وَاللَّهُ مُتِمُّ نُورِهِ — Allah will complete His light however much the disbelievers hate it.
  • Understand the "profitable trade" (تِجَارَة) that saves from the painful punishment: faith and striving with wealth and self.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: Word and Deed; the Solid Rank (2–4)

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لِمَ تَقُولُونَ مَا لَا تَفْعَلُونَ ۝ كَبُرَ مَقْتًا عِندَ اللَّهِ أَن تَقُولُوا مَا لَا تَفْعَلُونَ ۝ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ الَّذِينَ يُقَاتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِهِ صَفًّا كَأَنَّهُم بُنْيَانٌ مَّرْصُوصٌ

O you who believe, why do you say what you do not do? Greatly hateful in the sight of Allah is that you say what you do not do. Indeed, Allah loves those who fight in His cause in a [single] rank, as though they were a [solid] structure joined firmly.

al-Ṣaff 61:2–4

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Glad Tidings of Aḥmad; Allah's Light (6, 8)

وَإِذْ قَالَ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ يَا بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ إِنِّي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ إِلَيْكُم مُّصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَيْنَ يَدَيَّ مِنَ التَّوْرَاةِ وَمُبَشِّرًا بِرَسُولٍ يَأْتِي مِن بَعْدِي اسْمُهُ أَحْمَدُ ۖ فَلَمَّا جَاءَهُم بِالْبَيِّنَاتِ قَالُوا هَٰذَا سِحْرٌ مُّبِينٌ ۝ يُرِيدُونَ لِيُطْفِئُوا نُورَ اللَّهِ بِأَفْوَاهِهِمْ وَاللَّهُ مُتِمُّ نُورِهِ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ الْكَافِرُونَ

And [mention] when ʿĪsā, the son of Maryam, said, "O Children of Israel, indeed I am the Messenger of Allah to you, confirming what came before me of the Torah and bringing good tidings of a Messenger to come after me whose name is Aḥmad." But when he came to them with clear evidences, they said, "This is obvious magic." They wish to extinguish the light of Allah with their mouths, but Allah will perfect His light, however much the disbelievers dislike it.

al-Ṣaff 61:6, 8

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Profitable Trade (10–13)

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا هَلْ أَدُلُّكُمْ عَلَىٰ تِجَارَةٍ تُنجِيكُم مِّنْ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ ۝ تُؤْمِنُونَ بِاللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ وَتُجَاهِدُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ بِأَمْوَالِكُمْ وَأَنفُسِكُمْ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ ۝ يَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ ذُنُوبَكُمْ وَيُدْخِلْكُمْ جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ وَمَسَاكِنَ طَيِّبَةً فِي جَنَّاتِ عَدْنٍ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ الْفَوْزُ الْعَظِيمُ ۝ وَأُخْرَىٰ تُحِبُّونَهَا ۖ نَصْرٌ مِّنَ اللَّهِ وَفَتْحٌ قَرِيبٌ ۗ وَبَشِّرِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ

O you who believe, shall I guide you to a transaction that will save you from a painful punishment? [It is that] you believe in Allah and His Messenger and strive in the cause of Allah with your wealth and your lives. That is better for you, if you only knew. He will forgive you your sins and admit you to gardens beneath which rivers flow and pleasant dwellings in gardens of perpetual residence. That is the great attainment. And [you will obtain] another [favour] that you love — victory from Allah and an imminent conquest; and give good tidings to the believers.

al-Ṣaff 61:10–13

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Al-Saʿdī explains that this sūrah begins with a searching question to the believers: لِمَ تَقُولُونَ مَا لَا تَفْعَلُونَ — why do you say what you do not do? To claim a virtue, promise a deed, or profess a commitment and then fail to live up to it is كَبُرَ مَقْتًا عِندَ اللَّهِ — an act that earns Allah's intense displeasure. Al-Saʿdī: the believer's word and action must agree; the tongue that outruns the deeds is a mark of weak faith and a step toward hypocrisy. This is why the āyah is read as a foundational lesson in sincerity: profess only what you are prepared to do, and then do it.

بُنْيَانٌ مَّرْصُوصٌ — Ranks Like a Solid Wall

The sūrah takes its name from āyah 4: Allah loves those who strive in His cause صَفًّا كَأَنَّهُم بُنْيَانٌ مَّرْصُوصٌ — in a rank so tight and unified they are like a single wall whose stones are cemented together with no gap. Al-Saʿdī draws the lesson beyond the battlefield: Allah loves order, unity, and discipline in His servants — hearts joined, ranks unbroken, each standing firm in his place and not abandoning his brother. A community that acts as one solid structure, matching its words with united action, is beloved to Allah; one that is scattered, boastful in speech and divided in deed, is not. The mention of the solid rank right after the rebuke of empty words is deliberate: true faith shows itself in disciplined action together, not slogans.

ʿĪsā's Glad Tidings and the Name Aḥmad: The sūrah then recalls that the coming of the Prophet ﷺ was foretold by the prophets before him. Al-Saʿdī notes the two roles ʿĪsā عليه السلام declares of himself in āyah 6: he confirmed the Torah that came before him, and he gave glad tidings of a Messenger to come after him اسْمُهُ أَحْمَدُ — whose name is Aḥmad (one of the names of the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ). Yet when that awaited Messenger came with clear proofs, the deniers called it "obvious magic." Their rejection changes nothing of Allah's plan: يُرِيدُونَ لِيُطْفِئُوا نُورَ اللَّهِ بِأَفْوَاهِهِمْ — they try to blow out Allah's light with their mouths, as absurd as a man trying to extinguish the sun with a breath — وَاللَّهُ مُتِمُّ نُورِهِ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ الْكَافِرُونَ: Allah will complete and perfect His light no matter how they hate it. The next āyah adds that He sent His Messenger to make the religion of truth prevail over all others.

The Trade That Never Loses: Al-Saʿdī points out the tender way Allah calls the believers to sacrifice — as a merchant offering the deal of a lifetime: هَلْ أَدُلُّكُمْ عَلَىٰ تِجَارَةٍ تُنجِيكُم مِّنْ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ — "Shall I show you a trade that saves you from a painful punishment?" The "capital" invested is faith in Allah and His Messenger and striving in His cause with wealth and self; the "profit" is beyond any market: forgiveness of sins, Gardens beneath which rivers flow, and goodly dwellings in جَنَّاتِ عَدْنٍ — the eternal Gardens of ʿAdn. And Allah adds a bonus the believers love in this world too: نَصْرٌ مِّنَ اللَّهِ وَفَتْحٌ قَرِيبٌ, His help and a near victory. The sūrah closes as it fits: كُونُوا أَنصَارَ اللَّهِ — be the helpers of Allah, as the disciples of ʿĪsā answered his call, "We are the helpers of Allah." Those who supported the truth were made victorious over their enemies.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

يُرِيدُونَ لِيُطْفِئُوا نُورَ اللَّهِ بِأَفْوَاهِهِمْ وَاللَّهُ مُتِمُّ نُورِهِ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ الْكَافِرُونَ

A promise that has held for fourteen centuries: They wish to extinguish the light of Allah with their mouths, but Allah will perfect His light, however much the disbelievers dislike it. The enemies of the truth are like people huffing at the sun — the light only grows. Pair it with āyah 4: be part of the solid rank that carries that light.
