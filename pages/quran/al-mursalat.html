سورة المرسلات

Sūrat al-Mursalāt — The Emissaries

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what الْمُرْسَلَاتِ (those sent forth) refers to and how the opening oaths establish that the Day of Judgment is a certainty.
  • Recognise the refrain وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ (woe that Day to the deniers) — repeated ten times through the sūrah — and understand its function as a structural warning after each argument.
  • Know the description of Hell's three-columned shadow of smoke that awaits the deniers, offering no coolness and no shelter.
  • Know the reward of the مُتَّقِين — shades, springs, and fruit of their choosing — and contrast it with the fate of the deniers.
  • Reflect on the closing āyah: after the Qurʾān, what other statement could anyone believe in instead?
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Oath and the Day of Decision (1–15)

وَالْمُرْسَلَاتِ عُرْفًا ۝ فَالْعَاصِفَاتِ عَصْفًا ۝ وَالنَّاشِرَاتِ نَشْرًا ۝ فَالْفَارِقَاتِ فَرْقًا ۝ فَالْمُلْقِيَاتِ ذِكْرًا ۝ عُذْرًا أَوْ نُذْرًا ۝ إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَوَاقِعٌ ۝ فَإِذَا النُّجُومُ طُمِسَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا السَّمَاءُ فُرِجَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْجِبَالُ نُسِفَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الرُّسُلُ أُقِّتَتْ ۝ لِأَيِّ يَوْمٍ أُجِّلَتْ ۝ لِيَوْمِ الْفَصْلِ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا يَوْمُ الْفَصْلِ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ

By those [winds or angels] sent forth in gusts, and the winds that blow violently, and those that spread [clouds] far and wide, and those that separate [truth from falsehood] decisively, and those that deliver a reminder — as justification or warning: indeed, what you are promised will surely occur. So when the stars are obliterated, and when the heaven is split open, and when the mountains are blown away, and when the messengers are given their appointed time — for what Day has it all been deferred? For the Day of Decision. And what can make you know what the Day of Decision is? Woe, that Day, to the deniers.

al-Mursalāt 77:1–15

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: Former Peoples, Creation, and the Shadow of Smoke (16–40)

أَلَمْ نُهْلِكِ الْأَوَّلِينَ ۝ ثُمَّ نُتْبِعُهُمُ الْآخِرِينَ ۝ كَذَلِكَ نَفْعَلُ بِالْمُجْرِمِينَ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ أَلَمْ نَخْلُقكُّم مِّن مَّاءٍ مَّهِينٍ ۝ فَجَعَلْنَاهُ فِي قَرَارٍ مَّكِينٍ ۝ إِلَى قَدَرٍ مَّعْلُومٍ ۝ فَقَدَرْنَا فَنِعْمَ الْقَادِرُونَ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ أَلَمْ نَجْعَلِ الْأَرْضَ كِفَاتًا ۝ أَحْيَاءً وَأَمْوَاتًا ۝ وَجَعَلْنَا فِيهَا رَوَاسِيَ شَامِخَاتٍ وَأَسْقَيْنَاكُم مَّاءً فُرَاتًا ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ انطَلِقُوا إِلَى مَا كُنتُم بِهِ تُكَذِّبُونَ ۝ انطَلِقُوا إِلَى ظِلٍّ ذِي ثَلَاثِ شُعَبٍ ۝ لَّا ظَلِيلٍ وَلَا يُغْنِي مِنَ اللَّهَبِ ۝ إِنَّهَا تَرْمِي بِشَرَرٍ كَالْقَصْرِ ۝ كَأَنَّهُ جِمَالَاتٌ صُفْرٌ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ هَذَا يَوْمُ لَا يَنطِقُونَ ۝ وَلَا يُؤْذَنُ لَهُمْ فَيَعْتَذِرُونَ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ هَذَا يَوْمُ الْفَصْلِ ۖ جَمَعْنَاكُمْ وَالْأَوَّلِينَ ۝ فَإِن كَانَ لَكُمْ كَيْدٌ فَكِيدُونِ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ

Did We not destroy the former peoples? Then We follow them with the later ones. Thus do We deal with the criminals. Woe, that Day, to the deniers. Did We not create you from a despised fluid, which We placed in a firm lodging for a known term? And We determined it — how excellent a determiner We are! Woe, that Day, to the deniers. Have We not made the earth a container, holding the living and the dead? And We placed therein firm, towering mountains and gave you sweet water to drink. Woe, that Day, to the deniers. [They will be told]: Proceed to that which you used to deny — proceed to a shadow of smoke having three columns, offering no cool shade and no shelter from the flame. It throws up sparks as huge as a fortress, as if they were yellowish camels. Woe, that Day, to the deniers. This is a Day they will not speak, nor will they be permitted to make excuse. Woe, that Day, to the deniers. This is the Day of Decision — We have assembled you and the former peoples. So if you have a plan, then plan against Me. Woe, that Day, to the deniers.

al-Mursalāt 77:16–40

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Reward of the Righteous and the Closing (41–50)

إِنَّ الْمُتَّقِينَ فِي ظِلَالٍ وَعُيُونٍ ۝ وَفَوَاكِهَ مِمَّا يَشْتَهُونَ ۝ كُلُوا وَاشْرَبُوا هَنِيئًا بِمَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ ۝ إِنَّا كَذَلِكَ نَجْزِي الْمُحْسِنِينَ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ كُلُوا وَتَمَتَّعُوا قَلِيلًا إِنَّكُم مُّجْرِمُونَ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ارْكَعُوا لَا يَرْكَعُونَ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثٍ بَعْدَهُ يُؤْمِنُونَ

Indeed, the righteous will be amid shades and springs, and fruits from whatever they desire — [being told]: Eat and drink in satisfaction for what you used to do. Indeed, We thus reward the doers of good. Woe, that Day, to the deniers. [O disbelievers], eat and enjoy yourselves a little; indeed, you are criminals. Woe, that Day, to the deniers. And when it is said to them, "Bow [in prayer]," they do not bow. Woe, that Day, to the deniers. Then in what statement after this [the Qurʾān] will they believe?

al-Mursalāt 77:41–50

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens with a series of oaths — وَالْمُرْسَلَاتِ عُرْفًا — by those sent forth in succession. Al-Saʿdī and other commentators explain these opening descriptions (الْمُرْسَلَات, الْعَاصِفَات, النَّاشِرَات, الْفَارِقَات, الْمُلْقِيَات) as referring to the winds Allah sends in stages, and to the angels He sends with revelation — separating truth from falsehood and delivering the reminder to the messengers as justification for the obedient and warning for the heedless. Whichever is meant, the point of the oath is the same: إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَوَاقِعٌ — what you are promised will surely happen. The signs that follow — stars obliterated, heaven split, mountains blown away, messengers given their appointed time — describe the sheer scale of the Day of Decision (يَوْمُ الْفَصْلِ), the Day every dispute between truth and falsehood is settled once and for all.

وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ — A Refrain Repeated Ten Times

This sūrah is built on a striking literary structure: after almost every argument or sign Allah presents, He closes it with the same short, piercing refrain — وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ, "woe, that Day, to the deniers." It occurs ten times, dividing the sūrah into ten short units, each ending on the same warning. الْوَيْل is a word of severe threat and ruin, used throughout the Qurʾān as a strong exclamation of warning rather than a simple curse. Repeating it after each proof — the destruction of former nations, man's own creation, the earth's provisions, the shadow of Hell, the reward of the righteous, the command to bow — hammers home a single message: denial of these plain signs carries real consequence, and the warning applies specifically to those who deny (الْمُكَذِّبِين), not merely to sinners in general.

انطَلِقُوا إِلَى ظِلٍّ ذِي ثَلَاثِ شُعَبٍ — Proceed to a Shadow with Three Columns: Among the sūrah's most vivid images is the command given to the deniers on the Day of Decision: proceed to a shadow of smoke rising in three columns — yet it gives no cool shade and shields nothing from the flame. Its sparks fly as huge as fortresses, as if they were yellow camels moving in the distance. Al-Saʿdī notes the irony: a "shadow" ordinarily brings relief from heat, but this one is smoke from the Fire itself, offering the illusion of shelter with none of its comfort — a fitting end for those who denied the very Day they now stand upon.

إِنَّ الْمُتَّقِينَ فِي ظِلَالٍ وَعُيُونٍ — the Righteous Amid Shades and Springs: Immediately after describing that false, scorching shadow, the sūrah turns to true shade — the مُتَّقِين surrounded by real shade, flowing springs, and fruit of their own choosing, with the invitation: eat and drink in satisfaction for what you used to do. Al-Saʿdī: the reward is earned, tied directly to their deeds — إِنَّا كَذَلِكَ نَجْزِي الْمُحْسِنِينَ, thus do We reward the doers of good. The sūrah closes by returning to the deniers one final time: told to bow and refusing, then addressed with the piercing question that ends the sūrah — after the Qurʾān, what other speech could they possibly believe in instead?

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

إِنَّ الْمُتَّقِينَ فِي ظِلَالٍ وَعُيُونٍ ۝ وَفَوَاكِهَ مِمَّا يَشْتَهُونَ ۝ كُلُوا وَاشْرَبُوا هَنِيئًا بِمَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ

Three āyāt of pure hope after so many warnings. Indeed, the righteous will be amid shades and springs — true comfort, not the smoky illusion promised to the deniers. And fruits from whatever they desire — a reward tailored to what the heart wants. Eat and drink in satisfaction for what you used to do — a reminder that every act of obedience in this life is remembered and repaid in the next.
