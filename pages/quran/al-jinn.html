Sūrah 72 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki · 28 āyāt

سورة الجن

Sūrat al-Jinn — The Jinn

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the occasion of this sūrah — a group of jinn overheard the Qurʾān being recited, believed in it, and returned to warn their own people, affirming tawḥīd in their own words.
  • Understand the jinn's testimony against shirk: that Allah has no wife or son, and that seeking refuge in jinn only increases a person in burden and error.
  • Know why eavesdropping jinn are now met with burning flames, and how this connects to the guarding of revelation from the heavens.
  • Understand the principle "مَسَاجِدَ لِلَّهِ" — mosques and all worship belong exclusively to Allah, with no partner invoked alongside Him.
  • Know the ʿaqīdah point in āyāt 26–28: Allah alone knows the unseen (الغيب) and discloses none of it except to an approved messenger through angelic transmission — the basis for rejecting fortune-telling and soothsaying.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Jinn Hear the Qurʾān (1–9)

قُلْ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ أَنَّهُ اسْتَمَعَ نَفَرٌ مِّنَ الْجِنِّ فَقَالُوا إِنَّا سَمِعْنَا قُرْآنًا عَجَبًا ۝ يَهْدِي إِلَى الرُّشْدِ فَآمَنَّا بِهِ ۖ وَلَن نُّشْرِكَ بِرَبِّنَا أَحَدًا ۝ وَأَنَّهُ تَعَالَى جَدُّ رَبِّنَا مَا اتَّخَذَ صَاحِبَةً وَلَا وَلَدًا ۝ وَأَنَّهُ كَانَ يَقُولُ سَفِيهُنَا عَلَى اللَّهِ شَطَطًا ۝ وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّا أَن لَّن تَقُولَ الْإِنسُ وَالْجِنُّ عَلَى اللَّهِ كَذِبًا ۝ وَأَنَّهُ كَانَ رِجَالٌ مِّنَ الْإِنسِ يَعُوذُونَ بِرِجَالٍ مِّنَ الْجِنِّ فَزَادُوهُمْ رَهَقًا ۝ وَأَنَّهُمْ ظَنُّوا كَمَا ظَنَنتُمْ أَن لَّن يَبْعَثَ اللَّهُ أَحَدًا ۝ وَأَنَّا لَمَسْنَا السَّمَاءَ فَوَجَدْنَاهَا مُلِئَتْ حَرَسًا شَدِيدًا وَشُهُبًا ۝ وَأَنَّا كُنَّا نَقْعُدُ مِنْهَا مَقَاعِدَ لِلسَّمْعِ ۖ فَمَن يَسْتَمِعِ الْآنَ يَجِدْ لَهُ شِهَابًا رَّصَدًا

Say: it has been revealed to me that a group of the jinn listened [to the Qurʾān] and said: "Indeed we have heard a wondrous Qurʾān — it guides to right conduct, so we have believed in it, and we will never associate anyone with our Lord. And exalted is the majesty of our Lord — He has not taken a wife nor a son. And the foolish among us used to say about Allah an outrageous lie. And we had thought that mankind and the jinn would never speak a lie about Allah. And [it used to be that] men from mankind would seek refuge with men from the jinn, so they only increased them in burden. And they thought, as you [mankind] thought, that Allah would never resurrect anyone. And we sought [to reach] the heaven but found it filled with powerful guards and burning flames. And we used to sit there in positions for listening, but whoever listens now will find a burning flame lying in wait for him."

al-Jinn 72:1–9

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Jinn's Belief, Its Divisions, and Mosques for Allah Alone (10–19)

وَأَنَّا لَا نَدْرِي أَشَرٌّ أُرِيدَ بِمَن فِي الْأَرْضِ أَمْ أَرَادَ بِهِمْ رَبُّهُمْ رَشَدًا ۝ وَأَنَّا مِنَّا الصَّالِحُونَ وَمِنَّا دُونَ ذَلِكَ ۖ كُنَّا طَرَائِقَ قِدَدًا ۝ وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّا أَن لَّن نُّعْجِزَ اللَّهَ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَن نُّعْجِزَهُ هَرَبًا ۝ وَأَنَّا لَمَّا سَمِعْنَا الْهُدَى آمَنَّا بِهِ ۖ فَمَن يُؤْمِن بِرَبِّهِ فَلَا يَخَافُ بَخْسًا وَلَا رَهَقًا ۝ وَأَنَّا مِنَّا الْمُسْلِمُونَ وَمِنَّا الْقَاسِطُونَ ۖ فَمَنْ أَسْلَمَ فَأُولَئِكَ تَحَرَّوْا رَشَدًا ۝ وَأَمَّا الْقَاسِطُونَ فَكَانُوا لِجَهَنَّمَ حَطَبًا ۝ وَأَلَّوِ اسْتَقَامُوا عَلَى الطَّرِيقَةِ لَأَسْقَيْنَاهُم مَّاءً غَدَقًا ۝ لِّنَفْتِنَهُمْ فِيهِ ۚ وَمَن يُعْرِضْ عَن ذِكْرِ رَبِّهِ يَسْلُكْهُ عَذَابًا صَعَدًا ۝ وَأَنَّ الْمَسَاجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدْعُوا مَعَ اللَّهِ أَحَدًا ۝ وَأَنَّهُ لَمَّا قَامَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ يَدْعُوهُ كَادُوا يَكُونُونَ عَلَيْهِ لِبَدًا

"And we do not know whether evil is intended for those on earth, or whether their Lord intends for them a right course. And among us are the righteous, and among us are [others] not so — we were [of] divided ways. And we have become certain that we will never cause failure to Allah on earth, nor can we escape Him by fleeing. And when we heard the guidance, we believed in it — so whoever believes in his Lord will fear neither loss nor injustice. And among us are Muslims [in submission], and among us are the unjust — whoever has submitted has sought the right course, but as for the unjust, they will be firewood for Hell." And [it was revealed] that if they had remained straight on the way, We would have given them abundant water to test them therein — and whoever turns away from the remembrance of his Lord, He will put him into an ever-increasing punishment. And [it was revealed] that mosques belong to Allah, so do not invoke anyone along with Him. And when the servant of Allah stood to invoke Him, they almost pressed in on him in crowds.

al-Jinn 72:10–19

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Boundaries of the Unseen (20–28)

قُلْ إِنَّمَا أَدْعُو رَبِّي وَلَا أُشْرِكُ بِهِ أَحَدًا ۝ قُلْ إِنِّي لَا أَمْلِكُ لَكُمْ ضَرًّا وَلَا رَشَدًا ۝ قُلْ إِنِّي لَن يُجِيرَنِي مِنَ اللَّهِ أَحَدٌ وَلَنْ أَجِدَ مِن دُونِهِ مُلْتَحَدًا ۝ إِلَّا بَلَاغًا مِّنَ اللَّهِ وَرِسَالَاتِهِ ۚ وَمَن يَعْصِ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ فَإِنَّ لَهُ نَارَ جَهَنَّمَ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا ۝ حَتَّى إِذَا رَأَوْا مَا يُوعَدُونَ فَسَيَعْلَمُونَ مَنْ أَضْعَفُ نَاصِرًا وَأَقَلُّ عَدَدًا ۝ قُلْ إِنْ أَدْرِي أَقَرِيبٌ مَّا تُوعَدُونَ أَمْ يَجْعَلُ لَهُ رَبِّي أَمَدًا ۝ عَالِمُ الْغَيْبِ فَلَا يُظْهِرُ عَلَى غَيْبِهِ أَحَدًا ۝ إِلَّا مَنِ ارْتَضَى مِن رَّسُولٍ فَإِنَّهُ يَسْلُكُ مِن بَيْنِ يَدَيْهِ وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِ رَصَدًا ۝ لِّيَعْلَمَ أَن قَدْ أَبْلَغُوا رِسَالَاتِ رَبِّهِمْ وَأَحَاطَ بِمَا لَدَيْهِمْ وَأَحْصَى كُلَّ شَيْءٍ عَدَدًا

Say: I only invoke my Lord and do not associate anyone with Him. Say: indeed I do not possess for you [the power of] harm or right guidance. Say: indeed no one can protect me from Allah, and I will never find refuge other than in Him — [my task is] only conveying from Allah and His messages. And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger — for him is the fire of Hell, abiding therein forever. Until, when they see what they are promised, they will know who is weaker in helpers and fewer in number. Say: I do not know whether what you are promised is near, or whether my Lord will grant it a distant term. [He is] Knower of the unseen, and He does not disclose His unseen to anyone, except a messenger whom He has approved — for indeed He sends before him and behind him observers, so that He may know that they have conveyed the messages of their Lord; and He has encompassed whatever is with them, and has enumerated all things in number.

al-Jinn 72:20–28

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

This sūrah opens with a command to the Prophet ﷺ: قُلْ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ — say, it has been revealed to me — that a group of jinn heard him reciting the Qurʾān and were struck by it. Al-Saʿdī: they described it in their own words as قُرْآنًا عَجَبًا, a wondrous Qurʾān, one that guides to right conduct — and immediately their hearts responded with belief and a declaration of tawḥīd: وَلَن نُّشْرِكَ بِرَبِّنَا أَحَدًا, we will never associate anyone with our Lord. This is a rare and remarkable window into the jinn's own testimony — creatures usually unseen, here speaking plainly about Allah's oneness, affirming that He has taken no wife and no son, exactly as the Qurʾān teaches mankind.

توحيد من لسان الجن — Tawḥīd From the Jinn's Own Tongue

Al-Saʿdī draws attention to how much sound ʿaqīdah this small group of jinn articulates after hearing only a portion of the Qurʾān: Allah's oneness, His freedom from having a wife or son, the danger of lying about Allah, and the impossibility of escaping Him by fleeing on earth. Just as tellingly, they warn against a specific form of shirk — رِجَالٌ مِّنَ الْإِنسِ يَعُوذُونَ بِرِجَالٍ مِّنَ الْجِنِّ فَزَادُوهُمْ رَهَقًا, men from mankind who used to seek refuge with men from the jinn, which only increased them in burden and error. This is a direct warning against seeking help, protection, or knowledge from jinn — the very foundation of magic and sorcery — since even the jinn themselves testify that doing so brings harm, not benefit. The only protection is in Allah alone.

Why Eavesdropping Jinn Now Face Flames: The jinn recount that they used to try to reach the heavens and sit in stations to listen in, but found the heaven filled with powerful guards and burning flames, so that مَن يَسْتَمِعِ الْآنَ يَجِدْ لَهُ شِهَابًا رَّصَدًا — whoever listens now finds a burning flame lying in wait for him. Al-Saʿdī: this heightened guarding coincided with the sending of the Prophet ﷺ and the revelation of the Qurʾān, protecting the revelation from any corruption or interference and closing off a channel that had previously allowed jinn to eavesdrop on decrees and feed fragments of true information to soothsayers mixed with many lies. The tightened guard is itself evidence that something of great importance — prophethood being sealed with the Qurʾān — was taking place.

Mosques Belong to Allah: وَأَنَّ الْمَسَاجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدْعُوا مَعَ اللَّهِ أَحَدًا — mosques belong to Allah, so do not invoke anyone along with Him. Al-Saʿdī: while the āyah speaks literally of places of prostration, its meaning extends to every act of worship — prayer, supplication, sacrifice, vows — all of it belongs exclusively to Allah and must not be directed to anyone besides Him, not to a prophet, not to an angel, and not to a jinn. This principle sits at the very center of the sūrah's message.

The Boundaries of the Unseen (āyāt 26–28): The closing āyāt establish a foundational ʿaqīdah principle: عَالِمُ الْغَيْبِ فَلَا يُظْهِرُ عَلَى غَيْبِهِ أَحَدًا إِلَّا مَنِ ارْتَضَى مِن رَّسُولٍ — Allah is the Knower of the unseen, and He discloses His unseen to no one except a messenger whom He has approved, and even then only through angelic observers placed before and behind the messenger to guard the revelation and confirm it has been faithfully conveyed. Al-Saʿdī: this closes the door firmly on any claim to unmediated knowledge of the unseen — no fortune-teller, astrologer, or sorcerer has genuine access to it. Whatever fragments they claim came historically from jinn eavesdropping, mixed with fabrication, and that channel has been sealed. True knowledge of the unseen belongs to Allah alone, conveyed only through His approved messengers.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

قُلْ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ أَنَّهُ اسْتَمَعَ نَفَرٌ مِّنَ الْجِنِّ فَقَالُوا إِنَّا سَمِعْنَا قُرْآنًا عَجَبًا ۝ يَهْدِي إِلَى الرُّشْدِ فَآمَنَّا بِهِ ۖ وَلَن نُّشْرِكَ بِرَبِّنَا أَحَدًا

A powerful, often-recited opening. Say: it has been revealed to me that a group of jinn listened and said: "Indeed we have heard a wondrous Qurʾān — even creatures unseen to us recognized its truth immediately. It guides to right conduct, so we have believed in it, and we will never associate anyone with our Lord" — a declaration of tawḥīd, in the jinn's own words, that every believer can hold as a reminder of what the Qurʾān is meant to do to a sincere heart.
