Sūrah 91 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 15 āyāt

سورة الشمس

Sūrat al-Shams — The Sun

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know all seven oaths — what each one points to and why the soul (النفس) is the climax.
  • Understand the two outcomes: success through purification vs. failure through corruption of the soul.
  • Know what al-Saʿdī says about the soul's inbuilt awareness of good and evil.
  • Know the Thamūd story and the warning it delivers about collective sin.
الآياتThe Āyāt

وَٱلشَّمْسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا ۝ وَٱلْقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا ۝ وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا ۝ وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَىٰهَا ۝ وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا ۝ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا ۝ وَنَفْسٍ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا ۝ فَأَلْهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقْوَىٰهَا ۝ قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا ۝ وَقَدْ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا ۝ كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ بِطَغْوَىٰهَآ ۝ إِذِ ٱنۢبَعَثَ أَشْقَىٰهَا ۝ فَقَالَ لَهُمْ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ نَاقَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَسُقْيَٰهَا ۝ فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمْدَمَ عَلَيْهِمْ رَبُّهُم بِذَنۢبِهِمْ فَسَوَّىٰهَا ۝ وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقْبَٰهَا

By the sun and its light — and by the moon as it follows it — and by the day as it displays it — and by the night as it conceals it — and by the heaven and Him Who built it — and by the earth and Him Who spread it — and by the soul and Him Who fashioned it — and inspired in it its wickedness and its righteousness — he will indeed attain true success who purifies it — and he will indeed fail who corrupts it. Thamūd rejected their Messenger, for they were transgressors — when the most wicked among them went forth [to kill the she-camel]. The Messenger of Allah said to them: Hands off the she-camel of Allah and her drink! But they disbelieved him and hamstrung her. So their Lord unleashed against them, for their sin, a punishment that reached them all — and He feared not the consequences thereof.

al-Shams 91:1–15

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

الأقسام السبعة — The Seven Oaths

القسم What it points to
الشمس وضحاهاSun and its light — the benefits it brings
القمر إذا تلاهاMoon following it — its light follows the sun's light
النهار إذا جلّاهاDay displaying it — makes the face of the earth manifest
الليل إذا يغشاهاNight concealing it — shrouds the earth in darkness
السماء وما بناهاHeaven and the One Who built it — Allah swears by His own creative act
الأرض وما طحاهاEarth and the One Who spread it — spread for man's benefit
نفس وما سوّاهاSoul and the One Who fashioned it — the climax oath

Al-Saʿdī: darkness and light, sun and moon, follow one another in precise order serving human interests — the greatest indication that Allah alone is deserving of worship. Everything worshipped besides Him is false.

وَنَفْسٍ وَمَا سَوَّاهَا فَأَلْهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقْوَاهَا (By the soul and Him Who fashioned it — inspired in it its wickedness and its righteousness): Al-Saʿdī says the soul is one of the great signs of Allah deserving to be sworn by. It is extremely subtle and light; it moves and changes easily; it is subject to desiring, wanting, loving, and hating. Without the soul, the body is merely an image with nothing good in it. Allah fashioned the soul and inspired within it an inbuilt awareness — it can distinguish between righteousness and wickedness on its own. It knows guilt. It recognises wrong. This is a mercy of Allah.

النتيجتان — The Two Outcomes

قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن زَكَّاها

True Success

He purified and cleansed the soul of sins and faults — elevating it through obedience to Allah, beneficial knowledge, and righteous deeds

وَقَدْ خَابَ مَن دَسَّاها

Failure and Loss

He corrupted his noble soul — subjected it to impurity, allowed faults and sins to develop, failed to perfect it, indulged in what brings it shame

كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ بِطَغْوَاهَا (Thamūd rejected their Messenger — for they were transgressors): The Thamūd are given as a warning: their transgression and rejection of truth led to collective punishment. When the most wicked among them (Qudār ibn Sālif) went forth to hamstring the she-camel — acting on the group's decision — Prophet Ṣāliḥ ﷺ had warned them clearly: "Hands off the she-camel of Allah and her drink!" But they disbelieved and hamstrung her.

فَدَمْدَمَ عَلَيْهِمْ رَبُّهُمْ بِذَنْبِهِمْ فَسَوَّاهَا (So their Lord unleashed punishment that reached them all): Allah sent a blast from above and an earthquake from below. They all perished — no one called out or responded. The punishment was equal for all because the sin was collective — the tribe agreed to the act, so all bore responsibility even though one person carried it out. وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقْبَاهَا — He (Allah) feared not the consequences. Al-Saʿdī: how could the Subduer, Who controls all created beings, fear the consequences of a just punishment?

Memorize & Reflect — احفظ وتأمّل

قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا · وَقَدْ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا

Seven of the greatest things in creation are sworn by — the sun, moon, day, night, heaven, earth — and they all build up to this: the soul. All of education, all of tarbiyyah, all of this school's work is in service of these two āyāt. Either we are purifying the soul or we are corrupting it. Nothing else we do is more important than this.
