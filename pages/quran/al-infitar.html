Sūrah 82 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 19 āyātYear 1

سُورَةُ الانْفِطَار

Sūrat al-Infiṭār — The Cleaving

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Know the four events at the onset of the Day of Judgment and what they lead to — "then every soul will know what it has done and left undone."
  • Understand the rebuke مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ الْكَرِيمِ — what deceived you about your Most Generous Lord? — and al-Saʿdī's explanation of ingratitude despite creation.
  • Know who the كرام كاتبين (honourable scribes) are, what they record, and why their presence should make a person act with care.
  • Know al-Saʿdī's explanation of the closing: no soul can do anything for another; all command belongs to Allah alone.
الآياتThe Āyāt

إِذَا السَّمَاءُ انفَطَرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْكَوَاكِبُ انتَثَرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْقُبُورُ بُعْثِرَتْ ۝ عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ مَّا قَدَّمَتْ وَأَخَّرَتْ ۝ يَا أَيُّهَا الْإِنسَانُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ الْكَرِيمِ ۝ الَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّاكَ فَعَدَلَكَ ۝ فِي أَيِّ صُورَةٍ مَّا شَاءَ رَكَّبَكَ ۝ كَلَّا بَلْ تُكَذِّبُونَ بِالدِّينِ ۝ وَإِنَّ عَلَيْكُمْ لَحَافِظِينَ ۝ كِرَامًا كَاتِبِينَ ۝ يَعْلَمُونَ مَا تَفْعَلُونَ ۝ إِنَّ الْأَبْرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٍ ۝ وَإِنَّ الْفُجَّارَ لَفِي جَحِيمٍ ۝ يَصْلَوْنَهَا يَوْمَ الدِّينِ ۝ وَمَا هُمْ عَنْهَا بِغَائِبِينَ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا يَوْمُ الدِّينِ ۝ ثُمَّ مَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا يَوْمُ الدِّينِ ۝ يَوْمَ لَا تَمْلِكُ نَفْسٌ لِّنَفْسٍ شَيْئًا ۖ وَالْأَمْرُ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلَّهِ

When the heaven is cleft asunder, and when the stars are dispersed, and when the seas burst forth, and when the graves are overturned — then every soul will know what it has done and what it has left undone. O man, what has deceived you concerning your Most Generous Lord, Who created you, fashioned you and made you well-proportioned, assembling you in whatever form He willed? Nay, but you deny the Last Judgement — while indeed there are watchers appointed over you, honourable scribes, who know all that you do. Verily the righteous will be in bliss, and verily the wicked will be in the blazing fire, which they will enter on the Day of Judgement and will never leave. And how could you know what the Day of Judgement is? Again, how could you know what the Day of Judgement is? It is the day when no soul will have the power to do anything for another, and on that day all command will belong to Allah alone.

al-Infiṭār 82:1–19

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens with four cosmic events at the onset of the Hour: the heaven is torn apart; the stars leave their orbits and collide; the seas burst forth and become one sea; the graves are overturned and cast forth the dead. When all of this happens — when the reality of things is uncovered and what was hidden is disclosed — every soul will know what it has gained or lost. The wrongdoer will see his deeds are invalid, his scale is light, and his evil deeds are piled against him. The pious will attain great triumph and eternal bliss.

مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ الْكَرِيمِ — What Deceived You?

Allah rebukes the human who falls short and disobeys: O man, what has deceived you about your Most Generous Lord? Is it heedlessness of your duty? Thinking little of His punishment? Disbelieving in His requital? Allah then answers His own question by listing His gifts: He created you, fashioned you in the best shape, made you well-proportioned and balanced — assembled in whatever form He willed. Al-Saʿdī: praise Allah for not giving you the shape of a dog or a donkey. It is only ignorance, wrongdoing, stubbornness and foolishness that leads man to be ungrateful for these gifts.

كِرَامًا كَاتِبِينَ — Honourable Scribes: Despite all exhortation and reminder, man still persists in denying the requital. But Allah has appointed over him noble angels who record every word and deed — and they know all that he does. Al-Saʿdī specifies: this includes actions of the heart — beliefs, thoughts, and emotions — as well as physical actions. So what is appropriate on man's part is to honour these scribes and respect their presence, and to act with awareness that everything is being recorded.

The Day of Judgment — يَوْمُ الدِّينِ: Allah emphasises the enormity of this Day by asking twice: and how could you know what the Day of Judgment is? Again, how could you know? It is the Day when no soul will have the power to do anything for another — even a father for a son, even someone sincerely loved. Each person will be preoccupied with saving himself and will seek salvation for no one else. وَالْأَمْرُ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِلَّهِ — all command on that Day belongs to Allah alone — it is He Who will judge between all people and settle every score.

Memorize & Hold On — اِحْفَظْ وثبت

يَا أَيُّهَا الْإِنسَانُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ الْكَرِيمِ · الَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّاكَ فَعَدَلَكَ

These two āyāt are a mirror. Look at what you have: a body created in perfect proportion, faculties that function, a form assembled by Allah. Then ask: am I giving this Most Generous Lord what He deserves? What is deceiving me? Every time disobedience feels tempting, these āyāt are the answer — return to gratitude, return to awareness.
