سورة المدثر

Sūrat al-Muddaththir — The Cloaked One

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the occasion of this sūrah — among the very earliest revelations, traditionally understood as the beginning of the Prophet ﷺ's public mission of warning, shortly after the opening of Sūrat al-ʿAlaq.
  • Know the five foundational instructions in āyāt 2–7 (قُمْ فَأَنذِرْ, وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرْ, وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرْ, وَالرُّجْزَ فَاهْجُرْ, وَلَا تَمْنُنْ تَسْتَكْثِرُ, وَلِرَبِّكَ فَاصْبِرْ) as a mini curriculum for every caller to Islam.
  • Trace the psychological portrait of the arrogant disbeliever in āyāt 11–25 — a man given every worldly favor who still turned away from clear truth, and understand how pride blocks recognition of truth.
  • Know what Saqar is, why the number of its guardians was made a trial, and the four causes the criminals confess for their punishment.
  • Understand the closing principle: the Qurʾān is a reminder for whoever wills to take heed, and that guidance is only by Allah's will.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: Arise and Warn (1–10)

يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُدَّثِّرُ ۝ قُمْ فَأَنذِرْ ۝ وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرْ ۝ وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرْ ۝ وَالرُّجْزَ فَاهْجُرْ ۝ وَلَا تَمْنُن تَسْتَكْثِرُ ۝ وَلِرَبِّكَ فَاصْبِرْ ۝ فَإِذَا نُقِرَ فِي النَّاقُورِ ۝ فَذَلِكَ يَوْمَئِذٍ يَوْمٌ عَسِيرٌ ۝ عَلَى الْكَافِرِينَ غَيْرُ يَسِيرٍ

O you who covers himself [with a garment] — arise and warn, and your Lord magnify, and your clothing purify, and uncleanliness avoid, and do not confer favor seeking increase [in return], and for your Lord be patient. And when the trumpet is blown, that Day will be a difficult day — for the disbelievers, not easy.

al-Muddaththir 74:1–10

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Arrogant Disbeliever and Saqar (11–31)

ذَرْنِي وَمَنْ خَلَقْتُ وَحِيدًا ۝ وَجَعَلْتُ لَهُ مَالًا مَّمْدُودًا ۝ وَبَنِينَ شُهُودًا ۝ وَمَهَّدتُّ لَهُ تَمْهِيدًا ۝ ثُمَّ يَطْمَعُ أَنْ أَزِيدَ ۝ كَلَّا ۖ إِنَّهُ كَانَ لِآيَاتِنَا عَنِيدًا ۝ سَأُرْهِقُهُ صَعُودًا ۝ إِنَّهُ فَكَّرَ وَقَدَّرَ ۝ فَقُتِلَ كَيْفَ قَدَّرَ ۝ ثُمَّ قُتِلَ كَيْفَ قَدَّرَ ۝ ثُمَّ نَظَرَ ۝ ثُمَّ عَبَسَ وَبَسَرَ ۝ ثُمَّ أَدْبَرَ وَاسْتَكْبَرَ ۝ فَقَالَ إِنْ هَذَا إِلَّا سِحْرٌ يُؤْثَرُ ۝ إِنْ هَذَا إِلَّا قَوْلُ الْبَشَرِ ۝ سَأُصْلِيهِ سَقَرَ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا سَقَرُ ۝ لَا تُبْقِي وَلَا تَذَرُ ۝ لَوَّاحَةٌ لِّلْبَشَرِ ۝ عَلَيْهَا تِسْعَةَ عَشَرَ ۝ وَمَا جَعَلْنَا أَصْحَابَ النَّارِ إِلَّا مَلَائِكَةً ۙ وَمَا جَعَلْنَا عِدَّتَهُمْ إِلَّا فِتْنَةً لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا لِيَسْتَيْقِنَ الَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْكِتَابَ وَيَزْدَادَ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِيمَانًا ۙ وَلَا يَرْتَابَ الَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْكِتَابَ وَالْمُؤْمِنُونَ ۙ وَلِيَقُولَ الَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضٌ وَالْكَافِرُونَ مَاذَا أَرَادَ اللَّهُ بِهَذَا مَثَلًا ۚ كَذَلِكَ يُضِلُّ اللَّهُ مَن يَشَاءُ وَيَهْدِي مَن يَشَاءُ ۚ وَمَا يَعْلَمُ جُنُودَ رَبِّكَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۚ وَمَا هِيَ إِلَّا ذِكْرَى لِلْبَشَرِ

Leave Me with the one I created alone, and to whom I granted extensive wealth, and children present [with him], and spread [everything] before him, easing [his life] — then he desires that I should add more. No! Indeed, he has been obstinate toward Our verses. I will cover him with arduous torment. Indeed, he thought and deliberated — so may he be destroyed for how he deliberated; then again may he be destroyed for how he deliberated. Then he considered [again]; then he frowned and scowled; then he turned back and was arrogant, and said: "This is nothing but magic imitated [from others]; this is nothing but the word of a human being." I will drive him into Saqar. And what can make you know what Saqar is? It leaves nothing and spares nothing — blackening the skins — over it are nineteen [angels]. And We have not made the keepers of the Fire except angels, and We have not made their number except as a trial for those who disbelieve — that those given the Scripture will be convinced, and those who believed will increase in faith, and those given the Scripture and the believers will not doubt, and that those in whose hearts is disease, and the disbelievers, will say: "What did Allah intend by this as an example?" Thus does Allah leave astray whom He wills and guide whom He wills, and none knows the soldiers of your Lord but Him — and it is nothing but a reminder to humanity.

al-Muddaththir 74:11–31

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: An Oath, the Companions of the Right, and the Closing (32–56)

كَلَّا وَالْقَمَرِ ۝ وَاللَّيْلِ إِذْ أَدْبَرَ ۝ وَالصُّبْحِ إِذَا أَسْفَرَ ۝ إِنَّهَا لَإِحْدَى الْكُبَرِ ۝ نَذِيرًا لِّلْبَشَرِ ۝ لِمَن شَاءَ مِنكُمْ أَن يَتَقَدَّمَ أَوْ يَتَأَخَّرَ ۝ كُلُّ نَفْسٍ بِمَا كَسَبَتْ رَهِينَةٌ ۝ إِلَّا أَصْحَابَ الْيَمِينِ ۝ فِي جَنَّاتٍ يَتَسَاءَلُونَ ۝ عَنِ الْمُجْرِمِينَ ۝ مَا سَلَكَكُمْ فِي سَقَرَ ۝ قَالُوا لَمْ نَكُ مِنَ الْمُصَلِّينَ ۝ وَلَمْ نَكُ نُطْعِمُ الْمِسْكِينَ ۝ وَكُنَّا نَخُوضُ مَعَ الْخَائِضِينَ ۝ وَكُنَّا نُكَذِّبُ بِيَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ حَتَّى أَتَانَا الْيَقِينُ ۝ فَمَا تَنفَعُهُمْ شَفَاعَةُ الشَّافِعِينَ ۝ فَمَا لَهُمْ عَنِ التَّذْكِرَةِ مُعْرِضِينَ ۝ كَأَنَّهُمْ حُمُرٌ مُّسْتَنفِرَةٌ ۝ فَرَّتْ مِن قَسْوَرَةٍ ۝ بَلْ يُرِيدُ كُلُّ امْرِئٍ مِّنْهُمْ أَن يُؤْتَى صُحُفًا مُّنَشَّرَةً ۝ كَلَّا ۖ بَل لَّا يَخَافُونَ الْآخِرَةَ ۝ كَلَّا إِنَّهُ تَذْكِرَةٌ ۝ فَمَن شَاءَ ذَكَرَهُ ۝ وَمَا يَذْكُرُونَ إِلَّا أَن يَشَاءَ اللَّهُ ۚ هُوَ أَهْلُ التَّقْوَى وَأَهْلُ الْمَغْفِرَةِ

No! By the moon, and the night when it departs, and the morning when it brightens — indeed it is one of the greatest [afflictions], a warning to humanity, to whoever among you wills to advance or lag behind. Every soul, for what it has earned, is retained — except the companions of the right, [who will be] in gardens, asking one another about the criminals: "What put you into Saqar?" They will say: "We were not of those who prayed, nor did we feed the poor, and we used to indulge in vain talk with those who indulged, and we used to deny the Day of Recompense, until certainty [death] came to us." So the intercession of intercessors will not benefit them. Then what is with them that they turn away from the reminder, as if they were alarmed donkeys fleeing from a lion? Rather every person among them desires to be given scriptures spread open [personally to him]. No! But they do not fear the Hereafter. No! Indeed it is a reminder — so whoever wills may remember it. And they will not remember except that Allah wills; He is worthy to be feared and worthy to forgive.

al-Muddaththir 74:32–56

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

This sūrah is well known as among the earliest pieces of revelation, coming shortly after the opening āyāt of Sūrat al-ʿAlaq. Where al-ʿAlaq established that the Prophet ﷺ was a recipient of revelation, al-Muddaththir launches him into his mission: يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُدَّثِّرُ — O you who covers himself. Al-Saʿdī: the Prophet ﷺ had wrapped himself in a garment, and the command comes at once — قُمْ فَأَنذِرْ — arise and warn. No more resting in privacy; the warner must stand and deliver the message.

منهج الدعوة — A Curriculum for the Caller (Āyāt 2–7)

Al-Saʿdī draws out six instructions packed into these short āyāt, forming a complete methodology for anyone who calls others to Allah: (1) قُمْ فَأَنذِرْ — arise and warn, meaning take action, do not merely intend; (2) وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرْ — magnify your Lord, meaning hold tawḥīd and Allah's greatness firmly in the heart before ever addressing others; (3) وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرْ — purify your garments, understood both literally (outward cleanliness) and by extension one's conduct and intentions; (4) وَالرُّجْزَ فَاهْجُرْ — avoid al-rujz, the filth of idols and disobedience; (5) وَلَا تَمْنُنْ تَسْتَكْثِرُ — do not give expecting a greater return, meaning sincerity — do not do good deeds or convey the message seeking worldly gain; (6) وَلِرَبِّكَ فَاصْبِرْ — be patient for your Lord's sake, because the caller will certainly be opposed. Every daʿwah effort still rests on these six pillars today.

The Man Who Had Everything Yet Turned Away (āyāt 11–25): This passage is traditionally understood to describe a specific proud chief of Makkah — commonly identified as al-Walīd ibn al-Mughīrah, a wealthy and influential leader of Quraysh. Allah gave him wealth stretched out before him and sons present at his side, made his life easy in every way — and still ثُمَّ يَطْمَعُ أَنْ أَزِيدَ, he craved yet more, and remained عَنِيدًا, obstinate against the Qurʾān. Al-Saʿdī highlights the sequence of verbs that follows as a case study in how pride operates on a heart that already recognizes the truth: فَكَّرَ وَقَدَّرَ — he thought and calculated [how to dismiss the Qurʾān]; ثُمَّ نَظَرَ — then he looked again; ثُمَّ عَبَسَ وَبَسَرَ — then he frowned and scowled; ثُمَّ أَدْبَرَ وَاسْتَكْبَرَ — then he turned his back and was arrogant. Each verb shows a man wrestling with a truth he could not honestly refute, choosing instead the false verdict that it was mere "magic" and "the word of a human." The Qurʾān answers this self-destructive calculation with a curse repeated twice: فَقُتِلَ كَيْفَ قَدَّرَ ۝ ثُمَّ قُتِلَ كَيْفَ قَدَّرَ — may he be destroyed for how he plotted.

Saqar and Its Nineteen Guardians: Saqar is one of the names of the Hellfire, described here as sparing nothing and leaving nothing unburned, blackening the skin of whoever enters it. Al-Saʿdī: Allah then explains that the guardians over it are angels, and that the specific number nineteen was made a trial — the disbelievers of Makkah mocked the number as strange, while it only ever increases the certainty of the People of the Book and the faith of the believers. This shows that even a small, apparently obscure detail of revelation is placed there by divine wisdom, and people's reactions to it reveal what is already in their hearts: sound hearts increase in faith, diseased hearts increase in doubt and mockery.

The Confession of the Criminals in Saqar (āyāt 38–47): The companions of the right, safe in the gardens, will ask the criminals what put them into Saqar, and the answer names four causes: they did not pray, they did not feed the poor, they indulged in vain talk, and they denied the Day of Recompense — and they persisted in this until death arrived with certainty. Al-Saʿdī: this shows that neglecting the rights of Allah (prayer) and the rights of His creation (feeding the needy), together with idle speech and denial of the Hereafter, are enough on their own to bring a person to ruin. The sūrah closes by contrasting those who flee guidance like startled donkeys fleeing a lion with the simple truth that the Qurʾān is a reminder open to whoever wills to take heed — but no one takes heed except that Allah wills it, for He alone is worthy of being feared and He alone is worthy to forgive.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

قَالُوا لَمْ نَكُ مِنَ الْمُصَلِّينَ ۝ وَلَمْ نَكُ نُطْعِمُ الْمِسْكِينَ ۝ وَكُنَّا نَخُوضُ مَعَ الْخَائِضِينَ ۝ وَكُنَّا نُكَذِّبُ بِيَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ حَتَّى أَتَانَا الْيَقِينُ ۝ فَمَا تَنفَعُهُمْ شَفَاعَةُ الشَّافِعِينَ

The criminals' own confession in Saqar, naming four causes: we were not of those who prayed — the neglected right of Allah; nor did we feed the poor — the neglected right of creation; we used to indulge in vain talk with those who indulged — wasted speech and time; and we used to deny the Day of Recompense, until certainty came to us — disbelief held onto until death. A short, practical checklist against which to examine one's own life today, while there is still time to change it.
