سورة الحاقة

Sūrat al-Ḥāqqah — The Inevitable Reality

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what الْحَاقَّة (the Inevitable Reality) refers to, and which nations — Thamūd, ʿĀd, Firʿawn, and the overturned cities — are mentioned as destroyed for denying it.
  • Know the description of the Day the Trumpet is blown: the earth and mountains crushed, the sky split, and eight angels bearing the Throne.
  • Know the two outcomes of receiving one's record in the right hand versus the left hand, and what each person says.
  • Understand the pairing in āyah 34 of not believing in Allah with not encouraging the feeding of the poor — how tawḥīd and social compassion are connected.
  • Know the logical proof in āyāt 44–47 that the Qurʾān could not have been fabricated by the Prophet ﷺ and attributed to Allah.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Inevitable Reality and the Destroyed Nations (1–12)

الْحَاقَّةُ ۝ مَا الْحَاقَّةُ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الْحَاقَّةُ ۝ كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ وَعَادٌ بِالْقَارِعَةِ ۝ فَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَأُهْلِكُوا بِالطَّاغِيَةِ ۝ وَأَمَّا عَادٌ فَأُهْلِكُوا بِرِيحٍ صَرْصَرٍ عَاتِيَةٍ ۝ سَخَّرَهَا عَلَيْهِمْ سَبْعَ لَيَالٍ وَثَمَانِيَةَ أَيَّامٍ حُسُومًا فَتَرَى الْقَوْمَ فِيهَا صَرْعَىٰ كَأَنَّهُمْ أَعْجَازُ نَخْلٍ خَاوِيَةٍ ۝ فَهَلْ تَرَىٰ لَهُم مِّن بَاقِيَةٍ ۝ وَجَاءَ فِرْعَوْنُ وَمَن قَبْلَهُ وَالْمُؤْتَفِكَاتُ بِالْخَاطِئَةِ ۝ فَعَصَوْا رَسُولَ رَبِّهِمْ فَأَخَذَهُمْ أَخْذَةً رَّابِيَةً ۝ إِنَّا لَمَّا طَغَى الْمَاءُ حَمَلْنَاكُمْ فِي الْجَارِيَةِ ۝ لِنَجْعَلَهَا لَكُمْ تَذْكِرَةً وَتَعِيَهَا أُذُنٌ وَاعِيَةٌ

The Inevitable Reality — what is the Inevitable Reality? And what can make you know what is the Inevitable Reality? Thamūd and ʿĀd denied the Striking Calamity. As for Thamūd, they were destroyed by the overpowering blast; and as for ʿĀd, they were destroyed by a screaming, violent wind, which Allah imposed upon them for seven nights and eight days in succession, so that you would see the people therein fallen as if they were hollow trunks of palm trees. Then do you see any remains of them? And there came Firʿawn, and those before him, and the overturned cities [of Lūṭ's people] with sin, and they disobeyed the messenger of their Lord, so He seized them with an ever-increasing seizure. Indeed, when the water overflowed, We carried your ancestors in the sailing ship, that We might make it for you a reminder, and that a conscious, retaining ear would hold it in memory.

al-Ḥāqqah 69:1–12

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Day, and the Record in the Right Hand or the Left (13–37)

فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِي الصُّورِ نَفْخَةٌ وَاحِدَةٌ ۝ وَحُمِلَتِ الْأَرْضُ وَالْجِبَالُ فَدُكَّتَا دَكَّةً وَاحِدَةً ۝ فَيَوْمَئِذٍ وَقَعَتِ الْوَاقِعَةُ ۝ وَانشَقَّتِ السَّمَاءُ فَهِيَ يَوْمَئِذٍ وَاهِيَةٌ ۝ وَالْمَلَكُ عَلَىٰ أَرْجَائِهَا ۚ وَيَحْمِلُ عَرْشَ رَبِّكَ فَوْقَهُمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ ثَمَانِيَةٌ ۝ يَوْمَئِذٍ تُعْرَضُونَ لَا تَخْفَىٰ مِنكُمْ خَافِيَةٌ ۝ فَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِيَ كِتَابَهُ بِيَمِينِهِ فَيَقُولُ هَاؤُمُ اقْرَءُوا كِتَابِيَهْ ۝ إِنِّي ظَنَنتُ أَنِّي مُلَاقٍ حِسَابِيَهْ ۝ فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةٍ رَّاضِيَةٍ ۝ فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٍ ۝ قُطُوفُهَا دَانِيَةٌ ۝ كُلُوا وَاشْرَبُوا هَنِيئًا بِمَا أَسْلَفْتُمْ فِي الْأَيَّامِ الْخَالِيَةِ ۝ وَأَمَّا مَنْ أُوتِيَ كِتَابَهُ بِشِمَالِهِ فَيَقُولُ يَا لَيْتَنِي لَمْ أُوتَ كِتَابِيَهْ ۝ وَلَمْ أَدْرِ مَا حِسَابِيَهْ ۝ يَا لَيْتَهَا كَانَتِ الْقَاضِيَةَ ۝ مَا أَغْنَىٰ عَنِّي مَالِيَهْ ۝ هَلَكَ عَنِّي سُلْطَانِيَهْ ۝ خُذُوهُ فَغُلُّوهُ ۝ ثُمَّ الْجَحِيمَ صَلُّوهُ ۝ ثُمَّ فِي سِلْسِلَةٍ ذَرْعُهَا سَبْعُونَ ذِرَاعًا فَاسْلُكُوهُ ۝ إِنَّهُ كَانَ لَا يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ ۝ وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ ۝ فَلَيْسَ لَهُ الْيَوْمَ هَاهُنَا حَمِيمٌ ۝ وَلَا طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِنْ غِسْلِينٍ ۝ لَّا يَأْكُلُهُ إِلَّا الْخَاطِئُونَ

Then when the Trumpet is blown with one blast, and the earth and the mountains are lifted and leveled with a single blow — on that Day the Event will occur, and the heaven will split, for that Day it is frail, and the angels will be at its edges, while eight will bear the Throne of your Lord above them that Day. That Day you will be exhibited [for judgment]; not hidden among you is anything concealed. As for one who is given his record in his right hand, he will say: here, read my record! I was certain that I would meet my account — so he will be in a pleasant life, in an elevated garden, its fruit hanging near. Eat and drink in satisfaction for what you put forth in the days past. But as for one who is given his record in his left hand, he will say: oh, I wish I had not been given my record, and had not known what my account was; I wish it had been the decisive end. My wealth has not availed me; gone from me is my authority. [It will be commanded]: seize him and shackle him, then drive him into Hellfire, then insert him into a chain whose length is seventy cubits — for indeed he did not believe in Allah, the Most Great, and did not encourage the feeding of the poor. So there is not for him here this Day any devoted friend, nor any food except discharge from wounds, which none will eat except the sinners.

al-Ḥāqqah 69:13–37

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Oath on the Qurʾān's Divine Origin (38–52)

فَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِمَا تُبْصِرُونَ ۝ وَمَا لَا تُبْصِرُونَ ۝ إِنَّهُ لَقَوْلُ رَسُولٍ كَرِيمٍ ۝ وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوْلِ شَاعِرٍ ۚ قَلِيلًا مَّا تُؤْمِنُونَ ۝ وَلَا بِقَوْلِ كَاهِنٍ ۚ قَلِيلًا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ ۝ تَنزِيلٌ مِّن رَّبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ وَلَوْ تَقَوَّلَ عَلَيْنَا بَعْضَ الْأَقَاوِيلِ ۝ لَأَخَذْنَا مِنْهُ بِالْيَمِينِ ۝ ثُمَّ لَقَطَعْنَا مِنْهُ الْوَتِينَ ۝ فَمَا مِنكُم مِّنْ أَحَدٍ عَنْهُ حَاجِزِينَ ۝ وَإِنَّهُ لَتَذْكِرَةٌ لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ ۝ وَإِنَّا لَنَعْلَمُ أَنَّ مِنكُم مُّكَذِّبِينَ ۝ وَإِنَّهُ لَحَسْرَةٌ عَلَى الْكَافِرِينَ ۝ وَإِنَّهُ لَحَقُّ الْيَقِينِ ۝ فَسَبِّحْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الْعَظِيمِ

So I swear by what you see, and what you do not see — indeed, the Qurʾān is the word of a noble Messenger. It is not the word of a poet — little do you believe — nor the word of a soothsayer — little do you remember. It is a revelation from the Lord of the worlds. And if he [Muhammad ﷺ] had fabricated about Us some sayings, We would have seized him by the right hand, then cut from him the life vein, and not one of you could have withheld Us from him. And indeed, it is a reminder for the righteous, and indeed We surely know that among you are deniers, and indeed it will be [a cause of] regret upon the disbelievers, and indeed it is the truth of certainty. So exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great.

al-Ḥāqqah 69:38–52

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens with three short, hammering āyāt: الْحَاقَّةُ ۝ مَا الْحَاقَّةُ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الْحَاقَّةُ — the Ḥāqqah, what is the Ḥāqqah, and what can make you know what the Ḥāqqah is? Al-Saʿdī explains this name for the Day of Judgment comes from the root ḥaqq — truth, certainty — because it is the Day when truth is inevitably established: every promise and warning comes true, every soul receives exactly what it earned. The repeated questioning is a rhetorical style meant to fix the listener's attention on how immense and unlike anything ordinary this Reality is.

Allah then reminds the listeners of two nations who denied al-Qāriʿah (the Striking Calamity, another name for the same Day): ثَمُود, destroyed by a single overwhelming blast, and عَاد, destroyed by a furiously violent wind imposed on them for seven nights and eight days without pause, leaving their bodies scattered like hollow, fallen palm trunks. Firʿawn, the nations before him, and the overturned cities of Lūṭ's people are then named together as guilty of sin and disobedience to their messengers, seized with an ever-increasing punishment. Even the flood of Nūḥ's time is recalled — Allah carried the believers in the ship so that this rescue would remain a lasting reminder, held onto by every ear that truly listens (أُذُنٌ وَاعِيَةٌ).

الْيَمِين وَالشِّمَال — The Record in the Right Hand or the Left

This sūrah gives one of the Qurʾān's most vivid, memorable teaching scenes, repeated with variation across several similar sūrahs. The one given his record in his right hand is overjoyed, calling out for everyone to read it, because he was certain in this life that he would meet his account — so he is placed in a pleasant life, an elevated garden with fruit hanging within easy reach, told to eat and drink in satisfaction for what he sent forward in his days on earth. The one given his record in his left hand wishes he had never been given it at all, wishes his death had simply been final with no account to follow, and realizes in that moment that his wealth and his worldly authority — the very things he built his life around — cannot save him even slightly.

Two Paired Causes of Ruin (āyah 34): Allah gives the reason for this person's punishment in a single āyah that pairs two things together: إِنَّهُ كَانَ لَا يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ ۝ وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ — he did not believe in Allah, the Most Great, and he did not encourage the feeding of the poor. Al-Saʿdī and other scholars of tafsīr highlight this pairing: correct tawḥīd (belief in Allah alone) and genuine social compassion toward the poor are not separate, disconnected matters. A heart that truly believes in Allah's greatness is moved to mercy toward His creation; a heart empty of that belief has no real motivation to look after the deprived. The fitting punishment for withholding food from the poor is described starkly: he will have no food in the Hereafter except discharge from the wounds of the people of the Fire.

الدليل العقلي — The Logical Proof of the Qurʾān's Origin

The closing āyāt contain one of the strongest internal arguments in the Qurʾān for its own divine origin, used classically in works of ʿaqīdah and daʿwah. Allah swears by what is seen and unseen that the Qurʾān is the word of a noble Messenger — not the word of a poet, not the word of a soothsayer, but a revelation sent down from the Lord of the worlds. Then comes the decisive test: if the Prophet ﷺ had fabricated even a small saying and falsely attributed it to Allah, Allah would have seized him by the right hand and cut his life vein — his aorta — immediately, and no one on earth could have stopped that punishment from reaching him. Al-Saʿdī: this is a powerful proof, because the Prophet ﷺ delivered this exact revelation for over twenty years, under enormous pressure, without ever facing this promised seizure — showing plainly that every word truly came from Allah, not from the Prophet ﷺ himself.

The sūrah closes with four short, weighty statements about the Qurʾān: it is a reminder for the righteous (تَذْكِرَةٌ لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ), Allah already knows that deniers exist among the listeners, it will become a source of deep regret for the disbelievers on the Day they see its truth confirmed, and it is حَقُّ الْيَقِينِ — the very truth of certainty, the highest degree of certain knowledge. The sūrah ends where every reflection on Allah's greatness should end: فَسَبِّحْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الْعَظِيمِ — so exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great.

فَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِمَا تُبْصِرُونَ ۝ وَمَا لَا تُبْصِرُونَ ۝ إِنَّهُ لَقَوْلُ رَسُولٍ كَرِيمٍ ۝ وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوْلِ شَاعِرٍ ۚ قَلِيلًا مَّا تُؤْمِنُونَ ۝ وَلَا بِقَوْلِ كَاهِنٍ ۚ قَلِيلًا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ ۝ تَنزِيلٌ مِّن رَّبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

Allah's own oath establishing the Qurʾān's true nature: so I swear by what you see and what you do not see, indeed it is the word of a noble Messenger — it is not the word of a poet, little do you believe — nor the word of a soothsayer, little do you remember — it is a revelation from the Lord of the worlds. A strong, ready response whenever the Qurʾān's origin is doubted.
