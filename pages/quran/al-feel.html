سورة الفيل

Sūrat al-Fīl — The Elephant

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the story of Abraha's army and why they came to Makkah — and why they failed.
  • Understand what أَبَابِيل (birds in flocks) and سِجِّيل (baked clay) mean.
  • See why this event happened in the year of the Prophet's birth ﷺ — al-Saʿdī calls it a portent (precursor) of his mission.
  • Connect this sūrah to the one after it (Quraysh 106): the destruction was Allah's gift to Quraysh to secure their trade and protect the Ḥaram.
الآيات The Āyāt

أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَٰبِ ٱلْفِيلِ ۝ أَلَمْ يَجْعَلْ كَيْدَهُمْ فِى تَضْلِيلٍ ۝ وَأَرْسَلَ عَلَيْهِمْ طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ ۝ تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ مِّن سِجِّيلٍ ۝ فَجَعَلَهُمْ كَعَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍ

Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the army of the elephant? Did He not cause their evil plans to go awry? And He sent against them birds in flocks, pelting them with stones of baked clay — and He made them like a crop devoured and trampled.

al-Fīl 105:1–5

الخلفية The Story — عام الفيل

This took place in عام الفيل — the Year of the Elephant, the same year the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was born. Abraha al-Ashram, the Abyssinian governor of Yemen, built a great church in Ṣanʿāʾ intending it to draw the Arab pilgrims away from the Kaʿbah. When the Arabs refused and one man even defiled the church, Abraha led a massive army northward to destroy the Sacred House in Makkah — bringing with him an elephant (or several) at the front of the force.

When Abraha's army drew near Makkah, ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib — the Prophet's grandfather and the leader of Quraysh — came out to meet him. Abraha had taken his camels, and ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib came to retrieve them. Abraha was surprised: "You come to me about camels — and you say nothing about the House I have come to destroy?" ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib replied: "I am the lord of the camels. As for the House — it has its own Lord who will defend it." He then told the Makkans to evacuate to the mountains.

The elephant — whose name was Maḥmūd — refused to advance toward Makkah. When turned to face any other direction it would walk. When turned toward the Kaʿbah it knelt and would not move. Then Allah sent the birds.

التفسير Explanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ (Have you not seen how your Lord dealt…): The question is rhetorical and directed at the Prophet ﷺ — and through him, at all who hear. Al-Saʿdī says: this sūrah shows the might and power of Allah, His mercy towards His slaves, and evidence for His oneness — all compressed into five short āyāt.

أَلَمْ يَجْعَلْ كَيْدَهُمْ فِي تَضْلِيلٍ (Did He not cause their evil plans to go awry): كَيْد = a cunning, plotted plan. They had planned carefully, equipped themselves with the elephant, marched an army the Arabs could not match — and Allah turned their plan into تَضْلِيل (total failure and ruin). It did not just fail; it was utterly nullified.

وَأَرْسَلَ عَلَيْهِمْ طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ (He sent against them birds in flocks): Al-Saʿdī says these came in scattered groups — أَبَابِيل means flocks coming one after another. Each bird carried three stones of سِجِّيل (baked clay, hardened like fired brick): one in its beak and one in each claw.

تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ مِّن سِجِّيلٍ (pelting them with stones of baked clay): Al-Saʿdī says the birds pelted the army from the first to the last of them — not a single soldier escaped. Each stone struck its target, bore through the helmet and through the man. The army was struck all at once.

فَجَعَلَهُمْ كَعَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍ (and made them like a crop devoured and trampled): عَصْف is the straw or chaff of a crop that has been eaten away — dried out, hollow, crushed underfoot. This is the vivid image Allah gives: an entire professional army, reduced to hollow husks. Abraha himself fled and died on the way back, his body rotting as he went.

A portent of the Prophet's mission

Al-Saʿdī notes: this event took place in the very year the Prophet ﷺ was born, and it became one of the portents and precursors of his mission. The House was protected so that it would still be standing when the final Prophet came to purify it. The destruction of Abraha was the first of many signs that the earth was being prepared for the last of the Messengers ﷺ.

Memorize & Tell — احفظ وحدّث

أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَٰبِ ٱلْفِيلِ · فَجَعَلَهُمْ كَعَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍ

Memorize this sūrah and then retell the story of عام الفيل in your own words — the church, the elephant, ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib's reply, the birds, the stones, and what became of the army. Children who can retell this story have already begun to know Islamic history.
