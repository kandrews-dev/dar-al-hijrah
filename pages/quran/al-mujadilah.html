سورة المجادلة

Sūrat al-Mujādilah — The Pleading Woman

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the incident that opens the sūrah — the woman who pleaded about her husband — and what it teaches about Allah's hearing.
  • Understand الظِّهَار (ẓihār), why Allah abolished it as a means of divorce, and its كفّارة (expiation).
  • Grasp Allah's all-encompassing knowledge and presence: no secret counsel of three but He is the fourth.
  • Know the etiquette of gatherings and the honour Allah gives: He raises those who believe and are given knowledge by degrees.
  • Understand حِزْب الله (the party of Allah) versus حِزْب الشيطان, and that faith outweighs even the closest blood ties.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Complaint Allah Heard — Ẓihār (1)

قَدْ سَمِعَ اللَّهُ قَوْلَ الَّتِي تُجَادِلُكَ فِي زَوْجِهَا وَتَشْتَكِي إِلَى اللَّهِ وَاللَّهُ يَسْمَعُ تَحَاوُرَكُمَا ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ بَصِيرٌ

Allah has certainly heard the speech of the one who pleads with you concerning her husband and directs her complaint to Allah. And Allah hears your dialogue; indeed Allah is Hearing and Seeing.

al-Mujādilah 58:1

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: His Knowledge Encompasses Every Secret (7)

أَلَمْ تَرَ أَنَّ اللَّهَ يَعْلَمُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۖ مَا يَكُونُ مِن نَّجْوَىٰ ثَلَاثَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ رَابِعُهُمْ وَلَا خَمْسَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ سَادِسُهُمْ وَلَا أَدْنَىٰ مِن ذَٰلِكَ وَلَا أَكْثَرَ إِلَّا هُوَ مَعَهُمْ أَيْنَ مَا كَانُوا ۖ ثُمَّ يُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا عَمِلُوا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ

Have you not considered that Allah knows whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth? There is no private conversation of three but that He is the fourth of them, nor of five but that He is the sixth of them — and no less than that nor more except that He is with them wherever they are. Then He will inform them of what they did on the Day of Resurrection. Indeed Allah is Knowing of all things.

al-Mujādilah 58:7

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: Etiquette of Gatherings; the Rank of Knowledge (11)

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا قِيلَ لَكُمْ تَفَسَّحُوا فِي الْمَجَالِسِ فَافْسَحُوا يَفْسَحِ اللَّهُ لَكُمْ ۖ وَإِذَا قِيلَ انشُزُوا فَانشُزُوا يَرْفَعِ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَالَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ دَرَجَاتٍ ۚ وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ خَبِيرٌ

O you who believe, when you are told, "Make room in the gatherings," then make room; Allah will make room for you. And when you are told, "Rise up," then rise up; Allah will raise those who have believed among you and those who were given knowledge by degrees. And Allah is Aware of what you do.

al-Mujādilah 58:11

الآيات — القسم الرابعPart Four: The Party of Allah (22)

لَّا تَجِدُ قَوْمًا يُؤْمِنُونَ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ يُوَادُّونَ مَنْ حَادَّ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ وَلَوْ كَانُوا آبَاءَهُمْ أَوْ أَبْنَاءَهُمْ أَوْ إِخْوَانَهُمْ أَوْ عَشِيرَتَهُمْ ۚ أُولَٰئِكَ كَتَبَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ الْإِيمَانَ وَأَيَّدَهُم بِرُوحٍ مِّنْهُ ۖ وَيُدْخِلُهُمْ جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا ۚ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ ۚ أُولَٰئِكَ حِزْبُ اللَّهِ ۚ أَلَا إِنَّ حِزْبَ اللَّهِ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُونَ

You will not find a people who believe in Allah and the Last Day loving those who oppose Allah and His Messenger, even if they were their fathers, their sons, their brothers, or their kindred. Those — He has decreed faith within their hearts and supported them with a spirit from Him. And He will admit them to gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding eternally therein. Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him. Those are the party of Allah. Unquestionably, the party of Allah — it is they who are the successful.

al-Mujādilah 58:22

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Al-Saʿdī explains that the sūrah opens with a real event that magnifies the greatness of Allah's hearing. A woman — Khawlah bint Thaʿlabah رضي الله عنها — came to the Prophet ﷺ pleading about her husband, who had pronounced ẓihār against her (an old Arab formula in which a man declared his wife "like the back of my mother," treating her as forbidden yet leaving her trapped, neither wife nor free). She argued her case and poured out her complaint to Allah, and Allah revealed the opening āyah: قَدْ سَمِعَ اللَّهُ قَوْلَ الَّتِي تُجَادِلُكَ فِي زَوْجِهَا — "Allah has indeed heard the words of the woman who pleads with you." ʿĀʾishah رضي الله عنها marvelled that Allah, above the seven heavens, heard the quiet complaint of this woman in the corner of the room. Al-Saʿdī: this is the perfection of Allah's Name السَّمِيع — no voice, however faint, and no grievance, however private, is beyond His hearing.

الظِّهَار — A Jāhilī Cruelty Abolished

Al-Saʿdī explains that Allah responded to Khawlah's distress not only with comfort but with legislation that lifted the injustice of ẓihār. He declared the formula a false and sinful speech — مُنكَرًا مِّنَ الْقَوْلِ وَزُورًا — for a wife does not become a mother by a man's words. The marriage is not dissolved; rather, if the husband wishes to return to his wife, he must first pay a كفّارة (expiation) as a deterrent from such careless cruelty: (1) freeing a believing slave; if unable, (2) fasting two consecutive months; if unable, (3) feeding sixty poor persons. Al-Saʿdī: notice the mercy and the wisdom — a jāhilī custom that had held women hostage is turned into a measured ruling that both restores the wife's rights and teaches the husband to weigh his words. The Sharīʿah hears the wronged and answers the wrong.

Nothing Is Hidden — He Is With Them Wherever They Are: From His hearing the sūrah moves to His knowledge. Al-Saʿdī reflects on the sweeping āyah of النَّجْوَى (secret counsel): مَا يَكُونُ مِن نَّجْوَىٰ ثَلَاثَةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ رَابِعُهُمْ — there is no whispered gathering of three but Allah is their fourth, nor of five but He is their sixth, nor fewer nor more but He is with them wherever they are, by His knowledge. This is the maʿiyyah (with-ness) of Allah's knowledge that encompasses every private word and plan — a comfort to the believer who is never alone, and a warning to the plotter who imagines his scheme is unseen. Then, on the Day of Resurrection, يُنَبِّئُهُم بِمَا عَمِلُوا — He will inform them of all they did. The one who truly believes his secret counsels are heard and recorded guards his tongue and his intentions.

Making Room, and the Rank of Knowledge: The sūrah teaches an etiquette of the believers' assemblies: when asked to make space, تَفَسَّحُوا — widen the circle graciously, and Allah will widen for you His mercy and provision; when asked to rise for someone or for a good purpose, do so. Al-Saʿdī draws the famous promise that follows: يَرْفَعِ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَالَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ دَرَجَاتٍ — Allah raises the believers, and especially those given knowledge, by many degrees. Faith raises a person; faith joined to beneficial knowledge raises him higher still. This is among the clearest āyāt on the honour of seeking sacred knowledge and acting by it — the knowledge that lifts its people in this world and the next.

Two Parties, One Success: The sūrah closes by dividing mankind into two camps. Earlier it named those who ally with the enemies of Allah حِزْبُ الشَّيْطَانِ — the party of Shayṭān, the losers. Against them stands حِزْبُ اللَّهِ. Al-Saʿdī: their defining mark is stated with striking force — a true believer cannot give his heart's loyalty to those who war against Allah and His Messenger, even if they were his own fathers, sons, brothers, or clan. This is not hatred of kin, but that loyalty of the heart in religion belongs to Allah above blood. Of such people Allah says: كَتَبَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ الْإِيمَانَ وَأَيَّدَهُم بِرُوحٍ مِّنْهُ — He has inscribed faith in their hearts and strengthened them with a spirit from Him — and He seals it: رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ, Allah is pleased with them and they with Him. أَلَا إِنَّ حِزْبَ اللَّهِ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُونَ — truly, the party of Allah are the successful.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

يَرْفَعِ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَالَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ دَرَجَاتٍ ۚ وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ خَبِيرٌ

The promise for every student of the religion: Allah raises those who believe among you, and those who are given knowledge, by degrees. Faith lifts a person; faith carried on beneficial knowledge lifts him higher still — in this world and the next. Pair it with the sūrah's seal: the party of Allah, it is they who are the successful.

All nine sūrahs of Juzʾ 28 (sūrahs 58–66: al-Mujādilah → al-Taḥrīm) are now covered with tafsīr, vocabulary, and quizzes — a juzʾ rich in the law and manners of the believing community: the household, allegiance and disavowal, the Friday prayer, sincere striving, and the party of Allah.
