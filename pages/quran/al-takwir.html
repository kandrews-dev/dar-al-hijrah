سورة التكوير

Sūrat al-Takwīr — The Rolling Up

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the twelve cosmic events that open this sūrah and understand why they culminate in "then every soul will know what it has presented."
  • Understand the verse of the girl buried alive (الْمَوْءُودَةُ) — what al-Saʿdī says about her innocence and the gravity of the crime — and its connection to accountability on that Day.
  • Know al-Saʿdī's explanation of the three oaths (retreating stars, receding night, breathing morning) and what they testify to: that this is the word of noble Jibrīl, not madness and not a devil.
  • Memorize the closing two āyāt about human will and Allah's will — the correct balance between striving for righteousness and surrendering to Allah's decree.
الآياتThe Āyāt

إِذَا الشَّمْسُ كُوِّرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا النُّجُومُ انكَدَرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْعِشَارُ عُطِّلَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْبِحَارُ سُجِّرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا النُّفُوسُ زُوِّجَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْمَوْءُودَةُ سُئِلَتْ ۝ بِأَيِّ ذَنبٍ قُتِلَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الصُّحُفُ نُشِرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا السَّمَاءُ كُشِطَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْجَحِيمُ سُعِّرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا الْجَنَّةُ أُزْلِفَتْ ۝ عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ مَّا أَحْضَرَتْ ۝ فَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِالْخُنَّسِ ۝ الْجَوَارِ الْكُنَّسِ ۝ وَاللَّيْلِ إِذَا عَسْعَسَ ۝ وَالصُّبْحِ إِذَا تَنَفَّسَ ۝ إِنَّهُ لَقَوْلُ رَسُولٍ كَرِيمٍ ۝ ذِي قُوَّةٍ عِندَ ذِي الْعَرْشِ مَكِينٍ ۝ مُّطَاعٍ ثَمَّ أَمِينٍ ۝ وَمَا صَاحِبُكُم بِمَجْنُونٍ ۝ وَلَقَدْ رَآهُ بِالْأُفُقِ الْمُبِينِ ۝ وَمَا هُوَ عَلَى الْغَيْبِ بِضَنِينٍ ۝ وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوْلِ شَيْطَانٍ رَّجِيمٍ ۝ فَأَيْنَ تَذْهَبُونَ ۝ إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكْرٌ لِّلْعَالَمِينَ ۝ لِمَن شَاءَ مِنكُمْ أَن يَسْتَقِيمَ ۝ وَمَا تَشَاءُونَ إِلَّا أَن يَشَاءَ اللَّهُ رَبُّ الْعَالَمِينَ

When the sun is rolled up and its light is gone; when the stars fall and scatter; when the mountains are set in motion; when the pregnant she-camels are abandoned; when the wild beasts are gathered; when the seas are set ablaze; when souls are paired; when the girl buried alive is asked, for what sin she was killed; when the scrolls of deeds are spread open; when the heaven is stripped away; when Hell is blazing fiercely; when Paradise is brought near — then every soul will know what it has presented. So I swear by the receding stars, those that run their courses and hide, by the night as it withdraws, by the morning as it breathes — it is indeed the word of a noble messenger, possessed of power, well-established with the Lord of the Throne, obeyed in the heavens and trustworthy. Your companion is not mad — he saw him on the clear horizon. He is not withholding the Unseen. And it is not the word of an accursed devil. So where are you going? It is nothing but a reminder to all the worlds, for whoever of you wishes to walk straight — but you cannot will anything unless Allah wills it, Lord of all the worlds.

al-Takwīr 81:1–29

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens with twelve signs that will occur as the Day of Judgment draws near, building in intensity. The first six involve the physical world: the sun is extinguished and rolled up, losing its light; the stars tumble and scatter, losing their places; mountains float through the air; the she-camels — the most prized possessions of the Arabs — are abandoned as their owners are seized with terror; wild animals are gathered and mixed together; the seas are set ablaze. The next six involve human souls and the unseen: souls are paired with their deeds and their like; the girl buried alive is asked — not to condemn her but to condemn her killer; the scrolls of deeds are opened; the heaven is peeled away; Hell is ignited; and Paradise brought close. Then the result: عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ مَّا أَحْضَرَتْ — every soul will know what it has presented.

وَإِذَا الْمَوْءُودَةُ سُئِلَتْ — The Girl Buried Alive Is Asked

The Jāhiliyyah Arabs would bury infant daughters alive — fearing poverty or shame. Allah does not ask her: why were you buried? He asks: for what sin were you killed? Al-Saʿdī: the question is directed at the victim, not to blame her, but to expose the injustice of the killer by making clear she committed no sin. On that Day, every act of oppression and murder will be accounted for, and no innocent soul will have been lost without its killer being held to account. The act is mentioned alongside the rolling up of the sun because it represents the darkest injustice — the murder of the most helpless — and Allah will settle it all.

رَسُولٍ كَرِيمٍ — Noble Messenger: After describing the Day of Judgment, Allah defends the Quran from the Quraysh's slanders. They called the Prophet ﷺ a madman; they said the Quran came from a devil. Allah swears by three things of recurring beauty — the retreating stars (الْخُنَّسِ الْجَوَارِ الْكُنَّسِ), the night as it pulls back at dawn, the morning as it expands — that this Quran is the word of Jibrīl ﷺ: noble, of tremendous strength, well-established and honored before the Lord of the Throne, obeyed by all the angels, and perfectly trustworthy. The Prophet ﷺ saw Jibrīl in his true form on the clear horizon. He does not hold back any part of the revelation. And it is certainly not from a devil — for a devil cannot approach the Preserved Tablet.

فَأَيْنَ تَذْهَبُونَ — So Where Are You Going? Al-Saʿdī: this is a rebuke to those who turn away from the Quran. The path is clear, the reminder is complete, the messenger is truthful — so where else do you turn? The Quran is a reminder for all creation, open to whoever wishes to walk straight. Yet the closing āyah is a crucial balance: وَمَا تَشَاءُونَ إِلَّا أَن يَشَاءَ اللَّهُ رَبُّ الْعَالَمِينَ — you cannot will anything unless Allah wills it, Lord of all the worlds. Human will is real and consequential, but it operates entirely within Allah's will and knowledge. This prevents both fatalism (abandoning effort) and self-sufficiency (thinking the choice is entirely yours).

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

لِمَن شَاءَ مِنكُمْ أَن يَسْتَقِيمَ ۝ وَمَا تَشَاءُونَ إِلَّا أَن يَشَاءَ اللَّهُ رَبُّ الْعَالَمِينَ

Two āyāt that carry the whole theology of human effort and divine will. For whoever of you wishes to walk straight — your wanting is real; the path is open; the choice is yours to make. But you cannot will anything unless Allah wills it — your ability to want good, your success in doing good, your being guided to these words right now: all of it is by His will and mercy. This balance is the Salaf's position: no compulsion and no self-sufficiency.
