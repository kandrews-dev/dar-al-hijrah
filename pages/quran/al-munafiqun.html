سورة المنافقون

Sūrat al-Munāfiqūn — The Hypocrites

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand what نِفَاق (hypocrisy) is: outward Islam concealing inner disbelief — and why Allah calls the hypocrites liars even when they utter true words.
  • Know how the Qurʾān describes the hypocrites: false oaths used as a جُنَّة (shield), impressive outer shells, hearts like propped-up timber, and constant suspicion.
  • Understand their scheming — withholding spending and threatening to expel the believers — and the reply: وَلِلَّهِ الْعِزَّةُ وَلِرَسُولِهِ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ.
  • Take to heart the first closing warning: لَا تُلْهِكُمْ أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَلَا أَوْلَادُكُمْ عَن ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ.
  • Take to heart the second: spend in Allah's cause before death arrives and the wish to return comes too late.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The False Oath and the Divine Verdict (1)

إِذَا جَاءَكَ الْمُنَافِقُونَ قَالُوا نَشْهَدُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُ اللَّهِ ۗ وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُهُ وَاللَّهُ يَشْهَدُ إِنَّ الْمُنَافِقِينَ لَكَاذِبُونَ

When the hypocrites come to you, they say, "We testify that you are indeed the Messenger of Allah." And Allah knows that you are His Messenger, and Allah testifies that the hypocrites are surely liars.

al-Munāfiqūn 63:1

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: Their Portrait — Beware Them (4)

وَإِذَا رَأَيْتَهُمْ تُعْجِبُكَ أَجْسَامُهُمْ ۖ وَإِن يَقُولُوا تَسْمَعْ لِقَوْلِهِمْ ۖ كَأَنَّهُمْ خُشُبٌ مُّسَنَّدَةٌ ۖ يَحْسَبُونَ كُلَّ صَيْحَةٍ عَلَيْهِمْ ۚ هُمُ الْعَدُوُّ فَاحْذَرْهُمْ ۚ قَاتَلَهُمُ اللَّهُ ۖ أَنَّىٰ يُؤْفَكُونَ

And when you see them, their forms please you, and if they speak, you listen to their speech. [But] they are like propped-up timber. They think every shout is against them. They are the enemy, so beware of them. May Allah destroy them; how are they deluded?

al-Munāfiqūn 63:4

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: Their Scheming and the Truth of Honour (8)

يَقُولُونَ لَئِن رَّجَعْنَا إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ لَيُخْرِجَنَّ الْأَعَزُّ مِنْهَا الْأَذَلَّ ۚ وَلِلَّهِ الْعِزَّةُ وَلِرَسُولِهِ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَلَٰكِنَّ الْمُنَافِقِينَ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ

They say, "If we return to Madinah, the more honoured will surely expel therefrom the more humble." But to Allah belongs [all] honour, and to His Messenger and to the believers — but the hypocrites do not know.

al-Munāfiqūn 63:8

الآيات — القسم الرابعPart Four: Two Warnings to the Believers (9–11)

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تُلْهِكُمْ أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَلَا أَوْلَادُكُمْ عَن ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ ۚ وَمَن يَفْعَلْ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْخَاسِرُونَ ۝ وَأَنفِقُوا مِن مَّا رَزَقْنَاكُم مِّن قَبْلِ أَن يَأْتِيَ أَحَدَكُمُ الْمَوْتُ فَيَقُولَ رَبِّ لَوْلَا أَخَّرْتَنِي إِلَىٰ أَجَلٍ قَرِيبٍ فَأَصَّدَّقَ وَأَكُن مِّنَ الصَّالِحِينَ ۝ وَلَن يُؤَخِّرَ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِذَا جَاءَ أَجَلُهَا ۚ وَاللَّهُ خَبِيرٌ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ

O you who believe, let not your wealth or your children divert you from the remembrance of Allah. And whoever does that — it is they who are the losers. And spend from what We have provided you before death approaches one of you and he says, "My Lord, if only You would delay me for a brief term so I would give charity and be among the righteous." But Allah will never delay a soul when its time has come. And Allah is Aware of what you do.

al-Munāfiqūn 63:9–11

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Al-Saʿdī explains that this sūrah lays bare the منافقون — the hypocrites of Madinah who displayed Islam outwardly while concealing disbelief. Their opening move is telling: they come swearing نَشْهَدُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُ اللَّهِ, "we testify you are truly the Messenger of Allah." The statement itself is true — so why does Allah call them liars? Because, al-Saʿdī clarifies, they were lying about their own hearts: their tongues declared a testimony their hearts did not hold. Allah affirms His Messenger's truth (وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُهُ) and, in the same breath, exposes theirs (وَاللَّهُ يَشْهَدُ إِنَّ الْمُنَافِقِينَ لَكَاذِبُونَ). They then اتَّخَذُوا أَيْمَانَهُمْ جُنَّةً — took their oaths as a shield to hide behind and to block others from Allah's path.

خُشُبٌ مُّسَنَّدَةٌ — Hollow on the Inside

Al-Saʿdī draws attention to the vivid image of āyah 4. Outwardly the hypocrites were impressive: تُعْجِبُكَ أَجْسَامُهُمْ — their physiques and bearing pleased the eye, and their eloquence held the ear. But Allah likens them to خُشُبٌ مُّسَنَّدَةٌ — beams of dead timber propped against a wall: fine to look at, but lifeless, rootless, bearing no fruit and giving no shade. Beneath the polished shell there was no faith. And their guilty conscience betrayed them: يَحْسَبُونَ كُلَّ صَيْحَةٍ عَلَيْهِمْ — they imagined every raised voice was a plot against them, because a heart that hides treachery can never feel safe. The verdict is blunt: هُمُ الْعَدُوُّ فَاحْذَرْهُمْ — they are the [real] enemy, so beware of them. The enemy who prays beside you is more dangerous than the one who faces you in the open.

Their Scheming and the Truth of Honour: The sūrah records the hypocrites' whispering during an expedition — their refusal to spend on the poorer believers around the Prophet ﷺ (thinking to starve them into leaving), and their leader's boast that on return to Madinah "the mighty will drive out the weak," casting himself as the honoured one and the believers as the lowly. Al-Saʿdī: Allah overturns their entire calculation in one clause — وَلِلَّهِ الْعِزَّةُ وَلِرَسُولِهِ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ. All honour, might and dominance belong to Allah, then to His Messenger and the believers by extension. The hypocrites had it exactly backwards, but they do not know it. Real ʿizzah is never in numbers, wealth or reputation; it is in faith and nearness to Allah.

The Two Closing Warnings to the Believers: Having exposed the hypocrites, the sūrah turns to the believers with two protective commands so they never drift toward that ruin. First: لَا تُلْهِكُمْ أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَلَا أَوْلَادُكُمْ عَن ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ — do not let wealth and children distract you from remembering Allah; whoever lets them do so is among الْخَاسِرُونَ, the true losers, for they traded the eternal for the passing. Second: وَأَنفِقُوا مِن مَّا رَزَقْنَاكُم مِّن قَبْلِ أَن يَأْتِيَ أَحَدَكُمُ الْمَوْتُ — spend now, in the days of ability, before death arrives and the soul cries out for just a little more time — "so I would give charity and be among the righteous." Al-Saʿdī: this is the longing of every negligent person at the end, and it is refused: وَلَن يُؤَخِّرَ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِذَا جَاءَ أَجَلُهَا. The time to act is while you still can; the wish to return is never granted.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تُلْهِكُمْ أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَلَا أَوْلَادُكُمْ عَن ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ ۚ وَمَن يَفْعَلْ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْخَاسِرُونَ

A warning for every believer, in a sūrah about hypocrites: Let not your wealth or your children divert you from the remembrance of Allah — whoever does that, it is they who are the losers. The things we love most are exactly what can pull us away; keep them in their place, and keep dhikr in its place.
