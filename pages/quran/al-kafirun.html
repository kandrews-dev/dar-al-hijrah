Sūrah 109 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 6 āyāt

سورة الكافرون

Sūrat al-Kāfirūn — The Disbelievers

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand what سورة الكافرون (Sūrat al-Kāfirūn) is declaring and why it was revealed.
  • Learn why the negation is repeated twice — and what each repetition adds.
  • Understand that لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ وَلِيَ دِينِ is a statement of separation, not religious tolerance of shirk.
  • Know the Sunnah: reciting this sūrah in Fajr's first rakʿah and before sleep.

The leaders of Quraysh once came to the Prophet ﷺ with a proposal: worship our gods for a year, and we will worship yours for a year. This sūrah was the answer — a total, public, and permanent disavowal of everything they worshipped besides Allah.

الآيات The Āyāt — سورة الكافرون

قُلْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ ۝ لَآ أَعْبُدُ مَا تَعْبُدُونَ ۝ وَلَآ أَنتُمْ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعْبُدُ ۝ وَلَآ أَنَا۠ عَابِدٌ مَّا عَبَدتُّمْ ۝ وَلَآ أَنتُمْ عَٰبِدُونَ مَآ أَعْبُدُ ۝ لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ وَلِىَ دِينِ

Say: O disbelievers! I do not worship what you worship, nor do you worship what I worship. Nor will I worship what you worship, nor will you worship what I worship. To you your religion, and to me my religion.

al-Kāfirūn 109:1–6

التفسير Explanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

قُلْ (Say) — this is a direct command to the Prophet ﷺ to proclaim, openly and without softening the words. Not in private, not gently, but publicly — a clear declaration.

لَا أَعْبُدُ مَا تَعْبُدُونَ (I do not worship what you worship): al-Saʿdī explains this is a disavowal — both outwardly and inwardly — of their objects of worship. Not just "I don't worship your idols in public" but "there is no trace of their worship in my heart either."

Why is the negation repeated twice? Al-Saʿdī explains the two pairs serve different purposes. The first pair (āyāt 2–3) negates the action at the present moment. The second pair (āyāt 4–5) negates any possibility of it — it confirms the attitude is deeply settled and will not change. Together they close every door: not now, not ever, not even in a compromise.

وَلَا أَنتُمْ عَابِدُونَ مَا أَعْبُدُ (nor do you worship what I worship): al-Saʿdī points to something profound here. They may say the name "Allah" — the Quraysh knew Allah — but their worship was mixed with shirk. True worship of Allah requires إخلاص (ikhlāṣ — sincerity), and worship that is mixed with shirk is not worship of Allah at all.

لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ وَلِيَ دِينِ — what does this mean?

This is not a concession that their religion is valid — it is a declaration of complete separation. As al-Saʿdī notes, it is like the āyah: «قُل كُلٌّ يَعمَلُ عَلى شاكِلَتِه» — "Say: each one acts according to his own way" (al-Isrāʾ 17:84). Two paths, completely separate, no overlap, no compromise.

The Sunnah of this sūrah: The Prophet ﷺ would recite سورة الكافرون in the first rakʿah and سورة الإخلاص in the second rakʿah of the two sunnah rakʿāt of Fajr, and also before sleep. This is why it is called "the sūrah that absolves from shirk" — it is a daily renewal of your complete disavowal of all worship except to Allah alone.

الدرس لنا The Lesson for Us

This sūrah teaches the family something essential: تعددية (pluralism) about what to eat or wear is fine — but in عبادة (ʿibādah — worship), there is no middle ground and no compromise. We worship Allah alone, as He commanded, and everything else is shirk. The clearest way to teach children this distinction is through this sūrah — recite it with them, explain it, and make it part of the daily routine before sleep.

Memorize & Use Daily — احفظ واعمل به

قُلْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ · لَآ أَعْبُدُ مَا تَعْبُدُونَ · لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ وَلِىَ دِينِ

Memorize this sūrah and make it the family's nightly recitation before sleep — exactly as the Prophet ﷺ practised. It is a declaration that we renew every day: we worship Allah alone, in the way He commanded, with nothing mixed in.
