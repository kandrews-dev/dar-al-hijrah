سُورَةُ اللَّيْل

Sūrat al-Layl — The Night

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Know the three oaths and what they attest to: human striving has divergent ends.
  • Understand the two contrasting paths: the giver who fears Allah vs. the miser who thinks himself independent.
  • Know al-Saʿdī's explanation of "made easy" — how it works in both directions.
  • Know the reference to Abu Bakr al-Ṣiddīq and why these āyāt apply more broadly.
الآياتThe Āyāt

وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا يَغْشَىٰ ۝ وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا تَجَلَّىٰ ۝ وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلْأُنثَىٰٓ ۝ إِنَّ سَعْيَكُمْ لَشَتَّىٰ ۝ فَأَمَّا مَنْ أَعْطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ ۝ وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلْحُسْنَىٰ ۝ فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلْيُسْرَىٰ ۝ وَأَمَّا مَنۢ بَخِلَ وَٱسْتَغْنَىٰ ۝ وَكَذَّبَ بِٱلْحُسْنَىٰ ۝ فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلْعُسْرَىٰ ۝ وَمَا يُغْنِى عَنْهُ مَالُهُۥٓ إِذَا تَرَدَّىٰ ۝ إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا لَلْهُدَىٰ ۝ وَإِنَّ لَنَا لَلْءَاخِرَةَ وَٱلْأُولَىٰ ۝ فَأَنذَرْتُكُمْ نَارًا تَلَظَّىٰ ۝ لَا يَصْلَىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلْأَشْقَى ۝ ٱلَّذِى كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ ۝ وَسَيُجَنَّبُهَا ٱلْأَتْقَى ۝ ٱلَّذِى يُؤْتِى مَالَهُۥ يَتَزَكَّىٰ ۝ وَمَا لِأَحَدٍ عِندَهُۥ مِن نِّعْمَةٍ تُجْزَىٰٓ ۝ إِلَّا ٱبْتِغَآءَ وَجْهِ رَبِّهِ ٱلْأَعْلَىٰ ۝ وَلَسَوْفَ يَرْضَىٰ

By the night as it conceals — and by the day as it discloses — and by Him Who created male and female — verily your endeavours have divergent ends. As for the one who gives in charity and fears Allah, and believes in the ultimate good — We will make easy for him the path of salvation. But as for the one who is miserly and thinks he has no need of Allah, and denies the ultimate good — We will make easy for him the path of perdition. And his wealth will avail him nothing when he falls. It is for Us to show the path of guidance — and to Us belong the hereafter and the present world. So I warn you of a raging fire — which none will enter except the most wretched, who denied and turned away. But the most righteous will be kept away from it — the one who gives his wealth seeking to purify himself, owing no one any favour to repay, but seeking only the pleasure of his Lord, the Most High. And he will in time be well pleased.

al-Layl 92:1–21

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The three oaths: By the night that conceals (each creature returns to its home and rests from toil); by the day that discloses (people go about their business in its light); and by the One Who created male and female (Allah swears by Himself as Creator, Who paired all creatures for survival of their species). What is sworn is: إِنَّ سَعْيَكُمْ لَشَتَّى — your endeavours are widely divergent. They differ in their deeds, in their quality and intensity, and in their purpose: done for Allah (lasting) or for transient aims (rendered invalid).

الطريقان — The Two Paths

Path of Salvation · اليُسْرى Path of Perdition · الْعُسْرى
أَعْطَى — gives in charity (zakāh, ṣadaqah, spending on family, good causes, and all physical acts of worship) بَخِلَ — is miserly in what he is instructed to spend; has no desire to give what Allah enjoins
اتَّقَى — fears Allah regarding prohibitions اسْتَغْنَى — thinks he has no need of Allah; does not submit; does not feel his soul's desperate need for its Lord
صَدَّقَ بِالْحُسْنَى — believes in لا إله إلا الله and all it entails of beliefs and hereafter reward كَذَّبَ بِالْحُسْنَى — denies the goodly beliefs Allah has commanded
فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُ لِلْيُسْرَى: Allah makes all good easy; all evil easy to avoid فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُ لِلْعُسْرَى: Allah makes bad characteristics easy; disobedience and carrying away with evil easy

Al-Saʿdī: he takes measures that lead to each path — so Allah makes that path easy. The direction of ease is earned by what comes before it.

وَمَا يُغْنِي عَنْهُ مَالُهُ إِذَا تَرَدَّى (his wealth will avail him nothing when he falls): The very wealth that made him arrogant and miserly becomes a source of regret when he dies, because he did not send any of it ahead to the hereafter. Only righteous deeds accompany a person after death.

إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا لَلْهُدَى وَإِنَّ لَنَا لَلْآخِرَةَ وَالْأُولَى: It is for Allah to show the path of guidance. As for misguidance — it bars from Allah and leads only to severe punishment. He owns both the hereafter and the present world — no partner. So let those with aspirations turn to Him, not to created beings.

The most righteous kept away from fire: He gives his wealth يَتَزَكَّى — seeking to purify himself from sins and seeking the pleasure of Allah. He does not owe anyone any favour to repay — his deeds remain sincerely for Allah alone. Al-Saʿdī: the one who owes people favours will inevitably do things for them that undermine his sincerity towards Allah.

صاحب الآية — The Companion in this verse

Al-Saʿdī notes: the verse referring to the most righteous who gives his wealth to purify himself was revealed about Abu Bakr al-Ṣiddīq رضي الله عنه. He did not owe anyone any favour — not even the Messenger ﷺ (apart from the favour of calling him to Islam which could never be repaid). However, the verse is general — it applies to everyone possessing this quality.

Memorize & Contrast — اِحْفَظْ وقارن

فَأَمَّا مَنْ أَعْطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلْحُسْنَىٰ فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلْيُسْرَىٰ

Three traits unlock the easy path: give, fear Allah, believe. Three traits lock the hard path: withhold, feel independent of Allah, deny. This sūrah is a masterclass in how internal states (feeling no need of Allah) lead to external outcomes (path of perdition made easy). Teach children: generosity isn't just about money — it starts with recognising your need for Allah.

Tafsīr based on Taysīr al-Karīm al-Raḥmān of Imam ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Saʿdī · explained in our own words

لِأَغْرَاضِ التَّعْلِيمِ فَقَطْ — هَذَا المَوْقِعُ لَا يُفْتِي. لِلْفَتَاوَى، يُرْجَعُ إِلَى العُلَمَاءِ المَوْثُوقِين.
