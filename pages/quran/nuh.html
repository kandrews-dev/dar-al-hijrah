سورة نوح

Sūrat Nūḥ — Noah

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know why Nūḥ (عليه السلام) was sent to his people and understand the complete methodology of daʿwah this sūrah models — calling by night and day, publicly and privately.
  • Know the promise Nūḥ made linking اِسْتِغْفَار (seeking forgiveness) to provision: abundant rain, increased wealth and children, gardens and rivers.
  • Know the eight signs of Allah's power that Nūḥ presents to his people — their own stages of creation, the seven heavens, the moon and sun, and the earth as an expanse.
  • Know the origin of the five idols — وَدّ، سُوَاع، يَغُوث، يَعُوق، نَسْر — as righteous men later venerated and eventually worshipped, and how this became the well-known root of shirk in Nūḥ's time.
  • Know Nūḥ's closing prayer and reflect on his patience — traditionally understood from the Qurʾān as centuries of calling with only a small number believing.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Call, Night and Day, Public and Private (1–12)

إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوْمِهِ أَنْ أَنذِرْ قَوْمَكَ مِن قَبْلِ أَن يَأْتِيَهُمْ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ ۝ قَالَ يَا قَوْمِ إِنِّي لَكُمْ نَذِيرٌ مُّبِينٌ ۝ أَنِ اعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ وَاتَّقُوهُ وَأَطِيعُونِ ۝ يَغْفِرْ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمْ وَيُؤَخِّرْكُمْ إِلَىٰ أَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى ۚ إِنَّ أَجَلَ اللَّهِ إِذَا جَاءَ لَا يُؤَخَّرُ ۖ لَوْ كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ ۝ قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي دَعَوْتُ قَوْمِي لَيْلًا وَنَهَارًا ۝ فَلَمْ يَزِدْهُمْ دُعَائِي إِلَّا فِرَارًا ۝ وَإِنِّي كُلَّمَا دَعَوْتُهُمْ لِتَغْفِرَ لَهُمْ جَعَلُوا أَصَابِعَهُمْ فِي آذَانِهِمْ وَاسْتَغْشَوْا ثِيَابَهُمْ وَأَصَرُّوا وَاسْتَكْبَرُوا اسْتِكْبَارًا ۝ ثُمَّ إِنِّي دَعَوْتُهُمْ جِهَارًا ۝ ثُمَّ إِنِّي أَعْلَنتُ لَهُمْ وَأَسْرَرْتُ لَهُمْ إِسْرَارًا ۝ فَقُلْتُ اسْتَغْفِرُوا رَبَّكُمْ إِنَّهُ كَانَ غَفَّارًا ۝ يُرْسِلِ السَّمَاءَ عَلَيْكُم مِّدْرَارًا ۝ وَيُمْدِدْكُم بِأَمْوَالٍ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ جَنَّاتٍ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ أَنْهَارًا

Indeed, We sent Nūḥ to his people, [saying]: warn your people before there comes to them a painful punishment. He said: O my people, indeed I am to you a clear warner — worship Allah, fear Him, and obey me. He will forgive you your sins and delay you for a specified term; indeed the time set by Allah, when it comes, will not be delayed, if only you knew. He said: My Lord, indeed I invited my people night and day, but my invitation increased them not except in flight. Every time I invited them that You may forgive them, they put their fingers in their ears, covered themselves with their garments, persisted, and were arrogant with great arrogance. Then I invited them publicly, then I announced to them and also confided to them secretly, and said: seek forgiveness from your Lord — indeed He is ever a Perpetual Forgiver. He will send [rain from] the sky upon you in abundance, and give you increase in wealth and children, and provide for you gardens, and provide for you rivers.

Nūḥ 71:1–12

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: Signs of Allah's Power in Creation (13–20)

مَّا لَكُمْ لَا تَرْجُونَ لِلَّهِ وَقَارًا ۝ وَقَدْ خَلَقَكُمْ أَطْوَارًا ۝ أَلَمْ تَرَوْا كَيْفَ خَلَقَ اللَّهُ سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ طِبَاقًا ۝ وَجَعَلَ الْقَمَرَ فِيهِنَّ نُورًا وَجَعَلَ الشَّمْسَ سِرَاجًا ۝ وَاللَّهُ أَنبَتَكُم مِّنَ الْأَرْضِ نَبَاتًا ۝ ثُمَّ يُعِيدُكُمْ فِيهَا وَيُخْرِجُكُمْ إِخْرَاجًا ۝ وَاللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ الْأَرْضَ بِسَاطًا ۝ لِّتَسْلُكُوا مِنْهَا سُبُلًا فِجَاجًا

What is [the matter] with you that you do not attribute to Allah [due] grandeur, while He has created you in stages? Do you not consider how Allah has created seven heavens in layers, and made the moon therein a light and made the sun a burning lamp? And Allah has caused you to grow from the earth a [progressive] growth. Then He will return you into it and extract you [another] extraction. And Allah has made for you the earth an expanse, that you may follow therein roads of passage.

Nūḥ 71:13–20

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Scheme, the Idols, the Flood, and Nūḥ's Prayer (21–28)

قَالَ نُوحٌ رَّبِّ إِنَّهُمْ عَصَوْنِي وَاتَّبَعُوا مَن لَّمْ يَزِدْهُ مَالُهُ وَوَلَدُهُ إِلَّا خَسَارًا ۝ وَمَكَرُوا مَكْرًا كُبَّارًا ۝ وَقَالُوا لَا تَذَرُنَّ آلِهَتَكُمْ وَلَا تَذَرُنَّ وَدًّا وَلَا سُوَاعًا وَلَا يَغُوثَ وَيَعُوقَ وَنَسْرًا ۝ وَقَدْ أَضَلُّوا كَثِيرًا ۖ وَلَا تَزِدِ الظَّالِمِينَ إِلَّا ضَلَالًا ۝ مِّمَّا خَطِيئَاتِهِمْ أُغْرِقُوا فَأُدْخِلُوا نَارًا فَلَمْ يَجِدُوا لَهُم مِّن دُونِ اللَّهِ أَنصَارًا ۝ وَقَالَ نُوحٌ رَّبِّ لَا تَذَرْ عَلَى الْأَرْضِ مِنَ الْكَافِرِينَ دَيَّارًا ۝ إِنَّكَ إِن تَذَرْهُمْ يُضِلُّوا عِبَادَكَ وَلَا يَلِدُوا إِلَّا فَاجِرًا كَفَّارًا ۝ رَّبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيْتِيَ مُؤْمِنًا وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ وَلَا تَزِدِ الظَّالِمِينَ إِلَّا تَبَارًا

Nūḥ said: My Lord, indeed they have disobeyed me and followed him whose wealth and children increased him not except in loss. And they conspired an immense conspiracy, and said: never leave your gods — never leave Wadd, nor Suwāʿ, nor Yaghūth, and Yaʿūq, and Nasr. And already they have misled many; and, [my Lord], do not increase the wrongdoers except in error. Because of their sins they were drowned and put into the Fire, and they found not for themselves besides Allah [any] helpers. And Nūḥ said: My Lord, do not leave upon the earth from among the disbelievers an inhabitant — indeed, if You leave them, they will mislead Your servants and not beget except [every] wicked one and [confirmed] disbeliever. My Lord, forgive me and my parents and whoever enters my house a believer, and the believing men and believing women, and do not increase the wrongdoers except in destruction.

Nūḥ 71:21–28

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Nūḥ (عليه السلام) is the first of the five messengers given the title أُولُو الْعَزْم — those of firm resolve — and this sūrah is his own account of his daʿwah, given in his own words before his Lord. He calls his people to the single foundation of every message: عِبَادَة (worship) of Allah alone, تَقْوَى (fear of Him), and obedience to His messenger. Al-Saʿdī notes that Nūḥ's report to Allah — دَعَوْتُ قَوْمِي لَيْلًا وَنَهَارًا, I invited my people night and day — shows a caller who never rested, using every means: open calling before crowds (جِهَارًا), and quiet, personal calling in private (إِسْرَارًا). Yet the response was flight, covered ears, and arrogance — a lesson that rejection of the truth is not new, and does not mean the message was flawed.

اسْتِغْفَار وَالرِّزْق — Seeking Forgiveness and Provision

Nūḥ's central offer to his people links two things that seem unrelated at first: اسْتَغْفِرُوا رَبَّكُمْ إِنَّهُ كَانَ غَفَّارًا — seek forgiveness from your Lord, He is ever a Perpetual Forgiver — followed immediately by the promise of abundant rain, increased wealth and children, gardens, and rivers. Al-Saʿdī and other scholars of tafsīr have long pointed to this āyah as establishing that سُؤَال الْمَغْفِرَة (asking forgiveness) is a means Allah has placed for provision and relief to descend, not merely for the wiping of sin in the Hereafter. The link is not automatic magic — it is Allah's promise to those who turn back to Him.

خَلَقَكُمْ أَطْوَارًا — He created you in stages: Nūḥ reminds his people of their own creation — from a drop, to a clinging clot, to a lump, through stages known to every one of them, and yet they refuse to attribute وَقَار (grandeur, due reverence) to the One who made them. He then points them outward: seven heavens built in layers one above the other, the moon set as a reflected light and the sun as a blazing lamp, and finally back to the earth itself — made an expanse (بِسَاطًا) so mankind might travel its roads. Al-Saʿdī: every one of these is a plain, everyday sign that a caller can point to without needing any special proof beyond what is already in front of the listener.

أصل الشرك — The Origin of the Five Idols

وَدّ، سُوَاع، يَغُوث، يَعُوق، نَسْر — Wadd, Suwāʿ, Yaghūth, Yaʿūq, and Nasr — are named by Nūḥ's people as the gods they refuse to abandon. It is well known and reported generally from Ibn ʿAbbās (رضي الله عنهما) that these were originally the names of righteous men among Nūḥ's people. After they died, their communities — grieving and wishing to remember their good example — made images of them so people would recall their righteousness and be moved to worship as they had worshipped. Over the generations that followed, the true purpose of the images was forgotten, and the images themselves came to be venerated, then worshipped as gods besides Allah. This narration is one of the clearest lessons in the Qurʾān on how shirk can creep into a community gradually, even starting from an intention that was not itself evil — which is why the Prophet ﷺ was so strict in blocking any means that could lead to the veneration of graves or images of the righteous.

Nūḥ's Patience and Closing Prayer: Sūrat al-ʿAnkabūt (29:14) tells us Nūḥ remained among his people a thousand years less fifty — that is, roughly 950 years of calling — and only a small number believed. This sūrah shows us his own exhausted, grief-filled report of that calling to his Lord. After the flood drowned the obstinate disbelievers, Nūḥ's prayer closes the sūrah: رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيْتِيَ مُؤْمِنًا — forgive me, my parents, and whoever enters my house believing, and the believing men and women. Al-Saʿdī notes the breadth of this prayer — it does not stop at Nūḥ's own household, but reaches out to every believer, in every time, who would ever carry the name of īmān. It is a prayer every believer today is included within.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

فَقُلْتُ اسْتَغْفِرُوا رَبَّكُمْ إِنَّهُ كَانَ غَفَّارًا ۝ يُرْسِلِ السَّمَاءَ عَلَيْكُم مِّدْرَارًا ۝ وَيُمْدِدْكُم بِأَمْوَالٍ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ جَنَّاتٍ وَيَجْعَل لَّكُمْ أَنْهَارًا

Three āyāt every believer should carry: and I said, seek forgiveness from your Lord, indeed He is ever a Perpetual Forgiver — He will send [rain from] the sky upon you in abundance — and give you increase in wealth and children, and provide for you gardens, and provide for you rivers. Whenever provision feels tight or a matter feels stuck, this is the first door to knock: turn back to Allah in istighfār before anything else.
