سُورَةُ القَدْر

Sūrat al-Qadr — The Night of Decree

Objectives — أَهْدَافُ الدَّرْس

  • Understand the two reasons why this night is called لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْر.
  • Know what "better than a thousand months" means — and why al-Saʿdī calls it "utterly astounding."
  • Know what happens on this night: the Quran descended, decrees are set, angels descend, peace reigns until dawn.
  • Know when this night is and what the Prophet ﷺ did to seek it.
الآيات The Āyāt

إِنَّآ أَنزَلْنَٰهُ فِى لَيْلَةِ ٱلْقَدْرِ ۝ وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا لَيْلَةُ ٱلْقَدْرِ ۝ لَيْلَةُ ٱلْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ ۝ تَنَزَّلُ ٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ ۝ سَلَٰمٌ هِىَ حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ ٱلْفَجْرِ

Verily, We sent it [the Quran] down on the Night of Decree. And how could you know what the Night of Decree is? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months. On that night the angels and the Spirit [Jibreel] descend by their Lord's leave with all decrees. It is a night of peace and blessing until the break of dawn.

al-Qadr 97:1–5

التفسير Explanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

إِنَّا أَنزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ (Verily, We sent it down on the Night of Decree): Al-Saʿdī opens by noting this is said to highlight the virtue and high status of the Quran — not just what it says, but the occasion of its revelation. Allah began sending the Quran down in Ramadan on Laylat al-Qadr, and by it bestowed comprehensive mercy upon His slaves for which they cannot give sufficient thanks. It is also supported by al-Dukhān 44:3: إِنَّا أَنزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةٍ مُبَارَكَةٍ — We sent it down during a blessed night.

لماذا سُمِّيت ليلة القدر؟ — two reasons

Al-Saʿdī gives two reasons this night is named القدر:

(1) Its immense status and virtue before Allah — it is of great significance, which is why Allah says "and how could you know what it is?" — the rhetorical question signalling something beyond ordinary description.

(2) On this night He decrees what will happen during the coming year — deaths, provision, and all other divine decrees for the year ahead are written on this night. القَدَر = decree/measure.

خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْر Better than a thousand months

لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ: Al-Saʿdī explains: in its virtue, this night is equivalent to a thousand months — deeds performed on this night are better than deeds performed in a thousand months that do not contain it. One thousand months is approximately eighty-odd years — the lifespan of a person who lives to old age.

Al-Saʿdī says this is something utterly astounding and amazing: Allah has blessed this weak Ummah with a night on which good deeds are equivalent to and surpass a thousand months — the entire lifespan of a long-lived person. A person who worships on this night, year after year, earns deeds equivalent to hundreds of lifetimes.

ما يحدث في هَذِهِ الليلة What happens on this night

تَنَزَّلُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَالرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ (the angels and the Spirit descend by their Lord's leave with all decrees): The angels — frequently, in great numbers — descend along with Jibreel (الرُّوح), by Allah's permission, carrying all decrees of blessings. The entire heavens become alive with activity that night.

سَلَامٌ هِيَ حَتَّى مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ (it is peace and blessing until the break of dawn): Al-Saʿdī says: this night is free of all ills and evils — because of its great goodness. It begins at sunset and ends with the break of dawn. The entire night, from Maghrib to Fajr, is سَلَام — safety, peace, blessing.

متى هي؟ — when is Laylat al-Qadr?

Al-Saʿdī says: there are many mutawātir ḥadīths that speak of its virtue. What is established:

  • It is in Ramadan
  • It is in the last ten nights
  • It is especially likely on the odd-numbered nights
  • It will continue to occur every year until the onset of the Hour

Therefore the Prophet ﷺ used to observe iʿtikāf (seclusion in the masjid) and do a great deal of worship during the last ten days and nights of Ramadan, hoping to coincide with it.

Memorize & Revive — اِحْفَظْ وأحيِ

لَيْلَةُ ٱلْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ · سَلَٰمٌ هِىَ حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ ٱلْفَجْرِ

Al-Saʿdī calls this "utterly astounding" — Allah gave the weakest Ummah a night worth an entire lifetime of deeds. The family practice: in the last ten nights of Ramadan, stay up together. Pray. Read Quran. Make duʿāʾ. The Prophet ﷺ did iʿtikāf searching for this night. We should search too. One night caught = decades of deeds on the scale.
