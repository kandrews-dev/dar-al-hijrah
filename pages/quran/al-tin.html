Sūrah 95 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 8 āyātYear 1

سورة التين

Sūrat al-Tīn — The Fig

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what the three oaths refer to — why these three lands and which Prophets were sent from them.
  • Understand أَحْسَنِ تَقْوِيم — what does "best of conditions" mean?
  • Know what أَسْفَلَ سَافِلِين means and who is exempted.
  • Understand al-Saʿdī's argument: does Allah's wisdom permit leaving mankind without purpose and requital?
الآياتThe Āyāt

وَٱلتِّينِ وَٱلزَّيْتُونِ ۝ وَطُورِ سِينِينَ ۝ وَهَٰذَا ٱلْبَلَدِ ٱلْأَمِينِ ۝ لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ فِىٓ أَحْسَنِ تَقْوِيمٍ ۝ ثُمَّ رَدَدْنَٰهُ أَسْفَلَ سَٰفِلِينَ ۝ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمْ أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ ۝ فَمَا يُكَذِّبُكَ بَعْدُ بِٱلدِّينِ ۝ أَلَيْسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَحْكَمِ ٱلْحَٰكِمِينَ

By the fig and the olive — and by Mount Sinai — and by this secure city — verily We have created man in the best of conditions — then We reduce him to the lowest of the low — except those who believe and do righteous deeds; theirs is a never-ending reward. Then what makes you, O man, deny the Day of Judgement? Is not Allah the most just of judges?

al-Tīn 95:1–8

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

الثلاثة أقسام — three oaths, three holy lands

القسم Land Prophet sent from it
التِّين والزَّيْتُون The fig and olive — land of Syria (al-Shām) ʿĪsā ibn Maryam ﷺ
طُور سِينِين Mount Sinai Mūsā ﷺ
هَذَا الْبَلَد الْأَمِين This secure city — Makkah Muḥammad ﷺ

Al-Saʿdī says: Allah swears by these holy places which He chose and from which He sent the best and noblest of His Prophets.

لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْإِنسَانَ فِي أَحْسَنِ تَقْوِيمٍ (We created man in the best of conditions): Al-Saʿdī explains: in the best of ways, with limbs in proportion and an upright stance — lacking in nothing either inwardly or outwardly. Man is given the best physical form of all creatures, and the best inward qualities of reason and capacity. These are great blessings deserving gratitude.

ثُمَّ رَدَدْنَاهُ أَسْفَلَ سَافِلِينَ (then We reduce him to the lowest of the low): Despite these blessings, most people fail to give thanks. They are distracted by amusement and play, content with the trivial. Al-Saʿdī says: so Allah will reduce them to the lowest of the low — the lowest part of Hell — a place for sinners who rebel against their Lord.

إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ فَلَهُمْ أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ (except those who believe and do righteous deeds — a never-ending reward): They will attain high status, unceasing reward, constantly-available pleasures, joys coming one after another, blessings in abundance — eternal bliss that will never end; its food is perpetual, and so is its shade. غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ = never cut off, never interrupted.

أَلَيْسَ اللَّهُ بِأَحْكَمِ الْحَاكِمِينَ (Is not Allah the Wisest of the wise?): Al-Saʿdī poses the argument: does His wisdom dictate leaving humankind without purpose, with no commands or prohibitions, never to be rewarded or punished? Or will the One Who created them in stages, bestowed innumerable blessings, and took good care of them — inevitably bring them back to their final abode? The answer is obvious: Judgement Day is demanded by His wisdom.

Memorize & Reflect — احفظ وتأمّل

لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ فِىٓ أَحْسَنِ تَقْوِيمٍ · إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمْ أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ

Two outcomes for the best of creations: the lowest of the low, or a never-ending reward. Nothing in between stays neutral. The exception clause — except those who believe and do righteous deeds — is where this school sits. Every lesson learned, every prayer prayed, every good deed done is movement away from أَسْفَلَ سَافِلِين toward أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُون.
