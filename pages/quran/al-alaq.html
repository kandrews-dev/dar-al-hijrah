سورة العلق

Sūrat al-ʿAlaq — The Clinging Clot

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know that this is the first sūrah revealed — and the circumstances of the first revelation to the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Understand what اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ means and why the pen (القلم) is honoured.
  • Know the root disease described: man transgresses when he thinks himself self-sufficient (غَنِيًّا).
  • See the story of Abu Jahl trying to stop the Prophet ﷺ from praying — and the divine response.
الآياتThe Āyāt

ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ۝ خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ ۝ ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ ۝ ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ ۝ عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ ۝ كَلَّآ إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰ ۝ أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰ ۝ إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰ ۝ أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ ۝ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰ ۝ أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰ ۝ أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰ ۝ أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ ۝ أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ ۝ كَلَّا لَئِن لَّمْ يَنتَهِ لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ ۝ نَاصِيَةٍ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ ۝ فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ ۝ سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ ۝ كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب

Recite in the name of your Lord Who created — created man from a clinging clot. Recite, for your Lord is the Most Generous — Who taught the use of the pen — taught man what he knew not. Indeed man transgresses all bounds when he believes himself to be self-sufficient. Verily unto your Lord is the return. Have you seen the one who tries to stop a slave of Allah from praying? What if he is following guidance and enjoining righteousness? What if the one stopping him disbelieves and turns away — does he not realise that Allah sees? Nay; if he does not desist, We will surely drag him by his forelock — a lying, sinful forelock. So let him call his associates; We will call the angels of punishment. Nay; pay no heed to him — prostrate and draw near.

al-ʿAlaq 96:1–19

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

أول ما نزل من القرآن — the first revelation

Al-Saʿdī says: this sūrah was the first of the Quran to be revealed to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. It came at the very beginning of his prophethood, when he knew nothing about the Book or faith. Jibreel came with the message and commanded him to recite. The Prophet replied: «مَا أَنَا بِقَارِئٍ — I am not a reciter.» But Jibreel kept urging him until he recited. (Bukhārī)

اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ (Recite in the name of your Lord Who created): Allah singles out man — created from ʿalaq (a clinging clot) — to show that the One Who created man and cared for him must also care for him by issuing commands and prohibitions. Hence: Messenger and Book. Then: الَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِالْقَلَمِ — He taught the use of the pen, through which knowledge is preserved, rights and duties are regulated, and messages are exchanged. He gave man hearing, sight and intellect, and made available the means of acquiring knowledge.

كَلَّا إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ لَيَطْغَى أَن رَّآهُ اسْتَغْنَى (man transgresses when he thinks himself self-sufficient): Despite all these blessings — creation, knowledge, provision — when man thinks he has become independent of means, he transgresses and behaves arrogantly. He forgets the return to his Lord and does not fear punishment. He may even reject guidance himself and tell others not to pray. Then the reminder: إِنَّ إِلَى رَبِّكَ الرُّجْعَى — verily to your Lord is the return.

أَرَأَيْتَ الَّذِي يَنْهَى عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّى (Have you seen the one who tries to stop a slave from praying?): Revealed about Abu Jahl who tried to stop the Prophet ﷺ from praying in the Ḥaram. Al-Saʿdī says: is it appropriate to stop someone who is following true guidance and enjoining righteousness? Such a thing cannot be done except by someone drifting from guidance or telling others to do what is contrary to piety.

أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ اللَّهَ يَرَى (does he not realise that Allah sees?): A stark warning. Then: if he does not desist — لَنَسْفَعَن بِالنَّاصِيَة — We will drag him by his forelock. Then it is described: نَاصِيَةٍ كَاذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍ — a lying, sinful forelock, because he is lying in what he says and sinning in what he does. Let him call his associates — Allah will call the angels of punishment (الزَّبَانِيَة, the keepers of Hell).

كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَاسْجُدْ وَاقْتَرِب (pay no heed to him — prostrate and draw near): The answer to the one who is being told not to pray: ignore him completely. Prostrate — for prostration and all acts of worship bring one closer to Allah and nearer to His pleasure. Al-Saʿdī says this is general and applies to everyone who tells people not to worship, even though it was originally about Abu Jahl.

Memorize & Begin — احفظ وابدأ

اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ · كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَاسْجُدْ وَاقْتَرِب

The first word of revelation is اقْرَأْ — Read. And it ends with وَاسْجُدْ وَاقْتَرِب — prostrate and draw near. The whole religion lives between these two commands: seek knowledge, then humble yourself before the One who gave it. Whoever stops you from that — pay no heed.
