سورة القيامة

Sūrat al-Qiyāmah — The Resurrection

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the two things Allah swears by at the opening of this sūrah: the Day of Resurrection and النَّفْسُ اللَّوَّامَة (the reproaching soul), and how each points to the certainty of resurrection.
  • Understand the instruction given to the Prophet ﷺ not to hasten his tongue with the Qurʾān during revelation, and the etiquette of patience it teaches in receiving knowledge.
  • Know the description of radiant faces looking at their Lord, and understand this as the position of Ahl al-Sunnah that the believers will see Allah in the Hereafter.
  • Know the description of the moment of death given in this sūrah and its intended effect on the heart.
  • Know the closing proof of resurrection: the One who created man from a drop of fluid is able to give life to the dead.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Oath and the Certainty of Resurrection (1–15)

لَا أُقْسِمُ بِيَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ ۝ وَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِالنَّفْسِ اللَّوَّامَةِ ۝ أَيَحْسَبُ الْإِنسَانُ أَلَّن نَّجْمَعَ عِظَامَهُ ۝ بَلَى قَادِرِينَ عَلَى أَن نُّسَوِّيَ بَنَانَهُ ۝ بَلْ يُرِيدُ الْإِنسَانُ لِيَفْجُرَ أَمَامَهُ ۝ يَسْأَلُ أَيَّانَ يَوْمُ الْقِيَامَةِ ۝ فَإِذَا بَرِقَ الْبَصَرُ ۝ وَخَسَفَ الْقَمَرُ ۝ وَجُمِعَ الشَّمْسُ وَالْقَمَرُ ۝ يَقُولُ الْإِنسَانُ يَوْمَئِذٍ أَيْنَ الْمَفَرُّ ۝ كَلَّا لَا وَزَرَ ۝ إِلَى رَبِّكَ يَوْمَئِذٍ الْمُسْتَقَرُّ ۝ يُنَبَّأُ الْإِنسَانُ يَوْمَئِذٍ بِمَا قَدَّمَ وَأَخَّرَ ۝ بَلِ الْإِنسَانُ عَلَى نَفْسِهِ بَصِيرَةٌ ۝ وَلَوْ أَلْقَى مَعَاذِيرَهُ

I swear by the Day of Resurrection, and I swear by the reproaching soul [to the certainty of resurrection]. Does man think that We will not assemble his bones? Yes — able even to proportion his very fingertips. But man desires to persist in sin, asking, "When is the Day of Resurrection?" So when vision is dazzled, and the moon is eclipsed, and the sun and the moon are joined together, man will say on that Day, "Where is the escape?" No! There is no refuge. To your Lord, that Day, is the only resting place. Man will be informed that Day of everything he sent forward and left behind. Rather, man is a witness against himself, even if he presents his excuses.

al-Qiyāmah 75:1–15

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: Receiving the Qurʾān and the Two Kinds of Faces (16–25)

لَا تُحَرِّكْ بِهِ لِسَانَكَ لِتَعْجَلَ بِهِ ۝ إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا جَمْعَهُ وَقُرْآنَهُ ۝ فَإِذَا قَرَأْنَاهُ فَاتَّبِعْ قُرْآنَهُ ۝ ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا بَيَانَهُ ۝ كَلَّا بَلْ تُحِبُّونَ الْعَاجِلَةَ ۝ وَتَذَرُونَ الْآخِرَةَ ۝ وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ نَّاضِرَةٌ ۝ إِلَى رَبِّهَا نَاظِرَةٌ ۝ وَوُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ بَاسِرَةٌ ۝ تَظُنُّ أَن يُفْعَلَ بِهَا فَاقِرَةٌ

Do not move your tongue with it, [O Muhammad], to hasten with it. Indeed, upon Us is its collection and its recitation. So when We have recited it, then follow its recitation. Then indeed, upon Us is its clarification. No! But you love the immediate life, and leave behind the Hereafter. Some faces, that Day, will be radiant, looking at their Lord. And some faces, that Day, will be contorted, expecting that a back-breaking calamity will be done to them.

al-Qiyāmah 75:16–25

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Moment of Death and the Closing Proof (26–40)

كَلَّا إِذَا بَلَغَتِ التَّرَاقِيَ ۝ وَقِيلَ مَنْ رَاقٍ ۝ وَظَنَّ أَنَّهُ الْفِرَاقُ ۝ وَالْتَفَّتِ السَّاقُ بِالسَّاقِ ۝ إِلَى رَبِّكَ يَوْمَئِذٍ الْمَسَاقُ ۝ فَلَا صَدَّقَ وَلَا صَلَّى ۝ وَلَكِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّى ۝ ثُمَّ ذَهَبَ إِلَى أَهْلِهِ يَتَمَطَّى ۝ أَوْلَى لَكَ فَأَوْلَى ۝ ثُمَّ أَوْلَى لَكَ فَأَوْلَى ۝ أَيَحْسَبُ الْإِنسَانُ أَن يُتْرَكَ سُدًى ۝ أَلَمْ يَكُ نُطْفَةً مِّن مَّنِيٍّ يُمْنَى ۝ ثُمَّ كَانَ عَلَقَةً فَخَلَقَ فَسَوَّى ۝ فَجَعَلَ مِنْهُ الزَّوْجَيْنِ الذَّكَرَ وَالْأُنثَى ۝ أَلَيْسَ ذَلِكَ بِقَادِرٍ عَلَى أَن يُحْيِيَ الْمَوْتَى

No! When the soul has reached the collarbone, and it is said, "Who will cure him?" and he becomes certain that it is the parting, and the leg is wound about the leg — to your Lord, that Day, is the driving. For he had neither believed nor prayed, but denied and turned away, then went to his people, swaggering in pride. Woe to you, and woe! Then woe to you, and woe! Does man think that he will be left neglected? Was he not a drop of ejaculated semen? Then he was a clinging clot, and [Allah] created and proportioned him, making from him two mates, male and female. Is that [Creator] not able to give life to the dead?

al-Qiyāmah 75:26–40

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens with two oaths: by يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ (the Day of Resurrection) itself, and by النَّفْسِ اللَّوَّامَةِ, the reproaching soul. Al-Saʿdī: the reproaching soul is the soul that blames and reproaches itself — even the believer, after committing a sin, finds his own soul turning against him in regret. This inner experience of self-reproach is itself pointed to as a sign of the Day when every soul will reckon with itself in full. The sūrah then answers the disbeliever's doubt directly: does man think his scattered bones will never be reassembled? Yes — Allah is able to do something even more precise than that: restore the very fingertips, down to their fine, unique lines. Yet man does not really doubt Allah's power; he doubts because he wants to keep sinning without consequence, so he mockingly asks, "when is the Day of Resurrection?"

لَا تُحَرِّكْ بِهِ لِسَانَكَ — Patience in Receiving Knowledge

Āyāt 16–19 record an instruction given to the Prophet ﷺ during the very process of revelation. Eager not to forget any of it, he would move his tongue along with Jibrīl's recitation before it was finished. Allah instructed him: do not rush your tongue to hasten memorising it — its collection in your heart and its recitation are guaranteed by Us; so when it is recited to you, follow that recitation attentively, and its explanation, too, is upon Us. Al-Saʿdī: this is a foundational āyah on how the Qurʾān was preserved — its wording and its meaning both guaranteed by Allah — and it carries a lasting lesson in etiquette: patience in receiving knowledge, listening fully before responding or rushing to conclusions, rather than racing ahead of the one who is teaching.

وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ نَّاضِرَةٌ إِلَى رَبِّهَا نَاظِرَةٌ — Radiant Faces, Looking at Their Lord: After noting that man loves the immediate world over the Hereafter, the sūrah describes two kinds of faces on that Day. The first: radiant, looking at their Lord. This āyah is one of the clearest proofs Ahl al-Sunnah cite that the believers will see Allah with their own eyes in the Hereafter — a reality affirmed plainly, as it comes, without distortion and without asking "how." The second: faces that Day contorted, certain that a back-breaking calamity is about to befall them — the fear of the disbelievers as they await their reckoning.

The closing section gives one of the Qurʾān's most vivid descriptions of the moment of death: the soul reaching the collarbone, those gathered around asking desperately who can cure him, the dying person now certain that this is the final parting, hardship compounding upon hardship — وَالْتَفَّتِ السَّاقُ بِالسَّاقِ. Al-Saʿdī: this describes a man who denied and turned away in this life, then goes on to describe the double warning, أَوْلَى لَكَ فَأَوْلَى, repeated twice — a severe threat matching his own arrogance. The sūrah then returns, in its final āyāt, to the very question it opened with: does man think he will be left neglected, without purpose or accountability? It answers by pointing to his own beginning — a drop of fluid, then a clinging clot that Allah shaped and proportioned into male and female. The One who did all of that with no difficulty is certainly able to bring the dead back to life — closing the sūrah on the very proof it opened with.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

أَيَحْسَبُ الْإِنسَانُ أَن يُتْرَكَ سُدًى ۝ أَلَمْ يَكُ نُطْفَةً مِّن مَّنِيٍّ يُمْنَى ۝ ثُمَّ كَانَ عَلَقَةً فَخَلَقَ فَسَوَّى ۝ فَجَعَلَ مِنْهُ الزَّوْجَيْنِ الذَّكَرَ وَالْأُنثَى ۝ أَلَيْسَ ذَلِكَ بِقَادِرٍ عَلَى أَن يُحْيِيَ الْمَوْتَى

The sūrah's closing proof of resurrection. Does man think that he will be left neglected? — without purpose, without command, without account. Was he not a drop of ejaculated semen, then a clinging clot which He created and proportioned, making from it two mates, male and female? Every human being carries the proof of his own resurrection in the story of his own creation. Is that not able to give life to the dead? Yes indeed.
