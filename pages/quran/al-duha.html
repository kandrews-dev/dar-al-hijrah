Sūrah 93 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 11 āyātYear 1

سورة الضحى

Sūrat al-Ḍuḥā — The Morning Brightness

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the context: revelation paused for ~15 days; the disbelievers mocked "his Lord has forsaken him."
  • Understand the three things Allah reminds the Prophet ﷺ of from his past — and what each teaches us.
  • Know the three duties commanded at the end: how to treat orphans, beggars, and Allah's blessings.
  • Know al-Saʿdī's insight: negating something emphatically implies its perfect opposite.
الآياتThe Āyāt

وَٱلضُّحَىٰ ۝ وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا سَجَىٰ ۝ مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ ۝ وَلَلْءَاخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلْأُولَىٰ ۝ وَلَسَوْفَ يُعْطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضَىٰ ۝ أَلَمْ يَجِدْكَ يَتِيمًا فَـَٔاوَىٰ ۝ وَوَجَدَكَ ضَآلًّا فَهَدَىٰ ۝ وَوَجَدَكَ عَآئِلًا فَأَغْنَىٰ ۝ فَأَمَّا ٱلْيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقْهَرْ ۝ وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّآئِلَ فَلَا تَنْهَرْ ۝ وَأَمَّا بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثْ

By the morning brightness — and by the night when it grows still — your Lord has not forsaken you, nor does He hate you. And the hereafter will be better for you than this present life. And your Lord will surely give you so that you will be well pleased. Did He not find you an orphan and take care of you? And find you unaware of the path and guide you? And find you in need and make you independent? So as for the orphan — do not mistreat him. And as for the one who asks — do not speak harshly to him. And as for the blessing of your Lord — proclaim it.

al-Ḍuḥā 93:1–11

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

سبب النزول — Context of Revelation

Revelation paused for approximately fifteen days. The disbelievers of Makkah mocked the Prophet ﷺ saying: "His Lord has forsaken him and now hates him." This sūrah was revealed to reassure and comfort the Messenger of Allah ﷺ — and through him, to console every believer who feels abandoned during a period of hardship.

مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَى (Your Lord has not forsaken you, nor does He hate you): Al-Saʿdī says: the emphatic negative — مَا فَعَلَ (He has not done such-and-such) — does not simply deny the thing; it implies the perfect opposite. "He has not abandoned you" implies "He has always been caring for you." "He does not hate you" implies "He has always loved you." This is not a temporary consolation — it is a statement about Allah's permanent, ongoing care and love for His Messenger ﷺ since before prophethood.

وَلَلْآخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ لَكَ مِنَ الْأُولَى (the hereafter will be better for you than this life): Al-Saʿdī notes that the word آخرة could also mean "every future moment" — so his next state was always better than his previous one. His status kept rising, and Allah kept supporting him, guiding his affairs, and helping him against his enemies. And beyond worldly life: وَلَسَوْفَ يُعْطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضَى — your Lord will give you until you are well pleased; this is too great to describe except in these comprehensive words.

Allah then reminds the Prophet ﷺ of three blessings from his past — three times He found him in need and took care of him:

١

أَلَمْ يَجِدْكَ يَتِيمًا فَآوَى

Found you an orphan — and gave you refuge

His father and mother had died when he could not yet care for himself. Allah arranged his grandfather ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib to care for him; then when he died, his uncle Abu Ṭālib; then Allah supported him with the believers. Lesson: He who arranged refuge for His Prophet from infancy will not abandon him now.

٢

وَوَجَدَكَ ضَالًّا فَهَدَى

Found you unaware of the path — and guided you

The Prophet ﷺ always believed in tawheed, even before prophethood. But he did not know the path of Islam — its rulings and teachings — until Allah sent him revelation. Then He taught him what he did not know, and enabled him to attain the best of deeds and character.

٣

وَوَجَدَكَ عَائِلًا فَأَغْنَى

Found you in need — and made you independent

Al-Saʿdī: He found him poor, and then made him independent by what Allah enabled him to conquer of lands from which wealth and taxes came. The One Who took away these problems will take away all other problems.

Then Allah draws three duties from these three blessings:

فَأَمَّا الْيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقْهَرْ: Do not deal harshly with the orphan; do not feel impatient with him or scorn him. Rather honour him, give what you can, and treat him as you would like your child to be treated after you die.

وَأَمَّا السَّائِلَ فَلَا تَنْهَرْ: The سَائِل (one who asks) includes both one who asks for money AND one who asks for knowledge. Al-Saʿdī specifically notes: teachers are enjoined to treat learners kindly and gently, for this helps the learner achieve his goal and reflects kindness towards one who is striving to benefit people.

وَأَمَّا بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثْ: Proclaim the blessings of your Lord. Al-Saʿdī: this includes both spiritual and worldly blessings. Speaking of Allah's blessings prompts gratitude for them, and leads to love of the One Who bestowed them — for people are naturally inclined to love the one who does them favours.

Memorize & Apply — احفظ وطبّق

مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَى · وَلَسَوْفَ يُعْطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضَى

This sūrah was revealed to a man who was mocked, isolated, and whose revelation had gone silent for weeks. Allah's answer was not an explanation of the delay — it was a reminder of three past favors and a promise of a future the Prophet ﷺ couldn't yet see. Teaching practice for this family: when a child feels forgotten or unseen, read them these āyāt.
