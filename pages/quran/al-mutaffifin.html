سورة المطففين

Sūrat al-Muṭaffifīn — The Defrauders

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the warning for those who give short measure — and understand al-Saʿdī's extension of this to fairness in argument and debate.
  • Know the two record books: سجِّين (Sijjīn — constricted, the record of the wicked) and عليُّون (ʿIlliyyūn — high, the record of the righteous), and what each tells us.
  • Know the hadith about sin staining the heart (recorded by Aḥmad and al-Tirmidhī, authenticated by al-Albānī) and how repeated sin blinds a person to truth.
  • Understand the reversal on the Day of Judgment: those who mocked the believers in this world will be laughed at in the hereafter.
الآياتThe Āyāt

وَيْلٌ لِّلْمُطَفِّفِينَ ۝ الَّذِينَ إِذَا اكْتَالُوا عَلَى النَّاسِ يَسْتَوْفُونَ ۝ وَإِذَا كَالُوهُمْ أَو وَّزَنُوهُمْ يُخْسِرُونَ ۝ أَلَا يَظُنُّ أُولَٰئِكَ أَنَّهُم مَّبْعُوثُونَ ۝ لِيَوْمٍ عَظِيمٍ ۝ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ النَّاسُ لِرَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ كَلَّا إِنَّ كِتَابَ الْفُجَّارِ لَفِي سِجِّينٍ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا سِجِّينٌ ۝ كِتَابٌ مَّرْقُومٌ ۝ وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِّلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ الَّذِينَ يُكَذِّبُونَ بِيَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ وَمَا يُكَذِّبُ بِهِ إِلَّا كُلُّ مُعْتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ ۝ إِذَا تُتْلَىٰ عَلَيْهِ آيَاتُنَا قَالَ أَسَاطِيرُ الْأَوَّلِينَ ۝ كَلَّا بَلْ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُوا يَكْسِبُونَ ۝ كَلَّا إِنَّهُمْ عَن رَّبِّهِمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ لَّمَحْجُوبُونَ ۝ ثُمَّ إِنَّهُمْ لَصَالُو الْجَحِيمِ ۝ ثُمَّ يُقَالُ لَهُمْ هَٰذَا الَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِ تُكَذِّبُونَ ۝ كَلَّا إِنَّ كِتَابَ الْأَبْرَارِ لَفِي عِلِّيِّينَ ۝ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا عِلِّيُّونَ ۝ كِتَابٌ مَّرْقُومٌ ۝ يَشْهَدُهُ الْمُقَرَّبُونَ ۝ إِنَّ الْأَبْرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٍ ۝ عَلَى الْأَرَائِكِ يَنظُرُونَ ۝ تَعْرِفُ فِي وُجُوهِهِمْ نَضْرَةَ النَّعِيمِ ۝ يُسْقَوْنَ مِن رَّحِيقٍ مَّخْتُومٍ ۝ خِتَامُهُ مِسْكٌ ۖ وَفِي ذَٰلِكَ فَلْيَتَنَافَسِ الْمُتَنَافِسُونَ ۝ وَمِزَاجُهُ مِن تَسْنِيمٍ ۝ عَيْنًا يَشْرَبُ بِهَا الْمُقَرَّبُونَ ۝ إِنَّ الَّذِينَ أَجْرَمُوا كَانُوا مِنَ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا يَضْحَكُونَ ۝ وَإِذَا مَرُّوا بِهِمْ يَتَغَامَزُونَ ۝ وَإِذَا انقَلَبُوا إِلَىٰ أَهْلِهِمُ انقَلَبُوا فَكِهِينَ ۝ وَإِذَا رَأَوْهُمْ قَالُوا إِنَّ هَٰؤُلَاءِ لَضَالُّونَ ۝ وَمَا أُرْسِلُوا عَلَيْهِمْ حَافِظِينَ ۝ فَالْيَوْمَ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنَ الْكُفَّارِ يَضْحَكُونَ ۝ عَلَى الْأَرَائِكِ يَنظُرُونَ ۝ هَلْ ثُوِّبَ الْكُفَّارُ مَا كَانُوا يَفْعَلُونَ

Woe to those who give short measure — who, when they take by measure from people, demand it in full, but if they measure or weigh for others, give them less than their due. Do these people not realise that they will be resurrected on a momentous day — a day on which humanity will stand before the Lord of the worlds? Indeed the record of the wicked is in Sijjīn — and how could you know what Sijjīn is? — a clearly inscribed record. Woe that day to the deniers, those who deny the Day of Judgement — no one denies it except every transgressor steeped in sin. When Our revelations are recited to him, he says: tales of the ancients! Nay — their hearts have been covered with the stain of their misdeeds. Nay — on that day they will be barred from seeing their Lord. Then they will surely enter the blazing fire, and it will be said: this is what you used to deny. Indeed the record of the righteous is in ʿIlliyyūn — and how could you know what ʿIlliyyūn is? — a clearly inscribed record, witnessed by those brought near to Allah. Verily the righteous will be in bliss, seated on couches gazing in wonder. You will see on their faces the radiance of bliss. They will be given to drink pure wine from a sealed container, of which the seal is musk — for that, let all who have aspirations compete! — mixed with water from Tasneem, a spring from which those close to Allah will drink. Verily the evildoers used to laugh at those who believed; when they passed by them, they would wink at one another in mockery; when they returned to their homes, they returned exulting; when they saw the believers, they said: these people have indeed gone astray — though they were not sent to be their keepers. But on that day those who believed will laugh at the disbelievers, while sitting on couches gazing around. Are the disbelievers requited for anything other than what they used to do?

al-Muṭaffifīn 83:1–36

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Allah opens with a severe warning: وَيْلٌ لِلْمُطَفِّفِينَ — Woe to the short-measurers. They are those who, when receiving from others, demand every last bit — but when giving to others, cheat and give less. This is theft of people's wealth and is being unfair to them. Al-Saʿdī notes that the general meaning extends beyond commerce: in argument and debate too, a person usually wants their own view heard in full, and by the same token they must also point out any proof that supports their opponent's argument. Fairness in discourse is included in this principle. What makes people bold enough to give short measure? Their lack of belief in the Last Day — if they truly believed they would stand before Allah and be asked about every small and great deed, they would stop immediately.

الرَّان — The Stain on the Heart

The Prophet ﷺ said: "If the believer commits a sin, a black spot appears on his heart. If he repents, gives it up and seeks forgiveness, his heart is cleansed. But if he does more, it increases until it covers his heart — that is the stain which Allah mentioned: 'their hearts have been covered with the stain of their misdeeds.'" (Recorded by Aḥmad and al-Tirmidhī; authenticated by al-Albānī)

When the heart is covered by this stain, facts are turned upside down: falsehood looks like truth, and truth looks like falsehood. This is one of the punishments for sin itself — before the hereafter even arrives. The disbeliever hears the Quran — clear, undeniable proof — and says: "tales of the ancients." His heart cannot process it anymore.

سجِّين vs. عليُّون — Two Record Books: Sijjīn is the record of the wicked — kept in the lowest, most constricted place. ʿIlliyyūn is the record of the righteous — kept in the highest and most spacious place, with the noble angels and the souls of the Prophets, the strong in faith, and the martyrs. Allah declares the renown of the righteous among those on high. The record itself being elevated indicates that their deeds are raised and honoured before Allah.

Three punishments for the wicked on that Day: (1) the punishment of hell; (2) the punishment of blame and rebuke — "this is what you used to deny"; (3) being veiled from seeing their Lord, which al-Saʿdī says is worse for them than the fire, because it implies His anger. This also confirms — by clear logical implication — that the believers will see their Lord on that Day and in paradise, and the joy of gazing upon His Countenance will be greater than all other pleasures, as is established by mutawātir reports.

The Reversal — المحاكاة بالعكس: In this world, the evildoers laughed at the believers, winked in mockery, returned home exulting, and called them misguided — though they were not appointed as their keepers and had no evidence for their claims. In the hereafter, the believers will laugh at them while seated on adorned couches, gazing at bliss. هَلْ ثُوِّبَ الْكُفَّارُ مَا كَانُوا يَفْعَلُونَ — Are the disbelievers requited for anything other than what they used to do?

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

كَلَّا بَلْ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُوا يَكْسِبُونَ · وَفِي ذَٰلِكَ فَلْيَتَنَافَسِ الْمُتَنَافِسُونَ

Two of the most important āyāt in this sūrah. The first is a warning: every sin is a stain. Guard your heart by repenting immediately — don't let the spots accumulate until the heart is covered. The second is a call to ambition: the sealed wine of paradise, its seal of musk, the spring of Tasneem — for THAT, let those who have ambitions compete. Channel your competitive instinct toward the hereafter.
