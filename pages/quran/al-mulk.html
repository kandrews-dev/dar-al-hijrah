سورة الملك

Sūrat al-Mulk — The Sovereignty

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the meaning of تَبَارَكَ الَّذِي بِيَدِهِ الْمُلْكُ and that death and life were created as a test of which of us is best in deed.
  • Know the description of the seven heavens and the challenge to search the creation for any flaw or inconsistency.
  • Know what the keepers of Hell ask every group thrown into it, and how the deniers will admit their sin.
  • Know the sign of the birds held aloft by none but الرَّحْمَن, and what it teaches about tawakkul (reliance upon Allah).
  • Know the well-known, authentically reported virtue of this sūrah as a protector and intercessor for its reciter.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: Blessed Is He in Whose Hand Is Dominion (1–5)

تَبَارَكَ الَّذِي بِيَدِهِ الْمُلْكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ ۝ الَّذِي خَلَقَ الْمَوْتَ وَالْحَيَاةَ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْغَفُورُ ۝ الَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ طِبَاقًا ۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِي خَلْقِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ مِن تَفَاوُتٍ ۖ فَارْجِعِ الْبَصَرَ هَلْ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٍ ۝ ثُمَّ ارْجِعِ الْبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيْنِ يَنقَلِبْ إِلَيْكَ الْبَصَرُ خَاسِئًا وَهُوَ حَسِيرٌ ۝ وَلَقَدْ زَيَّنَّا السَّمَاءَ الدُّنْيَا بِمَصَابِيحَ وَجَعَلْنَاهَا رُجُومًا لِّلشَّيَاطِينِ ۖ وَأَعْتَدْنَا لَهُمْ عَذَابَ السَّعِيرِ

Blessed is He in whose hand is dominion, and He is over all things competent — [He] who created death and life to test you [as to] which of you is best in deed, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Forgiving — [and] who created seven heavens in layers. You will not see in the creation of the Most Merciful any inconsistency, so return your vision [to the sky] — do you see any breaks? Then return your vision twice again; your vision will return to you humbled while it is fatigued. And We have certainly beautified the nearest heaven with lamps and made them missiles against the devils, and have prepared for them the punishment of the Blaze.

al-Mulk 67:1–5

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Punishment of the Deniers and the Signs on Earth and Sky (6–19)

وَلِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا بِرَبِّهِمْ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ ۖ وَبِئْسَ الْمَصِيرُ ۝ إِذَا أُلْقُوا فِيهَا سَمِعُوا لَهَا شَهِيقًا وَهِيَ تَفُورُ ۝ تَكَادُ تَمَيَّزُ مِنَ الْغَيْظِ ۖ كُلَّمَا أُلْقِيَ فِيهَا فَوْجٌ سَأَلَهُمْ خَزَنَتُهَا أَلَمْ يَأْتِكُمْ نَذِيرٌ ۝ قَالُوا بَلَىٰ قَدْ جَاءَنَا نَذِيرٌ فَكَذَّبْنَا وَقُلْنَا مَا نَزَّلَ اللَّهُ مِن شَيْءٍ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَالٍ كَبِيرٍ ۝ وَقَالُوا لَوْ كُنَّا نَسْمَعُ أَوْ نَعْقِلُ مَا كُنَّا فِي أَصْحَابِ السَّعِيرِ ۝ فَاعْتَرَفُوا بِذَنبِهِمْ فَسُحْقًا لِّأَصْحَابِ السَّعِيرِ ۝ إِنَّ الَّذِينَ يَخْشَوْنَ رَبَّهُم بِالْغَيْبِ لَهُم مَّغْفِرَةٌ وَأَجْرٌ كَبِيرٌ ۝ وَأَسِرُّوا قَوْلَكُمْ أَوِ اجْهَرُوا بِهِ ۖ إِنَّهُ عَلِيمٌ بِذَاتِ الصُّدُورِ ۝ أَلَا يَعْلَمُ مَنْ خَلَقَ وَهُوَ اللَّطِيفُ الْخَبِيرُ ۝ هُوَ الَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ الْأَرْضَ ذَلُولًا فَامْشُوا فِي مَنَاكِبِهَا وَكُلُوا مِن رِّزْقِهِ ۖ وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ ۝ أَأَمِنتُم مَّن فِي السَّمَاءِ أَن يَخْسِفَ بِكُمُ الْأَرْضَ فَإِذَا هِيَ تَمُورُ ۝ أَمْ أَمِنتُم مَّن فِي السَّمَاءِ أَن يُرْسِلَ عَلَيْكُمْ حَاصِبًا ۖ فَسَتَعْلَمُونَ كَيْفَ نَذِيرِ ۝ وَلَقَدْ كَذَّبَ الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ فَكَيْفَ كَانَ نَكِيرِ ۝ أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا إِلَى الطَّيْرِ فَوْقَهُمْ صَافَّاتٍ وَيَقْبِضْنَ ۚ مَا يُمْسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا الرَّحْمَٰنُ ۚ إِنَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ بَصِيرٌ

For those who disbelieve in their Lord is the punishment of Hell, and wretched is the destination. When they are thrown into it, they hear from it a dreadful inhaling while it boils up, almost bursting with rage. Every time a group is thrown into it, its keepers ask them, "Did there not come to you a warner?" They say, "Yes, a warner had come to us, but we denied and said, 'Allah has not sent down anything — you are not but in great error.'" And they say, "If only we had listened or reasoned, we would not be among the companions of the Blaze." So they will admit their sin — away with the companions of the Blaze. Indeed those who fear their Lord unseen will have forgiveness and great reward. Whether you conceal your speech or publicize it, He is Knowing of that within the breasts. Does He who created not know, while He is the Subtle, the Acquainted? It is He who made the earth tame for you — so walk among its slopes and eat of His provision — and to Him is the resurrection. Do you feel secure that He who is above the heaven would not cause the earth to swallow you, and suddenly it would sway? Or do you feel secure that He who is above the heaven would not send against you a storm of stones? Then you would know how [severe] was My warning. And already those before them denied, and how [terrible] was My reproach. Do they not see the birds above them, spreading their wings and folding them in? None holds them up except the Most Merciful — indeed He is, of all things, Seeing.

al-Mulk 67:6–19

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: Who Can Help You but Allah? (20–30)

أَمَّنْ هَٰذَا الَّذِي هُوَ جُندٌ لَّكُمْ يَنصُرُكُم مِّن دُونِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ ۚ إِنِ الْكَافِرُونَ إِلَّا فِي غُرُورٍ ۝ أَمَّنْ هَٰذَا الَّذِي يَرْزُقُكُمْ إِنْ أَمْسَكَ رِزْقَهُ ۚ بَل لَّجُّوا فِي عُتُوٍّ وَنُفُورٍ ۝ أَفَمَن يَمْشِي مُكِبًّا عَلَىٰ وَجْهِهِ أَهْدَىٰ أَمَّن يَمْشِي سَوِيًّا عَلَىٰ صِرَاطٍ مُّسْتَقِيمٍ ۝ قُلْ هُوَ الَّذِي أَنشَأَكُمْ وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ السَّمْعَ وَالْأَبْصَارَ وَالْأَفْئِدَةَ ۖ قَلِيلًا مَّا تَشْكُرُونَ ۝ قُلْ هُوَ الَّذِي ذَرَأَكُمْ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَإِلَيْهِ تُحْشَرُونَ ۝ وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا الْوَعْدُ إِن كُنتُمْ صَادِقِينَ ۝ قُلْ إِنَّمَا الْعِلْمُ عِندَ اللَّهِ وَإِنَّمَا أَنَا نَذِيرٌ مُّبِينٌ ۝ فَلَمَّا رَأَوْهُ زُلْفَةً سِيئَتْ وُجُوهُ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا وَقِيلَ هَٰذَا الَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِ تَدَّعُونَ ۝ قُلْ أَرَأَيْتُمْ إِنْ أَهْلَكَنِيَ اللَّهُ وَمَن مَّعِيَ أَوْ رَحِمَنَا فَمَن يُجِيرُ الْكَافِرِينَ مِنْ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ ۝ قُلْ هُوَ الرَّحْمَٰنُ آمَنَّا بِهِ وَعَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْنَا ۖ فَسَتَعْلَمُونَ مَنْ هُوَ فِي ضَلَالٍ مُّبِينٍ ۝ قُلْ أَرَأَيْتُمْ إِنْ أَصْبَحَ مَاؤُكُمْ غَوْرًا فَمَن يَأْتِيكُم بِمَاءٍ مَّعِينٍ

Who is it that could be an army for you to aid you other than the Most Merciful? The disbelievers are not but in delusion. Or who is it that could provide for you if He withheld His provision? But they have persisted in insolence and aversion. Then is one who walks fallen on his face better guided, or one who walks erect on a straight path? Say, "It is He who produced you and made for you hearing and vision and hearts — little are you grateful." Say, "It is He who multiplied you throughout the earth, and to Him you will be gathered." And they say, "When is this promise, if you should be truthful?" Say, "The knowledge is only with Allah, and I am only a clear warner." But when they see it approaching, the faces of those who disbelieve will be distressed, and it will be said, "This is that for which you used to call." Say, "Have you considered: whether Allah should cause my death and those with me, or have mercy upon us — who can protect the disbelievers from a painful punishment?" Say, "He is the Most Merciful; we have believed in Him, and upon Him we have relied. And you will come to know who it is that is in clear error." Say, "Have you considered — if your water was to become sunken [into the earth], then who could bring you flowing water?"

al-Mulk 67:20–30

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens with تَبَارَكَ الَّذِي بِيَدِهِ الْمُلْكُ — Blessed is He in whose hand is dominion. Al-Saʿdī: تَبَارَكَ describes the abundance and permanence of Allah's blessing, greatness, and goodness — He alone possesses complete sovereignty over all creation, and He is able to do whatever He wills. The very next āyah gives the purpose of this present life: الَّذِي خَلَقَ الْمَوْتَ وَالْحَيَاةَ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًا — who created death and life to test you as to which of you is best in deed. Notice the āyah does not say "most" in deed, but "best" — أَحْسَنُ — meaning the deed most sincerely and correctly performed, not merely the most numerous. This is the purpose of the whole of worldly life summarized in a single line: a test, not a destination.

قدرة الخالق — Power in Creation, Proof of Ability to Punish or Resurrect

The central movement of this sūrah alternates between describing Allah's mastery over creation and warning the heedless — because every proof of His power to create is equally a proof of His power to punish and to resurrect. The seven heavens built in perfect layers, with no flaw however long one searches the sky, show a Creator of total precision. The birds suspended in flight, held aloft by nothing visible — مَا يُمْسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا الرَّحْمَٰنُ, none holds them up except the Most Merciful — is one of the simplest, most repeatable signs available to anyone who looks up: something with weight, staying aloft, by an unseen power alone. Al-Saʿdī draws the same lesson from the earth being made ذَلُولًا (tame, manageable) for walking and cultivation: the same Lord who tamed the earth beneath your feet could just as easily cause it to swallow you, or send a storm of stones, exactly as He destroyed the nations before. Every mercy shown is also a warning: the One who can give can also withhold, and the One who created life and death is fully able to resurrect.

سُؤَال خَزَنَةِ جَهَنَّم — The Question of Hell's Keepers: Every time a group of deniers is thrown into Hell, its keepers ask them أَلَمْ يَأْتِكُمْ نَذِيرٌ — did there not come to you a warner? Al-Saʿdī: this question is asked not because the keepers lack knowledge, but to make the deniers themselves confess and admit that the warning did reach them and that they rejected it with full awareness — they answer بَلَىٰ, yes, it came, but we denied. Their own admission — لَوْ كُنَّا نَسْمَعُ أَوْ نَعْقِلُ, if only we had listened or reasoned — shows their loss was not from lack of evidence but from refusing to use the hearing and intellect Allah gave them. This is contrasted immediately with the believers: إِنَّ الَّذِينَ يَخْشَوْنَ رَبَّهُم بِالْغَيْبِ لَهُم مَّغْفِرَةٌ وَأَجْرٌ كَبِيرٌ — those who fear their Lord unseen (without needing to see Him or the punishment directly) have forgiveness and great reward. Fearing Allah in private, when no one is watching, is the mark distinguishing sincere īmān from outward show.

The sūrah closes with a simple, humbling question: أَرَأَيْتُمْ إِنْ أَصْبَحَ مَاؤُكُمْ غَوْرًا فَمَن يَأْتِيكُم بِمَاءٍ مَّعِينٍ — have you considered: if your water was to sink away into the earth, who could bring you flowing water? Al-Saʿdī: water is among the most basic necessities of life, freely available and easily taken for granted, yet entirely beyond human power to create or restore once its natural source is lost. No wealth or technology could force it back up if Allah willed otherwise. The sūrah's final image mirrors its opening — the One in whose hand is all dominion is the same One who provides this most basic mercy, moment to moment, without anyone even noticing.

فضل السورة — The Virtue of Sūrat al-Mulk

Sūrat al-Mulk has a well-known and authentically reported virtue: it is narrated in the collections of Abū Dāwūd and al-Tirmidhī, graded ṣaḥīḥ by Shaykh al-Albānī, that the Prophet ﷺ used to recite it every night before sleeping, and that it is a protector and intercessor for its reciter, pleading on his behalf until he is forgiven. For this reason it is also known as الْمُنْجِيَة (the Saviour) and الْوَاقِيَة (the Protector). A short, regular habit of reciting and reflecting on these thirty āyāt before sleep connects the last moments of the day to the greatest realities: Allah's total dominion, the nearness of death, and complete reliance upon Him alone.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

تَبَارَكَ الَّذِي بِيَدِهِ الْمُلْكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ ۝ الَّذِي خَلَقَ الْمَوْتَ وَالْحَيَاةَ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْغَفُورُ

Two āyāt that open the sūrah and summarize the purpose of this entire worldly life. Blessed is He in whose hand is dominion, and He is over all things competent — a declaration of Allah's complete sovereignty and ability. Who created death and life to test you as to which of you is best in deed — the single sentence that explains why we are here at all: not to accumulate, but to be tested in the quality of our deeds — and He is the Exalted in Might, the Forgiving, able to punish, yet ever ready to forgive whoever turns back to Him.
