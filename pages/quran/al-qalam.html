Sūrah 68 · Juzʾ 29 · Makki · 52 āyātYear 2

سورة القلم

Sūrat al-Qalam — The Pen

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the occasion of the oath بِالْقَلَمِ (by the pen) and how it opens the sūrah in defence of the Prophet ﷺ against the accusation of madness.
  • Know the description of the Prophet ﷺ as being upon خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ (a great moral character), and why this praise is among the greatest given to him in the Qurʾān.
  • Know the parable of أَصْحَابُ الْجَنَّةِ (the People of the Garden) and the lesson it teaches about charity to the poor and saying إِن شَاءَ اللَّه.
  • Know the brief reference to صَاحِبُ الْحُوتِ (the companion of the fish) and the command to the Prophet ﷺ to be patient, unlike him.
  • Know that the disbelievers' hostility — even their intense stares — does not change that the Qurʾān is nothing but a reminder to all the worlds.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Oath by the Pen and the Great Character (1–16)

نٓ ۚ وَالْقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسْطُرُونَ ۝ مَا أَنتَ بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ بِمَجْنُونٍ ۝ وَإِنَّ لَكَ لَأَجْرًا غَيْرَ مَمْنُونٍ ۝ وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ ۝ فَسَتُبْصِرُ وَيُبْصِرُونَ ۝ بِأَييِّكُمُ الْمَفْتُونُ ۝ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِ وَهُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِالْمُهْتَدِينَ ۝ فَلَا تُطِعِ الْمُكَذِّبِينَ ۝ وَدُّوا لَوْ تُدْهِنُ فَيُدْهِنُونَ ۝ وَلَا تُطِعْ كُلَّ حَلَّافٍ مَّهِينٍ ۝ هَمَّازٍ مَّشَّاءٍ بِنَمِيمٍ ۝ مَّنَّاعٍ لِّلْخَيْرِ مُعْتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ ۝ عُتُلٍّ بَعْدَ ذَٰلِكَ زَنِيمٍ ۝ أَن كَانَ ذَا مَالٍ وَبَنِينَ ۝ إِذَا تُتْلَىٰ عَلَيْهِ آيَاتُنَا قَالَ أَسَاطِيرُ الْأَوَّلِينَ ۝ سَنَسِمُهُ عَلَى الْخُرْطُومِ

Nūn. By the pen and what they inscribe — you [O Muhammad] are not, by the favor of your Lord, a madman. And indeed for you is a reward uninterrupted, and indeed you are of a great moral character. So you will see and they will see which of you is afflicted [by a devil]. Indeed your Lord is most knowing of who has strayed from His way, and He is most knowing of the guided. So do not obey the deniers. They wish that you would soften [your stance], so they would soften [toward you]. And do not obey every worthless habitual swearer, scorner going about with malicious gossip, preventer of good, transgressing and sinful, cruel and moreover base-born — merely because he is a possessor of wealth and children. When Our verses are recited to him, he says, "Legends of the former peoples." We will brand him upon the snout.

al-Qalam 68:1–16

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Parable of the People of the Garden (17–34)

إِنَّا بَلَوْنَاهُمْ كَمَا بَلَوْنَا أَصْحَابَ الْجَنَّةِ إِذْ أَقْسَمُوا لَيَصْرِمُنَّهَا مُصْبِحِينَ ۝ وَلَا يَسْتَثْنُونَ ۝ فَطَافَ عَلَيْهَا طَائِفٌ مِّن رَّبِّكَ وَهُمْ نَائِمُونَ ۝ فَأَصْبَحَتْ كَالصَّرِيمِ ۝ فَتَنَادَوْا مُصْبِحِينَ ۝ أَنِ اغْدُوا عَلَىٰ حَرْثِكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ صَارِمِينَ ۝ فَانطَلَقُوا وَهُمْ يَتَخَافَتُونَ ۝ أَن لَّا يَدْخُلَنَّهَا الْيَوْمَ عَلَيْكُم مِّسْكِينٌ ۝ وَغَدَوْا عَلَىٰ حَرْدٍ قَادِرِينَ ۝ فَلَمَّا رَأَوْهَا قَالُوا إِنَّا لَضَالُّونَ ۝ بَلْ نَحْنُ مَحْرُومُونَ ۝ قَالَ أَوْسَطُهُمْ أَلَمْ أَقُل لَّكُمْ لَوْلَا تُسَبِّحُونَ ۝ قَالُوا سُبْحَانَ رَبِّنَا إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَالِمِينَ ۝ فَأَقْبَلَ بَعْضُهُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍ يَتَلَاوَمُونَ ۝ قَالُوا يَا وَيْلَنَا إِنَّا كُنَّا طَاغِينَ ۝ عَسَىٰ رَبُّنَا أَن يُبْدِلَنَا خَيْرًا مِّنْهَا إِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا رَاغِبُونَ ۝ كَذَٰلِكَ الْعَذَابُ ۖ وَلَعَذَابُ الْآخِرَةِ أَكْبَرُ ۚ لَوْ كَانُوا يَعْلَمُونَ ۝ إِنَّ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ جَنَّاتِ النَّعِيمِ

Indeed We have tried them as We tried the companions of the garden, when they swore to cut its fruit in the [early] morning, without making exception [for the will of Allah]. So there came upon the garden an affliction from your Lord while they were asleep, and it became as though [already] reaped. And they called one another at morning: "go early to your crop if you intend to cut the fruit." So they set out while lowering their voices: "let no poor person enter it upon you today" — and they went early in determination, assuming themselves able. But when they saw it, they said, "Indeed we are lost — rather, we have been deprived." The most moderate among them said, "Did I not say to you, why do you not exalt [Allah]?" They said, "Exalted is our Lord — indeed we were wrongdoers." Then they approached one another, blaming each other. They said, "O woe to us — indeed we were transgressors. Perhaps our Lord will substitute for us [one] better than it; indeed we are desirous toward our Lord." Such is the punishment [of this world] — and the punishment of the Hereafter is greater, if only they knew. Indeed for the righteous with their Lord are the Gardens of Pleasure.

al-Qalam 68:17–34

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Day of Uncovering and the Command to Patience (35–52)

أَفَنَجْعَلُ الْمُسْلِمِينَ كَالْمُجْرِمِينَ ۝ مَا لَكُمْ كَيْفَ تَحْكُمُونَ ۝ أَمْ لَكُمْ كِتَابٌ فِيهِ تَدْرُسُونَ ۝ إِنَّ لَكُمْ فِيهِ لَمَا تَخَيَّرُونَ ۝ أَمْ لَكُمْ أَيْمَانٌ عَلَيْنَا بَالِغَةٌ إِلَىٰ يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ ۙ إِنَّ لَكُمْ لَمَا تَحْكُمُونَ ۝ سَلْهُمْ أَيُّهُم بِذَٰلِكَ زَعِيمٌ ۝ أَمْ لَهُمْ شُرَكَاءُ فَلْيَأْتُوا بِشُرَكَائِهِمْ إِن كَانُوا صَادِقِينَ ۝ يَوْمَ يُكْشَفُ عَن سَاقٍ وَيُدْعَوْنَ إِلَى السُّجُودِ فَلَا يَسْتَطِيعُونَ ۝ خَاشِعَةً أَبْصَارُهُمْ تَرْهَقُهُمْ ذِلَّةٌ ۖ وَقَدْ كَانُوا يُدْعَوْنَ إِلَى السُّجُودِ وَهُمْ سَالِمُونَ ۝ فَذَرْنِي وَمَن يُكَذِّبُ بِهَٰذَا الْحَدِيثِ ۖ سَنَسْتَدْرِجُهُم مِّنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ ۝ وَأُمْلِي لَهُمْ ۚ إِنَّ كَيْدِي مَتِينٌ ۝ أَمْ تَسْأَلُهُمْ أَجْرًا فَهُم مِّن مَّغْرَمٍ مُّثْقَلُونَ ۝ أَمْ عِندَهُمُ الْغَيْبُ فَهُمْ يَكْتُبُونَ ۝ فَاصْبِرْ لِحُكْمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تَكُن كَصَاحِبِ الْحُوتِ إِذْ نَادَىٰ وَهُوَ مَكْظُومٌ ۝ لَّوْلَا أَن تَدَارَكَهُ نِعْمَةٌ مِّن رَّبِّهِ لَنُبِذَ بِالْعَرَاءِ وَهُوَ مَذْمُومٌ ۝ فَاجْتَبَاهُ رَبُّهُ فَجَعَلَهُ مِنَ الصَّالِحِينَ ۝ وَإِن يَكَادُ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا لَيُزْلِقُونَكَ بِأَبْصَارِهِمْ لَمَّا سَمِعُوا الذِّكْرَ وَيَقُولُونَ إِنَّهُ لَمَجْنُونٌ ۝ وَمَا هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكْرٌ لِّلْعَالَمِينَ

Then shall We treat the Muslims like the criminals? What is [the matter] with you — how do you judge? Or do you have a scripture in which you learn that indeed for you is whatever you choose? Or do you have oaths [binding] upon Us, extending until the Day of Resurrection, that indeed for you is whatever you judge? Ask them which of them, for that [claim], is responsible. Or do they have partners? Then let them bring their partners, if they should be truthful. [Consider] the Day the shin will be uncovered and they are invited to prostration but the disbelievers will not be able — their eyes humbled, humiliation covering them, for they used to be invited to prostration while they were sound. So leave Me [O Muhammad] with whoever denies this statement; We will progressively lead them [to punishment] from where they do not know. And I will give them time — indeed My plan is firm. Or do you ask of them a payment, so they are by debt burdened down? Or do they have [knowledge of] the unseen, so they write it down? So be patient for the decision of your Lord, and be not like the companion of the fish, when he called out while he was distressed. Had it not been that a favor from his Lord overtook him, he would have been thrown onto the naked shore while he was blameworthy — but his Lord chose him and made him of the righteous. And indeed those who disbelieve would almost make you slip with their eyes when they hear the message, and they say, "Indeed he is mad." But it is nothing except a reminder to the worlds.

al-Qalam 68:35–52

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens with the letter نٓ (Nūn) — one of the disjointed letters that open several sūrahs, whose full meaning Allah alone knows fully, though scholars note they demonstrate the miraculous nature of the Qurʾān: composed from the same simple letters the Arabs used daily, yet inimitable. Allah then swears وَالْقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسْطُرُونَ — by the pen and what they inscribe. Al-Saʿdī: the pen is one of the greatest blessings of Allah upon mankind — through it knowledge is recorded, preserved, and transmitted across generations; without it, the sciences of the dīn and the world would be lost. Allah swears by it and by what is written with it to establish the truth of what follows: a direct defence of the Prophet ﷺ against the disbelievers' accusation that he was مَجْنُون (mad) — a claim contradicted by everything they knew of his intellect, honesty, and character before revelation even began.

وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ — Of a Great Moral Character

This āyah is among the most beloved descriptions of the Prophet ﷺ in the entire Qurʾān. Al-Saʿdī: خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ refers to the Prophet's ﷺ character as shaped entirely by the Qurʾān — his patience, mercy, honesty, humility, generosity, and justice in every dealing, private and public. ʿĀʾishah رضي الله عنها was later asked about his character and answered that his character was the Qurʾān itself — he embodied its commands and avoided its prohibitions in his daily life. This āyah teaches a principle that runs through the whole dīn: good character (أخلاق) is not a decoration on top of correct ʿaqīdah — it is inseparable from prophethood and from the daʿwah. A caller to Islam whose manners are harsh or dishonest contradicts his own message; the strongest daʿwah is a good example lived out, exactly as the Prophet ﷺ was praised here before any law was even legislated to him.

أَصْحَابُ الْجَنَّةِ — The People of the Garden: Al-Saʿdī explains this parable as a trial (بلوى) sent to test how people who are blessed with wealth respond to it. The owners of this garden swore among themselves to harvest it early in the morning, deliberately concealing their intention from the poor so that no beggar could come and claim a share, and — critically — وَلَا يَسْتَثْنُونَ, without saying "إِن شَاءَ اللَّه" (if Allah wills). Their sin was twofold: withholding the right of the poor from the harvest that Allah had blessed them with, and relying on their own plan as though its success were guaranteed without reference to Allah's will. So Allah sent a night-time affliction that destroyed the garden entirely before they arrived. Their regret, their mutual blame, and their return to tawbah (repentance) — saying سُبْحَانَ رَبِّنَا إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَالِمِينَ — form the turning point of the parable: worldly loss became the means of their return to Allah. This story remains a living lesson: withholding charity from the poor invites loss of the very wealth withheld, and forgetting to attach "إِن شَاءَ اللَّه" to one's plans is forgetting that all provision is by Allah's will, not human scheming.

وَلَا تَكُن كَصَاحِبِ الْحُوتِ — Be Not Like the Companion of the Fish: Near the close of the sūrah, Allah commands the Prophet ﷺ to be patient in delivering the message and enduring the harm of his people, and not to be hasty like صَاحِبُ الْحُوتِ — the companion of the fish, a reference to the Prophet Yūnus عليه السلام, who left his people before Allah gave him permission and was later swallowed by the fish, calling out to Allah in distress until his Lord's favor overtook him and delivered him. His story is told in full detail elsewhere in the Qurʾān; here the mention is brief, only to draw the lesson of patience: the Prophet ﷺ is commanded to bear the mockery of his people — including their claim that he is mad — with the same patience, trusting that Allah's plan (كَيْدِي) is firm, and that the Qurʾān remains, regardless of their hostility, nothing but ذِكْرٌ لِّلْعَالَمِينَ — a reminder to all the worlds.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

نٓ ۚ وَالْقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسْطُرُونَ ۝ مَا أَنتَ بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ بِمَجْنُونٍ ۝ وَإِنَّ لَكَ لَأَجْرًا غَيْرَ مَمْنُونٍ ۝ وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ

Four āyāt that open the sūrah with an oath and a defence. Nūn — by the pen and what they inscribe — an oath tied to knowledge and its preservation. You are not, by the favor of your Lord, a madman — a direct rebuttal of the disbelievers' slander. And indeed for you is a reward uninterrupted — a promise of endless recompense for enduring their harm. And indeed you are of a great moral character — the seal of the passage, and one of the greatest praises given to any human being.
