Sūrah 76 · Juzʾ 29 · Madani · 31 āyātYear 2

سورة الإنسان

Sūrat al-Insān — Man

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know that this sūrah is also called سورة الدهر (al-Dahr) and understand its opening reminder that man was once not even a thing worth mentioning.
  • Know that man was created from a نُطْفَة أَمْشَاج (a mixed drop) as a test, and was given hearing, sight, and guidance to the way — whether he is grateful or ungrateful.
  • Identify the four qualities of the الْأَبْرَار (the righteous) described in this sūrah: fulfilling vows, fearing the Day, feeding others despite their own need, and doing so purely for Allah's sake without seeking reward or thanks.
  • Know the description of Paradise given in this sūrah — its couches, shade, fruit, drink, and garments.
  • Know the closing lesson: no one wills anything except that Allah wills it, and Allah admits into His mercy whom He wills.

This sūrah is also known as سورة الدهر (Sūrat al-Dahr), taking its name from the opening word هَلْ أَتَىٰ عَلَى الْإِنسَانِ حِينٌ مِّنَ الدَّهْرِ — "has there come upon man a period of time..."

الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: Man's Origin and the Righteous Who Give (1–11)

هَلْ أَتَى عَلَى الْإِنسَانِ حِينٌ مِّنَ الدَّهْرِ لَمْ يَكُن شَيْئًا مَّذْكُورًا ۝ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَا الْإِنسَانَ مِن نُّطْفَةٍ أَمْشَاجٍ نَّبْتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلْنَاهُ سَمِيعًا بَصِيرًا ۝ إِنَّا هَدَيْنَاهُ السَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرًا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا ۝ إِنَّا أَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكَافِرِينَ سَلَاسِلَ وَأَغْلَالًا وَسَعِيرًا ۝ إِنَّ الْأَبْرَارَ يَشْرَبُونَ مِن كَأْسٍ كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا كَافُورًا ۝ عَيْنًا يَشْرَبُ بِهَا عِبَادُ اللَّهِ يُفَجِّرُونَهَا تَفْجِيرًا ۝ يُوفُونَ بِالنَّذْرِ وَيَخَافُونَ يَوْمًا كَانَ شَرُّهُ مُسْتَطِيرًا ۝ وَيُطْعِمُونَ الطَّعَامَ عَلَى حُبِّهِ مِسْكِينًا وَيَتِيمًا وَأَسِيرًا ۝ إِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ اللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمْ جَزَاءً وَلَا شُكُورًا ۝ إِنَّا نَخَافُ مِن رَّبِّنَا يَوْمًا عَبُوسًا قَمْطَرِيرًا ۝ فَوَقَاهُمُ اللَّهُ شَرَّ ذَلِكَ الْيَوْمِ وَلَقَّاهُمْ نَضْرَةً وَسُرُورًا

Has there come upon man a period of time when he was not a thing worth mentioning? Indeed, We created man from a mixed drop that We may test him, so We made him hearing and seeing. Indeed, We guided him to the way — whether he be grateful or ungrateful. Indeed, We have prepared for the disbelievers chains, shackles, and a blazing fire. Indeed, the righteous will drink from a cup mixed with kāfūr — a spring from which the servants of Allah will drink, making it gush forth in abundance. They fulfill their vows and fear a Day whose evil is widespread. And they feed food, despite their love for it, to the needy, the orphan, and the captive, [saying]: "We feed you only for the countenance of Allah; we wish neither reward nor thanks from you. Indeed, we fear from our Lord a Day austere and distressful." So Allah protected them from the evil of that Day and gave them radiance and joy.

al-Insān 76:1–11

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Description of Paradise (12–21)

وَجَزَاهُم بِمَا صَبَرُوا جَنَّةً وَحَرِيرًا ۝ مُّتَّكِئِينَ فِيهَا عَلَى الْأَرَائِكِ ۖ لَا يَرَوْنَ فِيهَا شَمْسًا وَلَا زَمْهَرِيرًا ۝ وَدَانِيَةً عَلَيْهِمْ ظِلَالُهَا وَذُلِّلَتْ قُطُوفُهَا تَذْلِيلًا ۝ وَيُطَافُ عَلَيْهِم بِآنِيَةٍ مِّن فِضَّةٍ وَأَكْوَابٍ كَانَتْ قَوَارِيرَا ۝ قَوَارِيرَ مِن فِضَّةٍ قَدَّرُوهَا تَقْدِيرًا ۝ وَيُسْقَوْنَ فِيهَا كَأْسًا كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا زَنجَبِيلًا ۝ عَيْنًا فِيهَا تُسَمَّى سَلْسَبِيلًا ۝ وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيْهِمْ وِلْدَانٌ مُّخَلَّدُونَ إِذَا رَأَيْتَهُمْ حَسِبْتَهُمْ لُؤْلُؤًا مَّنثُورًا ۝ وَإِذَا رَأَيْتَ ثَمَّ رَأَيْتَ نَعِيمًا وَمُلْكًا كَبِيرًا ۝ عَالِيَهُمْ ثِيَابُ سُندُسٍ خُضْرٌ وَإِسْتَبْرَقٌ ۖ وَحُلُّوا أَسَاوِرَ مِن فِضَّةٍ وَسَقَاهُمْ رَبُّهُمْ شَرَابًا طَهُورًا

And He will reward them for their patience with a garden and silk garments — reclining therein on adorned couches, seeing there neither burning sun nor freezing cold. Its shade is close above them and its clusters of fruit are made to hang low, easy to reach. They are served with vessels of silver and cups that are clear as glass — clear glasses made of silver, measured out exactly to their desire — and given to drink a cup mixed with ginger, from a spring therein named Salsabīl. Eternal youths circle among them; when you see them, you would think them scattered pearls. And when you look there, you see pleasure and a vast dominion. Upon them are green garments of fine silk and brocade, and they are adorned with bracelets of silver, and their Lord gives them a pure drink.

al-Insān 76:12–21

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: Patience, Remembrance, and the Will of Allah (22–31)

إِنَّ هَذَا كَانَ لَكُمْ جَزَاءً وَكَانَ سَعْيُكُم مَّشْكُورًا ۝ إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا عَلَيْكَ الْقُرْآنَ تَنزِيلًا ۝ فَاصْبِرْ لِحُكْمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تُطِعْ مِنْهُمْ آثِمًا أَوْ كَفُورًا ۝ وَاذْكُرِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ بُكْرَةً وَأَصِيلًا ۝ وَمِنَ اللَّيْلِ فَاسْجُدْ لَهُ وَسَبِّحْهُ لَيْلًا طَوِيلًا ۝ إِنَّ هَؤُلَاءِ يُحِبُّونَ الْعَاجِلَةَ وَيَذَرُونَ وَرَاءَهُمْ يَوْمًا ثَقِيلًا ۝ نَّحْنُ خَلَقْنَاهُمْ وَشَدَدْنَا أَسْرَهُمْ ۖ وَإِذَا شِئْنَا بَدَّلْنَا أَمْثَالَهُمْ تَبْدِيلًا ۝ إِنَّ هَذِهِ تَذْكِرَةٌ ۖ فَمَن شَاءَ اتَّخَذَ إِلَى رَبِّهِ سَبِيلًا ۝ وَمَا تَشَاءُونَ إِلَّا أَن يَشَاءَ اللَّهُ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمًا ۝ يُدْخِلُ مَن يَشَاءُ فِي رَحْمَتِهِ ۚ وَالظَّالِمِينَ أَعَدَّ لَهُمْ عَذَابًا أَلِيمًا

Indeed, this is a reward for you, and your effort has been appreciated. Indeed, it is We who have sent down the Qurʾān to you in stages. So be patient for the decision of your Lord, and do not obey among them a sinner or a disbeliever. And remember the name of your Lord morning and evening, and prostrate to Him during part of the night, and glorify Him through the long night. Indeed, these [disbelievers] love the immediate life and leave behind them a heavy Day. We created them and strengthened their forms, and if We willed, We could replace them entirely. Indeed, this is a reminder, so whoever wills may take a path to his Lord. And you do not will except that Allah wills — indeed, Allah is ever Knowing and Wise. He admits whom He wills into His mercy, and the wrongdoers — He has prepared for them a painful punishment.

al-Insān 76:22–31

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens by reminding man of his own humble beginning: there was a time he was not even a thing worth mentioning. Al-Saʿdī: this is a call to humility — the same man who now argues and disbelieves was once nothing, then a نُطْفَةٍ أَمْشَاجٍ (a mixed drop, the union of male and female fluid), created specifically نَّبْتَلِيهِ — that Allah may test him. From that low origin Allah gave him hearing, sight, and — most importantly — guidance to the way, leaving him to choose gratitude or ingratitude. The two paths then unfold: chains and blazing fire for the disbelievers, and for the الْأَبْرَار (the truly righteous, those outstanding in good) a drink mixed with كَافُور from a spring the righteous make flow at will.

أربع صفات — Four Qualities of the الْأَبْرَار

Āyāt 7–9 describe four qualities of the righteous, forming one of the Qurʾān's clearest lessons on sincerity (إِخْلَاص): (1) they fulfill their vows — following through on what they have committed to Allah; (2) they fear a Day whose evil is widespread — their good deeds are paired with humility, not self-satisfaction; (3) they feed the needy, the orphan, and the captive despite their own love for that food — giving from what they themselves want, not merely their surplus; (4) and — the deepest point — they say إِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ اللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمْ جَزَاءً وَلَا شُكُورًا, "we feed you only for the countenance of Allah; we wish neither reward nor thanks from you." Al-Saʿdī: true charity seeks no praise, no repayment, and no even a word of gratitude from the one who receives it — the reward is sought from Allah alone. This is the standard every act of giving should be measured against.

The middle of the sūrah paints one of the Qurʾān's richest descriptions of Paradise: couches, shade, fruit hanging low enough to pick without effort, vessels of silver, cups clear as glass yet made of silver, a drink flavoured with زَنْجَبِيل from a spring called سَلْسَبِيل, eternal youths who serve the people of Paradise, green silk garments, silver bracelets, and finally a pure drink given directly by their Lord. Al-Saʿdī: every detail answers a discomfort of this world — heat, cold, effort, thirst, ageing — with its opposite in the Hereafter, all as a reward, جَزَاءً وَكَانَ سَعْيُكُم مَّشْكُورًا, for effort that was appreciated by Allah.

The closing section addresses the Prophet ﷺ directly: the Qurʾān was sent down to him in stages, so he must be patient for his Lord's decision and not obey any sinner or disbeliever who calls him from it. He is commanded to remember Allah's name morning and evening and to prostrate and glorify Him through the night — a description of the night prayer (تَهَجُّد). Al-Saʿdī: the sūrah then closes on a profound point of ʿaqīdah — وَمَا تَشَاءُونَ إِلَّا أَن يَشَاءَ اللَّهُ, you do not will except that Allah wills. Man has a real will and real choice, and is rewarded or punished for it, but that will itself only operates within and by the permission of Allah's greater will — He admits into His mercy whom He wills, and has prepared a painful punishment for the wrongdoers.

وَيُطْعِمُونَ الطَّعَامَ عَلَى حُبِّهِ مِسْكِينًا وَيَتِيمًا وَأَسِيرًا ۝ إِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ اللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمْ جَزَاءً وَلَا شُكُورًا

Two āyāt that define sincere charity. And they feed food, despite their love for it, to the needy, the orphan, and the captive — giving from what they themselves want, not merely their leftovers. We feed you only for the countenance of Allah; we wish neither reward nor thanks from you — the heart's intention is what makes an act of charity accepted, not the size of the gift or the praise that follows it.
