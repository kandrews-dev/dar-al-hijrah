Sūrah 62 · Juzʾ 28 · Madani · 11 āyātYear 3

سورة الجمعة

Sūrat al-Jumuʿah — The Congregation (Friday)

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the great favour of Allah raising among the أُمِّيِّين (the unlettered Arabs) a Messenger from among themselves.
  • Know the Messenger's ﷺ four tasks named in āyah 2: reciting the āyāt, purifying the people (تزكية), and teaching them the Book and the wisdom.
  • Understand the parable of those who were entrusted with the Torah but did not act on it — like a donkey carrying books.
  • Know the command and etiquette of صلاة الجمعة (the Friday prayer): hastening to it and leaving off trade at the call.
  • Understand the balance the sūrah teaches — worship first, then dispersing to seek Allah's bounty while remembering Him often.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: A Messenger Raised Among the Unlettered (1–2)

يُسَبِّحُ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ الْمَلِكِ الْقُدُّوسِ الْعَزِيزِ الْحَكِيمِ ۝ هُوَ الَّذِي بَعَثَ فِي الْأُمِّيِّينَ رَسُولًا مِّنْهُمْ يَتْلُو عَلَيْهِمْ آيَاتِهِ وَيُزَكِّيهِمْ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ الْكِتَابَ وَالْحِكْمَةَ وَإِن كَانُوا مِن قَبْلُ لَفِي ضَلَالٍ مُّبِينٍ

Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth glorifies Allah, the Sovereign, the Holy, the Exalted in Might, the Wise. It is He who sent among the unlettered a Messenger from themselves, reciting to them His verses and purifying them and teaching them the Book and wisdom, although they were before in clear error.

al-Jumuʿah 62:1–2

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Parable of Knowledge Without Action (5)

مَثَلُ الَّذِينَ حُمِّلُوا التَّوْرَاةَ ثُمَّ لَمْ يَحْمِلُوهَا كَمَثَلِ الْحِمَارِ يَحْمِلُ أَسْفَارًا ۚ بِئْسَ مَثَلُ الْقَوْمِ الَّذِينَ كَذَّبُوا بِآيَاتِ اللَّهِ ۚ وَاللَّهُ لَا يَهْدِي الْقَوْمَ الظَّالِمِينَ

The example of those who were entrusted with the Torah and then did not carry it is like that of a donkey carrying volumes [of books]. Wretched is the example of the people who deny the signs of Allah. And Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people.

al-Jumuʿah 62:5

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Friday Prayer (9–10)

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَاةِ مِن يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا إِلَىٰ ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ وَذَرُوا الْبَيْعَ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ ۝ فَإِذَا قُضِيَتِ الصَّلَاةُ فَانتَشِرُوا فِي الْأَرْضِ وَابْتَغُوا مِن فَضْلِ اللَّهِ وَاذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ كَثِيرًا لَّعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُونَ

O you who believe, when [the adhān] is called for the prayer on the day of Jumuʿah, hasten to the remembrance of Allah and leave off trade. That is better for you, if you only knew. And when the prayer has been concluded, disperse throughout the land and seek from the bounty of Allah, and remember Allah often, that you may succeed.

al-Jumuʿah 62:9–10

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Al-Saʿdī explains that the sūrah opens by declaring that all creation glorifies Allah — الْمَلِكِ الْقُدُّوسِ, the true Sovereign, the Holy One free of every fault. From this it moves to one of His greatest favours upon the Arabs and all mankind: هُوَ الَّذِي بَعَثَ فِي الْأُمِّيِّينَ رَسُولًا مِّنْهُمْ — He raised, among a people who had no scripture and could barely read, a Messenger from among their own kind. Al-Saʿdī lists the four tasks the āyah gives him, and calls them the sum of the Messenger's mission: (1) يَتْلُو عَلَيْهِمْ آيَاتِهِ — reciting Allah's revelation to them; (2) وَيُزَكِّيهِمْ — purifying them, cleansing their hearts of shirk and bad character and raising them upon faith and virtue (tazkiyah); (3–4) وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ الْكِتَابَ وَالْحِكْمَةَ — teaching them the Book (the Qurʾān) and the wisdom (the Sunnah and its understanding). The transformation is captured in the closing clause: وَإِن كَانُوا مِن قَبْلُ لَفِي ضَلَالٍ مُّبِينٍ — a people once in manifest error became teachers of mankind.

كَمَثَلِ الْحِمَارِ يَحْمِلُ أَسْفَارًا — Carrying Without Understanding

Al-Saʿdī draws out the sobering parable of āyah 5. Those who were entrusted with the Torah — given knowledge and revelation — yet did not carry it (did not act on it or honour its guidance) are likened to a donkey carrying volumes of books. The donkey bears the weight of great wisdom on its back but understands nothing of it and benefits nothing from it. Al-Saʿdī: this is a warning to this ummah as much as to those before — knowledge that is memorised but not acted upon is a burden, not a light. The value of revelation is in living it, not merely carrying it. And it comes right before the command of Jumuʿah as if to say: do not be a community that has the guidance yet neglects to gather for it.

The Honour and Command of Jumuʿah: The sūrah gives Friday its rank as the weekly gathering of the believers. Al-Saʿdī explains the command إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَاةِ مِن يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا إِلَىٰ ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ وَذَرُوا الْبَيْعَ: once the adhān for Jumuʿah is called, it becomes obligatory to hasten to the prayer and the khuṭbah (the "remembrance of Allah" here includes the sermon) and to leave off trade and anything that would distract from it. "Hasten" (فَاسْعَوْا) means with concern and resolve, not literally running. And the reason is stated with perfect brevity: ذَٰلِكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ — turning from your shop to your Lord is better for you, if only you knew what you were gaining.

Worship and Livelihood in Balance: Islam does not command withdrawal from the world. The very next āyah releases the believer back into it: فَإِذَا قُضِيَتِ الصَّلَاةُ فَانتَشِرُوا فِي الْأَرْضِ وَابْتَغُوا مِن فَضْلِ اللَّهِ — when the prayer is finished, spread out in the land and seek Allah's bounty (lawful earning). Al-Saʿdī: notice the order — the call of Allah first, then the pursuit of provision — and notice the seal on both: وَاذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ كَثِيرًا لَّعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُونَ, remember Allah much, in the marketplace as in the mosque, so that you succeed. The sūrah closes by rebuking those early Muslims who, when a trade caravan and its entertainment arrived during the khuṭbah, hurried out and left the Prophet ﷺ standing — a reminder that no bargain of this world outweighs standing before Allah.

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَاةِ مِن يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا إِلَىٰ ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ وَذَرُوا الْبَيْعَ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ

The āyah every believer answers each week: When the call is made for the Friday prayer, hasten to the remembrance of Allah and leave off trade — that is better for you, if you only knew. The shop will still be there after the prayer; the standing before your Lord will not wait.
