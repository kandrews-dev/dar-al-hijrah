سورة الفجر

Sūrat al-Fajr — The Dawn

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the four oaths and what al-Saʿdī says about the "ten nights."
  • Know the three nations destroyed — their shared characteristic and what destroyed them.
  • Understand the key theological point: wealth and poverty are a test, not signs of Allah's approval or disapproval.
  • Know the closing address to the peaceful soul — النَّفْسُ الْمُطْمَئِنَّة.
الآياتThe Āyāt — Selected Key Passages

وَٱلْفَجْرِ ۝ وَلَيَالٍ عَشْرٍ ۝ وَٱلشَّفْعِ وَٱلْوَتْرِ ۝ وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا يَسْرِ ۝ هَلْ فِى ذَٰلِكَ قَسَمٌ لِّذِى حِجْرٍ ۝ أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِعَادٍ ۝ إِرَمَ ذَاتِ ٱلْعِمَادِ ۝ ٱلَّتِى لَمْ يُخْلَقْ مِثْلُهَا فِى ٱلْبِلَٰدِ ۝ وَثَمُودَ ٱلَّذِينَ جَابُوا۟ ٱلصَّخْرَ بِٱلْوَادِ ۝ وَفِرْعَوْنَ ذِى ٱلْأَوْتَادِ ۝ ٱلَّذِينَ طَغَوْا۟ فِى ٱلْبِلَٰدِ ۝ وَأَكْثَرُوا۟ فِيهَا ٱلْفَسَادَ ۝ فَصَبَّ عَلَيْهِمْ رَبُّكَ سَوْطَ عَذَابٍ ۝ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَبِٱلْمِرْصَادِ

By the dawn — and by the ten nights — and by the even and the odd — and by the night when it comes — are these oaths not sufficient for one who is possessed of reason? Have you not heard how your Lord dealt with ʿĀd — of Irām, who were people of great stature and strength — the like of whom had never been created in the land — and Thamūd, who hewed out rocks in the valley — and Pharaoh, lord of the stakes (his mighty hosts)? All of them transgressed in the land and made therein much mischief — so your Lord unleashed upon them a scourge of punishment. Verily your Lord is ever vigilant.

al-Fajr 89:1–14

فَأَمَّا ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ إِذَا مَا ٱبْتَلَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَأَكْرَمَهُۥ وَنَعَّمَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّىٓ أَكْرَمَنِ ۝ وَأَمَّآ إِذَا مَا ٱبْتَلَىٰهُ فَقَدَرَ عَلَيْهِ رِزْقَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّىٓ أَهَٰنَنِ ۝ كَلَّا ۝ يَٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفْسُ ٱلْمُطْمَئِنَّةُ ۝ ٱرْجِعِىٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةً مَّرْضِيَّةً ۝ فَٱدْخُلِى فِى عِبَٰدِى ۝ وَٱدْخُلِى جَنَّتِى

As for man, when his Lord tests him by bestowing generous bounties upon him, he says: my Lord has honoured me. But when He tests him by restricting his provision, he says: my Lord has humiliated me. Nay! ... O soul at peace — return to your Lord, well pleased and pleasing to Him — join My slaves — and enter My paradise.

al-Fajr 89:15–17 (partial), 27–30

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The four oaths: By the dawn (at which time there is a prayer of great virtue — al-Fajr prayer), by the ten nights (the last ten nights of Ramadan during which Laylat al-Qadr occurs, or the first ten nights of Dhul-Hijjah during which the standing in ʿArafah occurs), by the even and the odd (these are among the most significant days of the year), and by the night when it comes (bringing rest by the mercy and wisdom of Allah). The question هَلْ فِي ذَلِكَ قَسَمٌ لِذِي حِجْرٍ — are these not sufficient for one possessed of reason? Al-Saʿdī: yes, any part of that would suffice for one who understands and listens with full presence of mind.

الأمم الثلاث — Three Destroyed Nations

الأمة Their power Their end
عَاد إِرَم People of Irām — great stature and strength, the like of whom had not been created Transgressed, spread mischief → scourge of punishment
ثَمُود Hewed dwellings out of rocks in the valley with their strength Transgressed, spread mischief → destroyed
فِرْعَوْن ذو الأوتاد Commanded troops — mighty Pharaoh Transgressed, spread mischief → scourge of punishment

All three: strayed from the Messengers, barred people from the path of Allah. Result: إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَبِالْمِرْصَاد — your Lord is ever vigilant, watching those who disobey. He gives them a brief respite, then seizes them with the grip of One Who is Almighty, Omnipotent.

The misconception about wealth: When man receives abundance he says "my Lord has honoured me." When his provision is restricted he says "my Lord has humiliated me." Allah says: كَلَّا — Nay! Al-Saʿdī explains: not everyone given abundance in this world is dear to Allah, and not everyone whose provision is restricted is worthless before Allah. Rather wealth and poverty are a trial — to see who gives thanks and who is patient. The reward or punishment follows from the response, not the situation.

Four traits of the heedless: (1) you do not show kindness to the orphan — indicating lack of compassion; (2) you do not urge one another to feed the poor — because covetousness is deeply entrenched; (3) you devour inheritance indiscriminately — without leaving anything to others who had a right; (4) you have an insatiable love of wealth. Al-Saʿdī: love of this world and giving it precedence over the hereafter is the root of all sin.

يَا أَيَّتُهَا النَّفْسُ الْمُطْمَئِنَّة (O soul at peace): Al-Saʿdī: this soul found peace in the remembrance and love of Allah, and was content with His decrees. It will be told: ارْجِعِي إِلَى رَبِّكِ — return to your Lord Who cared for you and by Whose grace you became one of His close friends. رَاضِيَةً مَرْضِيَّة — well pleased (with Allah and with His reward), and Allah is pleased with you (for your righteous deeds). Then: فَادْخُلِي فِي عِبَادِي وَادْخُلِي جَنَّتِي — join My slaves and enter My paradise. These words are addressed to the soul on the Day of Resurrection and at the time of death.

Memorize & Seek — احفظ واطلب

يَٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفْسُ ٱلْمُطْمَئِنَّةُ ٱرْجِعِىٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةً مَّرْضِيَّةً فَٱدْخُلِى فِى عِبَٰدِى وَٱدْخُلِى جَنَّتِى

The whole sūrah moves from mighty destroyed empires to a single soul — quietly at peace. All the power of ʿĀd and Thamūd counted for nothing. The soul that found peace in Allah, and was content with His decrees, is called home. That is the goal. Teach the children to want this address more than any worldly honour.
