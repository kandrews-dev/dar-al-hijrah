Sūrah 94 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 8 āyātYear 1

سورة الانشراح

Sūrat al-Inshirāḥ — The Opening of the Breast

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the three blessings Allah recalls to the Prophet ﷺ — expanded breast, lifted burden, exalted renown.
  • Understand the grammar insight: why having the same hardship repeated twice with two different "eases" means ease will always outnumber hardship.
  • Know the two interpretations of "when you are free, strive" — and why both are valid.
  • Apply the promise of this sūrah to the family's own hardships.
الآياتThe Āyāt

أَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ ۝ وَوَضَعْنَا عَنكَ وِزْرَكَ ۝ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنقَضَ ظَهْرَكَ ۝ وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ ۝ فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَٱنصَبْ ۝ وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرْغَب

Have We not filled your heart with reassurance and comfort — and relieved you of your burden that weighed down your back — and exalted for you your renown? So truly with hardship comes ease — truly with hardship comes ease. So whenever you are free, strive in worship — and to your Lord alone turn in hope.

al-Inshirāḥ 94:1–8

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Al-Saʿdī says: Allah reminds His Messenger ﷺ of three great blessings that He had already granted him, to reassure him and give him gratitude in his heart.

١

أَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ

Have We not filled your heart with reassurance?

Al-Saʿdī: made you feel at ease with the teachings of religion, when calling people to Allah, granted the noblest characteristics, caused you to focus on the hereafter, and made it easy to attain goodness. His heart was not constrained or uneasy.

٢

وَوَضَعْنَا عَنكَ وِزْرَكَ

Relieved you of your burden that weighed your back

The burden of sin — related to what Allah says in al-Fatḥ 48:2: "that Allah may forgive you your past and future sins." The weight that would have broken the back is lifted entirely.

٣

وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ

Exalted for you your renown

Al-Saʿdī: Allah is not mentioned except that His Messenger ﷺ is mentioned alongside — in the shahādah, the adhān, the iqāmah, in khuṭbahs, and in all other matters. No one among all humankind has ever attained this level of renown. And in the hearts of the Ummah there is love and veneration directed toward no one else after Allah.

فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا — why it is repeated twice

Great glad tidings: every hardship is accompanied by ease. Al-Saʿdī says even if hardship entered a lizard hole, ease would enter upon it and expel it. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Verily there is relief with distress, and verily there is with hardship ease." (Tirmidhi, authenticated)

The grammar insight: الْعُسْر (hardship) appears in the definite form both times = the same one hardship. يُسْرًا (ease) appears in the indefinite form both times = two different eases. One hardship — two eases. Hardship can never overwhelm plural ease.

فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَانصَبْ وَإِلَى رَبِّكَ فَارْغَب (when you are free, strive — and to your Lord turn in hope): Al-Saʿdī gives two interpretations: (1) when free from errands and business, with nothing left in the heart to distract it — then strive hard in worship and supplication; (2) when you complete your prayer — then strive in duʿāʾ and turn to your Lord alone to ask for all you need. Do not be among those who, when free, indulge in amusement and turn away from their Lord.

Memorize & Trust — احفظ وثِق

فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا · إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

This sūrah was revealed to comfort the Prophet ﷺ when the disbelievers said Allah had forsaken him. His response was not to complain — it was this promise. Memorize these two āyāt and teach the children: when something is hard, it does not come alone. Ease is already with it. The hardship is one (definite). The eases are plural (indefinite). This is the promise of Allah.
