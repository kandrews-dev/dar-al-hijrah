سورة البلد

Sūrat al-Balad — The City

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the two oaths and what is attested — that man was created in toil and hardship.
  • Know the great gifts already given to man: eyes, tongue, lips, and knowledge of the two ways.
  • Know what الْعَقَبَة (the steep path) is — its three components.
  • Know the conditions for being on the right vs. the left — and al-Saʿdī's note about تواصي بالمرحمة.
الآياتThe Āyāt

لَآ أُقْسِمُ بِهَٰذَا ٱلْبَلَدِ ۝ وَأَنتَ حِلٌّۢ بِهَٰذَا ٱلْبَلَدِ ۝ وَوَالِدٍ وَمَا وَلَدَ ۝ لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ فِى كَبَدٍ ۝ أَيَحْسَبُ أَن لَّن يَقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ أَحَدٌ ۝ يَقُولُ أَهْلَكْتُ مَالًا لُّبَدًا ۝ أَيَحْسَبُ أَن لَّمْ يَرَهُۥٓ أَحَدٌ ۝ أَلَمْ نَجْعَل لَّهُۥ عَيْنَيْنِ ۝ وَلِسَانًا وَشَفَتَيْنِ ۝ وَهَدَيْنَٰهُ ٱلنَّجْدَيْنِ ۝ فَلَا ٱقْتَحَمَ ٱلْعَقَبَةَ ۝ وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْعَقَبَةُ ۝ فَكُّ رَقَبَةٍ ۝ أَوْ إِطْعَٰمٌ فِى يَوْمٍ ذِى مَسْغَبَةٍ ۝ يَتِيمًا ذَا مَقْرَبَةٍ ۝ أَوْ مِسْكِينًا ذَا مَتْرَبَةٍ ۝ ثُمَّ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْمَرْحَمَةِ ۝ أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ أَصْحَٰبُ ٱلْمَيْمَنَةِ ۝ وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا هُمْ أَصْحَٰبُ ٱلْمَشْـَٔمَةِ ۝ عَلَيْهِمْ نَارٌ مُّؤْصَدَةٌ

Verily I swear by this city — and you are a resident of this city — and by parent and offspring — We have indeed created man in toil and hardship. Does he think no one can ever have power over him? He says: I have squandered enormous wealth! Does he think no one saw him? Have We not given him two eyes — and a tongue, and a pair of lips — and shown him the two ways? But he has not attempted the steep path — and how could you know what the steep path is? Freeing a slave — or giving food on a day of privation — to an orphan near of kin — or one who is poor and destitute — while being among those who believe and exhort one another to be steadfast and to show compassion. Such are the People of the Right. And those who reject Our signs will be the People of the Left — with fire closing in upon them.

al-Balad 90:1–20

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The two oaths: By this secure city — Makkah al-Mukarramah, the best of all cities, especially with the Messenger ﷺ living there; and by parent and offspring — Adam and his progeny. What is attested: We have created man in toil and hardship. Al-Saʿdī: this means the hardships he will go through in this life, in al-barzakh, and on the Day of Resurrection. So man should strive to do what will bring relief from these hardships — or he will continue in severe punishment for all eternity.

The arrogance of self-sufficiency: Created in the best form to deal with tough situations — yet he becomes arrogant and thinks this will last forever. أَيَحْسَبُ أَن لَّن يَقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ أَحَدٌ? Does he think no one can ever overpower him? He boasts: "I have squandered enormous wealth!" Al-Saʿdī: Allah describes spending on desires and sins as "squandering" because the spender gains nothing and only gets regret, loss, and exhaustion — unlike spending on good causes (which is as if making deals with Allah Who returns many times more).

The gifts given — yet the path not taken: Two eyes (to see), a tongue and two lips (to speak beautifully), and shown the two ways — guidance distinct from misguidance. These immense blessings require him to fulfil his duties towards Allah and not use them in disobedience. Yet despite all this — فَلَا اقْتَحَمَ الْعَقَبَة — he has not attempted the steep path, because he is following his desires.

وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الْعَقَبَة — What is the steep path?

Climbing a steep path is difficult — a metaphor for striving against one's own desires and against Shayṭān, and persisting in righteous deeds. Al-Saʿdī lists three components:

١
فَكُّ رَقَبَة — Freeing a slave. Or ransoming Muslim prisoners held by disbelievers (al-Saʿdī says this is even more appropriate today)
٢
إِطْعَام فِي يَوْمِ ذِي مَسْغَبَة — Feeding food on a day of severe hunger. Specified: to an orphan who is also a close relative (يَتِيمًا ذَا مَقْرَبَة), or to someone poor and destitute (مِسْكِينًا ذَا مَتْرَبَة — literally: rubbing against the ground)
٣
ثُمَّ كَانَ مِنَ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْمَرْحَمَة — While being among those who: (a) believe in their hearts in all that is obligatory; (b) exhort one another to be steadfast in obeying Allah, refraining from disobedience, and accepting painful decrees; (c) exhort one another to show compassion — giving to the needy, teaching the ignorant, loving for others what you love for yourself

الفئتان — Two groups: Those who climb the steep path are أَصْحَابُ الْمَيْمَنَة — the People of the Right (blessed). Those who reject Allah's signs are أَصْحَابُ الْمَشْأَمَة — the People of the Left — عَلَيْهِمْ نَارٌ مُؤْصَدَة: fire closes in upon them. They are confined behind doors barred with huge beams that cannot be opened — trapped in distress and hardship.

Memorize & Practice — احفظ وطبّق

فَلَا ٱقْتَحَمَ ٱلْعَقَبَةَ · وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْعَقَبَةُ · فَكُّ رَقَبَةٍ · أَوْ إِطْعَٰمٌ فِى يَوْمٍ ذِى مَسْغَبَةٍ

The steep path is steep precisely because it costs something. Notice what makes it hard: freeing someone else, feeding someone else, caring about someone else. The disease of this sūrah is self-sufficiency (أَيَحْسَبُ أَن لَّن يَقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ أَحَدٌ) — and the cure is getting out of yourself. This family: who can we free? Who can we feed? Whom can we show compassion?
