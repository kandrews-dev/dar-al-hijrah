سورة الطلاق

Sūrat al-Ṭalāq — The Divorce

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the just conduct Allah commands in divorce: divorcing at the right time, counting the عِدَّة (waiting period), and not expelling women from their homes.
  • Understand the three great promises to مَن يَتَّقِ اللَّهَ (whoever fears Allah): a مَخْرَج (way out), provision from where he does not expect, and ease after hardship.
  • Grasp وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ — whoever relies on Allah, He is sufficient for him — and how tawakkul relates to taqwā.
  • Know why this sūrah is called Sūrat al-Nisāʾ al-Ṣughrā (the lesser Sūrat al-Nisāʾ).
  • Understand the closing warning of past nations and the greatness of Allah's power and all-encompassing knowledge.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Just Conduct of Divorce (1)

يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ إِذَا طَلَّقْتُمُ النِّسَاءَ فَطَلِّقُوهُنَّ لِعِدَّتِهِنَّ وَأَحْصُوا الْعِدَّةَ ۖ وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ رَبَّكُمْ ۖ لَا تُخْرِجُوهُنَّ مِن بُيُوتِهِنَّ وَلَا يَخْرُجْنَ إِلَّا أَن يَأْتِينَ بِفَاحِشَةٍ مُّبَيِّنَةٍ ۚ وَتِلْكَ حُدُودُ اللَّهِ ۚ وَمَن يَتَعَدَّ حُدُودَ اللَّهِ فَقَدْ ظَلَمَ نَفْسَهُ ۚ لَا تَدْرِي لَعَلَّ اللَّهَ يُحْدِثُ بَعْدَ ذَٰلِكَ أَمْرًا

O Prophet, when you [believers] divorce women, divorce them at [the onset of] their waiting period, and count the waiting period, and fear Allah, your Lord. Do not expel them from their houses, nor should they [themselves] leave, unless they commit a clear immorality. These are the limits [set by] Allah, and whoever transgresses the limits of Allah has wronged himself. You know not — perhaps Allah will bring about, after that, a new matter.

al-Ṭalāq 65:1

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Promise to the God-Fearing (2–3)

وَمَن يَتَّقِ اللَّهَ يَجْعَل لَّهُ مَخْرَجًا ۝ وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ بَالِغُ أَمْرِهِ ۚ قَدْ جَعَلَ اللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدْرًا

And whoever fears Allah — He will make for him a way out, and will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah — then He is sufficient for him. Indeed Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a measure.

al-Ṭalāq 65:2–3

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: Spending, Ease After Hardship (7)

لِيُنفِقْ ذُو سَعَةٍ مِّن سَعَتِهِ ۖ وَمَن قُدِرَ عَلَيْهِ رِزْقُهُ فَلْيُنفِقْ مِمَّا آتَاهُ اللَّهُ ۚ لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا مَا آتَاهَا ۚ سَيَجْعَلُ اللَّهُ بَعْدَ عُسْرٍ يُسْرًا

Let a man of wealth spend from his wealth, and he whose provision is restricted — let him spend from what Allah has given him. Allah does not burden a soul beyond what He has given it. Allah will bring about, after hardship, ease.

al-Ṭalāq 65:7

الآيات — القسم الرابعPart Four: The Warning and the Greatness of Allah (8–12)

اللَّهُ الَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ وَمِنَ الْأَرْضِ مِثْلَهُنَّ يَتَنَزَّلُ الْأَمْرُ بَيْنَهُنَّ لِتَعْلَمُوا أَنَّ اللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ وَأَنَّ اللَّهَ قَدْ أَحَاطَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عِلْمًا

It is Allah who created seven heavens and of the earth the like of them. The command descends among them, that you may know that Allah is over all things competent, and that Allah has encompassed all things in knowledge.

al-Ṭalāq 65:12

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Al-Saʿdī notes that this sūrah is often called Sūrat al-Nisāʾ al-Ṣughrā (the "lesser" Sūrat al-Nisāʾ) because it lays down key rulings of divorce and the عِدَّة (the waiting period a woman observes after divorce). Even in the painful matter of separation, Allah commands justice and mercy: a man should divorce لِعِدَّتِهِنَّ — at the time from which the ʿiddah can properly begin (not during menstruation, and not in a cleanliness in which he has been intimate), count the period carefully, and — most strikingly — not expel the woman from her home nor should she leave it during the ʿiddah. Al-Saʿdī draws out the wisdom in لَا تَدْرِي لَعَلَّ اللَّهَ يُحْدِثُ بَعْدَ ذَٰلِكَ أَمْرًا — keeping the couple under one roof leaves the door open for hearts to soften and for reconciliation, which is often "the new matter" Allah brings about. Divorce in Islam is not a slamming of doors; its rules are designed to preserve dignity and the possibility of return.

مَن يَتَّقِ اللَّهَ — Taqwā and Its Rewards

Al-Saʿdī highlights that this sūrah, in the middle of legal rulings, gives some of the most beloved promises in the whole Qurʾān. Three times it says مَن يَتَّقِ اللَّهَ — whoever fears Allah — and each time attaches a reward: (1) يَجْعَل لَّهُ مَخْرَجًا — He will make for him a way out of every distress and difficulty; (2) وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ — and provide for him from directions he never counted on; (3) later, يَجْعَل لَّهُ مِنْ أَمْرِهِ يُسْرًا (He makes his affair easy) and يُكَفِّرْ عَنْهُ سَيِّئَاتِهِ وَيُعْظِمْ لَهُ أَجْرًا (removes his sins and magnifies his reward). Al-Saʿdī: taqwā is the cause, and these are its fruits — so the one who fears Allah in his marriage, his divorce, his spending and his whole life is never truly cornered. Coupled with this is وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ — whoever entrusts his affairs to Allah, Allah Himself is enough for him. Ḥasb means "sufficiency": the One relied upon suffices the one who relies.

Provision, Ease, and Not Being Overburdened: The sūrah returns to practical mercy in matters of money. A husband must spend on the divorced woman during her ʿiddah, and on nursing arrangements for their child, each according to his means: لِيُنفِقْ ذُو سَعَةٍ مِّن سَعَتِهِ — the one of ample wealth from his ampleness, and the one whose provision is tight from what Allah has given him. Al-Saʿdī: this is the mercy of the Sharīʿah — لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا مَا آتَاهَا, Allah does not obligate anyone beyond what He has granted them — and it closes with a promise every struggling believer clings to: سَيَجْعَلُ اللَّهُ بَعْدَ عُسْرٍ يُسْرًا, Allah will bring ease after hardship. Difficulty is never the final word.

The Warning and the Majesty of the Lawgiver: Before its end, the sūrah recalls nations that arrogantly defied the command of their Lord and His messengers and were taken to a severe account — a reminder that Allah's rulings on family and everything else are not to be treated lightly. Then it closes on a breathtaking horizon: the One who legislates these detailed laws of marriage and divorce is the same One who created the seven heavens and their like of the earth, between which His command descends. Al-Saʿdī: this is placed here so that you obey these rulings لِتَعْلَمُوا أَنَّ اللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ — knowing that the Lawgiver has power over everything and قَدْ أَحَاطَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عِلْمًا, has encompassed all things in knowledge. The God who counts the seven heavens is not unaware of how you treat a divorced woman.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

وَمَن يَتَّقِ اللَّهَ يَجْعَل لَّهُ مَخْرَجًا ۝ وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ

Among the most consoling āyāt in the Qurʾān. Whoever fears Allah, He will make for him a way out, and provide for him from where he does not expect; and whoever relies upon Allah, He is sufficient for him. Carry it into every tight place: taqwā opens doors, and tawakkul makes Allah Himself your sufficiency.
