Sūrah 111 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 5 āyātYear 1

سورة المسد

Sūrat al-Masad — The Palm Fibre

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand what سورة المسد (Sūrat al-Masad) says about Abu Lahab and why.
  • Learn why wealth and lineage cannot protect anyone from Allah's punishment.
  • See how this sūrah is a miracle: a prophecy of the unseen fulfilled exactly.
  • Take the lesson: what matters to Allah is تقوى (taqwā — God-consciousness), not status.

This short sūrah is one of the most striking in the Qur'an. Abu Lahab was the Prophet's own uncle — a man of wealth, status and tribal power. And yet Allah condemned him by name. Why? And what does this teach us?

الآيات The Āyāt — سورة المسد

تَبَّتْ يَدَآ أَبِى لَهَبٍ وَتَبَّ ۝ مَآ أَغْنَىٰ عَنْهُ مَالُهُۥ وَمَا كَسَبَ ۝ سَيَصْلَىٰ نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ ۝ وَٱمْرَأَتُهُۥ حَمَّالَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ ۝ فِى جِيدِهَا حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍ

May the hands of Abu Lahab perish — and may he perish! His wealth will not avail him, nor what he earned. He will burn in a fire of blazing flame — and his wife too, the carrier of firewood, with a rope of palm fibres around her neck.

al-Masad 111:1–5

التفسير Explanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

أبو لهب (Abu Lahab) — "Father of Flame" — was the Prophet's paternal uncle. He was one of the most powerful men of Quraysh, with wealth, tribal standing, and every worldly advantage. He was also one of the most vicious enemies of the Prophet ﷺ, denying his message and trying his utmost to harm him — without any fear of Allah or regard for their close kinship.

تَبَّتْ يَدَاهُ وَتَبَّ (May his hands perish — and may he perish): al-Saʿdī explains this as a declaration of doom and disgrace — he will be wretched and will never prosper. The "hands" stand for his actions: everything he used against the Prophet ﷺ.

مَالُهُ وَمَا كَسَبَ (his wealth and what he earned): neither the wealth he hoarded nor any deeds he amassed could ward off Allah's punishment when it came upon him. Wealth is only as good as what it is spent on — and if it was spent in war against the truth, it becomes a burden, not a blessing.

حَمَّالَةَ الْحَطَب (carrier of firewood): his wife was equally hostile. She cooperated with him in plotting against the Messenger ﷺ, preparing and carrying her own load of sin — like a gatherer of firewood who then ties the bundle to her neck. Al-Saʿdī also offers a literal meaning: in the next life she will carry fuel for her husband's punishment in the fire, with a rope of palm fibres around her neck.

A sign of Allah's knowledge — آية من علم الله

This sūrah is one of the signs of the Qur'an's divine origin. It was revealed while Abu Lahab and his wife were still alive — and it stated plainly that they would enter the fire, which could only come true if they died as disbelievers. Had either of them accepted Islam, the prophecy would have been proven false. But Allah, Who knows the unseen, revealed it in absolute certainty — and events unfolded exactly as He said. Neither of them ever became Muslim. This is a proof of the Qur'an's divine source.

الدرس لنا The Lesson for Us

Abu Lahab had everything the world values: wealth, family, power, and even proximity to the Prophet ﷺ as his uncle. None of it helped him. What Allah looks at is not what a person has — it is what a person does with it, and whether the heart submitted to Him. Every lesson on this site comes back to this: the purpose of knowledge is to know Allah and submit to Him alone.

Memorize & Reflect — احفظ وتدبّر

تَبَّتْ يَدَآ أَبِى لَهَبٍ وَتَبَّ · مَآ أَغْنَىٰ عَنْهُ مَالُهُۥ وَمَا كَسَبَ

Memorize these opening āyāt. Remind yourself and the children: the one who harms the Messenger ﷺ and denies the truth — his wealth will not save him on the Day of Judgement. What saves us is تقوى (taqwā) and following the Prophet ﷺ.
