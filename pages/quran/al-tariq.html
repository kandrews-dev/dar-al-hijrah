Sūrah 86 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 17 āyātYear 1

سورة الطارق

Sūrat al-Ṭāriq — The Night Star

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what al-Ṭāriq is — and what the oath attests to: every soul has a keeper.
  • Know the argument from creation: man was created from fluid — so resurrection is certain.
  • Understand: the Quran is a decisive word that resolves all disputes — not to be taken lightly.
  • Know the closing: the disbelievers are plotting — so is Allah — and patience is commanded for a little while.
الآياتThe Āyāt

وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ ۝ وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ ۝ ٱلنَّجْمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ ۝ إِن كُلُّ نَفْسٍ لَّمَّا عَلَيْهَا حَافِظٌ ۝ فَلْيَنظُرِ ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ ۝ خُلِقَ مِن مَّآءٍ دَافِقٍ ۝ يَخْرُجُ مِنۢ بَيْنِ ٱلصُّلْبِ وَٱلتَّرَآئِبِ ۝ إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَجْعِهِۦ لَقَادِرٌ ۝ يَوْمَ تُبْلَى ٱلسَّرَآئِرُ ۝ فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن قُوَّةٍ وَلَا نَاصِرٍ ۝ وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلرَّجْعِ ۝ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ذَاتِ ٱلصَّدْعِ ۝ إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوْلٌ فَصْلٌ ۝ وَمَا هُوَ بِٱلْهَزْلِ ۝ إِنَّهُمْ يَكِيدُونَ كَيْدًا ۝ وَأَكِيدُ كَيْدًا ۝ فَمَهِّلِ ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَ أَمْهِلْهُمْ رُوَيْدًا

By the heaven and the night star — and how could you know what the night star is? It is the star of piercing brightness — for every soul there is a keeper watching it. So let man consider that from which he was created — he was created from an ejected fluid — originating from between the backbone and the ribs. Surely Allah has the power to bring him back to life — on the day when people's inner secrets will be examined — then man will have no power to help himself nor anyone to help him. By the sky that returns — and by the earth that cracks open — verily it is a decisive word — and is not to be taken lightly. Surely they are plotting and planning — but I too am planning. So give respite to the disbelievers — grant them delay for a little while.

al-Ṭāriq 86:1–17

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

وَالسَّمَاءِ وَالطَّارِق (By the heaven and that which appears by night): Then the explanation: وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا الطَّارِق — then: النَّجْمُ الثَّاقِب — the star of piercing brightness. Al-Saʿdī says this is a generic term for all stars of piercing brightness (light that pierces through until seen on earth). It is called al-Ṭāriq because it appears at night (from yatruqu — to appear by night).

إِن كُلُّ نَفْسٍ لَمَّا عَلَيْهَا حَافِظٌ (For every soul there is a keeper watching it): This is what is attested to by the oath — every soul has a keeper who records its deeds (both righteous and otherwise), and that soul will be requited for what is recorded against it. The angels are appointed to watch and record every action.

فَلْيَنظُرِ الْإِنسَانُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ (Let man consider that from which he was created): The argument for resurrection: he was created from an ejected fluid (semen) originating from between the backbone and the ribs. Al-Saʿdī: the One Who created man from this insignificant fluid that emerges from this place is able to recreate him in the hereafter and resurrect him for the requital.

يَوْمَ تُبْلَى السَّرَائِر (On the day when people's inner secrets will be examined): What is hidden in people's hearts of good or evil will be disclosed and made manifest on their faces. In this world many matters are concealed, but on the Day of Resurrection the righteousness of the righteous and the evil of evildoers will be made manifest — all matters laid open. On that day: فَمَا لَهُ مِن قُوَّةٍ وَلَا نَاصِرٍ — no power to help himself, no one to support him.

وَالسَّمَاءِ ذَاتِ الرَّجْعِ وَالْأَرْضِ ذَاتِ الصَّدْعِ (By the sky that returns and the earth that cracks open): The sky brings back rain every year; and the heaven brings down divine decrees at all times. The earth cracks open for plants to grow on which people and livestock live; and the earth will split open for the dead on the Day of Resurrection. What is attested: إِنَّهُ لَقَوْلٌ فَصْلٌ — the Quran is a decisive word. It contains clear and plain truth. It is the decisive word that settles disputes between different factions and views. وَمَا هُوَ بِالْهَزْلِ — it is a serious matter, not a joke.

إِنَّهُمْ يَكِيدُونَ كَيْدًا وَأَكِيدُ كَيْدًا (They are plotting — and I too am planning): The disbelievers are plotting to defeat the truth and support falsehood. But Allah plans to cause the truth to prevail — even if the disbelievers hate it — and to ward off the falsehood they have brought. Humans are too weak to resist the Most Strong, All-Knowing. Command: فَمَهِّلِ الْكَافِرِينَ أَمْهِلْهُمْ رُوَيْدًا — bear with them for a short time. They will come to know their fate when the punishment descends.

Memorize & Trust — احفظ وثِق

إِنَّهُمْ يَكِيدُونَ كَيْدًا · وَأَكِيدُ كَيْدًا · فَمَهِّلِ ٱلْكَٰفِرِينَ أَمْهِلْهُمْ رُوَيْدًا

The opponents of this religion have always had plots, plans, and schemes. This sūrah was revealed in Makkah when the believers were weak, mocked, and outnumbered. Allah's answer: I too am planning. Bear with them for a little while. Every family teaching this deen in hard times — hold these three āyāt. Allah's plan is already in motion.
