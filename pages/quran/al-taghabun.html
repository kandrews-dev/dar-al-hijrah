سورة التغابن

Sūrat al-Taghābun — Mutual Loss & Gain

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know that all creation glorifies Allah, and that He fashioned mankind and perfected their forms (فَأَحْسَنَ صُوَرَكُمْ).
  • Understand the answer the Qurʾān gives to those who deny the resurrection: "Say: Yes, by my Lord, you will surely be resurrected."
  • Know why the Day of Gathering is named يَوْمُ التَّغَابُن — the Day of Mutual Loss and Gain — and who wins and loses on it.
  • Grasp مَا أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ ۗ وَمَن يُؤْمِن بِاللَّهِ يَهْدِ قَلْبَهُ — every calamity is by Allah's leave, and faith earns the guidance of the heart.
  • Understand family and wealth as a فِتْنَة (trial), and the command to fear Allah as much as you are able and to guard against the greed of the soul.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: All Glorifies Allah; He Perfected Your Forms (1–3)

يُسَبِّحُ لِلَّهِ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۖ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ ۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ ۝ هُوَ الَّذِي خَلَقَكُمْ فَمِنكُمْ كَافِرٌ وَمِنكُم مُّؤْمِنٌ ۚ وَاللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ بَصِيرٌ ۝ خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ بِالْحَقِّ وَصَوَّرَكُمْ فَأَحْسَنَ صُوَرَكُمْ ۖ وَإِلَيْهِ الْمَصِيرُ

Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth glorifies Allah. To Him belongs dominion, and to Him belongs [all] praise, and He is over all things competent. It is He who created you, and among you is the disbeliever, and among you is the believer. And Allah, of what you do, is Seeing. He created the heavens and the earth in truth and formed you and perfected your forms; and to Him is the [final] return.

al-Taghābun 64:1–3

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: The Certainty of Resurrection; the Day of Taghābun (7, 9)

زَعَمَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا أَن لَّن يُبْعَثُوا ۚ قُلْ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّي لَتُبْعَثُنَّ ثُمَّ لَتُنَبَّؤُنَّ بِمَا عَمِلْتُمْ ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ عَلَى اللَّهِ يَسِيرٌ ۝ يَوْمَ يَجْمَعُكُمْ لِيَوْمِ الْجَمْعِ ۖ ذَٰلِكَ يَوْمُ التَّغَابُنِ ۗ وَمَن يُؤْمِن بِاللَّهِ وَيَعْمَلْ صَالِحًا يُكَفِّرْ عَنْهُ سَيِّئَاتِهِ وَيُدْخِلْهُ جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا ۚ ذَٰلِكَ الْفَوْزُ الْعَظِيمُ

Those who disbelieve claim that they will never be resurrected. Say: "Yes, by my Lord, you will surely be resurrected; then you will surely be informed of what you did. And that, for Allah, is easy." [Mention] the Day He will assemble you for the Day of Assembly — that is the Day of Mutual Loss and Gain. And whoever believes in Allah and does righteousness — He will remove from him his misdeeds and admit him to gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein forever. That is the great attainment.

al-Taghābun 64:7, 9

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: No Calamity Except by His Leave (11)

مَا أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ ۗ وَمَن يُؤْمِن بِاللَّهِ يَهْدِ قَلْبَهُ ۚ وَاللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ

No disaster strikes except by permission of Allah. And whoever believes in Allah — He will guide his heart. And Allah is Knowing of all things.

al-Taghābun 64:11

الآيات — القسم الرابعPart Four: Family as a Trial; Fear Allah As Much As You Can (15–16)

إِنَّمَا أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَأَوْلَادُكُمْ فِتْنَةٌ ۚ وَاللَّهُ عِندَهُ أَجْرٌ عَظِيمٌ ۝ فَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ وَاسْمَعُوا وَأَطِيعُوا وَأَنفِقُوا خَيْرًا لِّأَنفُسِكُمْ ۗ وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفْسِهِ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُونَ

Your wealth and your children are but a trial, and Allah has with Him a great reward. So fear Allah as much as you are able, and listen and obey and spend [in His cause]; it is better for yourselves. And whoever is protected from the greed of his own soul — it is those who are the successful.

al-Taghābun 64:15–16

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Al-Saʿdī explains that the sūrah opens by declaring that everything in the heavens and earth glorifies Allah — His is the dominion, His is all praise, and He is capable of all things. From this majesty it moves straight to the human being: He created you, and — by His wisdom and testing — فَمِنكُمْ كَافِرٌ وَمِنكُم مُّؤْمِنٌ, some of you disbelieve and some believe. He created the heavens and earth بِالْحَقِّ (in truth, with purpose, not in vain) and He فَأَحْسَنَ صُوَرَكُمْ — perfected your very forms, a bounty demanding gratitude. And the sentence that hangs over it all: وَإِلَيْهِ الْمَصِيرُ — to Him is the return. A creation this purposeful cannot end in nothingness.

The Resurrection Denied and Affirmed: The deniers claim (زَعَمَ — a word the Qurʾān uses for baseless assertion) that they will never be raised. Allah answers with one of only three places in the Qurʾān where the Prophet ﷺ is told to swear by his Lord: قُلْ بَلَىٰ وَرَبِّي لَتُبْعَثُنَّ — "Say: Yes, by my Lord, you will certainly be raised, then informed of everything you did." Al-Saʿdī: and this, though it staggers the deniers, is عَلَى اللَّهِ يَسِيرٌ — utterly easy for Allah, who created you the first time.

يَوْمُ التَّغَابُن — The Day of Mutual Loss and Gain

The sūrah takes its name from āyah 9, which calls the Day of Gathering يَوْمُ التَّغَابُنِ. Al-Saʿdī explains taghābun from the root of غَبْن — being cheated or getting the losing end of a bargain. On that Day the true winners and losers of every transaction of life are finally revealed: the believers gain Paradise, and the disbelievers lose everything, "cheated" out of the eternal happiness they sold for a fleeting world. Even the believers who fell short experience a lesser taghābun — they see the higher ranks they could have reached had they done more, and recognise what their negligence cost them. It is the ultimate day of accounts, where no gain is imagined and no loss is hidden. The comfort follows at once: وَمَن يُؤْمِن بِاللَّهِ وَيَعْمَلْ صَالِحًا — whoever believes and acts righteously has his sins wiped away and enters Gardens forever: ذَٰلِكَ الْفَوْزُ الْعَظِيمُ, that is the great triumph.

Calamity, Decree, and the Guided Heart: One of the sūrah's most quoted āyāt teaches the believer's response to hardship: مَا أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ — nothing befalls anyone except by Allah's leave and decree. Al-Saʿdī draws the second half as the fruit of the first: وَمَن يُؤْمِن بِاللَّهِ يَهْدِ قَلْبَهُ — whoever truly believes in Allah, He guides his heart. This is why some interpreted it precisely of calamity: the one who knows the affliction came by Allah's permission is granted a heart that submits, is content, and says innā lillāhi wa innā ilayhi rājiʿūn — so his heart is guided to patience and tranquillity even in loss. Faith transforms disaster from despair into steadiness.

Wealth, Children, and the Greed of the Soul: The sūrah then warns, gently, that even the people closest to us can pull us from Allah's obedience, and that إِنَّمَا أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَأَوْلَادُكُمْ فِتْنَةٌ — your wealth and children are a test, not an excuse. The response is a beautifully realistic command: فَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ — fear Allah as much as you are able. Al-Saʿdī: this shows the mercy of the religion — you are asked for your utmost, not the impossible. It is followed by three practical duties — listen, obey, and spend — and closed with the key to it all: وَمَن يُوقَ شُحَّ نَفْسِهِ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُونَ. Whoever is guarded from شُحّ — the ingrained greed and stinginess of the soul that makes a person cling to wealth and withhold rights — those are the truly successful.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

مَا أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ ۗ وَمَن يُؤْمِن بِاللَّهِ يَهْدِ قَلْبَهُ

The believer's anchor in every hardship: No calamity strikes except by Allah's leave; and whoever believes in Allah, He guides his heart. Nothing reaches you outside His decree, and faith turns the heart from panic to peace. Pair it with āyah 16 — fear Allah as much as you are able — the command that meets you exactly where your strength ends.
