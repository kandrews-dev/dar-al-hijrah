سورة الفاتحة

Al-Fātiḥah — The Opening

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Read and understand the seven āyāt of al-Fātiḥah.
  • See the three categories of tawḥīd within the sūrah.
  • Understand ʿibādah (worship) and istiʿānah (seeking help).
  • Know the three groups: the blessed, those who earned anger, and the astray.

Al-Fātiḥah is recited in every rakʿah of every prayer. Imam al-Saʿdī shows that although it is short, it gathers the foundations of the whole religion. We take it section by section.

البسملة The Basmalah — āyah 1

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

al-Fātiḥah 1:1

We begin by the name of Allah, seeking His help and blessing. Allah is the One truly deserving of all worship, named for His perfect attributes. al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm are two names of mercy: a mercy so vast it encompasses all creation, and a special mercy Allah has decreed for the believers who follow His Messengers. (Based on al-Saʿdī.)

الحمد والربوبية والملك Praise, Lordship & Sovereignty — āyāt 2–4

ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ ۝ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ

All praise belongs to Allah, Lord of all the worlds — the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful — Master of the Day of Recompense.

al-Fātiḥah 1:2–4

All praise belongs to Allah — praise for His perfect attributes and for all His actions, which flow from generosity and justice. Lord of all the worlds: He created everything that exists, gave it all it needs, and sustains it. Al-Saʿdī notes that this nurturing (rubūbiyyah) is of two kinds — a general care for all creation, and a special care for His friends, the believers, whom He nurtures with faith and guidance.

Master of the Day of Recompense: the Owner and Sovereign of the Day of Resurrection, when every person is requited for their deeds. On that Day His complete sovereignty, justice and wisdom become clear to all creation.

العبادة والاستعانة Worship & Seeking Help — āyah 5

إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ

You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help.

al-Fātiḥah 1:5

Placing "You" first conveys exclusivity: we worship You alone and ask help from You alone, no one else. ʿIbādah is everything Allah loves and is pleased with, of words and deeds, outward and inward. Istiʿānah is relying on Allah to gain what benefits and repel what harms. Worship is mentioned before seeking help — the general before the specific, and Allah's right before the servant's need. Al-Saʿdī says these two together are the means to all lasting happiness.

الصراط المستقيم The Straight Path — āyāt 6–7

ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝ صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

Guide us to the straight path — the path of those You have blessed, not of those who earned anger, nor of those who went astray.

al-Fātiḥah 1:6–7

Guide us to the straight path: show us the clear path that leads to Allah and His Paradise, and keep us firm upon it — which means knowing the truth and acting upon it. This is why we ask for it in every rakʿah; we are always in need of it.

Those You have blessed are the prophets, the truthful (ṣiddīqūn), the martyrs and the righteous. Those who earned anger are those who knew the truth but abandoned it; those who went astray are those who strayed through ignorance. So we ask Allah to keep us with the first group and away from the other two.

A gem from al-Saʿdī — جوهرة

Though brief, al-Fātiḥah gathers the foundations of the religion. It contains the three categories of tawḥīd: al-Rubūbiyyah (Lordship) in "Lord of all the worlds"; al-Ulūhiyyah (worship) in "All praise belongs to Allah" and "You alone we worship"; and al-Asmāʾ wa-l-Ṣifāt (names & attributes) in "All praise." It affirms prophethood ("guide us…"), recompense ("Master of the Day…"), the divine decree with human responsibility, and sincerity in worship and seeking help.

Memorize & Reflect — احفظ وتدبّر

ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ

"Guide us to the straight path." You ask Allah for this in every rakʿah — knowing the truth and acting upon it. Recite al-Fātiḥah today with this meaning present in your heart.
