سورة البيّنة

Sūrat al-Bayyinah — The Clear Proof

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand what البيّنة (the clear proof) is — and what it consists of.
  • Know why the People of the Book did not submit even after the clear proof arrived — and what al-Saʿdī says about the nature of their rejection.
  • Know the one religion they were commanded — and why prayer and zakāh are singled out.
  • Memorize the two final verdicts: worst of people vs. best of people — and what distinguishes them.
الآيات The Āyāt

لَمْ يَكُنِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ مِنْ أَهْلِ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ وَٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ مُنفَكِّينَ حَتَّىٰ تَأْتِيَهُمُ ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ ۝ رَسُولٌ مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ يَتْلُوا۟ صُحُفًا مُّطَهَّرَةً ۝ فِيهَا كُتُبٌ قَيِّمَةٌ ۝ وَمَا تَفَرَّقَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُوا۟ ٱلْكِتَٰبَ إِلَّا مِن بَعْدِ مَا جَآءَتْهُمُ ٱلْبَيِّنَةُ ۝ وَمَآ أُمِرُوٓا۟ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ حُنَفَآءَ وَيُقِيمُوا۟ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤْتُوا۟ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ دِينُ ٱلْقَيِّمَةِ ۝ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ مِنْ أَهْلِ ٱلْكِتَٰبِ وَٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ فِى نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ ۚ أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمْ شَرُّ ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ ۝ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمْ خَيْرُ ٱلْبَرِيَّةِ ۝ جَزَآؤُهُمْ عِندَ رَبِّهِمْ جَنَّٰتُ عَدْنٍ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدًا ۚ رَّضِىَ ٱللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا۟ عَنْهُ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنْ خَشِىَ رَبَّهُۥ

Those who disbelieve among the People of the Book and the polytheists were not going to desist from disbelief until there came to them clear proof — a Messenger from Allah, reciting pure pages on which there were texts of unerring soundness. Yet those who were given the Book did not part ways except after clear proof came to them — even though they were enjoined only to worship Allah with sincere devotion, being true in faith to Him alone, to establish prayer and give zakāh — and that is the true religion. Verily those who disbelieve among the People of the Book and the polytheists will be in the fire of Hell, to abide therein forever — it is they who are the worst of people. Verily those who believe and do righteous deeds — it is they who are the best of people. Their reward with their Lord is gardens of perpetual abode through which rivers flow, to abide therein forever. Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him. That is for those who fear their Lord.

al-Bayyinah 98:1–8

التفسير Explanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

البيّنة — what is the clear proof?

Al-Saʿdī explains: the clear proof is the Messenger ﷺ — sent by Allah to call people to the truth, reciting to them صُحُفًا مُطَهَّرَةً (pure pages), protected from the devils approaching them, which no one may touch except the purified. On those pages: كُتُبٌ قَيِّمَةٌ — texts of unerring soundness: true stories and just commands that guide to the truth and the straight path.

Three layers: (1) a Messenger from Allah → (2) reciting pure pages → (3) containing sound and righteous texts. The proof was complete, clear, and from the highest source.

وَمَا تَفَرَّقَ الَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْكِتَابَ إِلَّا مِن بَعْدِ مَا جَاءَتْهُمُ الْبَيِّنَةُ (yet those given the Book did not part ways except after clear proof came): Al-Saʿdī makes a critical point here: their division and rejection did not happen from ignorance — it happened after the clear proof arrived. The seeker of truth became distinct from the one not seeking it. Those who chose disbelief chose it having seen the evidence. Their stubborn wickedness meant that clear proof only increased them in misguidance — insight only increased them in blindness.

دين القيّمة The one religion — what were they commanded?

وَمَا أُمِرُوا إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ الدِّينَ حُنَفَاءَ (they were enjoined only to worship Allah with sincere devotion, being true in faith): Al-Saʿdī says: in all their acts of worship — both outward and inward — they were commanded to seek only the pleasure of Allah and to draw near to Him. حُنَفَاء means turning away from all religions contrary to pure monotheism.

وَيُقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَيُؤْتُوا الزَّكَاةَ (establish prayer and give zakāh): Al-Saʿdī explains why prayer and zakāh are singled out from all the acts of worship included in "worshipping Allah": because they are the two acts that, if a person undertakes them, he will undertake all other religious duties. Prayer is the greatest act of worship of the body; zakāh is the greatest act of worship of wealth. Together they represent the entire religion in practice.

وَذَلِكَ دِينُ الْقَيِّمَةِ (and that is the true religion): The religion of uprightness — the straight path that leads to the gardens of bliss. All other religions are paths that lead to hell.

النهايتان The two final verdicts

شَرُّ الْبَرِيَّة

The worst of people

Those who disbelieve among the People of the Book and the polytheists — in Hell, abiding forever. They learned about the truth but ignored it.

خَيْرُ الْبَرِيَّة

The best of people

Those who believe and do righteous deeds — gardens of perpetual abode, rivers flowing, forever. They worshipped Allah and learned who He is.

رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ (Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him): Al-Saʿdī explains both directions: Allah is pleased with them because of what they did of deeds that earn His pleasure. They are pleased with Him because of what He has prepared for them of all kinds of honour and great reward. The mutual pleasure — رضوان — is the highest attainment. As Allah says elsewhere: وَرِضْوَانٌ مِنَ اللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ — and the pleasure of Allah is the greatest thing of all. (al-Tawbah 9:72)

Memorize & Reflect — احفظ وتأمّل

وَمَآ أُمِرُوٓا۟ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ حُنَفَآءَ · رَّضِىَ ٱللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا۟ عَنْهُ

This sūrah gives the whole picture in eight āyāt: the proof that came, the command that was given, and the two final outcomes. The family question: which of the two groups are we working toward? The command is simple — sincere worship, prayer, zakāh. The outcome is رِضْوَان: mutual pleasure with Allah. That is the goal of this school and this family.
