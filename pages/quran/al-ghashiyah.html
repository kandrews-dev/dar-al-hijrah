سورة الغاشية

Sūrat al-Ghāshiyah — The Overwhelming Event

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the two groups on the Day of Resurrection — their faces, their state, and what they eat and drink.
  • Know the description of Paradise's comforts: springs, couches, goblets, cushions, carpets.
  • Know the four things to reflect on — and al-Saʿdī's note about the earth being round vs. spread out.
  • Know the role of the Prophet ﷺ according to the closing āyāt — and the limits of that role.
الآياتThe Āyāt

هَلْ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلْغَٰشِيَةِ ۝ وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ خَٰشِعَةٌ ۝ عَامِلَةٌ نَّاصِبَةٌ ۝ تَصْلَىٰ نَارًا حَامِيَةً ۝ تُسْقَىٰ مِنْ عَيْنٍ ءَانِيَةٍ ۝ لَّيْسَ لَهُمْ طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِن ضَرِيعٍ ۝ لَّا يُسْمِنُ وَلَا يُغْنِى مِن جُوعٍ ۝ وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ نَّاعِمَةٌ ۝ لِسَعْيِهَا رَاضِيَةٌ ۝ فِى جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٍ ۝ لَّا تَسْمَعُ فِيهَا لَٰغِيَةً ۝ فِيهَا عَيْنٌ جَارِيَةٌ ۝ فِيهَا سُرُرٌ مَّرْفُوعَةٌ ۝ وَأَكْوَابٌ مَّوْضُوعَةٌ ۝ وَنَمَارِقُ مَصْفُوفَةٌ ۝ وَزَرَابِىُّ مَبْثُوثَةٌ ۝ أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى ٱلْإِبِلِ كَيْفَ خُلِقَتْ ۝ وَإِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ كَيْفَ رُفِعَتْ ۝ وَإِلَى ٱلْجِبَالِ كَيْفَ نُصِبَتْ ۝ وَإِلَى ٱلْأَرْضِ كَيْفَ سُطِحَتْ ۝ فَذَكِّرْ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُذَكِّرٌ ۝ لَّسْتَ عَلَيْهِم بِمُصَيْطِرٍ ۝ إِلَّا مَن تَوَلَّىٰ وَكَفَرَ ۝ فَيُعَذِّبُهُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلْعَذَابَ ٱلْأَكْبَرَ ۝ إِنَّ إِلَيْنَآ إِيَابَهُمْ ۝ ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيْنَا حِسَابَهُمْ

Has there come to you the story of the Overwhelming Event? Some faces on that day will be humiliated — toiling and weary — as they burn in an intensely hot fire — given to drink from a boiling hot spring — having no food except dry thorns — that will neither nourish nor satisfy hunger. And other faces on that day will be joyful — content with the result of their past endeavours — in a garden on high — where they will hear no idle talk — in it a flowing spring — in it raised couches — and goblets placed ready — and cushions set in rows — and fine carpets spread out. Do they not consider the camels, how they are created — the heaven, how it is raised high — the mountains, how they are firmly set up — and the earth, how it is spread out? So admonish, for you are but sent to admonish — you have no power over them. But whoever turns away and disbelieves — Allah will punish him with the greatest punishment. Verily to Us will be their return — then it is for Us to call them to account.

al-Ghāshiyah 88:1–26

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Al-Saʿdī opens: the Overwhelming Event (الغاشية) will overwhelm people with its hardships on the Day of Resurrection. They will be divided into two groups and requited for their deeds.

الفريقان — The Two Groups

People of the Fire · أصحاب النار People of Paradise · أصحاب الجنة
Faces: humiliated, subject to shame and disgrace Faces: joyful — radiance of bliss; bodies show physical well-being
Toiling and weary — exhausted from punishment, dragged on faces through fire Content with result of their past endeavours — reward stored up and multiplied
Drink: boiling hot spring — intensely hot (cf. al-Kahf 18:29) Garden on high — sublime location, lofty dwellings one above another
Food: dry thorns — neither nourishes nor satisfies hunger; bitter, foul-smelling No idle talk heard — only good and beneficial talk. Springs, raised couches, goblets, cushions, fine carpets.

أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ (Do they not consider?): Allah urges those who do not believe to reflect on four great signs pointing to His oneness: (1) the camels — brilliant creation, made to serve people in many ways; (2) the sky — raised high in an amazing manner; (3) the mountains — firmly set up, giving stability to the earth and protecting it from shaking, with great benefit deposited within; (4) the earth — spread out and made broad and smooth so creatures may settle and people may till, plant, build, and travel.

فائدة علمية — al-Saʿdī on the earth being round

When this sūrah says the earth is "spread out" (سُطِحَتْ), some thought this contradicted it being round. Al-Saʿdī explicitly clarifies: the fact that it is spread out does not contradict the fact that it is round. The earth is an extremely large body — it can be round and spread out at the same time. These are not mutually exclusive, "as is well known to experts." This is a tafsīr written over 100 years ago affirming what modern science confirms.

فَذَكِّرْ إِنَّمَا أَنتَ مُذَكِّر (So admonish — for you are but sent to admonish): The Prophet ﷺ is to remind, exhort, warn and give glad tidings — but he was not sent to control people or be in charge of their deeds. If he does what is obliged of him, there is no blame on him after that. But whoever turns away and disbelieves — Allah will punish him with the greatest punishment. Then: to Us is their return, and it is for Us to call them to account.

Memorize & Contrast — احفظ وقارن

وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ خَٰشِعَةٌ عَامِلَةٌ نَّاصِبَةٌ · وُجُوهٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ نَّاعِمَةٌ لِسَعْيِهَا رَاضِيَةٌ

Two groups — described by their faces. One group's faces are exhausted and disgraced; the other's are joyful and content. The same word يَوْمَئِذٍ (on that day) applies to both — the same day, two completely different experiences. This is the power of choice now. Ask the children: which face do we want? Then: what choices lead there?
