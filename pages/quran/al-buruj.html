سورة البروج

Sūrat al-Burūj — The Constellations

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know the story of the People of the Trench — who they were, what they did, and why al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī marvelled at Allah's generosity toward them.
  • Understand the Names of Allah in this sūrah: al-Ghafūr, al-Wadūd, Dhū al-ʿArsh, al-Majīd, al-Faʿʿāl.
  • Know the closing: the Quran is in the Preserved Tablet — what this tells us about its protection.
  • Know al-Saʿdī's comment on the love of Allah restoring alongside His forgiveness.
الآياتThe Āyāt

وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلْبُرُوجِ ۝ وَٱلْيَوْمِ ٱلْمَوْعُودِ ۝ وَشَاهِدٍ وَمَشْهُودٍ ۝ قُتِلَ أَصْحَٰبُ ٱلْأُخْدُودِ ۝ ٱلنَّارِ ذَاتِ ٱلْوَقُودِ ۝ إِذْ هُمْ عَلَيْهَا قُعُودٌ ۝ وَهُمْ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفْعَلُونَ بِٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ شُهُودٌ ۝ وَمَا نَقَمُوا۟ مِنْهُمْ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلْحَمِيدِ ۝ ٱلَّذِى لَهُۥ مُلْكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ شَهِيدٌ ۝ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ فَتَنُوا۟ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنَٰتِ ثُمَّ لَمْ يَتُوبُوا۟ فَلَهُمْ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ وَلَهُمْ عَذَابُ ٱلْحَرِيقِ ۝ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمْ جَنَّٰتٌ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَٰرُ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْفَوْزُ ٱلْكَبِيرُ ۝ إِنَّ بَطْشَ رَبِّكَ لَشَدِيدٌ ۝ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ يُبْدِئُ وَيُعِيدُ ۝ وَهُوَ ٱلْغَفُورُ ٱلْوَدُودُ ۝ ذُو ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱلْمَجِيدُ ۝ فَعَّالٌ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ ۝ هَلْ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلْجُنُودِ ۝ فِرْعَوْنَ وَثَمُودَ ۝ بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ فِى تَكْذِيبٍ ۝ وَٱللَّهُ مِن وَرَآئِهِم مُّحِيطٌ ۝ بَلْ هُوَ قُرْءَانٌ مَّجِيدٌ ۝ فِى لَوْحٍ مَّحْفُوظٍ

By the heaven with its constellations — and by the promised day — and by the witness and what is witnessed — cursed be the makers of the trench — of the fire fed with fuel — as they sat beside it — witnessing what they were doing to the believers — and their only grievance against them was that they believed in Allah, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy — to Whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth; and Allah is Witness over all things. Verily those who persecute the believing men and believing women, then do not repent, will have the punishment of Hell and the punishment of the scorching fire. And those who believe and do righteous deeds will have gardens through which rivers flow — that is the great triumph. Verily the vengeance of your Lord is severe. Verily it is He Who originates creation then repeats it — and He is the Oft-Forgiving, the Loving — the Lord of the Throne, the Glorious — He does whatever He wills. Has there not come to you the story of the hosts — of Pharaoh and Thamūd? Nay, those who disbelieve persist in denial — and Allah encompasses them on all sides. Nay, it is a glorious Qur'ān — inscribed in a Preserved Tablet.

al-Burūj 85:1–22

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

Allah swears by the heaven with its constellations (the sun, moon, and heavenly bodies indicative of His perfect might, mercy, knowledge, and wisdom), by the promised Day of Resurrection (which cannot be changed), and by the witness and what is witnessed. What is attested: cursed be the makers of the trench.

أصحاب الأخدود — The People of the Trench

Disbelieving tyrants lived among believers. They pressured the believers to abandon their faith, but the believers refused. So the disbelievers dug a trench in the ground, lit a fire in it, and sat around it. They threw in whoever persisted in faith. Whoever surrendered to their demands was let go. They sat as witnesses to their own atrocity.

Al-Saʿdī: their only grievance against the believers was that they believed in Allah, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy — for which they deserved to be praised, not punished. This was one of the worst types of tyranny and hardheartedness: combining stubborn rejection of Allah's signs with fighting believers and burning them alive while watching. Yet they were also asked to repent. Hence al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī (may Allah have mercy on him) said: "Look at this generosity and kindness — they killed His close friends, yet He is still calling them to repent."

إِنَّ الَّذِينَ فَتَنُوا الْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ ثُمَّ لَمْ يَتُوبُوا: Those who persecute believing men and believing women — then do NOT repent — will have the punishment of Hell AND the punishment of the scorching fire. The condition for escape is repentance. They were given the opportunity.

Names of Allah in this sūrah: الْغَفُور — forgives all sins for the one who repents and pardons all bad deeds; الْوَدُود — loves His loved ones and they love Him with unmatched love. Al-Saʿdī notes the pairing: alongside al-Ghafūr He mentions al-Wadūd — so that no one thinks forgiveness is granted but love is not restored. Rather Allah rejoices over the repentance of His slave more than a man rejoices over finding his lost camel in the wilderness. ذُو الْعَرْشِ الْمَجِيد — the Throne is the greatest of all created things, closest to Allah; its greatness encompasses the heavens and earth and the Kursī. فَعَّالٌ لِمَا يُرِيد — He does whatever He wills, says to it "Be!" and it is.

بَلْ هُوَ قُرْآنٌ مَجِيدٌ فِي لَوْحٍ مَحْفُوظٍ (A glorious Quran inscribed in a Preserved Tablet): The Quran is comprehensive in its meanings, containing a great deal of goodness and knowledge. The Preserved Tablet is protected from any change, addition, or subtraction, and guarded against devils. This indicates the majesty and greatness of the Quran — and its high esteem before Allah.

Memorize & Hold On — احفظ وثبت

وَمَا نَقَمُوا مِنْهُمْ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤْمِنُوا بِٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلْحَمِيدِ · وَهُوَ ٱلْغَفُورُ ٱلْوَدُودُ

The believers in the trench were burned alive. Their crime: believing in Allah. These āyāt were revealed to comfort believers who were oppressed, mocked, and hurt for their faith. Allah's response is two things: a warning of severe punishment for the oppressors, and a reminder of His own names — Forgiving, Loving, Lord of the Throne, All-Powerful. This family teaches these āyāt so children know: the One they believe in is worth everything.
