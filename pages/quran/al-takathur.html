سورة التكاثر

Sūrat al-Takāthur — Competing for More

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand what التكاثر means — and why it covers everything people compete for, not just money.
  • See al-Saʿdī's insight about the word زُرْتُمُ الْمَقَابِرَ (you visit your graves) — why "visit" is a profound word choice.
  • Understand why كَلَّا is repeated and what يَقِين (certainty) has to do with being distracted.
  • Know the question that will be asked on the Day of Judgement: about النعيم — the bounties of this life.
الآيات The Āyāt

أَلْهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ ۝ حَتَّىٰ زُرْتُمُ ٱلْمَقَابِرَ ۝ كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ ۝ ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ ۝ كَلَّا لَوْ تَعْلَمُونَ عِلْمَ ٱلْيَقِينِ ۝ لَتَرَوُنَّ ٱلْجَحِيمَ ۝ ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَيْنَ ٱلْيَقِينِ ۝ ثُمَّ لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ يَوْمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

Competition for worldly gain distracts you — until you come to your graves. Nay; you will come to know. Again, nay; you will come to know. Nay; if you only knew with the knowledge of certainty [you would not be so distracted]. You will surely see the blazing fire; again, you will surely see it with the eye of certainty. Then on that day you will surely be asked about worldly bounties.

al-Takāthur 102:1–8

التفسير Explanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

أَلْهَاكُمُ التَّكَاثُرُ (Competition for worldly gain distracts you): Al-Saʿdī says Allah rebukes His slaves here for being distracted from what they were created for — worshipping Him alone, knowing Him, and turning to Him. The word أَلْهَى means to distract, to make heedless. التكاثر is the mutual competition to accumulate and to boast about more: more wealth, more children, more supporters, more status. Allah does not specify what they compete in — to include everything people race to accumulate when the aim is not sincerely seeking His pleasure.

حَتَّى زُرْتُمُ الْمَقَابِرَ — "until you visit your graves"

Al-Saʿdī draws attention to the word زُرْتُمُ — visit, not "dwell in" or "settle in." He says: Allah describes them as visitors to their graves, not permanent residents. This points to the resurrection — the grave is a layover, not the destination. The realm of eternity lies beyond it. The distraction lasts until death; but death itself is only a transit point to what comes next.

كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ · ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ (Nay — you will come to know. Again, nay — you will come to know): The repetition is for emphasis — a sharp double rebuke. You will know, you will know. But by the time you know, the window for acting on that knowledge will have closed.

كَلَّا لَوْ تَعْلَمُونَ عِلْمَ الْيَقِينِ (if you knew with certain knowledge): Al-Saʿdī's key insight here: it is not that people have no information. It is that their knowledge has not reached يَقِين — certainty that impacts the heart. If they truly knew with conviction that the fire was real and coming, they would not be distracted by competing for more. Distraction is a disease of uncertain knowledge.

لَتَرَوُنَّ الْجَحِيمَ · ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَيْنَ الْيَقِينِ (you will surely see the blazing fire — then you will surely see it with your own eyes): Two levels. First: you will see it. Then: you will see it عَيْنَ الْيَقِين — the certainty of one's own eyes, the highest form of direct knowledge. As Allah says elsewhere about the wrongdoers: they will see the fire and know they are bound to fall into it, with no escape. (al-Kahf 18:53)

ثُمَّ لَتُسْأَلُنَّ يَوْمَئِذٍ عَنِ النَّعِيمِ (then on that day you will surely be asked about worldly bounties): Al-Saʿdī explains: every bounty of this world will be asked about. Did you give thanks for it? Did you fulfil your duty to Allah concerning it? Did you use it to obey Him? Or were you deceived by it, failed to be grateful, and perhaps even used it to disobey Him? Every gift becomes a question.

الدرس لنا The Lesson for Us

The disease this sūrah describes is modern by default: more followers, more money, more house, more car, more status. Al-Saʿdī identifies the root: التكاثر is any competition to accumulate that does not sincerely seek Allah's pleasure. And the cure is exactly what the sūrah implies — يَقِين: the certainty that the fire is real, that the grave is a visit, that the bounties will be questioned. A family that recites this sūrah together and asks each other "what bounty did we receive today, and did we thank Allah for it?" has begun living the answer.

Memorize & Reckon — احفظ وحاسب

أَلْهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ حَتَّىٰ زُرْتُمُ ٱلْمَقَابِرَ · ثُمَّ لَتُسْـَٔلُنَّ يَوْمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ

Family practice: at the end of the day, name three نعيم (bounties) you received. Then ask — did we thank Allah for each one? Did we use it in obedience? The question at the end of this sūrah will be asked about every one of them. Rehearsing it now is the preparation.
