سورة الكوثر

Sūrat al-Kawthar — The Abundance

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand what الكوثر (al-Kawthar) means and what Allah gave His Prophet ﷺ.
  • Learn what الحوض (al-ḥawḍ — the Cistern) is, and its description from the Sunnah.
  • See the two acts of worship singled out — صلاة (prayer) and نحر (sacrifice) — and why.
  • Understand what الأبتر (al-abtar — cut off) means, and who is truly cut off from all good.

The enemies of the Prophet ﷺ taunted him after his son died, calling him الأبتر — "the one cut off," with no legacy or descendants. This three-āyah sūrah answered them with the greatest reversal in history.

الآيات The Āyāt — سورة الكوثر

إِنَّآ أَعْطَيْنَٰكَ ٱلْكَوْثَرَ ۝ فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنْحَرْ ۝ إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ

Indeed We have given you the Kawthar. So pray to your Lord and sacrifice to Him alone. Indeed it is the one who hates you who is cut off.

al-Kawthar 108:1–3

التفسير Explanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

إِنَّا أَعْطَيْنَاكَ الْكَوْثَرَ — "We have given you the Kawthar." Al-Saʿdī explains that الكوثر means an overwhelming abundance of good — everything Allah gave His Prophet ﷺ of the highest honour, the greatest mission, and the largest following. Among the most specific of these gifts is the river called al-Kawthar in الجنة (al-jannah — Paradise), and the الحوض (al-ḥawḍ — the Cistern) on the Day of Resurrection.

الحوض — The Prophet's Cistern on the Day of Resurrection

From the authentic Sunnah: the Cistern has a length of a month's journey and a width of a month's journey. Its water is whiter than milk and sweeter than honey. Its vessels are as numerous and bright as the stars of the sky. Whoever drinks from it will never thirst again. The believers will come to it — and some people will be turned away. This Cistern is one of the gifts from الكوثر that Allah gave specifically to the Prophet ﷺ.

فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَانْحَرْ (So pray to your Lord and sacrifice to Him alone): having reminded him of these great gifts, Allah commands two acts of worship as thanks. Al-Saʿdī explains why these two are singled out above all others: الصلاة (prayer) is the greatest act of the body — in it you humble yourself, bow, and prostrate, showing complete submission. النحر (sacrifice/offering) is the greatest act involving wealth — you give the most valuable of what you love, in the name of Allah alone, directing it to no one else. Both together represent تعظيم الله (magnifying Allah) with the body and with wealth.

إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الأَبْتَرُ — "It is the one who hates you who is cut off." الشانئ (al-shāniʾ) is the one who despises and hates. الأبتر is the one cut off — with no legacy, no descendants, no one to remember him. The taunt they threw at the Prophet ﷺ — "he is the cut-off one" — is turned back on them. No deed of theirs will continue. No one remembers them with love or honour. But Muḥammad ﷺ — his name is recited in every أذان (adhān) on earth, his Ummah numbers in the billions, and his legacy is the Qur'an and Sunnah until the Day of Judgement.

الدرس لنا The Lesson for Us

This sūrah teaches two things back-to-back: first, remember what Allah has given you; then, respond by worshipping Him — not by resting or becoming proud. شكر (shukr — gratitude) is expressed in action, not just feeling. For this family: every blessing — health, knowledge, children, income — is from الكوثر that Allah pours upon His servants. The response is prayer and sacrifice, a heart turned completely to Allah.

Memorize & Reflect — احفظ وتدبّر

إِنَّآ أَعْطَيْنَٰكَ ٱلْكَوْثَرَ · فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنْحَرْ · إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلْأَبْتَرُ

Memorize this sūrah and reflect with the family: Allah has given us abundance — children, health, knowledge of الإسلام (al-Islām). The response is not pride; it is prayer and giving for His sake. And those who mock the believers? History has forgotten them. Islam remains.
