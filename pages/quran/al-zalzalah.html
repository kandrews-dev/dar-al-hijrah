Sūrah 99 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Madani · 8 āyāt

سورة الزلزلة

Sūrat al-Zalzalah — The Earthquake

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand what happens to the earth on that Day — the earthquake, the burdens cast out, and the levelled plain.
  • Know what it means that the earth will "testify" — and by whose command.
  • Grasp the principle of the atom's weight: what it means that even the smallest deed — good or evil — will be seen.
  • Memorize al-Saʿdī's statement about this verse: the greatest encouragement for small good deeds and the greatest deterrent from minor evil ones.
الآيات The Āyāt

إِذَا زُلْزِلَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ زِلْزَالَهَا ۝ وَأَخْرَجَتِ ٱلْأَرْضُ أَثْقَالَهَا ۝ وَقَالَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ مَا لَهَا ۝ يَوْمَئِذٍ تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا ۝ بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا ۝ يَوْمَئِذٍ يَصْدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا لِّيُرَوْا۟ أَعْمَٰلَهُمْ ۝ فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُۥ ۝ وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا يَرَهُۥ

When the earth is shaken with a cataclysmic earthquake — and the earth throws out its burdens — and man says: What is the matter with it? — on that day it will tell all its news — because your Lord commanded it to do so. On that day all people will proceed in scattered groups to be shown the results of their deeds. So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it — and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it.

al-Zalzalah 99:1–8

التفسير Explanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

إِذَا زُلْزِلَتِ الْأَرْضُ زِلْزَالَهَا (when the earth is shaken with a cataclysmic earthquake): Al-Saʿdī explains that the earth will be shaken and convulse to such an extent that everything on it — all structures and all mountains — will collapse. The mountains will crumble and the hills will be flattened, becoming a smooth and levelled plain with no depression or elevation anywhere.

وَأَخْرَجَتِ الْأَرْضُ أَثْقَالَهَا (the earth throws out its burdens): Its burdens are the dead — those buried in it — who will be brought forth for the resurrection. What the earth contained of bodies will be cast out.

وَقَالَ الْإِنسَانُ مَا لَهَا (and man says: What is the matter with it?): Man will be shocked by the immense changes — everything he knew of the earth's stability and firmness is gone. He asks in bewilderment: what has happened to it?

تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا — the earth testifies

يَوْمَئِذٍ تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا (on that day it will tell all its news): Al-Saʿdī says the earth will testify — it will speak of everything that was done on its surface, both good and evil. The earth will be among the witnesses who testify concerning people's deeds.

بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوْحَى لَهَا (because your Lord commanded it): Allah will instruct the earth to speak — and it will not disobey His command. Every inch of ground that witnessed a deed — a prayer performed, a sin committed, a kindness given, a wrong done — will bear witness.

يَوْمَئِذٍ يَصْدُرُ النَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا لِّيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ (on that day all people will proceed in scattered groups to be shown their deeds): From the place of standing, after Allah passes judgement among them, they will go in scattered groups — so that Allah may show them what they did of good and evil deeds, and show them the appropriate requital.

مثقال ذرة The Atom's Weight — the greatest principle

فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُ · وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا يَرَهُ (whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it — and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it): Al-Saʿdī says these two āyāt are general in meaning and include all good and all evil — without exception. The logic: when even an atom's weight is requited — the most insignificant of things — then anything greater than that will surely be requited. Nothing is too small to escape.

Al-Saʿdī's statement on these āyāt

"This verse offers the strongest encouragement to do good, even if it is little, and the greatest deterrent against doing evil, even if it is minor."

He then cites two supporting āyāt: "On the day when every soul will find itself faced with all the good it has done, and all the evil it has done, it will wish there were a great distance between it and its evil" (Āl ʿImrān 3:30), and "They will find all they did recorded there" (al-Kahf 18:49).

Memorize & Count the Atoms — احفظ واحسب

فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُ · وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا يَرَهُ

These two āyāt are arguably the most comprehensive statement about accountability in the Quran. Memorize them and apply them as a family: the kind word that took three seconds — it is recorded. The harsh glance — it is recorded. The small sadaqah — it is recorded. The small lie — it is recorded. Al-Saʿdī says this sūrah is the greatest encouragement for small acts of good. Start there: one atom of good, every day.
