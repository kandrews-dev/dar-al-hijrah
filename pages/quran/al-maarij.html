سورة المعارج

Sūrat al-Maʿārij — The Ascending Stairways

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what a questioner asked about at the opening of the sūrah, and what ذِي الْمَعَارِج (Owner of the Ways of Ascent) means.
  • Understand the description of the Day whose extent is fifty thousand years, and why the criminal will wish to ransom himself with everyone he loves.
  • Know the default anxious nature of man described in this sūrah, and the list of qualities that lift the believer out of that default state.
  • Reflect on prayer, charity, chastity, trustworthiness, and truthful testimony as transformative character traits, not isolated acts.
  • Know the closing scene of the disbelievers rushing forward in scorn, and Allah's oath that He is able to replace them with better than them.
الآيات — القسم الأولPart One: The Punishment Bound to Happen (1–18)

سَأَلَ سَائِلٌ بِعَذَابٍ وَاقِعٍ ۝ لِّلْكَافِرِينَ لَيْسَ لَهُ دَافِعٌ ۝ مِّنَ اللَّهِ ذِي الْمَعَارِجِ ۝ تَعْرُجُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَالرُّوحُ إِلَيْهِ فِي يَوْمٍ كَانَ مِقْدَارُهُ خَمْسِينَ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍ ۝ فَاصْبِرْ صَبْرًا جَمِيلًا ۝ إِنَّهُمْ يَرَوْنَهُ بَعِيدًا ۝ وَنَرَاهُ قَرِيبًا ۝ يَوْمَ تَكُونُ السَّمَاءُ كَالْمُهْلِ ۝ وَتَكُونُ الْجِبَالُ كَالْعِهْنِ ۝ وَلَا يَسْأَلُ حَمِيمٌ حَمِيمًا ۝ يُبَصَّرُونَهُمْ ۚ يَوَدُّ الْمُجْرِمُ لَوْ يَفْتَدِي مِنْ عَذَابِ يَوْمِئِذٍ بِبَنِيهِ ۝ وَصَاحِبَتِهِ وَأَخِيهِ ۝ وَفَصِيلَتِهِ الَّتِي تُؤْوِيهِ ۝ وَمَن فِي الْأَرْضِ جَمِيعًا ثُمَّ يُنجِيهِ ۝ كَلَّا ۖ إِنَّهَا لَظَىٰ ۝ نَزَّاعَةً لِّلشَّوَىٰ ۝ تَدْعُو مَنْ أَدْبَرَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ ۝ وَجَمَعَ فَأَوْعَىٰ

A questioner asked about a punishment bound to happen, for the disbelievers — of it there is no preventer — [it is] from Allah, Owner of the Ways of Ascent. The angels and the Spirit ascend to Him during a Day the extent of which is fifty thousand years. So be patient with gracious patience; indeed they see it as distant, but We see it as near. On the Day the sky will be like murky oil, and the mountains will be like wool, and no close friend will ask about a friend — they will be shown to each other. The criminal will wish he could ransom himself from the punishment of that Day with his children, and his wife and his brother, and his nearest kindred who sheltered him, and everyone on earth entirely — if only that would save him. No! Indeed, it is the Flame [of Hell] — a remover of the scalp, calling him who turned his back and went away [from obedience], and collected [wealth] and withheld it.

al-Maʿārij 70:1–18

الآيات — القسم الثانيPart Two: Man's Anxious Nature and the Successful Believer (19–35)

إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ خُلِقَ هَلُوعًا ۝ إِذَا مَسَّهُ الشَّرُّ جَزُوعًا ۝ وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ الْخَيْرُ مَنُوعًا ۝ إِلَّا الْمُصَلِّينَ ۝ الَّذِينَ هُمْ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمْ دَائِمُونَ ۝ وَالَّذِينَ فِي أَمْوَالِهِمْ حَقٌّ مَّعْلُومٌ ۝ لِّلسَّائِلِ وَالْمَحْرُومِ ۝ وَالَّذِينَ يُصَدِّقُونَ بِيَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ وَالَّذِينَ هُم مِّنْ عَذَابِ رَبِّهِم مُّشْفِقُونَ ۝ إِنَّ عَذَابَ رَبِّهِمْ غَيْرُ مَأْمُونٍ ۝ وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ لِفُرُوجِهِمْ حَافِظُونَ ۝ إِلَّا عَلَىٰ أَزْوَاجِهِمْ أَوْ مَا مَلَكَتْ أَيْمَانُهُمْ فَإِنَّهُمْ غَيْرُ مَلُومِينَ ۝ فَمَنِ ابْتَغَىٰ وَرَاءَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْعَادُونَ ۝ وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ لِأَمَانَاتِهِمْ وَعَهْدِهِمْ رَاعُونَ ۝ وَالَّذِينَ هُم بِشَهَادَاتِهِمْ قَائِمُونَ ۝ وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمْ يُحَافِظُونَ ۝ أُولَٰئِكَ فِي جَنَّاتٍ مُّكْرَمُونَ

Indeed, mankind was created anxious — when evil touches him, impatient, and when good touches him, withholding — except those who pray, those who are constant in their prayer, and those within whose wealth is a known right for the petitioner and the deprived, and those who believe in the Day of Recompense, and those who are fearful of their Lord's punishment — indeed the punishment of their Lord is not that from which one is safe — and those who guard their private parts except with their spouses or those their right hands possess — for then they are not blameworthy — but whoever seeks beyond that, then they are the transgressors — and those who are attentive to their trusts and their promises, and those who are upright in their testimonies, and those who [carefully] maintain their prayer. Those will be in gardens, honored.

al-Maʿārij 70:19–35

الآيات — القسم الثالثPart Three: The Scorn of the Disbelievers and the Promised Day (36–44)

فَمَالِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا قِبَلَكَ مُهْطِعِينَ ۝ عَنِ الْيَمِينِ وَعَنِ الشِّمَالِ عِزِينَ ۝ أَيَطْمَعُ كُلُّ امْرِئٍ مِّنْهُمْ أَن يُدْخَلَ جَنَّةَ نَعِيمٍ ۝ كَلَّا ۖ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَاهُم مِّمَّا يَعْلَمُونَ ۝ فَلَا أُقْسِمُ بِرَبِّ الْمَشَارِقِ وَالْمَغَارِبِ إِنَّا لَقَادِرُونَ ۝ عَلَىٰ أَن نُّبَدِّلَ خَيْرًا مِّنْهُمْ وَمَا نَحْنُ بِمَسْبُوقِينَ ۝ فَذَرْهُمْ يَخُوضُوا وَيَلْعَبُوا حَتَّىٰ يُلَاقُوا يَوْمَهُمُ الَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ ۝ يَوْمَ يَخْرُجُونَ مِنَ الْأَجْدَاثِ سِرَاعًا كَأَنَّهُمْ إِلَىٰ نُصُبٍ يُوفِضُونَ ۝ خَاشِعَةً أَبْصَارُهُمْ تَرْهَقُهُمْ ذِلَّةٌ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ الْيَوْمُ الَّذِي كَانُوا يُوعَدُونَ

So what is [the matter] with those who disbelieve, rushing toward you [O Muhammad], from the right and from the left in separate groups? Does every one of them aspire to be admitted to a garden of pleasure? No — indeed, We have created them from that which they know. So I swear by the Lord of [all] risings and settings, indeed We are able to replace them with better than them, and We are not to be outrun. So leave them to converse vainly and amuse themselves until they meet their Day which they are promised — the Day they will emerge from the graves rapidly, as if they were racing toward a raised marker, their eyes humbled, overtaken by humiliation. That is the Day which they had been promised.

al-Maʿārij 70:36–44

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

The sūrah opens with a questioner demanding the punishment promised to the disbelievers — asked either in mockery or impatience. Allah answers that it is coming, bound to happen, from none but Him, ذِي الْمَعَارِج — the Owner of the ways of ascent. Al-Saʿdī explains al-maʿārij as the elevated ways and stairways by which the angels ascend to Allah, befitting His majesty above His creation, and that the same title reflects His supreme, all-encompassing rule over the heavens. The angels and the Spirit — Jibrīl (عليه السلام) — ascend to Him during a Day whose extent is fifty thousand years. This is not describing angels experiencing time as we do; rather, it conveys the sheer immensity and difficulty of the Day of Judgment for those who denied it, while for the believer it will be light, as related in well-known reports. Allah then commands the Prophet ﷺ: فَاصْبِرْ صَبْرًا جَمِيلًا — be patient with a beautiful patience, one free of complaint.

لَوْ يَفْتَدِي — The Rejected Ransom

On that Day the criminal will wish he could buy his way out of punishment with his children, his wife, his brother, his nearest kin who sheltered him in this life, and everyone else on earth — every relationship he valued, offered in exchange for himself. Al-Saʿdī: this shows the terror of that Day will be so overwhelming that a person would give up everyone he loves, without hesitation, just to be spared. And yet Allah answers with a single decisive word — كَلَّا, no — the Fire will not release anyone for any ransom. It is described as لَظَى (a blazing Flame) that strips away the scalp, calling out specifically to whoever turned his back on the truth and hoarded wealth rather than spending it as Allah commanded.

هَلُوعًا — Man's Anxious Default: Allah then describes the raw nature man is created with before faith reshapes it: هَلُوع — anxious, impatient when touched by harm, stingy and withholding when given good. Al-Saʿdī: this is the human being left to his own base disposition, swinging between panic and greed. But immediately Allah lists the exception — إِلَّا الْمُصَلِّينَ, except those who pray — and what follows is one continuous description of the believer's character: constant in prayer, giving a known right of their wealth to the one who asks and the one too proud to ask, believing firmly in the Day of Recompense, ever fearful of their Lord's punishment, guarding their chastity except within the bounds Allah permitted, faithful to trusts and promises, upright and truthful in testimony, and careful guardians of their prayer from beginning to end.

From Anxiety to Honor — A Full Character, Not Isolated Acts

Notice the list opens and closes with prayer (āyāt 23 and 34) — a structure many scholars of tafsīr have observed frames the whole passage, with charity, belief in the Day of Recompense, fear of Allah, chastity, trustworthiness, and truthful testimony held inside it. This mirrors the similar description of the successful believers at the opening of Sūrat al-Muʾminūn. The lesson is that these are not a checklist of disconnected good deeds — they describe a transformed character, where regular prayer, sincere fear of Allah, and honesty in dealings all grow from the same root: real īmān that has replaced the raw haluʿ nature described at the start.

The Closing Scene: The sūrah ends by returning to the disbelievers of Quraysh, rushing toward the Prophet ﷺ in small scornful groups from every side, as if any of them could simply wish his way into a garden of bliss without believing. Allah answers plainly: they were created from what they already know — a mere fluid, weak and lowly in origin — so what right have they to arrogance? Allah swears by the Lord of every point of sunrise and sunset across the year that He is able to replace them with better than them, and nothing can outrun His decree. The sūrah closes with the resurrection: people rising from their graves in a rush, as if racing toward a raised marker, their eyes lowered and humiliation covering them — the very Day they had been promised and had denied.
