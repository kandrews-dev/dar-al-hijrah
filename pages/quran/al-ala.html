Sūrah 87 · Juzʾ ʿAmma · Makki · 19 āyāt

سورة الأعلى

Sūrat al-Aʿlā — The Most High

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Know what سَبِّحِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ الْأَعْلَى commands — and what glorification of Allah includes.
  • Know the promise of Allah to His Messenger ﷺ: We will teach you and you will not forget.
  • Understand the two categories of reminder-recipients and the condition for reminding being beneficial.
  • Know al-Saʿdī's statement: love of this world and giving it precedence over the hereafter is the root of all sin.
الآياتThe Āyāt

سَبِّحِ ٱسْمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلْأَعْلَى ۝ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ ۝ وَٱلَّذِى قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ ۝ وَٱلَّذِى أَخْرَجَ ٱلْمَرْعَىٰ ۝ فَجَعَلَهُۥ غُثَآءً أَحْوَىٰ ۝ سَنُقْرِئُكَ فَلَا تَنسَىٰٓ ۝ إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ إِنَّهُۥ يَعْلَمُ ٱلْجَهْرَ وَمَا يَخْفَىٰ ۝ وَنُيَسِّرُكَ لِلْيُسْرَىٰ ۝ فَذَكِّرْ إِن نَّفَعَتِ ٱلذِّكْرَىٰ ۝ سَيَذَّكَّرُ مَن يَخْشَىٰ ۝ وَيَتَجَنَّبُهَا ٱلْأَشْقَى ۝ ٱلَّذِى يَصْلَى ٱلنَّارَ ٱلْكُبْرَىٰ ۝ ثُمَّ لَا يَمُوتُ فِيهَا وَلَا يَحْيَىٰ ۝ قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ ۝ وَذَكَرَ ٱسْمَ رَبِّهِۦ فَصَلَّىٰ ۝ بَلْ تُؤْثِرُونَ ٱلْحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنْيَا ۝ وَٱلْءَاخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَىٰ ۝ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَفِى ٱلصُّحُفِ ٱلْأُولَىٰ ۝ صُحُفِ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ

Glorify the name of your Lord, the Most High — Who created and fashioned in due proportion — and Who made all things according to a measure, then guided them — and Who brings forth the pasturage — then makes it wither after it was lush and green. We will teach you, so you will not forget — except what Allah wills, for He knows what is open and what is hidden — and We will guide you to the easy path. So remind people, if the reminder will be of benefit. The one who fears Allah will pay heed — while the wretched one will avoid it — the one who will enter the great fire — in which he will neither die nor live. He will indeed attain true success who purifies himself — and remembers the name of his Lord and prays. But you prefer the life of this world — even though the hereafter is better and more lasting. Verily this was in the earlier scriptures — the scriptures of Ibrāhīm and Mūsā.

al-Aʿlā 87:1–19

التفسيرExplanation — based on Imam al-Saʿdī

سَبِّحِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ الْأَعْلَى (Glorify the name of your Lord, the Most High): Al-Saʿdī explains: glorifying Allah includes remembering Him, worshipping Him, humbling oneself before His Majesty, and submitting to His greatness. That glorification should befit His greatness by mentioning His names — which are more sublime than any other names because of their beautiful and great meanings. Among His actions He mentions: He created and perfected all creatures; He made all things according to a measure and guided them to attain what is in their best interests (general guidance); He brings forth pasturage then makes it wither — showing His complete control over the cycle of life.

سَنُقْرِئُكَ فَلَا تَنسَى (We will teach you, so you will not forget): Great glad tidings to the Messenger ﷺ — Allah would teach him knowledge and not cause him to forget it, so that the Quran would remain preserved in his heart. Except what Allah wills (according to His wisdom, for an ultimate purpose). Then: وَنُيَسِّرُكَ لِلْيُسْرَى — We will guide you to the easy path. Al-Saʿdī: Allah would guide His Messenger to the easy option in all his affairs, and would make His law and religion easy.

فَذَكِّرْ إِن نَفَعَتِ الذِّكْرَى (Remind — if the reminder will be of benefit): Al-Saʿdī: remind so long as the reminder will be accepted and listened to. The implication: if the reminder will lead to more wickedness or detract from goodness, then it is not enjoined; rather it is prohibited. People fall into two categories: (1) those who fear Allah — they will pay heed; (2) the wretched one — he avoids it. The wretched one will enter the great fire where he will neither die nor live.

قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّى وَذَكَرَ اسْمَ رَبِّهِ فَصَلَّى (He will indeed attain true success who purifies himself): The one who purifies himself of polytheism, wrongdoing, and bad characteristics, and in whom remembrance of Allah becomes second nature — motivating him to do what is pleasing to Allah, especially prayer (the foremost indicator of faith).

قاعدة كبرى من السعدي — a major principle

"Love of this world and giving it precedence over the hereafter is the root of all sin."

Al-Saʿdī draws this principle from بَلْ تُؤْثِرُونَ الْحَيَاةَ الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَى. The hereafter is better than this world in all respects, and more lasting because it is eternal and pure. The wise believer does not choose that which is inferior over that which is superior, nor fleeting pleasure at the expense of eternal misery.

إِنَّ هَذَا لَفِي الصُّحُفِ الْأُولَى صُحُفِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَمُوسَى: The sublime commands in this sūrah were already in the scriptures of Ibrāhīm and Mūsā (the two noblest Messengers after Muḥammad ﷺ). Al-Saʿdī: these commands lead to the best outcome in both realms, and serve people's interests in all times and places. This is why they have been in every Prophet's teaching from the beginning.

Memorize & Hold Fast — احفظ وتمسّك

The formula for true success in six words: purify yourself → remember Allah's names → pray. And the warning in contrast: you prefer this world when the next is better and more lasting. This sūrah was in the scriptures of Ibrāhīm and Mūsā. This family school is teaching what all the Prophets taught.
