ar-Raḥīq al-Makhtūm · The Sealed Nectar

السيرة النبوية

The Life of the Prophet ﷺ

The life of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ in order, told through ar-Raḥīq al-Makhtūm (The Sealed Nectar) of al-Mubārakfūrī — from Arabia before Islam to the Farewell Pilgrimage, with the lessons drawn from each stage. All six stages, from his noble birth to his passing ﷺ, are complete.
