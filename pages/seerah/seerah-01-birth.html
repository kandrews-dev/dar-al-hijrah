Stage 1 · Lesson 1

الجاهلية والمولد

Arabia & the Noble Birth

Objectives — أهداف الدرس

  • Understand the state of Arabia before Islam (al-Jāhiliyyah).
  • Know the noble lineage of the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Learn about the Year of the Elephant and his birth.
  • Trace his early life and the title al-Amīn.
جَزِيرَةُ العَرَبِ قَبْلَ الإِسْلَام Arabia Before Islam

The الكَعْبَة (Kaʿbah) was first raised by the Prophet إِبْرَاهِيم (Ibrāhīm) and his son إِسْمَاعِيل (Ismāʿīl) — peace be upon them — as the first house built for the worship of Allah alone. Over the centuries the Arabs drifted from that pure monotheism into idol-worship, until some 360 idols stood around it and tribes called on the likes of اللَّاتَ والعُزَّى وهُبَل (al-Lāt, al-ʿUzzā, Hubal). This age is called الجَاهِلِيَّة (al-Jāhiliyyah) — the age of ignorance. The Arabs held real virtues — generosity, courage, loyalty, honouring the guest, and mastery of their language — yet alongside them were grave evils: shirk, tribal warfare, oppression of the weak, and in some tribes the burying of baby girls alive. Into this world the light of guidance was about to come. The قُرَيْش (Quraysh) were the custodians of the Kaʿbah and the leading tribe of مَكَّة (Makkah).

نَسَبُهُ الشَّرِيف His Noble Lineage

مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَبْدِ المُطَّلِبِ بْنِ هَاشِمٍ

Muḥammad, son of ʿAbdullāh, son of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib, son of Hāshim — of بَنِي هَاشِم (Banū Hāshim), the noblest branch of Quraysh.

His lineage traces back to إِسْمَاعِيل بْنِ إِبْرَاهِيم (Ismāʿīl ibn Ibrāhīm). Allah chose him ﷺ from the best of the Arabs, from the best clan, from the purest of households — so his nobility of descent matched the nobility of his character.

عَامُ الفِيلِ والمَوْلِد The Year of the Elephant & His Birth

The year of his birth (around 570 CE) is known as عَامُ الفِيل (ʿĀm al-Fīl) — the Year of the Elephant. It was named after أَبْرَهَة (Abraha), the ruler of Yemen, who marched on Makkah with an army — including an elephant — to destroy the Kaʿbah. Allah destroyed them before they could: He sent against them flocks of birds pelting them with stones of baked clay, as told in سُورَةِ الفِيل (Sūrat al-Fīl). The Sacred House was protected by its Lord.

His father, عَبْدُ اللَّه (ʿAbdullāh), had passed away before he was born; his mother was آمِنَةُ بِنْتُ وَهْب (Āminah bint Wahb). The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was born in Makkah, on a Monday in the month of رَبِيعِ الأَوَّل (Rabīʿ al-Awwal) — an orphan, يَتِيم (yatīm), from his very first breath.

نَشْأَتُهُ His Early Life

As was the custom of Quraysh, the infant was given to حَلِيمَةَ السَّعْدِيَّة (Ḥalīmah as-Saʿdiyyah) of بَنِي سَعْد (Banū Saʿd) to be nursed in the desert — a place of clean air and pure Arabic. Her household was blessed by his presence. His mother آمِنَة died when he was about six; his grandfather عَبْدُ المُطَّلِب (ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib) then cared for him, but passed away when he was about eight. His uncle أَبُو طَالِب (Abū Ṭālib) then took him in, loved him, and protected him for many years to come.

Even before prophethood — الصَّادِقُ الأَمِين

He ﷺ grew up keeping away from the idols and evils of his people, known to all for his honesty, until they called him الصَّادِق (aṣ-Ṣādiq — the truthful) and الأَمِين (al-Amīn — the trustworthy). They would entrust their wealth to him for safekeeping. This pure character — before a single verse was revealed — was a sign of the one Allah was preparing to carry His final message.
