معنى الزكاة ومكانتهاThe Meaning & Status of Zakāh

Zakāh (زكاة) is the third pillar of Islam. The word itself (root ز-ك-و) means both purification and growth — for giving zakāh purifies the wealth and the soul of the giver, and causes blessing and growth in what remains. In the Quran, zakāh is paired with prayer in over thirty places — the link between a Muslim's duty to Allah and to fellow people.

خُذْ مِنْ أَمْوَالِهِمْ صَدَقَةً تُطَهِّرُهُمْ وَتُزَكِّيهِم بِهَا

"Take from their wealth a charity by which you purify them and cause them to grow."

Sūrat al-Tawbah 9:103 · سورة التوبة

Zakāh is not a tax or a favour — it is a right the poor have over the wealth of the rich, ordained by Allah. Denying its obligation is disbelief, and the Companions fought those who withheld it after the Prophet ﷺ.

شروط وجوب الزكاةWhen Zakāh Becomes Obligatory

Zakāh is due when these conditions are met in the owner and the wealth:

الإسلام والحرية

A free Muslim (zakāh is an Islamic act of worship).

الملك التام

Full ownership of the wealth.

بلوغ النصاب

The wealth reaches the niṣāb (minimum threshold).

حولان الحول

A full lunar year passes on it (except crops & fruits — due at harvest).

النصاب ومقدار الزكاةThe Niṣāb & the Rate

The niṣāb is the minimum amount of wealth a person must own before zakāh is due. Below it, no zakāh is owed. The standard rate on money, gold, silver, and trade goods is 2.5% — called رُبُع العُشْر (a quarter of a tenth).

WealthNiṣāb (threshold)Rate due
الذهب Gold85 grams (20 mithqāl / dinars)2.5%
الفضة Silver595 grams (200 dirhams)2.5%
النقود Cash / savingsEquivalent of the gold or silver niṣāb in value2.5%
عروض التجارة Trade goodsValue reaches the niṣāb2.5% of stock value
الزروع والثمار Crops & fruits5 wasq (~612 kg)10% if rain-fed, 5% if irrigated

Practical note: for cash savings today, scholars often advise using the silver niṣāb (lower threshold) so more poor people benefit. A simple way: if your savings have sat above the niṣāb for one lunar year, you give 2.5% of the total. Livestock (camels, cattle, sheep) has its own detailed scales.

Items of personal use — your home, car, clothes, and furniture — carry no zakāh, because they are not wealth being grown or saved. Zakāh targets surplus wealth that grows or is stored.

تجب الزكاة في بيت السكن والسيارة الشخصية. Zakāh is due on one's home and personal car.
