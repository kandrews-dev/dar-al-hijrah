مَصَارِفُ الزَّكَاةِ وَزَكَاةُ الفِطْر

The Recipients of Zakāh & Zakāt al-Fiṭr

Year 2+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Name the eight categories who may receive zakāh (al-Tawbah 9:60)
  • Explain who may NOT be given zakāh
  • Explain Zakāt al-Fiṭr — its amount, timing, and purpose
  • Distinguish Zakāt al-Māl from Zakāt al-Fiṭr
آيَةُ المَصَارِفThe Verse of the Eight Recipients

In L.0 we learned who pays zakāh and how much. Now: who receives it? Allah did not leave this to people's choice — He named the recipients precisely in one verse, the famous آية المصارف (the verse of the eight categories):

إِنَّمَا الصَّدَقَاتُ لِلْفُقَرَاءِ وَالْمَسَاكِينِ وَالْعَامِلِينَ عَلَيْهَا وَالْمُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمْ وَفِي الرِّقَابِ وَالْغَارِمِينَ وَفِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ وَابْنِ السَّبِيلِ ۖ فَرِيضَةً مِّنَ اللَّهِ

"Zakāh is only for the poor, the needy, those who collect it, those whose hearts are to be reconciled, for freeing captives, for those in debt, for the cause of Allah, and for the stranded traveller — an obligation from Allah."

Sūrat al-Tawbah 9:60 · سورة التوبة

الأَصْنَافُ الثَّمَانِيَةThe Eight Categories
1

الفُقَرَاء

The poor — those with little or no income who cannot meet their basic needs.

2

المَسَاكِين

The needy — those whose income covers only part of their needs (a degree above the absolute poor, per most scholars).

3

العَامِلُونَ عَلَيْهَا

Those who collect it — the workers appointed by the authority to gather and distribute zakāh; paid from it even if not poor.

4

المُؤَلَّفَةُ قُلُوبُهُم

Those whose hearts are to be reconciled — new Muslims or those inclined toward Islam, given to strengthen their faith.

5

فِي الرِّقَاب

Freeing necks — helping slaves buy their freedom and ransoming Muslim captives.

6

الغَارِمُون

The debt-laden — those burdened by debt they cannot repay (for a lawful or reconciliatory cause).

7

فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّه

In the cause of Allah — primarily striving in Allah's path (jihād) and its needs; many scholars include broader avenues of supporting the religion.

8

ابْنُ السَّبِيل

The stranded traveller — one cut off on a journey with no access to his wealth, given enough to reach home.

مَنْ لَا تُعْطَى لَهُمُ الزَّكَاةWho May NOT Receive Zakāh
  • The wealthy and the able-bodied who can earn — "there is no share in it for a rich person nor for a strong, able earner" (Abū Dāwūd).
  • One's own dependants whom he is already obliged to support — parents, children, and spouse (you cannot give your obligatory zakāh to those you must spend on anyway).
  • Non-Muslims for the obligatory zakāt al-māl (general charity, ṣadaqah, to them is encouraged — but not the obligatory zakāh, except category 4 in its scope).
زَكَاةُ الفِطْرZakāt al-Fiṭr — the Fast-Breaking Charity

Separate from the zakāh on wealth (zakāt al-māl) is Zakāt al-Fiṭr — a charity tied to the person, given at the end of Ramadan. The Prophet ﷺ made it obligatory on every Muslim — young and old, male and female, free and bonded.

What & how much

One ṣāʿ (~2.5–3 kg) of the staple food — dates, barley, wheat, rice — per person. Paid on behalf of oneself and one's dependants.

When

From the night before ʿĪd, and best before the ʿĪd prayer. If delayed past the prayer, it counts only as ordinary charity.

Why

"...a purification for the fasting person from idle and indecent talk, and food for the poor" (Abū Dāwūd, Ibn ʿAbbās). It lets the poor share in the joy of ʿĪd.

فَرَضَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ زَكَاةَ الْفِطْرِ صَاعًا مِنْ تَمْرٍ أَوْ صَاعًا مِنْ شَعِيرٍ

"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ made Zakāt al-Fiṭr obligatory — a ṣāʿ of dates or a ṣāʿ of barley..."

Narrated by al-Bukhārī & Muslim — from Ibn ʿUmar · متفق عليه

Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
مصارف الزكاة ثمانية ذُكرت في سورة التوبة. The recipients of zakāh are eight, named in Sūrat al-Tawbah.
يجوز دفع الزكاة الواجبة للغني القادر على الكسب. It is allowed to give obligatory zakāh to a wealthy, able-bodied earner.
زكاة الفطر صاع من طعام عن كل شخص. Zakāt al-Fiṭr is one ṣāʿ of food per person.
الأفضل إخراج زكاة الفطر قبل صلاة العيد. It is best to give Zakāt al-Fiṭr before the ʿĪd prayer.
يجوز دفع الزكاة الواجبة للوالدين والأبناء الذين تجب نفقتهم. One may give obligatory zakāh to parents and children one is obliged to support.
