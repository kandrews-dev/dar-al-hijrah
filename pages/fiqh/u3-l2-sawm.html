الصيام

Ṣawm — Fasting the Month of Ramadan

Year 2+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Define ṣawm and explain why it is the fourth pillar
  • State on whom fasting is obligatory and who is excused
  • List what breaks the fast and what does not
  • Describe the recommended acts and the virtue of Laylat al-Qadr
معنى الصيام ومكانتهThe Meaning & Status of Fasting

Ṣawm (صيام) is the fourth pillar of Islam. The word (root ص-و-م) means to abstain. In the Sharīʿah it is to abstain from food, drink, and marital relations — with the intention of worship — from the break of true dawn (Fajr) until sunset (Maghrib), throughout the blessed month of Ramadan.

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ

"O you who believe, fasting is prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, so that you may attain taqwā (God-consciousness)."

Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:183 · سورة البقرة

The goal named in the verse is taqwā. In a ḥadīth qudsī, Allah says: "Every deed of the son of Ādam is for him, except fasting — it is for Me, and I shall reward it" (Bukhārī & Muslim) — for fasting is a secret between the servant and his Lord.

على من يجب الصيام؟Who Must Fast — and Who Is Excused

✓ Obligatory on

Every Muslim who is adult (bāligh), sane, able, resident (not travelling), and free of impediments. Children are encouraged to fast to train.

⊘ Excused (make up later / fidyah)

The sick and the traveller (make up the days — 2:185). The menstruating woman and one in nifās (make up later). The elderly or chronically ill who cannot fast feed a poor person (fidyah) per day.

فَمَن شَهِدَ مِنكُمُ الشَّهْرَ فَلْيَصُمْهُ ۖ وَمَن كَانَ مَرِيضًا أَوْ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٍ فَعِدَّةٌ مِّنْ أَيَّامٍ أُخَرَ

"So whoever witnesses the month, let him fast it; and whoever is ill or on a journey — then a number of other days [to make up]."

Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:185 · سورة البقرة

مفسدات الصومWhat Breaks the Fast (Mufaṭṭirāt)

✗ Breaks the fast

  • Eating or drinking deliberately
  • Marital relations (intercourse — also requires kaffārah)
  • Deliberate vomiting
  • The onset of menses or nifās
  • Intending to break the fast

✓ Does NOT break it

  • Eating/drinking by forgetfulness
  • Unintentional vomiting
  • Rinsing the mouth/nose (without exaggeration)
  • Tasting food without swallowing, miswāk, injections of non-nourishment (per many scholars)

«مَنْ نَسِيَ وَهُوَ صَائِمٌ فَأَكَلَ أَوْ شَرِبَ فَلْيُتِمَّ صَوْمَهُ، فَإِنَّمَا أَطْعَمَهُ اللهُ وَسَقَاهُ»

"Whoever forgets while fasting and eats or drinks, let him complete his fast — for it was Allah who fed him and gave him drink."

Narrated by al-Bukhārī & Muslim · متفق عليه

سنن الصيامRecommended Acts of Fasting

🌙

Suḥūr

the pre-dawn meal — "there is blessing in it"

🌅

Hasten ifṭār

break the fast quickly at sunset, on dates & water

🤲

Duʿāʾ

the fasting person's supplication is answered

📖

Quran & charity

the Prophet ﷺ was most generous in Ramadan

🕌

Tarāwīḥ

the night prayer of Ramadan

Fasting is not only of the stomach. The Prophet ﷺ warned: "Whoever does not give up false speech and acting upon it, Allah has no need of his giving up food and drink" (Bukhārī). Guard the tongue, eyes, and heart.

ليلة القدرLaylat al-Qadr — the Night of Decree

In the last ten nights of Ramadan lies a night greater than a thousand months — Laylat al-Qadr. The Prophet ﷺ would strive in worship in these nights more than any other. Seek it especially on the odd nights (21, 23, 25, 27, 29).

لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ

"The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months."

Sūrat al-Qadr 97:3 · سورة القدر

The duʿāʾ the Prophet ﷺ taught for this night: اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي — "O Allah, You are Pardoning and love to pardon, so pardon me."
