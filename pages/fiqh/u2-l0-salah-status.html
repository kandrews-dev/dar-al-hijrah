The Status & Times of Prayer

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Explain why prayer is the greatest pillar after the shahādah
  • Name the five daily prayers and the number of rakʿahs in each
  • State the time of each of the five prayers

مَكَانَةُ الصَّلَاةِ فِي الإِسْلَامThe Status of Prayer in Islam

Having learned how to purify ourselves in Unit 1, we now turn to what purification prepares us for: ṣalāh (صلاة). Prayer is the second pillar of Islam, and the greatest pillar after the testimony of faith. Its rank is unique:

⚖️

First thing judged

"The first deed for which a servant is brought to account on the Day of Judgement is the prayer." (al-Tirmidhī, al-Nasāʾī)

🕋

Decreed above the heavens

It is the only act of worship Allah ordained directly to the Prophet ﷺ during al-Isrāʾ wa-l-Miʿrāj — a sign of its honour.

🚪

The dividing line

"Between a man and shirk and disbelief is the abandonment of prayer." (Muslim) — it separates faith from its loss.

إِنَّ الصَّلَاةَ كَانَتْ عَلَى الْمُؤْمِنِينَ كِتَابًا مَّوْقُوتًا

"Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers a decree of specified times."

Sūrat al-Nisāʾ 4:103 · سورة النساء

الصَّلَوَاتُ الخَمْسThe Five Daily Prayers

Allah obligated five prayers every day and night. Though originally fifty, Allah reduced them to five in deed but kept the reward of fifty — a mercy and generosity. Here they are, with the number of obligatory rakʿahs:

الفجر

Fajr

2

الظهر

Ẓuhr

4

العصر

ʿAṣr

4

المغرب

Maghrib

3

العشاء

ʿIshāʾ

4

Total: 17 obligatory rakʿahs each day — 2 + 4 + 4 + 3 + 4.

مَوَاقِيتُ الصَّلَاةThe Prayer Times

Each prayer has a window of time set by signs in the sky, taught by the Prophet ﷺ (when Jibrīl led him at the start and end of each time over two days). Praying before its time is invalid; delaying it past its time without excuse is a major sin.

PrayerBeginsEnds
الفجر Fajr (2)True dawn (al-fajr al-ṣādiq) — light spreading across the horizonSunrise (ṭulūʿ al-shams)
الظهر Ẓuhr (4)The sun passes its zenith (zawāl — begins to decline from midday)When an object's shadow equals its own length
العصر ʿAṣr (4)End of Ẓuhr (shadow = object's length)Sunset — but pray it before the sun yellows
المغرب Maghrib (3)Sunset (ghurūb al-shams)The red twilight (al-shafaq al-aḥmar) disappears
العشاء ʿIshāʾ (4)Disappearance of the red twilightMiddle of the night (and, by necessity, until Fajr)

أَقِمِ الصَّلَاةَ لِدُلُوكِ الشَّمْسِ إِلَىٰ غَسَقِ اللَّيْلِ وَقُرْآنَ الْفَجْرِ ۖ إِنَّ قُرْآنَ الْفَجْرِ كَانَ مَشْهُودًا

"Establish prayer at the decline of the sun until the darkness of the night, and [recite] the Qur'an at dawn. Indeed, the recitation of dawn is ever witnessed."

Sūrat al-Isrāʾ 17:78 · سورة الإسراء

This single verse gathers the timings of all five: dulūk al-shams (Ẓuhr & ʿAṣr), ghasaq al-layl (Maghrib & ʿIshāʾ), and Qurʾān al-fajr (Fajr).

Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
الصلاة هي الركن الثاني من أركان الإسلام. Prayer is the second pillar of Islam.
أول ما يحاسب عليه العبد يوم القيامة الصلاة. The first deed a servant is judged for is the prayer.
صلاة المغرب أربع ركعات. The Maghrib prayer is four rakʿahs.
للصلاة أوقات محددة لا تصح قبلها. Prayer has fixed times before which it is not valid.
يبدأ وقت الفجر بطلوع الفجر الصادق وينتهي بطلوع الشمس. Fajr begins at true dawn and ends at sunrise.
