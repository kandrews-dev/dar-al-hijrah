مناسك الحج والعمرة

The Rites of Ḥajj & ʿUmrah

Year 2+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Name the three types of Ḥajj
  • List the four pillars (arkān) of Ḥajj
  • Put the day-by-day rites of Ḥajj in order
  • Describe the rites of ʿUmrah
أنواع الحج الثلاثةThree Types of Ḥajj

A pilgrim chooses one of three ways to perform the Ḥajj:

التمتع

Tamattuʿ

ʿUmrah first, exit iḥrām, then enter iḥrām again for Ḥajj. (Includes the sacrifice; recommended for most.)

القِران

Qirān

ʿUmrah and Ḥajj together in one iḥrām without exiting. (Includes the sacrifice.)

الإفراد

Ifrād

Ḥajj alone, with no ʿUmrah joined to it. (No sacrifice required.)

أركان الحج الأربعةThe Four Pillars of Ḥajj

These four are the essentials; the Ḥajj is not complete without them — especially the standing at ʿArafah:

الإحرام

Iḥrām (intention to begin)

الوقوف بعرفة

Standing at ʿArafah

طواف الإفاضة

Ṭawāf al-Ifāḍah

السعي

Saʿy (Ṣafā & Marwah)

«الحَجُّ عَرَفَةُ»

"The Ḥajj is ʿArafah."

Narrated by al-Tirmidhī, al-Nasāʾī & others — ṣaḥīḥ · رواه الترمذي

Whoever misses the standing at ʿArafah in its time has missed the Ḥajj. This is why it is the greatest pillar.

أيام الحج بالترتيبThe Rites Day by Day
8th Dhū al-Ḥijjah · يوم التروية

الإحرام والذهاب إلى منى

Enter iḥrām & go to Minā

Pilgrims enter iḥrām for Ḥajj and spend the day and night at Minā, praying the five prayers (shortened).

9th · يوم عرفة

الوقوف بعرفة

Standing at ʿArafah

The greatest day: stand on the plain of ʿArafah from noon to sunset in duʿāʾ and remembrance. "The Ḥajj is ʿArafah." Then depart to Muzdalifah.

Night of 10th · المزدلفة

المبيت بمزدلفة

Night at Muzdalifah

Pray Maghrib & ʿIshāʾ together, rest the night, and gather pebbles for the stoning.

10th · يوم النحر (العيد)

الرمي والنحر والحلق وطواف الإفاضة

Stoning, sacrifice, shaving, ṭawāf

The day of ʿĪd: stone Jamrat al-ʿAqabah, offer the sacrifice (hady), shave/trim the hair, then perform Ṭawāf al-Ifāḍah and the saʿy.

11th–13th · أيام التشريق

المبيت بمنى ورمي الجمرات

Days of Tashrīq at Minā

Spend the nights at Minā and stone the three Jamarāt each afternoon. One may leave after the 12th (taʿajjul) or stay for the 13th.

Departure · الوداع

طواف الوداع

The Farewell Ṭawāf

The last act before leaving Makkah — a farewell circuit of the Kaʿbah, so the last covenant with the House is its circling.

العمرةʿUmrah — the Lesser Pilgrimage

ʿUmrah is a shorter visit to the Kaʿbah that can be done at any time of year. It has four simple steps:

1. Iḥrām from the mīqāt 2. Ṭawāf (7 circuits of the Kaʿbah) 3. Saʿy (Ṣafā ↔ Marwah, 7 times) 4. Shave or trim the hair

The Prophet ﷺ said: "ʿUmrah to ʿUmrah is an expiation for what is between them, and an accepted Ḥajj has no reward but Paradise" (Bukhārī & Muslim). With this, the five pillars of Islam — shahādah, ṣalāh, zakāh, ṣawm, and Ḥajj — are complete.

يكون الوقوف بعرفة في اليوم التاسع من ذي الحجة. The standing at ʿArafah is on the 9th of Dhū al-Ḥijjah.
العمرة لا تصح إلا في أشهر الحج. ʿUmrah is only valid during the months of Ḥajj.
