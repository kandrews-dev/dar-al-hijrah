Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • State the situations in which it is allowed

رخصة عند فقد الماءA Mercy When There Is No Water

Purification normally uses water — but what if there is no water, or a person cannot use it? Allah, in His mercy, gave the Muslims tayammum (root ي-م-م, "to head toward"): purifying with clean earth instead of water. It stands in for both wudūʾ and ghusl when water is out of reach. This was a special honour given to this ummah.

فَلَمْ تَجِدُوا مَاءً فَتَيَمَّمُوا صَعِيدًا طَيِّبًا فَامْسَحُوا بِوُجُوهِكُمْ وَأَيْدِيكُم مِّنْهُ

"...and you find no water, then make tayammum with clean earth — wipe your faces and your hands with it."

Sūrat al-Māʾidah 5:6 (also al-Nisāʾ 4:43) · سورة المائدة

«وَجُعِلَتْ لِيَ الأَرْضُ مَسْجِدًا وَطَهُورًا»

"The earth has been made for me a place of prayer and a means of purification."

Narrated by al-Bukhārī & Muslim · متفق عليه

متى يجوز التيمم؟When Tayammum Is Allowed

🏜️

فَقْد الماء

No water is available — in the desert, travelling, or after a genuine search.

🤕

العجز عن استعماله

Water exists, but one cannot use it — illness or a wound that water would worsen.

🥶

الخوف من الضرر

Severe cold with no means to warm water, where using it would cause harm (as ʿAmr ibn al-ʿĀṣ did).

💧

الماء قليل يُحتاج إليه

The little water available is needed for drinking (one's own or others').

صفة التيممHow to Perform Tayammum

It is beautifully simple — just three steps:

  • النِّيَّة + التَّسْمِيَة

    Intend purification and say "Bismillāh."

  • ضرب الأرض بالكفّين

    Strike the clean earth/dust once with both palms.

  • مسح الوجه ثم الكفين

    Wipe the face, then wipe the hands (the backs and palms, up to the wrists — the stronger view).

This single tayammum replaces wudūʾ and ghusl — whether your state is minor or major ḥadath, the same simple tayammum suffices to let you pray. Notice it uses only the face and hands — the same two limbs named in the verse, mirroring the start of wudūʾ.

نواقض التيممWhat Nullifies Tayammum
  • 1. Everything that nullifies wudūʾ (from L.1) — using the toilet, deep sleep, etc.
  • 2. Finding water (if it was absent) or becoming able to use it (if one was unable). The Prophet ﷺ said: «فَإِذَا وَجَدَ الْمَاءَ فَلْيَتَّقِ اللهَ وَلْيُمِسَّهُ بَشَرَتَهُ» — "When he finds water, let him fear Allah and let it touch his skin."
  • Note: if a person prayed validly with tayammum and only found water after the prayer, that prayer is complete and need not be repeated.
طبّق — Apply ItWater, Khuffs, or Tayammum?

For each case, tap what this person should do. Feedback appears after you choose.

1. A traveller in the desert needs to pray Ẓuhr. He has only a small bottle of water — just enough to drink and survive the journey.

Tayammum. The little water is needed for drinking, so he purifies with clean earth and prays — a clear case of Allah's mercy.

2. A patient has a fresh wound across both arms; doctors warned that water would seriously harm it. Prayer time has come.

Tayammum. Water is present but he is unable to use it without harm — exactly the case the concession was given for. Prayer is never delayed past its time for this.

3. A man made wudūʾ this morning, put on thick socks, and has been at work all day. Water is plentiful. He has minor ḥadath and it is still within the day.

Wipe over the khuffs (L.2). Water is available and he is a resident within the time limit, with socks put on in purity — so he simply renews wudūʾ and wipes over the socks.

4. A person is in a state of janābah at home, with plenty of warm water and no illness. It is time to pray.

Ghusl with water (L.3). Major ḥadath with water available and no excuse — only a full ghusl will do; tayammum and wiping do not apply here.

Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
التيمم يقوم مقام الوضوء والغسل عند فقد الماء. Tayammum replaces both wudūʾ and ghusl when water is unavailable.
يُمسح في التيمم الوجه والكفان. In tayammum one wipes the face and the hands.
من صلى بالتيمم ثم وجد الماء بعد الصلاة وجب عليه إعادتها. One who prayed with tayammum then found water afterward must repeat the prayer.
المرض الذي يضرّه الماء يبيح التيمم ولو كان الماء موجوداً. An illness harmed by water permits tayammum even if water is present.
وجود الماء والقدرة على استعماله ينقض التيمم. Finding water and being able to use it nullifies tayammum.
