آية الوضوءThe Verse of Wudūʾ

Wudūʾ (root و-ض-أ, linked to waḍāʾah — radiance and cleanliness) is the washing of specific limbs that lifts the minor ḥadath (from L.0). Its obligatory acts are taken directly from a single verse:

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا قُمْتُمْ إِلَى الصَّلَاةِ فَاغْسِلُوا وُجُوهَكُمْ وَأَيْدِيَكُمْ إِلَى الْمَرَافِقِ وَامْسَحُوا بِرُءُوسِكُمْ وَأَرْجُلَكُمْ إِلَى الْكَعْبَيْنِ

"O you who believe, when you rise to pray, wash your faces and your hands up to the elbows, wipe your heads, and [wash] your feet up to the ankles."

Sūrat al-Māʾidah 5:6 · سورة المائدة

فرائض الوضوءThe Obligatory Acts (Farāʾiḍ)

From this verse, scholars derive the obligatory acts of wudūʾ — the parts that must be done for it to be valid:

غسل الوجه

Wash the face

غسل اليدين إلى المرفقين

Wash arms to the elbows

مسح الرأس

Wipe the head

غسل الرجلين إلى الكعبين

Wash feet to the ankles

Scholars add a few more from the Sunnah: the intention (niyyah — "Actions are but by intentions"), doing them in order (tartīb) as the verse lists them, and without long pauses (muwālāt). The madhhabs differ on which of these are obligatory versus recommended — but the four limbs of the verse are agreed by all.

صفة الوضوءHow to Perform Wudūʾ — Step by Step

The complete prophetic description — obligatory acts plus the recommended sunan, as the Prophet ﷺ did:

  • النِّيَّة + التَّسْمِيَة

    Intend wudūʾ in the heart and say "Bismillāh."

  • غَسْلُ الكَفَّيْن ثلاثاً

    Wash both hands up to the wrists three times.

  • المَضْمَضَة والاسْتِنْشَاق

    Rinse the mouth and sniff water into the nose (then blow it out) three times.

  • غَسْلُ الوَجْه ثلاثاً

    Wash the face three times — from hairline to chin, ear to ear. farḍ

  • غَسْلُ الذِّرَاعَيْن إلى المرفقين

    Wash the right then left arm, including the elbows, three times. farḍ

  • مَسْحُ الرأس والأُذنين

    Wipe the head once with wet hands (front to back and back), then wipe the ears. farḍ (head)

  • غَسْلُ الرِّجلين إلى الكعبين

    Wash the right then left foot, including the ankles, three times — rubbing between the toes. farḍ

  • الشهادة بعده

    Finish with the testimony: "Ashhadu an lā ilāha illā Allāh..." — the gates of Paradise open for whoever says it after wudūʾ (Muslim).

farḍ marks the four obligatory limbs from the verse. The threefold washing and the mouth/nose/ears are sunnah — extra reward, but wudūʾ is still valid with one wash of each obligatory part in order.

نواقض الوضوءWhat Nullifies Wudūʾ (Nawāqiḍ)

Once you have wudūʾ, it remains until something breaks it. The main nullifiers, agreed upon by the scholars:

  • 1. Anything exiting the two private parts — urine, stool, or wind (gas). «لَا يَنْصَرِفْ حَتَّى يَسْمَعَ صَوْتًا أَوْ يَجِدَ رِيحًا» — "he should not leave until he hears a sound or finds a smell" (al-Bukhārī & Muslim).
  • 2. Deep sleep that removes awareness (lying down) — light dozing while seated does not break it.
  • 3. Loss of consciousness — fainting, intoxication, or anything that removes the mind.
  • 4. Eating camel meat — per the authentic hadith (Muslim). (Some other matters — like touching the private part — are points of scholarly difference.)

Doubt does not break wudūʾ. The Prophet ﷺ taught that if someone is unsure whether they passed wind, they should not leave the prayer "until he hears a sound or finds a smell" (Bukhārī & Muslim) — certainty is not removed by doubt.

التمارينExercises
Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
فرائض الوضوء مأخوذة من آية المائدة 5:6. The obligatory acts of wudūʾ are taken from Sūrat al-Māʾidah 5:6.
الرأس يُمسح ولا يُغسل في الوضوء. The head is wiped, not washed, in wudūʾ.
الشك في خروج الريح ينقض الوضوء. Merely doubting whether wind was passed nullifies wudūʾ.
أكل لحم الإبل ينقض الوضوء. Eating camel meat nullifies wudūʾ.
غسل كل عضو ثلاثاً فرض لا يصح الوضوء بدونه. Washing each limb three times is obligatory and wudūʾ is invalid without it.
