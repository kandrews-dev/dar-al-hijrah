صفة الصلاة (١) — من القيام إلى الركوع

The Description of Prayer (1): Standing to Rukūʿ

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Perform the prayer correctly from the opening takbīr to the bowing
  • Recite the takbīr, opening duʿāʾ, al-Fātiḥah, and the rukūʿ adhkār
  • Identify which actions are pillars (arkān) and which are sunnah
  • Explain why al-Fātiḥah is essential in every rakʿah
كيف نقرأ الوسومHow to Read the Tags

We now learn to pray exactly as the Prophet ﷺ prayed, who said: «صَلُّوا كَمَا رَأَيْتُمُونِي أُصَلِّي» — "Pray as you have seen me pray" (al-Bukhārī). Each step below carries a tag so you know its weight:

RUKN · ركن — pillar (prayer invalid without it) WĀJIB · واجب — obligation (fixed by sujūd al-sahw) SUNNAH · سنة — recommended

We will study the full arkān/wājibāt/sunan classification in L.3. For now, the tags guide your practice.

الخطواتStep by Step — Standing to Bowing
1
القِيَامStandingRUKN

Stand upright facing the qiblah. Standing is a pillar for whoever is able; one who cannot stand prays sitting, then lying on the side (Bukhārī).

2
تكبيرة الإحرامThe Opening TakbīrRUKN

Raise the hands to the shoulders/ears and say the takbīr. This enters you into the prayer — after it, worldly speech and action are forbidden.

اللهُ أَكْبَر

"Allah is the Greatest."

3
وضع اليدين + دعاء الاستفتاحHands & Opening DuʿāʾSUNNAH

Place the right hand over the left on the chest. Then say a quiet opening supplication (istiftāḥ), such as:

سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَى جَدُّكَ، وَلَا إِلَهَ غَيْرُكَ

"Glory and praise be to You, O Allah; blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, and there is no god but You."

4
التعوذ والبسملةSeeking Refuge & BasmalahSUNNAH

Quietly say the taʿawwudh, then the basmalah, before reciting:

أَعُوذُ بِاللهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ ۝ بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

"I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Shayṭān. In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Bestower of Mercy."

5
قراءة الفاتحةReciting al-FātiḥahRUKN

Recite Sūrat al-Fātiḥah in every rakʿah. The Prophet ﷺ said: "There is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book" (Bukhārī & Muslim). After it, say آمِين (Āmīn).

6
قراءة سورةReciting a SūrahSUNNAH

After al-Fātiḥah, recite a sūrah or some verses — in the first two rakʿahs only. In Fajr, Maghrib, and ʿIshāʾ this recitation is aloud; in Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr it is silent.

7
الركوعThe Bowing (Rukūʿ)RUKN

Say "Allāhu Akbar", then bow — back straight and level, hands gripping the knees, with calm stillness (ṭumaʾnīnah, itself a pillar). Say at least three times:

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ

"Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great." (×3)

8
الرفع من الركوع (الاعتدال)Rising from RukūʿRUKN

Rise to stand fully upright, saying while rising, then while standing:

سَمِعَ اللهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ ۝ رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ

"Allah hears the one who praises Him. Our Lord, and to You belongs all praise."

لماذا الفاتحة في كل ركعة؟Why al-Fātiḥah in Every Rakʿah?

Al-Fātiḥah is the heart of the prayer's recitation. In a beautiful ḥadīth qudsī, Allah says He has divided the prayer between Himself and His servant: when the servant says "al-ḥamdu lillāhi Rabb al-ʿālamīn," Allah says "My servant has praised Me" — verse by verse, a living conversation. This is why no rakʿah is complete without it.

«لَا صَلَاةَ لِمَنْ لَمْ يَقْرَأْ بِفَاتِحَةِ الْكِتَابِ»

"There is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book (al-Fātiḥah)."

Narrated by al-Bukhārī & Muslim — from ʿUbādah ibn al-Ṣāmit · متفق عليه
