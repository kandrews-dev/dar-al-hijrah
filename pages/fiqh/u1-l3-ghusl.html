الغُسْل

Ghusl — The Ritual Bath

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Define ghusl and explain that it lifts major ḥadath
  • List the situations that make ghusl obligatory
  • State the obligatory acts of a valid ghusl
  • Describe the complete prophetic ghusl step by step
مَعْنَى الغُسْلWhat Is Ghusl?

Ghusl (root غ-س-ل, "to wash") is washing the entire body with water, with the intention of lifting the major ḥadath (from L.0). Where wudūʾ handles the minor ḥadath, ghusl is what restores purity after the greater states such as janābah. A complete ghusl with intention also lifts the minor ḥadath at the same time.

وَإِن كُنتُمْ جُنُبًا فَاطَّهَّرُوا

"And if you are in a state of janābah, then purify yourselves [by bathing the whole body]."

Sūrat al-Māʾidah 5:6 · سورة المائدة

مُوجِبَاتُ الغُسْلWhen Ghusl Becomes Obligatory

Ghusl is obligatory in these situations:

الجَنَابَة

Janābah — sexual discharge or intercourse.

الحَيْض والنِّفَاس

The end of menses (ḥayḍ) and post-natal bleeding (nifās).

المَوْت

Death — the deceased Muslim is washed (a duty on the living).

دخول الإسلام

Entering Islam — ghusl is taken (recommended; some hold obligatory).

There are also recommended (mustaḥabb) ghusls — most famously the ghusl for Jumuʿah (Friday prayer), and for the two ʿĪds and iḥrām. These earn reward but are not obligatory.

فَرَائِضُ الغُسْلThe Obligatory Acts of Ghusl

A valid ghusl needs only two things — it can be as simple as a sincere, complete wash:

النِّيَّة

The intention to lift the major ḥadath — in the heart, not spoken.

تعميم البدن بالماء

Washing the entire body with water — every part, including rinsing the mouth and nose and wetting the hair to the roots.

That is all that is strictly required. Everything else in the next section is the recommended sunnah way the Prophet ﷺ performed it.

صِفَةُ الغُسْلِ الكَامِلThe Complete Prophetic Ghusl

Based on the descriptions of ʿĀʾishah and Maymūnah (may Allah be pleased with them) in al-Bukhārī and Muslim:

  • النِّيَّة + التَّسْمِيَة

    Intend to lift the major ḥadath and say "Bismillāh."

  • غَسْلُ اليدين والفرج

    Wash the hands, then wash the private parts and remove any filth.

  • الوُضُوء

    Perform a complete wudūʾ as for prayer (L.1).

  • إفاضة الماء على الرأس ثلاثاً

    Pour water over the head three times, working it through the hair to the roots.

  • غسل سائر الجسد

    Pour water over the rest of the body — the right side, then the left — ensuring no spot is left dry.

  • غسل القدمين

    Finally, wash the feet (if not already washed in the wudūʾ step, or move aside and wash them last).

Key point: a complete ghusl with the intention for both states removes the need for a separate wudūʾ — you may pray with it directly, as long as nothing nullified your wudūʾ during the bath.
