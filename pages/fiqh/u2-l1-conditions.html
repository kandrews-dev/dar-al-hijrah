شروط الصلاة

The Conditions of Prayer

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Explain what a sharṭ (condition) is and how it differs from a rukn (pillar)
  • List the conditions that must be met before a valid prayer
  • State the rules for covering the ʿawrah for men and women
  • Explain facing the qiblah and its exceptions
الشرط والركنCondition vs. Pillar

Before we learn how to pray (L.2–L.3), we must learn what makes a prayer eligible to even begin. These are the conditions (شروط, shurūṭ). A sharṭ comes before the prayer and must continue throughout it — unlike a rukn (pillar, L.3), which is a part inside the prayer.

Simple way to remember: a condition is like having a valid ticket before you board — purity, covering, qiblah. A pillar is part of the journey itself — the standing, the bowing, the prostrating. If a condition is missing, the prayer never validly starts.

شروط صحة الصلاةThe Conditions for a Valid Prayer
1

الإسلام والعقل والتمييز

Being a Muslim, of sound mind, and old enough to discern (tamyīz — children are taught at seven). Worship is not accepted from a non-Muslim or one who has lost their reason.

2

دخول الوقت

The time has entered (from L.0). Praying before a prayer's time is invalid — "prayer is a decree of specified times" (4:103).

3

الطهارة من الحدث والنجس

Purity — from ḥadath (by wudūʾ, ghusl, or tayammum — all of Unit 1!) and from najāsah on the body, clothing, and place of prayer.

4

ستر العورة

Covering the ʿawrah — the parts of the body that must be concealed (detailed below).

5

استقبال القبلة

Facing the qiblah — turning toward the Kaʿbah in Makkah (detailed below).

6

النية

The intention in the heart to perform that specific prayer — "Actions are but by intentions" (Bukhārī & Muslim). It is never spoken aloud.

ستر العورةCovering the ʿAwrah

Allah commands beautifying oneself for prayer: خُذُوا زِينَتَكُمْ عِندَ كُلِّ مَسْجِدٍ — "Take your adornment at every place of prayer" (7:31).

الرجل

For the man

At minimum, from the navel to the knee. It is also required to cover the shoulders if able ("none of you should pray with nothing on his shoulders" — Bukhārī).

المرأة

For the woman

Her whole body except the face and hands (and the feet, per many scholars). "Allah does not accept the prayer of a mature woman without a head-cover" (Abū Dāwūd, al-Tirmidhī).

استقبال القبلةFacing the Qiblah

The worshipper turns his face and body toward the Kaʿbah. For those far away, facing its direction is enough (they need not face the exact building). Allah commanded the change of qiblah toward it:

فَوَلِّ وَجْهَكَ شَطْرَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ ۚ وَحَيْثُ مَا كُنتُمْ فَوَلُّوا وُجُوهَكُمْ شَطْرَهُ

"So turn your face toward al-Masjid al-Ḥarām. And wherever you are, turn your faces toward it."

Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:144 · سورة البقرة

Exceptions (Allah's ease): facing the qiblah is excused for the one who cannot determine it and prays after sincere effort, for one in intense fear/danger, and for voluntary prayer while riding/travelling. If a person strives to find the qiblah and prays, then later learns they were mistaken, their prayer is valid.
