صِفَةُ الصَّلَاةِ (٢) — مِنَ السُّجُودِ إِلَى السَّلَام

The Description of Prayer (2): Sujūd to Salām

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Complete the prayer from prostration to the closing salām
  • Recite the sujūd adhkār, the tashahhud, and the ṣalāh on the Prophet ﷺ
  • Name the seven bones of prostration
  • Classify prayer's parts into arkān, wājibāt, and sunan
الخُطُوَاتStep by Step — Prostration to Salām

Continuing from L.2, after rising from rukūʿ:

9
السجودProstration (Sujūd)RUKN

Say "Allāhu Akbar" and prostrate on seven bones: the forehead (with the nose), the two palms, the two knees, and the toes of both feet. Keep the arms off the ground. The closest a servant is to his Lord is in sujūd — so supplicate. Say at least three times:

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى

"Glory be to my Lord, the Most High." (×3)

10
الجلوس بين السجدتينSitting Between the Two ProstrationsRUKN

Say "Allāhu Akbar", sit calmly, and say:

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي، رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي

"My Lord, forgive me; my Lord, forgive me."

11
السجدة الثانية ثم القيامSecond Prostration, then StandRUKN

Prostrate a second time (same dhikr), then rise saying "Allāhu Akbar" for the next rakʿah. This completes one rakʿah. Repeat steps 5–11 for each remaining rakʿah (al-Fātiḥah is recited in every one).

12
التشهد الأولThe First TashahhudWĀJIB

After two rakʿahs (in 3- and 4-rakʿah prayers), sit and recite the tashahhud, then rise to complete the prayer:

التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَى عِبَادِ اللهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ

"All greetings, prayers, and good things are for Allah. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of Allah. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger."

13
التشهد الأخير + الصلاة على النبي ﷺFinal Tashahhud + Ṣalāh on the Prophet ﷺRUKN

In the final sitting, recite the tashahhud above, then send blessings on the Prophet ﷺ, then seek refuge from four things before supplicating:

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

"O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Ibrāhīm and the family of Ibrāhīm; indeed You are Praiseworthy, Glorious." (then seek refuge from the punishment of Hell, the grave, the trials of life and death, and the Dajjāl)

14
التسليمThe Closing SalāmRUKN

End the prayer by turning the face to the right, then the left, saying each time. This exits you from the prayer.

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ

"Peace be upon you, and the mercy of Allah." (right, then left)

الأَرْكَانُ وَالوَاجِبَاتُ وَالسُّنَنPillars, Obligations & Recommended Acts

Not every part of the prayer carries the same weight. Scholars classify them into three levels — and the difference decides what happens if a part is left out:

الأركان · Pillars

Cannot be dropped — even by mistake. Must be done over.

  • Standing (if able)
  • Takbīrat al-iḥrām
  • Reciting al-Fātiḥah
  • Rukūʿ & rising from it
  • Sujūd, sitting between, & ṭumaʾnīnah
  • Final tashahhud & sitting for it
  • The salām

الواجبات · Obligations

If left by mistake, fixed by sujūd al-sahw (L.4), not repeating the prayer.

  • The takbīrs of moving between postures
  • Saying "Samiʿa Allāhu liman ḥamidah" & "Rabbanā wa laka al-ḥamd"
  • "Subḥāna Rabbiya al-ʿAẓīm/al-Aʿlā" once
  • The first tashahhud & sitting for it

السنن · Recommended

Extra reward; nothing is owed if left.

  • The opening duʿāʾ (istiftāḥ)
  • Reciting a sūrah after al-Fātiḥah
  • Raising the hands at the takbīrs
  • Saying the dhikr more than once
  • Supplications in sujūd

The rule in one line: leave a pillar → the rakʿah/prayer is invalid and must be redone; leave an obligation by mistake → make sujūd al-sahw; leave a sunnah → nothing is owed. (This sets up L.4.)

التَّمَارِينExercises
Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
السجود يكون على سبعة أعظم. Prostration is on seven bones.
يقول في السجود: سبحان ربي الأعلى. In sujūd one says: Subḥāna Rabbiya al-Aʿlā.
التشهد الأول ركن تبطل الصلاة بتركه سهواً. The first tashahhud is a pillar; forgetting it invalidates the prayer.
التسليم ركن تنتهي به الصلاة. The salām is a pillar by which the prayer ends.
ترك السنة لا يوجب شيئاً. Leaving a sunnah does not require anything (no sujūd al-sahw).
