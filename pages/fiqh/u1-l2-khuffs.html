المَسْحُ عَلَى الخُفَّيْن

Wiping over the Khuffs

Year 1+

After this lesson you will be able to · بعد هذا الدرس ستتمكن من

  • Explain what wiping over the khuffs is and why it is a mercy
  • State the conditions that make it valid
  • State the time limits for the resident and the traveller
  • Describe how to wipe and what nullifies it
رُخْصَةٌ وَرَحْمَةA Concession from Allah

In L.1 we learned that washing the feet is one of the obligatory acts of wudūʾ. But Allah, out of mercy and ease, allowed a concession (رُخْصَة, rukhṣah): once you have put on your khuffs (foot-coverings) while in a state of purity, you may simply wipe over them instead of washing the feet — for a set period. This spares the believer from repeatedly removing footwear, especially in cold or while travelling.

This ruling is so well-established that it was considered a mark of Ahl al-Sunnah, narrated by a large number of Companions. The خُفّ (khuff) is leather footwear; most contemporary scholars extend the ruling to thick socks (jawrab) that cover the foot.

الدَّلِيلThe Evidence

أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ تَوَضَّأَ وَمَسَحَ عَلَى الخُفَّيْنِ

"The Prophet ﷺ performed wudūʾ and wiped over the two khuffs."

Narrated by al-Bukhārī & Muslim — from al-Mughīrah ibn Shuʿbah · متفق عليه

In the famous incident, al-Mughīrah went to remove the Prophet's khuffs to wash his feet, but he ﷺ said: «دَعْهُمَا فَإِنِّي أَدْخَلْتُهُمَا طَاهِرَتَيْنِ» — "Leave them, for I put them on while my feet were pure" — and he wiped over them. This single sentence gives us the ruling and its key condition.

شُرُوطُ المَسْحThe Conditions for Wiping

For the wiping to be valid, these conditions must be met:

1

أن يلبسهما على طهارة

They must be put on while in a state of complete purity (you made wudūʾ first, then put them on) — as in the hadith above.

2

أن يكونا طاهرين ساترين

The footwear must be pure (free of najāsah) and cover the part of the foot that must be washed (up to the ankles).

3

أن يكون في الحَدَث الأصغر

Wiping replaces washing the feet only for the minor ḥadath (wudūʾ). It does not work for major ḥadath — janābah requires removing them and doing ghusl (L.3).

4

أن يكون في المُدَّة المحددة

It must be within the allowed time period (next section).

مُدَّةُ المَسْحThe Time Limits

The Prophet ﷺ set clear limits, counted from the first wipe after the ḥadath (the stronger view):

1 day

المُقِيم — يوم وليلة

The resident: one day and night (24 hours).

3 days

المُسَافِر — ثلاثة أيام

The traveller: three days and nights (72 hours).

«جَعَلَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ ثَلَاثَةَ أَيَّامٍ وَلَيَالِيَهُنَّ لِلْمُسَافِرِ، وَيَوْمًا وَلَيْلَةً لِلْمُقِيمِ»

"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ set three days and their nights for the traveller, and one day and night for the resident."

Narrated by Muslim — from ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib · رواه مسلم

صِفَةُ المَسْحِ وَنَوَاقِضُهHow to Wipe — and What Breaks It

✓ How to wipe

With wet hands, wipe over the top of each foot once (right then left), from toes toward the shin. You do not wipe the bottom or heel — "if the religion were by opinion, the bottom would be more deserving of wiping than the top" (ʿAlī, Abū Dāwūd).

✗ What ends it

  • The time period ending
  • Removing the khuffs
  • Anything requiring ghusl (major ḥadath)

A subtle point: when the time period ends, your wudūʾ does not automatically break — but you can no longer wipe. The next time you need wudūʾ, you remove the khuffs, wash your feet, and may put them back on afresh to start a new period.

التَّمَارِينExercises
Exercise Aصحيح أم خاطئ
يُشترط لبس الخفين على طهارة. It is a condition to put on the khuffs while in a state of purity.
يجوز المسح على الخفين من الجنابة. One may wipe over the khuffs to lift janābah (major ḥadath).
مدة المسح للمقيم يوم وليلة وللمسافر ثلاثة أيام. The wiping period is one day/night for the resident and three days for the traveller.
يُمسح أسفل الخف لا أعلاه. One wipes the bottom of the khuff, not the top.
خلع الخفين ينهي المسح. Removing the khuffs ends the validity of wiping.
